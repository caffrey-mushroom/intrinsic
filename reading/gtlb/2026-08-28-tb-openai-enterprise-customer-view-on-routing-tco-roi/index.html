原标题：OpenAI – Enterprise Customer Perspective – Real-World Utilisation, Architecture & ROI

#llm #agents #software

Third Bridge · 2026-08-28

核心摘要

  1. 专家是 T-Mobile 软件工程高级总监，从企业买方角度讲 OpenAI 和其他模型怎么选、怎么分流、怎么算 ROI，访谈日 8/13。
  2. OpenAI 的优势在客服和面向客户的流程，对话延迟、上下文保持更好，立刻见效的 ROI 高于软件开发场景。
  3. 写代码他们主要用 Anthropic：约 95% 的任务 Sonnet 3.7 就够，再 3% 用 Opus 4.6，只有约 2% 的全新复杂任务要用最前沿的 Fable。
  4. 开源模型今天处理约 20% 的企业查询，而且在涨；harness 越来越商品化后，靠 A/B 测试和自动路由，把简单、窄领域的任务转给开源，降低推理成本。
  5. 他认为 token 商品化会把价值推向云厂商、Scale AI / Distyl AI 这类驻场集成商和治理平台；Salesforce、ServiceNow 靠自有数据做 grounding 守住份额。
  6. 厂商之间基准接近时就没有理由付溢价，推理成本必须趋同，除非能给出行业专属能力。
  7. 他拿 GitLab 举例：成熟的 SDLC 平台、能 on-prem 也能 SaaS、按 license 分档开功能，是企业想要的一体化形态；现在 AI 这套还得自己用 LangChain、Azure AI、Arize、Langfuse 拼。
  8. 他估计 OpenAI 企业收入（现约 40%）9-12 个月内能和消费者收入持平，前提是做出 SIEM/SOAR 这种企业急需的爆款功能。

Agenda

  • Key criteria when selecting OpenAI over – or in addition to – competing models, such as those from Anthropic, Google Gemini (NASDAQ: GOOG) and open-source options
  • Commoditisation and dynamic routing shift
  • SaaS data moats and implementation value
  • Platform gap and revenue mix trajectory

Key Insights

  • OpenAI holds a distinct advantage in contact centre and customer-facing workflows due to superior conversational latency, context retention and higher immediate ROI over software development tasks
  • Enterprises actively manage model costs. Anthropic's lower-cost Claude model – Sonnet 3.7 – handles c95% of coding tasks adequately and Opus 4.6 covers another c3%, leaving only c2% requiring frontier models such as Claude's advanced offering, Fable, for high-complexity, unprecedented edge cases
  • Open-source models handle c20% of enterprise queries today, which is expected to grow significantly. Automated AI dynamic routing will increasingly direct routine, narrow-domain workloads to open source, reducing inference costs
  • Token commoditisation shifts value towards hyperscalers, forward-deployed integrators – such as Scale AI, and Distyl AI – and governance platforms. Incumbent SaaS providers, such as Salesforce and ServiceNow, defend market share using proprietary data grounding
  • OpenAI's enterprise segment could reach revenue parity with consumer offerings within 9-12 months as enterprise demand shifts towards full-stack AI life cycle management platforms
