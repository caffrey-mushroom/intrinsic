核心摘要

  1. 专家是加拿大一家金融服务公司（约 800 亿美元 AUM）的数字产品 AVP，同时在用 Factory AI 和 Cognition (Devin)，也部署了 Cursor，访谈日 9/8。
  2. Factory 的优点是好集成（直接接 Jira、Confluence）、数据主权强、价格比 Cognition 低；缺点是迭代速度大约只有 Cognition 的 75%，界面不如 Devin 直观。
  3. Devin 和 Factory 功能上接近持平，Devin 在部分应用（如 Guidewire、Ion）上略好，但 credit / token 消耗明显更多。
  4. 受监管和风控限制，目前只有约 35% 的开发工作交给 agent 自主完成，其余 65% 仍要人参与。
  5. Claude Code 功能可比、更便宜，但在他们看来缺数据主权，只用在非客户流程；所以受监管行业选 Factory 或 Cognition 而不是 Claude Code。
  6. 他认同有客户因为已有 Claude 企业 license 而把 Factory 合并到 Claude Code，但认为这些多是不受强监管的公司，他们自己会保持混合方案。
  7. GitHub Copilot 没有异步能力，满足不了 DevOps 场景；功能评分约为 Factory 的 80-90%，他认为 Cortex Code 是最大的新兴威胁。
  8. 长期看他预期 AI 能力提升会减少人类开发者数量。

SUMMARY

Overview

The conversation focuses on the use of AI platforms, specifically Factory AI and Cognition, within a financial services firm. The expert, a chief AI officer, discusses the integration and application of these platforms in their operations, highlighting their roles in improving efficiency and supporting development tasks. The discussion also touches on the competitive landscape, comparing Factory AI and Cognition with other AI solutions like Claude Code, Cortex Code, and GitHub Copilot.

Key Insights

The expert emphasizes the strategic importance of adopting AI platforms like Factory AI and Cognition to enhance application modernization and drive efficiencies in development processes. There is a strong focus on the integration capabilities of Factory AI, particularly its ease of integration with existing DevOps tools and its competitive pricing. Cognition is highlighted for its user-friendly interface and dual modality capabilities, supporting both autonomous and human- assisted operations. The expert notes that while both platforms can handle complex workflows, regulatory and risk considerations currently limit their use to less complex tasks. The potential for reducing human developer headcount in the long term is discussed, with an expectation of increased AI capabilities over time.
