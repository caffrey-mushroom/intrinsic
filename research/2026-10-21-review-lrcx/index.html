Executive Summary

Sep Q 小幅 beat，但质量一般；Dec Q 指引只对齐 Street，没到 buyside。收入 $8.22bn，比指引中值高 1.5%，和我们的 8,229 几乎一致；Systems $5.73bn (+35% q/q) 比我们高 0.4%，CSBG $2.49bn 基本持平，和管理层 7 月说的一样。

问题有三个。第一，中国占比从 26% 跳到 35%，贡献了 q/q 增量约 $1.5bn 里的 $1.13bn，非中国收入只增长约 7%。第二，税率 16.5% (non-GAAP，GAAP 16.9%)，高于 mid-teens：经营层面 beat，但 EPS $2.20 只比中值高 2.3%，是已核对 EPS 指引的 5 个季度里最弱的 beat (此前 +5% 到 +11%)；我们 NI 2,988 高估了 8.5%，几乎全部来自税率。第三，Dec Q 指引 $8.60bn (+4.6% q/q)，和 Street 一致，但 TMTB 说 buyside 在 $9.0-9.1bn。

电话会：CY26 WFE mid-$150bn，CY27 初步 "more than $200 billion" (约 +30%)，增速 DRAM > 先进 foundry/logic > NAND，是受洁净室和零部件约束的数，1 月给更精确的。我们 CY27 WFE 231 (+48%) 明显高于管理层起点，更像全年顺利上调的情景。Dec Q 偏软是供给问题：CFO 说指引 "would have been a bit higher if not for some near supply chain constraints"，没发的货 "we will ship that in the March quarter"，且 "nothing unique, one-off, or episodic"，需求没变；另外中国本土客户 Dec Q 会明显回落 (管理层说在预期内)。CFO 确认 non-GAAP 税率 16.5%，并提醒大家把模型更新到这个水平。

对模型：按指引中值，CY26 收入 $29.4bn / EPS $7.82，比我们的 $30.1bn / $8.46 低 2.4% / 7.6%。税率应上调到 16.5%，CY27 WFE 基准下调到 205-215，CY27 收入按管理层框架推算约 $37-41bn (推断)，低于我们的 $44.3bn。采购专家说的 Q3 新设备约 $4.2bn 被证伪 (实际 $5.73bn)，他的季度口径不再可信。

5. 采购专家的 Q3 新设备约 $4.2bn：不成立

实际 Systems 收入 $5.73bn，比他说的 $4.0-4.3bn (中值约 $4.15bn，和 Q2 持平) 高约 $1.6bn，即 +38%。他的 2025 年数 ($13.0-13.5bn) 和 2Q26 数 (约 $4.2bn vs 实际 $4.25bn) 都对得上，所以口径应该就是 Systems 收入。

  • 结论：他的季度口径作废。他的理由是 "Q3增速有限并非需求疲弱，而是供应链改善速度较慢"，但实际出货和库存消化都比他说的快。
  • 管理层部分印证了他的供给约束：零部件 "a little bit tight"，压了 Dec Q 指引，但 "we'll be caught up very shortly"。
  • 推断：他的年度节奏 (2026 / 2027 出货 +22-25% / +20%) 是我们 CY27 +47% (final preview 44.3 / 30.1) 最大的反证，现在可信度也应下调。但也可能是他只看到部分产品线或工厂，年度节奏仍有参考价值。
  • 一个反向信号：非中国收入只 +7% q/q。如果他主要覆盖非中国客户，他的 "持平" 可能并不离谱。这是猜测，没有证据说明他的覆盖范围。

7. 电话会：Dec Q 判断与全部管理层 color

来源：FactSet CallStreet raw transcript，2026-10-21。中文为转述，英文为原文；"推断" 是我的判断，不是管理层说的。

Dec Q 偏软：供给顺延，不是需求出问题

Dec Q 偏软是供给受限，出货顺延到 March 季 (CY1Q27)，不是需求或逻辑出问题。管理层三次说明：指引本可更高、被零部件约束压低；没发出去的货下一季发；Dec Q 没有一次性因素、和客户需求一致。另外叠加了一个管理层说是预期之内的因素：中国本土客户在 Dec Q 明显回落。注意它说的是顺延一个季度 (March quarter)，不是笼统的 "delay 到 CY27"，也没给被压掉的金额。

  1. Dec Q 收入指引原本会更高一些，被近期的供应链约束压低了。
    "I would mention that this revenue guide would have been a bit higher if not for some near supply chain constraints that we are actively managing." — Bettinger，开场白
  2. UBS 问是否压掉 $300-400mn、March 季能否回到 Systems +10% 左右。CFO 不给数，只说因时间原因 12 月没发的货会在 March 季发。
    "I'm not going to put a specific number on it. In the script, I said it would have been a little bit higher. So it would have been a little bit higher. And yeah, Tim, to the extent that it's something we would have shipped in December because of timing, we will ship that in the March quarter." — Bettinger，回答 UBS Arcuri
  3. TD Cowen 问 Dec Q 年化 WFE 接近 $180bn，是否有一次性收入或涨价。CFO：没有一次性因素，就是客户需求。
    "Krish, no. There is nothing unique, one-off, or episodic, to use your word, in the December quarter. It is just consistent with customer demand, frankly. And like I alluded to, it would have been a little bit higher if not for some of these supply chain constraints, but there is not anything one-off." — Bettinger，回答 TD Cowen Sankar
  4. 中国 Dec Q 会明显下降，主要是本土客户；外资在华客户仍然较强。
    "I expect in December it will be meaningfully down, driven by the domestic customers in China. I expect in the December quarter the multinationals in China likely continue to be reasonably strong, but the percent of revenue in China will be meaningfully down in the December quarter." — Bettinger，回答 TD Cowen
  5. Sep Q 中国走强不是意外，完全符合预期。
    "Krish, to us, it was not a surprise. It was exactly as expected." — Bettinger
  6. 推断：中国回落、总收入仍 +4.6% q/q，说明非中国在加速。若中国回到 28-30%，非中国约 $6.0-6.2bn，比 Sep Q 高 13-16%。UBS 猜的 $300-400mn 如果成立，不受约束的 Dec Q 大约 $8.9-9.0bn，正好是 buyside 的预期 (管理层没确认这个数)。

需求与周期

  • AI 带来前所未有的半导体需求，器件复杂度上升。
    "AI continues to drive unprecedented semiconductor demand and increasing device complexity." — Archer
  • 行业处在多年投资周期的早期，客户可见度延伸到 2028 年。
    "we believe the industry is in the early stages of a multiyear investment cycle, with customer visibility extending through 2028." — Archer
  • 2028 年的可见度是需求端的；现在给精确 WFE 数还太早，是因为洁净室和供应链进度还要看。
    "we have visibility through 2028. That's really from the demand side. ... by the January call ... We'll have a better view of cleanroom schedules of supply chain constraints" — 管理层 (转写稿未标注)，回答 Goldman
  • 据报道过去 18 个月平均每周 token 用量增长约 250 倍；agentic 工作流扩大算力、内存、存储需求。
    "It is reported that average weekly token usage is up roughly 250-fold in the past 18 months." — Archer
  • "$100bn AI capex 对应约 $10bn WFE" 的模型下，明年同样 $10bn 里 Lam 的机会比今年更大。
    "the important thing is that $10 billion next year, Lam's opportunity in that $10 billion would be even greater than it is this year because of the device evolution." — Archer

供给与产能

  • 进入 CY27，洁净室空间和部分零部件的供应约束会继续限制芯片厂和设备商满足终端需求。
    "As we move into calendar year 2027, we see cleanroom space availability and supply chain constraints for certain components continuing to limit chipmakers' and equipment companies' ability to fully meet end demand." — Archer
  • 需求太强，供应链有点紧；不说具体哪个环节，本季只是变紧了一点。
    "given how strong things have been, we're seeing a little bit of tightness. I'm not going to get into specifically where it is ... But it just got a little bit tight in the quarter." — Bettinger，回答 Deutsche
  • 必要时愿意投资供应商，但多数供应商资金充足。
    "if we needed to make some level of investments, we'd be open to that. But frankly, a lot of our suppliers are well-capitalized and can manage this on their own" — Bettinger
  • 和疫情时期的短缺完全不同；近两个月和全球供应商开了几次大会，交代未来几年的增长预期。
    "for those of us who are around during the COVID shortages, this is nothing like that. ... just in the last two months, we've hosted a couple of big events with our global suppliers" — Archer
  • 瓶颈在自家增长特别快的产品线，比如先进封装：部分供应商收到的需求增速远快于 Lam 自身；很快会追上。
    "you can find certain suppliers that are seeing percentage growth of our demand to them rising even much faster than Lam itself is growing. ... I think we'll be caught up very shortly." — Archer
  • 今年主要受洁净室约束；明年年初到年底有 10 座新 Tier 1 晶圆厂陆续投产，同时有新出现的供应链约束。
    "When we look in the next year, we see 10 new Tier 1 fabs coming online between the beginning the year and the end of the year. ... This year has been primarily cleanroom constrained. And then again you see like we said some emerging supply chain constraints" — 管理层 (未标注，开头说 "I'll let Tim add")，回答 Goldman
  • 主动备货：库存增加 $274mn，为预期需求做准备；库存周转 3.4 次，近 7 年最高。
    "Inventory ... was an increase of $274 million from the June quarter, as we're building inventory to meet the expected future demand for our products. ... inventory turns continued to strengthen to 3.4 times" — Bettinger
  • 员工增加约 1,900 人到 24,300，主要在工厂和现场；capex $258mn，扩实验室和全球产能。
    "Headcount increased primarily in our factory and field teams to support expanding manufacturing activities and growing tool installations." — Bettinger
  • 推断：供给约束是真的但幅度不大，管理层的口径是 "一个季度的时点问题"。和采购专家说的 "出货受关键零部件约束" 方向一致，但他的季度数错了。

WFE

  • CY26 WFE 约 mid-$150bn。
    "For calendar year 2026, we expect WFE in the mid $150 billion range." — Archer
  • CY27 初步 >$200bn，增速 DRAM 第一，其次先进 foundry/logic，再次 NAND。
    "Our preliminary 2027 WFE outlook is more than $200 billion of spending, with percentage growth led by DRAM, followed by leading-edge foundry/logic and NAND." — Archer
  • 今年 WFE 从 mid-130 一路上调到 mid-150，是因为客户挤出更多洁净室产能、项目提前。
    "We started mid-130 and then we went to a little bit more than 140 and then a little more than 150. And then we just took you to mid-150s. Basically what we saw happening across all aspects of our customer base is everybody squeezed a little more out of the clean rooms that they had and/or pulled projects in" — Bettinger，回答 BofA
  • >200 只是起点，通常不会这么早给；下季度电话会给更精确的数。
    "with the more than 200 number, we're giving you our initial starting point, more than 200. We normally don't give you this much specificity this early in the year and we'll give you a sharper pencil on that when we get to the earnings call next quarter." — Bettinger
  • 不受约束的需求会更高，但不给数。
    "I'm not going to put a number on what the unconstrained demand would be. It probably – it – not probably, it would be higher." — 管理层 (未标注)，回答 Bernstein
  • DRAM 和 foundry/logic 的差距没有因为新逻辑厂公告而收窄，这些项目他们事先就知道。
    "all of these projects, perhaps that people are now publicly hearing about. We knew about in advance of that. So it really hasn't changed much." — Archer，回答 Citi
  • 推断：去年起点 mid-130、最终 mid-150 (约 +15%)。同样的上调幅度，CY27 会到约 $230bn，即我们 231 的水平。所以 231 是 "全年顺利上调" 的情景，不是基准。

Lam 跑赢 WFE / Systems

  • 算上 Dec Q 指引中值，CY26 Systems 收入增长远超 40%。
    "Including the midpoint of our December quarter guidance, we expect calendar year 2026 systems' revenue to grow well over 40% year-on-year." — Archer
  • 过去几年 SAM 占 WFE 提高 4 个点；2026 年是连续第三年跑赢 WFE。
    "have driven a 4 point expansion of our served available market or SAM, as a percentage of WFE. ... we expect 2026 to mark our third consecutive year of WFE outperformance." — Archer
  • 明年跑赢的驱动是所有器件的沉积和刻蚀强度上升，尤其是先进封装。
    "I would say it's driven by the increase in deposition and etch intensity across all devices. ... Lam's exposure in the advanced packaging space is extremely high." — Archer，回答 RBC
  • 预计下一个日历年继续跑赢。
    "an expectation that we're going to continue to drive those things into the next calendar year." — Ganesh，结束语

各 vertical

  • Memory 占 Systems 46%，和上季持平；金额创纪录，同比 +120%。
    "September quarter Systems revenue in memory was 46%, which was consistent with the prior quarter. On a dollar basis, this represented a record level for memory, and it was up 120% from the same period in 2025." — Bettinger
  • NAND (非易失存储) 占 26% (上季 23%)，金额环比约 +40%，主要是向 256 层以上转换，开始有 384 层支出。
    "non-volatile memory accounted for 26% of our Systems revenue, up from the June quarter's level of 23%. On a dollar basis, NAND revenue increased approximately 40% sequentially ... We are seeing initial spending on 384-layer class devices." — Bettinger
  • DRAM 金额创纪录，占比从 23% 降到 20%；支出集中在 1-alpha / 1-beta / 1-gamma 升级，服务 DDR5、LPDDR5、HBM。
    "DRAM revenue reached a record, growing sequentially on a dollar basis, but down a little bit on a percentage basis from 23% of Systems revenue in the June quarter to 20% in the September quarter." — Bettinger
  • Foundry 创纪录，占比从 44% 升到 46%；来自先进制程 (含先进封装) 和中国成熟制程。
    "Its share of systems revenue increased to 46% from 44% in the June quarter. Strength in the quarter was driven by investment in leading-edge nodes, including advanced packaging, as well as mature node spending in China." — Bettinger
  • Logic / other 占 8% (上季 10%)，主要是先进制程。
    "logic and other were 8% of our systems revenue in the September quarter, slightly down from the prior quarter's level of 10%." — Bettinger
  • Foundry/logic 和 DRAM 占年初至今 Systems 增量的约 80%。
    "These two market segments have accounted for approximately 80% of Lam's systems revenue growth year to date." — Archer
  • 中国以外的成熟制程今年不会明显增长，明年会强。
    "Probably not in a meaningful way this year, Harlan. I do expect as we get into next year, that mature node spending outside of China will be strong" — Archer，回答 JPM
  • NAND 升级周期 2027 年完成，之后滚动到下一轮 (300-400 层)。
    "we said that we would be finishing that in 2027. ... then you just roll to the next upgrade cycle." — Archer，回答 Deutsche
  • SLC 存储层级：NAND bit 每转 1 个点到 SLC，约需 2% 更多晶圆产能；NAND 潜力还没完全释放。
    "for each percentage point mix shift of NAND bits to SLC-class devices, approximately 2% more installed wafer capacity will be needed to support the same bit demand." — Archer
  • 先进封装今年 +70% 不变；明年不增长会 "震惊"。面板级封装已出货给 20+ 客户。
    "70% for this year is still the right number. And I would be stunned if advanced packaging didn't grow nicely next year." — 管理层 (未标注)，回答 Jefferies
  • 3D DRAM vs 4F²：无论哪条路线，刻蚀和沉积强度都上升；直接从 6F² 跳到 3D 会升得更快。
    "if really we're making a leap right from 6F Square into 3D DRAM, then etch and dep intensity is going to rise even faster, even sooner than we would have thought" — Archer，回答 UBS
  • Foundry/logic 每片晶圆 SAM 翻倍的路线大概走了一半。
    "that 2X was a multi-generational shift. ... maybe halfway through" — 管理层 (未标注)，回答 MS

中国与地区

  • 中国占 35% (上季 26%)，本土和外资客户都有强投资。
    "China contributed 35% of revenue, up from the June quarter level of 26%. We saw strong investments here by both domestic and multinational customers." — Bettinger
  • Dec Q 中国占比会显著低于 Sep Q。
    "In the December quarter, we expect China will represent a significantly lower percentage of our total revenue compared with the September quarter." — Bettinger
  • 韩国 20% 持平，台湾 18% (上季 27%)。
    "Korea's contribution to revenue in the September quarter remained unchanged from the June quarter level at 20%. The Taiwan region contributed 18% of revenue, down from the June quarter at 27%." — Bettinger

CSBG

  • CY26 CSBG 增长超过 30%，是上次投资者日长期目标的两倍多；升级、备件、服务全面增长。
    "annual revenue expected to grow more than 30% year-over-year. This is more than twice the rate of our long-term target set at our last Analyst Day." — Archer
  • CY27 CSBG 增长超过 15%。
    "We expect CSBG momentum to continue in calendar year 2027, with revenue growing over 15% year-over-year." — Archer
  • Sep Q CSBG 近 $2.5bn，连续第四季创纪录，环比小增，各组成部分都强。
    "This was up slightly from the June quarter and 40% higher than the same period in 2025. Each component of CSBG remained strong with the prior quarter." — Bettinger
  • 备件仍是最大组成；NAND 升级强；Reliant (成熟制程) 上季表现很好，趋势延续到明年。
    "I've always said spares is the biggest individual component and that continues to be true ... Reliant did quite nicely last quarter, given the strength of investment at mature node spending" — Bettinger，回答 JPM
  • NAND 本季增长里新设备和升级都是重要贡献。
    "Both were meaningful contributors." — 管理层 (未标注)，回答 Bernstein
  • 设备智能和 Dextro 协作机器人帮助拿下多家存储厂的按效果付费服务合同。
    "helped us secure performance-based service contracts with multiple memory manufacturers." — Archer

毛利率与定价

  • Sep Q GM 52.4% 创纪录，来自有利的客户和产品 mix；继续平衡成本和定价。
    "The September quarter came in at an all-time record of 52.4%, improving from the June quarter level of 52% due to favorable customer and product mix. Additionally, we continue to balance our cost and pricing postures." — Bettinger
  • Dec Q GM 52.5%，在客户 mix 逆风下仍小幅改善。
    "This is improving a little bit in spite of headwinds related to customer mix." — Bettinger
  • 短期 GM 在当前水平上下波动；几年内做到 mid-50s。
    "in the near-term, Srini, I think you're going to be plus or minus the level that we're at. We did suggest over the next several years, we see an opportunity to expand gross margin into the mid 50s." — Bettinger，回答 RBC
  • 抓手：新产品 (Akara、Aether)、靠近客户制造 (还有一点空间)、定价。
    "We've talked about our close to customer strategy ... still has a little bit of juice left to squeeze there. And then we've also talked about the fact that we're working on pricing." — Bettinger
  • 推断：TD Cowen 问到涨价是否推高了 Dec Q 隐含 WFE，管理层没有正面回答，只说没有一次性因素。

费用与税率

  • Opex $1.0bn (上季 $916mn)，主要是盈利提高带来的激励薪酬；R&D 约占三分之二。
    "The increase was primarily driven by higher incentive compensation due to our increased profitability levels." — Bettinger
  • non-GAAP 税率 16.5% (上季 11%)，原因是美国对海外收入税率上升和利润地区结构变化；Dec Q 及以后维持，请更新模型。
    "The non-GAAP tax rate was 16.5% for the quarter compared to 11% in the June quarter ... We expect the tax rate to remain in this range for the December quarter and likely beyond. I'd encourage everyone to make sure they have their earnings models updated to this higher tax rate." — Bettinger
  • 营业外收益约 $16mn 收入 (上季 $19mn 费用)，来自汇率、利息和创投组合。
    "Other income and expense for the September quarter was approximately $16 million in income" — Bettinger

资产负债表与资本回报

  • 递延收入 $2.66bn，+$227mn，主要来自销量增长，部分被客户预付款减少抵消。
