原标题：PE Sector Update: Indian SaaS Contract Life Cycle Management Sector

#software #agents

Third Bridge · 2026-11-13

核心摘要

  1. SirionLabs工程负责人估计印度CLM（合同全生命周期管理）市场去年规模约2亿美元，未来五年年增速约10%，驱动力来自文档存储与审批流程数字化、义务/续约可视化、与ERP/CRM集成以及生成式AI。
  2. 三大模块高度重叠、通常打包在同一产品中：基础文档管理约占使用量20-25%，完整CLM约50-60%，AI合同审阅约10-20%；全球AI审阅增长最快，而印度未来2-3年增长仍集中在完整CLM和基础文档管理。
  3. 竞争格局分散：Icertis凭深度集成、复杂与本地部署定位大型企业，估计份额10-20%，并通过收购Dioptra强化AI剧本与红线审阅；SpotDraft主打快速部署与中小客户，份额不足5%。
  4. 大型企业CLM渗透率约70-80%、中小企业约20-30%；增速方面大企业约20%、中小及中型市场约30%，成熟供应商收入约70-80%来自大企业。
  5. 行业结构上IT与科技占客户约20-25%，金融和制造各约10-15%；银行与金融服务过去采用最慢，但随审计透明与治理标准提升，预计成为增长最快的垂直行业。
  6. 印度CLM厂商ARR增速约20-25%，主要来自新客户；大企业销售周期6个月至2年，中小企业3-6个月；2026年NRR估计约105-110%，扩张足以抵消收缩与流失，高切换成本抑制流失。
  7. 收入结构约70-80%为订阅/许可、其余为专业服务，理想状态是90/10；主要合规驱动包括《数字个人数据保护法》和《信息技术法》下的电子签名与审计要求。
  8. AI初期预计提升毛利率，因为AI功能带来的变现超过推理成本；AI套餐可收取20-30%溢价，AI自动化可将适用流程的实施成本降低10-50%，Icertis 2025年AI ARR同比增长近40%。

Agenda

  • Indian CLM (contract life cycle management) market structure analysis, including consumer adoption and impact of AI
  • Pricing model, NRR (net revenue retention) and logo churn rate drivers
  • Competitive landscape, positioning of key players and long-term scalability of the sector

Key Insights

  • Indian CLM market is expected to grow at c10% annually over next five years, driven by increasing digitisation, workflow automation and adoption of generative AI
  • Banking and financial services, historically the slowest CLM adopters, are expected to become the fastest-growing vertical as audit transparency requirements and governance standards accelerate digitisation
  • Expert estimates that Indian enterprise CLM vendors have an NRR of 105-110% for 2026, with account expansion currently offsetting revenue contraction and churn. High switching costs help limit churn, while greater enterprise penetration is expected to support healthier NRR
  • AI is expected to initially improve gross margins, given that higher monetisation from AI features will likely outweigh incremental inference costs. Vendors could charge 20-30% premiums for AI-enabled packages, while AI-led automation could reduce implementation costs by 10-50% for suitable workflows
