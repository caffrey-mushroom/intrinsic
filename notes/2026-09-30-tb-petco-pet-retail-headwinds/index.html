Agenda

  • Pet retail industry update amid continued discretionary spending pressures
  • Petco’s (NYSE: WOOF) turnaround update and positioning against bricks and mortar and e-commerce competitors
  • Private label and services expansion
  • Mid-term outlook

Key Insights

  • Cat ownership acceleration comes with lower customer lifetime value but does not outweigh industry tailwinds in humanisation and overall pet ownership. Freeze-dried and even air-dried foods continue to grow in consumables
  • Petco and Petsmart alike have struggled with market share as younger consumers drive online shifts. In both bricks and mortar retailers "I wouldn't expect store unit growth" similar to how Best Buy’s business gradually shifted
  • A long-tail assortment, especially in private label products, increases SKU complexity to build credibility with consumers. Fresh offerings have plenty of runway for growth and can be delivered same-day from stores, but take longer to show ROI than shelf-stable products
  • Cross selling consumables and hard goods through services is largely untapped, especially in consumables
  • Petco Perks revamp was needed to be easier to understand, helping retail experience while working well online. Product newness, exclusive products and experiences drive repeat visits
