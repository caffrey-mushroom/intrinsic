Peter McNally (PM) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • KLX Energy Services' (NASDAQ: KLXE) oilfield service portfolio with in-depth focus on completion and production solutions segment
  • Wireline solutions, competitive landscape and emerging technologies
  • Market growth options for KLX Energy's completions business with a capital constrained balance sheet
  • Outlook for oilfield services in North America and potential shifts in customer demand

Key Insights

  • KLX operates in an extremely competitive environment in wireline where even larger players are existing because the margins in the service are thin. The company rarely bundles multiple services for the same customer, offering little integration or efficiency with Drilling and Production & Intervention segments
  • The company’s equipment is state-of-the-art and it has expanded logging capabilities, including pulsed neutron and H2S-rated nickel cable, which came from acquiring QES. KLX has its own service centre to repair tools in Fort Worth, something only larger players have
  • Expert notes KLX would always have 2-3 spare trucks which would allow the company to respond to customer needs but also reduces utilisation
  • Domestically, expert sees the best opportunity for KLX in Texas, but even there, more customer operators are performing well analysis internally. International would be a viable option for the company's equipment and technology
