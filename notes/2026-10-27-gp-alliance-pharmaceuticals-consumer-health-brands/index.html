SUMMARY

Overview

Alliance Pharma’s sale of its pharmaceutical operations has enabled greater investment in priority consumer health brands, including scar care, scalp care, dry skin, eye health and women’s health. Resources previously supporting pharmaceutical products are now available for marketing, innovation, regulatory work and scientific substantiation.

Scar care and medicated dry-skin products appear to offer the strongest combination of growth and margin potential. Geographic expansion, e-commerce development and consumer-led marketing are central opportunities, although the company remains reliant on distributors and its scar-care business in China.

Key Insights

Kelo-Cote and ScarAway have strong growth prospects because of premium positioning, professional endorsement and scientific credibility. Dependence on Kelo-Cote in China represents the most important concentration risk. The portfolio has pricing power, but limited patent protection leaves successful products exposed to well-funded imitators. Hydromol offers attractive European expansion potential, supported by professional credibility and scientific evidence. MacuShield is harder to scale internationally because established eye-care competitors have substantial budgets and global reach. Women’s health products may require costly reformulation before entering markets with different ingredient requirements. E-commerce capabilities are improving but were not yet considered best in class or operating at the intended scale. Management should prioritize geographic diversification, product innovation, e-commerce, stronger distributors and broader brand-building communications.
