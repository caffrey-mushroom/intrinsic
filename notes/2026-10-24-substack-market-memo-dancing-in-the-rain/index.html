2] Alcoholic ARR

Some of this info is basic but writing it up anyway for those unaware.

In AI, there is a process called “distillation” where you can copy most of the intelligence of a model with fewer parameters by feeding the same inputs into the teacher (target) model and your own student model.

Turns out, distillation is surprisingly effective and efficient. Both Anthropic and OpenAI use this strategy heavily. Broadly speaking, public models such as Fabel and Astra are distilled from internal models such as Mythos and Bel.

The two primary reasons Anthropic and OpenAI distil their own models are:

  1. The original model is too large to serve economically.2. Keeps their best stuff private to slow down others (Chinese, other western AI labs) from distilling them.

Vast majority of Chinese models use distillation against Anthropic+OpenAI heavily. Meta is regularly accused of distilling Anthropic. Nobody distills Gemini because it’s garbage.

This leads to interesting economic consequences. All the numbers in following bullets are made up by me but directionally correct. Use it to build intuition but please DO NOT take the numbers are accurate.

  • Option A: Spend $5-$10B in R&D to train a new AI model.- Option B: Spend $0.5-$1B in R&D to distil someone else’s AI model.Most of that R&D spend shows up as revenue for the target company who is getting distilled.- Distillation is a lot faster than regular training flow.

A non-trivial portion of Anthropic+OpenAI ARR is distillation. You can see both of them loudly claiming they are making progress in preventing this but in my opinion they knew and wanted to juice their numbers.

Part of why Anthropic IPO has been delayed so many times is they kept trying to back out Chinese+Meta distillation revenue. This revenue is “dirty”.

  • Hello investors. A large portion of our revenue is from other companies copying our shit, undercutting us 10x in R&D spend, and open-sourcing the end result.- We have a great business here please give us money.

There is this old anti-piracy AD campaign.

Yes I wouldn’t download a car, but in high school I may or may not have downloaded a cars worth of video games and engineering software. Fun fact, an old torrent for MATLAB (all the toolboxes lol) kept the installer. When asked for license key, notes TXT said input “123456”. I know this from a friend, don’t infer anything. Statute of limitations long expired anyway.

AI Distillation is not quite like traditional software piracy. Let’s use the video game analogy.

Let’s say it takes 100 people a year to make a video game.

Piracy is one person cracking the DRM (editing small amount of code that prevents copying the game) and uploading a Torrent for anyone to download for free.

Distillation is 10 people paying for the game legally, spending a month playing it repeatedly, and re-creating the game at a fraction of the cost but with some modest quality degradation.

Distillation is interesting when compared to piracy.

The funny part (ant most important from near-term investor perspective) is the first row.

In my opinion, both Anthropic and OpenAI are sophisticated entities who either can easily detect distillation or are liars.

You don’t get to claim RSI and all of this nonsense while failing to block distillation. Why yes, you have created a god model that will kill us all but are too incompetent to stop the Chinese from copying your homework at 1/10th the cost on a 1-3 month delay.

Really?

Anthropic and OpenAI chose to take the distillation money to juice their ARR. You could said they have ARR alcoholism. This has backfired and now Anthropic is trying to shut off distillation and back out that revenue as a “temporarily 6-8% headwind” in their IPO. With some success mind you.

Amusingly, that distillation revenue is not gone. It simply migrated from Anthropic to OpenAI.
