Agenda

  • Okta's (NASDAQ: OKTA) commercial strategy, including portfolio upselling and market expansion
  • Auth0 post-mortem and go-to-market course corrections
  • Competitive landscape and dynamics – best-of-breed vs ecosystem bundling
  • Capitalising on the growth of AI agents and NHIs (non-human identities)

Key Insights

  • Organic double-digit ARR growth is sustainable as past integration and execution headwinds abate. FY27 revenue guidance of 10-11% appears slightly conservative, though return to 20%-plus remains challenging in near term but possible by end of the decade
  • Okta's neutral, best-of-breed positioning insulates it against bundled suites such as Microsoft Entra, defending premium pricing and driving momentum in scrutiny-sensitive accounts such as the US federal sector, one of the company's fastest-growing segments
  • Okta Identity Governance is primary organic growth vector against SailPoint, growing at c2x company ARR, whereas Privileged Access Management struggles against CyberArk
  • Expert advises Okta consider placing its lagging Privileged Access Management module on life support to redirect R&D towards high-growth NHI and AI agent governance
  • Rapid NHI proliferation expands TAM to offset potential human seat-count contractions, requiring eventual transition from per-seat licensing to outcomes-based pricing as enterprises demand measurable ROI from agents, similar to evaluating human worker productivity
