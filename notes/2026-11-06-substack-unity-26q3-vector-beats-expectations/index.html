原标题：Review|Unity 26Q3: Vector Growth Exceeds Expectations

#gaming #internet #software

Substack · 2026-11-06

核心摘要

  1. Unity 26Q3收入6.22亿美元，同比增长32%，比总收入指引上限高约9%，调整后EBITDA为2.57亿美元，利润率41%。
  2. Vector收入同比增长126%、环比增长40%，是此前约20%环比增长指引的两倍。
  3. 管理层将超预期归因于模型更新、基础设施效率提升以及Day 28 ROAS产品的持续强劲势头，并表示仍有许多与D28同等影响力的产品改进待推出。
  4. 第三季度Vector广告主账户数环比增长27%，但90%的增长来自现有广告主，新广告主刚开始投放，有望成为后续增长动力。
  5. 管理层表示增长主要来自安装量和玩家价值提升，而非take rate变化。
  6. 新建项目的Runtime数据共享选择率保持在90%以上，Vector前1000大投放客户中已有30%与Unity共享Runtime数据。
  7. 公司给出Q4 Vector环比增长21%至23%的指引，作者根据Q4早期广告主支出反馈认为实际增速可能再次显著超出指引。
  8. 作者提示AppLovin与Unity之间的数据争议可能从2027年上半年开始对Unity产生影响。

Results at a Glance

  • Revenue of $622m, up 32% YoY and about 9% above the high end of total revenue guidance.- Vector revenue grew 40% QoQ, versus guidance of approximately 20%.- Adjusted EBITDA of $257m, with a 41% margin.- Q4 Vector revenue growth guidance of 21% to 23% QoQ.

Earnings Call Highlights

Vector Outperformance and Model Improvements

Subscribed

  • Vector significantly exceeded expectations: “Vector revenue grew 126% year-over-year and 40% quarter-over-quarter, which was double our guidance for 20% quarterly growth. Vector performance continues to be driven by three pillars: constant optimization of our self-learning AI models, new customer-centric product releases, and unique high-quality data.”- Models continue to improve with better data: “As our models continuously retrain on larger, higher-quality datasets, they unlock superior targeting precision. ... Better return on advertising spend enables customers to acquire more players more profitably, which in turn fuels increased spend on our platform.”

D28 and Further Product Improvements

  • Models, infrastructure and D28 drove quarterly outperformance: “Our third quarter Vector outperformance was powered by highly effective model updates, major advancements in infrastructure efficiency, and continued robust momentum in our Day 28 ROAS product.”- Further improvements could be as impactful as D28: “In a given quarter, we might have six, depending on the quarter, 10 significant changes to the product. ... the Day 28 offering ... we view as a fundamental table stakes product, right? You can’t expect to be a player at scale without a product like that. ... there’s still a really long list of fundamental changes like that are there for us, and that are going to be as impactful as Day 28 has been.”

Customer Growth and Take Rate

  • Existing advertisers increased spending as new advertisers joined: “In fact, Vector grew the number of advertiser accounts 27% quarter-on-quarter in the third quarter. ... What I would also add is that 90% of the growth that we experienced in the quarter was from existing advertisers. So, those new advertisers who came on board have just started to spend with us, and we expect that that is a growth tailwind that we will get.”- Growth primarily came from installs and player values: “Vector’s accelerating growth is primarily a result of greater volume of installs and higher player values, not changes in take rate. ... revenue continues to scale without impacting our customers’ margins.”

Runtime Data

  • Runtime data sharing continues to expand: “... our opt-in around runtime, newly created projects remains over 90%. Of the top 1,000 spenders on Vector, 30% are now sharing runtime data with us, and we expect this number to grow over time.”

What We’re Watching

Q4 Vector Growth Could Again Significantly Exceed Guidance

Our preview pointed to strong growth in Unity Ads, but the results still exceeded our expectations. Based on advertiser feedback on spending early in Q4, we believe Vector revenue growth could again significantly exceed management’s guidance of 21%–23% QoQ.

The Dispute with AppLovin Could Have an Impact in 27H1

We discussed changes in the competitive dynamics between the two companies in our November 5 AppLovin review. If the feedback we have received proves accurate, the dispute between AppLovin and Unity could begin to have an impact in 27H1. See that review for more detail.
