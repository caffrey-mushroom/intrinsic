原标题：Palo Alto Networks – Fortress or Friction? – Inside the AI Push & Growth Trajectory

#security #software

Analyst-led • 20 Oct 2026

Companies: PANW US, CRWD US, MSFT US, FTNT US, ZS US, SSE GB, NTSK US

Shiva Mandalam (SM) Independent Consultant

Luke Fragnito (LF) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Palo Alto Networks' (NASDAQ: PANW) platformisation strategy and enterprise adoption
  • Fundamentals of the four core software pillars – Strata, Cortex, Prisma and Idira – and growth trajectory of NGS (next-generation security)
  • Dissecting the displacement runway across legacy SIEMs (security information and event management), the structural moat of dual network-and-endpoint data lakes and compute or storage cost pressures
  • Navigating enterprise trade-offs and competitive moats across key vectors – SASE (secure access service edge), DLP (data loss prevention) and EDR (endpoint detection and response)

Key Insights

  • Initial USD 10m-plus platformisation contracts rely on upfront discounting, making three-year contract renewals and expansion contingent on active customer adoption rather than unused shelfware
  • Cortex extended security intelligence and automation management gross margins projected to trend below Palo Alto's aggregate 75% non-generally accepted accounting principles level, as heavy ongoing cloud compute, telemetry ingestion and storage costs pressure security operations centre automation profitability
  • While Palo Alto leverages its network base to challenge Zscaler in SASE, it faces competitive disadvantage against CrowdStrike in dedicated endpoint detection and Netskope in enterprise DLP
  • Palo Alto's proprietary telemetry combining network and endpoint data from 70,000-plus customers maintains competitive moat against foundational AI labs, which can identify vulnerabilities but lack operational enforcement points and remediation mechanisms
  • Palo Alto's target of USD 20bn in NGS ARR by FY30 faces structural headwinds if enterprise IT pivots back from consolidation to best-of-breed architectures, or if security operations centres consolidate around endpoint-native platforms
