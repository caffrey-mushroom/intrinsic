核心摘要

  1. Hydra Host CEO认为中期选举后联邦层面对美国数据中心建设的实际约束有限，土地、电力和许可均由州和地方管辖，真正有约束力的是州和地方层面的禁令与规则。
  2. 由于数据中心开发周期通常为3–5年，政策影响会逐步显现：未来约6个月内已订购GPU的项目基本照常推进，风险主要集中在明年二季度之后。
  3. 德州电网在大部分地区是放开的，县市拥有较大自主权，州长或铁路委员会可以拖慢和增加难度，但若无州议会立法很难全面禁止数据中心。
  4. 地方反对正在重塑项目形态，例如俄亥俄部分城镇禁止25MW以上设施、Boulder县门槛甚至低至500kW，专家预计未来更多是25–50MW的小型、分布式、本地化运营设施，超大园区减少。
  5. 专家认为今年包括Oracle新墨西哥项目在内的取消潮主要源于政治情绪而非需求疲弱，并否认行业存在传统需求驱动的泡沫，强调AI需求高度缺乏弹性。
  6. 算力将更加昂贵和稀缺，H100/H200等旧GPU的残值和租赁价值上升，GPU产能正常化时间从原先预期的十年内推迟到约2032年或更晚。
  7. 受冲击最大的是非投资级的AI原生企业和NeoCloud，超大云厂商凭借IG信用更易融资，但也倾向通过子公司和外部债务而非自有现金投资基础设施。
  8. 专家预计NeoCloud需将GPU所有权与云运营拆分，私募信贷将承担更大角色、直接持有基础设施并要求约30%的回报率，交易参与方数量也会压缩以降低执行风险。
  9. 国际上G20及美国盟友市场将更受青睐，专家认为能否获得GPU大致取决于是否属于可购买F-35的盟友范围。

SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, who leads a GPU-rental and data-center operations business, discussed how election-related opposition and local policy could affect US data-center construction. The conversation covered federal versus state authority, Texas and local moratoriums, international site selection, GPU availability and pricing, and financing models.

The expert expects policy changes to affect projects gradually because data centers take years to develop. Near-term projects with GPUs already ordered are viewed as relatively secure, while later-stage plans face greater uncertainty. The expert anticipates a shift away from a few very large campuses toward smaller, more distributed, locally operated facilities.

Key Insights

State and local rules are the most consequential constraints on US data-center development; federal influence over domestic land, power, and permitting is limited, in the expert’s view. Local opposition may slow or reshape projects, but the expert considers a broad, lasting retreat from data centers unlikely. They argue that consumer demand for AI will ultimately support continued investment. The expert expects compute to become more expensive and scarce, with AI-native businesses particularly exposed. They forecast that capacity constraints could persist beyond the end of the decade. The expert views recent project setbacks mainly as a consequence of political sentiment rather than weak underlying demand, and rejects the idea that the industry is experiencing a conventional demand-driven bubble. The moderator pursued follow-ups on project cancellations, GPU pricing, and financing, reflecting interest in the durability of supply constraints.
