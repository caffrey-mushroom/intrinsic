Agenda

  • Market structure analysis, including category growth, repeat purchases and consumer adoption
  • Omnichannel expansion, pricing and margins
  • Competitive landscape, positioning of key players and long-term scalability of the sector

Key Insights

  • Men's grooming market in India is expected to grow at c15% CAGR over the next 3-5 years, with hair removal growing at 10-12% and categories such as fragrance at 17-18%
  • Expert believes quick commerce is positively impacting higher-priced appliance purchases, with trimmers priced at cINR 3,000 increasingly becoming impulse purchases as consumers can receive them within ten minute, rather than treating them as traditional durables
  • Marketplaces and quick commerce platforms have lower customer acquisition cost and higher conversion, supported by stronger purchase intent. Meta platforms have higher customer acquisition cost and lower conversion, but remain important for reaching consumers earlier in the purchase funnel and generating demand that's ultimately captured through high-intent channels
  • Expert believes offline expansion erodes D2C brands' agility advantage, given that established players have stronger distributor-retailer relationships and can better absorb longer inventory and feedback cycles
  • Acquisitions are expected for brands in the INR 100 crore-500 crore revenue range, while the sub-INR 100 crore segment will remain fragmented
