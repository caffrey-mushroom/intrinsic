Can you describe what Overland AI does and the products and services it provides?

Expert: Overland is a ground autonomy company that focuses on defence and national security. They provide an autonomous ground mobility stack, software stack and compute and sensors, which can be added to any vehicle to make it autonomous. They also provide fully autonomous vehicles with the autonomy integrated onto the vehicle itself, including with different payloads and software behaviours.

Expert: They are trying to remove service members from the dull, dirty and dangerous work of warfare. They replace humans with robots to perform tasks on the battlefield.
