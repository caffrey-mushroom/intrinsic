SUMMARY

Overview

The discussion assessed Genmab’s Rina-S following Phase 2 RAINFOL-01 data in platinum-resistant ovarian cancer. The expert reviewed treatment selection, biomarker testing, sequencing with Elahere, safety, and emerging competition among antibody-drug conjugates.

Treatment decisions currently depend on prior toxicities, neuropathy, hematologic tolerance, dosing frequency, quality of life, and biomarker expression. Molecular profiling is generally performed at diagnosis and may be repeated after recurrence because tumor heterogeneity can reveal new treatment targets.

Key Insights

The expert viewed Rina-S efficacy as highly encouraging despite a lower response rate in the larger cohort than in the earlier dose-finding cohort. Responses after prior Elahere treatment suggest potential for sequencing ADCs with different payloads. Rina-S could gain broad adoption because it may work across folate receptor alpha expression levels and does not require Elahere’s high-expression threshold. The absence of notable ocular toxicity, neuropathy, or interstitial lung disease may provide a meaningful convenience advantage. Low-expression disease was identified as a crucial competitive opportunity. Strong results in this subgroup could move weekly chemotherapy-based regimens later in treatment. The expert expects efficacy among emerging ADCs to become increasingly similar, making safety, convenience, and health- system formulary selection major differentiators.
