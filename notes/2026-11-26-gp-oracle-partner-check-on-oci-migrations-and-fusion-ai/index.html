核心摘要

  1. C&A Technology业务发展与销售总监Joshua Marcus表示，11月季度其OCI业务按计划实现约20%的同比增长，预计2月季度增速将维持在20%或更高，而非OCI的评估与EDI等业务同比仅增长5%至10%，2月季度预计维持5%至10%的增长。
  2. 两年前OCI尚未进入其客户讨论，如今已成为每笔交易必谈内容，但传统本地部署JD Edwards等遗留ERP客户的决策周期长达6个月至近1年，部分年底集中签约推动当季目标达成。
  3. AI战略热潮正促使长期坚守本地绿屏AS/400架构的制造业等传统客户重新考虑上云，其中受数据中心供电需求拉动的燃料电池等能源制造企业以及医疗健康设备企业需求最为活跃。
  4. 尽管市场存在对Project Jupiter不可抗力通知的担忧，专家在JD Edwards等遗留系统客户群中未观察到OCI容量瓶颈、可靠性问题或普遍延期，依托One-Click Provisioning工具多数项目按期交付，仅偶发证书路径或验证测试等项目级延迟，且实施时需保持应用与数据库部署邻近性以保障性能。
  5. 相比JD Edwards迁移可直接保留原有应用流程，大型E-Business Suite部署对高可用和基础设施规划要求更高，此外渠道商常利用Oracle协同营销激励以非OCI评估切入客户后再引导至OCI迁移。
  6. Oracle AI World发布的Fusion Companion与Agent主要面向Fusion Cloud客户，JD Edwards等遗留客户无法直接调用，需通过JD Orchestrator、OCI生成式AI及预计明年推出的Release 27（支持MCP）获取部分AI能力，虽有部分ERP客户关注Oracle CX和Fusion HCM，但AI功能及Oracle AI Units新定价目前尚不足以驱动遗留客户放弃JDE全面转向Fusion。

SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, who works with Oracle legacy applications and cloud migrations, discussed Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI) demand, migration execution, customer interest in AI, and trends in related consulting services. The expert’s experience is concentrated in JD Edwards, with additional exposure to E-Business Suite, PeopleSoft, and other ERP platforms.

Key Insights

OCI sales were tracking to plan, with the expert’s expected growth of about 20% year over year. Long sales cycles for on- premise legacy customers mean some deals take six months to a year to close. Interest in OCI has broadened: the expert said it is now discussed in every deal they handle. AI interest is also prompting some traditionally on-premise companies to reconsider cloud migration. Energy and healthcare-related businesses were identified as especially active areas of customer interest. The expert had not observed material OCI capacity constraints, reliability issues, or widespread delays among legacy-system customers. They emphasized that keeping applications and databases sufficiently close is important for performance. Migration schedules have generally held. Delays described were tied to project-specific testing or configuration issues rather than OCI itself. Oracle Fusion AI features are not generally available to legacy JD Edwards customers in the same way as Fusion Cloud users. The expert said customers can pursue some AI capabilities through OCI and JD Edwards tools, but AI alone is not currently a strong reason for many to switch to Fusion. Pricing for Oracle AI units had not generated notable pushback in the expert’s experience.

Competitive Landscape

Company or offering

Positioning and observations

Oracle OCI

Increasingly included in legacy ERP discussions. The expert sees healthy demand and no significant capacity or reliability impact in their customer base.

JD Edwards and other Oracle legacy applications

Customers can modernize infrastructure while retaining existing applications. Migration complexity is often manageable, though some larger E-Business deployments may require more infrastructure planning.

Oracle Fusion Cloud

Offers newer AI features, but legacy customers may not have direct access to all of them. Migration decisions depend on broader process, integration, and implementation considerations.

Salesforce

Oracle CX is being considered by some customers as an alternative for sales applications.

SAP and Acumatica

Mentioned as other ERP practices the expert’s firm supports; no specific competitive comparison was provided.
