Key Insights

  • Expert sees AI accelerating Asurion's innovation cycle, faster software delivery, proactive device monitoring, automated claims and personalisation which would lead to cost savings but most likely reinvested into the company but potentially expanding products and coverage

  • Long term, "things like virtual reality, other devices that are becoming more agentic-like devices. I see people needing coverage for things that they're running agents"
