Agenda

  • Saipem (BIT: SPM) examining offshore E&C (engineering and construction) projects in Qatar and Saudi Arabia, touching on project execution risks and completion timeline
  • Upcoming E&C tenders in the region and competitive environment, including pricing strategy and capacity constraints
  • Impact of geopolitical tensions and transit disruption on project milestones, highlighting raw material shortages

Key Insights

  • Splitting the Comp 2 topside into two pieces for heavy-lift purposes drastically reduces yard commissioning progress from 60-70% down to a much lower, achievable threshold
  • Regarding the NFPS schedule risk, past projects such as EPCOL experienced >1.5 years of delay, and similar overruns of 12-16 months should be expected across Comp 2, 3 and 5 campaigns
  • Converting turbines to run temporarily on diesel in-yard costs cUSD 200,000-250,000 per machine but could prevent critical-path failures discovered too late once platforms are installed offshore
  • Expert warns that vendor personnel from companies such as Solar Turbines may be travel-restricted during regional escalations, creating hard stops in offshore commissioning that remote assistance cannot adequately replace
  • Fabricating platforms in China vs regional yards can save 20-25% on construction costs alone, although transportation expenses partially offset gains for Middle East project deliveries
