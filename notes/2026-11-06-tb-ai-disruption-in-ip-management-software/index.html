2026.11.06 | TB | Software | AI Disruption in IP Management Software

原标题：IP Management Software Market – Platform Consolidation & Modernisation

#software #llm #agents

Third Bridge · 2026-11-06

核心摘要

  1. 受访专家为担任Questel CEO长达25年、现任其董事会成员的Charles Besson，他认为2026年的核心变化是AI的颠覆性已迫使行业重新设计检索服务和IPMS，而非仅在现有产品中加入AI。
  2. 他预计未来一到三年内厂商仍以在现有产品中嵌入AI为主，此后律师将直接在Claude或Harvey等平台上工作，IPMS可能沦为记录系统、检索服务只剩数据库。
  3. 专家指出，在AI辅助数据清洗与统一下，过去需要400万至500万美元和2至3年才能建成的全球专利与商标数据库，如今可能只需100万至200万美元、一年即可复制。
  4. 翻译业务是最明确的风险，海外专利局仍要求译文，翻译量不会下降，但价格将大幅下滑，计价模式可能从按字计价转向按项目计价。
  5. 对检索业务而言，最直接的威胁并非新进入者，而是AI效率提升导致许可证数量减少，例如原本需要10个许可证的工作现在2个即可完成。
  6. 专利撰写和审查答复工具是最大的增量机会，DeepIP、Solve Intelligence等新玩家从撰写切入并向审查、检索和docketing延伸，律师借助撰写工具可节省20%至50%的时间。
  7. 目前IP厂商收入排序依次为年费缴纳、IPMS和检索服务，专家预计审查工具收入将很快超过检索，最终可能升至第一或第二位。
  8. 年费与续展缴费业务规模大且稳定，基本不受AI冲击，而AI还能改善其内部利润率。
  9. 专家认为Anaqua、Questel、Clarivate等传统厂商目前多是在现有方案上叠加AI，尚无真正原生AI的玩家，并建议它们转型为直接服务企业客户的“超级科技律所”。

Analyst-led • 28 Oct 2026

Companies: CLVT US, SIE DE

Charles Besson (CB) Board Member at QUESTEL

Saad Maqsood (SM) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Market share dynamics and platform consolidation across enterprise IPMS (IP management software) vendors
  • Commercial adoption momentum for generative AI-driven tools and features
  • Customer transition velocity from legacy on-premises installations to unified SaaS platforms, such as Anaqua's AQX and Clarivate's (NYSE: CLVT) IPfolio
  • Cross-selling opportunities across technology-enabled foreign filing, patent annuity payments and trademark renewal services
  • Retention dynamics, law firm vs corporate buyer preferences and displacement threats from emerging AI-native legal tech start-ups

Key Insights

  • AI could make traditional IPMS and search interfaces potentially obsolete as lawyers shift to Claude or Harvey as primary interfaces, and IPMS becomes "a system of record and the search service will just be a database"
  • Translation volumes won't decline because of AI, as foreign patent offices still require translations, but pricing will drop dramatically as automation improves
  • Building a competitive patent database, which was once a multi-year, multi-million-dollar moat, could now be replicated for USD 1m-2m within one year using AI-assisted data harmonisation
  • Patent drafting and prosecution tools represent a larger revenue opportunity than search. Expert believes the biggest immediate search revenue threat isn't new entrants, but AI-driven efficiency reducing licence counts, with teams doing the same work with two licences instead of ten, for example
  • Payments and annuities remain the largest and most stable revenue segment for IP vendors and are largely insulated from AI disruption
