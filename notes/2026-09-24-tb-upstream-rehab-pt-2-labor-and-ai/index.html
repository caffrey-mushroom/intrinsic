  • While retaining 18-19% EBITDA margins is plausible, meaningful investment is required to drive absolute EBITDA growth, particularly into staff salaries, with 6-7% pay update required to get Upstream into more competitive labour market positioning.   • Whoever wins labour race will lead the market in PT space, making recovering damage done to Upstream's labour market reputation a central priority
  • Expert has low conviction in management, with good organisational bones but execution across hiring and retention, productivity and technology required to drive meaningful deleveraging ahead of 2029 and 2030 maturities
