Agenda

  • Q3 2026 growth trends in the European sports retail wholesale channel across lifestyle and performance products, highlighting sales and discounting dynamics
  • Competitive dynamics across Adidas (ETR: ADS), Nike (NYSE: NKE) and Puma (ETR: PUM)
  • Cost and inventory management, including gross margin impact

Key Insights

  • Q3 2026 European sportswear demand has slowed materially after the World Cup, with underlying consumer caution rather than demand pull-forward driving weaker traffic. Sector sales are still up low-single digits with limited discounting
  • Adidas remains clear outperformer, with Q3 sales up roughly 6-7%, supported by lifestyle and running. Q4 is expected to remain at around mid-single-digit growth, despite limited traction from Superstar and Stan Smith
  • Nike has reverted to roughly flat growth post World Cup as lifestyle footwear remains weak – product quality has improved, but brand heat and consumer consideration remain below historical levels
  • Puma remains weakest of the three, down low-single digits in Q3 as lifestyle underperforms. Inventory remains manageable, but higher discounts will likely be required to stabilise sales into Q4
  • 2027 could be more challenging across the sector, given absence of a major sporting event and continued macro pressure, implying more moderate growth and greater reliance on brand-led demand creation
