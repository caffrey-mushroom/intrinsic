Companies: STRL US

Agenda

  • Managing long lead times for transformers and switchgear in data centre builds
  • Tariff dynamics and cost pass-through strategies in front-end bidding
  • Transition from offshored supply chains to domestic US manufacturing
  • Labour shortage as a key industry-wide headwind

Key Insights

  • Electrical dominates CEC's revenue mix at roughly 75%. All other mechanical, electrical or plumbing business units collectively comprise the remaining 25% of total work
  • Hyperscalers are capping contractor profit margins at 12-15% on data centre contracts, using promises of follow-on work as leverage to compress engineering, procurement and construction returns significantly
  • Regarding Sterling-CEC integration, cross-selling civil and electrical services together through Sterling and CEC integration is limited. The business units remain largely siloed throughout
  • Expert believes CEC’s execution ceiling are projects around USD 400m-500m electrical contracts, warning that taking on more work risks operational slippage across active projects
  • While prefabrication and manufacturing can allow for shorter project timelines and less labour, CEC's prefab manufacturing facility may be too small for their backlog scale
