SUMMARY

Overview

The discussion assessed Elligo Health Research as a clinical trial site and recruitment partner, particularly in neuroscience, psychiatry, and rare disease studies. The expert has extensive clinical research experience across sponsors and contract research organizations and has used Elligo for feasibility, site selection, patient recruitment, and study execution.

Elligo’s core advantage is access to patients through healthcare-practice networks and electronic health record data. Its overall performance was considered solid, although results varied materially by site.

Key Insights

Elligo can accelerate feasibility, startup, and recruitment when its network contains the appropriate patient population. Electronic health record screening provides more realistic patient-pool estimates than investigator judgment alone, but identified patients do not always convert into enrolled participants. Screen-failure rates were lower than with traditional recruitment approaches. The principal concern is inconsistent execution across sites. Investigator engagement, coordinator capacity, documentation quality, and protocol adherence can differ significantly. Centralized infrastructure generally reduces sponsor workload compared with fragmented independent sites. Strong patient access may reduce the need to activate rescue sites. The expert supported continued use of Elligo, while emphasizing that future allocation should depend on startup speed, enrollment, retention, and data quality. Long-term success in CNS and psychiatry will require consistent raters, investigators, coordinators, retention, protocol compliance, and safety reporting.
