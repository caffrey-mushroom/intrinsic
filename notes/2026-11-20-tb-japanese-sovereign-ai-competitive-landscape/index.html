核心摘要

  1. 专家（前Google日本AI专家）将主权AI分为数据主权、模型主权和运营主权三类，认为日本难以在前沿模型上追赶美中，但在行业专用AI上仍有空间。
  2. Sakana AI擅长模型融合与编排，Preferred Networks以MN-Core芯片实现从半导体到模型的垂直整合，NTT聚焦日语模型与公共/电信领域，NEC深耕国防与政府，富士通则携手Cohere主攻本地部署的制造和金融场景。
  3. 金融、医疗和国防客户的需求正回流本地化和封闭网络部署，据路透社超过50%的机构已在使用开放权重模型以降低对GPT的依赖，Qwen和DeepSeek因精度较高也被采用。
  4. Anthropic与NEC约在6月达成合作共建行业AI，但专业化需要时间，OpenAI和Anthropic进入日本企业市场也才刚开始。
  5. 日本的主要瓶颈包括资金规模比美中低约两个数量级、GPU依赖Nvidia、电价约为美国的2倍以及人才短缺。
  6. 推理成本正在压低利润率，重度用户反而拖累盈利能力。
  7. 三菱重工与Preferred Networks的合作设定了100亿日元的投资框架（专家先说1000亿后更正），MN-Core在图像分析等特定任务上能效出色，但缺乏类似CUDA的生态。
  8. 台积电熊本二厂瞄准3nm制程，有望改善本土算力供给。
  9. Sakana在IPO前完成200亿日元B轮融资，估值约4000亿日元，收入以API为主且API成本略高于OpenAI，其IP和海外潜力受认可，但美国AI巨头的突然跃进可能抹平其优势；与之对比，Mistral则以欧洲和欧盟AI法案为重心。

Agenda

  • Japanese sovereign AI competitive landscape, including Sakana AI and Preferred Networks
  • Customer trends in AI implementation and cost analysis
  • Growth opportunities and downside risks for Japanese AI companies

Key Insights

  • Japanese AI is gaining attention amid movement to strengthen national security and collaboration with domestic corporations. Sakana AI excels in model merging technology, Preferred Networks in vertical integration from semiconductors and NTT, NEC, and Fujitsu leverage existing customer bases and business expertise
  • Accelerating shift back to on-premise closed networks and demand for lightweight large language models in sectors handling sensitive data such as finance, healthcare and defence. Open-weight models such as DeepSeek and Qwen are rapidly expanding in closed environments, with over 50% of companies planning to break away from GPT dependence
  • While Anthropic is partnering with NEC to incorporate financial and manufacturing know-how, perfecting industry-specific AI takes time, limiting encroachment of foreign AI companies into Japanese enterprises
  • Ahead of Sakana AI's IPO, its proprietary model merging technology and potential for overseas expansion are highly valued, although there are concerns about losing its technological advantage due to sudden performance leaps by US AI giants
