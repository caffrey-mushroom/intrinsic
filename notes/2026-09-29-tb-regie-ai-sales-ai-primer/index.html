Can you describe what Regie.ai does and the products and services it provides?

Expert: Regie has two main platforms. It has RegiGo and RegiOne, but, essentially, it is an all-inclusive SEP combined with agentic AI for the purpose of personalised outreach and running outbound workflows.

Analyst: What is a SEP [sales engagement platform]?

Expert: SEP is a sales engagement platform. It's sort of like what Salesforce is to a CRM, where all of your transactions, all of your data is kept. The sales engagement platform is sort of that equivalent, but it's where you do all of your, its home-based for where you would do all of your work as far as in sales and business development roles. It used to be fairly standalone and then you would need to go to maybe three, four, five other websites to do things like list-building, source contacts, find who they are, go to a different website to find their contact information, go to different sites to research the company, and now that has sort of all been integrated into a single platform.

What is the value proposition of the company's products and services?

Expert: The value proposition is helping reps and teams to streamline sales developments and sales processes in one streamlined platform using customised agentic AI to take the busy work out of a rep's day so that they can actually spend time closing business, talking to customers, rather than spend time building lists, doing research, entering data and that kind of thing.

Analyst: What is agentic AI? How does it get integrated in the platform?

Expert: Agentic AI is the idea that you have an AI agent that is built to help execute customised workflows. Agentic AI could be used in any industry, in any context. In this context, it means that the agentic AI will learn your voice, you will train it to understand who your ICP, your ideal customer profile is, what the buyer personas are, what the roles that you sell to are. Almost think of it as a matrix or a grid, so depending on whether we're selling to a VP of revenue or a sales manager, for example, what are the personas associated with each of those roles and how would we speak to each of those roles personally because they care about different aspects of the whole engine.

The agentic AI's trained to understand really all the things that the reps were doing, trying to figure out what the workflows within companies are, so everything from that and how to speak to them to actually executing a sequence that would be sent out for an outbound campaign and then also sourcing all of the data that one needs to do that. The contact e-mail, the name of the people in the department, how to reach them, how long they have been there, all of that research is to be done sort of outside of the platform manually and now the agents do that for you. Really, ideally, what it does is it just means that it allows companies to hire people that are good at a certain skillset, which may be closing or negotiating, or sort of narrating and storytelling about the value proposition. If that's their skillset, it's almost a waste of time to have them entering data and researching phone numbers and pushing play on campaigns. It's automating that process so that they can actually do what they're skilled at.
