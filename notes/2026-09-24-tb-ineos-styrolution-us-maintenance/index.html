Olly Anibaba (OA) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Ineos Styrolution plant maintenance schedule for Texas City and Bayport facilities, including downtime, operational efficiency enhancement and CAPEX requirements
  • Feedstock sourcing capabilities for Ethylene and Benzene, highlighting impact on netback position
  • Organisational structure analysis – potential consolidation of business units, including synergies on offer and management style

Key Insights

  • Extending turnaround cycles to 4-5 years is being driven by cash constraints rather than optimisation – a short-term decision that increases variable costs and sacrifices long-term operating performance
  • A 15-20% turnaround cost reduction at Bayport's cUSD 35m-40m turnaround is achievable by transitioning from time-based to risk-based inspection, reducing unnecessary vessel entries and pressure relief valve overhauls. The next major turnaround is unlikely within 18-24 months, given its extended 3-5-year cycle targets
  • Altamira can back up nearly all polymer production across product lines except certain high-speciality ASA grades, which are exclusively produced at Bayport's newer world-scale integrated facility
  • China-based styrene monomer producers have been selling at negative margins on open markets, creating an extremely difficult competitive dynamic for US Gulf Coast producers, which cannot compete with loss-tolerant state-backed capacity
  • Expert believes Ineos's decentralised procurement structure is inefficient – centralising it could unlock BASF or Dow-scale vendor pricing
