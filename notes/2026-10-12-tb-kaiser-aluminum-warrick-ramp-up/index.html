Agenda

  • Roll Coat 4 ramp-up and throughput, focusing on ramp towards full run rate, operational bottlenecks and efficiency gains vs legacy lines
  • Upstream bottlenecks and asset quality, including ingot production, hot and cold mills and ageing equipment constraining downstream capacity
  • Kaiser Aluminum's (NASDAQ: KALU) incremental CAPEX and potential upgrades needed to unlock capacity
  • Low-carbon aluminium economics, customer demand, Canadian supply, tariffs and implications for Kaiser's costs

Key Insights

  • Roll Coat 4 is structurally constrained below its 1,000ft-per-minute design speed due to undersized OEM fume mitigation system. Fixing this will require >USD 5m to replace large incinerator components
  • Transitioning to bisphenol A-free coatings reduces legacy line speeds by c50%. Roll Coat 4 lacks specific design features to run bisphenol A-free products faster, structurally limiting sustainable throughput without future process modifications
  • Upstream ingot production is the primary bottleneck, exacerbated by obsolete variable frequency drives in hot and cold mills. Replacing obsolete drives can take up to one year for engineering and procurement
  • Expert believes Kaiser's USD 60m-80m sustaining CAPEX target is a positive signal, suggesting serious commitment to upgrading ageing systems rather than continuing to defer critical investments
  • Installing chip baler and securing coated scrap are primary domestic alternatives to offset Canadian low-carbon aluminium tariffs, although competing new facilities are rapidly intensifying demand for scrap supply
