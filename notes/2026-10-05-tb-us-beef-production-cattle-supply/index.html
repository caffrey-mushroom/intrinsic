Companies: TSN US

Mike Tanner (MT) Member of Board of Directors at Heartland Prime LLC

Natasha Nair (NN) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Market conditions, trade policy decisions and regulatory shifts surrounding US cattle supply
  • Meatpacking margin pressures and price elasticity
  • Long-term impact of weather events and crop yields on pasture conditions, herd dynamics and dairy industry
  • Near-term growth outlook, changing consumer demand dynamics and major supply chain risks

Key Insights

  • Dairy farms breeding cows with beef animals creates year-round, steady calf supply entering beef pipelines, partially offsetting seasonal nature of traditional beef cattle production
  • Mid-level processing segment is critically underdeveloped, leaving regional beef producers stuck between small local processors lacking capacity and major packers requiring volumes they cannot yet supply
  • Purely domestic processors face existential pressure, with more facility closures expected within next 6-8 months
  • "[I]t might be 3-6 months […] let's say the packing house has to adjust their price 50%. That retailer may adjust their price to the consumer 10% […] taking advantage of that price discrepancy"
  • Bubble exists across dairy and crop farming, where operators borrow annually against inflated land assets just to maintain cash flow. "[U]nless we miraculously get an influx of young farmers and ranchers […] ranches are going to be sold" and "we'll be moving production to other countries from a beef standpoint"
