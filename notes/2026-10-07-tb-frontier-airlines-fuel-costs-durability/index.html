Companies: ULCC US, FLYY.Q US, UAL US, AAL US, DAL US

Gabriel Cristerna Valencia (GCV) Founder & President at Soliel Hospitality Group Ltd

Liam Dorsey (LD) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Leased fleet changes and impacts on Frontier Airlines' (NASDAQ: ULCC) long-term growth plans
  • Stabilising revenue scenarios and broader drivers
  • Ability to remain capacity competitive in new and existing markets
  • Low-cost capacity update and consolidation outlook

Key Insights

  • Growing aircraft utilisation to mid-2027 targets of c11.5 hours is happening slower than expected due to higher fuel costs and requires off-peak flying to be profitable. Reaching that utilisation target requires fuel subsiding to cUSD 2.50 per gallon
  • Positive RASM/cost per available seat-mile ex-fuel spread is possible in full-year 2027, but depends more on non-fuel costs growing lower than historical levels, given sustaining the anomalous 28% RASM growth in Q2 2026 is very uncertain for Q2-Q3 2027
  • Lost bid for Spirit's LaGuardia slots is a "blessing in disguise", with expert noting an unclear path to profitability. Weak connectivity and inferior products have led to very limited upside in ancillary revenue despite the early nature of these programmes
  • If current fuel and fares were to hold for most of 2027, Frontier could improve cost per available seat-mile ex-fuel figures, which should turn the airline marginally positive. Outlook is likely stable and sustainable for the next 12-18 months even with jet fuel likely normalising
