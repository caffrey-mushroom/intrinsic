  2. 单绿色方案是目前量产主力，光机、光波导和SoC成本占比相近。
  3. 8英寸硅基氮化镓单绿microLED良率约70%。

Agenda

  • BOM (bill of materials) structure differences for monochrome AI glasses vs full-colour ones and for monocular AI glasses vs binocular ones, plus cost proportions and cost-reduction potential of microLED optical engines and optical waveguides
  • Actual yield rates and cost metrics of 8-inch GaN-on-silicon, impact of capacity expansion and depreciation on cost-reduction effectiveness, plus economics tipping point for mass production
  • Mass production maturity, efficiency and reliability gaps between RGB (red, green and blue) combiner and monolithic full-colour solutions, plus timeline and conditions for technical route shifting
  • Cost and power consumption trade-offs for microLED and LCoS (liquid crystal on silicon) solutions, changes in hardware selection criteria for display-equipped AI glasses and 2027 volume growth expectations
  • Key process and equipment bottlenecks during mass production, China-based supply chain’s capability gaps and potential beneficiary segments

Key Insights

  • The binocular full-colour microLED optical waveguide solution is not yet mature, with the optical engine and optical waveguide together accounting for 70-80% of its BOM. High costs hinder large-scale commercialisation, making this a key industry challenge at present
  • The single-green solution is currently the main driver of mature volume production. The cost shares of the optical engine, optical waveguide and SoC are similar, which will benefit first once market volume ramps up. Single-green binocular models are currently used more for ecosystem refinement
  • The yield of 8-inch GaN-on-Si single-green micro LEDs is c70%. After meeting yield and monthly capacity targets, the cost is expected to be better than that of 6-inch products. The 12-inch roadmap is expected to take another 4-5 years to materialise, and mass production conditions will not be met in the short term
  • Large AI models are driving demand for AI glasses, while non-display models help cultivate the market. The turning point for a large-scale uptick in industry shipments and ecosystem development is expected in 4-5 years. Optical engine makers and complete-device manufacturers with terminal resources deserve more attention
