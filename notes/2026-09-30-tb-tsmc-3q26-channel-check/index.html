Key Insights

  • Expert believes TSMC will face little resistance if raising prices by another 5-10% in 2027. A situation where demand exceeds supply makes rising upstream wafer and equipment costs the main driving factor
  • TSMC's 3nm capacity continues to expand. All eight phases at Fab 18B are expected to be fully loaded by the end of the year. Each phase will handle c25,000 wafers, bringing the projected total capacity to 200,000 wafers. However, there is limited room to increase Taiwan's 3nm capacity in the short term
  • The mass production timeline for 3nm at its US P2 fab may be advanced by one to two quarters. It is actively benchmarking against Fab 18B in Taiwan. Equipment installation and validation are progressing smoothly. Under an optimistic estimate, it could enter mass production in H1 2027. TSMC's learning curve is expected to keep shrinking, which may allow its future P3 fab to begin production ahead of schedule
  • The 2nm capacity expansion shows a clear trend of accelerating. Capacity expansion for P3 and P4 at Fab 22 is moving faster than it was two months ago. Combining all four phases at Fab 22 with Fab 20, capacity could easily exceed 120,000 wafers by the end of 2027. This surpasses the market estimate of 110,000 wafers, driven by exceptionally strong demand
  • A manpower shortage has become TSMC's greatest challenge and the core bottleneck restricting its global expansion speed. There are no noticeable delays on the equipment side
