原标题：Pinewood Technologies – Automotive DMS SaaS Scaling & International Ramps

#Pinewood #DMS

Third Bridge · 2026-11-10

核心摘要

  1. Pinewood在英国经销商管理系统市场份额约20–25%，龙头占60–65%。
  2. 专家认为到2030年最差情况也能拿下英国前20大经销商集团中的5家。
  3. Marshall Motor Group上线延迟更多是时间安排和多个大项目并行，而非产品失败。
  4. Pinewood在实施、授权和支持三方面都比龙头便宜。
  5. 第三方应用减少了单用户需求，按用户收费的模式在受侵蚀，所有厂商都要重想经常性收入结构。

Analyst-led • 22 Oct 2026

Chris White (CW) Commercial Lead at Jigcar Ltd

Saad Maqsood (SM) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Pinewood Technologies' (LON: PINE) competitive positioning vs Keyloop and other DMS (dealer management system) vendors
  • Commercial roll-out velocity and customer adoption across top 20 UK dealer groups
  • Platform and delivery organisation maturity for Lookers and Marshall Motor Group implementation
  • Platform product differentiation and feature expansion spanning opportunities

Key Insights

  • Pinewood holds c20-25% UK DMS market share, while the incumbent dominates at 60-65%, with the remaining share split among legacy providers and newer disruptors such as Tekion
  • Pinewood's worst-case scenario by 2030 is only holding five of the top 20 UK dealer groups as customers, given that losing existing large clients would be highly unlikely considering its DMS stickiness
  • Marshall Motor Group's roll-out delay likely reflected timing concerns on the company's side and Pinewood managing simultaneous large implementations, rather than a singular product failure by either party
  • Pinewood positions itself as cheaper than the incumbent on all three fronts – implementation, ongoing licensing and support costs to encourage new business wins. Per-user DMS licensing models are eroding as third-party apps reduce individual user needs, forcing all vendors including Pinewood to rethink their recurring revenue structures
  • From a feature functionality perspective, ease of plugging in third-party applications to Pinewood is "appealing and attractive," plus the costs around doing it
