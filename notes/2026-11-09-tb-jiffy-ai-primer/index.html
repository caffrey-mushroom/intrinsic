Questions

Could you describe what Jiffy.ai does and the products and services it provides?

Expert: Jiffy is an automation company. Its main goal is to go into different business verticals and build automations based on workflows that are inefficient. Primarily, in the beginning, they were using RPA, robotic process automation and have moved to agentic.

Analyst: What is the difference between RPA [robotic process automation] and agentic automation?

Expert: Robotic process automation is where you physically, specifically designate, design and do every step of the workflow where there's not much training or probabilistic or deterministic suggestions or activities. An RPA workflow and automation will handle steps A to Z, but an agentic automation could give you decisioning and options within that workflow, if that makes sense.

What is the value proposition of Jiffy's products or services?

Expert: Specifically for Jiffy, when [we were] focused on the media vertical, the value prop was not only speed to execution of your processes, but also mitigating or removing errors completely, human errors. With the media business, that speed to execution equals more revenue. That is essentially the value prop. You can also eliminate duplicate positions. You can handle more scale with less. That is the value prop.

Analyst: What is the value proposition of the whole business, not only the media vertical?

Expert: It's really the same across the whole business. That's essentially the value prop, it is taking all these workflows in different business verticals, whether it's wealth management, whether it's media, whether it's finance or banking, which all have very large human-driven, step-heavy workflows that are inefficient and error-prone and replacing them with either RPA or robotic process automation or agentic automation to eliminate those gaps.

What strategies would you use to improve Jiffy's margins?

Expert: Implementation costs are something that we at Jiffy have always focused on to increase the margins. Year one of a contract is very heavy in cost and the margins are very low. The goal is to have the length of the contract and have it be multiple years and really focus on the margins growing in year two and three.
