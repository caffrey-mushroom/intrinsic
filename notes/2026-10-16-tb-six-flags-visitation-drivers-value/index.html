Third Bridge · 2026-10-16

Companies: DIS US, PRKS US, FUN US

Daryle Powers (DP) Founder & Chief Marketing Strategist at Powers Advisory Solutions LLC

Patrick Ricciardi (PR) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • US theme park visitation and per cap spending outlook as experiences remain paramount
  • Six Flag’s (NYSE: FUN) portfolio direction and competitive positioning vs peers like Disney Parks (NYSE: DIS) and Seaworld (NYSE: PRKS)
  • Pricing power, pass sales dynamics and profitability environment
  • Near-to-mid-term outlook

Key Insights

  • Recent ride closures and safety issues seem idiosyncratic. Six Flags' portfolio lacks safety redundancies such as lap belts found on Cedar Fair rides of the same manufacturer
  • Fall is becoming similarly critical to attendance as the summer. This fall will likely be strong at the expense of discounted tickets or lower per caps
  • Guest demographics are bifurcated in spending power. When discretionary spending pressures increase, the bottom end falls out, but the higher end does not trade into theme parks from other more expensive vacations
  • Some parks have poor perception, being run-down, dirty and lacking investment. Mexico, New Jersey and New England parks could make sense to divest but might run into reputational risk
  • "Both Cedar Fair and Six Flags had entertainment in their names, but they've cut back so much on entertainment. I think you bring the entertainment back, you start to change how people perceive the park"
