Agenda

  • Grower demand analysis and application behaviour

  • Mosaic's (NYSE: MOS) biologicals and biostimulants as nutrient alternatives and 2027 nutrient demand outlook

Key Insights

  • Corn yields below 176 bushels per acre would strongly confirm that nutrient under-application has materially damaged soil productivity, validating commentary around depleted soil banks across the Corn Belt
  • Expert believes corn prices reaching a USD 6 per bushel threshold would be necessary to trigger aggressive fertilisation rates, as growers would quickly shift their focus strictly towards maximising crop yields
  • High NPK prices are driving greater adoption of Mosaic's biologicals, with real-time combine yield data giving growers faster evidence that biostimulants can perform on high-value Midwest acres
  • Expert believes potential US deals for Belarusian potash present zero immediate threat to North American pricing dynamics, given tight global supplies and the logistical hurdles of redirecting shipments for fall applications
  • The retail channel contains sufficient application capacity, but wet fall weather driven by El Niño threatens to compress harvest windows, severely jeopardising crucial pre-plant fertiliser application volumes in 2026
