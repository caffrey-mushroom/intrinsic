Agenda

  • CGC's (Compañía General de Combustibles') key assets in upstream and midstream segments
  • Production growth potential for CGC's O&G (oil and gas) reserves and capital development programme
  • Development challenges, access to equipment and portfolio optimisation
  • Regulatory backdrop and broader developments in Argentina's O&G sector, including M&A

Key Insights

  • CGC is pivoting from low-profit conventional fields to higher-margin non-conventional assets through the Palermo Aike formation in the Austral Basin and a JV in Vaca Muerta/Neuquén
  • CAPEX is weighted towards mature assets – c50% in Golfo San Jorge, requiring massive water treatment infrastructure for fluid that is 97-99% water, c30% in the Austral Basin and 20-25% in the Neuquén non-conventional JV
  • Conventional wells in Austral/San Jorge decline 20-50% over first two years before stabilising to c5% annually. Non-conventional wells decline faster, such as 40-50% in year one, and require rapid transition to artificial lift systems
  • Maintaining baseline output requires 40-50 wells annually. Scaling Palermo Aike requires c50 non-conventional wells per year and massive JV capital to lower drilling costs
  • Argentina's new VMOS export pipeline and President Milei's pro-investment policies provide strong macro tailwinds with potential to double or triple oil and gas exports
