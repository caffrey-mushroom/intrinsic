原标题：Latigo Biotherapeutics – LTG-001 Deep Dive & Outlook for Non-opioid Pain Medications

#pharma

Third Bridge · 2026-09-28

Analyst-led • 17 Sep 2026

Companies: LTGO US, VRTX US

Sandra Sacks (SS) Pain physician at University of California Los Angeles (UCLA)

Will Coupe (WC) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • TAM sizing for non-opioid therapeutic options in moderate-to-severe acute pain
  • Physician prescriber update on Vertex Pharmaceuticals' (NASDAQ: VRTX) Journavx (suzetrigine), highlighting typical use cases and overall real-world experience thus far
  • Deep dive into Latigo Biotherapeutics' (NASDAQ: LTGO) investigational NaV1.8 (voltage-gated sodium channel subtype 1.8) inhibitor
  • Analysis of phase 2 abdominoplasty trial data and expectations for phase 3 bunionectomy trial
  • Expansion potential for non-opioid pain meds outside of moderate-to-severe acute pain

Key Insights

  • Expert uses Journavx early in 60-70% of inpatients due to formulary coverage, estimating that c20% of patients avoid opioids as a result
  • Journavx efficacy has been variable but low side effect profile is attractive, yielding "amazing" results in c20% of patients. Journavx being on formulary is an exception rather than the norm
  • 20% of patients stop taking Journavx before two-week prescription expires due to pain improvement. Expert avoids represcribing Journavx based on insurance only covering it "three times a year"
  • 50% better analgesic effect than Vicodin in phase 2 is significant. Despite not running a larger phase 3 abdominoplasty trial, results are strong enough to support onzotrigine's approval, assuming positive bunionectomy readout
  • Onzotrigine and Journavx target populations would be very similar, given shared mechanism of action. Tolerability, onset of action and efficacy data, in that order, would dictate prescribing trends if both were approved. Expert would consider onzotrigine IV in Journavx-refractory patients, given different route of administration
