原标题：Republic Services – Commercialising Environmental Services & Pricing Power Execution

#industrials

Third Bridge · 2026-11-19

核心摘要

  1. 前Republic Services马萨诸塞中西部总经理（管理约6500万美元收入、150名员工）认为，公司2026年第三季度核心定价6.3%（开放市场7.6%、受限定价4.1%）已接近价格弹性上限，可能遭遇客户抵制。
  2. 客户对涨价的抵制呈哑铃型：大型全国性客户议价能力强，小商户会转向低价独立运营商，而中型和区域性客户是最能继续承受涨价的群体。
  3. 东北部等处置能力受限地区处置成本更高、需要更大幅度涨价，因此客户更早触及承受极限；新客户通常要求年涨幅低于5-7%并争取价格锁定。
  4. 拥有填埋场和转运站带来双重优势：既控制自身成本，又通过转运站价格挤压竞争对手；东北部不再新建填埋场，Republic通过铁路把垃圾运往密歇根、俄亥俄和南卡罗来纳的自有填埋场。
  5. 在波士顿市场Republic份额接近50%，北新英格兰则由Waste Management和Casella主导，Waste Connections通过收购的Harvey品牌经营中部马萨诸塞。
  6. AI动态定价对按吨计量精确的roll-off和压缩箱客户最有效，前装式路线和市政住宅数据只能按站点平均；去中心化的分部P&L结构被视为优势而非弊端。
  7. 环境服务业务第三季度有机收入增长21%、调整后EBITDA利润率约25.4%，但整合远慢于预期，CRM、客户群和销售激励不统一阻碍了交叉销售。
  8. 今年迄今收购约11亿美元，小型收购整合后客户流失约5-10%，大型复杂收购最坏可达50%；劳动力短缺和工会纠纷仍是长期结构性逆风。

Analyst-led • 13 Nov 2026

Companies: RSG US, WCN US

John Williams (JW) Regional President at H2O Care Partners LLC

Isaiah Rodgers (IR) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Republic Services' (NYSE: RSG) national expansion across commercialised environmental services via strategic regional acquisitions
  • Price setting capabilities enabled by landfill ownership to box out competitors
  • Strategic implementation of environmental recovery fees across large vs small accounts
  • Operational disruptions and persistent risk factors stemming from local labour disputes

Key Insights

  • Republic's open-market pricing at 7.6% is likely near its elasticity ceiling, with pushback expected, especially in markets where higher costs accelerate customer breaking points
  • Expert believes Republic's Environmental Services integration took far longer than intended, with separate CRMs, customer bases and misaligned sales compensation preventing meaningful cross-selling
  • AI-enabled dynamic pricing will be most effective for roll-off and compactor accounts, where per-customer tonnage, mileage and turnaround data are precise, vs front-load routes, where data is averaged across stops
  • Republic's decentralised P&L structure, where every division fights independently on performance, is viewed as a competitive strength rather than a weakness, contrasting with more centralised pricing models of rival hauliers
  • Operators in the Northeast who control disposal assets and can either train or truck tonnes out of the market will continue to have significantly more pricing power, as no new landfills are being built
