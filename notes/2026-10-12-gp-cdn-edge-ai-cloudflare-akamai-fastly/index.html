SUMMARY

Overview

The discussion assessed Cloudflare’s third-quarter 2026 channel performance, fourth-quarter outlook, AI gateway adoption, zero- trust positioning, and broader competition in CDN, edge computing, and agentic AI security.

Cloudflare’s third-quarter bookings were described as roughly flat, reflecting delayed purchasing decisions and shifting customer priorities rather than lost business. Momentum and pipeline activity improved entering the fourth quarter, supporting a healthy full- year outlook.

Enterprise security budgets are increasingly focused on governing AI agents, machine communication protocols, access permissions, rogue behavior, and token consumption. However, adoption remains early, pricing models lack consistency, and customers are overwhelmed by rapid product launches.

Key Insights

The expert expects Cloudflare’s annual business to remain healthy despite third-quarter deceleration. Agentic AI poses a more immediate concern than frontier models because agents can obtain broad access and act autonomously within enterprise environments. Cloudflare’s AI gateway is viewed as technically promising, but customer response remains limited and its control capabilities require further maturity. AI-native startups currently offer faster deployment and quicker value than large platforms. This prompted substantial follow-up discussion about their competitive impact. Startup durability is uncertain. Customers may eventually consolidate with broad platforms to reduce dashboard complexity. Cloudflare has notable advantages in global reach, threat intelligence, difficult international markets, and reduced outbound data-transfer costs. Identity security remains a significant portfolio gap. The expert suggested an acquisition could accelerate Cloudflare’s position. Demand for bot visibility and content-scraping controls is strengthening, particularly among media customers.
