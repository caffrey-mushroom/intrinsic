  • Computer use and terminal execution improvements post-Astra release – performance gains, latency reductions, premium pricing scenarios and user adoption rates
  • DeepSeek-V4-Flash-Vision screenshot comprehension, element positioning success rates, and supported agent task boundaries

Key Insights

  • The bottleneck for computer use lies within the model itself, rather than compute or agent frameworks. Astra takes about 10 seconds per click, which needs to drop to around two seconds to be truly viable. Expert anticipate reaching this tipping point within six months. DeepSeek's performance on long-horizon tasks is roughly on par with GPT-5.5 Sol, making it highly usable for short-context workflows
  • Expert believes that Tencent, ByteDance and Alibaba are the top-ranked Chinese providers of work agents. Among them, WorkBuddy stands out for its open ecosystem, which allows users to choose their own models and plugins. In contrast, Doubao is leveraging its computer-use capabilities to catch up; however, it currently does not support model swapping, leaving it highly vulnerable if a stronger competing model emerges
  • Expert believes data collection's growth upside has been underestimated. Currently, China still lacks high-quality data collection, which explains why its computer use capabilities are lagging. FDE projects serve as another growth driver. Yet, traditional tech service vendors like Chinasoft and iSoftStone are struggling to pivot; project outsourcing usually requires clients to provide clear, upfront requirements, whereas FDE's business model requires vendors to figure out requirements independently, with talent reserves also under pressure
