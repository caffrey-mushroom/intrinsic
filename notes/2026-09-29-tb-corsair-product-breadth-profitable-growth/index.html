Thomas Miller (TM) Chief of Staff to the CTO at Kairos Power LLC

Agenda

  • Corsair's (NASDAQ: CRSR) peripherals growth, premium positioning and competitive differentiation
  • Product simplification, software integration and quality execution
  • Elgato, Fanatec and ecosystem monetisation
  • Memory cyclicality, supply chain costs and margin durability

Key Insights

  • Q2 2026 memory revenue growth of 17% is unsustainable, with current 15-20% growth rates expected to normalise as AI-driven chip scarcity eases. Memory gross margins should ultimately revert towards c10-15%, well below the 25-40% range achievable in peripherals
  • Stream Deck has meaningful runway beyond creators into professional studios and enterprise productivity workflows, with expansion into new end markets viewed as the primary long-term growth driver
  • Corsair's AI Workstation hardware is competitive, but scaling Corsair Pro requires enterprise business development and service capabilities the company has not historically built, with high repair and replacement costs remaining a key obstacle
  • Corsair may sustain 15-20% near-term growth, but long-term company growth is closer to c5% as core gaming categories mature, requiring new products, acquisitions or end-market expansion to sustain higher growth
