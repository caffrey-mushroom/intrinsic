原标题：Palo Alto Networks (PANW) First Take

#security #software

Substack · 2026-11-18

核心摘要

  1. Palo Alto F1Q业绩大超预期并上调指引，盘后股价上涨9%，营收超预期幅度为12个季度以来最大。
  2. 净新增下一代安全（NGS）ARR为6.3亿美元，远超4.4-4.6亿美元指引、市场预期的4.48亿美元和约5.88亿美元的买方预期；NGS ARR达97.3亿美元，同比增长66%，连续第四个季度加速。
  3. F1Q营收34.45亿美元，同比增长39.2%，高于市场预期的33.06亿美元，比指引中值高4.2%；RPO为210亿美元，同比增长35%。
  4. FY27 NGS ARR指引上调3.75亿美元至114.75-115.25亿美元，上调幅度是F1Q超额（1.8亿美元）的2.1倍，高于市场的111.1亿美元。
  5. F2Q NGS ARR指引102-102.2亿美元、营收指引35.3-35.5亿美元（中值同比+36.5%），均高于市场预期。
  6. 公司同时宣布以45亿美元全股票收购Applied Compute（开源模型后训练与推理平台），预计F3Q27完成，约稀释1.6%。
  7. 空头关注6.3亿美元中有多少来自大型LLM客户迁移至Chronosphere的一次性贡献，以及F2Q隐含约29%营业利润率略低于预期和自1月以来已完成六笔收购。

PANW +9%: Blowout beat and raise that cleared every buyside bogey, with the FY27 NGS ARR raise twice the size of the F1Q beat and revenue biggest beat in 12 quarters. Stock was +9% today but this should embolden already confident bulls that thread environment is driving significant demand for cyber vendors.

PANW also announced a $4.5B all-stock deal for Applied Compute (a platform for post-training and inference of open-weight AI models), expected to close in F3Q27.

Net new Next-Gen Security (NGS) ARR of $630M beat the $440-460M guide, Street ($448M) and the ~$588M bogey (NGS ARR $9.73B, +66% y/y reported, a 4th straight reported acceleration). F1Q revenue of $3,445M (+39.2% y/y reported, from 34.4%) beat Street ($3,306M) and the guide mid by 4.2%, the biggest beat in 12+ quarters, and RPO of $21.0B (+35%) beat Street ($20.81B) and the ~$20.9B bogey.

FY27 NGS ARR was raised $375M to $11.475-11.525B, which beat Street ($11.11B) and the ~$11.24B bogey: a raise 2.1x the $180M F1Q beat on the guide mid (the bogey assumed ~0.8x).

The F2Q NGS ARR guide of $10.20-10.22B beat Street ($9.90B) and the ~$10.075B bogey (~$480M of net new off the actual F1Q vs. the ~$388M bogey), and the F2Q revenue guide of $3.53-3.55B (+36.5% y/y at the mid) beat Street ($3.41B, +31.6%).

Bulls will point to $630M of net new NGS ARR ($180M over the $450M guide mid and ~$42M over the ~$588M bogey), revenue +1.0% q/q in a quarter that usually falls 2-4% q/q, and an FY27 NGS ARR raise of $375M (2.1x the beat) that still leaves the implied 2H net new of ~$1.29B ~$80M above Street, so the raise isn’t just F1Q.

Bears will lean on how much of the $630M was the tail of a large LLM customer’s move onto Chronosphere (PANW’s observability business, bought in January), which the CFO said in September would make F1Q “larger than normal”, with no ex-acquisition split in the release; an F2Q EPS guide that implies ~29% op margin vs. Street’s 29.3%; and a $4.5B all-stock deal (~1.6% dilution at today’s close) on top of six acquisitions closed since January.
