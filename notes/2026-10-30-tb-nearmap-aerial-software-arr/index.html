Key Insights

  • Expert expects annual Australian aerial software market revenue growth across traditional sectors such as government and architecture, engineering and construction to reach 9-12%, while property insurance and risk solutions expand at 24-30% over next 2-3 years
  • Nearmap's Australian ARR is projected to grow at 12-15% annually over next 2-3 years, with potential to reach up to 20%, depending on new product adoption speed
  • Core revenue across Australia remains anchored by architecture, engineering and construction – c30% – followed by balanced contributions of c15% each across government, utilities and property management
  • Australia lags North American product adoption by 18-24 months, with Nearmap targeting insurance product revenue in Australia to rise from single-digit levels today to 20-30% within 2-3 years, potentially exceeding 50% over five-year horizon
  • Nearmap holds c30% market share of photogrammetry and remote sensing, but dominates c80% of visible, subscription-based aerial imagery segment due to proprietary end-to-end camera design and aerial capture supply chain
