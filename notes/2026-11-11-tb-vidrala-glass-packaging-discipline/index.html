2026.11.11 | TB | Packaging | Vidrala Glass Packaging Discipline

原标题：Vidrala – Navigating Demand Shifts & Energy Volatility in Glass Packaging

#packaging #materials #consumer

Third Bridge · 2026-11-11

核心摘要

  1. Berlin Packaging专家认为，2022年高通胀后欧洲食品饮料包装需求自2023年4月起持续收缩，2024年玻璃价格可能下跌两位数，行业普遍陷入被动和恐慌式管理。
  2. Vidrala过去约12个季度EBITDA表现优于Verallia、O-I、Ardagh和Zignago，关键在于定价纪律、组合精简和不惜代价追逐低利润销量。
  3. Vidrala 2026年1-9月有机销售下降3%，欧洲收入下降约6%，其中价格降幅不到2%，销量下降约2%-3%，主要由市场而非竞争驱动。
  4. 剥离意大利产能并扩张南美业务弥补了欧洲疲软，专家预计南美可能在2027年继续支撑利润率。
  5. Vidrala约50%销量由合同覆盖，明显高于同行，受益于可靠的生产计划、交付稳定性和稳健的能源对冲；英国Encirc灌装业务锁定基础销量，抵御进口冲击。
  6. 终端需求上葡萄酒和烈酒最弱，食品更稳定；即饮、低醇无醇和软饮领域正向铝罐和PET替代，德语区和法国压力大，西班牙和意大利更具韧性。
  7. 定制瓶与标准瓶的利润差传统约10%-20%，但近年已收窄；客户合同期限缩短至6个月至1年，电加热比例目标为现有炉10%-15%、新炉约25%、十年内40%。
  8. 经过三年通缩，天然气和原材料成本上升，专家预计2027年玻璃制造商有望提价；最大风险是Vidrala在长期压力下放弃纪律、转而追逐销量。

Analyst-led • 4 Nov 2026

Companies: VID ES, VRLA FR

Matteo Vitale (MV) Segment Leader, Food & Beverage, EMEA at Berlin Packaging LLC

Georgia Beattie (GB) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Vidrala's (BME: VID) market positioning and customer demand trends across F&B
  • Impact of material substitution and product portfolio rationalisation on volume
  • Contract restructuring, energy pass-through formulas and pricing power in core markets
  • Operational efficiency, furnace maintenance and regulatory drivers under the EU PPWR (Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation)

Key Insights

  • Vidrala outperformed peers such as Verallia, O-I and Ardagh in EBITDA by prioritising disciplined pricing and portfolio rationalisation over chasing low-margin volumes – "they have been not looking for volumes at any cost". The biggest risk going forwards is Vidrala abandoning its disciplined strategy under prolonged market pressure
  • Divesting Italian assets and expanding into South America has compensated for weak European demand, where volumes declined 2-3%, and is expected to support margins into 2027
  • Approximately 50% of Vidrala's volume is secured under contracts – significantly higher than competitors – supported by reliable operational planning, delivery consistency and robust energy hedging
  • Vidrala's ownership of UK-based Encirc provides captive filling capabilities, locking in baseline volumes and creating an effective moat against low-cost container imports
  • After multi-year price deflation, glass makers face rising gas and raw material costs, creating pressure and opportunity for industry-wide price increases in 2027 if market discipline holds
