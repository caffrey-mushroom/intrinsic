核心摘要

  1. KNAPP时尚解决方案销售总监Hannes Kracher（16年行业经验）称，欧洲仓储自动化需求自2026年初起已走出2024-2025年低谷并趋于稳定，严肃招标项目数量回升且预期未来数季维持平稳。
  2. 订单呈现小型化与分段特征：主流招标额集中于2,000万至4,000万欧元，目前市场已无超1亿欧元大单；项目结构约60%为新建（含空置库房内部搭建）、40%为棕地改造扩建（如先建4,000万欧元，1.5年后再扩2,000万欧元）。
  3. 为规避高利率下的前置CapEx压力，更多品牌商转向第三方物流（3PL）主导招标并按件支付服务费，部分交易引入租赁公司或由终端客户出资20%-25%资本开支；履约端则加速向门店微履约中心（MFC）等全渠道延伸。
  4. 竞争格局生变：除了KNAPP、TGW、Dematic、Vanderlande、SSI Schaefer“五大集成商”与咨询机构外，AutoStore、海柔创新（Hai Robotics）、极智嘉（Geek+）、Exotec等“单品公司”凭单一产品研发聚焦和低价快速切入并挖角大厂人脉。
  5. 传统大厂短期通过集成或白牌海柔等机器人方案快速响应需求（如SSI Schaefer白牌海柔），但专家警示合作断裂风险（参照TGW与OPEX合作终止后的运维难题），且新锐厂商在10-15年老系统改造及电池寿命方面尚待验证；WES（仓库执行系统）成为跨品牌调度与增销关键。
  6. 设备形态上，AMR/AGV正取代地面固定输送线以满足柔性布局（料箱600x400mm与托盘800x1200mm均如此），爬架机器人则在3,000箱/小时以下中低吞吐区替代传统穿梭车与mini-load；Locus Robotics以纯租赁模式口碑与装机量领先。
  7. AI落地仍处起步阶段：专家指出行业内“AI一词被提及的频率远高于真正部署的比例”，虽然Agentic编程、虚拟预测试及销售报价提速（从数天缩至2-3小时）已见初效，但尚未验证实质性大规模人工削减。

SUMMARY

Overview

The discussion covered European warehouse automation demand, project structures, mobile robotics, competitive positioning, and AI use in logistics. The expert, an experienced industry sales professional, described a market recovering from a slowdown, with customers weighing investment costs and increasingly relying on third-party logistics providers (3PLs). Automation projects span new facilities, expansions, and upgrades to operating warehouses.

Key Insights

The expert said demand has improved compared with the preceding two years, with more serious tenders emerging. Projects are generally smaller, and the expert expects activity to remain stable in coming quarters. 3PL-led projects are becoming more common because they can take on capital investment and charge end customers by unit handled. Some end customers still participate in system design or fund part of the project. E-commerce continues to grow, but fulfillment is becoming more omnichannel. Retail stores may serve as local fulfillment points for online orders. Mobile robots are increasingly replacing fixed conveyors where customers value flexible layouts. Bot-based storage systems are also competing with shuttle and mini-load solutions. The expert sees AI as early-stage but already useful in some live installations. Initial efficiency gains are appearing, though substantial labor savings are not yet established.

Competitive Landscape

Company or group

Market position and observations

Established integrators, including KNAPP, TGW, Dematic, Vanderlande, and SSI Schaefer

Focused automation providers, including AutoStore, Hai Robotics, Geek+, and Exotec

Offer broader portfolios and integration capabilities. Their experience maintaining and retrofitting older systems is an advantage, especially for complex projects.

Compete with specialized robotics or storage products, often at lower prices and with focused development. Long-term maintenance and retrofit experience remains less proven.

Locus Robotics

The expert viewed its robot offering and leasing model favorably, citing broad market visibility and a substantial installed base.

Quicktron and Agilox

Additional mobile-robot providers active in the market.

Integrator partnerships

Warehouse software

Reselling or integrating specialist systems can help established players meet demand quickly. The expert cautioned that dependence on a partner may create future service and upgrade risks if the relationship ends.

Warehouse Execution Systems can coordinate equipment from multiple vendors, creating an opportunity for integrators to support mixed-technology facilities.
