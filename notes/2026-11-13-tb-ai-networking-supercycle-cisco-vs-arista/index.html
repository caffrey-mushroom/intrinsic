原标题：Networking Infrastructure Update – Who Wins the AI-driven Upgrade Cycle?

#networking #datacenter #asic

Third Bridge · 2026-11-13

核心摘要

  1. 专家预计未来三年超大规模云厂商网络设备资本开支年增约30%，新兴云厂商年增25%-40%，大型企业约8%，电信运营商约5%。
  2. 每100美元新增网络支出中约50%-55%由AI驱动、25%为传统更新、15%-20%为安全，而超大规模和新兴云厂商的AI占比高达75%-85%。
  3. AI网络超级周期预计在2029-2030年见顶，此后年增速将从扩张期的10%-15%回落至5%-7%，属于放缓而非收缩。
  4. 前10-20家超大规模和新兴云客户将贡献未来3-4年全球网络收入增量的60%-70%，客户集中度较高令专家略感担忧。
  5. 企业AI基础设施支出预计年增30%-40%，到2028-2029年约35%-45%的企业AI工作负载将运行在本地、私有云或混合环境中。
  6. 专家认为2030年AI scale-out网络市场规模约750亿至850亿美元，低于1000亿美元的市场预测，原因是高密度交换机和每比特成本下降抑制了收入增长。
  7. 2030年每100美元AI网络支出中，前端约15美元、后端scale-out约40美元、scale-across约20美元、scale-up约25美元，其中scale-up仍由英伟达NVLink主导。
  8. 未来三年Arista有望提升4-5个百分点的全球数据中心网络份额，思科约2-3个百分点，HPE约1-2个百分点，新增收入中思科与Arista各占约30美元、HPE约15美元。
  9. 思科AI网络收入份额有望从2026年上半年的13%升至18%-20%，以太网AI端口份额可从22%升至30%以上，而Arista份额可能从22%小幅降至20%-22%。
  10. 到2029-2030年高端AI以太网交换中商用芯片仍将占约60%，博通凭借Tomahawk和Jericho保持领先，而Arista因高度依赖超大规模客户而在资本开支放缓时最为脆弱。

Analyst-led • 5 Nov 2026

Companies: CSCO US, NVDA US, HPE US, ANET US, AVGO US, DELL US

Vitali Amare (VA) Co-founder & Principal Advisor, AI & Cyber Risk at Context Zero

Colin Duncan (CD) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • AI networking demand across hyperscalers, neoclouds and enterprises
  • Competitive positioning across Cisco (NASDAQ: CSCO), HPE-Juniper (NYSE: HPE), Arista Networks (NASDAQ: ANET) and Nvidia (NASDAQ: NVDA)
  • AI networking architectures, merchant silicon and scale-out market opportunities
  • Market share gains, spending sustainability and the networking growth outlook

Key Insights

  • Neocloud networking CAPEX could grow 25-40% annually over next three years vs approximately 30% for hyperscalers, driven by GPU cluster expansion and high-speed Ethernet demand
  • AI networking supercycle could peak around 2029-30, with annual growth normalising to 5-7% from 10-15% during the expansion phase, reflecting slower spending growth rather than a contraction
  • Arista could gain 4-5pp of global data centre networking share over the next three years, vs 2-3pp for Cisco and 1-2pp for HPE, supported by stronger hyperscaler relationships
  • AI scale-out networking could reach USD 75bn-85bn by 2030, below USD 100bn forecasts, as higher-density switches and declining cost per bit limit revenue growth relative to bandwidth demand
  • Merchant silicon could retain approximately 60% of high-end AI Ethernet switching by 2029-30, with Broadcom maintaining leadership, despite growing competition from Cisco Silicon One and Nvidia Spectrum.
