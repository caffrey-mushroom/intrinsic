原标题：Airbnb – 12-24-month Outlook vs Booking.com & Expedia

Companies: EXPE US, ABNB US, BKNG US

Stephanie Kovach (SK) Independent Consultant

Albie Amankona (AA) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Differentiated supply defence – leveraging non-commoditised, unique home listings to maintain faster room-night growth than hotel-heavy OTAs (online travel agencies) such as Booking.com (NASDAQ: BKNG) and Expedia (NASDAQ: EXPE)
  • AI search insulation – utilising proprietary inventory to protect direct web traffic as agentic AI search tools disrupt standard hotel discovery
  • Adjacencies and experiences scale – relaunching local experiences, guest services and co-host networks to unlock non-accommodation revenue ahead of Vrbo
  • Regulatory pivots and long stays – scaling 28-plus day rentals and expanding emerging international markets to buffer against strict urban short-term rental caps

Key Insights

  • Airbnb's proprietary inventory creates unique insulation from economic downturns, enabling staycation flexibility and price comparison avoidance that traditional OTAs such as Booking.com and Expedia simply cannot replicate
  • On mid-term rentals, shifting towards 28-30-day stays under residential frameworks could bypass strict short-term rental restrictions in cities such as New York and Barcelona, preserving platform revenue in regulated markets
  • Embedding one-click add-ons such as early check-in, late checkout and airport transfers could capture 10-20% higher ancillary revenue per booking, with low acquisition costs, given that customers are already on-platform
  • On AI investment returns, trajectory follows loss, then neutrality, then profitability — with marketing gains emerging within 12 months but product-level benefits taking 18-24 months to materialise meaningfully
  • Airbnb's host commission rates remain competitively lower than rivals — Vrbo charges around 8%, while Booking.com sits in high teens and Sykes Cottages charges 20-25%-plus, organically attracting more unique inventory
