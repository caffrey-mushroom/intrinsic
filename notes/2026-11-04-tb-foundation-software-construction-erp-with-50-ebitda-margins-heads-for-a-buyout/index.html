2026.11.04 | TB | Software | Foundation Software: Construction ERP With 50% EBITDA Margins Heads for a Buyout

原标题：Foundation Software – Laying a Foundation for a Buyout?

#erp #pe #construction

Third Bridge · 2026-11-04

核心摘要

  1. Foundation Software已从建筑会计软件扩展为多模块建筑ERP平台，尽管仍以中小客户为主，留存率超过90%。
  2. 更换供应商首年的服务费用可达软件本身成本的2至4倍，构成很强的转换壁垒。
  3. 建筑会计贡献最多70%的收入，依托庞大的CPA合作伙伴网络，这一渠道优势很难复制。
  4. 2020至2026年靠并购实现约25%的收入复合增速，未来预计放缓到5%至10%，原因是宏观不确定和市场拥挤。
  5. EBITDA利润率从2020年的20%升到2023至2024年的45%，2026年达50%，专家认为在PE推动的管理和系统改造下可维持甚至继续提升。

Analyst-led • 30 Oct 2026

Companies: ORCL US, SAP DE

Michael Hines (MH) VP, Global Partnerships & Alliances at Deltek Inc

Scott Kessler (SK) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Construction accounting and ERP (enterprise resource planning) software industry considerations
  • Foundation Software's offerings and customers
  • Revenue and EBITDA margin performance

Key Insights

  • Foundation Software has shifted from a construction accounting solution to a construction ERP platform with many modules and offerings, leading to over 90% retention rates, despite a continuing focus on SMBs. Expert notes that first-year service expenses to switch providers could amount to 2-4x the cost of the software itself
  • Foundation Software's construction accounting contributes up to 70% of revenues, aided by a "vast network of CPA partners". That competitive advantage is very difficult to replicate and match
  • Following a c25% revenue CAGR from 2020-26 driven by M&A, growth is expected to slow to 5-10% going forwards, reflecting an uncertain macroeconomic backdrop and a crowded marketplace
  • EBITDA margins have widened from 20% in 2020 to 45% in 2023-24 to 50% in 2026, and they are seen as being sustained or even improved upon, reflecting changes in leadership and systems driven by the PE sponsor
