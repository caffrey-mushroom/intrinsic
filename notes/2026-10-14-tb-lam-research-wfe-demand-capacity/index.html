Agenda

  • Lam Research's (NASDAQ: LRCX) growth outlook across memory, leading-edge foundry and logic and advanced packaging
  • Etch and deposition inflection points and competitive positioning
  • Component bottlenecks, supplier capacity and ability to support customer ramps
  • Sustainability of AI-related WFE demand and broader semicap cycle

Key Insights

  • Expert estimates that Lam Research holds c90% of the electrofill market, positioning Sabre 3D to benefit from increasing through-silicon via and copper interconnect demand in AI advanced packaging
  • Scaling NAND from 20 to 70 layers might require c3x more etchers to maintain wafer throughput at unchanged tool productivity
  • Lam's advanced Vantex etchers exceed 100kW radio frequency power, increasing subsystem complexity, while Advanced Energy and MKS Instruments remain difficult to interchange within qualified tool configurations
  • Lam typically needs 1-2 years to develop new tools vs roughly six-month AI chip design cycles, while DirectDrive development can take 6-7 years
  • While Lam's full order book prompts supplier capacity expansion, expert warns an AI spending reversal in 2027 or 2028 could leave excess inventory and production capacity
