SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, a former senior sales executive at Planview, discussed customer renewals, competition, AI, product consolidation, and growth prospects. Planview sells project and strategic portfolio management software to enterprises. The expert described a mature market where many customers already use several competing or internally available tools, and where buyers are increasingly weighing software costs against what AI and existing platforms can provide.

Key Insights

AI is making some less complex portfolio-management tools easier to replace or build. The expert sees the greatest risk among customers using only basic features; deeply integrated enterprise deployments are harder to displace. Planview’s broad product range has been complicated by acquisitions, with some products feeling disconnected and adoption falling short. The expert sees product simplification and a more unified user experience as important. Planview’s strongest opportunity is its enterprise portfolio-management offering, particularly if it can demonstrate distinctive AI capabilities and help customers consolidate applications. The expert cautioned that positioning the company as an AI leader requires evidence in the product. Customer retention is under pressure. Some renewals have included additional capabilities at no extra charge, but customers often do not implement them, limiting their value as a retention measure. The expert expects limited near-term growth, while arguing that a more realistic long-term growth target would be 10–12%, rather than 20–30%. The moderator probed repeatedly on customer losses, pricing, and renewal risk.
