Agenda

  • Developments post UK duty changes
  • Flutter Entertainment's (NYSE: FLUT) brand portfolio, strengths and weaknesses
  • Licensed landscape shifts in the mid-term and Flutter's market share
  • UK strategic direction and role in global Flutter

Key Insights

  • On Sky platform migration, completion itself signals success relative to industry norms, although short-term disruption likely exceeded forecasts. CX recovery with full benefits are to flow through over next two years
  • "Flutter should be able to learn faster than everybody else if they're doing it right because they've got more brands", constituting key structural advantage and enabling faster product experimentation
  • Marketing cuts likely represent over 50% of targeted USD 200m UK savings, but could easily account for full amount. Cuts will materialise slowly due to contracted commitments and competitive monitoring
  • Flutter's strong UK sportsbook market share is difficult to improve on, however iGaming presents real opportunity, with 2-5pp points of share achievable within three years
  • Black market losses to a similar degree as the Netherlands is worst-case scenario and could happen if there were to be an advertising ban. Otherwise, leakage will be significant, with every operator losing players
