核心摘要

  1. ICF商业能源业务占收入约60%，此前约40%，主要因联邦卫生和USAID项目削减。
  2. 公用事业项目和服务占商业能源收入90–95%，高毛利气候咨询仅5–10%。
  3. 住宅能效市场份额约35%。

Agenda

  • Trends and developments impacting ICF International (NASDAQ: ICFI) and the broader public sector consulting environment
  • Utility and grid modernisation demand
  • Lingering federal consulting headwinds
  • Mid-to-long-term growth outlook

Key Insights

  • ICF's commercial energy business now represents c60% of total revenue, up from c40%, largely due to federal health and US Agency for International Development programme cuts under the current administration shifting the balance
  • ICF's Utility Programmes and Services division accounts for c90-95% of commercial energy revenue, with the high-margin climate advisory arm contributing only 5-10%, despite its outsized strategic influence, according to the expert
  • In the residential energy efficiency market, ICF holds c35% market share, driven by winning large, portfolio-style contracts as utilities consolidate fragmented programmes into single, holistic engagements
  • Recompete win rates in utility efficiency programmes were "90-100%" during late 2020-25, with c60-70% of new opportunities being sole-source extensions rather than competitive bids
