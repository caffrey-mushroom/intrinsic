核心摘要

  1. Switch战略副总裁认为，电力仍是数据中心供需失衡的主因，但中期选举后地方反对和许可阻力正成为同等重要甚至最大的延迟风险。
  2. 多数延迟是成熟开发商约3个月的小幅推迟，但缺乏土地、设备、合同和设计的新晋开发商，18个月计划可能拉长到36个月。
  3. 专家估计2028年计划交付的项目中约30%可能被推迟或迁址，2027年项目因电力、设备订单和许可已基本落实，受影响较小。
  4. 约20MW的小型数据中心更易获批和接入现有电网，也更适合推理负载；先建50MW再评估扩建的分期方式更容易被社区接受。
  5. 300MW园区通常由4到6栋楼组成，可按每季度交付一栋75MW楼的节奏爬坡；表后供电方案如Bloom Energy、燃气轮机和光储正获更多关注，但开发商不愿自担发电风险。
  6. AI需求依旧强劲，资本开支总额不变但会摊薄到2029年及以后，2028年支出预期可能下修而2030年后更乐观，稀缺产能利好有交付记录的开发商定价。
  7. 超大规模云厂商对NeoCloud依赖下降，转向自研TPU和Trainium及AMD方案，并向成熟开发商租赁设施以保留硬件控制权、减轻资产负担。
  8. 内存、中压变压器、施工人工等各环节成本全面上涨；H100等旧卡已完全折旧但租价坚挺，数据中心按15年折旧、合同12到15年，GPU财务寿命假设3到5年但实际在延长。
  9. 专家认为供给短期内不会追上需求，2027年前的重要产能已全部售出，买家正在洽谈2028年上半年的产能。

SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, a data center strategy executive with experience in construction and hyperscale development, discussed constraints on AI infrastructure growth. Power availability remains a major challenge, but local opposition and permitting hurdles are emerging as equally significant risks. Construction labor, equipment costs, and project execution also affect delivery timelines.

The expert expects strong AI demand to continue, while capacity additions and related capital spending may be spread over a longer period. Smaller facilities could gain traction because they are easier to permit and may better serve AI inference workloads.

Key Insights

Local opposition may become a leading cause of delays, especially for projects still in permitting. The expert believes established projects already under construction are less exposed. Delays vary widely: experienced developers may face modest schedule slips, while newer developers without secured land, equipment, contracts, or designs may underestimate delivery timelines substantially. The expert remains positive on longer-term AI infrastructure demand, despite expecting some later projects to be delayed or relocated. Scarcer available capacity could benefit developers with proven delivery records. Hyperscalers are becoming less reliant on NeoClouds, while leasing facilities from established developers can let them retain control of their hardware and reduce asset ownership. The expert does not expect near-term supply to catch up with demand, citing continued requests for capacity extending into 2028.
