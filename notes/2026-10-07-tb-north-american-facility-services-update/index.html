Agenda

  • Proactive asset management and capital planning
  • IFM (integrated facilities management) market dynamics and service delivery
  • Technology and AI-enabled integration

Key Insights

  • Deferred capital investment is systemic issue, as owners often follow "if it's not broken" mindset, increasing future costs and operational risks. Proactive maintenance, sensors and targeted investment can extend equipment life by 2-3 years
  • Geographic expansion is challenging, particularly in rural US markets, where maintaining dedicated technicians for small client base creates diseconomies of scale and competing with established local providers is difficult
  • IFM is sticky, with integrated contracts typically lasting five-plus years and procurement processes taking up to one year. Clients commonly retain capital planning, compliance and design oversight internally
  • Hard services such as heating, ventilation and air conditioning, electrical, elevators and fire safety carry greater procurement weight than soft services. BGIS's in-house engineering and trade capabilities have helped drive rapid growth and major Canadian contract wins
  • Technology offers next major opportunity, with fragmented computerised maintenance management systems, building automation, remote monitoring and project management tools ripe for consolidation into integrated, AI-driven platform
