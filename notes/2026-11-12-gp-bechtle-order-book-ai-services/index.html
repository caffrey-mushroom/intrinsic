  7. 与Computacenter、CANCOM相比，Bechtle在基础设施、托管服务、全行业安全资质和中型客户方面更强，但在C-level咨询、绿地转型和SAP顾问（竞争对手有100-2,000名SAP顾问）方面落后。

SUMMARY

Overview

The expert discussed Bechtle’s acquisition of a UK Microsoft AI specialist, its opportunity to expand AI services, and the factors affecting demand and delivery. Bechtle’s established strengths are hardware, IT infrastructure, workplaces and managed services. The acquired specialist adds expertise in data engineering, large language models and business use cases.

The expert sees AI adoption as early-stage: many companies are testing small projects while assessing returns. Converting pilots into larger deployments will depend on consultants who can identify valuable business processes, as well as sound data management and technical delivery. Public-sector transformation and school digitization offer further opportunities, while weak business investment, chip shortages and rising hardware costs could delay projects.

Key Insights

The acquisition brings complementary capabilities under one organization, potentially simplifying customer relationships and improving coordination. Bechtle’s existing customer and Microsoft relationships may help it access AI opportunities, but technical credentials alone are insufficient. Business architects and data specialists are essential to winning projects. Public-sector work was the expert’s safest growth assumption because opportunities were already signed; execution and staffing remain important. Backlog conversion ranked next, with AI-driven growth viewed as least certain. Bechtle is stronger in technical infrastructure, managed services, security and mid-sized customers than in senior business consulting, greenfield transformation and SAP. Expert follow-up emphasized that talent shortages, regulation and customer-side readiness may constrain delivery as much as funding does.
