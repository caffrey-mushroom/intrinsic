SUMMARY

Overview

The discussion examined how changes in Google and AI-assisted search are reshaping customer acquisition in pest control. Search algorithms increasingly favor businesses with localized content and community presence rather than national scale, review volume, or high review scores.

Regional operators appear to be benefiting, while national brands such as Rollins, Orkin, Rentokil, and Terminix face reduced visibility. Google remains the dominant acquisition channel in less technology-oriented markets, although AI search adoption is increasing.

Key Insights

The expert strongly believes search changes, rather than weaker pest-control demand, explain much of Rollins’ slowdown in residential and one-time services. National brands are largely absent from tested AI recommendations, even when they maintain local branches. Local landing pages, community-specific content, local staff imagery, and branch addresses are becoming more important. Review-solicitation programs using repetitive employee references are being filtered, disproportionately affecting larger operators. No marketing provider appears to have developed a reliable formula for AI-search optimization. Rollins and other large operators are expected to adapt, as they successfully navigated earlier transitions from paid search toward local service advertising. Affordability pressure is increasing cancellations, but urgent services such as bed bug, cockroach, termite, and wildlife treatment remain resilient. Financing materially supports conversion for expensive ancillary treatments.
