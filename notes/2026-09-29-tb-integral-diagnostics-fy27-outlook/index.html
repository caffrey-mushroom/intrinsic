Agenda

  • Integral Diagnostics' (ASX: IDX) FY27 revenue expectations – volume, pricing and mix considerations amid increasing competitive intensity
  • FY27 EBITDA margin outlook – post-merger incremental synergies, labour productivity headroom and wage pressures
  • Network strategy – New Zealand strategic review, funding model and market drivers

Key Insights

  • New CEO is expected to curb radiologist wage inflation, where GP-trained predecessor struggled to negotiate with specialists – although minimum 12 months are required to build relationships and mitigate churn through retirement or teleradiology. 80% of Integral's pre-merger radiologists were on fixed salaries vs 20% variable – inverse of market
  • FY27 EBITDA margin outlook is conservative, signalling issues in realising cost synergies, with cAUD 5m-6m in additional costs and competitive pressure from MRI deregulation
  • 70% of IDXt's throughput services internal volumes, supporting broader business expense of higher-margin external scans, where incremental growth is expected. IDXt is 50% cheaper than Everlight
  • Limited ability to improve revenue-per-scan in New Zealand, due to standardised payer pricing and practical challenges influencing mix
  • Integral has yet to tap greenfield expansion opportunities in New Zealand, which peers have actively pursued, with RHCNZ's "monopoly" eroded by Affinity, Beyond and Reform over past 3-4 years. Existing Auckland footprint possesses diverse sub-specialty capabilities and strong referral networks
