核心摘要

  1. JST Power Equipment 执行副总裁认为中期选举后民主党并非反对数据中心，而是要求其负责任发展，新规更可能导致项目延期而非取消。
  2. 专家预计未来 0-12 个月项目基本照常推进，12-36 个月内部分项目可能因需自建发电、承担输电升级或保护本地电价用户而推迟 3-5 个月。
  3. 数据中心可能直接拥有变电站和输电接入，或以更高电价承担电网升级成本，这会改变设备采购方，但变压器、开关柜和高压断路器的总需求量基本不变。
  4. 小型电力变压器（80 MVA 以下）交期稳定在约 70 周，大型电力变压器约 30-36 个月，未来 12-18 个月新增产能有望使交期回落，厂商积压订单预计在两年内逐步降至 12 个月以内。
  5. 数据中心签署的长期供货协议最长，通常为 3-5 年；客户将继续使用长期框架协议，只是年度采购量可能从 3000 台调整到 2200 台之类的水平。
  6. 高压断路器需求比五年前高出约 60%-70%，交期从历史上约 25 周拉长到 2-5 年；韩国 HICO 重返美国市场，部分中国厂商在数据中心和新能源项目中有所斩获，但公用事业仍不接受中国断路器。
  7. 当前头号瓶颈是微处理器继电器，SEL、ABB、Siemens 等约 10 家供应商被数据中心开关柜需求压垮，专家预计约需一年产能才能匹配需求。
  8. 由于核电需 7-10 年、燃气轮机交期 4-5 年，数据中心配套发电可能更多依赖风电、光伏和现场发电，专家预计 2027 年将是新风电项目装机最高的一年。
  9. 变压器价格已基本稳定并与铜、钢、铝指数挂钩，行政令 14421 的能源部细则（预计 12 月 24 日前公布）和关税仍是主要不确定因素。

SUMMARY

Overview

The expert discussed how political changes and potential data-center regulation could affect demand for transformers and related electrical equipment. The view was that data-center growth should continue, while some projects may face local permitting, infrastructure, or cost requirements. Utilities are also investing to meet rising electricity demand and replace aging grid equipment. Other topics included renewable generation, equipment pricing, supplier capacity, and bottlenecks in breakers and protective relays.

Key Insights

New data-center rules are more likely to delay projects than cancel them. The expert expects limited near-term effects, with some projects potentially pushed back several months over the medium term. Data centers may increasingly fund or own substations and transmission connections, or pay higher utility rates to cover upgrades. This could shift who buys equipment without substantially changing unit demand. Long-term supply agreements remain central to procurement. Data-center customers are signing especially long contracts, and the expert expects this practice to persist even as annual order volumes change. Renewable projects may benefit if data centers are required to add generation. The expert sees wind and solar as practical options because new nuclear and turbine capacity take longer to bring online. Transformer lead times and pricing appear to be stabilizing, while high-voltage breakers and microprocessor relays remain serious constraints.

Competitive Landscape

Market area

Positioning and developments

Transformers

Manufacturers are adding capacity, and the expert expects supply growth to ease lead times. Large buyers with blanket orders are better positioned than smaller projects buying on the spot market.

High-voltage breakers

Established global suppliers have expanded output, but demand still exceeds supply. A Korean supplier has re-entered the US market; some Chinese suppliers have found sales in data-center and renewable projects, but utilities remain cautious about Chinese equipment.

Microprocessor relays

A limited supplier base is struggling to meet demand. New long-term purchasing agreements may improve planning but can tighten immediate availability.

Renewable equipment

Added wind and solar generation could support demand for distribution transformers, medium- voltage switchgear, and related equipment.
