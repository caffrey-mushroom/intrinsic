原标题：Pulse: European Data Centre 12-24-month Outlook – Power Availability & Grid Constraints

#datacenter #europe #power #grid #cooling

Third Bridge · 2026-11-24

核心摘要

  1. 欧洲数据中心采用闭环冷却系统，2024年英国环境署数据显示英国数据中心仅消耗全国0.03%的饮用水，远低于水务公司18.3%的管道漏损率，当地对耗水、噪音及污染的反对声音主要受美国蒸发冷却案例误导及资金充沛环保组织的游说影响。
  2. 专家指出美国中期选举后对数据中心的政治反弹（引发纳斯达克单日下跌5.5%、SK海力士跌超10%、三星跌6.4%）反而可能推动开发商转向政策更友好、注重数字主权的英国与欧洲市场。
  3. 传统FLAPD（法兰克福、伦敦、阿姆斯特丹、巴黎、都柏林）枢纽在电力与规划审批上高度受限（目前仅巴黎相对活跃），开发商正加速向具备现成棕地电力基础设施及税收优惠的波兰、罗马尼亚、塞尔维亚、捷克等东欧二级及三级市场转移。
  4. 欧洲电力核心瓶颈并非发电装机（大量可再生能源处于搁浅容量状态），而是高压输电与电网接入能力；相比之下光纤更容易铺设，开发商极度追捧目前已通电或配有现成变电站（如0.5GW规模）的站点而非2030年才供电的项目。
  5. 供应链端，大功率变压器（需将100kV至300kV降至低压）、天然气轮机以及3MW至4MW以上大型发电机均面临至少约24个月的交货周期，而CDU液冷分配单元交期约6个月且供应链相对充裕，迫使大型运营商在未锁定租户前提前预购甚至囤积发电机与变压器。
  6. 专家批评PUE仅衡量建筑配电与制冷开销而非实际算力能效，且过去20年行业仍未找到替代指标；过去10年单位有效算力功耗已下降约1,000倍，未来10年若转向光子计算功耗有望再降至当前的20%以下，同时量子计算有望在5年内开启商业化辅助应用。
  7. 此外，数据中心建设与运维还面临全球性工程人才短缺问题，既懂传统工程又具备数据中心经验的复合型技术人员尤为稀缺。

Agenda

  • Geographic expansion – growth trajectories across primary FLAPD (Frankfurt, London, Amsterdam, Paris and Dublin) hubs compared to emerging secondary markets
  • AI and high-density infrastructure – rack power density and liquid cooling adoption vs global operators such as Equinix (NASDAQ: EQIX) and Digital Realty (NYSE: DLR)
  • Grid access and compliance – navigating power connection backlogs, land availability and strict EU environmental directives
  • Yields and land capacity – pre-leasing trends, land acquisition costs and long-term colocation ROI

Key Insights

  • European data centres use closed-loop cooling systems, meaning water consumption concerns are largely misplaced. UK data centres account for only 0.03% of potable water consumption nationally
  • On public opposition, environmental groups are described as "very well-organised and well-funded", deliberately spreading misinformation, with their funding sources and motivations being openly questioned by industry insiders
  • US midterm election data centre backlash could paradoxically benefit Europe – developers facing US resistance may redirect investment towards more welcoming European markets, particularly former eastern European industrial sites
  • Large high-capacity transformers, gas turbines and generators all carry approximately two-year lead times, creating significant bottlenecks that are forcing developers to pre-order equipment without confirmed tenants
  • Expert thinks power usage effectiveness is a fundamentally flawed efficiency metric, measuring only building overhead rather than actual compute efficiency. The industry still lacks a viable replacement metric after 20 years of attempts
