Companies: HSIC US, XRAY US, AMZN US

Chad Bushman (CB) Head, Business Development, Dental at Stonehaven Cozmix Group AG (SC Group)

Bryce Victor (BV) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • DSO (dental service organisation) consolidation trends and outlook for Patterson Dental's equipment contract renewal with Dentsply Sirona (NASDAQ: XRAY) in the face of continued industry headwinds
  • Strategic positioning in high-growth specialty dental segments to offset market share loss to Henry Schein (NASDAQ: HSIC)
  • Operational restructuring, capital allocation trends and bolt-on specialist acquisition strategy under private ownership
  • Eaglesoft and Fuse cloud software capacity and trends regarding the lock-in effect of software on consumables sales
  • Competitive landscape dynamics and threats from online marketplaces, specialised digital procurement platforms and "gray market" dealers

Key Insights

  • Expert believes Patterson Dental's consumables revenue share is likely overstated at 80-85%. Miscategorisation of small equipment such as sterilisers, compressors and handpieces as consumables may be inflating that figure
  • Shift to DSOs continues to grow, but at a slower rate, resulting from a sparsity of "low-hanging fruit" private practices to purchase and overleveraged DSOs that have struggled to find efficiencies and synergies as intended
  • Higher order volumes from DSOs cannot outweigh Patterson Dental's margin erosion. Large national DSOs are trending towards low-single-digit margins and mid-market DSOs are trending towards low-to-mid double digits over the next 2-3 years
  • Patterson Dental has a major growth opportunity in investment to build out its digital ecosystem and automation through e-commerce, digital marketing and an omnichannel approach
  • Expert predicts c5% margin expansion in FY27 as a result of workforce optimisation and procurement initiatives, starting slow and picking up momentum in H2 2027
