Third Bridge · 2026-11-17

核心摘要

  1. 专家（国内AIGC动漫短剧公司CTO）指出，当前所有主流视频生成模型都基于扩散（DiT）架构，技术路线差异不大，真正的差距在于数据集选择、标注、数据治理和后训练策略。
  2. 视频模型扩大规模远比LLM困难，因为视频数据必须标注，且高质量标注依赖具备审美素养的人工；专家认为Seedance 2.0是视频模型从早期迈入应用阶段的分水岭。
  3. 目前排名第一的是字节Seedance 2.5，是唯一的“T0”模型；其次为可灵、阿里HappyHorse和万相，MiniMax开源的海螺H3因成本极低也进入第一梯队，海外Veo因价格过高较少使用。
  4. 字节领先源于火山引擎算力、全国最高的视频模型人才密度、抖音积累的数据治理能力，以及红果短剧带来的反馈飞轮——视频领域近一半token流向红果，形成“越用越强”的循环。
  5. Seedance约占国内视频模型销售的近80%，但整个市场吸收能力仅约200多亿元人民币且仍在下滑；9月以来因红果削减分成、动漫短剧退潮，Seedance用量下降，可灵收入主要来自海外。
  6. LibTV等工具层本质是API包装，以低至约3折的价格销售Seedance API：其向火山支付约6-7折，差额由地方政府补贴和自掏腰包填补，并靠“健身房式”月度积分包盈利；专家认为工具层短期前景不佳。
  7. 训练200-300亿参数的视频模型，预训练阶段约需600-800TB粗筛数据，微调阶段1万条左右即可见效；专家认为数据质量决定约80%的效果，并将人才、数据、算力的权重排为约5:3:2。
  8. 当前多模态内容约50%为动漫短剧、50%为电商与互动引流；AIGC输出的是不可编辑的MP4，难以融入传统影视工作流，单段生成仍不超过30秒，角色与音色一致性是中长内容的关键。
  9. Seedance 3.0预计年底或春节前后发布；世界模型按秒计费成本过高，连续运行每月可达数百万元，短期缺乏商业价值；专家认为AI应用变现整体已在H2开始降温，可灵因去年下半年人事变动停滞近半年而掉队。

00:01:55] I want to start with the technology behind video models. Could you help us map out how the industry's technical approaches for video models have evolved? Which areas have now reached an industry-wide consensus?

Expert [00:01:55]: Initially, video models evolved based on image generation models, as it was the release of Sora that first triggered this trend. In general, the video and image approaches are of the same lineage, essentially following the diffusion architecture. Before diffusion became popular, video generation mainly relied on the GAN architecture. Although that architecture was relatively inexpensive, it was less stable and had weaker capacity for handling ultra-large-scale datasets. Therefore, after 2024, they gradually transitioned to diffusion-centric architectures, for instance, models such as Vidu, Kling, Seedance, HappyHorse and Wanx. At present, all the video generation models still on the market are based on diffusion-style block stacking.

There are some subtle differences among them. For example, there are some differences in the design of VAE or Text Encoder, as well as details such as flash attention and the attention mechanism itself. Some may be sparse, some not sparse and some may have dual-stream, but the core difference is not big. They all take diffusion as the core. In deep learning technology, if the architecture is similar, assuming that the data scale and model parameters are roughly the same, the theoretical training results should be roughly the same even if there are fluctuations. Therefore, the mainstream technical solutions at this stage do not differ much, but more in dataset selection, annotation, data governance and post-training strategies. This is the general technical framework at present.
