原标题：Gartner – Core Contract Value Growth Slows Amid Macro Pressure & AI Uncertainty

#gartner #advisory #ai #retention #sales

Third Bridge · 2026-11-24

核心摘要

  1. Gartner的核心护城河并非单一研究报告，而是约90%具有专有性的分析师一对一咨询（占价值主张的80%）、数万次客户对话及合同定价基准，而非占比较低且易被LLM复制的通用研究内容（约占价值主张的20%）。
  2. 2026年Q3财务数据显示Gartner钱包留存率依然强劲，GTS和GBS分别达到98%和99%，但新业务同比增速放缓至仅4%和3%（整体Q3同比增长2.5%），主要受宏观关税、DOGE联邦支出压力及客户对AI投资的观望瘫痪影响而非客户流失。
  3. 公司内部GTS（IT销售）与GBS（业务部门销售）分立构成潜在竞争短板，随着AI及首席AI官角色跨部门融合，客户对需对接多个销售团队日益不满。
  4. Gartner已免费向所有持证用户推出AskGartner，大幅提升C-level高管使用效率与续约率，并推动过去一年EBITDA提升近2%，但公司暂未像S&P Global那样通过MCP向外部AI代理开放内容。
  5. 在许可模式上，Gartner过去3至7年淘汰了低触达和无限量研究席位（如GTP无限许可），全面转向高触达个人席位（CIO席位约14万美元、基础席位约1万美元），要求客户经理仅服务2至5家客户并至少每月面访一次；金融服务业两年期以上合同占比超80%、三年期以上约45%。
  6. 销售组织方面，Saul Pyatt已接替任职约12个月的Dick van Ham领导GTS，新销售入职30天内即背负指标并要求90天内完成首单，同时公司维持每年3至4%的长期提价能力。
  7. 专家认为若不通过并购AI培训或咨询平台以触达企业基层，Gartner内生合同价值增速上限将被锁定在10%以下（难以重返历史12%至16%的目标），且公司过去四个季度已回购17亿美元股票并握有超20亿美元现金储备，未来12至24个月存在主动并购或被私募及战略买家收购的可能。

Agenda

  • Trends and developments impacting Gartner (NYSE: IT) and the broader research and advisory industry operating environment
  • AI positioning, including threats vs opportunities and GTS (Global Technology Sales) vs GBS (Global Business Sales) divide
  • Forward-looking contract value growth expectations
  • Mid-to-long-term outlook

Key Insights

  • Gartner's true moat isn't research alone, but analyst access, where no competitor can replicate several thousands of proprietary client conversations and contract pricing benchmarks accumulated over decades
  • Wallet retention remains remarkably strong at 98% GTS and 99% GBS, but new business growth has fallen to just 3-4% YoY, suggesting AI paralysis driving slowdown, not client dissatisfaction
  • On the GTS vs GBS structural divide – as AI blurs IT and business functions, clients are increasingly frustrated by separate Gartner sales channels. Internal fragmentation is an underappreciated competitive vulnerability
  • Gartner's shift from unlimited research licences towards high-touch individual seats, while counterintuitive, directly correlates with superior retention vs competitors offering unlimited models with poor renewal rates
  • Reaching mid-teens contract value growth again likely requires M&A — specifically an AI training or advisory platform acquisition — otherwise Gartner's contract value growth ceiling is capped around the high-single-digit range
