Agenda

  • Key operating trends for independent O&G (oil and gas) producers in West Africa
  • Panoro Energy's (OSE: PEN) portfolio and recent acquisition in Equatorial Guinea
  • Vaalco Energy's (NYSE: EGY) operating strategy and ability to grow its reserve base from existing assets
  • Growth opportunities and fiscal changes in key West African oil-producing countries, including Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Ivory Coast and others

Key Insights

  • High oil prices and super major exits make West Africa's proven basins the premier choice for exploration over expensive frontier regions such as US Gulf of Mexico. However, growth is constrained by supply chain delays, local corruption and small regional pool of qualified oilfield service providers – such as Halliburton
  • Offshore facilities are severely aged, rusted and lacking basic maintenance, which risks environmental incidents and inflates operational lead times – 3-4x longer than in the US – reflecting years of underinvestment during low-oil-price periods
  • Modern processing technologies enable safe extraction of high-sulfur H2S reserves – previously untouched due to environmental risks – fuelling a production mini-boom in Gabon and Equatorial Guinea
  • Kosmos operates purely as a "find it, discover it, sell it" exploration company, making Block G divestiture strategically consistent, while Panoro gains needed reserve replacement
  • West African countries are abandoning unrealistic European-dictated CO2 timelines to protect national revenues, softening regulatory friction
