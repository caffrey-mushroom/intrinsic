Agenda

  • Viewer reception of AI-generated content across formats and production styles
  • Productivity improvement impact on key metrics within Indian OTT (over-the-top)
  • AI stack, token usage and cost optimisation considerations

Key Insights

  • AI content generation hasn't yet undercut human production costs when averaged across all formats. It can cut costs by up to 50% only in specific formats, such as creating a one-minute AI-generated "new world" scene. However, longer-duration content built with AI will still take 1-1.5 years to reach quality and consistency benchmarks for OTT usage
  • In expert's view, mixing open-source and frontier models across multi-step content generation pipelines can reduce overall costs by c30-40% without meaningfully sacrificing final output quality
  • Churn prediction models have plateaued at c40-50% accuracy, essentially "a coin toss" at scale, with diminishing returns expected from further AI improvements
  • Video understanding models such as Gemini can now analyse uploaded content and classify scenes, emotions and objects out of the box, unlocking potential for scalable contextual ad targeting without manual annotation
