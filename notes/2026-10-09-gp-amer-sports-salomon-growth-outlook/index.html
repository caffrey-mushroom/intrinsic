SUMMARY

Overview

The discussion examined Salomon’s rapid expansion in China, led by footwear, store openings, wider online distribution, and strong brand marketing under Anta. The expert described Salomon as a globally diversified outdoor and ski brand that is positioned in China primarily as a fashionable trail-running footwear label.

Growth remains strong despite some seasonal slowing. Expansion into lower-tier cities is increasing the importance of accessible price bands. Apparel and skiing represent longer-term opportunities, but footwear and store rollout remain the immediate priorities.

Key Insights

The expert expects overall sales momentum to remain strong because planned stores, leases, and inventory are already largely secured. Products below RMB900 are the main growth driver, reflecting Salomon’s expansion beyond major cities. Footwear contributes most sales, while apparel is being developed gradually through localized Chinese design and production. Salomon’s mountain credibility is a major advantage, but its shoes are comparatively narrow, firm, and less suitable for urban commuting. This could become a risk if fashion demand weakens. Gravel-running promotion has limited practical relevance in China but was viewed as effective marketing that reinforces technical credibility. Apparel currently lacks strong product differentiation. Its growth depends heavily on converting existing footwear customers. Skiing is strategically important for winter demand and global brand consistency, although China’s ski market is currently contracting. The long-term per-store revenue ambition was considered extremely difficult and potentially dependent on eventually reducing store count while improving productivity.
