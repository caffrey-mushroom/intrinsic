SUMMARY

Overview

A large restaurant group described its selection and use of Workstream for applicant tracking, job postings, and onboarding. The organization needed a simpler and more cost-effective platform as it expanded beyond 200 locations. Workstream replaced parts of a previous hiring system across nearly three-quarters of its brands and was integrated with Dayforce, the group’s primary HRIS.

The main selection criteria were usability, pricing, onboarding functionality, customization across jurisdictions, and integration with existing systems.

Key Insights

The expert rated Workstream highly because its interface is easy for restaurant managers to learn and use. Customer support was identified as Workstream’s strongest differentiator. Responses typically begin within minutes, and technical staff remain accessible when issues arise. Workstream handles hiring and onboarding effectively across a geographically dispersed restaurant network, including location-specific workflows and connections to major recruiting sites. The platform contributed to a modest improvement in management hiring speed. The expert showed strong interest in AI-based candidate screening and automated interview scheduling. These tools could reduce manual work for the company’s small recruiting team. Switching vendors is unlikely unless an alternative is substantially easier to use, less expensive, and equally capable across hiring, onboarding, HRIS connectivity, customization, and AI. Workstream’s payroll offering was considered insufficiently robust for the organization’s needs.
