原标题：Epiq – AI Review Automation & Market Share Dynamics

#software #agents

Third Bridge · 2026-11-20

核心摘要

  1. 前Epiq全球服务交付负责人表示，电子取证（eDiscovery）业务占Epiq总收入50%以上，其中数据托管和托管审阅是两大子业务，二者正同时面临收入和利润率的多重压力。
  2. Epiq约80-90%的托管数据位于高利润的Relativity Server环境，按GB计费约2-10美元（常见8美元/GB），毛利率约60%；而RelativityOne起步价约12-13美元/GB但包含处理费，利润率显著更低。
  3. 自2028年1月1日起Relativity Server不再接受新案件，客户新案件将大多转向RelativityOne；全面采用RelativityOne的竞争对手已提供零处理费定价，Epiq过去价值数百万美元的处理费收入将基本消失。
  4. 客户更多使用ECA和冷存储预先过滤数据，过去约18个月进入审阅工作区的数据量下降约25%或更多。
  5. 专家指出数据在供应商之间迁移时约60%会被备份而不再重新上线，因此Epiq的存量托管收入比表面上更脆弱。
  6. 托管审阅因新冠后远程化消除了实体审阅中心的壁垒、按文档计价兴起，以及AI接管一级审阅而遭受冲击，原本百万级文档的人工审阅可能缩减至约2万份的二次复核。
  7. 内部调查业务正快速放弃一级人工审阅，客户直接用AI查询生成风险画像；Epiq正通过自研AI平台、信息治理咨询、网络安全和事件响应寻找替代增长。
  8. 与Consilio、Lighthouse、KLD不同，Epiq同时拥有业内最大的理赔管理和电子取证业务，这一多元结构提供了战略选择权，包括出售部分资产为转型融资。

Companies: KLDI US, LAW US

Agenda

  • Epiq's (NASDAQ: EPIQ) competitive positioning as a legal technology software and service provider across corporate legal departments and tier 1 law firms
  • Commercial adoption momentum and subscription ARR growth for its proprietary SaaS e-discovery platform, Epiq Discover
  • Generative AI integration trajectory across Epiq and outlook for automated document review tools in earely case assessment
  • Pricing model dynamics, gross margin profiles and potential pressure from customers shifting to RelativityOne

Key Insights

  • Epiq's eDiscovery business exceeds 50% of total revenue, with hosting and managed review being two largest sub-segments, both now facing simultaneous revenue and margin pressures from multiple directions
  • Epiq's legacy hosting revenue base may be more fragile than appears, as c60% of data migrated between vendors is never put back online
  • Expert believes internal investigations, major revenue source, are rapidly shifting away from first-level human review entirely, with clients now using AI queries to generate risk profiles instead
  • Processing fees, historically worth millions to Epiq, will effectively disappear due to RelativityOne mandate, as competitors already offer zero-processing pricing. Competitors fully committed to RelativityOne will actively poach Epiq's Relativity Server clients by highlighting cost savings on processing and cold storage, accelerating customer attrition significantly
  • Epiq's unique multi-business structure, spanning claims administration, eDiscovery and consulting, provides strategic optionality competitors such as Consilio and Lighthouse lack, including potential asset sales to fund transformation
