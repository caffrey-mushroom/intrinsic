Agenda

  • Marathon Petroleum's (NYSE: MPC) asset quality and regional competitiveness, focusing on Garyville and Galveston Bay
  • Margin capture analysis and further optimisation opportunities
  • High utilisation implications for refinery reliability and profitability, including recent weather events

Key Insights

  • Deferring turnarounds into spring appear unlikely, given losses can be up to 4x higher from contractor reassignment and the risk of missing driving season, leaving winter deferrals more attractive despite the lost margin opportunity
  • Galveston Bay can process up to c80% Venezuelan crude, given its extensive coking capacity, but carries risk of poisoning FCC catalysts without careful blending and sufficient zeolite supply
  • Meaningful renewable diesel expansion remains constrained by fixed-bed hydrotreater exothermic limits and massive wastewater requirements, limiting yield upside to 3-4% without heavy capital investment
  • Pushing jet fuel yields too far cannibalises diesel in a "cliff trend" drop-off, though Marathon’s advanced process controls allow it to operate safely at this absolute margin boundary without degradation
  • Henry Hub gas prices ultimately cap the economics of heavier crude slates, with even a USD 1 per million BTU increase putting pressure on the hydrogen economics needed to process and upgrade the crude
