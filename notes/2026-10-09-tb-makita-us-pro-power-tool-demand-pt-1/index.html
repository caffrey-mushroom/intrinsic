Agenda

  • Makita's (TYO: 6586) US professional power tool market outlook
  • Makita battery ecosystem go-to-market strategy and 40V/80V XGT business outlook
  • Market positioning in the US vs leading competitors, such as TTI's (HKG: 0669) Milwaukee and Stanley Black & Decker's (NYSE: SWK) Dewalt

Key Insights

  • Makita is positioned to outperform broader US professional power tool market growth of 5-7% YoY with projected sales growth of 7-10% over the next 2-3 years, driven by 40V XGT platform and a strategic return to promotional pricing at retail
  • Makita is expected to expand its market share within the big three – Milwaukee, DeWalt and Makita – from 25-30% to 30-35%, primarily capturing commercial/industrial contractors and residential outdoor power users
  • Makita holds a meaningful 3-5-year competitive lead over Milwaukee and DeWalt in high-voltage, high-draw cordless SKUs and >40 XGT SKUs vs competitors. Makita's speed-to-market is constrained by over-engineering and a design philosophy where every specific tool uses a custom motor, unlike TTI/DeWalt who reuse standardised motors across lines to accelerate R&D and lower costs
  • Home Depot shelf is dominated by TTI, and Makita only holds single-digit shares
