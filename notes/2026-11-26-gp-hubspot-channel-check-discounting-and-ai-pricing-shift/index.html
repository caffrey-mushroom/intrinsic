SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, who leads a HubSpot partner agency, described a largely flat recent quarter and continued difficulty winning new clients. Strong retention and additional work from existing customers offset weaker new-client acquisition. Demand has softened among technology firms and mid-market customers, while some traditional home and commercial service businesses are showing increased interest.

The discussion covered customer demand, HubSpot’s product mix and pricing, AI adoption, competition, and the agency’s growth plans.

Key Insights

AI is putting pressure on agency pricing. Prospective clients increasingly expect AI-driven efficiency to lower implementation costs, even when projects still require substantial expertise. The expert said this perception is making deals harder to close. Customer priorities are shifting toward sales. Sales Hub demand has risen to roughly the level of Marketing Hub demand, as organizations emphasize sales processes and forecasting while scaling back some marketing activity. HubSpot’s billing is becoming less straightforward. The expert believes credit-based usage and additional product charges weaken the simplicity that historically attracted customers. Clients sometimes need help understanding the new pricing structure. The agency plans to reposition around sales. Its goal is to return to growth, with an approximately 10% growth target for the next planning period. It expects to emphasize sales strategy, coaching, and HubSpot sales workflows rather than relying mainly on traditional marketing services. AI creates opportunity but also uncertainty. The agency uses AI, yet the expert has not settled on a strategy for competing in a fast-changing market and does not plan to position the business as an AI implementation specialist.
