2026.11.23 | TB | CSU | Constellation Software's Shifting M&A Playbook

原标题：Constellation Software – Is the North Star of Acquiring Smaller & Decentralised VMS Companies Slightly Shifting?

#software

Third Bridge · 2026-11-23

核心摘要

  1. 前Constellation交易团队成员认为，Q3 2026有机增长仅2%反映其1995年以来收购的大量应用层VMS资产在AI时代面临增长压力，公司可能对部分资产采取不再投资、持续收割现金的日落策略。
  2. 市场仍预期其收入2026年增长19%、2027年16%、2028年15%，但专家指出增长主要依赖并购，非品类领导者的VMS业务正常有机增速仅约5%至8%。
  3. Constellation以收购收入低于2,000万美元的小型VMS公司见长，平均收购价约1.8倍ARR，主要通过提价和裁员提升利润，客户满意度不佳。
  4. 如今卖家可借助AI工具获知估值，Banyan、Valsoft、Beacon等竞争对手抢走北美优质资产，Constellation被迫转向越南、巴西、阿根廷、墨西哥等二三线市场。
  5. 美国和加拿大收入占比可能从2025年约50%降至未来几年的约三分之一。
  6. 公司已持有1,600多家企业但高度分散，数据仅用于KPI汇报，难以形成AI规模效应；新任首席AI官和垂直化重组更多是为了理顺并购寻源，避免七个运营集团重叠竞争。
  7. 市场预期2026至2028年EBITDA利润率维持在约27%至27.5%，专家认为垂直化尚未触及运营整合，现金流利润率则可通过自有支付、自保保险和税务结构接近40%。
  8. 过去五年公司完成了单笔约1亿至7.25亿美元的较大型交易，并以PEMS模式持有Sabre约13%股权，但专家认为大型交易往往只在缺乏竞争或资产有瑕疵时才能成交。
  9. 专家认为每年约20亿美元自由现金流给了公司试错空间，对总裁Mark Miller推动再投资和重塑声誉持乐观态度，但大型组织转向速度有限。

Agenda

  • Increasing competition with (TSE: CSU)
  • ﻿Efforts related to AI and verticalisation
  • Implications of the leadership changes that occurred a little more than a year ago

Key Insights

  • Constellation Software historically focused on acquiring hundreds of sub-scale vertical market software companies per year, raising prices and cutting headcount to improve revenues and margins, but customers don't like that approach
  • However, reflecting competitive pressures, in light of multiple would-be company buyers and AI options, Constellation Software has made multiple larger-sized acquisitions and minority investments over the past five years, valued at around USD 100-725m per piece
  • Additionally, Constellation Software has been focusing on AI and it hired a Chief AI Officer. The company has also largely completed a verticalisation restructuring, which should help with sourcing and acquisition execution in particular
  • Nonetheless, Constellation Software's Q3 2026 organic growth of 2% indicates that expected growth in the mid-to-high teens could be in question. The company has increasingly been looking outside its core US and Canada markets, whose revenue contributions could fall from around 50% of revenues in 2025 to one-third over the next few years
