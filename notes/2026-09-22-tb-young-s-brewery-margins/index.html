Agenda

  • H2 2026 and 2027 outlook for the UK pub industry, focusing on London and the Southeast
  • Cost inflation trends, pricing and profitability challenges facing the Young's (LON: YNGA) estate
  • Update on the acquired City Pubs Group properties
  • Estate expansion plans and opportunities including further M&A scenarios

Key Insights

  • London operates as an "atypical bubble" for UK pub economics – higher costs are offset by greater pricing power, making it far more resilient than regional markets during cost pressures
  • Regarding the business rates outlook, a fundamental restructuring could shift the burden from high-street bricks-and-mortar onto big-box retailers and offshore commerce, significantly benefiting London pub operators
  • Expert believes Young's like-for-like sales growth will remain positive at 4-5%, although comparables will be challenging given 2025's exceptional World Cup trading and record summer weather
  • Young's food pricing has reached a point where, surprisingly, some London diners now compare pub meal costs favourably against decent restaurants – potentially redirecting spend back towards formal dining
  • Young's rooms strategy is described as "genius" – hiding price inflation in room rates is far easier than on pints, and tourism demand for authentic pub stays supports strong RevPAR growth towards GBP 90
