Snehasish Dutta (SD) Chief Airports Officer at Reliance Infrastructure Ltd

Shahid Pathan (SP) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Passenger traffic growth, airline capacity additions and medium-term industry outlook across metro and tier 2 and 3 airports
  • Airport expansion pipeline, utilisation ramp-up and privatisation
  • Aeronautical vs non-aeronautical revenue growth, tariffs and commercial monetisation opportunities
  • Digitisation, automation and smart airport infrastructure driving operational efficiency

Key Insights

  • Expert believes subdued passenger traffic is a temporary bottleneck caused by grounded fleets and delivery delays. As new narrow- and wide-body aircraft arrive, operators can expect domestic and international passenger growth to be upwards of at least 7-8% and 9-10% annually, respectively, for next 5 years
  • Aerocity structures at major hubs such as Hyderabad and Mumbai will become critical non-aero revenue drivers over next 5 years. However, city-side real estate development at Mumbai Airport faces execution delays primarily due to land encroachments and physical obstructions
  • Expert notes future aviation growth will pivot from saturated metro hubs towards tier 2-3 cities. Smaller airlines utilising regional UDAN routes with ATR aircraft will drive this expansion, achieving operational profitability at 70% occupancy
  • Airport operators are pivoting revenue models to achieve 50-55% non-aeronautical contribution. Primary objective is to more than double current spend per passenger from USD 8 to global benchmark levels of USD 18-20 to maximise wallet share
