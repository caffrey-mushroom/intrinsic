Analyst-led • 2 Oct 2026

Companies: HD US, PLRM IL

David Kotler (DK) Head, Marketing at Admetec Solutions Ltd

Quinton Flore (QF) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Where polycarbonate's strength ends and the weaker PVC and renewable positions begin within the portfolio
  • Home patio and outdoor living demand against flat agriculture and older display end markets
  • Why Plaskolite's international business leans on distributors rather than the direct store relationships it holds in the US
  • Competitive positioning against European and Israeli sheet extruders on cost, breadth and speed-to-market

Key Insights

  • Plaskolite's revenue splits c40-50% polycarbonate, 40% acrylic and 10-20% PVC, with c60% residential. Expert sees CAGR lifting to 7-10% from 5-7% over 3-5 years, above a c6.2% market, on acquired polycarbonate know-how
  • Big-box retailers take 50-60% of volume and lumber yards 20-30%, locked in through long, restrictive contracts. The key shelf risk is speciality shapes, colours and textures, a known weak spot
  • Agriculture is shrinking, with 70-80% of the decline attributed to Asian imports priced 30-40% below US product. Expert sees Asian suppliers threatening residential and commercial lines in 5-10 years
  • Plaskolite only began post-consumer products 2-3 years ago and trails Palram and Exolon on green reputation. Sustainable content is 5-10% of project material, so buying a European brand would be a branding fix
  • Integration of Plazit-Polygal was smooth, retaining expertise despite losing some conflicted customers, with gradual consolidation and no major cuts
