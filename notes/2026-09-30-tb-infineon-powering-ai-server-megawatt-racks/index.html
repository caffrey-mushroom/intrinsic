Companies: IFX DE, NVDA US, TXN US

Paul Arts (PA) Head, Research Unit Integrated Photonics Technologies at Silicon Austria Labs GmbH (SAL)

Saad Maqsood (SM) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Infineon's (XETRA: IFX) market share expansion and competitive positioning across high-density power supply units and power management ICs (integrated circuits) for next-generation AI servers
  • Demand trajectory and design-win momentum for silicon, SiC (silicon carbide) and GaN (gallium nitride) power architectures
  • Competitive dynamics and technology differentiation
  • Revenue contribution, order visibility and margin uplift opportunities

Key Insights

  • Expert anticipates Infineon's AI servers will surpass targets and potentially double revenues again in FY27
  • On the 800V DC shift, Infineon's margins are largest in high-voltage rack supply requiring 10kV line conversion, with "hardly any competition" at those voltage levels
  • Transition from component supplier to full system provider was driven primarily by speed-to-market needs. Infineon shortened development cycles to keep pace with Nvidia's GPU generations, winning major accounts
  • Expert believes supply constraints pose the biggest near-term risk, as capacity-limited sales could invite China-based competitors into the gaps that Infineon cannot fill quickly enough. For gallium supply chain vulnerability, Infineon may leverage European strategic initiatives to mitigate it
  • Texas Instruments poses a credible threat, specifically to the IC and intelligence layer, where Infineon remains comparatively weaker despite moving R&D talent towards customer-facing technical marketing roles
