Companies: CSCO US, NOKIA FI, ERIC B SE, CIEN US, NVDA US, ANET US

Chris Gibson (CG) Independent Consultant

Agenda

  • Nokia's (HEL: NOKIA) optical and hyperscaler expansion – integrating Infinera to capture high-bandwidth AI data centre interconnect demand ahead of Ciena (NYSE: CIEN) and Cisco (NASDAQ: CSCO)
  • AI-RAN and 5G-advanced commercialisation – partnering with Nvidia (NASDAQ: NVDA) to deploy AI-native radio platforms and regain mobile market share from Ericsson (STO: ERIC B) and Huawei
  • Enterprise IP routing scaling – expanding 800G switching and routing platforms to capture cloud and data centre network spend from Arista (NYSE: ANET) and Cisco
  • High-margin IP patent monetisation – leveraging Nokia Technologies' 5G/6G licensing cash flows to fund network infrastructure R&D

Key Insights

  • Nokia's AI and cloud order intake reached EUR 2.8bn in Q2 2026, more than doubling YoY, but sustaining 100% QoQ growth is mathematically implausible – 30-40% QoQ is more realistic
  • Expert believes Nokia's stated 50% order conversion target over 12 months is deliberately conservative, suggesting 60-75% conversion is achievable, rating confidence at 10/10 for the 50% target
  • Acquisition of Infinera dramatically strengthened Nokia's North American presence and web-scaler relationships, with customers "flocking" to Nokia booths post closing, validating the strategic rationale immediately
  • DDR memory constraints, driven by factory capacity shifting towards high-bandwidth memory for AI servers, remain a critical bottleneck through H1 2027, with high-bandwidth memory requiring roughly 3x the manufacturing capacity of standard DDR
  • Network Infrastructure gross margins could realistically reach the mid-50s within 24 months, up from c43% today, driven by new product innovation reducing cost-per-bit rather than component cost reductions
