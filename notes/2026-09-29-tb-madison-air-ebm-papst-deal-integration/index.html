Agenda

  • Likely rationale behind Madison Air Solutions' (NYSE: MAIR) August 2026-announced acquisition of EBM-Papst
  • Long-term synergy potential

Key Insights

  • "I don't see really much synergies between [Madison Air Solutions and EBM-Papst]" – expert believes cost synergies will be USD 20m-30m, not USD 160m as expected
  • Pricing across end markets can increase 10-20%, with fan demand expected to increase 30-40% in 2-3 years, but Madison Air/EBM-Papst will only be able to capture 20% growth due to North American supplier constraints
  • North American local sourcing remains weak with c40-50% of components sourced locally in North America; could improve by c20pp over 2-3 years with Madison's help. Expert notes lead times for existing customers are 1-2 years and new customers are three years with key competitor Ziehl-Abegg already facing supply issues
  • Expert notes EBM-Papst is developing high-speed compressors running at 200,000 revolutions per minute, representing a potential USD 1bn-2bn new market opportunity, but this technology will take c5 years to commercialise
