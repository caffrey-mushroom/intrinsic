原标题：Vista – Financial Management Strategies & Cloud Transformation Monetisation

#software

Third Bridge · 2026-11-03

核心摘要

  1. 受访者是前Vista集团CFO，讨论影院软件商Vista的云化转型。
  2. 从本地许可转向Vista Cloud后，定价从按影院收维护费改为收入分成，大型客户一般谈到收入的1%以下。
  3. 由于整合了营销和运营能力，这一转型带来相对历史维护费约3倍的收入提升。
  4. 专家预计未来3–4年收入同比增12–15%，现有客户迁移时间表可见度高。
  5. FY27底超过50%的已装站点将迁到云端，中期变现转向集成支付、AI动态票价和自动排片等数字附加服务。
  6. Cinemex回归Vista说明自建方案成本高，专家预计未来2–3年市场份额每年扩大1–2个百分点，重点是欧洲、韩国和日本。

Companies: VGL NZ, NFLX US, CGX CA

Matthew John Harvey Cawte (MJHC) Independent Consultant

Joanna Chan (JC) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Vista's (NZX: VGL) financial resilience and R&D allocation strategies
  • Legacy-to-cloud pricing strategy and customer migration frictions
  • ARPU growth levers and secondary monetisation opportunities

Key Insights

  • Moving from on-premise licensing to Vista Cloud shifts pricing model from per-site maintenance to revenue sharing, where larger enterprises typically negotiate rates <1% of revenue. This transition delivers a 3x revenue uplift over historical maintenance fees due to integrated marketing and operational capabilities
  • Revenue growth is projected to track at 12-15% YoY over next 3-4 years, supported by clear visibility into existing customer migration schedules
  • With more than 50% of installed sites projected to transition to cloud infrastructure by end of FY27, expert believes medium-term monetisation will pivot towards digital add-ons such as integrated payments, AI-driven dynamic ticket pricing and automated film scheduling
  • Expert thinks Cinemex's return to Vista demonstrates high costs of attempting bespoke in-house builds, reinforcing Vista's enterprise competitive moat. Market share is expected to expand by 1-2% annually over next 2-3 years, targeting Europe, Korea and Japan
