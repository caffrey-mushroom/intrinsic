Agenda

  • Air Liquide's (EPA: AI) global business model and key product lines
  • Regional volume outlook, end-market demand trends and market share dynamics
  • Energy cost pass-through mechanics, pricing power and inflation impact on profitability
  • Competitive positioning and strategic differentiation across product portfolio

Key Insights

  • Linde outpaces Air Liquide in profitability due to its Praxair legacy, locking in long-term global client contracts and utilising fully-depreciated assets to offer lower prices, maintaining superior margins. Air Liquide also lags behind Linde in major project execution due to centralised, risk-averse approval process
  • Severe regional deficits in helium, argon, CO2 and hydrogen enable Air Liquide to boost margins through pre-sale contracts, transport surcharges for complex geographies and savings-based efficiency fees
  • With South American facilities running at 90-95% utilisation, masked demand creates prime conditions for asset expansion. Management projects annual volume growth of 4-5% in core molecules, 10% in CO2, and 20% in hydrogen, anchored by standard EUR 65m air separation unit investments
  • Air Liquide's multi-gen facility in Colombia, capturing industrial CO2 while generating client-side energy and utilities, represents unique, self-powered asset with strong margin potential and global scalability
