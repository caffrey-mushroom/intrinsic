2026.11.16 | TB | Energy | ITM Power Electrolyser Demand & Chronos

原标题：ITM Power – Electrolyser Demand Trends & R&D Investment Priorities

#energy #industrials

Third Bridge · 2026-11-16

核心摘要

  1. ITM Power前运营财务总监表示，交通出行需求几乎崩塌：英国仅有5-10座加氢站、网络过于分散，垃圾车、公交等商用场景也因建站资本开支大且缺乏政府资金而难以推进，市场普遍转向电池车。
  2. 专家估计炼油应用约占ITM预期收入的60-70%，工业化学品（如合成氨）约占20-30%，与Rheinmetall的国防合作尚处早期；大规模Trident及未来Chronos产品将是主要收入来源。
  3. 公司公布的合同积压全部为具约束力的合同，MoU转化为订单的关键在于客户获得（往往依赖政府的）资金和锁定购氢方；EPC风险通常转移给合作方，交付罚款风险管理良好。
  4. 需求对欧盟氢能银行、德国核心管网和英国氢能商业模式等政策延误相当敏感，德国、英国和挪威等北欧国家的项目最活跃；Sheffield工厂需要更多订单量来摊薄成本。
  5. 面对中国碱性电解槽厂商，ITM无法在价格上取胜，但可凭总拥有成本和日益增多的参考电厂形成差异化，专家预计若中国产品形成不公平竞争，英国可能设立关税壁垒。
  6. 专家认为Chronos电堆是Trident的演进而非全新产品，2028年推出目标可以实现，预计将带动毛利率改善；目前大部分合同已实现正毛利，但旧产品毛利仍承压，研发支出占收入比重未来应下降。
  7. 长期服务合同机会可观，未来可能贡献约20-40%的收入，但参考电厂数量少、长期现场数据有限且易受异常值影响，限制了客户采纳。
  8. 铱等原材料价格风险通过合同中的价格调整和指数化条款基本对冲，已签合同的贵金属均已采购锁定；专家估计在不出现采购延误的情况下可承接约0.3-0.4GW的订单，并建议加强闭环回收而非囤积库存投机。

Companies: ITM GB

Simon Holmes (SH) Director, Finance at Harworth Group plc

Olly Anibaba (OA) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Electrolyser demand across key applications and market growth outlook
  • R&D investment on product innovation, highlighting potential adoption rate and earnings implications
  • Cost inflation pressures – raw materials and components required for electrolysers production, including mitigating options available

Key Insights

  • Mobility demand has nearly collapsed, with hydrogen refuelling stations failing to gain traction due to network fragmentation and insufficient government funding to sustain infrastructure build-out across the UK
  • Refining applications now represent approximately 60-70% of ITM's projected revenue, with industrial chemicals accounting for 20-30%, marking a dramatic shift from historically diversified end-market exposure
  • On Chinese competition, ITM cannot win on price against alkaline manufacturers, but total cost of ownership and growing number of reference plant installations provide defensible differentiation in European industrial markets
  • Raw material price exposure is largely mitigated through contractual escalation and indexation clauses, meaning iridium price spikes should not significantly erode gross margins on contracted projects
  • Service contracts represent a good opportunity, though limited long-term in-field performance data hinders adoption. Data analytics capabilities exist, but the small number of reference plants limits the statistical pool and can lead to outlier-skewed data
