Agenda

  • Belron's potential IPO analysis, assessing the valuation and pros and cons
  • Sustainability of H1 2026's 23.0% operating margin amid ADAS (advanced driver-assistance systems) calibration tailwinds and labour inflation
  • Feasibility of international expansion into Asia and bolt-on acquisition returns in Europe
  • Gross margin resilience against US auto tariffs, glass supply disruptions and energy cost inflation

Key Insights

  • EV manufacturers are making windshield glass thinner to offset heavy battery weight, significantly increasing windshield vulnerability to minor road impacts and elevating replacement frequency across newer vehicle fleets
  • ADAS recalibration adds c40% more gross margin per transaction, with recalibration services averaging USD 275-300, making newer car park penetration increasingly critical to Belron's margin expansion story
  • The US market achieves superior operational leverage over Europe by using a mobile model, where one dispatcher manages fifteen van technicians, eliminating real estate and administrative storefront overhead
  • While vehicle OEMs attempt to gatekeep ADAS recalibrations by charging diagnostic access toll fees, Belron bypasses high costs through third-party aggregators offering consolidated access across multiple OEM security gateways
