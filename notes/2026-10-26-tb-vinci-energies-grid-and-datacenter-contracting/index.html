原标题：Vinci Energies – Omexom & Actemium – Energy Infrastructure, Power Grids & Industrial Manufacturing

#grid #power #datacenter

Third Bridge · 2026-10-26

核心摘要

  1. Omexom电网业务到2030年有望保持接近两位数增长。
  2. 电网投资是主要增长驱动。
  3. 数据中心承包合同利润率偏低，不是利润的主要来源。
  4. Vinci集团2025年营收约750亿欧元，其中Vinci Energies约220亿欧元，Omexom约占30%。
  5. 专家认为约10%的EBITDA利润率是现实的，高利润的Axians IT业务平衡了低利润的工业和电网承包。

Analyst-led • 16 Oct 2026

Companies: DG FR, ENR DE, SPIE FR, J US

Klaus Trentler (KT) General Manager Germany at Beck & Pollitzer Germany GmbH

Louis Knight (LK) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Demand overview and core growth opportunities
  • Vinci's (EPA: DG) competitive landscape, noting Omexom and Actemium key differentiators
  • Margin profile and long-term accretion potential
  • Recent and future M&A deals

Key Insights

  • Omexom's grid business is expected to sustain near-double-digit organic growth through 2030, driven by renewable energy, data centres and EV infrastructure demanding massive European grid upgrades
  • Data centre contracts are unlikely to be highly profitable for Vinci Energies, as specialist engineering, procurement and construction firms such as Mercury and Jacobs aggressively compress subcontractor margins through procurement power
  • Actemium's industrial automation segment faces structural headwinds, with long-term organic growth likely capped at 1-3% as German automotive and chemical manufacturing relocates to India and Mexico
  • Omexom's key competitive advantage over OEMs such as Siemens Energy is Vinci Group's ability to bundle civil construction with electrical work, offering customers a single integrated delivery partner
  • 10% EBITDA margin is realistic, driven largely by the highly profitable Axians IT brand balancing lower-margin industrial and grid contracting
