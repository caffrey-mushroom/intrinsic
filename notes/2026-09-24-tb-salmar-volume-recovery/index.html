Companies: SALM NO, LSG NO, MOWI NO

Håvard Stranden (HS) Board Chairman at Piscada Aqua AS

Yanmei Tang (YT) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • SalMar's (OSL: SALM) volume growth outlook, cost normalisation, salmon pricing and potential contribution to the 2026 earnings recovery
  • Scope to capture more value through secondary processing rather than relying primarily on raw salmon sales
  • Profitability and utilisation potential across farming, processing, branding and downstream distribution
  • Ability to improve site utilisation and extract more production from the current asset base

Key Insights

  • The global salmon supply growth is expected to slow materially over the next 12-18 months, constrained by regulation, biology and production limits – Chile isn't expected to flood the market or cap Norwegian pricing
  • SalMar's 2026 performance partly reflects unusually favourable biology, but its lean staffing, strong biomass management, smolt control and highly efficient processing infrastructure remain structural operational advantages
  • Strong 2026 harvesting could mechanically create future biomass pressure, although SalMar's recent guidance implies it expects biological performance and production volumes to remain sustainable, despite that industry-level risk
  • SalMar's acquisition of Måsøval should strengthen SalMar's regional biomass position and site utilisation, with meaningful operational synergies expected, although full integration could take 1-2 years and require incremental investment
  • Expert believes submersible pen technology is unlikely to succeed in the long term, while closed-cage systems combined with traditional grow-out finishing phases represent the most promising future production method
