Agenda

  • RWS's (LON: RWS) platform cross-sell and monetisation
  • Customer use cases and contract value as AI translation grows in prominence
  • Sales strategy, synergy opportunities and maintaining the customer base

Key Insights

  • RWS's acquisition of Acolad is strategically defensive rather than a quality or capability upgrade, as both companies offer comparable translation services and cross-sell is expected to be limited. Translation negative growth driven by pricing is expected even if customers are retained
  • Expert anticipates no financial synergies between Acolad and RWS in the near term, with Acolad continuing its c7% revenue decline and RWS maintaining 2-3% organic growth, netting to 2% organic growth post-acquisition. Generate upsell is the clearest opportunity, however, execution has been lacking
  • RWS structurally dismantled horizontal cross-selling in 2025 by verticalising into three silos – Generate, Transform and Protect – eliminating the "quarterback" role that previously coordinated growth across enterprise accounts
  • RWS's new leadership is copying a hyperscaler "utility meter" playbook fundamentally misaligned with expert's view of RWS as a services-based, relationship-driven solution
