核心摘要

  1. Ramboll数据中心战略顾问（28年行业经验）表示，SC 2026上行业关注点首次从GPU性能转向配套关键基础设施，包括供电、液冷、系统集成、可靠性以及机柜密度提升对整体设计和调试的影响。
  2. 他对2028年及以后的长期增长保持乐观，但提醒要区分宣布、获得融资和签约的项目：德州宣布的数据中心规模约40GW，而ERCOT预计只能提供约11GW，部分园区会被推迟、缩小规模或重新评估。
  3. 美国大选后各州和县的审查、暂停令及环保要求将会加强，开发商会更谨慎地投入资本，并优先选择工业用地和有现实许可路径的地块。
  4. 专家对表后(behind-the-meter)天然气发电作为过渡电源持怀疑态度，许可、环评和设备供应链使其开发周期也需要3-5年，与等待电网接入相差无几，部分项目已暂停或取消。
  5. 社区沟通仍然不足，居民关心的是用水、噪音、电费、交通和长期就业，而不只是税收；美国一家运营商承诺向社区投入约1亿美元，欧洲有开发商为社区建造体育场和学校。
  6. 冷却方案需要结合负载、气候、可靠性和全生命周期成本来定制：干式冷却节水但占地更多；更大的CDU可以减少连接点、提升效率，但会集中单点故障风险；数据厅将分别采用液冷和风冷两套策略。
  7. 下一代AI设备越来越多指定更高温的液冷回路，英伟达宣布的45°C回路在合适的气候下可大幅减少甚至取消压缩机制冷，但存量混合IT设施的转变会较慢，冷水机组仍会作为备份。
  8. OCP白皮书提出单相直触芯片冷却在单die约1,000W时效率下降、约4,000W时达到物理极限，专家不认为这是通用阈值；两相冷却传热性能好，但成本高、缺乏可靠性数据，需先通过试点验证。
  9. 最大的交付风险在于IT规格与设施设计日益深度绑定，灵活性降低，且多厂商液冷控制系统让调试更加复杂，合格的调试人员成为瓶颈；施耐德、Vertiv在推广撬装预制模块，但运输尺寸和重量限制了预制程度。
  10. OEM可以通过可量化的节能效果、预测性维护和控制软件来变现（例如按节省金额分成），施耐德、Vertiv、江森自控等正在收购软件公司，Datadog、Dynatrace等可观测性平台也在切入，关键在于开放协议、互操作性和网络安全，而不是再做一个仪表盘。

SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, a mechanical engineer with extensive data center experience, discussed how rising AI workloads are shifting industry attention from chip performance alone to the infrastructure needed to support high-density computing. Key topics included power access, liquid cooling, permitting, community concerns, project feasibility, prefabrication, and operational software.

The expert remains positive about longer-term data center growth but cautioned that announced capacity should not be confused with funded or contracted projects. Development is likely to vary by region, with US projects facing greater local scrutiny and some campuses delayed, resized, or reconsidered.

Key Insights

Data center design is becoming an integrated challenge: IT specifications increasingly determine facility power, cooling, redundancy, and commissioning requirements. The expert warned that this tighter connection reduces flexibility and increases delivery risk, particularly as commissioning teams must handle systems from multiple vendors. Behind-the-meter natural gas should not be assumed to provide a quick bridge to grid power. Permitting, environmental reviews, and equipment supply can make its timeline comparable to utility connections. Community engagement remains inadequate. Developers need credible answers on water, electricity costs, noise, traffic, and local employment, rather than relying mainly on financial or civic incentives. Cooling decisions should be tailored to workload, climate, reliability needs, and lifecycle cost. Water-saving dry cooling can require more land, while larger cooling distribution units can improve installation efficiency but concentrate risk. Two-phase direct-to-chip cooling offers promising heat transfer, but expense and limited operating evidence currently constrain broad adoption. OEMs may monetize measurable energy savings, predictive maintenance, and controls software. The expert emphasized interoperability, cybersecurity, and demonstrated performance over additional dashboards.
