原标题：AccentCare – PCS & Hospice Disposals & RemainCo Positioning

#healthcare

Third Bridge · 2026-10-23

Analyst-led • 19 Oct 2026

Companies: ADUS US

Chris Oltmans (CO) Chief Population Health Officer at NxtCare LLC

Benjamin Parsons (BP) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Likely acquirers and multiples for PCS (personal care services) and hospice disposals
  • Implications of disposals for RemainCo positioning, in terms of referral volumes, EHR (electronic health record) and RCM (revenue cycle management)
  • Labour market positioning, reimbursement trends and operational levers for the home health business

Key Insights

  • EBITDA margins likely sit c3% for NY PCS business, 5% for home health and 7-9% for hospice
  • Expert sees 12x EBITDA multiple as the floor for hospice sale, with 16x multiple "completely fair". Hospice licenses will not grow and demand for services is increasing. A Medicare Advantage payer makes sense as an acquirer. They can direct patients towards hospice, which is covered by Medicare, and save USD 25,000-30,000 on rehospitalisations
  • EHR and RCM workflows are 100% independent across assets. There are minimal obstacles to clean breakup. Despite some success, referral synergies were never materially realised across care settings, with c10% of hospice volumes from home health and 1% of home health from PCS
  • RemainCo priority ought to be focus on sales leadership, with repeated placement of leaders lacking home health experience leading to short-sighted, margin-focused referral source management hurting the business in the long run
