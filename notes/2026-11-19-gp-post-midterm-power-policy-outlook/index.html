原标题：Power Policy: What Changes Are Coming for Gas, Renewables, and Data Center Power? A Former FERC Chair Discusses

#power #datacenter #energy

核心摘要

  1. 前FERC主席认为此次中期选举的“蓝色浪潮”部分源于选民对数据中心推高电价及水、空气、噪音影响的担忧，已有40多个地方辖区投票禁止、限制或暂停数据中心开发，德州和内华达新任州长均以限制数据中心为竞选承诺。
  2. 专家预计输电选址与并网改革有两党支持，但年内难以通过，明年新国会大概率重新起草相关法案；强制所有公用事业加入ISO/RTO可大幅降低东南部消费者成本，但在现届政府下可能性很小。
  3. 以Entergy为例，研究显示其加入MISO可为消费者节省约7亿美元；此类改革会将收益从Southern、Duke、NextEra等公用事业转向消费者，压低其资本开支和回报。
  4. 专家认为并网速度将主要靠技术提升，如Pearl Street为MISO做的升级分析软件仅需约10分钟，而传统方式需6-8个月，加上德州式“先接入后管理”机制；但变压器交期仍长达24-36个月。
  5. 各州监管机构将以可负担性为核心，更严格审查早期项目，增加电价上限和递延回收，并推动VPP等非资本开支方案；报告称60GW电网边缘分布式资源可带来约260亿美元批发市场收益。
  6. Voltus与Google签订了1000MW的双边协议，容量来自沃尔玛、家得宝等800多家客户的负荷灵活性和大规模电池储能，而非新建电厂。
  7. 数据中心正转向表后自发电，如内华达一个600MW的燃气轮机项目以卡车运输压缩天然气，作为3-4年过渡方案；专家认为尽管审批放缓，2028年新增电力不会较2027年下降。
  8. 专家预计电网升级成本大部分将分摊给超大规模云厂商和数据中心开发商，PJM容量价格将维持在约550美元的上限附近，FERC可能批准延长价格区间。
  9. 可再生能源短期内难获税收支持，但可受益于并网和输电改革及供应链激励；现届政府的亲天然气政策预计将延续，限制天然气开发的立法难以通过。

SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, a former federal energy regulator and current energy-sector adviser, discussed how political pressure over electricity affordability may reshape data-center development, utility investment, and power-market rules. Key topics included transmission permitting, grid interconnection, distributed energy resources, and the outlook for gas and renewable generation.

Key Insights

Consumer affordability is becoming a central concern across political parties. The expert expects more scrutiny of data-center costs and greater efforts to prevent those costs from flowing to households. Transmission and interconnection reforms could move forward, with broad bipartisan interest in improving project timelines. However, requiring every utility to join a regional grid operator would face significant political resistance. Faster interconnection reviews may come from improved software and operational changes, including approaches that allow projects to connect while grid upgrades proceed. Regulators are likely to examine early-stage utility projects more closely, consider rate caps or delayed cost recovery, and encourage customer-side alternatives such as virtual power plants. Data-center demand is likely to continue driving new resources, even if local approvals slow. The expert expects more direct power contracts and on-site generation, alongside utility-scale development. The expert expects gas policy to remain supportive under the current administration and doubts that legislation restricting gas development would advance.
