Third Bridge · 2026-11-13

核心摘要

  1. 专家表示，2026年下半年中国推理需求的增量中，办公类Agent已超过编程，主要受千问办公、WorkBuddy等大厂通过token补贴、积分和任务模板大力推广驱动；编程和短剧占比有所下降，世界模型在应用侧仍很少。
  2. 按算力消耗计，短剧内容约占国内推理算力的60%，剩余部分中办公类任务不到20%，AI开发与编程约占30%；增量token中约80%来自存量用户，新获取的办公用户只贡献约20%。
  3. 今年token消耗大致每3-4个月翻一番，目前规模约为年初的4-5倍，但增速正在放缓，下一次翻倍可能需要6-8个月甚至更久；受算力（难以获得B200/B300）和高质量数据所限，国产模型半年内难以突破3T参数。
  4. 办公Agent任务的缓存命中率超过95%，一次典型的表格或PPT任务高峰期成本约2-3元人民币，上下文通常在20万至30万token；Agent自主调用子Agent使90%的token消耗来自AI调用AI，用户省了时间但花了更多钱。
  5. 新用户主要靠签到积分和免费额度获取，其收入难以覆盖获客和补贴成本；不超过10%的重度用户升级套餐带来的额外收入也难以覆盖其额外算力，利润主要来自中低档会员和闲置额度，例如9.9元会员。
  6. B端仍处于扩张期，以账号授权加token计费为主，叠加开户和合规成本后，等效毛利率可能低于C端；预计2027年企业将覆盖60-70%的员工，账号数会显著增加。
  7. WorkBuddy凭借先发优势、最全面的模型接入和中立开放策略保持领先，用户沉淀的工作空间资产形成留存，但其生态整合度不如阿里和字节；生态开放度排序为腾讯、阿里、字节，Kimi等基于CLI的Agent门槛偏高。
  8. 海外对算力的监管收紧（例如要求超过51%的算力供给非中国模型）将制约国产模型训练和出海，加剧AI生态割裂；AI眼镜等端侧设备至少还需两年才能成熟，但家用AI算力主机市场成熟更快。

Agenda

  • Chief use cases of AI workload consumption, inference usage growth drivers, contribution of high-growth task frequency and per-task token usage, plus sustained growth potential
  • User feedback on adoption of personal AI assistants and agentic AI, commercial monetisation models, ecosystem barriers and bottlenecks, plus on-device hardware integration prospects
  • Competition among model developers, independent agent vendors and vertical software providers, plus distribution of traffic entry points, workflows and commercial value

Key Insights

  • Short dramas account for about 60% of China's inference compute consumption; incremental growth from office agents exceeds that from coding, and about 80% of token usage growth comes from existing users. The token consumption doubling cycle is expected to lengthen from 3-4 months to 6-8 months or longer; in the near term, compute and high-quality data will be hurdles for Chinese models crossing the 3T parameter threshold
  • For office agents, revenue from new customers is hard to cover costs such as user acquisition and subsidies, and revenue from heavy-user upgrades may not cover additional compute. The B-end segment is still in the expansion phase, and its equivalent gross margin may be lower than that of the C-end segment. WorkBuddy maintains its edge through an open multi-model strategy and accumulated workspace assets, with an optimistic growth outlook for 2027
  • Tighter overseas regulatory controls on compute could affect Chinese model training and overseas expansion, raising the risk of AI ecosystem segregation
