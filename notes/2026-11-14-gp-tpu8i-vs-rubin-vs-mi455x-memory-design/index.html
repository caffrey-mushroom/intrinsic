核心摘要

  1. d-Matrix院士认为，英伟达Rubin保持288GB HBM、Rubin Ultra据报降至192GB，而AMD MI455X为432GB、谷歌TPU 8i Max达576GB，这种分化既源于HBM供应紧张，也源于工作负载从大批量训练转向推理、强化学习和后训练。
  2. 他指出HBM堆叠超过约4层后只增加容量不增加带宽，对交互式应用而言带宽比容量更关键，英伟达降低单卡内存是在保留高带宽的前提下适应市场与成本现实。
  3. 英伟达能这样做依赖其系统优势：机架已成为计算单元，NVLink可将72到576个器件组成低延迟的统一扩展域，再辅以闪存、NVMe、网络内存和Vera的系统内存等片外层级来弥补训练所需容量。
  4. 据报部分Blackwell系统有50–60%的内存容量未被使用，专家认为这取决于负载；以两颗小内存芯片替代一颗大内存芯片，因HBM是BOM中最贵的部分，总价不会翻倍，同时带宽也成倍增加。
  5. AMD和谷歌缺乏同等的短距离扩展网络，因此押注大容量芯片服务离线代理、长文档处理、代码库重构等对交互性要求低、更看重每美元token和并发的场景，这块市场正在快速扩大。
  6. AMD定位为高性价比方案，但仍需在CUDA、TensorRT、NCCL、vLLM等周边软件生态、集群互联和客户信任上追赶；谷歌TPU历经8代已在算力和带宽规格上追平英伟达，并依靠大规模横向扩展集群竞争。
  7. 英伟达宣称Rubin相比GB300的每兆瓦推理吞吐提升最高30倍，专家认为来自约2倍带宽提升、4bit数据类型、微架构改进和推测解码等软件技术的叠加。
  8. 专家判断HBM需求短期内不会下降，除非前沿模型实现大幅上下文压缩；CXL、高带宽闪存等新内存层级释放的容量会被新应用吞噬，符合杰文斯悖论，只是增速可能放缓。
  9. 高通、d-Matrix等押注3D堆叠DRAM，主要解决带宽和数据搬运能耗而非容量问题，相关产品明年上市；NVLink Fusion向第三方ASIC开放，可让部分采用DDR的加速器接入，对HBM需求影响有限。

SUMMARY

Overview

The discussion examined how memory capacity, bandwidth, and interconnect design shape AI accelerator strategies at NVIDIA, AMD, Google, and emerging chip vendors. The expert argued that shifting workloads—from large-batch training toward inference, reinforcement learning, and post-training—make bandwidth and system-level performance as important as memory capacity.

Key Insights

Capacity is not the only measure of value. Adding HBM layers beyond a certain point can increase capacity without increasing bandwidth. For interactive applications, bandwidth may matter more; larger capacity can benefit high-batch, less latency- sensitive workloads. NVIDIA’s lower-memory approach may be deliberate. The expert sees it as a response to HBM supply constraints, cost, and possible memory underuse, enabled by fast system interconnects and off-device memory tiers. It could encourage customers to use multiple accelerators, though this is a risky strategy. The rack is increasingly the unit of compute. NVIDIA’s NVLink can combine devices into a low-latency scale-up system. Google emphasizes larger scale-out clusters, where communication between devices has higher latency. Demand for AI memory is not expected to decline in the near term. Additional memory tiers and more efficient software could relieve capacity pressures, though growing applications may use up the capacity they release, reflecting a rebound effect. AMD and Google have an opportunity in throughput-oriented workloads. Larger-memory devices may appeal where cost per token, concurrency, and context size matter more than fast interaction. The expert cautioned that AMD still faces software, connectivity, and customer-transition hurdles.
