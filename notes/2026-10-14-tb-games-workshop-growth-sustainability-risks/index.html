Agenda

  • Trends and growth drivers for Games Workshop (LON: GAW)
  • UK market ceiling, North America scaling and broader opportunities
  • Group strategy and risk-appetite
  • Longer-term roadmap, market threats and execution risks

Key Insights

  • Expert believes Games Workshop can sustain low-to-mid-teens annual revenue growth mid-term, citing locked-in ecosystem dynamics, rising fandom culture and upcoming Amazon entertainment catalysts
  • The UK market is described as effectively mature, with only modest incremental growth expected and double-digit spikes on large releases. Adding occasional staffing for events in single-employee stores could improve new player acquisition and retention rates
  • The US could be accelerated through a different strategy involving additional flagship stores, increased local games stores activity and entering retail space in malls. Awareness is strong but accessibility will take a long time to scale
  • Space Marine 2's massive success was not fully capitalised upon – the licensing response was reactive rather than strategic, suggesting Games Workshop lacks sophisticated entertainment-to-retail conversion infrastructure currently
  • Cultural translation remains a significant barrier to international expansion. The British-centric product and marketing may limit penetration across non-English-speaking markets
