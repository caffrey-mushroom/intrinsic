  2. 受监管行业部署AI的关键在于数据路径、治理、审计与升级机制，医疗会区分临床与行政用途，生命科学关注FDA申报文件的可追溯性，金融则关注是否接触客户与交易。

  5. 专家认为中国模型技术能力很强：DeepSeek擅长推理且成本约为Anthropic的十分之一，Qwen用于影像和对话，Kimi适合代理任务，GLM几乎可替代Claude做编码；在本地GPU上运行的客户最满意，但对司法管辖和未来被禁的担忧仍在。

  7. 自行微调往往难以算账：某客户基于DeepSeek训练的决策模型年托管成本达六位数，而后来的商用模型性能相当、成本低至少1万倍；定制化大约只能领先一个模型世代，即约6个月。
  8. OpenAI的低价模型约0.10美元/百万token、约为前沿模型的百分之一，专家预计高量低智能任务最终将接近“电价加10%”，应按每个被接受结果的总成本而非token价格比较。
  9. 专家认为企业最需要的是可靠性而非基准领先，目前很少有厂商以企业期望的规模和价格提供可靠性，这是一个市场机会；除地缘因素外，他不会优先推荐Mistral而非阿里系模型。

SUMMARY

Overview

An enterprise AI expert who works with healthcare, life sciences, and financial-services organizations discussed how these sectors evaluate large language models. Adoption is expanding from low-risk assistance toward more consequential tasks, but deployment depends on governance, data protection, oversight, and reliable integration—not model capability alone.

The expert said model performance per dollar is improving rapidly, enabling agents to handle longer, more complex tasks. Regulated organizations nevertheless prioritize clear limits, escalation procedures, auditability, and controls. Examples include administrative healthcare tasks, preparation of regulated life-sciences documents, and financial workflows involving customer interaction or transactions.

Key Insights

Cloud-provider relationships often shape model selection more than model rankings. Organizations typically assess identity, access, networking, logging, and data controls before choosing a model. The expert sees the overall deployment system and approved workflow as the real unit of evaluation. A capable model is not useful if the organization cannot validate, govern, and operate it safely. Open and lower-cost models can be attractive when they meet task requirements and can run within acceptable data boundaries. The expert views Chinese models as technically capable, while noting concerns about jurisdiction, policy risk, and potential future restrictions. Fine-tuning and self-hosting are difficult to justify when commercial models deliver comparable results at much lower total cost. Custom approaches may provide a temporary advantage, but model improvements can erode it. The expert expects routine tasks to shift away from expensive frontier models. Buyers should compare cost per successful outcome, including retries and human review, rather than token prices alone. Reliability remains an unmet enterprise need. The expert argued that dependable operation may matter more to customers than leading benchmark performance.
