  • Coloplast's (CPH: COLO-B) Wound & Tissue Repair segment and deep dive into Advanced Wound Dressings and Biologics

Key Insights

  • Germany's pricing reforms threaten Coloplast's growth, although expert notes company is well-positioned to navigate them. A lot of the hit from reimbursement changes could be offset by using other products in the Coloplast portfolio
  • Coloplast was largest advanced wound dressing player in China before recall, making impact more significant. Growth will be in mid-single-digits "once they have washed the recall issue out of the baseline"
  • Coloplast's Biatain Silicone Fit with Vizient group purchasing organisation listing is driving US dressing growth through pressure injury prevention indication
  • Expert thinks biologics business "has the potential to grow certainly 15-20% YoY once […] reimbursement change challenges have settled"
  • Kerecis has product superiority over competitors, yet expert notes its market share remains only 5-10% in US due to commercial execution vs product quality. Expert adds Coloplast has "a better case for winning in the inpatient segment". "It's slightly less fragmented"
