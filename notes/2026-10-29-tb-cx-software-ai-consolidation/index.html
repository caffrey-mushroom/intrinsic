Agenda

  • Customer spending and priorities across CXM software
  • Qualtrics vs Medallia's products, adoption and competitive positioning

Key Insights

  • Expert estimates 80-90% of CX market is vulnerable to disruption over the next 2-3 years, with increasing commoditisation threatening legacy platforms through customer displacement and diversion of new spending
  • Market disruption coincides with CX budget ownership shifting away from Chief Experience Officers toward operations, finance and IT leaders, reflecting greater scrutiny of technology spending and declining C-suite prioritisation
  • Despite these pressures, Medallia and Qualtrics possess valuable customer data assets, though expert estimates clients utilise <50% of available platform capabilities, highlighting significant unrealised value
  • Medallia's restructuring, including debt reduction and USD 150m in fresh capital, represents a "night-and-day difference", enabling a shift from financial defensiveness toward meaningful product investment
  • Qualtrics' acquisition of Press Ganey strengthens its healthcare position, yielding potential expansion opportunities across insurance, public sector and other regulated industries
