原标题：Review|ASML 26Q3: Upgrades Lift Gross Margin; Capacity Expansion Awaits Further Clarity

#semicap

Substack · 2026-10-15

Summary

ASML’s Q3 gross margin and Q4 guidance both beat expectations. The company narrowed its 2026 revenue guidance to EUR 44–45b and raised its full-year gross margin guidance to around 56%, driven by continued strength in the upgrade business within Installed Base Management. The company stated that its plan to increase capacity by 30% in 2027, together with the further 30% increase in 2028 that had been under investigation, will still not fully meet customer demand, and it is now looking into expanding 2028 capacity beyond 30%. Customers have expressed interest in placing orders for 2029 and 2030, although ASML has not yet accepted those orders. We raise our 2026/27/28 EPS forecasts to EUR 42.5/61.4/76.9.

26Q3 Gross Margin Beat Driven by Upgrades

ASML’s Q3 revenue was EUR 11.66b, in line with market expectations, while gross margin and earnings significantly beat expectations; Q4 and full-year guidance were also above consensus. The company guided Q4 revenue at EUR 14.2–15.2b and narrowed its full-year 2026 revenue guidance from EUR 43–45b to EUR 44–45b, driven mainly by strong EUV demand and growth in the Installed Base Management business. Q3 net income also included an approximately EUR 0.5b valuation gain on ASML’s investment in Mistral AI, which should be distinguished from underlying operating performance.

ASML’s Q3 gross margin was 57.5%, above the market expectation of 55.7%. Q4 gross margin is guided at around 57%, slightly above the market expectation of 56.3%, and full-year gross margin guidance was raised from 54%–56% to around 56%. The gross margin beat was again driven by the Installed Base Management (IBM) business: benefiting from demand for EUV and DUV productivity upgrades, Q3 IBM revenue reached EUR 3.29b, around EUR 0.4b above company guidance. ASML expects IBM revenue to grow further to around EUR 3.7b in Q4, with full-year growth of around 50%, up from the more than 30% expected last quarter. Demand for upgrades that increase output within existing fabs remains strong, and ASML is actively developing new upgrade products to expand the addressable market within its installed base. In addition, ASML has completed value-based pricing negotiations with key customers; the price increases will be partially reflected in 2027 and fully reflected from 2028 onwards. Expansion costs could offset part of the gross margin benefit. Management will provide 2027 gross margin guidance at the January 2027 earnings call.

EUV: Strong Demand; Capacity Expansion Still Under Review

Subscribed

ASML is looking into expanding 2028 capacity beyond 30%; 2027 upside still being pursued. ASML maintained its target of shipping around 65 low-NA EUV units in 2026, and stated that its plan to increase capacity by 30% in 2027, together with the further 30% increase in 2028 that had been under investigation, will not fully meet customer demand. Although management did not provide new unit figures and noted that additional capacity is easier to realize in 2028, we maintain our assumption of 100/125 EUV units in 2027/28, including 93/115 low-NA units, above the roughly 85/110 low-NA units implied by the company’s original capacity assumptions. Management stated on the public earnings call that ZEISS optics are not currently a bottleneck for the planned expansion, and there is still room to shorten cycle times in the factory and improve utilization of the existing footprint. We therefore believe 2027 shipments could already exceed the company’s plan.

Down payments rise; customers express order interest through 2030. Non-current contract liabilities rose from EUR 3.57b to EUR 9.30b in 26Q3, an increase of around EUR 5.7b in a single quarter, mainly reflecting customer down payments for deliveries in 2027 and beyond; this also lifted 26Q3 free cash flow to EUR 11.35b. Management noted that order intake for 2028 continues to build and that customers also wish to place orders for 2029 and 2030. ASML has not yet accepted orders for 2029 and 2030, as it first needs to align demand with capacity. Interest comes from multiple customers, and new EUV customers continue to emerge in DRAM.

High-NA EUV production plans become clearer. Intel has exposed more than one million wafers using high-NA EUV, while Samsung and TSMC have announced plans to introduce it into high-volume manufacturing in 2028 and 2030, respectively. ASML and TSMC are jointly advancing a 12-inch photomask initiative targeting a pilot line by 2031 and full system readiness in 2033. High-NA EUV adopters and other major suppliers have expressed interest in joining. The larger mask can raise productivity for dual-mask exposures by more than 40%. We believe this initiative could support the use of high-NA EUV across more layers over the long term.

DUV: Immersion Shipments Recover; China and Memory Demand Remain Strong

Immersion DUV shipments recover; new tools could lift ASP. ASML shipped 40 Immersion DUV (ArFi) units in 26Q3, up approximately 74% from 23 units in 26Q2; Immersion’s share of system sales rose from 29% to 39%, or around EUR 3.3b. The company expects 2026 Immersion DUV shipments to be similar to 2025. Looking ahead to 2027, the company plans to increase Immersion DUV capacity by 30% and, from early 2027, raise the throughput of all versions of its critical Immersion DUV tools to 330 wafers per hour; pricing will scale with productivity, following the same model as EUV. We believe this will support DUV shipments, ASP, and gross margin.

China and memory demand support system sales growth. Memory system sales are expected to grow by around 75% in 2026, driven by customers’ capacity expansion and the increasing adoption of EUV on advanced DRAM nodes; memory revenue in the first three quarters reached EUR 10.3b, already exceeding the full-year 2025 level of EUR 8.4b. China accounted for 23% of net system sales in 26Q3, up significantly from 14% in 26Q2 in line with the Immersion DUV ramp; for the full year, the company continues to expect China to account for around 20% of total net sales, a different denominator from the quarterly system-sales figures. Management believes domestic demand in China for mainstream logic and memory is strong and will remain strong in 2027 and beyond.

Financial Forecasts and Valuation

We maintain our total EUV shipment estimates for 2026/27/28 at 70/100/125 units, respectively; in addition, because we raised our Installed Base Management revenue forecasts and incorporated the benefit of price increases phasing in from 2027, we also upgraded our expectations for gross margin. Our current EPS forecasts for 2026/27/28 are EUR 42.5/61.4/76.9, respectively, corresponding to P/Es of 36.0x/24.9x/19.9x based on the European share price on October 14.
