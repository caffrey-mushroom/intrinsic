SUMMARY

Overview

The discussion assessed Reflection AI’s Beam platform for AI development in mining, energy, natural resources, and other regulated sectors. The expert oversees technology adoption across multinational operations and recently began using Beam to develop internal AI applications and manage AI infrastructure.

Beam is primarily used for coding, reasoning, application customization, and modernization of legacy systems. The organization has integrated it with platforms including IBM Maximo, Oracle Finance, Micromine, MineSense, Energy Exemplar, GitLab, Baseten, and AWS.

Key Insights

The expert considers Beam particularly well suited to regulated industries because it can be self-hosted and controlled within private infrastructure. Beam was viewed as a better fit than competing models for the organization’s expanding mining use cases, including coal, nickel, bauxite, and copper. Its main advantages are privacy, security, customization, interoperability, and relatively low ownership costs. The expert reported occasional latency and integration issues but said these could generally be resolved through provider support. Interest in Beam appears to be increasing among the expert’s professional network, supported by early perceived benefits. Asimov was described as potentially valuable for understanding large codebases and organizational data, but access remains highly restricted. The organization is still awaiting approval and has not deployed it.
