原标题：PE Sector Update: Enterprise AI Safety & Governance - Market breakdown, Key players & Outlook

#security #agents #software

Third Bridge · 2026-11-18

核心摘要

  1. 前CalypsoAI首席商务官兼CFO将AI安全分为模型安全、AI应用与智能体安全、终端用户安全三大类，其中模型安全正被基础模型厂商自身吸收，独立厂商难以建立规模业务。
  2. 智能体安全正成为独立于应用安全的新品类，因为智能体行为动态、接近人类，而应用是静态的；数据泄露、提示注入、奖励投机（reward hacking）仍是主要风险。
  3. 专家估计2026年独立及被收购的智能体安全方案合计收入接近10亿美元，2027年可能增加数十亿美元；Gartner预测2027年和2028年AI安全支出分别为48亿和77亿美元，专家认为若只算智能体安全则偏乐观。
  4. 以OpenAI和Anthropic 2026年合计约1500亿美元收入估算，10亿美元的智能体安全支出不到其1%，增长空间较大。
  5. 整体安全预算目前基本持平或小幅增长，长期可能每年增长10–15%，且大量智能体安全支出可能计入业务部门成本，类似员工成本之外额外15–20%的附加费用。
  6. 欧盟AI法案有两年宽限期，专家预计2027年将是合规驱动AI安全支出的关键一年。
  7. 企业招标中最看重产品完整性、总拥有成本、延迟、误报率和召回率；Lasso等仅需CPU的护栏方案相比依赖GPU的方案有明显成本和定价优势，尤其适合后台类场景。
  8. 并购方面，Palo Alto于2025年7月收购Protect AI，F5于2025年9月收购CalypsoAI，SentinelOne于2025年9月收购Prompt Security；Workday、Mastercard、Accenture等非安全公司也在入场。
  9. HiddenLayer于2026年9月完成1亿美元B轮融资并扩展到智能体运行时安全，专家认为其已落后；Noma、Straiker试图覆盖全部品类，各家最终都需整合为单一平台。
  10. 最大风险在于智能体普及速度不及预期，以及基础模型厂商与独立安全厂商之间的持续博弈。

Key Insights

  • Model security is being absorbed by foundational model providers themselves, making it commercially unviable for independent vendors to build standalone businesses there. Agent security is emerging as a distinct category from application security, requiring fundamentally different approaches as agents behave dynamically such as humans, unlike static applications
  • Every platform will ultimately need to cover all of it, at least for now while the market is still nascent. They are absolutely behind the curve right now
  • Non-security acquirers such as Workday, Mastercard and Accenture are entering AI security M&A market, expanding the buyer pool well beyond traditional cybersecurity platform vendors
  • EU AI Act's two-year grace period makes 2027 a pivotal year for compliance-driven AI security spending, potentially becoming one of the largest regulatory demand catalysts
  • CPU-only guardrail architectures, such as those from Lasso, offer meaningful cost and pricing advantages over GPU-dependent solutions, particularly for back-office and non-customer-facing enterprise use cases
