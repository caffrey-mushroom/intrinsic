原标题：Cohesity – Enterprise Cyber Resilience, Veritas NetBackup Integration & Cloud ARR

#security #software

Third Bridge · 2026-11-18

核心摘要

  1. 前Cohesity美洲渠道副总裁表示，Cohesity于2024年2月宣布收购Veritas NetBackup业务、12月完成交割，整合初期两套团队并行，报价、MDF等系统在交割后一年多仍未打通，运营层面在头一两年较为混乱。
  2. Veritas约占合并后终端客户基础的70%，支持近1000种应用，而Cohesity DataProtect仅支持约300-400种；Veritas有24个代码版本、Cohesity仅个位数，专家认为把NetBackup代码完全并入DataProtect几乎不可能，更现实的是逐步淘汰。
  3. 最有效的留存手段是在Veritas续约时免费赠送相当于续约金额约25%-30%的DataProtect许可（如100万美元续约送25万美元），既能阻挡竞争对手，又让客户熟悉新平台；专家认为这一阶段客户流失不多。
  4. 收购后Rubrik对NetBackup客户最为激进，Commvault紧随其后（同样能支持老旧定制应用和大型数据库），Veeam较少参与；竞争对手常通过散布NetBackup将被淘汰的FUD和低配报价切入，之后再大幅提价。
  5. 专家估计约25%-40%的NetBackup客户不太可能迁移至DataProtect，主要原因是关键遗留应用尚不被支持或内部缺乏资源，大银行等将继续运行Veritas；同时维护两套产品意味着双倍研发成本。
  6. 未来12-24个月大量NetBackup续约到期，最大风险是公司将现场销售团队从续约中抽离以专注新业务，由内部团队远程维护关键企业关系，而Veritas以往每年10%的续约涨价也令客户不满。
  7. Cohesity的Gaia AI能力可挖掘备份数据供大模型使用，有报告称未来约30%的价值将来自挖掘备份数据，这是其核心差异化；行业已全面转向软件订阅，参考架构硬件价格在过去1.5年上涨300%-400%，Cohesity约95%通过合作伙伴销售，在EMEA、亚太和拉美仍有扩张空间。

Agenda

  • Cohesity's competitive positioning against Rubrik (NYSE: RBRK) and Commvault (NASDAQ: CVLT)
  • Integration progress, operational synergies and customer retention dynamics following the Veritas-NetBackup business combination
  • Commercial adoption momentum for AI-driven products and services
  • Subscription ARR growth and enterprise renewal rates across Fortune 500 accounts

Key Insights

  • Cohesity's "Gaia" AI capabilities and ability to mine backup data for large language models represent a key differentiator, with reports suggesting 30% of future value will come from mining this data
  • Expert believes fully merging NetBackup code into DataProtect is essentially impossible given 24 versions of Veritas code vs Cohesity’s single-digit versions, making a gradual phase-out more realistic
  • c25-40% of NetBackup customers are unlikely to migrate to DataProtect, primarily due to critical legacy applications DataProtect does not yet support or internal resource constraints
  • Removing field sales teams from renewal responsibilities to focus on new business created a vulnerability by leaving inside teams to manage critical enterprise relationships remotely. Rubrik was the most aggressive competitor targeting Veritas-NetBackup customers post-acquisition
  • Cohesity's strongest retention tactic was bundling free DataProtect licences worth c25% of Veritas renewal value, simultaneously blocking competitors while familiarising customers with the newer platform
