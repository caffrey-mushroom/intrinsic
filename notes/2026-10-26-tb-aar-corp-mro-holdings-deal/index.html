Companies: AIR US, LUV US, DAL US

Bill Tiffany (BT) CEO at Avix Aero LLC

Agenda

  • Follow-on investment outcomes for AAR (NYSE: AIR) after MROH (MRO Holdings) investment stake
  • Expanded network opportunities globally
  • Core distribution exposure and in-house maintenance dynamics
  • Leverage increase and long-term portfolio risk

Key Insights

  • AAR and MROH now control c50-60% of North American heavy airframe maintenance capacity, making them a dominant force comparable to Lufthansa Technik in Europe
  • AAR's USD 75m synergy target for 2031 is likely very conservative. Expert believes there will be another zero on revenue and margin synergies. Cost synergies are almost secondary to the growth opportunity
  • Component repair represents massive wallet share opportunity. Expert's 20-30% revenue uplift estimate is likely conservative, driven by an attractive complementary product suite. "There will be a large sucking sound leaving their competitor MRO facilities in the US and Canada and streaming towards these new facilities that they invest in"
  • EBITDA margins should be accretive at onset, with a somewhat natural path towards c20% overall. Debt leverage increase to c5x EBITDA in full-year 2026 is definitely concerning, but expert believes this is a once-in-a-generation opportunity to invest and build capabilities across multiple hangars in LATAM
