Companies: EQIX US, AMZN US, DLR US, META US, HD US, CM CA

Nader Balata (NB) Director, Product Operations at Docebo Inc

Dean Tsopanides (DT) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Trends and developments impacting Equinix (NASDAQ: EQIX) and the broader data centre operating environment
  • Proliferating state and municipal moratoria, raising entitlement risk – how do Equinix's 50-60MW metro builds face less scrutiny than gigawatt campuses?
  • Enterprise AI inference moving from pilots to deployment, lifting deal sizes towards 1-3MW and record pre-sales
  • Mid-to-long-term outlook

Key Insights

  • Equinix's "interconnection ecosystem flywheel" is its core moat – ecosystem density plus neutrality creates compounding network effects that competitors structurally cannot replicate without alienating cloud customers
  • Expert asserts that <20% of Equinix's existing data centres can be retrofitted for liquid cooling without unviable economics, creating a significant constraint as AI workloads demand higher power densities
  • Increasing cabinet power density accounts for c50% of MRR-per-cabinet gains, improved attach rates contribute c40%, while pure pricing increases contribute only c10%
  • Regarding recent neocloud fears and the potential for those to bleed over into colocation and broader data centre growth dynamics, expert notes CoreWeave has "no ability to actually follow through on their mandates […] we found out that CoreWeave was woefully behind […] Equinix does not play these high-stakes, high-debt games"
  • Expert believes that, relative to peers, current CEO Adaire Fox-Martin is underperforming, noting how Equinix had "the slowest acceleration in terms of revenue growth" despite being the dominant player in a booming data centre market
