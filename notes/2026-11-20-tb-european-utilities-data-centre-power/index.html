原标题：European Power Utilities – Who Is Best-positioned for Data Centre Investments?

#power #datacenter

Third Bridge · 2026-11-20

核心摘要

  1. 前Iberdrola全球首席投资官认为数据中心需求正在改变公用事业的发电和电网投资规划，西班牙等多年需求停滞的国家已开始恢复增长，但这种需求是离散式而非线性的。
  2. 西班牙数据中心申请容量超过13GW，但专家认为到2030年最乐观情况下实际装机仅2-3GW，大部分项目不会落地；各公用事业的潜在闲置发电站点合计即可达到约2GW。
  3. 西班牙CNMC规定新数据中心的供电须来自其商业运营前36个月内（原为18个月）新建的发电容量，且80%电量需按小时来自可再生能源（含储能），这实际上排除了以存量资产签订基荷PPA的方式。
  4. 由于数据中心信用资质好、承诺期长且会通过招标让多家竞争，超大规模云厂商的基荷PPA价格往往低于市场价，而只有拥有多元化发电组合的一体化公用事业才能提供基荷供电。
  5. 西班牙10年期光伏按发电量PPA可低于30欧元/MWh，风电低于50欧元/MWh，长期平价基荷PPA超过70欧元/MWh；欧洲燃气价格约为美国的10倍，燃气发电供能在西班牙不可行。
  6. 表后定制方案可为数据中心节省约20欧元/MWh过网费，例如基荷70欧元加20欧元过网费总成本90欧元，而表后PPA为70-80欧元，客户总成本更低而供应商利润更高，专家要求的超额回报约300bps，高于常规的100-200bps。
  7. RWE以约100万欧元/MW出售电网接入点，相当于数据中心1000万-1200万欧元/MW投资的约10%，专家认为这是短期稀缺溢价，长期将收敛至约20%的水平。
  8. 专家认为一体化公用事业最具优势，美国（如NextEra）、西班牙（Iberdrola居首）和北欧（水电、地热、气候凉爽）是最佳地区，但表后项目增多可能引发监管要求其分摊电网费用。

Agenda

  • Sizing up the data centre opportunity across southern Europe for grids, power generation and trading amid Spain’s CNMC (Comisión Nacional de los Mercados y la Competencia; National Commission on Markets and Competition) regulations
  • Sustainability of PPA (power purchase agreement) and site sale premiums
  • Positioning of key utilities in Europe to gain from data centre investment

Key Insights

  • Spain's realistic data centre capacity by 2030 is only 2-3GW, despite 13GW in applications filed – most projects will never materialise due to regulatory and market constraints
  • Spain's CNMC regulation now requires data centre power supply to come from generation capacity built within 36 months of commercial operation, effectively eliminating existing-asset baseload PPAs as an option
  • Integrated utilities with diversified generation portfolios are far better positioned than independent power producers for data centre PPAs, as baseload supply requires owning actual generation assets, not just trading contracts
  • Surprisingly, hyperscalers often receive discounts below market baseload PPA rates when supply is competitive – their strong credit quality becomes a negotiating weapon rather than a premium-justifying factor
  • RWE's cEUR 1m per megawatt grid connection sale price reflects short-term scarcity premiums that are fundamentally unsustainable, eventually converging towards c20% of that value long-term
