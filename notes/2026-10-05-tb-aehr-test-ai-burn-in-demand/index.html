Agenda

  • Aehr Test Systems' (NASDAQ: AEHR) current demand environment and key growth drivers
  • Wafer-level burn-in adoption across AI processors, customer qualification dynamics and barriers to broader penetration
  • Growth opportunities across Sonoma package-level burn-in, Fox WaferPak wafer-level solutions, silicon photonics and power semiconductors
  • Mid-to-long-term outlook

Key Insights

  • Winning roughly two additional AI customers comparable to the lead programme could support c5x-plus equipment revenue, with broader adoption potentially driving c10x system expansion
  • Wafer-level burn-in can screen a cUSD 800-1,000 defective die before it destroys a cUSD 15,000-20,000 AI package, creating potential 10-100x savings
  • Fox XP systems sell for cUSD 5m-6m, with c4-6-month lead times, making another follow-on order by year-end 2026 or early 2027 a key production-ramp proof point
  • WaferPaks likely last c5,000-10,000 touchdowns and cost cUSD 60,000-300,000, creating a potentially higher-margin recurring revenue stream from installed systems
  • If adoption is proven, silicon photonics demand could scale from low-single-digit systems to dozens of Fox XP units within c12-18 months, creating a meaningful secondary growth driver
