Agenda

  • Trends and developments impacting WhiteFiber (NASDAQ: WYFI) and the broader AI infrastructure operating environment
  • Capacity expansion expectations, factoring in constraints and capital challenges
  • Hyperscaler demand for specialised high-density GPU hosting expectations
  • Mid-to-long-term growth outlook

Key Insights

  • WhiteFiber's software stack was underdeveloped and heavily reliant on external consultants who held "the keys to the kingdom," creating operational fragility and more outages than necessary for a scaling neocloud
  • Regarding neocloud sustainability, breakeven on 3-5-year contracts typically arrives around year four or five, after which revenue becomes "pure profit". However, reaching that milestone is far from guaranteed
  • Expert believes the neocloud model alone is insufficient for strong investor returns, estimating only a 2x ROI vs software-driven businesses that can scale at 10-20x multiples
  • GPU depreciation is rapid and underappreciated – Hopper H100s already yield under USD 2 per GPU hour, while newer architectures such as GB300 and Vera Rubin make older fleets increasingly uncompetitive for large models
  • Regarding the broader neocloud risk, large AI companies such as Google and xAI are building proprietary chips and in-house data centres, threatening to eliminate demand from neocloud providers entirely over time
