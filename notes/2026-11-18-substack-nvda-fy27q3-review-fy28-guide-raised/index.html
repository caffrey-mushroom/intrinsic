原标题：Review|NVDA FY27Q3: FY2028 Growth Guidance Raised; Jensen Huang’s Potential Return Remains Key

#semis #ai-demand

Substack · 2026-11-18

核心摘要

  1. 英伟达FY27Q3营收1158亿美元，环比增长20.4%，FY27Q4指引1300亿美元±3%，FY2028营收增长指引从70%上调至约75%。
  2. 董事会在电话会开头表示无意让黄仁勋复任CEO，已启动涵盖内外部候选人的正式CEO遴选，并设立国家安全与出口管制委员会。
  3. 数据中心收入1080亿美元（环比+21%），其中超大规模客户600亿美元（环比+23%），云厂商积压订单环比增加5000亿美元至2.5万亿美元。
  4. FY27Q3非GAAP毛利率74.9%，Q4因履行旧价订单降至71%-72%，预计FY2028随新定价生效回升至72%-73%；中国专供Hopper收入将从24亿美元降至零。
  5. Vera Rubin首个量产季度贡献近200亿美元收入，推理每瓦产出为GB300的10倍，CPO交换机已量产，Rubin Ultra（2027）和Feynman（2028）按计划推进。
  6. 自5月以来token价格下降近60%，推动OpenRouter周用量同比增长35倍（智能体约占80%），Hopper多年续约价格最高达原价2倍，B300租金上涨超60%。
  7. FY27Q3股东回报280亿美元（回购220亿、分红60亿），新增1500亿美元回购授权，剩余2270亿美元计划在FY2028底前用完，自Q4起100%超额FCF返还股东。
  8. FUNDA预测FY2028 EPS为17.24美元、对应约9倍远期市盈率，但认为缺少黄仁勋的创始人模式将削弱长期产品路线和供应链整合优势，而现董事会下其回归概率较低。

Since our Preview report, NVIDIA has seen several important developments. Jensen Huang resigned, and the Board stated at the start of the earnings call that it does not intend to reinstate him as CEO. The Board has begun a formal CEO search covering internal and external candidates and established a National Security and Export Control Committee. Near-term results exceeded expectations: FY27Q3 revenue was $115.8b (+20.4% QoQ), and FY27Q4 revenue guidance was $130b ± 3%. FY2028 revenue growth guidance was also raised from 70% to approximately 75%. Kress took the unusual step of reassuring employees during the call and acknowledged internal disagreements, highlighting the challenge of retaining key talent. We continue to believe that whether Jensen Huang returns is an important factor in NVIDIA’s future competitiveness. Without Founder Mode, NVIDIA may find it harder over the long term to sustain the competitiveness of its product roadmap and its supply-chain ecosystem integration advantages.

Earnings Highlights

  • Data Center Momentum and Gross Margin Trough Confirmed: FY27Q3 Data Center revenue was $108b (+21% QoQ), including Hyperscale revenue of $60b (+23% QoQ); cloud providers’ backlog rose $500b QoQ to $2.5t. FY27Q4 guidance includes an additional week, while China-specific Hopper revenue is expected to fall to zero (from $2.4b in FY27Q3). FY27Q3 Non-GAAP gross margin was 74.9%. FY27Q4 gross margin guidance fell to 71%–72% due to costs associated with fulfilling legacy orders (Non-GAAP OpEx of approximately $10.7b). Gross margin is expected to recover to 72%–73% in FY2028 as new pricing takes effect.
  • Product Progress and Rising GPU Rental Prices: Vera Rubin contributed nearly $20b in revenue in its first quarter of production shipments, with inference output per watt reaching 10x that of GB300. CPO switches are in volume production, while Rubin Ultra (2027) and Feynman (2028) remain on schedule. Since May, Token prices have fallen nearly 60%, driving a 35x YoY increase in OpenRouter’s weekly usage (Agentic workloads account for approximately 80%). This increase has lifted compute utilization and pushed GPU rental prices higher. Multi-year renewal prices for Hopper GPUs have reached up to 2x their original level, while B300 rental prices have risen more than 60% since launch. Agentic demand is also expected to more than double FY2028 CPU revenue.

Share Repurchases

Total shareholder returns in FY27Q3 reached $28b ($22b in share repurchases and $6b in dividends), with a YTD FCF return ratio of 65%. The Board approved a record $150b increase in buyback authorization this quarter. Remaining authorization at quarter-end was $227b, which management plans to use in full by the end of FY2028. Starting in FY27Q4, 100% of excess FCF, after strategic uses, will be returned to shareholders.

Financial Forecasts

Subscribed

We now forecast NVIDIA FY2028 EPS at $17.24, implying a Forward P/E of 9x. We continue to believe NVIDIA needs Jensen Huang to remain competitive across subsequent product generations, for the reasons discussed in our previous report. Under the current Board, the likelihood of Jensen Huang returning to lead NVIDIA is relatively low. Any further developments warrant attention.

Sources: Company documents, FUNDA estimates
