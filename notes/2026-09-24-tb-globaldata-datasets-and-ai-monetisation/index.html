Agenda

  • GlobalData's (LON: DATA) win rates and points of differentiation when looking to win customers, highlighting data quality
  • AI product demands, platform integration and user monetisation
  • Pricing, discounts and evolving use cases for data providers

Key Insights

  • While GlobalData has a strong breadth of data assets, its market proximity and customer activation hold back full value realisation
  • Only the largest 25% of customers get fully differentiated data, typically leveraging GlobalData's breadth, while the middle 50% can be hit-or-miss. Outside of this, service is more of a commodity sale, presenting significant churn risk
  • Further AI embedding can improve usability. However, expert's 12-18-month outlook is cautious, expecting low-single-digit growth as GlobalData battles cultural, structural and macroeconomic changes
  • Beyond 18 months, high-single-digit growth is possible as AI functionality materially impacts customer wins and value renewal rates increase by a couple of percentage points. Volume renewal is expected to be flat
  • Holding margins for the next 12-18 months would enable faster change and increased mid-term growth. However, expectations are for slow margin expansion towards 30% in non-healthcare for the coming 3-4 years, delaying restructuring benefits
