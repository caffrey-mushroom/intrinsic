SUMMARY

Overview

The expert discussed Sinch and the broader communications-platform-as-a-service (CPaaS) market, particularly in Latin America. Businesses use SMS heavily for authentication and service notifications, while WhatsApp is a preferred channel for customer engagement and conversion. Rising messaging charges are pushing companies to reassess channel mix and seek alternatives such as RCS, company apps, and website chatbots.

Key Insights

WhatsApp price increases are a major market disruption. The expert expects businesses to pass costs to customers where possible, reduce usage, or shift interactions to other channels. WhatsApp may remain valuable for marketing because of its conversion performance. Passing through carrier costs is currently the safest CPaaS assumption. Providers have become less willing to absorb fee increases after several years of margin pressure. Contract terms increasingly support price adjustments. Enterprise AI is constrained more by integration than by the agents themselves. Complex connections to internal systems make large-company deployments difficult. The expert expects specialist providers to remain relevant for complex enterprise needs, while Meta may be better suited to simpler, high-volume use cases. Sinch faces pressure in AI and voice. The expert sees Twilio as better positioned in voice-enabled services today. Sinch’s enterprise relationships and carrier connections remain advantages, but it needs to develop additional offerings. Large-account relationships support customer retention. Switching providers can be difficult because of integrations and established carrier relationships. The expert said Sinch has strengthened its ties with major financial institutions.
