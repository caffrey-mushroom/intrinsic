Agenda

  • Demand trends and customer requirements in Australia and New Zealand cold chain logistics
  • Retailer in-housing activity and unit economic dynamics
  • Market consolidation and competitive landscape

Key Insights

  • Americold and Lineage hold combined 60%-plus market share, with NewCold as a growing competitor, given its emerging facilities. Expert thinks Australia and New Zealand's cold chain logistics market is 40-45% cold storage and 40-50% refrigerated transport, with <10% in last-mile delivery
  • Coles and Woolworths' cold storage in-housing activity will reduce third-party logistics volumes, with Americold and Lineage possibly relegated to handling seasonal demand spikes. Expert believes retailers will take around six months to in-house cold storage, after which third-party logistics will be impacted
  • Labour represents c50% of cost per pallet for traditional cold chain operators, with electricity being second-largest cost driver, creating significant margin pressure ahead. NewCold's fully automated facilities in Melbourne and Sydney handle only full pallet in, full pallet out operations, creating a distinct and lower-cost business model
  • Sydney's development application approval process takes more than one year vs around three months in Melbourne or Brisbane – any new cold storage capacity announced today wouldn't be operational for c2.5 years
