Agenda

  • Intrum Spain's (STO: INTRUM) potential success from capital-light strategy pivot and impacts on the wider market
  • NPL (non-performing loan) outlook, portfolio attractiveness and ability to drive greater IRRs through AI
  • Deleveraging ability, anticipating potential timelines and levers to cut leverage to 3x net debt and EBITDA by 2030
  • Competitive landscape, focusing on potentially better- and worse-placed players vs Intrum

Key Insights

  • Expert thinks Cerberus gets disproportionate benefits from co-investment deals, with access to pan-European deal flow and without operational burdens from running a servicing platform. Intrum should either open to several investors or renegotiate with Cerberus
  • Mismatch between NPL pipeline growth and run-off in Spain can’t be offset by 10x NPL market shrinkage from post-2008 peaks and bank delinquencies at historic lows, leading to excess capacity and fierce competition, with some firms winning business at expense of profits
  • Tailwinds are appearing in consumer finance and BNPL, with weaker credit quality on the rise, which could signal a market turn. However, read-through to banks will take longer due to better credit. Intrum lacks systems to service smaller ticket volumes efficiently, with AI offering a pathway to capture BNPL and other smaller-ticket volumes
  • Consolidation in Spain is accelerating, with c50% of current market players expected to disappear through mergers or business failure relatively soon
