原标题：Sony & TSMC – Imaging Sensor JV & Takeaways From JASM

#semis

Third Bridge · 2026-09-23

Analyst-led • 8 Sep 2026

Companies: 6758 JP, 2330 TW, 7203 JP

Yuan Kuei Huang (YKH) Senior Data Scientist at Texas Instruments Inc

Michelle Lee (ML) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Synergies from the new Sony (TYO: 6758) and Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co (TSMC; TWSE: 2330) JV based on JASM (Japan Advanced Semiconductor Manufacturing) experience
  • Analysing supply chain stability and cluster effects in Kumamoto
  • Possibility of future strategic partnership with physical AI customers

Key Insights

  • On the new TSMC-Sony CMOS image sensor JV, c70% of manufacturing experience from JASM can be transferred, but the unknown CMOS image sensor-specific process flow requires significant additional exploration and system modification
  • Setting up a semiconductor fab in Japan costs c35% more than that in Taiwan, largely due to vendor restrictions, lack of resource networks and mandatory prioritisation of local Japanese suppliers. Expert believes government subsidies encourage these localising operations, requiring additional time and operational adjustments
  • As the Kyushu domain relies on older N28 or N22 technologies, gross profits are lower. However, as transportation between Japan and Taiwan normally impacts COGS, expert estimates costs will be reduced by c30% due to operations being in the same place
  • TSMC recovered from the 2024 Kumamoto earthquake within days, while Sony took nearly a month, revealing a stark operational culture gap between the two partners
