Good afternoon. QQQs -2.9% opened -1.6% and leaked lower all day, closing just off the lows, as the AI infra complex got liquidated across the board with more of the same buyer’s strike we’ve seen over the last couple of weeks.

What caused the sell off today?

Some of it due to META’s -7% equity raise fears putting financing the infra trade back in focus and causing the stock to round trip its 6% pop; semicap moved lower on LRCX’s -12% guide; NOK -15% closed at lows after its mixed print despite a sizeable EBIT beat; and a slew of what seems like new hacking news every hour didn’t help sentiment across the space. Nothing was spared in semi land with most stocks down 5%+.

Sentiment continues be as low I’ve seen it all year although positioning still somewhat elevated: GS prime has total semi & semi equip US net exposure at 10%, down from highs of 14%, but still well above last 5 year average. Some more detail from GS Prime here on X. Hearing chatter MS has average TMT fund ~-5%+ MTD and GS -7-8% MTD. Lots of pain out there.

Some great discussion in TMTB Slack here on the state of the AI trade. Nobody’s really doubting token demand and adoption curve but debate continues to be around timing and whether it monetizes fast enough to pay for the buildout.

Moving on…

Software -9bps held in as AI losers got squeezed again (MNDY +6.7%; HUBS +7%; ADBE +6%; INTU +5.7%) and IT services which gets lumped in the same group caught a bid (ACN +7.7%; CTSH +6%). In internet, hypers were dragged down by META’s funding concerns with the read-through being AMZN -4.5% might have to raise capital too (race to who gets there first according to META’s 14C). SPOT +11% threaded the needle on a good print where GM bear fears were alleviated and ads on track to accelerate to dd growth in Q4, which is what bulls wanted to see. Bull case sounds a lot cleaner leaving the print and as we touched on yesterday, a good print was likely to met with buying as investors look for AI-safe havens and NFLX still in “do not touch” category among many investors.

Anthropic

Earlier, we gave our best first shot at modeling Anthropic’s #s given the very limited information we have before the S-1 gets filed (just going off press reports over the last week). What I took away from the exercise was a lot of big swing factors in what moves the #s and a very wide range of outcomes depending on the assumptions you tweak.

In terms of the ARR number for Dec, got a bit of pushback on my $86B # (shifting share recapture up a bit gets us to $88B). Most we talked to today said they are in the $90B+ range, which makes sense as I can get to $90-$92B pretty easily, but getting above $95B requires more aggressive assumptions.

In macro land, bonds got the safe haven bid all day: the 10Y -18bps to ~4.95%, back below 5% for the first time since 9/23 in what the biggest one-day drop in more than two years (Bessent’s 8D Chess as Akh put it). Odds of a 10/28 hike went to ~0% from ~16% yesterday and ~41% on 10/14, and odds of no more hikes this year rose to ~28% from ~10% (a December hike still ~72% priced, with 2027 hike bets fading too). WTI +2.4% to $99.47 after briefly trading above $100, as the NYT reported the US and Israel plan to strike Iran again after the midterms and Saudi Arabia reported attacks on Dammam airport and an Aramco field. BTC -4% to ~$81K.

Not a lot to call out on company specific news given broad nature of sell off, but let’s get to the ~~good~~ stuff…
