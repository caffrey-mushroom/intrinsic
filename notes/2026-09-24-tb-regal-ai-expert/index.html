Analyst: Could you provide a use case for the journey builder segment?

Expert: Journey builder is you're an insurance company, but you want to treat people who are using your auto insurance vs your home insurance vs your travel insurance very differently. You have in a database all of the people's information and you have all that information segmented in a CSV file somewhere. You can create three separate journeys for those people. When they come in, you can set up individualised journeys for each of them so that when a travel insurance customer dials into your contact centre for support, they don't have to press one for travel insurance. They automatically go through the travel insurance flow because the contact centre recognises them as a travel insurance customer and they automatically get the proper information and journey built for them. They get taken through a specific flow and they only get information that is relevant for them. They automatically get routed to the right type of customer service agent for travel insurance. It minimises wastage in terms of being routed to the wrong person, misuse of resources, etc. It also allows the contact centre to better resource more accordingly because you have a better understanding of what volume looks like in each of your segments. You know exactly how many travel insurance agents you need vs auto insurance vs home insurance. It allows you to provide a better customer experience to those specific customers without them feeling like they have to do all of the work to get the right type of service that they need.

Analyst: Could you provide a use case for the AI agents?

Expert: This is a little bit more standard, but basically, you can use very human-like AI agents to handle repetitive, sort of low touch ask. Things like scheduling, appointment reminders, lead qualification, basic tasks. The AI agents can be set up via voice, SMS or live chat on the website and they can take action within Regal. They can shift appointment times. They can append records. They can change information on lead record types. They can actually take actions on the back end and customers can engage with them. They can also work alongside human agents. You could have an AI agent and a human agent sort of working together. The human agent can be asking the more difficult questions while the AI agent is updating the record in the background, so that, let's say, for insurance, you can't use an AI agent to sell insurance because they have to be licensed in a specific geography in order to sell insurance on the phone. In this use case, an AI agent can do all of the back-end work of appending the notes and doing all of the record-keeping so that the licensed insurance agent can have twice as many conversations in the same day. It can be an efficiency play as well.
