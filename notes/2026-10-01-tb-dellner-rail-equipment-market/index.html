#industrials #transport

Third Bridge · 2026-10-01

Analyst-led • 24 Sep 2026

Companies: WAB US, ALO FR, SIE DE, KBX DE

Raj Gupta (RG) Independent Consultant.

Georgia Beattie (GB) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Dellner's (NYSE: WAB) global footprint and core product portfolio across passenger and freight markets
  • Regional volume outlook, end market demand trends and market share dynamics
  • OEM build fluctuations vs high-margin aftermarket stability and pricing power
  • M&A integration and strategic differentiation through digital and bundled solutions

Key Insights

  • Service and overhaul contracts yield 45-50% operating margins – EBITDA 20-30%-plus – vs 15-20% on OEM sales, making long-term agreements such as Dellner's 20-30-year contracts with Alstom/Siemens the main value driver
  • Wabtec is using C-suite relationships to bundle Dellner's couplers with locomotives, brakes and digital tools, demonstrated by a recent USD 50m-plus order with Kawasaki
  • Moving to European digital electrical couplers is the critical shift. Lagging behind key rivals Voith – tech lead – or Knorr-Bremse – strong OEM ties – poses a major threat
  • Expert expects Wabtec to pause M&A for 2-3 years to consolidate Europe and India plants to boost margins, before likely targeting North American freight assets
  • Regulatory-driven safety requirements boost demand for quality couplers, with derailment incidents in North America intensifying scrutiny on coupler design quality and reliability. However, entering Asian markets requires local plants due to strict domestic content mandates
