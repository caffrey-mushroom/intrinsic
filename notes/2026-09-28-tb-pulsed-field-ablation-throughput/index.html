Agenda

Key Insights

  • Expert views Boston Scientific's core PFA as structural. The company bought Farapulse as a startup, and it doesn't inherently have the R&D capabilities to create its own Farapulse system, nor to iterate on it. Expert is unsure how Boston Scientific will retain its market share unless it acquires another startup
  • Abbott's TactiFlex Duo entering the focal catheter space directly challenges Affera's only meaningful differentiation, potentially reshaping the complex-redo segment where single-shot systems fall short
  • Key opinion leader's concomitant PFA or left atrial appendage occlusion procedures dropped to 20-25% in 2026 from 30-40% of cases in 2025, with further decline expected as CHAMPION trial results disappointed and reimbursement fell 26%
  • Despite ambulatory surgery centre billing codes going live January 2026, no Florida sites have completed an outpatient atrial fibrillation ablation. Regulatory approvals, site visits and physician incentive misalignment remain significant barriers nine months later
