核心摘要

  1. AlphaSense前AI解决方案团队高管表示，Enterprise Intelligence最初是仅能检索客户内部文档的附加SKU（标价约15万美元、按合同金额33%计入），一年内增长超过300%，超过三分之二的客户上传了逾60万份内部文档。
  2. 单席位标价约2.5万美元，起初守在约1.8万美元，随着Hebbia、Rogo和Glean进入市场被压至1.6万美元，大型新客户偶有1.2万甚至9000美元；客户成功团队在续约上实现约97%的续约率且折扣较少。
  3. Hebbia和Rogo曾有6–9个月的高速增长，但因过度承诺、从演示到投产困难而失去势头，部分客户在AlphaSense扩展功能后回流，专家认为这类新进入者可能是最大输家。
  4. 2027年客户的ROI逻辑将是“减人或减平台”，真正的降本机会在于削减Bloomberg、FactSet、CapIQ终端，但从既有平台迁出数据技术难度大，传统终端推出AI功能后尚未明显丢失份额。
  5. 面对客户自建AI，AlphaSense将话术从“买而非建”转为“既买也建”，向客户的数据科学团队提供内容与检索能力；Agent API以Java代码形式返回GenSearch答案，需逐一获得券商研究授权，可用于GenSearch的券商数量已接近Boolean检索。
  6. 客户普遍要求MCP原始数据流，此前管理层担心沦为数据管道而拒绝，专家推测后来推出MCP是市场所迫，且可能对券商研究等内容做了限制。
  7. AlphaSense采用积分而非token计价并自行补贴推理成本，标准搜索约10积分、深度思考25–30积分、深度研究最多100积分，合同保留转向token计价的权利。
  8. API与代理AI正成为ARR增长的重要来源，贡献占比正从约10%向30%目标推进，但毛利率更低；积分采用慢于预期，原因是客户自建方案迟迟未能投产。

SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, a former international sales executive at AlphaSense, discussed how the company’s enterprise intelligence product has developed from a paid add-on for searching clients’ internal documents into a broader AI offering. The conversation covered pricing, competition, customer adoption, data licensing, APIs and MCPs, and the potential contribution of AI usage to recurring revenue growth.

Key Insights

Enterprise intelligence adoption grew rapidly: the expert said more than two-thirds of clients had uploaded internal documents, with over 600,000 documents on the platform. AlphaSense’s strongest near-term opportunity may be helping customers reduce overlapping software and financial research terminals. However, moving data out of incumbent platforms and consolidating workflows remains technically difficult. The expert believes newer AI competitors have lost momentum after some customers encountered delays moving products from demonstrations into production. AlphaSense has sought to compete by emphasizing trusted, deployable workflows and by complementing customers’ internally built AI tools. APIs deliver AlphaSense-generated search answers into customer applications; MCPs were described as a route to providing data more directly. The expert was not involved in MCP implementation and could not confirm its precise scope or licensing controls. The expert views APIs and agentic AI as a growing source of ARR, but says this revenue is lower margin. Adoption of paid credits was slower than hoped because customer-built solutions took time to reach production.

Competitive Landscape

Company or group

Positioning and competitive dynamics

AlphaSense

Combines external research and expert content with customer documents and AI search. Its curated, ranked results and workflow tools are key differentiators, though expanding data access requires careful licensing.

Hebbia, Rogo, and Glean

Pressured by production delays and pricing competition, according to the expert. Some customers reconsidered these tools after AlphaSense expanded its capabilities.

Bloomberg, FactSet, and other financial data platforms

Remain entrenched and are not clearly losing share. Customers want fewer terminals, but these platforms have introduced AI features and retain valuable data and capabilities.

Customer-built AI and foundation models

A potential alternative and partner. AlphaSense shifted its pitch from replacing internal builds to supplying content and search that can improve them.
