Agenda

  • Crescent Energy's (NYSE: CRGY) acquisition of Devon Energy's (NYSE: DVN) Eagle Ford shale portfolio
  • Remaining acreage assessment, investment priorities and potential synergies from the deal
  • Implications for Devon's remaining portfolio and Crescent's consolidation strategy
  • Key trends in the Eagle Ford shale and outlook for O&G (oil and gas) production in the basin

Key Insights

  • Crescent is acquiring an asset with "magic rock" but one that is also mature and unlikely to have a step-function change in production. The deal's timing was "fortuitous" as Devon's focus has moved to other assets
  • Cost savings are still a possibility through better procurement strategies as Crescent becomes a dominant player in the basin, but Devon has vastly improved costs per well after dissolving its BPX Energy JV in 2025
  • Increasing lateral lengths will be a potential strategy for the northeastern part of the acreage, but expert sees limited opportunities in the core Karnes and DeWitt County assets that have already been optimised
  • Refracs have been proven on the asset and there is room for growth, while enhanced oil recovery requires tens of millions of dollars in upfront infrastructure spending but offers potential
  • Eagle Ford's industry-wide oil volumes and drilling activity are likely to remain flat at current levels
