SUMMARY

Overview

The discussion assessed Genmab’s Rina-S following RAINFOL-01 Part C and considered its potential Phase III positioning. The expert treats many heavily pretreated ovarian cancer patients and emphasized that treatment options remain limited, particularly for tumors with low or medium folate receptor alpha expression.

Current sequencing generally begins with ELAHERE for high folate receptor alpha expression, followed by taxane-based combinations, HER2-directed therapy when applicable, chemotherapy, and clinical trials. Biomarker testing typically begins at first recurrence.

Key Insights

Rina-S produced highly encouraging and consistent efficacy in a heavily pretreated population, although randomized Phase III confirmation is required before it can alter practice. Its potential activity across folate receptor alpha expression levels could substantially expand eligibility beyond ELAHERE’s core population. For low or medium expressers, Rina-S could become the preferred initial treatment in platinum-resistant disease if Phase III confirms efficacy. For high expressers, ELAHERE would probably remain first unless Rina-S delivers clearly superior Phase III outcomes. Rina-S may be favored over weekly taxane regimens because it is administered every three weeks and appears to have a more manageable safety profile. The expert viewed a dramatic, practice-changing Phase III result as unlikely, estimating its probability below 10%. Phase III analyses by receptor expression and prior ELAHERE exposure were considered especially important. Payload-related cross-resistance may prevent routine sequencing of multiple topoisomerase-1 ADCs.
