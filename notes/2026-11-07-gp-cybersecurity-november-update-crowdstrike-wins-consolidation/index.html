SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, a cybersecurity sales and consulting leader with more than 15 years in the industry, described enterprise customer demand and vendor performance across the cybersecurity market. Vendor consolidation remains the main purchasing driver: customers want best-of-breed tools for key controls, supplemented by broader platforms.

The most active areas of discussion are identity, data security and AI governance, and vulnerability risk prioritization. AI initiatives are also expanding security work beyond dedicated security budgets, as organizations address data classification, access governance, and safeguards for AI use.

Key Insights

CrowdStrike is the clearest beneficiary of consolidation. The expert said its endpoint product performs well, its enterprise agreements make it easy to trial and adopt additional modules, and it integrates acquisitions quickly into one agent and console. AI-related vulnerability concerns are translating into demand. Interest in prioritization tools began earlier, with deals picking up later. The expert expects further spending as organizations plan budgets and respond to emerging risks. AI governance is crowded and confusing. Many vendors offer similar claims, and the expert expects substantial market shakeout. Platform providers may have an advantage if their products meet customer needs. Security spending is expected to increase materially in the next budget cycle. The expert believes a major, widely publicized cyber incident could accelerate investment in automated patching and other protections. Organizations should strengthen foundational controls. The expert emphasized least privilege, patching, vulnerability management, logging, and system hardening alongside newer technologies.
