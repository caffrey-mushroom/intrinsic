We tie our call on Google’s softer Search results with Open AI’s growing advertising operation.

Oct 29

READ IN APP

Our GOOG preview for institutional clients correctly called softer Search and ~101% GCP growth. Channel checks flagged the first signs of ChatGPT affecting ad revenue, and we deemed it important enough for the title of the report:

Another important signal is organizational: OpenAI has built out a full advertising operation—sales, self-serve platform, measurement, SMB acquisition, industry coverage (media, travel, financial services), and regional channels—with hiring accelerating sharply in Q2–Q3.

Though hiring does not prove ad revenue scale, Open AI’s accelerating advertiser acquisition investment will be relevant to Google analysts and investors. This report uses OpenAI’s advertising-related job postings to assess how its commercialization team is taking shape and compares the hiring evidence with channel feedback discussed in our GOOG 26Q3 preview.

Executive Summary

  1. OpenAI’s advertising hiring now spans sales, the ad-buying platform, recommendations, measurement, and customer operations, indicating that the company is assembling a cross-functional team.2. Hiring accelerated in 26Q2 and 26Q3. The second quarter focused on building the sales team; the third added SMB acquisition, marketing operations, and data-related roles. Industry sales coverage expanded from media and entertainment to travel and financial services, while regional roles took on local marketing and outsourced-channel management.3. As of October 26, 2026, the FUNDA hiring database listed 107 advertising-related postings. SMB and enterprise ad marketing lead roles appeared on April 29 and May 20, respectively. Japan and cross-regional SMB roles carry advertiser growth and revenue targets, while dedicated roles cover large accounts, agencies, outsourced sales, and lifecycle marketing.

Source: FUNDA

26Q2 Built the Sales Team; 26Q3 Added Marketing and Operations Roles

Monthly hiring data show 14 new advertising-related roles in 26Q1, 33 in 26Q2, and 54 in 26Q3. The 26Q3 total was 21 higher than in 26Q2, an increase of about 64%. Sales and customer roles remained at 23 in both quarters, while advertising and marketing roles rose from 5 to 14 and other functions increased from 5 to 17.

Source: FUNDA

Hiring in 26Q1 focused on systems and finance. Monetization engineering roles appeared in January, ad formats and revenue accounting roles in February, and a marketing science lead role in March. These positions covered parts of the work needed for ad launches, revenue recognition, and performance measurement. The U.S. ad pilot launched in February; on March 23, the first ad sales and customer success openings appeared in San Francisco and New York, with one role in each city.

In 26Q2, hiring shifted toward customer coverage and sales management. Sales and customer roles accounted for about 70% of new postings that quarter. OpenAI posted 13 sales roles between April 21 and 29, 11 of them outside the U.S. SMB and enterprise ad marketing lead roles followed, along with a global advertising outsourcing manager and a scaled sales lead in June. The self-serve ad platform’s public beta in May added another route for advertisers to access the offering.

26Q3 Expanded Acquisition and Measurement; October Added Industry and Regional Roles

Hiring in 26Q3 placed more emphasis on advertiser acquisition and ongoing account management. Of the 14 advertising and marketing postings, 11 appeared in September, including 6 focused on SMBs. Responsibilities included prospecting, agency certification, and lifecycle marketing. After adding sales leadership and cross-regional sales roles in 26Q2, OpenAI continued to build out channel development and customer operations in 26Q3. Conversion-tracking engineering roles in July and agency partnership roles in August point to parallel investment in acquisition and measurement.

The available 26Q4 data cover only October 1–26, during which 6 new roles appeared: 4 in sales and customer functions, 1 in advertising and marketing, and 1 in product and engineering. North America and APAC outsourcing leads, Japan SMB marketing, financial services sales, and Shop/Feed Ads engineering roles show further specialization by region, industry, and product integration. No new postings were identified after October 13, but the partial-quarter sample is too limited to establish a trend for the full quarter.

Source: FUNDA

Industry Sales Roles Expanded Beyond General Advertiser Acquisition

More specific industry sales roles began appearing in 26Q3: media and entertainment on August 7, travel on September 4, and financial services on October 12. A Shop/Feed Ads engineering role posted on October 6 covers retail and travel product catalogs, adding engineering support for product-based advertising.

The enterprise ad marketing lead role posted in 26Q2 already referenced consumer goods, retail, automotive, and telecommunications. At that stage, these industries appeared in the marketing remit; media and entertainment, travel, and financial services later had dedicated sales postings.

Source: FUNDA

Regional Hiring Expanded Beyond Sales into Local Marketing and Channel Operations

Hiring in 26Q2 initially focused on sales roles in markets outside the U.S. In 26Q3, regional responsibilities became more specific: a sales role appeared in Mumbai, while EMEA added marketing science and outsourcing leadership roles. Spanish-language customer success and sales roles were also posted in September. In October, OpenAI added North America and APAC outsourcing leads and a Japan SMB marketing role, extending the scope from sales coverage to local advertiser acquisition, customer service, and channel management.

Source: FUNDA

EMEA’s 26Q3 postings covered measurement, outsourced channels, and marketing. Japan’s October SMB marketing role explicitly included revenue and growth targets, while APAC added outsourced sales management responsibilities. These postings suggest that regional advertiser operations are being divided into more specialized functions.

Measurement and Product Data Integration Remain Hiring Priorities

Of the 17 roles associated with advertising measurement and related capabilities, 13 were posted after June, and 9 remained open as of October 26. One of the five conversion and attribution roles remained open, compared with two of three recommendation and relevance roles and three of four product catalog and advertiser data integration roles. The July conversion-tracking engineering role and October product-feed role indicate continued investment in measurement and product integration alongside sales-team expansion.

Source: FUNDA

Hiring Trends Echo Our GOOG 26Q3 Preview

Our earlier GOOG 26Q3 preview (available to institutional clients on the FUNDA platform) examined advertiser budgets and campaign performance. The OpenAI hiring data in this report provides some complementary insights.

Both sources point to increased commercial activity in 26Q3. Channel feedback indicated greater adoption of self-serve ad buying and higher test budgets with OpenAI. Roles in travel, financial services, and product feeds offer further clues about the industries OpenAI is targeting.

Interestingly, hiring in conversion tracking, recommendation models, product catalogs/feeds, and lifecycle marketing map to the OpenAI pain points discussed in the GOOG preview (e.g. limited volume/relevance, weak downstream conversion, high CPAs, low graduation from testing, and attribution gaps).
