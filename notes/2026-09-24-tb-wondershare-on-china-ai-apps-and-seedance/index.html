[00:23:11] For 3D models, do their foundation models still rely on GLM [general language model] or models such as DeepSeek V4?

Expert [00:23:11]: No, 3D models are new. For example, GLM is a semantic large AI model launched by Zhipu, called GLM-5.2 and GLM-5.1, but the video model uses the DiT architecture, namely diffusion image transformer. There will be new things in 3D. Normally, when AI generates a video, one second of video essentially generates 24 frames of images, which are combined by judging their spatio-temporal consistency, called optical flow, which is essentially planar. 3D is a bit more complex because 3D has vectors and physical models. Therefore, its architecture is different and the generated content is also different. At present, the most mainstream 3D model generations are called world models.

Client [00:24:24]: However, as for the vendors of such world models, they still distil other people's work.

Expert [00:24:24]: That's right. That still feels like a toy. 3D models might be used in industrial scenarios, but it is difficult to use them in C-end scenarios like this. As for what you mentioned about game modelling, industrial modelling or audio and video, I haven't seen them used.
