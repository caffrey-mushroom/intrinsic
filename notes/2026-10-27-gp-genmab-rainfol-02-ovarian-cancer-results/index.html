SUMMARY

Overview

The discussion assessed Genmab’s Rina-S following the RAINFOL-02 readout in platinum-resistant ovarian cancer. Topics included expected survival outcomes, biomarker subgroups, safety, sequencing after Elahere, and competition from other antibody-drug conjugates, or ADCs. The expert also reviewed broader efforts to move ADCs into platinum-sensitive and frontline treatment.

Key Insights

The expert considered the reported RAINFOL-02 hazard ratios exceptionally encouraging and potentially practice-changing, while cautioning against cross-trial comparisons. Rina-S could become the preferred initial treatment for patients with low, medium, or negative folate receptor-alpha expression if subgroup efficacy remains strong. Sequencing is less certain for high expressors. Elahere followed by Rina-S has supporting evidence, whereas Elahere’s effectiveness after Rina-S remains unknown. Biomarker-agnostic approval would simplify community use, although folate receptor testing remains easy and clinically useful. Rina-S may be favored over weekly regimens because of its every-three-week schedule and more manageable toxicity profile. The expert expects ADCs eventually to enter platinum-sensitive and frontline ovarian cancer settings, particularly where current targeted options are limited.
