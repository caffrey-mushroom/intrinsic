Substack · 2026-11-05

核心摘要

  1. AppLovin 盘后涨 22.2%：游戏业务同比增长近 50%，推动三季度收入 21.85 亿美元，高于约 21.0–21.2 亿美元的买方预期，是连续四个季度减速后的首次加速。
  2. AppLovin 四季度指引只计入已上线的模型收益，收入中值 23.8 亿美元、EBITDA 20.1 亿美元均高于买方预期；CEO 把新模型的意义比作 Axon 2.0，但没有给出抽成率、游戏与电商拆分和全面上线时间。
  3. 按 2026 年约 83.3 亿美元收入测算，市场对 2027 年约 101 亿美元的预期只隐含 21% 增长，低于 CEO 说的长期约 30% 复合增速。
  4. 高通盘后跌 6.9%：汽车收入创纪录 19.9 亿美元、同比增长 89%，数据中心首次给出季度指引超过 6.5 亿美元，但毛利率连续第四个季度下滑至 52.4%，QCT 毛利率回到 48–50% 低端的时间推迟到 FY27 下半年。
  5. ARM 盘后跌 1.4%：授权收入 7.73 亿美元、增长 50%，比公司预期高约 1 亿美元，但版税收入 6.7 亿美元低于预期，下季度版税只指引同比低个位数增长，原因是内存涨价打击中低端手机。
  6. ARM 把 FY27–28 AGI CPU 收入目标提高到 20 亿美元（7 月时为超过 10 亿美元），抵消了部分版税担忧。
  7. Coherent 盘后涨 7.6%：数据中心与通信业务同比增长 79%，首次披露 3 月季度 PhotonLink（CPO）收入将超过 1 亿美元，并签下从 2028 年开始、约 200 亿美元的五年长协，FY27 备考增速预计超过 60%。
  8. HubSpot 盘后跌 7.7%：三季度固定汇率增长 17%，但四季度指引 12% 低于约 14.5–15% 的预期，公司称 AI 改变了客户发现和购买软件的方式，并表示逆风将持续到 2027 年。

APP +22.2%: Gaming, where bears saw share loss to U, was the bright spot. It grew nearly 50% y/y (their first gaming growth disclosure in a year) on a partial rollout of new ad models, driving most of the 3Q beat ($2.185B vs. ~$2.10-2.12B bogeys), first acceleration after four straight decels (although the 2 yr stack decel’d) and biggest beat vs. street since 1Q’25. The 4Q guide, counting only model gains already live, beat bogeys on revs ($2.38B mid vs. ~$2.33-2.36B) and EBITDA ($2.01B vs. ~$1.93-1.96B). Foroughi said share-loss checks “tend to end up being flawed” and called 2Q’s miss “our own foot fault,” but mgmt gave no take rate, gaming vs. consumer split, full-rollout timing or e-com revenue: “You sort of have to make your own guesses.” Foroughi called the 3Q model rollout “a model transition as significant as Axon 2.0,” and asked if it’s Axon 3.0, put it on that level “from a technology standpoint in terms of technical complexity.” It’s “still in partial rollout,” the 4Q guide counts only gains “already live,” and there’s no timing on the full rollout.

Crowded HF short that ran 19% this week into the print, including +11% today but this should still be good enough for a stock that now trades at 15x P/E. The pushback is the 4Q guide’s +8-10% q/q (vs. +14% in 3Q), but their usual ~5.5% beat on the mid would take 4Q to ~+15% q/q. No 2027 number, but 2026 now tracks to ~$8.33B (nine months reported plus the 4Q guide), so Street’s ~$10.1B for 2027 assumes just +21% growth, well short of the “roughly 30%” a year Foroughi says the business can compound at long term. At 30%, 2027 revenue would be ~$10.8B. Overall, much better than feared after U share-loss scare, and the higher base de-risks 2027 and likely gets some bulls back on board hoping for e-com to ramp and beats to continue in ‘27. QCOM -6.9%: Revenue cleared the bar but the GM recovery was shifted out. Record auto ($1.99B, +89% y/y) drove the Sept-Q beat. DC’s first quarterly guide (>$650M vs. a ~$0.5B bogey) and only a mid-single-digit q/q handset decline put the Dec-Q guide at $10.8B, above the ~$10.05-10.25B bogeys. But GM fell a 4th straight quarter (52.4%), and the Dec-Q EPS guide ($2.25 mid) missed Street’s $2.33. On GM, the worry going in, the CFO said price increases only return QCT GM to “the low end” of 48-50% by 2H FY27 (vs. “over the next couple quarters” in July), before DC dilution, and declined to guide March, FY27 opex or the QCT operating-margin path.

Under-owned and a favored HF short, it squeezed +5% into the close as SOXX fell 3% in the post-election rotation. It slid from -5% to -8% within ten minutes of the GM answer. DC’s $5B FY27 target held; upside is “constrained by supply rather than demand,” the CFO said. Overall, better than feared on revenue, but the GM framework brings FY27 #s lower. More fuel for bears.

ARM -1.4%: License is carrying ARM while phones drag on royalties. License ($773M, +50%) ran “around $100 million higher than we had anticipated”, per the CFO, while royalty ($670M, +8%) missed the ~$705-712M bogeys. The $1.54B Dec-Q guide mid matched the top of the ~$1.50-1.54B bogeys, with implied op income ~$10M above Street. But the CFO guided royalty up just “low single digits year-on-year” (~$755M on last year’s $737M, vs. Street $818M). Add Street’s March quarter and FY27 royalty is ~$2.96B (+13%), below the ~$3.06-3.10B bogeys. Mgmt blamed memory prices hitting mid- and low-end phones and sees some recovery in the March quarter, but wouldn’t commit to license holding ~$770M a quarter next year and didn’t update July’s high-teens FY27 royalty view.

Positioning mixed going in with stock down 30% in Oct. AGI CPU offset the royalty miss: asked what drives the $15B FY31 target, the CFO said “we are increasing our confidence to deliver $2 billion” over FY27-28 (vs. >$1B in July), the top of the ~$1.5-2.0B bogeys. Overall, worse than feared on royalties but delivered the AGI CPU raise bulls wanted.

COHR +7.6%: In line on the quarter (GM missed by ~10-30bps), better guide. DC& Comms (+79% y/y) drove F1Q, and the Dec-Q guide beat the bogeys ($2.65B mid vs. ~$2.60-2.64B) with profit in line (op income ~$627-646M at the mids vs. ~$636M at the bogeys). The firsts: >$100M of PhotonLink (CPO) revenue in the March-Q, a ~$20B, five-year PhotonLink LTA from CY28 (customer unnamed), and FY27 pro forma growth >60%, which puts it at >~$11.2B on the ~$7.0B FY26 base vs. Street $10.63B. On GM, the worry going in, the 6-inch InP savings behind the new >45% target (by end-CY28) are still ahead: the CEO wouldn’t give the internal/external laser mix but said it’s “about the same” as a year ago, as doubled InP output kept pace with transceiver growth.

HFs were short this to fund LITE longs before the MRVL deal, and it fell 6% today with semis on post-election data center worries. Asked about them, the CEO said “almost every LTA” carries an upfront customer payment to help fund capacity; prepayments rose $270M in F1Q, a third of capex. Overall, better than feared, and it de-risks 2027-28 demand. AMD and ANET finished green despite the semis blood bath today, so would imagine should hold in ok tomorrow.

HUBS -7.7%: Put simply: the slowdown doesn’t end in 4Q feeding the AI loser bear case. 3Q grew 17% cc, beating the ~15.5-16% bogeys, but the 4Q guide of 12% missed the ~14.5-15%, which mgmt put on AI changing how customers find and buy software. KPIs got cut too: the CFO now sees FY NRR down ~2pts, net new ARR down y/y and 4Q net adds “below the 5,000 level”. Asked if 2027 starts at 12%, below the ~13% bar, she said the headwinds “will continue in Q4 and into 2027”; mgmt wouldn’t say if trends stabilized, give a 2027 number before February or date its lower-ASP seats-plus-credits pricing beyond new EMEA customers...
