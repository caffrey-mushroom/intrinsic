Decent set of Tech earnings today…

META +4%: Revenue beat both Street and the buyside bar, and the top of the 4Q guide is above the ~$75B bogey. Overall, solid set of numbers but investors remain braced for a potential equity raise (lets see if we get any info on the call on timing).

3Q came in above the top of the guide, driven mainly by pricing (price per ad +14%, impressions +10%). The FY26 expense guide goes to $175–179B, but that’s just the $9.9B AG charge added on top. Ex the charge it’s still $165–169B, a bit of a relief. Capex was narrowed by raising only the low end, so the midpoint is now $140B. That’s a little short of the $140–145B buyside had, so a slight letdown for AI semi bulls and a small positive for META longs.

On 2027, mgmt says it’s still building the budget and capacity plan and will keep “investing aggressively.” Remember bogeys around ~$215–220B opex / ~$250B capex and should get some deets on the call. The unchanged ex-charge guide implies 4Q expenses of ~$48–52B, up from $41.7B in 3Q. That’s already a ~$200B annualized run rate before any ‘27 growth. The Semafor share-class story that hit near the close was right: non-voting Class C stock, effective 1/19/27, created through a 1-for-1 stock dividend (effectively a 2-for-1 split), with capital raising listed first among the reasons. FCF was -$2.1B on $34.9B of capex.

3Q Revenue: $64.6B, +26% cc vs. Street $63.3B and bogey $64B+ 3Q Price per Ad / Impressions: +14% / +10% y/y 4Q Revenue Guide: $73–76B (+22–27% y/y with ~1pt FX headwind) vs. Street $73.6B and bogey ~$75B+ at the high end FY26 Total Expenses: $175–179B including the $9.9B charge ($165–169B ex, unchanged) FY26 Capex: $135–145B vs. prior $130–145B, Street ~$142B and bogey $140–145B FY27: no expense or capex guide; “strong overall revenue growth throughout 2027”; Reality Labs losses ~$15B vs. $18–19B in FY26 3Q EPS: $3.83 GAAP; $7.40 ex the charge

TXN flat: Revenue just matched the bogey, but GM and the 4Q guide were above. Solid print even for what was already crowded long heading into the print (still +60% YTD), which is why stock not up.

3Q revenue of $6.06B (+11% q/q, +28% y/y) beat Street by ~2% but landed exactly on the buyside’s $6.05–6.1B. So there’s no top-line upside to buyside numbers.

What was better was elsewhere:

  • GM was 64.2%, ~260bps over Street and ~170bps over the ~62.5% bogey.
  • The 4Q guide midpoint of $6.05B is flat q/q versus a normal ~-6% seasonal decline, and above the ~$5.95B bogey.
  • The 4Q EPS guide midpoint of $2.62 vs bogey of $2.40

At $6.05B, 4Q annualizes to ~$24B, so Street’s $25.1B for C27 needs only a modestly better-than-seasonal 1H to look conserative. FCF was $2.96B as capex fell to $463M from $1.2B a year ago. The 300mm build is rolling off, and the FCF inflection bulls playing for showing up. Analog grew +32% y/y and Embedded +21%.

3Q Revenue: $6.06B vs. Street $5.93B and bogey ~$6.05–6.1B 3Q GM: 64.2% vs. Street 61.6% and bogey ~62.5% 3Q EPS: $2.62 vs. Street $2.43 and bogey ~$2.50 3Q FCF: $2.96B vs. Street $2.26B 4Q Revenue Guide: $5.80–6.30B ($6.05B midpoint, flat q/q) vs. Street $5.76B and bogey ~$5.95B 4Q EPS Guide: $2.45–2.79 ($2.62 midpoint) vs. Street $2.31 and bogey ~$2.40

LRCX -7%: The September quarter beat by a little, but the December guide only matched Street at $8.6B while buyside sat at ~$9B.

September revenue of $8.22B and 52.4% GM were right on the bogeys and slightly above Street. However, guide whiffed: $8.60B ±$400M is ~+5% q/q, half the +10% buyside bogey. Margins and EPS in the guide just in line. Hearing some pushback on quality of the beat as China jumped to 35% of revenue from 26% in June, which makes China ~$1.1B of the ~$1.5B q/q increase; ex-China revenue grew ~7% q/q.

The stock was already ~40% off its June highs (semicaps almost flat YTD which is will), but this likely keeps semicap as the funding short in semis. Should keep some ‘27 framing on the call: $215B+ is what bulls want.

F1Q Revenue: $8.22B vs. Street $8.14B and bogey ~$8.2–8.25B F1Q GM: 52.4% vs. Street 51.8% and bogey ~52.5% F1Q EPS: $2.20 vs. Street $2.18 China Mix: 35% vs. 26% in the June quarter F2Q Revenue Guide: $8.60B ±$0.4B vs. Street $8.57B and bogey ~$9.0–9.1B F2Q GM Guide: 52.5% vs. Street 51.9% and bogey ~52–52.5% F2Q EPS Guide: $2.33 vs. Street $2.33

IBM +5%: Software bounced back to +9% cc from +4% in 2Q as the slipped deals closed. FCF outlook went up.

Revenue of $17.1B (+6% cc) beat Street by ~2%, with Software ~$200M ahead.

  • Software: Data (+20% cc), Transaction Processing (+10% cc) and Red Hat (+11%) better. Automation (-3%) was the soft spot.
  • Infrastructure: +5% cc vs. Street +2%, even with Z -19% cc in its off-cycle year, because Distributed grew +32%.
  • Consulting: +2% cc, in line, still no reacceleration.

Operating pre-tax margin rose 70bps y/y. FY26 revenue growth rei at 4–5% cc, and the FCF outlook now >$1B y/y growth. That’s despite 3Q FCF of $3.0B coming in a little under Street. After the 2Q software miss the bar was simply “show the deals closed,” and this clears should be enough for a non-crowded stock. Reminder: stock was +4% today.
