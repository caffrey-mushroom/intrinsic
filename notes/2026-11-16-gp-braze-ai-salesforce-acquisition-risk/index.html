核心摘要

  1. 一位 Braze 用户（企业 COO）评估了 Salesforce 收购 Braze 的影响，认为客户反应喜忧参半：同时使用 Salesforce 的客户期待更深整合，使用其他 CRM 的客户则担心被迫绑定 Salesforce。
  2. 专家表示目前判断收购对续约、采购或竞标短名单的影响还为时过早，因为多数招标流程已经结束，客户也不会在短期内更换工具。
  3. Braze AI 模块渗透率很高，专家估计超过 70%-80% 的客户已采用 Operator、Decisioning Studio 等部分或大部分模块，Agent Console 因客户不愿给予 AI 过多自主权而采用率略低。
  4. 专家认为 Braze 最强的差异化在于实时数据处理和 AI 个体化决策，相比预设旅程和 A/B 测试能根据实时信号调整是否以及如何触达客户。
  5. 其传统的跨渠道消息优势正在收窄，因为 Iterable、Klaviyo、MoEngage 等竞争对手已经补齐类似能力。
  6. Braze 主要在没有既有 CRM 的中型电商和科技企业中胜出，面对已部署 Salesforce 或 Adobe 的大型企业时较难竞争，对 Klaviyo 则在移动 App 推送上更强。
  7. 客户目前要求部署周期约 3-6 个月、投资回收期最长 18-24 个月，推动更大规模 AI 采用的最有说服力证据是转化率、收入和留存的提升。
  8. 收购的短期价值在于双方客户的交叉销售，长期价值在于将 Braze AI 与 Agentforce、Data 360 等技术整合；若 Salesforce 强制客户使用自家 CRM，将促使客户重新考虑平台。
  9. 专家建议未来 12-18 个月以客户留存率作为判断这笔并购是强化还是削弱 Braze 业务的核心指标。

SUMMARY

Overview

The expert discussed Braze’s customer-engagement platform, AI products, competitive position, and the potential effects of Salesforce’s agreement to acquire the company. Braze is used for customer data processing, campaign workflows, and coordinated messaging across channels such as email, text, and mobile push. The expert sees its strongest fit among mid-sized digital businesses that depend on timely customer interactions.

Customer reactions to the acquisition are mixed. Salesforce users may benefit from closer integration, while customers using other CRM systems are concerned that Braze could become less open or increasingly tied to Salesforce. It is too early to see clear changes in procurement or renewals.

Key Insights

The expert views Braze’s real-time data analysis and AI-driven decisioning as its strongest differentiators. In their view, adaptive AI can outperform preset customer journeys by adjusting whether and how to engage based on changing signals. The expert believes Braze’s cross-channel messaging advantage has narrowed as competitors have added similar capabilities. The most immediate acquisition opportunity is cross-selling to each company’s customer base. Longer term, the expert sees technology integration, particularly combining Braze’s AI and workflows with Salesforce products, as the greater potential value. Braze’s openness to different CRM systems is central to its appeal. A requirement to use Salesforce CRM could prompt customers to reconsider the platform. The expert identified customer retention as the most useful measure of whether the acquisition strengthens or weakens Braze’s position.
