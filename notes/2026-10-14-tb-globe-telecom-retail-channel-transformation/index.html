Agenda

  • Globe Telecom's (PSE: GLO) retail strategies and revenue growth drivers
  • B2C distribution channel economics
  • Mobile prepaid, postpaid and Globe At Home performance trends

Key Insights

  • Globe's go-to-market strategy is pivoting to ground-level street visibility following a pre-pandemic store downsizing to 99 from 200 locations. Expert anticipates Globe to rebuild its network to 150 locations over the next 2-3 years, deploying smaller-format stores to penetrate secondary cities
  • Expert projects 2-3% annual B2C revenue growth for Globe over the next 2-3 years, relying on data expansion among retained users rather than new acquisitions, stemming from elevated subscriber churn, with annual mobile churn running at 10-12% since the implementation of Philippine Mobile Number Portability laws
  • General trade commission rates sit at 30-35% of sales and are projected to compress towards 25% over the next 2-3 years as consumer purchasing shifts towards digital platforms
  • General trade accounts for 60% of B2C sales, physical retail represents 30% and online channels comprise 10%
  • Expert notes Globe outperforms PLDT in acquisition through lifestyle visibility and festivals, but faces retention challenges relative to PLDT's faster customer service
