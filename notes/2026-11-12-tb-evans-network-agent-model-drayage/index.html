2026.11.12 | TB | Transport | Evans Network Agent Model & Drayage

原标题：The Evans Network – Agent-based Business Models & Multimodal Expansion Challenges

#transport

Third Bridge · 2026-11-12

核心摘要

  1. 曾在The Evans Network任职约17年的前中西部副总裁表示，公司从干线运价中抽取的佣金约为15%，而非外界所说的25-35%；附加费用的抽成从2-4%到20-25%不等，规模大的代理商会持续施压要求提高分成比例。
  2. 轻资产代理模式下，每1美元收入中约70%归车主司机，15%归代理商（用于支付其人员和场地费用），15%归Evans；货量下降时Evans每1美元仅损失约0.15美元，85%的成本随之自动浮动。
  3. 专家在职时代理商平均拥有约25-30辆卡车，大量代理商只有12-18辆，5-8%的代理商拥有数百辆，最大的约450辆；年营收超过2000万美元即算大型代理商。
  4. Evans将100%的燃油附加费返还给车主司机，而竞争对手通常只返还95-97%，这是招募和留住司机的关键武器；当柴油价格超过每加仑6美元时，司机更倾向于短途线路。
  5. 近期运价开始回升，政府加强驾照审查导致运力收缩，最近三四周货量和运价走高，似乎重现了多年未见的节前旺季，但专家预计1月会进入淡季。
  6. 在信用额度内，坏账风险基本由Evans承担；超出额度或被列为“代理商风险客户”时则回扣到代理商，账期超过60天就会明显困难。
  7. 代理商离开时托运人是否跟随基本是“掷硬币”，最终由托运人决定；Evans在多式联运领域口碑极佳，但这反而不利于其拓展平板车和零担业务，且各业务单元彼此割裂，转型为真正的多式联运公司需要很长时间。
  8. 专家认为Evans应在铁路之间尽量保持中立，而非像Schneider绑定诺福克南方、JB Hunt绑定CSX那样站队；代理模式长期面临的最大风险是加州AB5等独立承包商立法，以及保险和执照要求持续收紧带来的车主司机供给压缩。

Agenda

  • How the variable cost structure of agent models provides financial resilience in turbulent freight markets
  • Role of technology investments in allowing drayage networks to track driver efficiency and maximise fleet utilisation
  • Potential headwinds as The Evans Network diversifies from drayage into flatbed and less-than-truckload
  • Long-term risks from state and federal regulatory changes in the US for independent contractor operations

Key Insights

  • The asset-light model means that when freight volumes drop, Evans only loses cUSD 0.15 per dollar – 85% of costs flex automatically with agent and owner-operator operations
  • Expert believes that while Evans' strong intermodal reputation is a competitive advantage, it works against multimodal expansion and makes him unsure whether the company can become a true multimodal competitor
  • Expert thinks Evans' true recruiting and retention edge comes from the company passing 100% of fuel surcharges directly to owner-operators, unlike competitors who retain 3-5%
  • When agents depart, whether shippers follow them or stay with Evans is essentially "a coin toss", with the shipper ultimately controlling the relationship based on what's best for their supply chain needs
  • Despite rising intermodal and rail alliances, such as with Schneider and Norfolk Southern, expert thinks it's in Evans' best interest to stay as neutral as possible
