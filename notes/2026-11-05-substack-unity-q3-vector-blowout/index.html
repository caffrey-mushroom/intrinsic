Details:

Vector grew +40% q/q (+126% y/y), vs. the ~25-27% q/q bogey and the 19-21% q/q in the 3Q guide (the CFO on the 2Q call: “Our guidance assumes 19%-21% sequential growth rates for Unity Vector”).

  • 2Q grew +23% vs. a 12-13% guide, after four straight quarters of ~15%. So 3Q beat the guide’s assumption by ~20pt vs. ~10pt in 2Q.

Greater China revenue was $157.4M, +74% y/y and +37% q/q, 25% of revenue vs. 21% in 2Q and 19% a year ago. That’s ~57% of the +$75.6M q/q revenue add; US was -3% q/q.

Strategic Grow revenue beat Street and the bogeys at $431.5M, +90.3% y/y (+31.2% q/q), vs. Street $385.9M (+70.5% y/y, +17.3% q/q), the $380-385M guide (+68-70%) and ~$395-402M bogeys (+20-22% q/q).

  • That’s $45.6M above Street, $29.5M above the ~$402M bogey top and $46.5M above the $385M guide top.
  • It’s a 12.8% beat on the $382.5M guide midpoint vs. 8.2% in 2Q ($329.0M vs. a $302-306M guide).
  • Y/y growth accelerated to 90% from 63% in 2Q. That’s not the comp: 3Q25 Strategic Grow grew 12.6% q/q ($201.3M to $226.8M), and this one grew 31.2% q/q. The q/q add of +$102.5M is ~4x last year’s +$25.5M.
  • Total Grow Solutions was $457M, +44% y/y. That’s Strategic Grow plus all $25.5M of non-strategic revenue (the ironSource Ads tail and Supersonic through the 8/4 sale).

Adj. EBITDA beat Street and the bogeys at $256.6M, a 41.25% margin, vs. Street $186.4M (32.86%), the $185-190M guide (~33%) and ~$198-204M bogeys (~34-35%).

  • That’s $70.2M above Street, $52.6M above the ~$204M bogey top and $66.6M above the $190M guide top. It’s a 36.9% beat on the $187.5M guide mid vs. 20.9% in 2Q ($160.2M vs. a $130-135M guide).
  • Incremental margin y/y was 97%: +$147.1M of EBITDA on +$151.5M of total revenue, which grew less than strategic because non-strategic fell $70M. On strategic revenue it’s 66% (+$147.1M on +$221.7M). Q/q it was 127% (+$96.4M on +$75.6M).
  • Where it came from: adj. gross margin of 84.58% (+205bps y/y), adj. S&M -28% y/y ($72.0M), adj. G&A -4% and adj. R&D +15%. SBC was 10.1% of revenue vs. 14% in 2Q and 19.5% a year ago.

First GAAP profit since the IPO: net income of $121.6M (20% margin), GAAP diluted EPS of $0.25, as guided on the 2Q call (pulled forward from 4Q). GAAP op income was $100.6M (16.2% margin) vs. a $125.9M loss a year ago.

  • One-time items: a $4.5M gain on the sale of Supersonic and $12.5M of restructuring (both excluded from adj. EBITDA). Ex the gain, net income was ~$117M. Below the line: $24.3M of interest and other income and a $2.7M tax benefit.
  • 9M GAAP is still a $248M loss on 1Q’s $279M intangible impairment.

4Q Strategic Grow guide beat Street and the bogeys: “Strategic Grow Revenue of $515 million to $520 million, up 105% - 107% year-over-year”, vs. Street $430.6M (+71.6% y/y) and ~$450-462M bogeys.

  • The $517.5M mid is $86.9M above Street and $55.5M above the ~$462M bogey top. Even the $515M low end is $53M above it.
  • In q/q terms: +19.9% at the mid off the actual 3Q of $431.5M (+19.4-20.5% across the range). Street had +11.6% off its own $385.9M 3Q, and the bogeys +13-16% off their ~$395-402M 3Q.

4Q adj. EBITDA guide beat Street and the bogeys: “Adjusted EBITDA of $290 million to $295 million, up 132% - 136% year-over-year”, vs. Street $205.6M (34.2%) and ~$212-222M bogeys (~34-35%).

  • Over the $688-696M total revenue guide (strategic plus the $5M non-strategic), that’s a ~42.3% margin at the mids (42.2-42.4% across the range; our math). That’s ~807bps above Street’s 34.2%, ~7.3-8.3pt above the bogeys, ~+100bps q/q vs. 3Q’s 41.25% and ~+1,742bps y/y (4Q25 ~$125.0M on $503.1M of revenue, 24.8%).
  • The $292.5M mid is $86.9M above Street (+42%) and $70.5M above the ~$222M bogey top. Even the $290M low end is $68M above it.
  • Incremental margin is ~89% y/y but ~51% q/q (+$35.9M of EBITDA on +$69.9M of revenue), after 127% q/q in 3Q.
