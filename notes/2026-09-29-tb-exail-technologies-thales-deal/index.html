Companies: HO FR, EXA FR, BA. GB, SAAB B SE

Lars Sørfang (LS) VP, Sales at Spectra Defense Technologies Inc

Louis Knight (LK) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Exail Technologies' (EPA: EXA) capacity to ramp up manufacturing and industry-wide bottlenecks
  • Product portfolio overview and potential vertical integration benefits with Thales (EPA: HO)
  • Uncrewed naval warfare – robotics in GNSS-denied (global navigation satellite system-denied) operations and autonomous detection programmes
  • Cooperation or competition – could neoprimes displace specialist hardware suppliers?

Key Insights

  • Exail's DriX autonomous surface vessel can be rapidly integrated into Thales' naval packages, accelerating deployment timelines and positioning it to compete directly with Saab, BAE Systems and Kongsberg on Europe's largest naval procurement programmes
  • Competing tier-1 primes are unlikely to stop purchasing Exail's inertial navigation systems post acquisition, given deep integration into long-cycle frigate programmes
  • Neoprimes such as Anduril, Shield AI and Helsing could capture c50% of the European autonomous naval market, leaving traditional primes splitting the remaining half. Expert estimates Thales-Exail could capture 25-30% market share vs incumbents
  • European demand for autonomous naval systems such as DriX is estimated at 30-60 units annually across NATO Europe, sustained over 6-7 years before fleets stabilise into replacement cycles
  • AI's value-add in naval applications is reducing operator cognitive load by continuously analysing sonar imagery, flagging anomalies and alerting crews, which human operators on rotation cannot match
