Agenda

  • Penguin Solutions' (NASDAQ: PENG) shift towards enterprise, neocloud and sovereign AI customers within Advanced Computing

  • Integrated memory demand, new customer wins and repeat business

Key Insights

  • Sovereign AI represents a USD 40bn market growing at a 20% CAGR, with Penguin potentially generating USD 1bn in annual revenue from a major deal despite limited reach beyond South Korea
  • Penguin's move towards larger AI hardware deals at 3-8% gross margins could compress consolidated margins from low-30s to mid-to-high-20s
  • Compute Express Link memory products targeting inference key-value cache could generate USD 100m-200m in annual revenue within two years if OEM partnerships gain traction
  • Memory shortages could temporarily boost Integrated Memory's gross margins by roughly 10pp, partially offsetting profitability pressures from larger AI hardware deployments
  • Expert views neocloud demand as a financing and customer demand bubble resembling the early internet era, with eventual consolidation making enterprise AI Penguin's more sustainable growth opportunity
