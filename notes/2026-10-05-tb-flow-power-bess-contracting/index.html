Agenda

  • Flower Power's growth strategy and BESS (battery energy storage systems) adoption implications
  • C&I (commercial and industrial) contract dynamics and services positioning
  • Competitive positioning vs AGL Energy (ASX: AGL), Shell Energy Australia (LON: SHEL), Engie Australia and Smartest Energy

Key Insights

  • Expert believes the build-everything-in-house technology philosophy, driven by a family-connected leadership structure spanning Chairman, CEO and CTO, poses a real scaling risk for Flow Power's customer-facing energy management solutions
  • Emissions accounting could transition from an annual basis to real-time to assess the emission intensity of energy demand, which will incentivise companies to actively manage timing of energy use. Time-matched power purchase agreements currently represent 10-20% of the C&I market but could increase to 30-50% with regulatory requirements
  • Electric trucking market is another opportunity for Flow Power, with the market concentrated in 5-10 players. Flow currently supplies energy to freight logistics companies using diesel trucks, providing energy for sites that are natural places for EV charging
  • Technology integration remains a primary operational hurdle for battery optimisation. Poor internet connectivity and basic price forecasting models capture c60% of perfect foresight value, yielding returns no better than fixed daily charge schedules
