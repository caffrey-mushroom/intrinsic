SUMMARY

Overview

The discussion examined demand trends in testing, inspection, and certification (TIC), and whether AI-related infrastructure investment and acquisitions can support a global TIC provider’s growth ambitions. The expert, who has experience in the industry, sees data centers, energy infrastructure, regulatory requirements, and recurring inspections as important demand drivers. He cautioned that recent growth should not be assumed to continue at the same pace indefinitely.

Key Insights

Demand appears stronger than earlier in the year, supported by technology testing, infrastructure investment, and tighter compliance requirements. The expert described the outlook as positive but emphasized that quarterly momentum is not a reliable forecast for future years. Regional diversification helps offset slower activity in Europe. North America and parts of Asia are benefiting from energy, industrial, and technology demand; the Middle East and Africa are also growing, while Europe is comparatively subdued. The expert views much of the buildings and infrastructure growth as structural, supported by building investment, asset monitoring, and recurring compliance work. Some project delays may cause volatility. Data-center services currently appear more weighted toward construction, commissioning, and quality control than ongoing operational work. Permitting and power availability could delay projects, though the expert believes these constraints are more likely to affect timing than underlying demand. The AI-related revenue ambition is expected to rely mainly on physical infrastructure services, with digital assurance, cybersecurity, and data-integrity services forming a smaller but strategic opportunity. The expert expects further acquisitions, likely several small or mid-sized deals. He stressed that speed matters: specialized digital services may become more standardized, reducing differentiation over time.
