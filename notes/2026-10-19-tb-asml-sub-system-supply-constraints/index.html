Companies: ASML NL, 2330 TW

Remco Overwater (RO) Independent Consultant

Saad Maqsood (SM) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • ASML's (AMS: ASML) supply chain readiness, component lead times and capacity allocation across critical sub-system suppliers such as Zeiss' optics and Trumpf's lasers
  • Manufacturing throughput and factory execution as ASML scales production
  • Demand visibility and long-term order intake commitments spanning high-NA (numerical aperture) EUV, low-NA EUV and immersion DUV systems through 2027-28
  • Installed base management, retrofits and software-led field upgrade capacity

Key Insights

  • Infrastructure delivery at the Brainport Industries campus, alongside energy grid and municipal permit execution, presents the primary physical bottleneck to meeting ASML's planned capacity expansion plans
  • Managing workforce expansion across the supply chain is critical, as one direct ASML employee requires c8-10 workers in the broader supply network, making international hiring and talent onboarding essential
  • Lower-tier, single-source suppliers pose severe single-point-of-failure risks, requiring active supply chain orchestration and strategic M&A – similar to Cymer – when PE or short-term investor horizons alienate key component vendors
  • To boost factory throughput without relying solely on new physical footprint, ASML is focusing on modular repetitiveness and independent module qualification to compress the current 6-7-week system assembly and tuning window. This would also alleviate training bottlenecks where mastering three core qualification skills traditionally takes an engineer up to three years. ASML can leverage standardised module repetitiveness to accelerate employee education and workforce scaling
