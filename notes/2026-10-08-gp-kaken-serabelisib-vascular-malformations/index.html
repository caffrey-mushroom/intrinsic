概要

脈管奇形は、毛細血管、静脈、動脈、リンパ管の形成異常と、その混合型を含む希少疾患群である。病変の部位、大きさ、進行速度、症状には大きな個人差があり、巨大病変や気道周辺の病変は治療困難または生命予後への影響を伴う。

診断は、毛細血管奇形では視診が中心で、その他は超音波、CT、MRI、血管造影、病理検査を組み合わせる。遺伝子検査は主に専門施設で行われる。難症例が一部の専門施設へ集中するため、一般病院での診療経験は限られる。

標準治療は手術、硬化療法、レーザー治療であり、治療困難例ではmTOR阻害薬が用いられる。国内では静脈奇形への硬化療法が保険診療の対象となり、今後の利用拡大が見込まれる。

主な見解

最大の未充足ニーズは、巨大病変や手術困難部位にも安全に使え、病変を大幅に縮小できる薬物療法である。現行薬は病変を完全に消失させる治療ではなく、十分に反応しない患者も多い。画像上の体積減少は試験評価に適する一方、患者にとっては疼痛や運動障害、生活の質の改善がより重要である。新規PI3Kα阻害薬は既存薬と同列の難治例向け治療になり得るが、価格、比較試験、副作用、長期成績が不明で、使い分けは判断できない。肝機能障害の可能性から軽症例には使いにくく、中等症から重症、または手術・硬化療法・レーザーが困難な患者が主な候補になる。外用薬は全身性副作用を抑えられる可能性があり、十分な効果が確認されれば扱いやすい選択肢となる。

KY: I'd like to move on to our next topic. I'd like to ask about current trends in vascular malformations in Japan, including epidemiology, diagnosis rates, and the typical diagnostic process. Could you explain what vascular malformations are and what kinds of symptoms they cause?

SK: Vascular malformations encompass a very broad range of conditions, including capillary malformations, venous malformations, arterial malformations, and lymphatic malformations. However, malformations of the thoracic aorta and vascular malformations of the central nervous system are generally excluded from this category. There are also mixed malformations, where patients have more than one type of malformation. Vascular malformations range from small lesions that can easily be removed surgically to multiple or very large lesions that infiltrate surrounding tissues and are difficult to treat. Lesions around the airway are particularly concerning because they can affect survival.

KY: Later in our discussion, we'll be talking about venous malformations, lymphatic malformations, and Klippel- Trenaunay syndrome. Could you explain these three conditions?

SK: Venous malformations, which were previously called cavernous hemangiomas, are masses composed of dilated veins that appear red or purple. Lymphatic malformations, formerly known as lymphangiomas, are mass-like lesions consisting primarily of lymphatic cysts of varying sizes, and they mainly occur in children. However, they're not considered true tumors. Biologically, they're thought to result from abnormal development of the lymphatic vessels.

Klippel-Trenaunay syndrome is a condition involving mesodermal abnormalities characterized by overgrowth of bone and soft tissue in the affected limb, along with slow-flow vascular malformations. Its characteristic features include large red birthmarks and enlargement of the affected limb. For example, a patient's left leg might be unusually longer and thicker than the right leg. Those are the kinds of changes that can occur with this condition.

KY: I'd like to ask about the causes and mechanisms underlying these vascular malformations, particularly venous malformations, lymphatic malformations, and Klippel-Trenaunay syndrome. How much do we currently understand about their pathogenesis and underlying mechanisms?

For example, venous malformations were the first condition in which genetic abnormalities were identified, and mutations in the TEK and PIK3CA genes have been reported as major contributors.
