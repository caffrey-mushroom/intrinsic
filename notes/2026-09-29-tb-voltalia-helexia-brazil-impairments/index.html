Louis Knight (LK) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Delay in commissioning Helexia Brazil plants – small solar plants for commercial – due to grid delays
  • Impairment of Brazilian solar assets due to grid curtailment
  • Extent to which grid delay and curtailment problems are improving or worsening in Brazil
  • Voltalia's (EPA: VLTSA) potential divestment of Helexia Brazil

Key Insights

  • EUR 8m impairment likely reflects a combination of pandemic-era supply chain shocks, doubled solar panel costs, currency devaluation, freight cost spikes and ongoing curtailment hitting a specific 2019-21 project vintage
  • Curtailment on centralised renewable assets jumped from roughly 5-6% pre-2023 blackout to approximately 20%. This structural problem is expected to persist until new transmission lines come online around 2029-30
  • Many small commercialisation companies have gone bankrupt, with 1-2 filing for bankruptcy each month, creating power purchase agreement renegotiation risks for Helexia's contracted revenues
  • Even a 20% discount to regulated tariffs isn't enough to attract retail energy clients, due to billing friction and low absolute savings for small consumers such as households and bakeries
  • Selling Brazilian assets now would likely produce "bad will" on Voltalia's balance sheet – buyers are demanding 25% equity IRRs vs the original 15-20% underwriting assumptions
