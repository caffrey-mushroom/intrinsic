Agenda

  • Key demand trends and dynamics across Cérélia's key markets
  • Cost dynamics, pass-through capability, negotiating power and subsequent margin implications
  • Maintenance CAPEX needs, facility utilisation and portfolio risk from shifting consumer health trends

Key Insights

  • Modern pricing contracts utilise open-book formula cards tracking flour, butter and energy, enabling price adjustments within 60-90 days once input costs breach a 3-5% rate
  • Co-manufacturing for FMCG brand owners yields higher, more durable margins than standard private label contracts because branded players maintain long-term supplier stability to safeguard complex product specifications
  • Expert projects Cérélia's EBIT margin to expand by 200-300bps annually, from its 10% baseline in 2025 into the low-teens, driven primarily by fixed-cost scale leverage
  • Maintenance CAPEX runs at c2-4% of sales, with European acquisitions historically delivering new capacity cheaply through depreciated, partially utilised assets acquired at attractive valuations relative to replacement cost
