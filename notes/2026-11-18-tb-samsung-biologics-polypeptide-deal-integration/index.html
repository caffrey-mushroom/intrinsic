原标题：Samsung Biologics' Pending Acquisition of PolyPeptide Group – Integration Considerations & Customer Retention Outlook

#pharma #healthcare

Third Bridge · 2026-11-18

核心摘要

  1. 前CordenPharma全球技术负责人认为三星生物收购PolyPeptide是补齐多肽产品组合、切入GLP-1高增长赛道的聪明之举，可比自建绿地工厂提前约3-4年进入市场，但修复旧厂质量问题会抵消部分时间优势。
  2. 收购价约14亿瑞郎，按2025年较低EBITDA计约35倍，按2026年预测EBITDA计约15-16倍，专家认为三星并未支付明显溢价。
  3. PolyPeptide 2026年EBITDA利润率接近22%，专家认为在三星管理下2027年即可达到25%目标，行业合理水平为25-30%。
  4. 溶剂约占多肽批次成本的30%，现场溶剂回收可将该成本减半（约节省15%总成本），但小型系统需2000-3000万欧元资本开支和两年时间，大型系统约1亿欧元。
  5. SPPS工艺是主要瓶颈：平均每天仅两次偶联，最终收率约40-45%，混合工艺可提升至55-60%，一批100kg API约需三个月；冻干环节收率约70%。
  6. PolyPeptide产线规模最大约1500-3000升，远小于Corden在科罗拉多Boulder的10000升产线，且Torrance等城市站点难以扩张，限制其短期抢夺大规模GLP-1合同。
  7. 专家估计未来3-5年约35-45%的商业化多肽需求将外包，但礼来、诺和诺德自建产能及口服小分子（如orforglipron，或占20%）会压低外包比例。
  8. 商业化项目技术转移约需两年、成本超100万瑞郎，高监管转换门槛保护现有合同；营收1-2亿瑞郎以上的产品才会考虑双供应商，礼来已对PolyPeptide与Corden双源采购。
  9. 专家估计PolyPeptide未来2-3年需约10亿瑞郎（可能高达20亿）投入自动化、溶剂回收和美印扩产，Braine-l'Alleud工厂仍是监管审查风险最高的站点。

Agenda

  • Samsung Biologics' (KRX: 207940) pending PolyPeptide Group (SWX: PPGN) acquisition – cost synergies, portfolio gaps and integration priorities
  • Equipment upgrade timelines, including costs, qualification and compliance considerations at production sites
  • Operational levers and automation investments needed to improve plant productivity
  • Commercial volume retention risks, customer dual-sourcing behaviours and competitive pricing dynamics vs CordenPharma and Bachem (SWX: BANB)

Key Insights

  • Acquiring PolyPeptide enables Samsung to enter the peptide market c3-4 years faster than building greenfield plants, although expert mentioned that fixing legacy plant quality issues offsets this timeline advantage
  • Solvents account for c30% of peptide batch costs, and it was noted that on-site solvent recycling could halve these expenses, despite requiring significant capital and multi-year installation timelines
  • Expert believes Samsung has the ability to stabilise PolyPeptide's high labour turnover while resolving legacy compliance bottlenecks, thereby improving plant efficiency and supporting margin recovery
  • It was indicated that c35-45% of commercial peptide demand will likely be outsourced over the next 3-5 years, facing pressure as major pharma sponsors expand internal capacity and oral alternatives emerge
  • PolyPeptide's smaller plant capacity limits near-term GLP-1 market share gains against larger competitors such as CordenPharma and Bachem, yet it was noted that high regulatory switching hurdles protect incumbent commercial contracts from customer churn
