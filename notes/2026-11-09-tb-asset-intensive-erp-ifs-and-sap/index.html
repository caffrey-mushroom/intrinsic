原标题：Asset-intensive ERP – IFS & SAP – How Customers are Prioritising Spend on Asset Lifecycle Software

#ERP #IFS #SAP

Third Bridge · 2026-11-09

核心摘要

  1. 预算更挑剔，IFS扩张依赖客户有机增长，SAP受益于迁移。
  2. 现有客户需求支持IFS约中20%收入增长，但销售周期9–12个月。
  3. AI实施集中在数据和运营支持系统，更利好集成商和超大云。
  4. AI治理可能占软件总支出的7–8%。
  5. 约20%的合同价值是可变的。

Analyst-led • 29 Oct 2026

Companies: SAP DE, ORCL US

Milan Parmar (MP) COO, Managed Services at Telefonaktiebolaget LM Ericsson

Philip Atkinson (PA) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • SAP (ETR: SAP) and IFS overlaps, niches and spend trajectory for asset-intensive ERP (enterprise resource planning) systems
  • AI implementation and who wins near-term priorities
  • Structural drivers and pricing models – navigating modern software contracts

Key Insights

  • Budgets are expected to become more selective. IFS expansion will be predicated on a customer's organic growth while SAP enjoys migration related uplifts. Current existing customer demand supports mid 20% IFS revenue growth, albeit with 9-12-month sales cycles going forwards, delaying revenue conversion
  • AI implementation is focusing on data and operational support systems, heavily favouring systems integrators, consultants and hyperscalers over independent software vendors. Companies looking at operational workflow optimisation and bottom-line impact are more inclined to look towards IFS
  • Governance, such as IFS' AI harness, is expected to be restricted to IFS-specific users on vertical-only applications, with hyperscalers encroaching with horizontal governance. AI governance can encompass 7-8% of total software spend
  • Roughly 20% of the current contract value is variable and linked to AI. Although AI functionality is largely un-monetised today, it's expected to become monetised in the near-to-mid-term, driving higher variability in the contract value
