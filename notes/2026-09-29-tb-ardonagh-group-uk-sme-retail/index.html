Companies: AJG US

Greig Marshall (GM) Senior Claims Executive at Marsh & McLennan Companies Inc (Marsh)

Max Harper (MH) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Deep dive on Ardonagh Group's UK retail and SME offering, anticipating success of inorganic growth actions, including implications from the merger between Atlanta and Markerstudy
  • Revenue outlook, looking at opportunities to expand customer value while keeping them sticky at the same time
  • Claims management process, focusing on levers to keep costs down and boost EBITDA margins
  • Competitive landscape, focusing on opportunities to gain market share through organic and inorganic methods

Key Insights

  • Around 25% of Ardonagh's book is UK retail and SME, with motor dominant at c65%, and 15% each for property and liability, with remainder being more minor or speciality lines. Expert doesn't see these numbers shifting significantly
  • 23% stake in Markerstudy has yielded little visible synergy or market impact, with no clear strategic rationale communicated internally or externally to justify the transaction
  • Towergate brand became "toxic" due to poor service reputation, driving rebrand to Everywhen. New name lacks meaningful market recognition or substance behind it, potentially requiring a large acquisition to build scale
  • Expert believes AI could disrupt upwards of 40% of Ardonagh's business, particularly noting AI's misuse in UK courts as an unexpected real-world financial and operational risk indicator
  • Reductions in claims may signal a shift to outsourcing claims services. Shifting to third-party administrators typically saves c15% on base costs, though it damages client relationships and brand reputation due to quality issues, making cost-savings questionable
