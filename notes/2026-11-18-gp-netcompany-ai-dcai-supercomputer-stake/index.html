核心摘要

  1. 前Netcompany顾问认为，丹麦公共部门IT招标在未来2-3年仍将以“工时加许可费”模式为主，竞争格局和定价模式的转变可能需要约2-4年。
  2. 短期内AI带来的效率提升将首先转化为供应商更高的利润率，随后竞争会压低软件价格，平台和经常性许可收入将变得更重要；公共部门出于风险控制更偏好固定费用和许可，而非按价值定价。
  3. 招标评分中价格权重通常约30%-80%，过往业绩和团队简历各约10%-20%，其余主要看项目计划和架构，Netcompany凭借长期政府业绩在信任维度得分较高。
  4. Netcompany宣布入股运营Gefion超算的DCAI 23.7%股权，可借此进入AI推理托管，并有望在本土基础设施上训练或微调模型；专家指出全球AI算力约75%在美国、欧洲仅约5%。
  5. 部分丹麦机构依法要求数据、AI推理和基础设施留在丹麦境内，AI推理继承数据的安全分级，这会决定哪些供应商有资格竞标，专家认为由丹麦控制的AI提供商出现是最确定的趋势。
  6. 目前AI活动最活跃的是市政层面，例如覆盖约90个市政的建筑许可系统（Deloitte重点竞标）和市民服务AI试点，中央政府层面尚缺乏整体计划；Halfspace等小型AI原生咨询公司正借小型试点切入。
  7. 在遗留系统迁移中，AI可使占约三分之一工作量的技术实现部分提速约5-10倍，分析阶段效率提升约20%-30%，整体项目成本或下降约30%；AI代理也可能直接访问数据库而绕过部分传统升级。
  8. 竞争方面，twoday擅长数据工程，Trifork在AI领域领先并已赢得政府共享AI助手项目；咨询公司需将新员工培训重点从编码和文档转向判断力、架构和安全原则。

SUMMARY

Overview

The expert discussed how AI could reshape Danish public-sector IT procurement, consulting economics and competition. The discussion also covered public spending, sovereign AI infrastructure, legacy-system modernization and talent needs. The expert drew on experience delivering public-sector IT projects and participating in tenders.

Key Insights

Public tenders are likely to retain time-and-materials and licensing arrangements in the near term. Fixed fees and licensing appear more compatible with public buyers’ preference for cost clarity and risk control than value-based pricing. The expert expects AI productivity gains to initially benefit vendors through higher margins. Over time, competition may lower software prices, while platforms and recurring licenses could become more important. Danish data-location requirements can determine which vendors qualify: some agencies require data, AI processing and infrastructure to remain within Denmark. The expert sees domestic AI capacity as strategically important. Netcompany’s planned stake in DCAI could provide a route into AI hosting and, potentially, models trained or adapted on domestic infrastructure. The expert considers a Danish-controlled AI provider likely, though not necessarily one using a Danish- developed model. The expert sees AI-native consultancies as credible public-sector challengers because they are less tied to legacy products. Netcompany’s established government track record remains a trust advantage. AI adoption is particularly active among municipalities, where smaller pilot projects create openings for specialist firms. Broader government-level plans were described as less developed. AI may either modernize legacy systems or let agents access underlying databases directly, reducing the need for some conventional upgrades. The expert said the outcome remains uncertain. Consulting firms may need to train new hires in judgment, architecture and security principles rather than primarily routine coding and documentation.
