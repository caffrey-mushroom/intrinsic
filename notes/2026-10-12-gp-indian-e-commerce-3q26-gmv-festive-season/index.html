SUMMARY

Overview

The discussion examined Amazon, Flipkart and Meesho during the third quarter of 2026, including GMV trends, festive demand, profitability, quick-commerce expansion and AI-enabled shopping. The expert described healthy underlying demand despite softer reported growth caused by the later festive calendar.

Growth is increasingly driven by more frequent, lower-value orders and expansion into non-metro markets. Platforms are using value assortments to acquire customers before major festivals, while premium electronics and fashion should support larger baskets during the festive quarter.

Key Insights

The expert argued that average order value is no longer the best measure of platform health. Orders and GMV per active user better indicate whether customers are becoming habitual. Festive demand was expected to be materially stronger, with growth concentrated at both the lowest and highest price points while mid-priced products face pressure. Profitability remained broadly stable quarter over quarter because blanket discounts were replaced by targeted bank offers, cashbacks and seller-funded promotions. Advertising revenue should more than offset some incremental logistics costs from expansion into smaller cities. Quick commerce can improve engagement and retention but risks cannibalizing planned, higher-value purchases unless assortment, pricing and delivery propositions are clearly separated. AI agents pose the greatest traffic and advertising risk to broad marketplaces because shoppers can compare products across platforms without beginning inside a marketplace application.
