Companies: AMZN US, UPS US, FDX US

Stuart Lund (SL) Independent Consultant

Isaiah Rodgers (IR) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Sustainability of operating margins following recent capacity rationalisation and building automation
  • United Parcel Service's (UPS's; NYSE: UPS) competitive dynamics against FedEx's (NYSE: FDX) network restructuring and Amazon's (NASDAQ: AMZN) entry into 3PL (third-party logistics)
  • Pricing power, surcharge complexity and retention strategies across account bases
  • Integrating international cross-border handling and global freight forwarding capabilities

Key Insights

  • B2B vs B2C parcel volume has flipped dramatically. Once 60/40 favouring B2B, it's now c60/40 favouring B2C, making SMB growth strategies structurally harder than management implies
  • Expert believes cost reductions regarding radio frequency identification are underappreciated. It is eliminating individual package scans across millions of daily shipments, creating significant per-unit handling savings beyond just customer visibility benefits
  • Middle-market shippers potentially pay double-digit percentage premiums per package vs enterprise customers, making them structurally more valuable despite lower individual volumes and negotiating leverage
  • Expert believes Amazon Supply Chain Services poses limited near-term threat to UPS or FedEx, but regional final-mile competitors such as OnTrac represent a more immediate, underappreciated pricing pressure
  • UPS Store franchise network combined with Happy Returns' software creates a reverse logistics moat that would require either massive capital investment or acquisition to meaningfully replicate competitively
