SHOP -18%: Not a good print - GMV missed Street and the bogeys at $119.1B (+29.5%), a second straight decel (from 34.8% in 1Q and 31.6% in 2Q) and the slowest growth since 1Q25, for a long built on GMV re-accelerating to ~33%. Revenue of $3.742B (+31.6%) was in line with Street, ending at least 12 straight quarters of revenue beats, and GP of $1.781B (+28.0%) was in line too, both well short of the ~+34-35% / +31-32% bogeys. The 4Q guide looks ok with the revenue bogey (low-thirties) but missed on GP (high-twenties vs. the ~+30-31% bogeys), with FCF margin guided flat y/y. Opex and FCF the two bright spots.

Positioning was LO-heavy (HFs lighter) but the GMV miss and decel (bulls wanted to see and accel) along with the GP miss will have faster money HFs staying away into the holiday season as consumer agentic buzz has slowed. Never want to see decel on a stock at 50x+. . Call at 9:30am

Bulls will point to revenue still growing ~2pts faster than GMV (3.14% of GMV vs. 3.09% a year ago), merchant solutions +35.5%, an MRR beat, a GMV 2-yr stack only ~0.7pt lower (61.5 vs. 62.2) on a 1.4pt tougher comp, opex at 32.4% of revenue vs. the 33-34% guide, a 22% FCF margin with $562M of buybacks, and a low-thirties 4Q revenue guide above Street’s +29.8% on a ~1pt easier comp.

Bears will lean on GMV slowing for a second straight quarter to +29.5% and missing Street for the first time since 1Q25, the end of at least 12 straight revenue beats, GP +28.0% vs. the +31-32% bogeys with a high-twenties 4Q GP guide vs. the ~+30-31% bogeys, subscription GM down ~262bps y/y, and FCF margin guided flat y/y.

$92.01B).

+31.0%). 2-yr stack 63.1 vs. 64.7 in 2Q.
