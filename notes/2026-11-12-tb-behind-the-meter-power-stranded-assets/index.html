原标题：Power & Utilities Sector – Behind-the-meter Generation Economics & Stranded Asset Risks

#power #datacenter #energy

Third Bridge · 2026-11-12

核心摘要

  1. 曾在xAI负责发电业务的专家认为，2026年11月中期选举民主党拿下参众两院后，已取得土地、空气和施工许可的数据中心项目基本不受影响，尚无许可的项目则面临取消或延期风险；许可审批时间可能拉长，但空气许可标准（NOx约2ppm、CO约2-2.5ppm）已很难再收紧。
  2. GE燃气轮机订货位价格已从每兆瓦170-190万美元上涨至约310万美元；有订货位但无许可的开发商可能退出，从而释放采购机会，并可能削弱GE Vernova、西门子、三菱的定价权。
  3. 算力成本极高，例如Anthropic为Colossus 1每月支付约11-12亿美元，Google为Colossus 2的部分容量每月支付约9.25亿美元，因此超大规模厂商当前最看重通电速度，对电价几乎不敏感。
  4. 表后发电目前以燃气轮机为主，其次是燃气往复式内燃机，再搭配储能；燃料电池开始进入组合，某客户在现场部署了约700MW的Bloom Energy燃料电池，但燃料电池无法跟随负荷调节。
  5. 电网大型发电机组并网协议（LGIA）通常需要3-5年，最差可达10年；车载临时发电可在约6个月内部署，但许可通常只允许现场使用1年，加装SCR脱硝装置后可延期或转为永久，简单循环机组从到货到首次点火约7个月。
  6. 专家咨询过的一份表后PPA价格约为每兆瓦时125美元，燃料成本转嫁给数据中心，按可用率而非发电量付费，并配置约30%的冗余容量；部分PPA已开始写入热耗率保证条款。
  7. 长期来看，“通电速度优先”将让位于“低成本、高效率”，表后市场将像ISO市场一样只有具备经济性的机组才会被调度，目前的溢价PPA价格将面临下行压力；数据中心PUE规划区间约为1.3-1.7。
  8. 数据中心一旦接入电网就可能撤出表后合同，造成搁浅资产风险；专家建议表后项目同步排队申请LGIA，以便将来转为向电网售电，大量高热耗率的过渡设备届时也将与电网侧机组直接竞争。

Agenda

  • Hyperscaler procurement criteria for behind-the-meter and onsite generation solutions
  • Unit economics and reliability comparison of grid-scale vs modular, distributed energy options
  • Potential stranded asset risk for behind-the-meter generation, including concerns about early counterparty exits

Key Insights

  • Post-midterm elections, data centre projects without land, air or construction permits face cancellation risk. Projects with aforementioned permits are likely insulated from regulatory headwinds
  • Three-plus-year interconnection queues and high compute costs mean data centres prize behind-the-meter solutions with lowest time-to-power. Currently smaller gas-turbines and reciprocating engines, with fuel cells are emerging. Wheeled generation can be deployed in six months, while selective catalytic reduction emission-reduction unit installations extending temporary power solutions into permanent ones
  • Expert believes that speed-to-power emphasis will be replaced long-term by preference for low-cost, efficient generators, potentially diluting current premium power purchase agreement pricing – anecdotally, up to USD 125 per megawatt-hour. Heat rate guarantees in recent power purchase agreements signal growing emphasis on factors beyond speed and reliability
  • Behind-the-meter data centre power purchase agreements are largely priced on availability, with long-term trend towards capacity-based payments as norm to secure "in the money" status for generator's project
