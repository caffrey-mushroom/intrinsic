  5. 非AI业务LoRa同步创纪录：Q3营收6,800万美元（QoQ +18%，YoY +69%，FY27末年化约3.21亿美元，长期指引30% CAGR），且目前几乎完全由工业/商业需求驱动，Amazon Sidewalk（Ring新传感器正扩至加/墨及欧/澳/日）仅占高个位数，构成未计价的独立增长缓冲。

SMTC had another very, very strong quarter.

They beat the higher guidance that they just gave last month, guided Q4 above expectations, and said that they are confident they will double data center rev again next year to over $1b.

This market is relentless on AI stocks. Every rip gets sold. And SMTC was not immune to this.

Earlier this year I first laid out my thesis on SMTC when it was in the 70s.

It peaked at $201 in October.

Today it closed at $86.46.

The roller coaster in the entire AI infra trade will be one for the history books.

But while the AI trade is unwinding, we still have work to do.

Because it is going to get to a point where the valuations become way too oversold, and the strongest companies with the most durable earnings will climb their way up.

Let’s see if SMTC could be one of those.

This was Semtech’s 11th straight quarter of rev growth and the operating leverage is starting to show up in a meaningful way.

Q3 rev - $423m (+24% Qoq, +58% YoY)

Adjusted gross margin - 59.3% (64% excluding low margin cellular mobile business they are selling)

Adjusted operating margin - 33%

Adjusted EPS $1.17 (+144% YoY)

Net debt down to $252m, net leverage down to .7x (it was 10.5x in FY24!)

$55m FCF while putting cash into inventory and supply agreements for FY28

Q4 guide of $480m (+13% QoQ, +75% YoY)

When I first saw that Q4 guide I was a bit disappointed because its slower % growth than Q3. What’s interesting is that while Q4 is a 14-week quarter (and you would expect that to increase rev) they said that the 14th week adds ~7% to Opex while give it almost no topline boost to DC or LoRa rev because these businesses dont ship linearly week to week.

Also, Q4 guide assumes they own the cellular module business for the full quarter. If it closes midquarter as expected, Q4 rev will come in below $480m. Need to remember that for the net release. EPS wont be affected and operating margins will jump, but headlign reading algos could misread it as a miss

Let’s start with the Data center

$145m in rev (+45% QoQ, +158% YoY) and is guided up another 40% sequentially. ~$812m annualized run rated exiting Fy27.

Back in August they said that FY28 was already over 70% booked which is why they feel comfortable guiding FY28 data center rev past $1b.

Let’s look at some of their products and key updates from this call:

  • FiberEdge (TIAs & Drivers). They grew their 800G TIA market share from 18% to 50% purely on performance. The 1.6T is now ramping quickly (combined 1.6T products already crossed 50% data center rev). We know that TIAs have been a constraint for the rest of the market and SMTC is one of the key players here to fill that need. They also locked in a multi year wafer deal with Tower semi that covers their supply plan past FY30- CopperEdge (linear redrivers). They are ramping active copper cables (ACC) with a lead hyper and just won two more platform with them. They also won a second leading hyper for an onboard/line card redriver on a major inference platform (ramping H1 FY28).- Photonics - Expanding capacity 3-4x by year end (support $25m/q run rate) and another 3-4x next year. big big expansion

LoRa

Data center is definitely the center of the thesis, but LoRa is a substantial second growth driver.

Also a record quarter. $68m (+ 18% QoQ, +69% YoY).

Something interesting is that Amazon Sidewalk (Ring’s new LoRa sensors which are now expanding into Canada, Mexico, and soon Europe/Austrial/Japan) is still a tiny part of LoRa rev (high single digit percent). LoRa is hitting record highs almost entirely on industrial/commercial. So Amazon is mostly unpriced upside.

If the market really wants to continue to punish AI data center exposure and crush those multiples, how about companies that have a growing, major segment that is not exposed to hyperscaler capex?

LoRa will be a ~$321m annualized run rate exiting FY27.

And their long term guide is a 30% CAGR for LoRa.

Not bad.

Besides just a complete repricing of the AI trade and concern around the speed of the data center buildout and financing and hyperscaler capex etc. etc.

There are some company specific risks as well.

They are expanding their photonics fab meaningfully. Remember, 3-4x this year and another 3-4x next year. Upfront ramp costs are keeping Q4 ex-module gross margins from jumpiogn more than 30bps and any yield hiccups would drag margins longer than expected.

There is also a potential 3nm DSP bottleneck. They said that they havent seen any pushouts from Thai assembly floods or 3nm DSP shortages, but 1.6T optical modules need DSPs to ship. If module makers get bottlenecked on DSPs, it could eventually slow orders for their TIAs.

Valuation:

Mkt cap - ~$8.46B

EV - $8.72B

FY27E - $3.72 adjusted EPS (~23.2x)

P/E on annualized Q4 run rate - ~16.3x

FY30E mgmt framework - ~6.8x EPS and ~7.8x FY30E EV/FCF

I personally want to buy more SMTC, but I still hold my concerns that Ive shared over the past few weeks about AI infra names and sizing in for my port.

Given what we are seeing today I still lean towards the possibility that QQQ tests $590 and SMH $375. I could absolutely be wrong, and I still maintain AI infra exposure just incase I am. But when I think about adding risk, today, that is roughly the level I want to see.

If we get down to those levels, names like SMTC would probably see another 15-20% drop.

That low 70’s level was where it based for a long time when I first took my position. I would see that as a potential area it could find support again.
