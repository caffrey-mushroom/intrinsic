Agenda

  • Workflow automation attach rates and ARR impact
  • Expansion for Materialise, such as Cardiac Simulation and TAVR (transcatheter aortic valve replacement) planning pipeline
  • Post-purchase sales motion, including utilisation and renewals

Key Insights

  • Patient care and certification are material differentiators, enabling 90%-plus customer retention and broad, defensible pricing premiums. Barriers to replicating software quality are high and expected to be unjustifiable for free solution providers
  • Hospitals are increasingly viewing Materialise as a must-have, shortening sales cycles, but it must overcome current "additional spend" characterisation. Expansion towards personalised health is typically a driver for increased selling opportunities
  • Pricing typically increases with inflation, with additional module uptake every 2-3 years on average. Typically, these are single modules carrying a 10-20% ACV expansion. If budgets tighten, expansion will likely be delayed but is still expected to occur
  • AI can be embedded across nearly all Materialise workflows, but ACV impact is uncertain and grounded in clinician ROI. Expert expects overall growth to accelerate beyond recent 10-12%
