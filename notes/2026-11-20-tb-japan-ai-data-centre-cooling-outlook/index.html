原标题：Japanese Data Centre Cooling – AI Data Centre Outlook (Conducted in Japanese)

#thermal #datacenter

Third Bridge · 2026-11-20

核心摘要

  1. Vertiv日本高级工程师（曾任职施耐德电气）表示，一台约1.5MW冷水机组的调试和负载测试约需40-60人日，大型CRAH约15-20人日，冷冻水列间空调约2-3人日、制冷剂型约5人日。
  2. 调试工程师费率约2万日元/小时，即每人每天约15万-16万日元，较3-4年前的约1.5万日元/小时上涨约30%；由于日本数据中心建设热潮和工程师短缺，专家预计未来2-3年涨幅相当甚至更大。
  3. 设备通常每半年例行检查一次，压缩机等制冷剂设备每年做一次含法定检查的详细检查；运动部件更换周期约7-10年，精密空调多为整机更换而非更换部件。
  4. 液冷系统维护因CDU停机会导致其下所有服务器停止冷却，需要关停服务器，停机协调成本上升；冷水机组越来越多采用全铝微通道换热器，铜用量将逐步下降。
  5. 冷水机组核心部件丹佛斯磁悬浮Turbocore压缩机全球紧缺，交期从约三年前的一个月延长到数月，未来2-3年竞争将更激烈，可能达到半年；其他关键部件交期约3-4周。
  6. 因客户原因延期或取消的项目比例低于10%；在日元贬值影响下，部件采购价格持续上涨。
  7. 液冷CDU市场由Vertiv（CoolTera）和施耐德（Motivair）两家主导，二者已与服务器厂商合作制定行业标准，日本厂商几乎没有进入机会。
  8. 超大规模云厂商倾向于直接采用Vertiv、施耐德等海外厂商的整套方案，随着液冷普及，一次侧冷水机组也将被海外机型替代，大金、三菱电机等日本厂商将局限于地方政府机房等小规模边缘项目。

Key Insights

  • Commissioning man-hours for 1.5MW chiller are 40-60 man-days, while those for large air-handling units are 15-20 man-days. Routine inspections are carried out twice a year. Given the engineer shortage, daily rate for engineers increased by c30% over the past 2-3 years, to JPY 150,000-160,000. For liquid-cooling systems, man-hours for maintenance have increased due to server shutdowns for adjustments and downtime
  • Lead time for Danfoss’s magnetic levitation compressors for chillers has increased from one month to several months. Project cancellation rate due to customer circumstances is <10%
  • Hyperscalers are adopting turnkey solutions from overseas manufacturers such as Vertiv and Schneider, and Japanese manufacturers are expected to see very limited adoption in large-scale data centres going forward. With CDUs spreading with the shift to liquid cooling, primary-side chillers are expected to be replaced by overseas-made models. Japanese manufacturers will be limited to small-scale projects, such as those for local governments

00:12:26] Do the maintenance, inspection and replacement of key components in data centre cooling equipment differ between air-cooled and liquid-cooled systems?

Expert [00:12:26]: Fundamentally, if you ask whether the machinery itself differs significantly, whilst the appearance does change – it might have pumps running or fans fitted, for instance – I don’t think there’s a major difference. One significant difference, however, is that air-cooled systems use air to cool the servers. As long as the airflow can be controlled, the surrounding equipment can support the cooling process. This means that, for example, if you shut down a single unit to inspect or repair it, the process can be carried out relatively easily and smoothly. With liquid cooling, however, as cooling fluid has to be circulated directly to the chips, if you shut down the CDU or any other equipment responsible for circulating the cooling fluid, cooling for all the servers below it stops completely. This means you’re forced to shut down all the servers or halt their operation entirely. This is a major difference in terms of maintenance services; my impression is that, when providing these services, we often have to go into considerable detail with the customer, and the costs associated with downtime – such as actually shutting down servers or waiting for them to resume operation – are on the rise.
