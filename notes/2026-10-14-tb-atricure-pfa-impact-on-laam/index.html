Agenda

  • Current clinical practice usage patterns for AtriClip and the shift from standalone to concomitant use alongside open-heart surgery
  • Expected impact of PFA (pulsed field ablation) adoption on hybrid LAAM (left atrial appendage management) procedures
  • Clinical evaluation of AtriCure's (NASDAQ: ATRC) pain management portfolio (Cryosphere Max, Cryo-ICE) across minimally invasive, thoracic and Maze procedures
  • Competitive landscape for LAAM devices, including Penditure and emerging entrants such as Edwards (NYSE: EW)

Key Insights

  • Reimbursement remains the primary barrier to universal AtriClip adoption. Once Medicare covers prophylactic clipping, private insurers typically follow, potentially unlocking mass adoption across cardiac surgery
  • Electrophysiologists hesitate to refer patients to surgeons in the fee-for-service US healthcare culture, preferring to use PFA as an alternative tool. Convergent hybrid procedures have seen meaningful referral declines since PFA's rise, although long-term PFA durability remains unproven, potentially swinging referrals back towards surgical approaches within years
  • Edwards Ecliptis launch could realistically capture double-digit market share within 12 months by leveraging existing structural heart sales relationships, particularly through bundling with popular devices such as Sapien 3
  • Cryoanalgesia's biggest adoption barrier for sternotomy cases is procedural time, potentially adding 25 extra minutes per case, making it difficult to justify despite strong narcotic-reduction and recovery benefits
