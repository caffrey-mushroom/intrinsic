2026.11.18 | TB | FORM | FormFactor HBM Probe Card Outlook

原标题：FormFactor – HBM Growth & Capacity Expansion

#semicap #memory

Third Bridge · 2026-11-18

核心摘要

  1. 前FormFactor定制设计工程副总裁表示，其在任期间新设计中约60%为DRAM、10%为HBM、30%为SoC，存储设计数量约为SoC的四倍；获英伟达认证后SoC设计占比可能上升。
  2. HBM需要对每颗裸片做全速测试并测试整个堆叠，因此所需探针卡比DDR5多50%-100%；HBM堆叠卡并行度、引脚数和探针密度更低（如约300个DUT并行，而DDR5卡约1500个DUT、约13万引脚），反而更易制造、良率更高，是FormFactor近1.5-2年毛利率显著提升的主要原因。
  3. HBM4接口宽度翻倍至2048位对探针卡影响不大，生产测试只压缩到约16个通道；DRAM裸片修复前良率为零，修复后约80%-90%，堆叠后才能发现的缺陷主要是TSV和互连问题。
  4. SmartMatrix加ATRE可将DDR5晶圆测试从2-4次触压减少到1次，每次触压约45分钟，是降低客户测试成本、提高吞吐量的重要杠杆，目前已成行业标准。
  5. GPU探针卡项目从首批出货到稳定量产通常只需3-6个月；高级MEMS垂直探针卡价格约为悬臂卡的2倍，ASIC引脚超过5000-10000个时基本需要垂直或MEMS垂直技术，探针卡通常因产品停产而非磨损而更换。
  6. 资格认证中首家供应商优势明显，测试程序会针对其探针卡调校；客户普遍采用双供应商，若后期认证失败，PCB重新设计约需6周，可能失去量产订单和复购；HBM4高并行全速测试可能需要转向垂直技术。
  7. Farmers Branch新厂目标到2027年底支持年4亿美元探针卡收入，大客户可能需约两个月生产数据完成认证，供应紧张时认证会放宽；专家认为主要风险是Technoprobe（预计2027年HBM收入至少3000万欧元）和MJC若证明具备HBM4全速测试能力将带来压力，目前FormFactor在该领域几乎没有竞争。

Agenda

  • FormFactor's (NASDAQ: FORM) positioning across HBM (high-bandwidth memory), conventional DRAM and foundry probe card markets
  • HBM4 testing complexity, SmartMatrix differentiation and Farmers Branch capacity expansion to support growing probe card demand
  • Competitive dynamics vs Technoprobe (BIT: TPRO) and Micronics Japan (TSE: 6871), including implications of FormFactor's Teradyne (NASDAQ: TER) partnership
  • Medium-to-long-term outlook

Key Insights

  • HBM could require 50-100% more probe cards than Double Data Rate 5, as at-speed and stack testing increase test intensity, while lower probe density can improve manufacturing yields and FormFactor's margins
  • GPU probe-card programmes can ramp to recurring production within 3-6 months, while advanced Micro-Electro-Mechanical System cards may command roughly 2x cantilever pricing, with vertical technologies increasingly favoured above 5,000-10,000 pins
  • Advanced tester resource enhancement can reduce Double Data Rate 5 testing from 2-4 touchdowns to one, with each touchdown taking c45 minutes, demonstrating customer throughput and test cost advantages
  • Probe-card qualification strongly favours the first supplier, with printed circuit board redesigns taking roughly six weeks and late qualification failures potentially causing FormFactor to lose production orders and reorders
