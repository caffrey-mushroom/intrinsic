Companies: ELV US

Jason Adams (JA) VP, Client Engagement at Certilytics Inc

Bryce Victor (BV) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Payment integrity market share shifts, catalysed by legacy incumbents such as Cotiviti and Optum (NYSE: UNH) integrating AI capabilities against aggressive disruption from native AI platforms such as Machinify
  • Primary defensive moats protecting Lyric's embedded footprint as payer focus shifts fundamentally from post-pay recovery to proactive pre-pay error prevention
  • Customer concentration risk profile and underlying software stickiness driven by entrenched legacy penetration
  • Analysis of synergies and margin expansion opportunities through Magnum Transaction Sub's acquisitions of ClaimShark and Concert
  • USD 5bn sale exploration outlook and growth opportunities under new ownership

Key Insights

  • ClaimsXten, comprising 85-90% of Lyric's revenue, dominates primary edits with nine of top 10 US payers and 80% of Blues plans, leaving limited room for further legacy offering growth. Future growth will be driven by Lyric42
  • Expert sees substantial opportunity in targeting smaller and regional payers that struggle to build in-house capabilities, allowing for a more aggressive pricing model
  • Machinify achieved via acquisitions what Lyric is pursuing via partnerships with Lyric42. Lyric's orchestration layer may unify products faster without full M&A integration, but is at more risk to large losses from partnership discontinuations
  • Expert believes Lyric's competitive moat lies in large customer network and superiority in capturing fringe edits "where a lot of dollars are made and lost", which native-AI can't manage well
  • Lyric's USD 5bn valuation, 20x EBITDA, appears inflated given recent acquisition volatility, with synergies from ClaimShark and Concert not yet reflected in performance
