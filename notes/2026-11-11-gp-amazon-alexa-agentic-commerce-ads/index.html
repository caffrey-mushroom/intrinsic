  8. 广告方面，搜索广告位会部分转移到agent体验中，未来几个季度增速可能承压，专家没有确认广告收入能维持高十几个百分点的增长；但长期只要亚马逊掌握客户，agent模块和通知等新广告位会带来增量。

SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, a former Amazon leader in AI and seller experiences, discussed how Amazon is integrating shopping agents into its customer experience, its resistance to third-party horizontal agents, and the potential impact of agentic shopping on advertising. The central strategic issue is who owns the customer relationship as shopping shifts toward AI-assisted discovery and purchasing.

Key Insights

The Alexa rebrand signals confidence in the shopping assistant’s early results, but the name itself matters less than deeper integration into search and new capabilities such as price alerts and purchases triggered by price conditions. Amazon is likely to pursue broad adoption and compete aggressively as shopping habits evolve. The expert characterized this period as pivotal for the future of e-commerce. The expert expects horizontal agents to pose a greater immediate challenge to Google’s role in product discovery than to Amazon’s marketplace. They may direct shoppers to products, while established retailers retain many final purchases. Amazon’s key advantage is customer trust in its fulfillment and delivery experience. Horizontal agents must prove shoppers can rely on their recommendations, payments, and third-party sellers. Amazon is unlikely to integrate with external agents unless its own growth slows or customers increasingly shift to those platforms. If it does participate, it may seek to retain control of checkout. Text and voice shopping are offensive moves to test new customer behaviors, not merely defensive responses to competitors. Advertising growth may face near-term disruption as placements shift between search and agent experiences. Longer term, the expert sees further ad opportunities if Amazon continues to own the customer relationship.
