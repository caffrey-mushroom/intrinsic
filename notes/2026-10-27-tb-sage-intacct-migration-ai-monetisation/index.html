Agenda

  • AI uptake and evolving product landscape amid AI-native solutions
  • Sage Intacct (LON: SGE) competitive dynamics and selection criteria, including product parity
  • Existing customer growth – farming accounts for monetisation

Key Insights

  • Sage Intacct represents only 2-5% of Sage France's customer base. Expert projects a modest growth to 7-10% over the next four years, with CFOs actively avoiding enterprise resource planning changes before France's September 2027 e-invoicing issuance deadline
  • Pennylane poses an existential competitive threat in France, migrating customers from Sage in under one week using automated file imports vs Sage Intacct's 1-6-month migration timeline
  • Expert believes FY26 organic revenue growth will land around 10.5%, above management's cautious "over 9%" guidance, but expects it to decelerate to c6.5% by FY30 due to rising churn, given opportunistic investments neglecting slower growth regions
  • Resellers in France capture 35-45% of Sage Intacct's ARR on average, with early incentive deals reaching as high as 80% annual contract value, significantly compressing Sage's net revenue per customer acquired
