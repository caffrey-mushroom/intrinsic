  2. 四季度整体增速预计超过8%，剔除政治广告后约为5.6%；若同时剔除政治和AI公司广告，基础增长仅为低个位数，消费品和汽车最疲弱。
  3. 政治广告总额约138亿美元；9月下旬至11月初AI公司约向市场投入8亿美元，ChatGPT每周广告支出约500万至1000万美元，主要投向体育直播等线性电视，11月起已明显回落。
  4. 假日季广告增长预期从6%-6.5%下调至4.5%，促销折扣深度已达约60%（去年约40%），零售商利润率承压，高端客户增速也比去年低约200-220个基点。
  5. 12月数据显示数字广告增长6.4%、CTV增长20%、线性电视下降5.5%，整体约增长4%-4.5%；媒体公司正通过CTV与线性电视打包销售对冲线性受众流失。

  7. Amazon广告放缓更多归因于高基数和消费者更谨慎，Netflix广告下调主要是库存受限和向广告版迁移较慢，而非需求不足；Reddit转向登录用户有利于长期变现。
  8. 大型代理集团面临客户结构偏向消费品和汽车的拖累，以及AI提效冲击按工时计费模式的结构性压力；2027年预计整体媒体增长约4%、数字5%-5.5%、线性电视（剔除政治）下降2%-3%。

SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, an independent media agency leader with experience at large agency groups, discussed client advertising trends across television, digital platforms, retail media, and agency services. The agency works across sectors including automotive, consumer goods, finance, technology, and entertainment. The central themes were strong reported growth distorted by political and AI-related advertising, softer underlying demand, and uncertainty about budgets for the next year.

Key Insights

Advertising growth looks much weaker when political and AI-company spending are excluded. The expert estimated underlying growth at low single digits, with pressure most evident in consumer goods, automotive, and some retail and financial advertisers. The expert sees higher interest rates and consumers becoming more cautious as important demand headwinds. Holiday spending expectations were reduced, while deeper promotions suggest pressure on retailers’ margins. AI tools are improving digital advertising performance, but search growth is slowing as consumer attention spreads across new services. The expert does not expect AI agents to abruptly displace retail media; future advertising or sponsored-product models may help these services generate revenue. Large agency groups face both an unfavorable mix of clients and structural pressure: AI can reduce labor needs, challenging business models based on billing staff hours. The moderator pursued this point, and the expert agreed that agencies may struggle to capture efficiency gains without reducing fees. The expert expects advertisers to remain cautious about next-year budgets and described the outlook as uncertain.
