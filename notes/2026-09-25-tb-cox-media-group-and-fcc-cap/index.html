原标题：Cox Media Group – Will FCC Ownership Cap Changes Lead to Long-awaited Sale?

#media

Third Bridge · 2026-09-25

Analyst-led • 17 Sep 2026

Companies: SBGI US, SSP US, GTN US, NXST US

Nathan Reed (NR) Independent Consultant

John Conca (JC) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • CMG's (Cox Media Group's) operating environment assessment, including core and political ad revenue outlook
  • Competitive dynamics with Nexstar (NASDAQ: NXST), Gray Media (NYSE: GTN), Sinclair (NASDAQ: SBGI), Scripps (NASDAQ: SSP) and others
  • Potential for sale to be completed, given FCC (Federal Communications Commission) ownership changes, noting potential suitors
  • 12-18-month outlook, highlighting digital sales growth and ability to offset declining broadcast revenues

Key Insights

  • Regarding the potential CMG sale, radio co-ownership may be deterring TV-focused acquirers unfamiliar with the medium, despite radio's synergistic value in cross-promoting audiences between broadcast TV and radio assets
  • Sinclair's strategy of regionalising or nationalising properties and eliminating local footprints directly causes audience erosion, giving competitors with strong local presence a measurable and sustained competitive advantage over Sinclair
  • Regarding ATSC 3.0 adoption, OEMs aren't yet required to standardise the technology, and the National Association of Broadcasters hasn't unified around mandating it, delaying mainstream market penetration despite infrastructure already being partially built out
  • Gray Media's decision to drop its CBS affiliation in Atlanta while building Assembly studios and launching PeachtreeTV represents operators realising independent content creation outweighs costly network affiliation fees
  • Traditional broadcast's attribution gap is closing. New tools can now aggregate campaign performance across TV, radio and digital, giving advertisers accountability that wasn't consistently possible even 10 years ago
