SUMMARY

Overview

The discussion examined optical circuit switching, co-packaged optics, optical modules, and AI data-center network architecture. Google is presented as the only major customer with long-term commercial OCS deployment, while NVIDIA is expected to accelerate adoption through its planned Feynman architecture.

China remains primarily in the planning, testing, and early deployment stage. Domestic suppliers nevertheless benefit from strong US demand and hold important positions across OCS manufacturing, precision optics, MEMS chips, and optical modules.

Key Insights

NVIDIA could become the largest catalyst for OCS by using co-packaged optics within racks and OCS for rack-to-rack and campus-scale connectivity. OCS reduces optical-electrical conversions, latency, and power consumption while remaining compatible with future transmission upgrades. Co-packaged optics may disrupt pluggable optical modules, although mass-production timing remains uncertain. Near-term AI construction is therefore supporting strong 1.6T module demand. China faces adoption barriers from limited technical expertise and the need to redesign networking, cooling, power, and facility architecture. MEMS is the proven OCS technology, but liquid-crystal systems could gain meaningful share where reliability is prioritized.
