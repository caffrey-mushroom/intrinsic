Agenda

  • Commvault's (NASDAQ: CVLT) customer demand dynamics as clients shift from on-premises backup to hybrid cloud cyber resilience platforms
  • Commercial adoption momentum, subscription ARR growth and net retention dynamics
  • Competitive displacement dynamics within mid-market and global enterprise accounts
  • Go-to-market execution and strategic co-selling alliances with cybersecurity providers
  • Gross margin resilience and operating margin expansion opportunities

Key Insights

  • Commvault's SaaS ARR growth is driven primarily by enterprise cloud data estates doubling every 3-5 years, with AI expected to accelerate data growth even further beyond historical rates
  • Air Gap Protect represents a significant growth driver but carries lower margins, given that Commvault must pay hyperscalers for storage, creating a challenging margin-mix balancing act
  • Pure products from cyber resilience portfolio such as Cleanroom and Cloud Rewind contribute "still very, very minimal" revenue across the whole industry, with vendors largely using them as conversation-openers rather than material revenue drivers
  • Rubrik's higher annual spend on SG&A and R&D than Commvault, compounded over 5-6 years, has created a substantial competitive advantages in go-to-market momentum and channel investment
  • Veritas and NetBackup renewal cycle over the next 12-24 months represents a significant displacement opportunity, with both Commvault and Rubrik actively targeting those customers for competitive wins
