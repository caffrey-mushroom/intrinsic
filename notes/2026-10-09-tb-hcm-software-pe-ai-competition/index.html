Agenda

Key Insights

  • Theme across HCM industry is "pressure", given macroeconomic, technological and competitive challenges, and those along with complex solutions have actually contributed to higher retention rates of 92-94% for enterprise players
  • Consequently, new logo business is harder to come by, especially given that larger growth opportunities have been based on expansions within existing customers, noting the importance of new offerings, including AI, adding to per employee per month. Some offerings are initially provided for free with payback over a four-year contract term
  • Category leaders over the next few years could include ADP with its extensive product set and customer opportunities, Workday with its potential in the learning management area and smaller players such as Rippling, HiBob and Gusto with strength in technology and marketing
  • In terms of laggards, expert believes the "Ps" – Paylocity, Paycom and Paychex – are "going to struggle", with "a lot of business moving from them"
