原标题：AI in Webhosting – What's Table-stakes? Assessing AI Expectations & Web-hosting AI Monetisation

#internet #hosting #agents

Third Bridge · 2026-11-03

核心摘要

  1. 前Ionos高管认为，欧洲数据主权虽有地缘顺风，但对建站托管客户的流向影响有限，易用性才是首要因素。
  2. Lovable、Bolt等AI建站工具已拿下约70%至80%的新网站增量，价格虽大幅补贴但客户粘性高，收入可能流失但客户不易流失。
  3. MCP连接目前是短期差异化，但很快会变成必备基础设施，没有MCP的托管商会在AI agent和合作生态中彻底“隐身”。
  4. 专家认为聊天机器人、AI前台、个人助理agent等AI增值产品是ARPU提升10%至15%的最清晰路径，执行好可到20%。
  5. 单靠AI建站本身无法驱动变现，合作伙伴型AI产品预计对利润率中性。

Analyst-led • 23 Oct 2026

Companies: OVH FR, GDDY US, WIX US

Elias Rendón Benger (ERB) CEO at Hypernode

Philip Atkinson (PA) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Solution packaging and pricing targeting AI upsell
  • Customer profiling and expected volume contributions from AI web-builders
  • Capacity expectations, build-out and acquisitions

Key Insights

  • European data sovereignty trends, despite geopolitical tailwinds, show limited meaningful impact on web hosting customer flows and preference, with ease of use as the primary driver
  • AI website builders such as Lovable and Bolt capture roughly 70-80% of new website volume growth. Despite heavily subsidised pricing, customer lock-in is high, so revenue may churn, but customers are unlikely to churn
  • Model context protocol connectivity is currently a short-term competitive differentiator, but it will quickly become mandatory infrastructure. Hosting companies without model context protocol connectivity become invisible to AI agents and partner ecosystems entirely
  • Expert believes AI-driven upsell products – chatbots, AI receptionists and personal assistant agents – represent the clearest path to 10-15% ARPU growth, potentially reaching 20% with strong execution. Web-building alone will not drive monetisation. Partnership-led AI products are expected to be margin neutral
