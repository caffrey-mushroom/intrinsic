Agenda

  • Monday.com's (NASDAQ: MNDY) growth, retention and continued move upmarket
  • Sidekick, AI agents and emerging AI monetisation
  • Shift from work management to a broader AI work platform
  • Expansion across CRM, service and development
  • Competitive positioning and potential for AI to reaccelerate long-term growth

Key Insights

  • Monday.com's core work management business still accounts for c85% of revenue, with CRM contributing c10% and service remaining negligible, suggesting diversification efforts have yet to meaningfully impact revenue mix
  • Customer acquisition costs rose consistently during expert's tenure, reflecting diminishing returns from traditional acquisition channels and declining SEO effectiveness as users increasingly adopt AI-powered search
  • Monday.com's SMB-heavy customer base creates structural retention challenges, potentially limiting sustainable growth as customer acquisition becomes more expensive and enterprise penetration remains limited
  • Despite management attributing 17% of net new ARR to AI, expert cautions that this includes more than consumption-based revenue and may overstate actual AI adoption and monetisation
  • Expert anticipates revenue growth to moderate towards 15-17% over next 2-3 years, with recent workforce reductions and senior leadership departures raising further questions about Monday.com's ability to sustain longer-term growth ambitions
