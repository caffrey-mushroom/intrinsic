原标题：WMS Software Development Prospects amid AI Trends – Flux Information Technology (Conducted in Mandarin)

#software #china

Third Bridge · 2026-11-13

核心摘要

  1. 前富勒（Flux）产品经理表示，今年国内WMS采购以集团多仓整合项目居多，周期一般3个月至1年，增长集中在医药和离散制造（尤其是汽车与电子），电商、零售、快消和3PL订单减少。
  2. 替换需求约占订单的70%，包括替换Infor等外资产品、信创驱动的国产化以及替换唯智、TTX等国内软件；医药GMP仓库多为空白新增，很多药企仍用手工台账或ERP管库存。
  3. 富勒年增速约20-30%（专家早前估计30-40%），高于行业平均，商机主要来自口碑与老客户转介绍；其核心优势是产品高度可配置和拥有8-10年经验的资深顾问团队，近两年高增长也受益于唯智和TTX业务收缩。
  4. 收入结构上WMS约占70%以上，TMS、DataHub、OMS等其他产品线约20-30%；WMS中新签约约占一半，运维约20-30%，扩仓约10-20%，年运维费为累计合同额的6-10%。
  5. 集团客户首期合同通常低价甚至亏损拿单，依靠后续扩仓和运维盈利；集团项目回款分阶段进行，上线时间常延长30-40%，单仓扩仓费用约15万元，许可费首期约10-19万元，顾问人天单价约2000-2500元。
  6. 利润率逐年明显下降：疫情前基本没有60万元以下的WMS项目，如今也做20-30万元的小项目，同时顾问薪酬和社保成本上升；公司员工人数控制在900人以内。
  7. AI目前主要用于提升内部效率，部分编码工作从一天缩短到1-3小时，顾问文档、流程图和PRD也大量使用AI；Luma AI Agent用于运维问答和单据识别，库位优化、波次规则等核心功能尚未落地，对外收费案例很少。
  8. 专家认为AI原生WMS出现将是拐点式而非线性的冲击，可能对富勒等厂商造成致命打击，但预计至少需2-3年；同行业模板复用仅能节省约20-30%工作量，调研、配置和CSV验证难以省去；唯智、TTX对同类需求报价约低30%，如富勒报150万元而对手约110万元。

Agenda

  • China’s domestic WMS (warehouse management system) procurement demand and market growth potential, new warehouse construction, old system replacement and import substitution trends, plus Flux’s client acquisition and upsell opportunities
  • Flux’s Luma AI Agent – application progress across documentation processing, Q&A and task scheduling, incremental value relative to traditional solutions, client willingness to pay, plus scale growth trends
  • Improvements in implementation man-hours and multi-warehouse replication efficiency driven by AI-assisted development, configuration and operations, plus scope for converting reduced delivery costs into project profit margin
  • Strategic differences between Flux and key competitors such as VTradex and Deep Insights, plus industry landscape shifts

Key Insights

  • This year, WMS software procurement showed structural patterns: growth was concentrated in pharma and discrete manufacturing sectors, with replacement demand occupying some 70%. Pharmaceutical GMP warehouses were mostly greenfield projects, while demand from e-commerce and retail weakened
  • Flux grew faster than the industry average, helped partly by the contraction of competitors such as TTX. Its core advantages remain industry expertise, product configurability, a senior consulting team and a strong delivery reputation. It won its first order from group-type clients at a low price, relying on subsequent warehouse expansion and O&M to improve profitability. Flux is highly competitive in the discrete manufacturing and pharmaceutical sectors, with competitors generally quoting prices about 30% lower
  • AI is currently used mainly to improve internal efficiency: time for some coding tasks has been reduced from a day to 1-3 hours. It has not yet been applied to core business processes such as inventory optimisation, and there are few external paid use cases. AI-native systems may be nearing a tipping point, expected to materialise only after 2-3 years
  • WMS remains dependent on customisation. Template replication among same-industry companies saves only about 20-30% of the workload; research, configuration and CSV validation are hard to eliminate, and consultants remain the primary component of delivery costs
