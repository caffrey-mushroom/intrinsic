核心摘要

  1. R1 RCM前销售区域副总裁认为，医疗系统虽认可供应商整合的理念，但出于人手短缺、成本压力和Change Healthcare式事件的运营风险，实践中通常同时使用10–15家、有时多达30–40家RCM供应商。
  2. 端到端外包市场主要由R1和Ensemble主导，Ensemble在NPR约5亿至20–30亿美元的体系中合作成功率较高；这类合作通常需转聘员工、承担全部风险并提供首日折扣，期限至少约7年、多为10年，粘性很强。
  3. 提供模块化服务、能多点切入的厂商更受益，缺乏全国覆盖、仅做中后端服务的小型厂商处于劣势；Waystar被视为清算所型技术厂商，并不提供编码等后端RCM服务。
  4. 专家认为AI更适合增强而非取代人工，例如代理拨打支付方电话可省去每次30–45分钟的等待，但目前尚未见到完全自主的后端流程，始终需要人在回路，且受支付方与系统集成成本限制。
  5. 多数医院缺乏自建代理AI的资源，内部IT可能需要约3年才能完成，因此仍依赖带有代理能力的传统RCM厂商；不以技术优先的厂商已难以进入招标名单。
  6. R1此前依靠Cloudmed在收入完整性领域以“先落地再扩展”模式增长，如今推广FAIR操作系统以整合客户的供应商；据专家听闻但未证实，R1可直接承诺在客户现有回款成本基础上降低50%。
  7. R1的自动化投入主要集中在FTE最密集的后端AR跟进与拒付处理，确实在降低成本、提升利润率，但部署需要时间，且现有客户可能因此要求重新谈判费率。
  8. 行业定价持续下行并转向基于结果的收费，客户要求在合同中加入SLA、KPI、罚则和收益分成；专家警告仅凭低价竞争会陷入恶性循环，客户往往3年后再次招标。

SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, a former revenue-cycle sales leader now working at another provider-services company, discussed how hospitals choose revenue-cycle management (RCM) partners. Health systems still value the idea of consolidating vendors, but staffing shortages, cost pressures, and concerns about operational risk often lead them to use multiple providers. The conversation also covered AI adoption, competition, and outcome-based pricing.

Key Insights

Modular services appear better positioned than broad end-to-end offerings as many health systems prefer to retain some functions in-house and diversify vendor risk. The expert noted that Ensemble has had success with larger integrated partnerships, while R1 and Ensemble remain prominent in the end-to-end market. AI is more likely to augment staff than fully automate the revenue cycle. Some back-end workflows show practical benefits, but human review remains necessary, and automation capabilities vary by workflow, payer, and system. R1’s automation may support lower costs and improved profitability, but deployment takes time and may prompt existing clients to renegotiate fees. The expert relayed an unverified report that R1 has offered a 50% reduction against a client’s current cost- to-collect. Outcome-based contracts are increasingly important for winning work. The expert cautioned that competing mainly on price can lead to unsustainable, short-lived partnerships. The moderator pressed on whether AI claims translate into customer-facing capabilities. The expert’s view was that R1’s emphasis has so far leaned more toward back-end efficiency than front-end customer tools.
