原标题：Amazon Advertising: Does Agentic Commerce Weaken or Strengthen Sponsored Ads?

核心摘要

  1. 曾在亚马逊广告部门工作约4年的专家认为，亚马逊9月推出的AI广告代理对缺乏代理商支持的中小广告主有帮助，但其建议基于后台预设的最佳实践，常会误判客户可接受的成本，无法替代人工判断。
  2. 全漏斗广告和DVA Plus等新型AI活动类型预计采用缓慢，因为广告主担心素材投放位置且缺乏认知；专家认为高单价、高生命周期价值或有配套产品的品牌更能从全漏斗策略中受益。
  3. 亚马逊的第一方购物数据被视为核心资产，通过DSP投放开放互联网库存有助于品牌认识亚马逊的广告能力；非站内销售品牌（non-endemic）广告增长最快，尤其是金融服务和医疗健康品牌，新增预算多为增量而非从其他渠道挪用。
  4. 站内销售品牌的广告组合大致为60%赞助商品或DSP、40%视频；专家客户在亚马逊的广告支出今年增长约10%，部分来自测试新产品。
  5. 由于亚马逊屏蔽ChatGPT、Meta Muse等外部购物代理，专家认为三、四季度赞助广告效果出现一定走弱，但ROAS并未大幅下降，客户也未明显削减支出。
  6. Meta的Muse购物代理据称已有约1000万用户，可在Walmart、Shopify等已开放接入的平台购物，专家认为长期可能分流亚马逊的电商份额和广告预算，并认为亚马逊屏蔽Muse是错误决定。
  7. 专家建议亚马逊开发能跨站搜索的购物代理，即便有时会把用户引导到其他零售商，也能证明亚马逊在许多情况下价格最低、配送最快；目前的Alexa购物功能仅限站内，对比价帮助有限。
  8. 专家认为亚马逊在效果型CTV广告上依托Prime Video和Fire TV处于领先，YouTube TV未来可能分走份额；未观察到客户预算从The Trade Desk转向Amazon DSP，预计2027年亚马逊广告需求总体平稳。

SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, a digital advertising professional with prior experience supporting Amazon advertisers, discussed Amazon’s AI campaign tools, video and display advertising, advertiser growth, and the potential effect of shopping agents on Amazon’s business.

Amazon’s AI tools could help advertisers manage campaigns, particularly businesses without agency support. However, their recommendations rely on preset inputs and may miss client-specific goals. The expert has not directly used Amazon’s newer full- funnel or Display, Video, and Audio Plus campaigns, and expects adoption of full-funnel campaigns to be gradual as advertisers build confidence in where their ads appear.

Amazon’s first-party shopping data was described as a major asset for targeting. The expert also sees room to expand non-endemic advertising, especially among financial services and healthcare brands, with education needed to show advertisers what Amazon offers and how results can be measured.

Key Insights

AI tools may improve results for advertisers lacking campaign expertise, but they are not reliable substitutes for human judgment. Advertisers are likely to retain control rather than fully trust automated recommendations. Amazon’s decision to block external shopping agents could create a long-term competitive risk. The expert believes Amazon should develop an agent that can search beyond its own marketplace, even if that means sometimes directing customers elsewhere. Agent-driven shopping may reduce ad exposure and affect campaign performance, but the expert has not seen major spending reductions among their clients. Video is considered a strong advertising format, and Amazon is well positioned in performance-oriented connected TV advertising. The expert expects video to remain attractive to advertisers.
