SUMMARY

Overview

The discussion examined WiseTech’s CargoWise platform amid supply-chain disruption, shifting pricing, artificial intelligence, and changing customer needs. The expert, drawing on freight-forwarding experience, described how geopolitical events have made shipping less predictable and increased demand for reliable, real-time shipment information.

The conversation also covered CargoWise’s Value Pack (CVP), opportunities among smaller forwarders and direct shippers, implementation constraints, customer retention, and competitive risks.

Key Insights

Reliable tracking is increasingly valuable. Customers want shipment information connected to carriers and transport providers, rather than relying solely on updates passed through forwarders. The expert believes customers may pay more for dependable data that helps prevent production delays and related losses. CVP pricing is easier to accept when bundled. Customers may resist small, separately itemized fees because of internal approvals, billing systems, and tax treatment. Annual or consolidated packages are likely to be simpler to purchase. SMEs could be a profitable growth opportunity. Large forwarders provide scale, but the expert believes smaller firms may tolerate higher prices and offer stronger returns. They will need more implementation support and tailored solutions. AI’s labor-savings claims appear overstated. The expert viewed a 50% workforce reduction as unrealistic, citing observed reductions of roughly 6–8% and suggesting 11–12% may be achievable within 18–24 months. They emphasized productivity gains over job cuts. Product updates are essential to retention. Switching systems is difficult after extensive implementation and training, but customers may consider alternatives if CargoWise does not keep pace with market needs. The expert advised caution around trade finance, given the need for market expertise and the potential financial downside.
