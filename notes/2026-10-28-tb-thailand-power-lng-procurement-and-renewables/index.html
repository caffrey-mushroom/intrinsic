Agenda

  • LNG procurement dynamics and plant MRO (maintenance, repair and overhaul) requirements
  • Data centre tariff implication and renewable energy mandate
  • Competitive dynamics between Gulf Energy (BKK: GULF) and Egco (BKK: EGCO)

Key Insights

  • Expert believes Thailand needs roughly eight years to lower LNG dependency, given slow government regulation and unclear power development. However, the gas pool pricing system blends domestic and imported LNG costs, slowly passing price increases to consumers rather than exposing them to full LNG market volatility
  • Gas turbine delivery lead times have doubled to 48-plus months, driven by both geopolitical supply chain disruptions and global data centre demand. Spare part prices for power generation equipment are expected to rise up to c50% over the next 12-24 months, but power producers are partially protected by existing long-term service agreements
  • Power producers are open to third-party suppliers or alternative OEMs for components, however, the Electricity Generating Authority of Thailand rates the technical score of trusted top 5 OEMs higher at a 1x multiplier vs secondary market suppliers at 0.8x
  • Data centre green energy requirements serve as the driver for grid access policy updates. Northeastern regions of Thailand offer the strongest solar irradiation conditions, while the Eastern Economic Corridor remains strategically important for industrial and data centre supply
