Agenda

  • Hansen Technologies' (ASX: HSN) platform perception and core architecture review
  • Customer renewal dynamics and pricing power
  • Competitive landscape and switching costs

Key Insights

  • Utility companies face "immense" operational risks and conversion costs of AUD 2m-5m when migrating billing engines, serving as a structural barrier to churn and preserving Hansen's legacy customer base
  • Cloud-native SaaS architectures allow energy companies to distribute regional regulatory and compliance updates across entire customer pool, reducing operating cost to serve by c20-50% vs legacy architectures that require customised builds
  • Expert estimates that updating model inputs – such as complex tariffs on legacy billing engines – requires 3-4x more time and cost as cloud-native platforms leverage unified data streams with fewer points of failure
  • Hansen's client stickiness lies in supporting complex distributed energy tariffs such as smart EV charging and battery integration, which are emerging as critical utility evaluation criteria even as mass adoption timing remains uncertain
  • While utilities dedicate 90-95% of billing budgets to compliance updates, Hansen's add-on suite growth is restricted by legacy system satisfaction and cross-regional integration friction
