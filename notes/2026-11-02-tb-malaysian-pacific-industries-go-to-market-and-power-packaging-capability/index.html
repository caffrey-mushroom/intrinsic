核心摘要

  1. 受访者是前Carsem销售总监，讨论马来西亚封测厂MPI的市场策略和功率封装能力。
  2. 专家预计到FY28 AI服务器模块将占MPI收入约25–28%，增长依赖欧美客户的量的扩张；汽车需求预计在2028年前后见顶。
  3. MPI中国工厂获得的政府补贴少于本土封测厂，而中国封测厂正在马来西亚设厂并靠补贴压价，这是MPI利润率的最大风险。
  4. 一般封测毛利率9–14%，来料加工约5–7%，AI服务器封装约25%，SiC和GaN约20%。
  5. 受电费和最低工资上涨影响，马来西亚封测厂无论产品结构和客户关系如何都在承受利润率压缩。
  6. MPI能为客户定制封装要求，比Amkor等坚持标准方案的大厂更灵活。

Analyst-led • 22 Oct 2026

Companies: MPI MY, AMCR US, 3711 TW, AMKR US, UNISEM MY, IFX DE, NVDA US

Rohit Kumar (RK) Head, Sales at Suchi Semicon Pvt Ltd

Jet Woo (JW) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • End-market demand trends and OSAT (outsourced semiconductor assembly and test) economics
  • Power packaging capability evolution and customer requirements
  • Malaysian Pacific Industries' (MPI; KLSE: MPI) competitive positioning vs Unisem (KLSE: UNISEM), ASE Technology (NYSE: ASX) and Amkor Technology (NASDAQ: AMKR)

Key Insights

  • Expert expects AI server modules to reach c25-28% of MPI's revenue by FY28, with growth relying on volume expansion with customers in Europe and the US. Automotive demand is expected to peak around 2028, mirroring pandemic-era full-capacity conditions
  • MPI's China facility receives fewer government subsidies vs local China-based OSATs, limiting its competitiveness in that market despite lower baseline costs. Additionally, China-based OSATs are establishing Malaysia operations, leveraging government subsidies to undercut pricing and representing the largest risk to MPI's margins going forwards
  • General OSAT margins range 9-14% with consignment margins at c5-7%, while AI server packaging achieves c25% gross margins and SiC and GaN c20%. Expert observes margin compression for Malaysian OSATs regardless of product mix or customer relationships, driven by electricity costs and minimum wages
  • MPI can customise packaging requirements for customers, providing more flexibility vs larger OSATs such as Amkor, which typically enforce standard packaging solutions
