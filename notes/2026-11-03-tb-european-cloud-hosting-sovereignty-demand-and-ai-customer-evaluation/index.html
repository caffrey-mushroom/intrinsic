原标题：European Cloud Hosting Sector – Sovereignty, Demand & AI – How Customers Are Evaluating Cloud Players

#datacenter #policy

Third Bridge · 2026-11-03

核心摘要

  1. 受访者是谷歌EMEA数据中心与经济发展负责人，讨论欧洲云托管商的需求。
  2. 专家预计选址竞争将加剧，土地溢价20–30%，质量较差、时延较高的站点需要给用户30–50%的价格让步。
  3. 主权需求对欧洲云托管商的实际需求影响很小，监管才是未来支出的核心决定因素。
  4. 美国社区和对美国超大云的反对可能使决策转向欧洲托管商，未来三年收入增速可能从10%加快到12–15%。
  5. 标准云预计增长5%，推理约为其两倍，训练增长15%但不在欧洲托管商核心能力之内。
  6. 客户选择以价格为主导，OVH和Ionos更有优势，专家认为OVH凭借更广的产品线和增值服务是主要赢家。

Analyst-led • 30 Oct 2026

Companies: OVH FR, IOS DE

Stef Murray (SM) Head, Data Centres & Economic Development, EMEA at Google LLC (Alphabet Inc)

Philip Atkinson (PA) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Customer preference for cloud hosting from OVHcloud (EPA: OVH) and Ionos (ETR: IOS) to Team.blue, Group.One and Your.world
  • Sovereignty as a demand driving hyperscaler partnerships and realised demand
  • AI workload scaling and time to launch new hosting centres

Key Insights

  • Site acquisition competition is expected to intensify without build-outs required. Land is expected to come at a 20-30% premium, with lower-quality, less competitive sites having latency and associated 30-50% pricing concessions to users
  • Sovereignty drivers have had minimal impact on demand for EU cloud hosters, with regulation a core determinant on future spend
  • Community pushback in the US and on US hyperscalers may swing decision-making towards EU cloud hosters, accelerating revenue growth to 12-15% from 10% in the coming three years. Standard cloud is expected to grow 5%, inference double that, while training grows at 15% but sits outside of core EU hoster capabilities
  • Customer decision-making for hosting will be price-led, with OVH and Ionos better-positioned, outside of AI self-hosting requirements. Expert sees OVH as the prime winner, given broader segment offerings and VAS
