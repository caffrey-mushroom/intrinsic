  2. 仅EFEM和真空机器人两类的市场规模约1万亿至3万亿日元，差异来自开放市场和设备厂自制部分是否计入。

Agenda

  • Recent trends and drivers in the wafer transfer and handling equipment sector
  • Technological changes' impact on wafer handling equipment requirements and economics
  • Growth opportunities across front- and back-end processes

Key Insights

  • Semiconductor end-user investment is linked to front-end semiconductor process equipment sales, but does not directly translate into demand for handling equipment used in the mid- and back-end
  • As printed circuit board functions are integrated into semiconductors, a new market for the automation of mid- and back-end processes using robots is emerging, driving fresh demand growth for handling equipment such as equipment front-end modules, transport robots and aligners
  • Advanced technologies such as through-silicon via processes, square panels and glass substrates are altering the nature of the items being handled; meeting new requirements, such as vibration prevention, could result in unit prices for handling equipment rising 2-3x
  • As HBM4 and subsequent generations require the same level of cleanliness and miniaturisation in back-end processes as in the front, standards and unit prices for back-end handling equipment likely to continue rising
