SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, an experienced security reseller serving large and midsize enterprises, discussed demand and competitive positioning across Palo Alto Networks, CrowdStrike, Fortinet, Check Point, and F5. The discussion centered on how AI adoption is creating new security risks, prompting customers to reassess protections and, in some cases, secure additional budget. The expert expects demand to strengthen, while noting that hardware availability and vendor-specific growth prospects vary.

Key Insights

Enterprises are adopting AI faster than they are securing it. Unmanaged AI tools, weak safeguards, and sensitive data access are increasing exposure. The expert sees AI-enabled attacks as a serious concern: automated threats may require equally fast defensive responses. Recent vulnerability disclosures have heightened customer urgency. Security budgets are opening unusually early, with boards asking about preparedness and some customers funding additional projects before their normal budgeting cycle. The expert expects better-than-anticipated results for most vendors discussed, but not F5. Hardware demand remains healthy in the expert’s customer base, though longer delivery times are driving earlier orders. Vendor replacement is difficult: customers often retain established firewall products because migration is costly and staff have experience with the incumbent.

Competitive Landscape

Company or offering

Positioning and observations

Fortinet

Firewall demand is steady; the expert noted increased interest in hardened equipment for industrial and other operational environments. In- house chip development may offer some supply-chain insulation, though memory constraints remain.

Palo Alto Networks

Identity security adds a potential advantage following its acquisition of CyberArk. The expert sees existing customers as the near-term opportunity. Its SASE offering faces competition from established providers and customers’ preference for best-of-breed products.

Check Point

CrowdStrike

Firewall and email security remain core sales. Proactive vulnerability management and automated remediation are attracting interest. A firewall- as-a-service model could appeal to customers with limited capital budgets.

Endpoint visibility and its broader modular platform support cross-selling. The expert expects greater uptake of additional modules and AI- related detection capabilities.

F5

Primarily associated with application delivery and application behavior. The expert did not provide a detailed performance outlook.

Other competitors

Zscaler and Netskope are established SASE choices; SentinelOne also competes in endpoint security. WatchGuard and SonicWall serve more midmarket firewall customers.
