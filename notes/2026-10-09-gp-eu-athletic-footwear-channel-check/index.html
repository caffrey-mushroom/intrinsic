SUMMARY

Overview

The expert leads a major Italian footwear retailer operating roughly 300 large-format stores. The discussion covered weak 3Q26 athletic-footwear demand, heavier discounting, changing consumer preferences, brand performance, inventory health and future orders.

Demand was hurt by economic uncertainty, energy costs and unusually warm weather. Athletic lifestyle footwear weakened as dress footwear showed early signs of recovery. Running remained the strongest athletic category.

Key Insights

The expert characterized 3Q26 as exceptionally poor, erasing most earlier gains despite modest unit growth. Higher markdowns reduced realized prices and revenue, even though the merchandise mix carried higher ticket prices. Running sales nearly doubled, while lifestyle and court-style footwear declined sharply. New Balance was the clear standout, supported by retro-running designs and retailer-specific products. Adidas was identified as the greatest strategic concern because it represents about one-quarter of sales but lacks compelling innovation. Skechers also presents material inventory risk due to weak sell-through and possible consumer movement toward dress footwear. Inventory is currently healthy following cancellations and returns negotiated with suppliers. Continued weak demand could require aggressive winter clearance activity. Supply availability remains stable, with no meaningful shipping disruption reported. The expert expects New Balance, Salomon and either On or Hoka to lead near-term performance.
