Agenda

  • Structural demand growth in frozen food, driven by shifting consumer habits towards convenience, freshness and improved product perception
  • Cost inflation dynamics & retailer pushback on brand price increases
  • Margin mix impact between high-growth branded meals and lower-margin Copack private label manufacturing
  • International playbook execution and distribution hurdles in expanding the Frosta (FRA: NLM) brand across Italy and Poland

Key Insights

  • Consumers increasingly view premium frozen meals not as expensive grocery items, but as cheap restaurant alternatives, effectively shielding Frosta from private label downtrading during periods of ongoing macroeconomic pressure
  • Frosta's upper revenue growth target of 17% is unrealistic, with expert expecting 11-13% growth, driven primarily by its strategic shift away from low-margin private label co-packing
  • Achieving the demanding 8% profit margin guidance would require aggressive structural changes, such as significant SKU rationalisation, reduced promotional activity or outsourcing internal functions such as call centres
  • "Frosta is really a company that just wants to spend the money that they have earned before. They almost do not have any bank loans or so", limiting its ability to invest in growth in international markets
  • AI-based production planning has significantly improved Frosta's demand forecasting recently, minimising excess internal costs and reducing waste
