Agenda

  • Indegene's (NSE: INDGN) AI go-to-market, commercialisation strategy and platform defensibility
  • Client adoption barriers – pilot to enterprise scaling challenges and strategies
  • Performance expectations – customer diversification, account expansion and near-term margin recovery

Key Insights

  • Margin recovery expected to emerge in Q4 FY27, though magnitude remains uncertain. Outcome-based revenue realisation most at risk in tech-intensive contracts, where 30-50% of revenue milestones encountered challenges
  • While Indegene is shifting towards higher tech reliance, human-in-the-loop service model still requires large full-time equivalent base to deliver outcomes, which is expected to drag margins over a couple of quarters before model is perfected
  • Cortex faces adoption barriers due to pharma clients' data-sharing reluctance and internal enterprise AI deployments. Hyperscalers surmount client aversion via direct, in-ecosystem client partnerships despite lacking domain expertise
  • Pharma clients are experimenting with offshore global capability centres, internal and third-party platforms and direct hyperscaler partnerships that risk displacing Indegene. Where Indegene demonstrates clear value, it can retain client spend. However, client budgets are expected to fragment across vendors, capturing narrow use cases
  • Platform pilots average 4-6 months, with only 40-50% succeeding due to significant capability gap between demo and production-grade execution, preventing commercial deployment
