原标题：Zeta Global – From Marketing Cloud to Enterprise AI Infrastructure

#Zeta #营销SaaS #Agent

Third Bridge · 2026-11-11

核心摘要

  1. 前Zeta高管预计公司未来12–24个月可维持15–25%的有机增长。
  2. 核心护城河是数十年第三方cookie时代积累的身份图谱，Salesforce和Adobe难以复制。
  3. 智能体营销可能把自有媒体收入贡献从10–15%提升到约33%，减少对昂贵付费媒体的依赖。
  4. 营销预算会从代理商和内部人员流向SaaS平台，Zeta数据云有望成为跨渠道编排层。
  5. 通过向CRM客户交叉销售付费媒体，净收入留存可能达到125%。

Analyst-led • 4 Nov 2026

Companies: ADBE US, CRM US, ZETA US, BRZE US

Jason Fordham (JF) Solutions Consultanting at Attentive Mobile Inc

Dylan Koehler (DK) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Sustainability of Zeta's (NYSE: ZETA) revenue growth and enterprise customer expansion
  • Athena adoption, AI monetisation and the new AthenaOS launch
  • International expansion and opportunities beyond traditional marketing
  • Competitive positioning, margin expansion and free cash flow generation

Key Insights

  • Expert expects Zeta to sustain 15-25% organic growth over the next 12-24 months, supported by its marketing suite and cross-selling opportunities. Agentic marketing could drive significant revenue shifts, favouring platforms first to deliver scalable, trusted autonomous decisioning
  • Zeta's identity graph remains its primary competitive moat, built over decades of third-party cookie tracking before restrictions tightened, making replication by Salesforce or Adobe exceptionally difficult
  • Agentic marketing could increase owned media's revenue contribution from 10-15% to 33%, as autonomous decisioning shifts spending away from expensive paid media channels for known customers
  • Expert expects marketing budgets to migrate from agencies and internal headcount towards SaaS platforms, positioning Zeta's data cloud as the central orchestration layer across marketing channels
  • Net revenue retention could reach 125% through cross-selling paid media capabilities into existing CRM accounts, with individual enterprise customers potentially generating USD 50m annually
