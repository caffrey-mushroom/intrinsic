Agenda

  • AI-server demand and backlog durability
  • Dell (NYSE: DELL) ISG's (Infrastructure Solutions Group's)competitive positioning and supply dynamics
  • Storage, networking and services attach
  • Revenue growth and margin outlook

Key Insights

  • Roughly half of Dell's 122% Q2 FY27 server and networking revenue growth might reflect component inflation and richer system configurations rather than higher unit shipments, potentially masking underlying pace of AI infrastructure demand
  • Dell's cUSD 95bn AI server backlog reflects strong underlying demand, but component shortages, rack integration requirements and customer infrastructure readiness could delay revenue recognition despite management's confidence in FY27 outlook
  • Integrated server, storage, networking and cooling capabilities give Dell a competitive advantage over hardware-focused rivals, as customers increasingly prioritise validated, full-rack AI infrastructure rather than standalone GPU servers
  • Storage revenue grew 26% vs 122% for servers and networking in Q2, but demand could accelerate as AI deployments transition into production, providing a higher-margin revenue opportunity through PowerScale and ObjectScale
  • Dell's 15% ISG operating margin appears sustainable through operational efficiencies and cost reductions, with higher-margin storage attachment potentially offsetting dilutive impact of rapidly growing AI server sales
