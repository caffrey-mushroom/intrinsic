Agenda

  • Amwins' international growth opportunities, noting announced acquisition of Steadfast Group (ASX: SDF)
  • Underwriting strategy and ability to leverage AI
  • Competitive differentiators and market share across speciality wholesale brokerages
  • Outlook for Q4 2026 and beyond, highlighting trends in speciality insurance lines

Key Insights

  • In a soft market, more growth comes from the underwriting side for Amwins, and overall growth should be 8-11%
  • Amwins' strength has been collecting data and sharing risk. Expert thinks CRC is lagging considerably – five years behind – given it was hindered by the banking relationship, but RT Specialty can follow fast
  • Amwins' announced acquisition of Steadfast was "something that they needed to do". Expert thinks it's less directly for growth in Australia but securing an underwriting platform to grow in the broader APAC region, noting "they're going to overdeliver on the international side" over the next 3-5 years
  • Retention should be in the 85-90% range from Steadfast. Expert sees minor risks with RT potentially making acquisitions or government intervention and regulation
  • With AI, success ratios could go from 66% to 70% with quotes being out quicker, potentially saving 1-1.5% on costs on the underwriting side over the next 12 months
