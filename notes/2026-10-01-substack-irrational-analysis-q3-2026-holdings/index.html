[Tier 3] Degen (~$50K to ~$100K)

I want to 2x/3x/5x all of these but again exercising some restraint.

Only new name is QMCO. Someone pinged me about this. Tape drives. Spent half an hour looking into it and its hilarious.

For those of you who are unaware, there are these things called tape drives. Much cheaper than HDD but also much slower. Most people have no need for such cheap and slow storage. However, the AI boom seems to have generated so much data, there is enough spillover where tapes start to make sense for more use cases.

The kicker is long-term storage and cybersecurity.

AI is causing chaos in cybersecurity. So how to secure data?

Well you could air-cap some HDD servers but that is not perfectly safe.

You can’t unplug the HDDs and store them in a vault or something but the lubricant and mechanical bits degrade. At best HDD is safe to store 3-5 years while powered off and that is honestly kind of questionable. SSD/NAND has the same problem. Bit rot. Look it up.

Properly stored tape drives can last for up to 30 years in offline storage.
