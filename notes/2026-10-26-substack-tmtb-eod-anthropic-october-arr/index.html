  • The S-1 in charts: here
  • FundaAI and SouthernValue X deep dive tweet threads here and here

A couple things that stood out to us:

  1. 48% revenue is generated through CSP marketplaces like AWS Bedrock, GOOGL’s Vertex, and MSFT’s Foundry.
  2. Customer concentration improving: The top five customers fell from ~36% of revenue in Q1'26 to ~21% in Q3'26.

Top-five revenue still roughly doubled, from ~$1.7B in Q1 to ~$3.5B in Q3, and each of the five hit a record in Q3.. Revenue from everyone else grew ~4.4x over the same two quarters, from ~$3.0B to ~$13.3B. Customers spending $100K+ a year went from >950 to >11,900 between September 2025 and September 2026.

  1. Cash levels/FCF positive after SBC, and operating leverage better than expected
  2. Total customer spend fell about 20% in the two weeks ended January 2, 2026, compared with the two weeks ended

December 19, 2025. This is important bc means Dec ARR likely will be understated ~10%...something to keep in mind

Overall, we think the release was largely positive as it reinforced Anthropic’s cash generation ability and assuaged some bear fears about Anthropic’s business model (despite a lot of it already being telegraphed in leaked reports).

Importantly, they left a spot open to fill in October ARR, which means we likely get it middle of next week when they filed updated S-1. We spent a good portion of our weekly yesterday detailing why Lab ARR matters so much for the AI trade and how it flows through to the financing and build out

Bulls will point to Ramp and OpenRouter data showing a big uptick so far in Oct:

Based on OpenRouter data:

  • The October window (Oct 4-31) is running ~2.7x September’s tokens.
  • Anthropic’s price per token is ~$0.80 per million tokens, a record low, against ~$1.55 on average in September. Part of that is the shift to cheap Haiku 5.5 tokens.
  • Tokens × price works out to ~+50% spend vs September.

Based on Ramp data, as of 10/11, Anthropic’s share of spend was 60.1%, up from 53.8% in the week of 9/13, back near its pre-Astra ~61-62%. Anthropic spend was up ~37% from the Astra low. If that held, October is ~+25% vs September. If it’s 65% share by end of the month, that implies +40% m/m spend

Remember though, OpenRouter is <1% of Anthropic’s ARR and Ramp is a sample of mid-market businesses paying Anthropic directly. Together they likely make up ~20%+ of ARR plus some subs

What’s more important is revenue from marketplace (48% of total) and revenue from top five customers (21% of revs).

On the first point, we will get reads later this week from GOOGL, AMZN, and MSFT which could give us insight into how Oct trending. It’s likely they all sound good.

On the 2nd point, they said “For the three months ended September 30, 2026, our revenue from each of our top five customers reached a record high"

This means 1 of 2 things:

  1. META fell off in Q3, which means majority of the hit was that Q (would be a good thing for “organic growth”) OR
  2. META still a stop 5 customer and full hit coming in Q4.

So lots of moving pieces…
