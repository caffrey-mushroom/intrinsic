Companies: SPT US

Suman Adhya (SA) SVP, Finance at Qlik Technologies Inc

Dylan Koehler (DK) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Demand trends across PR, media intelligence and communications software
  • Cision's positioning across media monitoring, press release distribution and influencer engagement
  • Customer retention, platform adoption and expansion opportunities
  • Cision's competitive positioning vs Meltwater, Muck Rack, Onclusive and other communications platforms

Key Insights

  • PR Newswire volumes have stabilised at around flat monthly growth following steep post-pandemic declines, suggesting the business has reached a floor but remains unlikely to recover pandemic-era peaks
  • Amplify is beginning to demonstrate measurable revenue contributions, offering a potential growth lever to offset structural press release volume declines and support broader product diversification
  • Mid-market customers spending USD 25,000-100,000 annually remain particularly vulnerable to churn, as budget pressures, weak onboarding and difficulties demonstrating ROI undermine retention, particularly within the first 90 days
  • Brandwatch's growth deterioration reflects misaligned product roadmaps and restricted Twitter data access, while aggressive competitor discounting and increasingly commoditised dashboards further pressure Cision's competitive positioning
  • Expert expects EBITDA margins to remain at c29-30%, with AI-driven automation, self-service adoption and lower headcount requirements helping offset flat-to-modest revenue growth
