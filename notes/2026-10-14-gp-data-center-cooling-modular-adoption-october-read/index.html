SUMMARY

Overview

The discussion covered the US data center cooling market, including heat-rejection systems, white-space cooling, supply constraints, pricing, and modular deployment. The expert designs cooling solutions for end users and works across dry coolers, cooling towers, heat exchangers, coolant distribution units, computer-room air conditioning, and related equipment.

AI infrastructure and hyperscale expansion are driving unprecedented demand. Suppliers are struggling to add manufacturing capacity quickly enough, while permitting, power, water, noise, and community concerns are pushing some projects toward remote locations.

Key Insights

The expert characterized market growth as exceptional, with demand at least tripling and potentially quadrupling over the past several years. Spending accelerated in the third quarter of 2026 as hyperscalers gained confidence in AI economics. Despite public discussion about slowing AI development, actual infrastructure investment appears to remain aggressive. Cooling suppliers are largely capacity-constrained through 2027. Customers planning for delivery before 2028 may already be too late, particularly for field-erected systems. The 2027 growth outlook has strengthened materially, supported by emerging AI applications expected to require substantially more computing capacity. Modular systems represent approximately 85% of current and planned projects because they shorten on-site installation. The moderator repeatedly explored this adoption shift and its implications. Established suppliers benefit from proven reliability at scale. New entrants face customer hesitation and may lack capacity to support very large projects. Manufacturers are passing through raw-material inflation with limited customer resistance.

Competitive Landscape

Segment

Market positioning and considerations

Field-erected cooling

Preferred for gigawatt-scale sites because elevated structures reduce hot-air recirculation and improve efficiency. Established global manufacturers dominate.

Factory-assembled cooling

Offers faster delivery but can require hundreds of units, increasing recirculation and site-density challenges.

Modular field-erected systems

Increasingly favored because major components arrive assembled and can be installed rapidly. The expert saw little meaningful differentiation among leading vendors.

New entrants

Reputation, demonstrated reliability, and production scale are major barriers. Established overseas suppliers entering the US are better positioned than startups.

Component suppliers

EC fans, steel tubing, large axial fans, and gear drives are current or emerging bottlenecks.
