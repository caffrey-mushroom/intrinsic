SUMMARY

Overview

A channel expert discussed demand for a major design-software provider across architecture, engineering, construction, and manufacturing. The expert reported modest improvement in the latest quarter, driven by mid-market activity and broader project types, while large AI-related data-center projects remained sluggish. Manufacturing was the strongest area, with demand spanning facility design, automation, simulation, and related services.

Key Insights

The expert expects the current quarter to improve, potentially reaching about 7% growth, but does not expect the full-year result to meet the prior 6.5% target because of a weak start. Manufacturing momentum is widespread across the US and is creating demand for both facility design and operational technology. However, the expert cautioned that it is unlikely to replace the rapid growth previously associated with AI data centers. Large construction projects remain vulnerable to delays. The expert estimated that a notable share of larger projects may be paused, while mid-market and manufacturing sales cycles are showing signs of shortening. AI is mostly embedded in existing products rather than adopted as a distinct offering. The expert sees project-wide data analysis as a potentially transformative future use, but said the industry is not close to that capability. The expert described a recently acquired building-maintenance product as a possible way to expand into facility operations, but reported no observable customer adoption or sales activity yet. The expert highlighted a future transition of legacy subscriptions as a significant opportunity, while emphasizing that the vendor must manage the change carefully and continue investing in products and acquisitions.
