SUMMARY

Overview

An enterprise AI consultant reviewed recent demand across Azure, AWS, and Google Cloud, focusing on cloud growth, AI tooling, capacity, and agentic workloads. Enterprise customers are increasingly evaluating clouds based on access to leading AI models, deployment simplicity, infrastructure capacity, and governance. The expert expects model providers to exert greater influence over cloud purchasing decisions.

Key Insights

Google Cloud delivered the strongest upside and is the expert’s top growth choice for the 2027 planning cycle. Demand is being driven by TensorFlow, TPUs, Vertex AI, and improving Gemini performance. Azure remains well positioned because Copilot integrates easily with Microsoft 365, Windows, SharePoint, Fabric, and Azure AI Foundry. However, its early advantage may weaken as customers adopt additional models and clouds. AWS growth was respectable but below expectations. The expert believes infrastructure capacity and less-developed AI tooling are limiting faster expansion. Anthropic was viewed as the emerging enterprise AI leader due to model performance, governance efforts, Claude Code, and broad adoption of its Model Context Protocol. The expert expects Anthropic and OpenAI to become more direct competitors to hyperscalers as their capital, infrastructure, and software portfolios expand. Enterprise agentic AI adoption remains less developed on AWS, while Claude is currently preferred there over OpenAI models.
