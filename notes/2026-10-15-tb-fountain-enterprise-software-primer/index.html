Could you describe what Fountain does and its products and services?

Expert: Fountain is a high-volume frontline ATS system that basically helps find the candidates, the hourly candidates, the plumbers, electricians, hourly workers, trade-type folks brings them through the process and then gets them ready for work and onboarded in a very rapid manner because a lot of companies don't have the ATS capabilities for the frontline population, which is 71-80% of the workforce.

What might be the value proposition of Fountain's products or services?

Expert: Back when a lot of these systems were built, if you look back, iCIMS, Workday, SuccessFactors, you can name any of the recruiting systems that are out there. When they were building their recruiting system, they actually did not optimise it for frontline employees because, at the time, not everybody had phones. I know that sounds crazy until now, but that's really what the argument was that we weren't really buying things or software for the frontline. Now that the frontline is very big in focus right now, with all the manufacturing jobs coming back and all the retail jobs that are starting to open up in DoorDash, Uber, etc. Their value prop is to bring people through the process quickly so you can make them offers and onboard them in a rapid timeframe, which you cannot do with your current ATS.

Expert: This is going to be all AI. The way that you approach this now, going forward vs building traditional SaaS software, there are so many things you can do now leveraging AI that make this process go so much faster. People are going to want to move very fast.

The way the market is going now is that you can take an AI layer and you can put it across your AI stack, and they can actually inject AI into the process without actually replacing the existing system, which nobody wants to do right now because it's a huge project, it takes like a year and plus you have to get money for it. They're all being pressured to put AI in the HR stack. This is a way to get AI in the HR stack without having to replace anything, but getting all the benefits of AI.
