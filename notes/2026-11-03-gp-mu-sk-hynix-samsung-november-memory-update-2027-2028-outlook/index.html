SUMMARY

Overview

The expert discussed the outlook for DRAM and NAND amid rapid AI infrastructure investment. Memory producers face rising customer requests but are cautious about expanding too quickly, since AI spending could slow and leave them with excess capacity. The discussion also covered pricing, long-term supply agreements, new memory architectures, CXL, and Chinese manufacturers.

Key Insights

The expert views current memory production as the main constraint. Estimated bit growth is about 20% for DRAM and slightly below that for NAND in 2027; growth could rise modestly in following years as new facilities and equipment become available. The timing of any AI spending slowdown is highly uncertain. The expert expects a memory downturn to follow a decline in AI demand, rather than new capacity arriving quickly enough to end the current upcycle. Contract pricing continues to rise, even as spot-market increases appear to be easing. Spot transactions represent only a small share of the market, and temporary lower-specification output during manufacturing transitions may contribute to spot price softness. Agentic AI could increase demand for tokens and large cache memory. However, the expert could not identify when hyperscaler spending might slow or how companies will fund continued investment. Long-term agreements may delay a downturn but cannot prevent one. Some include financial guarantees or escrow, though the terms and coverage vary.

Competitive Landscape

Company or technology

Positioning and developments

Micron, SK Hynix, Samsung

Leading suppliers navigating capacity constraints and rising demand. They are accelerating facility plans where possible but remain cautious about committing to expansion.

CXMT and YMTC

Chinese producers benefit from government support and strong margins. Restrictions on advanced tools remain a barrier, but the expert sees a potential path for CXMT to compete in HBM using less advanced processes at lower margins.

ASML

A key supplier of difficult-to-produce EUV equipment; tool availability and factory construction constrain capacity growth.

NVIDIA and AMD

Custom HBM could strengthen a buyer’s leverage if supply is dedicated to one customer. The expert sees shared designs across customers as more likely to preserve commodity-like competition.

CXL

Can reuse older memory and pool capacity, easing some demand for new server memory. Software requirements limit near- term adoption.
