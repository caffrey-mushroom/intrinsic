#financials

Analyst-led • 14 Sep 2026

Companies: FCH GB

Jon Dobson (JD) Managing Director at Ravenswood Trading

Agenda

  • Key dynamics affecting the SME alternative lending space and read-through for Funding Circle (LON: FCH)
  • Deep dive on customer base and core use cases, while looking at Funding Circle's ability to cross-sell and acquire customers
  • Product suite and platform analysis, identifying levers for growth to boost income
  • Competitive landscape, exploring Funding Circle's ability to gain market share

Key Insights

  • Despite CEO's departure, expert sees continuation of the same strategy, retaining strong culture. New CEO should look to have higher profile, with Alexander Allen viewed as the leading internal candidate
  • Automation around risk and underwriting is Funding Circle's key strength, though expert highlights delicate balance between AI and application speed, with instant decisions becoming a turn-off for customers due to mistrust, with presentation to customers vital
  • Recent changes to tax and potential tax changes are tailwinds for Funding Circle, with borrowing for tax purposes one of the largest draws of customers
  • Embedded finance is flagged as transformative but underfunded opportunity, with partnerships such as Sage failing to scale due to competing development priorities over lack of appetite
  • Funding Circle's pandemic-era Coronavirus Business Interruption Loan Scheme cohorts, typically on five-year terms, are now maturing. Retaining that lending volume over the next 3-5 years is critical but underappreciated challenge
