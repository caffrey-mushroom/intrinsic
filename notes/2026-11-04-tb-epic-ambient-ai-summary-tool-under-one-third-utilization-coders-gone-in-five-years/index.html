核心摘要

  1. UCSF急诊医学临床信息学主任表示，Epic的AI病历摘要工具每次就诊使用率不到三分之一，成本高但价值在不同复杂度病人间不稳定。
  2. 他认为AI会完全自动化计费和编码，“想象不到五年后编码员还有工作”，最快两年内这一职业类别就会被替代。
  3. 如果Epic内嵌一个能读病历、回答自然语言提问的GPT窗口，OpenEvidence这类工具会立刻过时。
  4. Epic与Amazon Pharmacy、Cost Plus合作的处方市场，可能重创Walgreens等传统药房，尤其是长期处方的门诊场景。
  5. 专家认为Epic自满、产品笨重，“足够大的公司进来完全可能挑战Epic”；访谈也讨论了OpenAI或Anthropic直接集成进Epic的影响。

Agenda

  • Epic Systems' emergency department AI tech stack, including native tools vs third-party integrations
  • How ambient documentation is evaluated, piloted and budgeted across health systems
  • Risk of Epic building ambient documentation natively and squeezing third-party vendors
  • 12-18-month outlook, including implications of direct OpenAI or Anthropic integration within Epic

Key Insights

  • Epic Systems' AI summary tool sees less than one-third utilisation per visit, despite significant cost, suggesting ambient AI chart summaries deliver inconsistent value across patient complexity levels
  • "I cannot imagine the coders are going to have jobs in five years". AI is expected to fully automate billing and coding, displacing an entire professional category, perhaps in as little as two years
  • Expert believes a contextual GPT window embedded directly in Epic, capable of reading patient charts and answering natural language queries, would immediately render tools such as OpenEvidence obsolete
  • Epic's prescription marketplace partnership with Amazon Pharmacy and Cost Plus could devastate traditional pharmacy chains such as Walgreens, particularly in ambulatory settings with long-term ongoing prescription referral pathways
  • "A big enough company coming in could definitely take on Epic because I think Epic is very complacent and makes a lot of mistakes […] a lot of their stuff feels very clunky"
