Portfolio

I want to share my portfolio so you can see how my research translates into what I own and how much conviction I have behind it. But I really don’t want people copying it. Everyone has different timelines and different levels of risk they’re comfortable taking.

It’s changed a lot since I started this Substack. Originally, I was extremely heavily weighted toward networking. I started adding Physical AI back in May, then gradually built into hyperscalers. More recently, I’ve added some healthcare, consumer exposure and bonds. So if the breakdown surprises you, it’s evolved along with what I’ve been writing about.

Many of my positions are quite smaller than people would be used to. A few are down around 50%, and I haven’t been averaging down on those just yet. That’s reflected in their weights. I still intend to build some of them over time (especially Physical AI), but right now I’ve been prioritizing more established businesses. I’m in no rush.

Position size also doesn’t tell you my time frame. Many of these are multi-quarter or multi-year investments. WMT, for example, is more tactical exposure outside of tech. That’s why I want to explain the decisions each week rather than just put a list of tickers in front of you.

I’m not trying to turn this into a trading service. The research is still the main focus. Sharing my portfolio gives you more context for it. I’m comfortable taking risks and sitting through swings that might be completely wrong for someone else, and some of these investments may not work out.

And yes again, I have a lot of positions. That’s what I’m comfortable with right now. I want exposure to the themes I believe in while limiting how much damage one company can do. Plenty of these stocks can still fall together, which is part of why I also hold cash and some defensives.
