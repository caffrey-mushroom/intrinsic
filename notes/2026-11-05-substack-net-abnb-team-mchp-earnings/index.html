2026.11.05 | Substack | Software | NET, ABNB, TEAM & MCHP Earnings

原标题：Cloudflare (NET) Airbnb (ABNB) Altassian (TEAM) MCHP First Takes

#software #internet #semis

Substack · 2026-11-05

核心摘要

  1. Cloudflare 3Q收入7.825亿美元、同比增长39.2%，为6个季度中第5次加速，较指引中值超出6.2%，cRPO增速加速至37%，股价盘后涨18%。
  2. Cloudflare 4Q收入指引8.45-8.46亿美元（同比+37.6%），比市场预期高约5,000万美元，但毛利率72.4%低于预期84bps、同比下降287bps。
  3. Airbnb 3Q间夜量增长16%至1.55亿、为1Q23以来最快，GBV增长21%至277亿美元，收入48.69亿美元、调整后EBITDA 25.09亿美元均超预期，并新增120亿美元回购。
  4. Airbnb 4Q收入指引32.2-32.8亿美元超预期，但4Q利润率指引同比略降，隐含EBITDA约9亿美元低于市场9.16亿美元，take rate由17.9%降至17.6%。
  5. Atlassian云收入13.025亿美元、同比增长30.5%，为六个季度来首次减速，虽超市场预期但未达买方预期，股价下跌20%。
  6. Atlassian F2Q云收入指引约27%、FY27云增长上调至约26%，隐含下半年云增速约23.6%、营业利润约8.55亿美元低于市场约9.34亿美元。
  7. Microchip 9月季度收入16.22亿美元（同比+42.2%），但其中约5,300万美元为不可重复的一次性收入，且税率仅5.7%帮助EPS达0.97美元。
  8. Microchip 12月季度收入指引中值环比-1%、毛利率指引降至64-65%，低于8月所说的66-67%区间，同比增速放缓至35.4%为9个季度来首次减速。

NET +18%: Best sw print of the season and arguably best print NET has put up in a long time. Clean beat and raise and acceleration on tougher comps. 3Q revenue of $782.5M (+39.2% y/y vs. 35.9% in 2Q, the 5th acceleration in 6 quarters and the “fastest revenue growth in nearly four years”) beat Street ($736.8M) and the ~$768-775M bogeys, a 6.2% beat on the guide mid, the biggest in 12+ quarters. cRPO growth accelerated to 37% vs. the ≥35% bogey. The 4Q revenue guide of $845-846M (+37.6% y/y) is ~$50M above Street ($795.9M) and ~$28M above the top of the ~$810-818M bogeys, FY was raised $96.5M at the mid (vs. $58M last quarter), and the 4Q op income guide of $156-157M (18.5% margin) beat Street’s $145.2M (18.2%). The blemish is GM: 72.4%, 84bps under Street and -287bps y/y.

With Q4 guided at 38%, implied exit rate would be low 30s and so bulls will think Q1 at least mid 40s growth. Tough market so who knows what happens tomorrow, but definitely a great print. Positioning had come down hard coming in (-21% in the five sessions since 10/29, incl. -8% today with QQQ -6.5%, and fast money out, and this is the print bulls needed: growth re-accelerated on a tougher comp and the 4Q guide is above the bogeys before their usual beat. Bulls will point to revenue +39.2% against a 3Q25 comp 2.9pts tougher than 2Q’s (2-yr stack 69.9 from 63.6), current RPO growth up to 37% from 35%, and a 4Q guide of +37.6% on the year’s toughest comp (4Q25 +33.6%) that tops 3Q’s growth with just a 1.2% beat on the mid (their smallest in 12 quarters was 1.5%). Bears will lean on GM of 72.4% (84bps under Street, -287bps y/y), an op margin only in line with Street at 17.5% even though revenue beat the guide mid by $46M (only ~16% of that fell to op income), FCF margin of 10.5% vs. 13.3% a year ago.

ABNB +12%: Blowout 3Q acceleration and EBITDA with solid Q4 top line guide that implies more accel. Nights grew 16% (155.0M), up from 10% in 2Q and the fastest since 1Q23, vs. Street 149.1M (+11.6%) and the ~150-151M bogeys. GBV grew 21% ($27.7B) vs. Street $26.41B and the ~$26.5-26.8B bogeys. Revenue of $4.869B beat Street ($4.747B) and the ~$4.78-4.82B bogeys, and adj. EBITDA of $2.509B (51.5% margin, up y/y when the guide said down slightly) beat Street ($2.364B) and the ~$2.39-2.44B bogeys. The 4Q revenue guide of $3.22-3.28B (+17% at the $3.25B mid) beat Street ($3.16B) and the ~$3.20-3.23B bogey. 4Q GBV is guided to mid-teens growth on at least low double-digit nights, above Street and at the top of the bogeys. The FY margin goes to ~36% (the bogey), and there’s a new $12B buyback. The one soft spot is 4Q profit: the margin is guided down slightly y/y on marketing timing, which implies ~$0.90B of 4Q EBITDA vs. Street $916M.

Crowded long that sold off into the print (-3.5% today with EXPE -14% on its 4Q guide, ~11% off the August high), and this is the print bulls wanted. Bulls will point to nights at +16% vs. a “low double-digit” guide, a 5.7pt acceleration on a 1.4pt tougher comp (2-yr stack 24.8 from 17.8), EMEA accelerating to mid-teens, North America at a four-year high, a 4Q revenue guide $88M above Street and a $12B buyback (~12% of the market cap). Bears will lean on 4Q profit: a ~36% FY margin on ~$14.41B of FY revenue (1Q-3Q actuals plus the 4Q mid) leaves ~$0.90B of 4Q EBITDA (27.6% margin, -70bps y/y) vs. Street’s $916M, so FY EBITDA rose only ~$124M vs. Street after a $145M 3Q beat. They’ll add GBV guided to slow to mid-teens from 21%, and the take rate (revenue / GBV) down to 17.6% from 17.9% as Reserve Now, Pay Later books GBV ahead of the stay. Reserve Now, Pay Later is pulling bookings earlier (“an extension of lead times”) and has “higher cancellation rates”.

Key quote (letter): “Although we’re closely monitoring ongoing developments in EMEA, underlying global travel demand remains healthy, and our product and growth initiatives continue to deliver results.”

Ops & support fell to 7.4% of revenue from 8.4%, helped by “lower agent contact volume resulting from increased use of artificial intelligence (‘AI’) in community support”.

TEAM -20%: Another sw miss as cloud beat Street but missed the bogeys and decelerated for the first time in six quarters, and the guide beat Street but missed the bogeys too. Cloud revenue of $1,302.5M (+30.5% y/y, from 30.8% in F4Q) beat Street ($1,283.7M, +28.7%) and the ~28.5% guide by 2.0pts, the smallest cloud beat since F3Q25, but missed the ~$1.31-1.32B bogeys (~31.5-32%). Total revenue of $1,772.2M beat Street ($1,712.0M) and was in line with the ~$1.76-1.78B bogeys, with $33M of the $62M beat on the guide midpoint from Data Center. The F2Q cloud guide of ~27% (~$1,355M) beat Street (26.5%) but missed the ~28-28.5% bogeys, and FY27 cloud went to ~26% from 25.5% (just the F1Q beat) vs. Street 25.7% and the ~27% bogey, which leaves the implied 2H at ~23.6% vs. Street’s 24.0%. FY27 non-GAAP OM of ~25.5% (OI ~$1.91B) beat Street’s 25.2% ($1,871M) and hit the low end of the ~25.5-26% bogeys, but the implied 2H OI of ~$855M (~22% margin) sits ~$79M below Street’s ~$934M.

Positioning had come down with software (~19% below the 10/23 high close and -3.5% today), but this is the deceleration bears were waiting for: cloud, TEAM’s hosted subscription revenue and ~73% of the total, slowed for the first time in six quarters on its smallest beat since F3Q25, and the FY27 cloud raise is only the F1Q beat. Bulls will point to the 2-yr cloud stack holding at 56.5 on a tougher comp, a profit beat everywhere (32.5% non-GAAP OM vs. Street 28.6%, FCF $463M vs. $351M), and an F2Q cloud guide implying just +4.0% q/q vs. ~+7% in each of the last two F2Qs (an F1Q-sized 2pt beat lands ~29%, above the bogeys). Bears will lean on that guide sitting 3.5pts under the print, which takes the 2-yr stack to ~53, and an implied 2H of ~23.6% cloud growth and ~22% OM (OI ~$855M vs. Street ~$934M, our math).

MCHP -3.0%: The Dec guide missed Street and the bogeys on revenue, GM and EPS, with GM guided down ~200bps q/q to 64-65%, below the 66-67% range mgmt said in August Dec could hold. Sept-Q revenue of $1.622B (+42.2% y/y, +9.2% q/q) beat Street ($1.608B) and was in line with the low end of the ~$1.62-1.63B bogeys, a 4th straight quarter above the top of the guide but by only 0.2%, and ~$53M of it was what mgmt calls “infrequent revenue items” that don’t repeat in Dec. GM of 66.5% was in line with Street (66.5%) and missed the ~67% bogey; EPS of $0.97 beat Street ($0.94) and was in line with the ~$0.96-0.98 bogeys, with help from a 5.7% tax rate (7.5% guided). The Dec-Q revenue guide is -1% q/q at the mid ($1.6055B; -3% to +1%) vs. Street $1.628B and ~$1.65-1.67B bogeys, and EPS of $0.87-0.95 ($0.91 mid; implied OI ~$606M vs. Street $635M) compares with Street $0.95 and ~$0.97-1.00 bogeys. y/y growth slows to +35.4% from +42.2%, the first slowdown in 9 quarters. Key for call will be explanation of GM step down.

Under-owned coming in and -13.9% today in the post-midterm semis flush, but this is worse than the bar: the Dec guide missed Street, not just the bogeys (ON on 11/2 at least beat Street), and GM drops below the 66-67% range the bogeys were built on. Bulls will point to a 4th straight quarter above the top of the revenue guide, book-to-bill “well above one” with lead times lengthening, PCIe Gen6 design wins doubling to 24, the ~$1B CY26 data center frame held, and a Dec guide of +2.4% q/q (our math) once Sept’s ~$53M of one-offs come out, vs. the usual -3% to -5%, at ~14x Street C27 EPS ($4.39). Bears will lean on a Dec profit guide below Street (implied OI ~$606M vs. $635M), the first y/y slowdown in 9 quarters, MCU, the biggest product line, coming in $28M under Street, and a Sept beat helped by the one-offs (ex them, under the guide’s low end) and a 5.7% tax rate (~$0.95 EPS at the guided 7.5%).

Key quote (release): “Our guidance of down 1% at the midpoint needs to be considered with the fact that we had about $53 million of infrequent revenue items in the September quarter which do not repeat in the December quarter. Taking this into consideration, the midpoint of our guidance for the December quarter represents guidance that is 5% to 7% better than normal seasonality.”
