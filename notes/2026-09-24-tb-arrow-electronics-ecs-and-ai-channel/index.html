Agenda

  • Arrow Electronics' ECS (Enterprise Computing Solutions) strategy and positioning within the IBM partner ecosystem
  • Enterprise AI and automation demand across the reseller channel
  • Reseller consolidation and implications for customer retention
  • Value-added services, channel conflict and profitability

Key Insights

  • Arrow's ECS portfolio is increasingly software-led, with the broader business already c75% software and potentially moving towards an 80/20 software-hardware mix. However, vendor strategies can disintermediate distribution – IBM recently moved committed-spend AWS and Azure marketplace sales away from distributors
  • Ageing founder-owners are accelerating reseller consolidation, concentrating purchasing leverage among larger platforms that demand annual margin concessions and can shift substantial business to competing distributors at contract renewal
  • Replacing recently ended cUSD 700m annual vendor relationship will likely take years rather than quarters – moving entrenched reseller accounts towards alternative vendor portfolios requires substantial investment and disrupts longstanding customer relationships
  • ArrowSphere simplifies complex subscription billing and renewals, creating high reseller switching costs as recurring software becomes more important. Europe's 22% billings growth vs mid-single digits in the Americas is also consistent with its historically more mature managed service provider and services channel
