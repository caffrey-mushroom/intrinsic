Agenda

  • NIQ Global Intelligence (NYSE: NIQ) operating environment, covering slowing intelligence revenue growth and disintermediation threats
  • Competitive dynamics with Circana and others, noting product stickiness and switching costs
  • AI-native solution growth trajectory, noting growth sustainability and ability to upsell core Intelligence base
  • Outlook for Q3 2026 and beyond, highlighting AI-driven margin expansion

Key Insights

  • NIQ's core growth challenge is structural – the scanning data market isn't growing and is essentially a two-player battle with Circana, where contracts are won or lost primarily on price rather than data differentiation
  • Expert sees NIQ's 5.8% organic growth in Q2 2026 as unsustainable, with prior tumultuous performance considered more predictive of NIQ's future than this recent bright spot
  • GLP-1s represent an underrated downstream risk for NIQ. It's uniquely capable of answering manufacturers' questions about shifting consumption, yet simultaneously vulnerable if CPG budgets shrink from declining category volumes
  • Expert thinks 270bps margin improvement from Q2 2026 likely represents only the surface of potential efficiency gains – AI has barely penetrated NIQ's manual workflows
  • Switching costs vary dramatically by client sophistication – large manufacturers deeply integrated with NIQ's analytics face significant switching hurdles, while data-savvy firms could migrate platforms within days
