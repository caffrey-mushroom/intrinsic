  3. 为抢占每月1亿至2亿英镑的数据中心投产营收，主权财富基金等开发方普遍在开工前推进设计（仅耗资500万–1,000万英镑）并超前锁定重型设备，甚至采取约50%以上的超额预订策略（如先报100台并付10万英镑订金或7,000万美元首付，最终仅提50台并甘愿缴纳违约金）。
  4. 这种“虚高预订”扰乱了制造商排产并抬高对外报价交期（实际约40周的制造周期被迫报成52周），而OEM在收取违约金后会将腾出的产能槽位以加急溢价形式重新分配给其他买家。
  5. 尽管卡特彼勒（Caterpillar）和康明斯（Cummins）正推进扩产，专家认为新增产能只会被更多积压订单填满，未来12个月难以缓解交期紧张，设备、原材料与蓝领技术工人短缺将持续至2032–2035年。
  6. 供需失衡赋予厂商极强定价权，叠加AI高算力机柜复杂度及铜、钢和人工通胀，专家预计未来12至24个月行业成本仍将上涨5%–10%；中国供应商因排放与认证不达欧美标准难以切入欧美市场，而Rolls-Royce、INNIO、Wärtsilä和Relco正赢得更多认可。
  7. 继与亚马逊AWS及另一家头部超大规模云厂商达成协议后，Generac在大型数据中心市场的行业信誉显著提升；在需求结构上，AI训练中心因任务可重启或迁移，备用柴油机组配置已降至传统云计算、推理及托管中心的约三分之一（例如从150台减至20–30台）。

SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, an experienced data center project delivery professional, described a market with strong demand but uneven progress from early-stage tenders to funded projects and construction. Grid availability and long lead times for electrical infrastructure are holding back some starts. Developers are increasingly ordering equipment and engaging commissioning teams before construction to secure delivery slots.

The expert sees signs of renewed growth after a recent pause in some UK projects, but emphasized that tender activity alone does not guarantee procurement or construction. Finalized designs and equipment orders are stronger indicators that projects are approaching execution.

Key Insights

Supply remains tight: Engine manufacturing slots are the primary generator constraint. Factory acceptance testing and load- bank availability can also delay delivery and commissioning. Over-ordering distorts planning: Developers may reserve more production capacity than they ultimately need, then reduce quantities after paying cancellation penalties. The expert said this makes demand harder for manufacturers to forecast and can inflate quoted lead times. Capacity expansion may not shorten waits soon: Caterpillar and Cummins are adding capacity, but the expert expects this to enable more orders rather than materially ease constraints. Costs are expected to rise: Scarcity, complexity, materials and labor are all contributing, with supplier pricing power viewed as the largest factor. Long-term demand outlook is strong: The expert expects constraints to persist into the early 2030s, potentially through the middle of that decade.
