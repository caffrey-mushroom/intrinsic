Dylan Koehler (DK) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Atlassian's (NASDAQ: TEAM) cloud migration, enterprise adoption and outlook for subscription growth
  • Rovo adoption, AI monetisation and impact of usage-based pricing
  • Service Collection growth and competition with ServiceNow
  • Teamwork Collection, cross-sell and expansion beyond developer teams
  • Agentic engineering and AI's impact on Jira's long-term role in software development

Key Insights

  • Enterprise app consolidation remains a major tailwind for Atlassian, as companies reduce vendor counts and favour broader platforms, allowing Atlassian to capture workloads from smaller competitors
  • Service Collection's growth trajectory remains exceptionally strong, with the expert viewing an increase from c15% to 20% of Atlassian's revenue mix by 2028 as realistic, supported by Jira Service Management's pricing and integration advantages
  • Customer success and support represent Jira Service Management's largest expansion opportunities beyond ITSM, given their substantial employee bases, while HR and finance offer more limited seat-based upside
  • Rovo's competitive advantage lies in its contextual access to Jira and Confluence data, potentially making it more effective than generic large language models for enterprise workflow management and project tracking
  • Despite these strengths, expert sees a longer-term threat from AI-native competitors such as Linear, Notion and potentially Anthropic, as younger developers increasingly bypass Jira, potentially weakening Atlassian's future customer pipeline
