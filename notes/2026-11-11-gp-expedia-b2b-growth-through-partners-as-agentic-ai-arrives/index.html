SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, a former business development leader at Expedia, discussed the company’s business-to-business (B2B) travel operations, competitive position, and exposure to agentic AI. Expedia serves partners through APIs and customized white-label travel platforms, providing access to lodging and expanding into flights, car rentals, vacation rentals, and other services. The discussion focused on how broader supply, stronger partner management, and technology investments could support growth.

Key Insights

The expert sees B2B as a significant growth opportunity, especially through increasing business with existing partners and adding travel services to their offerings. Expedia’s account-management teams are viewed as more important to B2B growth than new-business development teams. Their role is to increase the share of partner bookings handled by Expedia. The expert expects B2B growth to remain sustainable if Expedia continues expanding its product range, direct supplier relationships, and role as travel infrastructure for AI services. Agentic AI is considered more likely to complement Expedia than displace it in the near term. The expert emphasized the complexity of supplier connections, booking operations, customer support, and consumer trust. The expert expects margins to face modest pressure over the next couple of years as Expedia invests in supply, service, and technology, with potential improvement as those investments mature.

Competitive Landscape

Company or group

Position and competitive dynamics

Expedia

Competes through broad inventory, partner service, account management, and expanding API capabilities. Direct supplier connections may reduce intermediary costs and improve product flexibility.

Booking Holdings

Increasing investment in B2B technology and partner service. The expert considers it behind Expedia in several areas, though its greater focus could intensify competition for partner business and margins.

Switchfly

Offers white-label platforms that can combine multiple suppliers, an advantage for partners seeking flexible sourcing. Expedia’s bespoke white-label product instead uses Expedia inventory exclusively.

AI platforms

Could support travel discovery and planning, and potentially send bookings to Expedia. The expert sees direct competition as less immediate because building travel supply and service capabilities is complex.

Hotels and airlines

May work directly with AI platforms to attract customers, particularly loyal customers. Expedia may retain value through broad comparison, packaging, booking, and support services.
