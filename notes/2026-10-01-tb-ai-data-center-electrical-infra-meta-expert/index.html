00:27:50] What's the rationale for deploying an 800V architecture for hyperscalers?

Expert [00:28:10]: Let me give you a few reasons. The number one reason is the higher power efficiency. The 800V is expected to reach the power conversion efficiency of around 95%. The traditional non-800V, the efficiency is around 87-88%, so power conversion efficiency is number one reason. The second reason is the amount of copper cable required in the 800V is going to be almost 40-45% lesser compared to the traditional data centre, so copper will reduce. Thirdly, the 800V data centre will use very centralised power distribution system using battery energy storage system. The UPS will be eliminated from the data hall, but the battery energy storage system, which are considered to be more efficient, they will be deployed. The last reason is that because the rack power density is increasing from 100kW to greater than 1MW, it is practically not possible to manage the power operation as well as the thermal load using the traditional 48V. 800V is expected to be significantly much more efficient in terms of the thermal loss, power loss, etc. It is going to be much more efficient compared to the traditional voltages.
