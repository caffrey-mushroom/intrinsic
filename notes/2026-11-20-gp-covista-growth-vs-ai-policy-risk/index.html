核心摘要

  1. Covista（原Adtalem）前市场总监认为，AI对商业、科技和教育类证书及非执照项目的冲击最直接，如LinkedIn免费证书和行业协会证书更便宜，而需要执照的学位短期内风险较小。
  2. Walden过去13个多季度的增长主要由招生驱动，专家认为社工、心理咨询等执照相关项目仍有韧性，因为AI可以教授内容但不能颁发执照，雇主仍要求执照委员会认证。
  3. 专家认为公司与各州及认证机构的关系仍是重要护城河，相关认证和授权可能需要5年以上才能建立，新进入者即便借助线上多州授权，仍需线下技能实验室和带教培训。
  4. 公司AI举措并非噱头，已在Canvas课程平台并与Google Cloud合作投入技术，但投入规模尚不明确；专家认为AI是长期布局，而医疗系统合作、临床实习名额和学费资助才是当前首要任务。
  5. Chamberlain护理学院最近一个季度招生仅增长约1.3%，收入增长主要依赖价格与项目结构，价格更高的校园BSN吸引力增强，雇主也更偏好BSN学历。
  6. 专家认为医疗学位在经济下行时更具韧性，而价格较低的RN-to-BSN多依赖雇主学费报销，经济不佳时更易受冲击；公司的提价能力与雇主合作密切相关，合作若变化则定价能力可能削弱。
  7. 2026年7月1日起取消Grad PLUS并设研究生借款上限，现有借款人可获最长3年过渡期；专家初步估计约75-80%的护理借款人低于新上限，部分学生可能提前入学，但影响程度尚不清楚。
  8. 对于民主党主导的新国会，专家认为降低医疗成本的举措总体利好医疗执照教育，但外国医生准入等复杂议题短期难成焦点，Title IV与教育部拆分风险能否缓解仍为时过早。

SUMMARY

Overview

The discussion examined a for-profit education company’s prospects across online and campus-based programs, particularly its online university and nursing school. Topics included artificial intelligence, enrollment and tuition growth, employer partnerships, healthcare workforce demand, and possible changes to student financing and education policy.

Key Insights

The expert sees AI as a more immediate threat to certificates and non-licensure programs in fields such as business, technology, and education. Degrees tied to professional licensing appear more protected because employers and licensing boards still require formal credentials. The expert believes the company’s state approvals and accreditation relationships remain a meaningful competitive advantage. Establishing comparable credentials can take more than five years. Employer and health-system partnerships, clinical placements, and tuition support are near-term priorities for nursing education. AI investment matters for long-term competitiveness, but it cannot replace supervised clinical training. The expert views current AI initiatives as more than marketing, citing investment in course-platform technology. However, the scale of the company’s commitment was not established. Nursing demand and employment prospects may support higher-priced programs. Still, pricing power is linked to employer partnerships and could weaken if those arrangements change. The expert considers the effects of policy changes on graduate borrowing and federal student aid uncertain. Some prospective students may have accelerated enrollment ahead of financing changes, but the extent is unclear.

Positioning and considerations

Online university programs

Licensure-linked areas such as social work and counseling appear relatively resilient; non-licensure certificates and upskilling face cheaper alternatives.

Nursing schools

Campus-based nursing degrees can command higher prices and require clinical capacity. Employer partnerships can support enrollment and tuition affordability.

Low-cost certificates and AI-guided learning

Potential substitutes for some continuing education and non-licensure training, but their acceptance by employers remains uncertain.

New online education providers

Online delivery can ease market entry, while established state approvals and clinical-placement networks create barriers.
