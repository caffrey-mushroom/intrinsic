核心摘要

  1. Oracle合作伙伴Peloton Consulting的CIO/CISO表示，其Oracle渠道业务同比增长约15%，符合预期，2027年在手订单是历年同期最好的，预计明年增速与今年相当。

  3. Oracle年初把AI代理定价从按定制代理和用户数收费改为按AI Units计量消耗，客户更容易理解；AI World上推出的全代理化能力是一个标价50万美元的用量SKU，Fusion中已接近100个全自主代理，已有大型企业客户购买并部署。
  4. 专家认为Oracle在代理数量、安全性和完整性上明显领先Workday和SAP，这一点已经在帮助赢得新客户，但多数客户在完成数据迁移等基础工作后才会真正部署AI。
  5. 早期应用场景集中在应付/应收、考勤卡异常和员工福利问答等财务及HR运营环节；客户对由代理做供应链决策更为谨慎，仍在研究人工监督和安全控制。

  7. 专家认为Oracle的GPU容量可以在区域间通过骨干网调配，即使OpenAI未用满所建容量也能转给其他客户；市场消息称OpenAI相关的RPO一半以上已有资金覆盖，股价因此反弹。
  8. Oracle数据库在AWS和Azure上的托管服务正在推动多云采用（Google相对落后），客户无需开通OCI租户即可使用；数据库迁移像“掉头的游轮”，进展慢但一旦启动就不会停。
  9. 客户常把ERP上下游系统和15-20年的历史ERP数据归档实例放在OCI，按需开关、只付存储费用；合作伙伴也开始在项目早期的流程设计中就引入代理能力，以推动50万美元SKU的销售。

SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, an executive at a large implementation partner, described strong demand for the enterprise software and cloud provider’s products. Channel growth remained on plan, supported by ERP projects, with supply chain and human capital applications also performing well. The discussion covered cloud infrastructure capacity, conversion of contracted work into active projects, multi-cloud database services, and adoption of AI agents in Fusion applications.

Key Insights

The expert said channel growth was tracking expectations, and the coming year’s outlook was similarly positive. A strong pipeline and work continuing through multiple phases of large transformations supported this view. Customers are generally focused on moving to cloud, modernizing systems, and completing integrations and training. Future GPU availability and advanced AI use cases are not major customer concerns today. The expert believes the provider has a clear advantage over major ERP rivals in the breadth and integration of its agent capabilities. This positioning is already helping win deals, even though many customers will deploy AI later in their implementation. A shift to consumption-based pricing for standard AI agents has made the offer easier for customers to understand. The expert described the newer, higher-priced agentic offering as gaining traction among large enterprises. The expert emphasized that the provider can redistribute GPU capacity across its infrastructure network, reducing the risk of excess capacity in any single location.

Competitive Landscape

Area

Positioning and market observations

ERP applications

The provider was described as ahead of major ERP rivals in agent breadth, security, and completeness. Rivals include established enterprise application vendors.

Cloud infrastructure

The provider’s regional capacity-sharing model was presented as an advantage. US tenancy requests typically take a few weeks; international capacity and quota increases can take longer.

Multi-cloud databases

Managed database availability in major competing cloud environments is increasing. This lets customers use the database without adopting the provider’s broader cloud platform. Adoption was described as growing gradually.

Hyperscalers

The expert sees stronger enterprise adoption through the two largest competing cloud platforms than through the third. Database migration is a slow process, but demand may build over time.
