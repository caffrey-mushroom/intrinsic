Agenda

  • Short-to-mid-term market headwinds and tailwinds
  • Cost pressure, mitigation levers and execution risks
  • Like-for-like market outperformance and disruption threats
  • Mitchells & Butlers (M&B; LON: MAB) incremental opportunities for mid-to-long-term growth

Key Insights

  • Expert believes M&B's lack of value-oriented brands is a meaningful portfolio gap, with Harvester drifting away from affordable casual dining and Sizzling Pub & Grill and Stonehouse being too geographically sparse to meaningfully capture cost-conscious consumers
  • All Bar One remains a growth opportunity for M&B, despite late-night headwinds. There is scope to convert select locations that may not have previously been considered
  • "I don't see a lot of marketing at the moment aimed at younger, newer guests". Even if the brand isn't specifically geared towards younger consumers, M&B needs to drive increased focus here
  • Recruiting quality general managers is becoming increasingly difficult industry-wide. M&B's internal talent development has always been strong, but it can invest more and take this further to reduce hiring costs and operational disruption
  • In the longer term, M&B should at least be in line with the market and could sustain outperformance if it can focus on key portfolio pockets to meet shifting consumer trends
