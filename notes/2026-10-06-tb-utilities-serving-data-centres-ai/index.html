Rajeev Aluru (RA) Managing Director, Innovation & Analytics at Smartbridge LLC

Agenda

  • Data centre load growth impact on contract structures and project parameters for the power generation industry
  • Operating changes and optimisation needs to serve large-load offtakers
  • AI implementation use cases, including potential impacts on operating efficiency and margin enhancement

Key Insights

  • Amid material interconnection bottlenecks, expert sees data centres increasingly becoming flexible loads. Grid debottlenecking effect could be significant, as curtailing data centre load by even 0.25% could unlock roughly 50-70GW of new grid capacity
  • Data centre contracting has shifted away from traditional power purchase agreements benchmarked against power prices towards an emphasis on pushing infrastructure costs and stranded asset risk directly onto data centre customers via more bespoke structures, including capacity payments
  • Hybrid architectures combining grid connection, on-site generation and a control layer are emerging as a distinct project model, designed to facilitate partial workloads while awaiting grid connection. Interconnection queue clearance and CAPEX trends will dictate if behind-the-meter, on-site generation is a bridge or long-term solution
  • AI can generate meaningful margin enhancement for generators. AI-driven heat rate optimisation requires zero CAPEX but can improve gas-fired asset heat rates by up to 2-3%
