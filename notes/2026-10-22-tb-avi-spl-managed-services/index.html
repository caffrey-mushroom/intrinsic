Agenda

  • AVI-SPL's competitive positioning, including government account access, purchasing scale, labour rates and MSA (master services agreement) opportunities
  • Sizing the opportunity that the managed services line offers
  • AVI-SPL's streak of acquisitions and how well-integrated the company is
  • How much business leaves when senior leaders take jobs at competitors

Key Insights

  • Business is picking up with insurance companies, law firms, hospitality, healthcare and state & local government.  Complexity is increasing. While expert sees growth north of 4% for the next few years, the forecasts have been moving down as macro headwinds have started to weigh on the outlook
  • Smaller integrators that offer fibre connectivity, wiring, access control and integration on top of AV are eroding the share of large players such as AVI-SPL that are more focused
  • Managed service is a big driver of AVI-SPL and peer revenues as customers outsource maintenance and operation of systems to third parties. Expert believes AVI-SPL's labour rates are higher, driving pricing 25-30% above what a smaller integrator charges
  • Expert cited senior-level client-facing executive churn at AVI-SPL, putting renewal rates at risk because relationships are key to the business and clients typically follow the departing employee to their new company
