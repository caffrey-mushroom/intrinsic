  2. 农机OEM将发动机自产主要是为了提升价值链利润，尤其是利润丰厚的售后备件业务，专家认为已完成投资的内部化趋势将持续。
  3. 三季度发动机业务订单增长29%但收入仅增长0.7%，专家认为订单主要反映客户为锁定旧价格和确保供货而提前下单，可能在四季度取消或导致2027年一季度订单走低，不应等同于市场复苏。
  4. 针对做空报告质疑FFG子公司由乌克兰私人投资者持股超45%的问题，专家认为本地持股并非必需，且该前议员正受欧洲反腐机构关注，可能成为负担；DEUTZ已声明可100%并表，但少数股东的控制权与否决权安排仍不透明。

  6. 专家认为FFG将独立运营，短期内发动机协同有限，因现有装备按客户规格设计难以换装DEUTZ发动机，新项目也需一年以上；更现实的协同在于工程支持、采购整合，以及在培训到位后利用DEUTZ全球服务网络，但工会化可能影响FFG成本与灵活性。
  7. 能源业务三季度订单增长200%、收入增长近80%，主要来自收购Freck和Maxi等无机增长，专家预计长期有机增速约4-6%，且备用发电机组运行时间少、更换周期长达10-20年，售后空间有限。
  8. DEUTZ自身没有数据中心所需大功率发动机，只能外购并贴牌，服务能力是更大的隐忧；其相对卡特彼勒、康明斯的优势在于作为独立发动机供应商不与OEM在整机市场竞争，并拥有全球服务网络。

SUMMARY

Overview

The discussion covered demand across Deutz’s off-highway engine markets, its energy and defense businesses, and questions raised about its acquisition of FFG. The expert described a mixed market: construction and power generation are improving, material handling is stable to slightly positive, and agriculture remains weak. Deutz’s broad market exposure provides some balance, but growth prospects differ substantially by business line.

Key Insights

Engine orders may overstate underlying recovery. The expert attributed much of the recent increase in orders to customers securing supply and current prices, with possible cancellations or lower orders later. He cautioned against treating order growth as equivalent to sales growth. FFG’s outlook depends heavily on conflict-related repair work. The expert expects its service revenue to decline if the conflict eases, since the business has limited large-scale new-product activity. Near-term engine synergies from FFG appear limited. Existing equipment may be difficult to redesign around Deutz engines, and new defense programs would take time to develop. The more plausible opportunities are engineering support, some purchasing efficiencies, and potentially using Deutz’s service network, subject to training and partner economics. Energy demand has structural support but may moderate. Data centers and critical infrastructure need backup power, but these systems run infrequently and may not be replaced for many years. Deutz also relies on third-party engines for the largest applications. The expert viewed Deutz’s independence from equipment manufacturers as an advantage for smaller OEM customers that may not want a competitor’s engine in their products. Its global service network was also identified as a differentiator.
