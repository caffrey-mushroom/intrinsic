Pedro Elejabeitia (PE) Independent Consultant

Agenda

  • Retail banking proposition, including positioning across Openbank and the Santander (BME: SAN) brand
  • Customer onboarding channels and ability to keep deposits sticky
  • Income outlook and levers to grow NII and non-NII lines through cross-selling or product development
  • OPEX management, identifying opportunities to drive cost base rationalisation

Key Insights

  • Aim of Openbank Santander Consumer Finance integration is to enable customer cross-selling to Openbank products. Despite this, success appears limited – "Most customers don't know that they've become customers of Openbank […] the customer that is looking for a car is not looking for investments"
  • On the dual-brand strategy, the two businesses are naturally complementary. Rising interest rates benefit Santander's variable loan book while hurting Openbank, and falling rates do the opposite. Despite this, a cannibalisation threat exists, although expert views internal competition as positive and believes it has allowed Santander to grow internationally
  • Expert thinks Santander's market share in Spain has declined over past 10 years post-Banco Popular acquisition due to lack of profitable growth runway, as well as defensive competitors. Going forwards, focus is likely on efficiency enhancements. AI efficiency gains are challenging – mid-market management resists cost reduction strategies due to job security, requiring aggressive top-down pressure to succeed
