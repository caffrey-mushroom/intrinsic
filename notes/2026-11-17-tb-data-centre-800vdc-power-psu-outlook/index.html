原标题：APAC Data Centre Power Solutions – 800VDC, 2027 PSU Demand Outlook & Competitive Dynamics

#power #datacenter

Third Bridge · 2026-11-17

核心摘要

  1. 专家估算1GW数据中心总投资约100亿美元，800VDC最终架构可降低最多约15%的成本，但中间过渡架构基本没有成本节省。
  2. 800V所需电流约为230V的四分之一，可减少铜材用量和电能转换环节，提升效率。
  3. 中间架构预计2027-28年开始导入，超大规模云厂商渗透率约20-30%、托管数据中心约10%，此后每年提升约10个百分点；占容量约10%的私有云仍将沿用400VAC，超大规模和托管各占约45%。
  4. 2030年起基于固态变压器的最终架构开始落地，超大规模云厂商渗透率约30%、托管约5-10%，但目前相关产品仍处于原型阶段。
  5. 数据中心成本构成中土建约占30%、电气约占30%、冷却约占15-20%；液冷与800V架构相辅相成。
  6. AI负载会产生10-30%的功率尖峰，迫使电池超配或污染电网；超级电容削峰方案约每100kW需8000-10000美元，按10%过载计约每MW 1万美元。
  7. PSU成本约每kW 100美元；Nvidia采用0-800V方案、Google采用±400V方案，整体成本相近。
  8. 专家预计400VAC PSU需求将保持平稳，800VDC带来约20%的增量需求；数据中心容量年复合增速约10-15%，前十大市场中有5-6个位于东南亚和大洋洲，建设周期约12-18个月。
  9. 光宝以成本和12-14周交付周期竞争，而800V客户更看重技术与质量；行业正走向电网到芯片的一体化方案，例如施耐德收购Motivair。

Agenda

  • Key challenges facing the transition from 400V to 800V in data centres
  • Recent developments in HVDC (high-voltage direct current) adoption
  • Industry PSU (power supply unit) outlook and competitive dynamics

Key Insights

  • Building a 1GW data centre currently costs USD 10bn – transitioning to an 800VDC architecture can reduce costs by up to 15% while supporting significantly higher power
  • Hyperscalers will begin adopting intermediate 800VDC architectures at a 20-30% rate starting in 2027, while colocation providers are expected to lag at roughly 10%, and private clouds will remain almost entirely on 400VAC
  • AI workloads generate rapid 20-30% power spikes, forcing costly battery oversizing and causing grid-side harmonics and pollution. Supercapacitor banks can mitigate this through peak shaving, costing around USD 10,000 per megawatt
  • Total data centre power demand will grow 10-15% over next 2-3 years. PSUs cost approximately USD 1,000 per megawatt, with 800VDC units serving as a primary market catalyst
