原标题：Plaid – Payments Growth, Agentic Commerce & Pay by Bank Adoption

#Plaid #支付 #Agent

Third Bridge · 2026-11-05

核心摘要

  1. Plaid支付业务以账户对账户入金为主，如Venmo和Cash App，受金融科技和加密周期影响大。
  2. Plaid每笔约1美元，而标准ACH约0.05–0.08美元，大商户难以接受Pay by Bank的经济账。
  3. 专家认为可选消费的电商场景基本不会采用Pay by Bank，因为卡有拒付保护。
  4. Trustly拿下FanDuel和DraftKings后仍难以达到Plaid的渗透，最终退出美国。
  5. 讨论还涉及Plaid接入ChatGPT、Grok、Perplexity等AI应用后，在Agent支付中引导资金来源的角色。

Analyst-led • 30 Oct 2026

Companies: PYPL US, JPM US, VZ US, T US, PAY US

Matthew Watts (MW) Independent Consultant

Jonathan King (JK) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Operating environment for Plaid's payments business, discussing its core account linking, fraud and risk and money movement products
  • Key growth drivers over the next 2-3 years, including credit decisioning, fraud tooling and the integration of Plaid into AI applications such as ChatGPT, Grok and Perplexity
  • How could AI agents change the way consumers initiate payments, and what is Plaid's potential role in steering consumers to the optimal funding method at checkout?
  • Opportunity to expand Plaid's role in the payments value chain through partnerships with PSPs (payment service providers) such as Adyen (AMS: ADYEN), and which verticals are best-positioned

Key Insights

  • Plaid's payments business is dominated by account-to-account funding (Venmo, Cash App deposits), making it highly exposed to fintech and crypto market cycles, as seen in the 2022-23 downturn
  • Automated clearing house pricing creates structural barrier for Pay by Bank adoption. Plaid charges cUSD 1 per transaction vs USD 0.05-0.08 for standard automated clearing house, making large merchant economics difficult to justify
  • Regarding e-commerce opportunity, expert believes merchant adoption of Pay by Bank for discretionary purchases is largely "off the table" due to card chargeback protections and unfavourable automated clearing house reversal rules
  • Trustly's US retreat highlights Plaid's dominance. Despite winning FanDuel and DraftKings, Trustly "struggled to get to the same level of adoption" and mindshare Plaid built
  • Plaid's expansion into bill pay will likely remain gradual, as large merchants must justify significant checkout integration costs against limited incremental conversion, while existing automated clearing house pricing leaves little room for Plaid to command premium
