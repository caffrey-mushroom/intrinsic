核心摘要

  1. PwC英国保险咨询高级经理（PwC是Guidewire全球最大实施伙伴，20年来完成300多个项目）表示，截至9月的季度英欧需求基本符合预期，此前推迟的一笔大型全球许可证交易已签下，销售周期仍很长但管线健康。
  2. 核心保单和理赔套件仍是销售主力，客户通常在此基础上加购PricingCenter等产品，周边生态产品单独销售仍很少；本季度预计英欧不会有大单落地，但没有负面信号。
  3. 约12个月前“用ChatGPT/Anthropic自建核心系统”的讨论已基本平息，严格监管、安全要求和运营风险使保险公司更倾向选择成熟平台，尽管Guidewire往往比次优竞品贵出一个数量级。
  4. 竞争格局因地区而异：在美国、澳新和部分亚太地区主要对手是Duck Creek；欧洲很少见到Duck Creek，更多是伦敦市场的低价专业平台以及EIS、RGI；Sapiens和Majesco在英国很少正面交锋；大型全球一级保险公司几乎默认选择Guidewire。
  5. 收购Hyperexponential(HX)被专家评价为极具战略意义，它补足了专业险和复杂商业险在承保前的数据清洗、增强和定价能力，比Duck Creek收购的工作流工具Send更具战略价值；客户已在询问集成和许可方式，但细节尚未明确。
  6. Guidewire的AI在过去6-9个月开始落地，包括简化编码和升级的DevAssist、理赔摘要等前端代理，公开路线图几乎全部是AI；客户愿意为AI付费，但对消耗成本、AWS费用和许可影响仍不清楚，处于观望阶段。
  7. Guidewire提出AI可让实施成本降低30%，专家认为这一目标非常激进，但双位数降幅可能较快实现；云升级或单条业务线实施至少需要约8个月，约12个月后会有更清晰的证据。
  8. 欧洲约60%的Guidewire客户仍在本地部署，需要在未来3-4年完成云升级，客户讨论的是何时上云，而不是是否离开平台。
  9. 专家认为最大机会在于AI降低许可和实施成本，从而提升对价格敏感的中小型和专业保险公司的竞争力；最大风险是推进速度不够快，导致客户转向自建或新竞争者崛起，但她认为这一风险较低。

SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, an implementation partner deeply involved in Guidewire deployments, described demand across the UK and Europe as broadly in line with expectations. A large license agreement that had shifted from the prior quarter closed, while sales cycles remained lengthy and the pipeline appeared healthy. Core policy and claims products continue to drive sales; customers may add related tools, but standalone sales across the wider product ecosystem remain limited.

Insurers have largely moved away from considering whether to build core systems themselves. Regulatory obligations, security requirements, and operational risk make established platforms attractive despite their higher cost. Current customer decisions focus on demonstrating the business case, understanding AI’s role, and assessing implementation and cloud migration expenses.

Key Insights

AI could reshape Guidewire’s competitive position. The expert identified AI-enabled reductions in licensing and implementation costs as the company’s biggest opportunity, particularly with smaller insurers and specialty providers that are more price-sensitive. Execution is not yet proven. Customers are asking implementation partners to show how AI will reduce delivery time and cost. The expert considered a 30% reduction ambitious, though double-digit savings may emerge sooner. More persuasive evidence is expected after completed upgrades and implementations. The Hyperexponential acquisition drew a strongly positive response. The expert believes it extends Guidewire into specialty and complex commercial underwriting, particularly through data enrichment and pricing before a risk is bound. Customer questions about integration and licensing have already increased, though details remain unsettled. Cloud migration remains a substantial opportunity. The expert said customers’ main question is when and how to move to Guidewire Cloud, rather than whether to leave the platform.
