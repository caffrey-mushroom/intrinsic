SUMMARY

Overview

Electronic quality management systems help regulated life sciences companies document procedures, training, suppliers, product changes, complaints, and compliance activities. The broader regulated-technology market represents about $30 billion in global spending and includes roughly 10–15 major EQMS vendors.

Enzyme competes primarily in the mid-market, with particular strength in medical technology and software-based medical devices. Greenlight Guru acquired Enzyme in July 2025, although Enzyme remains separately available. The acquisition has made combined purchasing easier without materially changing Enzyme’s pricing structure.

Key Insights

Basic EQMS functions are highly commoditized and price-sensitive because most vendors provide similar document, training, and supplier workflows. Enzyme differentiates through capabilities supporting software-intensive medical devices, including connections with software development and product-design tools. Tiered packaging improves adoption by allowing emerging companies to purchase only the capabilities required at each development stage. Advanced integrations, continuous compliance monitoring, and regulatory support create greater differentiation and face less pricing pressure. Retention increasingly depends on AI-enabled automation, autonomous workflows, and expansion into adjacent compliance and regulatory functions. The expert viewed AI as the market’s most important emerging influence and suggested it warranted deeper follow-up analysis.
