Agenda

  • Trends and dynamics currently shaping the beer market in China
  • International brand owner longer-term growth opportunities and local competitive threats, including local player premiumisation strategies
  • Carlsberg's (CPH: CARL B) growth strategy and portfolio positioning, plus comparison to Heineken (AMS: HEIA)
  • Industry and Carlsberg supply chain repurposing in light of easing government subsidy support in local facilities

Key Insights

  • On premiumisation, premium beer holds only 15-17% market share in China, far below Europe's 60-70%, suggesting structural underpenetration rather than a saturated opportunity
  • Heineken, backed by CR Snow's distribution network, dominates on-trade visibility through Formula 1 sponsorships and pop-up activations, while Carlsberg focuses on regional guerrilla-style penetration strategies
  • In good bars with 6-8 taps, Heineken has significant visibility, but Carlsberg's local brands are able to penetrate more effectively into tier 3 and 4 towns
  • "Carlsberg has been doing regional plays in parts of China. [...] They do have restaurant tie-ups. They do have their own nightlife and bar tie-ups, especially for Carlsberg and Tuborg brands, which are more premium. They do have significant share of play in terms of modern retail as well as convenience and the O2O channel. I believe the overall strategy for Carlsberg is working more towards volume throughput and lesser on [...] premiumisation support"
