原标题：Lumen – Are Digital & AI Initiatives Going to Lead to Upsells?

#networking #datacenter

Third Bridge · 2026-10-06

Analyst-led • 23 Sep 2026

Companies: T US, LUMN US, CCOI US, MSFT US, VZ US

Tim Acker (TA) Operating Partner at BlueThread LLC

John Conca (JC) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Lumen's (NYSE: LUMN) operating environment, as well as hyperscaler and enterprise demand and retention trends
  • Competitive dynamics with Zayo, Cogent Communications (NASDAQ: CCOI), AT&T (NYSE: T) and others
  • Network upgrades and positioning for AI, noting NaaS (network-as-a-service) outlook and PCF (private connectivity fibre) deals
  • Outlook for Q3 2026, highlighting management's target of USD 700m of savings for 2026

Key Insights

  • Expanding consumption-based NaaS model beyond 3,000 enterprise accounts depends on hardware such as Project Berkeley to enable off-net provisioning across external local carrier loops
  • Additionally, legacy provisioning cycles, local loop economics and customer education gaps still remain primary friction points slowing broader NaaS adoption
  • Near-term strategic growth of 14.1% in Q2 2026 remains highly vulnerable to hardware supply constraints, while downstream edge AI applications lack sufficient maturity to offset potential hyperscaler spending pullbacks if they occur
  • Retiring legacy products carries hidden risks – enterprise and government customers embedded in older services face disruption, making product deprecation far slower and costlier than Lumen's originally anticipated transformation timelines
  • Monetising third-party software marketplaces represents a massive growth opportunity for telecom operators, yet historical attempts failed due to legacy billing friction, complex contracting and manual operational overhead, which Lumen's modernisation efforts could potentially address
