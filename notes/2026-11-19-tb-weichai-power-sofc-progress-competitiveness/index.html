Third Bridge · 2026-11-19

核心摘要

  1. 自2012年起从事SOFC研发的独立顾问介绍：Ceres金属支撑路线使用铈基电解质、工作温度约600°C，可激光焊接密封；Bloom Energy电解质支撑路线使用钪稳定氧化锆、工作温度约800-850°C，需玻璃陶瓷高温封装。
  2. 专家乐观预计潍柴动力SOFC首条产线将于2026年底打通，初始产能仅约2-3MW（与Ceres相当），单模块功率目标75-100kW，稳态效率约60%，短期完全复制Ceres的海外供应链，产能爬坡后再做国产替代。
  3. 潍柴此前在潍坊有210kW示范项目，西安和武汉医院也有示范，但未披露连续运行数据；专家估计其多套系统累计运行可能超过2万小时。
  4. Bloom Energy在美国的裸机售价约3000多美元/kW，电堆设计寿命约5万小时、系统寿命20年，这是国内厂商进入北美AIDC市场的成本红线；作为对比，内燃机不到1000元/kW，燃气轮机约1000-2000美元/kW。
  5. 考虑产品验证、北美认证（通常需一年以上）及70-80%本地化要求，专家认为潍柴SOFC进入北美AIDC市场至少还需两年，其柴油发电机作为备用电源与SOFC基荷电源形成互补。
  6. SOFC负载跟随和瞬态响应能力差，低于额定功率运行时效率会从60%降至50%左右，因此只适合作为数据中心的基荷电源，需配合储能或电网调峰。
  7. 潮州三环（CCTC）自研的氧化钇稳定氧化锆阳极支撑路线已完成超过2万小时连续验证，但作为Bloom Energy电解质片最大供应商（份额未达80%），其系统业务推广非常保守，仅在国内低调示范；佛燃能源使用三环电堆，未来可能需要培养其他供应商。
  8. 韩国斗山获得授权约四年才建成50MW产线且未满产，说明产能爬坡缓慢；Bloom商业模式包括设备销售和含换堆的运维服务，EMC模式回款慢、现金流较差。

Questions

00:03:03] What are the differences between Ceres's metal-support route and the metal-support route you previously worked on, for example in terms of structure, sealing scheme, and coating system? Apart from that, what other major differences are there?

Expert [00:03:30]: If we look from the most basic level, if we compare against Bloom Energy, the material systems of metal-supported and electrolyte-supported have very large differences, especially in the electrolyte material part. Ceres' electrolyte, or main electrolyte, is a ceramic material mainly based on ceria, while Bloom Energy mainly uses zirconia. Of course it is scandia-doped zirconia, not pure zirconia. From the perspective of material systems, the materials used are completely different, one is ceria-based, and one is zirconia-based. Because the materials are different, their operating temperatures are also different, and different materials have different conductivities at different temperatures. Ceres' operating temperature is around 600 degrees, while Bloom Energy's operating temperature is mostly above 800 degrees, probably around 800-850 degrees.

In terms of structure, this is more obvious. Bloom Energy's is called electrolyte support. It means that the electrolyte is the thickest among the three layers of cathode, anode and electrolyte. It serves not only as a functional layer but also as a structural layer to support the strength of the whole battery. The conductivity will be poorer if it is thicker, so the required operating temperature will also be higher. Ceres's is called metal-supported, meaning that its supporting or structural layer does not participate in any electrochemical reaction and exists only as a structural component. Metal plates serve as the support layer, while the functional layers are relatively thin, with the anode, electrolyte, and cathode each in the range of tens of microns.

The electrolyte of Bloom Energy may be less than 100μm or 200μm thick. This also leads to differences in their operating temperatures. Not only is the conductivity of the material poorer, but the electrolyte is thicker, so its operating temperature is relatively high. The materials and structures are different, which leads to great differences in use. Of course, the difference in temperature is the main thing for clients.

Ceres's is a metal support, so its metal is just a structural part. Only the parts involved in reaction are perforated, and the parts not involved in reaction are not perforated. Therefore, it can be directly laser welded with the flow field plate without side leakage problem. Relatively speaking, at least one side of it, such as the anode side, has stronger sealing reliability or processability.

However, Bloom Energy's is not so because its entire cell and the entire battery structure are supported by the electrolyte layer. It is just a pure ceramic cell, which cannot be quickly welded by laser because the material does not allow it. Therefore, it can only use a mixture of ceramic and glass for bonding, that is, high-temperature glass encapsulation to seal cathodes and anodes, which is relatively troublesome. In addition, its reliability and pressure resistance are not as high as those of laser welding. They have their own advantages and disadvantages in sealing. Of course, in terms of industrial reliability, laser welding will definitely be a good solution in the future.

00:29:07] Why is SOFC positioned as baseload power, while gas turbines are prime power? What characteristics does SOFC have that make it baseload power?

Expert [00:29:25]: Because SOFC is a device that should be fixed for a long time and output the same amount of electricity stably. Unlike a gas turbine which is rotating; at worst, it just spins slower, uses less fuel, and generates less electricity, the impact is not very big. However, SOFC is different; SOFC is best if, for example, when designed it is 65kW, it stays at 65kW, because when below 65kW, it cannot keep that 60% efficiency. If a 65kW device now only works at 30kW, then efficiency might not be 60%; maybe only 50% is left, operating efficiency becomes low, and costs become high.

Moreover, its load-following and transient response capabilities are also relatively poor. If a data centre's demand suddenly drops from 100kW to 30kW, the electricity generated cannot quickly follow; for example, going from 100kW to 150kW, it also cannot quickly ramp up from 100kW to 150kW, so it serves as baseload power. For example, if a data centre's daily electricity consumption is between 100-200kW, then I make 100kW, just steadily at the electricity level you demand.

The part that goes above that, either use energy storage to temporarily dispatch for it, when it suddenly increases or suddenly decreases, to dispatch, or use the grid to dispatch. I just continuously output this power, working at the most efficient place all the time, running at the lowest-cost place all the time, running for a long time; only then are costs low. Because cooling down and heating up take time, and changing load also takes time, it is not suitable for scenarios requiring fast response to electricity demand, so it can only serve as baseload power.
