核心摘要

  1. Adobe金牌合作伙伴Bluprintx北美CEO表示，三季度北美Workfront与Commerce业务同比增长接近15%，欧洲接近目标但略低，澳洲基本零增长。
  2. 北美强劲主要得益于过去12个月对本地团队和市场策略的投入，但收入越来越集中于少数大客户的大额订单，专家对此感到担忧，不过北美管道指标目前仍健康。
  3. 客户正从广泛的AI试验转向要求证明投资回报，AI在提升内容生产效率上效果明显，但能否转化为销售额增长仍难以量化。
  4. 随着交易金额变大，销售周期在拉长，内置AI功能使方案价格上升；Adobe在季末会积极协助推动成交，价格支持主要集中在新客户获取和大幅扩容上，而非续约。
  5. 合作伙伴已借助AI更快、更标准化地部署方案，客户对过去需1-3年完成的大型企业部署的时间预期正在缩短，目前节省主要体现为合作伙伴自身的效率。
  6. 零售客户使用AI最为激进，医疗和金融服务客户因出错代价高而更谨慎；客户尚未明显削减席位，更多是通过员工自然流失不补招，并灵活调配许可证。
  7. 专家表示Adobe所称Gen Studio经常性收入增长超过20%与其观察基本一致，Gen Studio与Workfront是其创意客户业务的核心。
  8. 过去12个月客户用AI自建工具替代小型简单功能的情况明显增多，构成新兴威胁，但内部工具依赖个别员工维护存在风险；专家对AI复刻的Adobe克隆品表示担忧。

SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, a leader at a consulting firm partnered with a major enterprise software provider, discussed recent performance across its regions and customer demand for marketing, commerce, workflow, and content tools. North America was strong, Europe was near target but slightly behind, and Australia was flat. The discussion also covered AI adoption, customer spending scrutiny, longer sales cycles, and emerging competition from internally built tools.

Key Insights

North American performance benefited from recent investment in the local team and sales approach, though the business relies increasingly on larger deals from fewer customers. The expert said this concentration is a risk, even though current pipeline indicators remain healthy. Customers are shifting from broad AI experimentation toward demanding evidence of returns. Productivity and faster content production are apparent benefits, but translating those gains into higher sales remains difficult to measure. AI is helping the consulting firm implement solutions more efficiently. The expert expects shorter implementation timelines to become more important to customers, though the methods are still developing. Adoption differs by industry: retailers are moving faster, while healthcare and financial services customers are more cautious because errors carry greater consequences. The expert said customers have not yet materially reduced software seats. Instead, they may allow staffing levels to decline through attrition, while managing licenses more flexibly. The moderator pursued concerns about AI-built substitutes and implementation savings. The expert described customer-built tools as an emerging threat, while emphasizing the maintenance risks of relying on internally created software.

Competitive Landscape

Offering or alternative

Market position and dynamics

Established enterprise software provider

Its tools remain difficult to replace and customer renewals are generally sticky. AI features are contributing to higher solution costs, but pricing is not always transparent to customers.

Customer-built AI tools and agents

Increasingly used to replace smaller, simpler software functions. They may erode established platforms gradually, but ongoing maintenance and reliance on individual employees are risks.

AI tools connected across platforms

Integrations with other business systems can expand AI use beyond the original platform and potentially reduce dependence on individual components.

AI-generated software replicas

The expert views these as concerning, with uncertain defenses and potential legal consequences.
