Agenda

  • Enterprise Advanced adoption, pricing uplift and customer expansion
  • Box Extract and Automate as drivers of new workflow adoption
  • OpenAI partnership and Box's (NYSE: BOX) role as an enterprise AI content layer
  • AI agent integrations, security and opportunities to replace legacy systems
  • competitive positioning and the potential for sustained revenue growth

Key Insights

  • Box experienced significant churn in 2023-24 as enterprises consolidated software spending, frequently favouring Microsoft's bundled offerings despite Box's differentiated capabilities
  • Microsoft's entrenched enterprise position remains Box's largest competitive obstacle, although its platform-neutral AI strategy and support for multiple large language models provide meaningful differentiation
  • Enterprise Advanced offers a compelling value proposition by consolidating e-signature, content management and document processing into one platform, potentially replacing more expensive standalone solutions
  • Financial services represents Box's largest vertical growth opportunity, with expert anticipating adoption to accelerate as early deployments validate the platform and encourage broader uptake across regulated institutions
  • Expert believes double-digit revenue growth is achievable over the near-term, supported by AI adoption, per-seat pricing increases and legacy content management replacement, particularly as bundled AI capabilities strengthen Box's SMB positioning
