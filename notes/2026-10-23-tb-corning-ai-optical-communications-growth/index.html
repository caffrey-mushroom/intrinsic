Companies: GLW US, 8053 JP, NVDA US, LITE US

Vishoka Balasubramanian (VB) Co-Founder and CRO at LoadCrest

Colin Duncan (CD) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Corning's (NYSE: GLW) Enterprise Networks growth and hyperscaler AI infrastructure demand
  • Optical connectivity content gains from scale-out and scale-up networking
  • Photonics opportunity, CPO (co-packaged optics) and NPO (near-packaged optics) adoption and competitive positioning
  • Growth normalisation, pricing power and margin outlook through 2028-30

Key Insights

  • Corning's 30-50% optical revenue growth could begin decelerating in 18 months, normalising towards 10-15% annually as supply constraints ease, hyperscaler spending matures and competition intensifies
  • Optical connectivity pricing could face c5% annual declines once supply normalises, while Corning's Optical Communications net income margin remains c20-21%, supported by automation and manufacturing efficiencies
  • Copper could dominate GPU scale-up networking for another five years, with near-packaged optics advancing ahead of co-packaged optics as reliability concerns and integration challenges delay adoption near compute
  • Corning's projected 1.3-1.5x increase in optical content per GPU by 2028 appears achievable, driven by larger AI clusters and fibre-based scale-out networking rather than near-term copper replacement within GPU racks
  • Corning's optical growth is unlikely to materially outpace the market in the long term, given five suppliers control 85-90% of AI data centre optical demand with limited technical differentiation
