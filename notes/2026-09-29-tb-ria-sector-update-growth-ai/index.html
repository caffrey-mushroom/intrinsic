Agenda

  • RIA (registered investment advisor) industry organic growth opportunities and winning net new assets
  • Ongoing M&A and asset valuations
  • AI and technology impacts on financial advisors
  • Outlook for Q4 2026 and beyond, highlighting advisor moves and demand for independence

Key Insights

  • EBITDA multiples should remain stable, with potential shifts coming from the cost of capital. More integrated and client-owned companies are getting higher valuations. Buyers are taking a closer look at the growth capabilities of the firms they're acquiring
  • Client retention will continue to be strong – in mid-to-high-90s – for RIA aggregators and integrators. Organic growth is 2-3%, but advisors leveraging AI could drive that to high-single digits
  • Expert sees a potential pricing shift away from 1% of assets under management to a fixed-fee model for younger advisors and newer, smaller assets-under-management clients. Advisors are comfortable using AI and technology to engage with the whole family and have more time being client-facing
  • "There could be a second breakaway movement where the large national RIAs, when you start looking at them compared to the classic wirehouse […] they start feeling and looking the same from a bureaucracy perspective"
