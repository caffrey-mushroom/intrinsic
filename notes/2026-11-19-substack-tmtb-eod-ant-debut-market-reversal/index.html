Happy Anthropic IPO day… for about 30 minutes. QQQs -2.2% in a nasty outside reversal after trading up as much as 2.4% in the first half hour, with semis -2.7% round-tripping a +4.8% morning high and software -1% after +4% open. No one had any good reasons for the fade, just more of the same concerns around the AI trade coupled with a worsening macro as charts continue to turn south. Things were already going down when Hunterbrook’s AMD short report hit at 10:30am; QQQs then went down 1.5% in less than 5 minutes after ANT’s opening trade, and after that it was just a steady grind lower throughout the afternoon that finished below Tuesday’s lows and lowest close since late April. SOX still holding above Tuesday’s lows. No hiding spots either: SPY -2%; IWM -2.2%, SPY’s lowest close since 4/29, with UNH, V and housing all hit. VIX closed at 28.44, +16%, its highest close since 3/30.

ANT +17% finished at $97 vs its $83 IPO price, traded as high as $115 but was below $100 within the first half hour and closed near the lows. Down 2% in AH so far. Welcome to the public markets, Dario.

Yesterday, we called the mood despondent. Today somehow managed to make yesterday feel optimistic despite the early celebratory mood around ANT, and frustration spread outside of just AI semi investors.

To cheer up the bulls, we went on the hunt for some short-term positive tea leaves. A few stats:

  • NDX was up 2%+ intraday and closed down 2%+, which has happened only 4 since 2009: 3/20/20, 7/13/20, 11/20/25 and today. The last one was a year ago almost to the day, the NVDA-print reversal, which was also the bottom. 3/20/20 and 11/20/25 both marked the closing lows. 7/13/20 was more a reversal from a record high. In both of the closign lows, the final intraday low came the next session, so a lower low tomorrow wouldn’t break the pattern.
  • NDX was up 2%+ intraday and closed down 2%+, which has happened 30 times since 1985. The next 5 days had a median of +4.6%
  • NDX opened up 1.5%+ and still closed down 2%+, which has happened 6 times since 2000: 10/17/01, 7/10/02, 1/25/08, 10/14/08, 11/20/25 and today. 4 out 5 of previous times were at least a 5 day closing low, but only ‘25 and ‘01 held for the next 3 months.

  • In early Oct, QQQs made new highs while SMH didn’t (neg divergence). Today QQQs made new lows while SMH didn’t (pos divergence). QQQs closed just 0.4% below its 11/5 close, but every gauge was less extreme than on 11/5. QQQs are making post-election lows, while majority of semis still above the 11/5 close:

Not making any calls here (we’re in cash in our ST/MT accounts, its dangerous out there) just posting the stats. Don’t shoot the messenger.

In macro land, the data didn’t help things: the Philly Fed’s factory gauge sank to 6.3 vs. 23.2 expected (lowest since May) while prices paid rose to 58.2, and continuing claims rose 70K to 1.763M, the highest since late August. December hike odds still at ~90% for 25bp, ~10% for 50bp after yesterday’s hawkish minutes with ~80% odds of a cumulative 50bp by the January meeting.
