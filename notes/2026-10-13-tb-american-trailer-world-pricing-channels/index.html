Agenda

  • ATW's (American Trailer World's) pricing and margin trends
  • Inventory and channel dynamics
  • Market share outlook for customers and dealers
  • Raw material impacts and price-pass-on potential

Key Insights

  • "[A] lot of the challenges that they see on the price front are actually driven by the way that they've consolidated their production into a few manufacturing sites that have limited production capabilities, and maybe they're not optimising their freight". Heightened freight costs from 0.5-10% are driving weak pricing post capacity rationalisation
  • Regarding potential 2027 sales weakness based on key indicators, "I see a lot of aged inventory that's been sitting out there that's got some heavy discounts". "[B]ased on what I see in their inventory online, they're going to have to take further margin hits"
  • Expert estimates ATW has rationalised 20% capacity and now have c60-75% utilisation across factories – 80% is the best-in-class metric. Expert believes Carry-On lost distribution at one major retailer, which is also driving under-utilisation
  • "[T]ruck beds, they've got to leverage that. It's got the largest margin opportunity of all of the product offerings that they have"
