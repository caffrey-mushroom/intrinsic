Companies: EFX US, ABG US

Agenda

  • Tekion's progress in disrupting legacy DMS (dealership management system) platforms with its cloud-native ARC (Automotive Retail Cloud)
  • Dealer adoption, retention, expansion and the opportunity to consolidate more workflows onto one platform
  • Competitive positioning vs CDK, Reynolds and Reynolds and other automotive software providers
  • How Tekion's agentic AI and T1 Pro interface could automate dealership workflows and strengthen its platform advantage

Key Insights

  • Tekion's early growth benefited from CDK and Reynolds' poor customer relations, but premium pricing, long-term contracts and deteriorating support have increasingly alienated its original customer base
  • Implementation challenges compound these concerns, with expert questioning the scalability of Tekion's resource-intensive Asbury deployment and growing reliance on outsourced implementation teams
  • Despite these issues, expert expects 12-15% rooftop growth to translate into 20-25% ARR growth, supported by higher pricing on new contracts relative to legacy customers
  • Tekion's unified platform offers a potential AI advantage, but limited third-party data integration and persistent data consolidation issues constrain its ability to deliver differentiated capabilities
  • Looking ahead, Cox's acquisition of Fullpath could intensify competitive pressure, while failure to deliver promised AI features at National Automobile Dealers Association show could undermine Tekion's growth momentum and dealer confidence
