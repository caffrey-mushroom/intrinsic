#software #agents

GuidePoint · 2026-11-11

核心摘要

  1. 一家咨询公司的数据与AI副总裁表示，其Salesforce相关业务第三季度增长19%，落在18%-20%的预期区间内，略低于上季度的23%；第四季度情况大致相同，管道中约有4个潜在大单。
  2. 增长主要来自Data 360/Data Cloud的大型实施项目，目的是为Agentforce做数据准备：企业必须先把SharePoint、PDF、视频、ERP、S3日志等结构化和非结构化数据导入并治理好，否则下游agent会因为“垃圾进垃圾出”而失败。
  3. 专家认为Data Cloud是Salesforce最关键的切入点和领先指标：客户一旦上了Data Cloud，就会被交叉销售Agentforce（按解决次数计费并消耗token）、Informatica（替代ETL）、MuleSoft、Slack等产品，形成长尾收入。
  4. 续约率估计在80%以上，客户进入Data Cloud后的留存和续签率约80%-90%；收购Informatica后，Salesforce作为数据公司的定位更容易被客户接受。
  5. 数据准备工作复杂且昂贵，需要零拷贝接入Snowflake、BigQuery、Redshift、Databricks，通过MuleSoft连接传统系统，建立规范数据模型，并按部门严格控制权限。
  6. 竞争方面，Salesforce适合愿意大投入的大企业；HubSpot、Workday、ServiceNow在价格敏感的中小企业更有优势，但专家认为它们只能用上约10%的企业数据，客户日后升级到大平台需要付出二次迁移成本。
  7. Dreamforce的亮点是与Anthropic的合作和Data Cloud插件，专家认为Anthropic的MCP已成为连接agent与工具的事实标准，Salesforce采取模型中立策略；后续需要关注中端客户从HubSpot等平台迁移到Salesforce的趋势。

SUMMARY

Overview

The expert discussed Salesforce-related consulting growth, demand for Data Cloud implementations, and enterprise adoption of Agentforce. The central theme was that companies need to consolidate, organize, and govern their data before AI agents can reliably support business workflows. The discussion also covered Salesforce’s competitive position, customer retention, and developments highlighted at Dreamforce.

Key Insights

The expert said Q3 growth met expectations and Q4 appeared broadly similar, with several potentially large deals in the pipeline. Data Cloud was presented as Salesforce’s key entry point for broader customer adoption and cross-selling. Once organizations invest in it, they may expand into Agentforce and other Salesforce products. Preparing data for AI is complex and costly. It involves integrating structured and unstructured information, defining how data relates, and controlling access across departments. The expert viewed this foundational work as essential to getting value from AI. Salesforce’s breadth may give it an advantage with large enterprises, while its price and implementation demands can make smaller platforms more attractive to budget-conscious customers. The expert highlighted Anthropic’s Model Context Protocol as an emerging standard for connecting AI tools and agents, and described Salesforce’s approach as allowing customers to use different models. The expert identified migration from mid-market platforms to larger enterprise systems as an important trend to monitor.
