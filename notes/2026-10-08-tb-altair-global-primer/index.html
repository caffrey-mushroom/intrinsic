Could you describe what Altair Global does and its products and services?

Expert: Altair Global are a global relocation management company. They offer end-to-end relocation services and coordinate all of those services from end to end. They also provide additional services such as destination services in EMEA and APAC. They also offer industry consulting, support things like business travellers and they have an Affinity Program, which is where they can connect people to their supply chain without them being involved. They benefit really from their vetted supply chain and work directly with their supply chain.

What is the value proposition of Altair's products and services?

Expert: The value proposition is really to support corporates in attracting, retaining and developing their talent. It's using relocation to onboard and attract new talent. It gives the possibility to actually develop through international exposure and international experiences. What I'm seeing from Altair, and what they push, is that they are an experienced management company. They use four key pillars, really, to drive that experience. It's the client, which is corporates they work with directly, mobility teams. It's the customer, which is a relocating employee. It's then the partner, which is their supply chain and then the team pillar, which obviously relates to their employees. Very much push the global expertise, so being in every region. So that's EMEA, which is Europe, the Middle East and Africa, the Americas, so APAC and they also stand out from a perspective of being almost right-sized. They are large enough in terms of their coverage, but in our industry, being the biggest isn't always the best. They are of a size where clients won't feel like they are lost or that they are not big enough to actually work without… I would say one callout, which might be of interest when looking on their website, is that they have called out being independent in terms of their supplier management. What they are pushing is that they will always look towards the best partners and not based on their own sort of P&L.
