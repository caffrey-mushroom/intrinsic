#financials

Third Bridge · 2026-10-16

Analyst-led • 9 Oct 2026

Companies: OSB GB, LINV GB, MTRO GB, SHAW GB, PAG GB

Scot Reilley (SR) Operational Risk Specialist, 2LoD at Crown Agents Bank Ltd

Max Harper (MH) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Product mix analysis, anticipating logic and potential success around diversifying beyond core buy-to-let
  • Income outlook, focusing on NIM (net interest margin) and drivers of expansion or contraction arising from the funding mix
  • Operating infrastructure, assessing ability to gain a competitive advantage from transformation programme due to completion in 2027 and outlook for AI gains
  • Competitive landscape – identifying strengths and weaknesses over peers and anticipating potential appetite for market consolidation

Key Insights

  • Credit discipline is generally good, with buy-to-let loanees more likely to be professional landlords than small-scale landlords. Despite this, tail risk is a factor, with collections and arrears being largely manual. Market headwinds may overwhelm systems, putting OSB under pressure
  • OSB's transformation programme, targeting completion by 2027 at GBP 190m, is likely 1-2 years behind schedule, with estimated 20-30% additional spend needed to deliver original success criteria. The platform is also seen as key to unlocking loan book diversification in line with strategy, and in line with peers, but not ahead tech-wise
  • OSB's regional talent pool in Chatham and Wolverhampton is seen as structural constraint, limiting access to high-calibre professionals needed to support strategic growth and transformation ambitions
  • Retail deposit stickiness from legacy Kent Reliance customers provides some funding stability, but competition from fintechs for new money poses growing risk to OSB's 89% retail deposit funding base
