Agenda

  • DataSnipper's competitive positioning, focusing on breadth vs depth
  • Barriers to switching, NRR (net revenue retention) outlook and AI impact
  • Market penetration and growth opportunities

Key Insights

  • Financial buyers would see value in expected 15-25% ARR growth – 20-30% of which comes from new verticals – in a base case scenario, accelerating to a doubling of revenue in 2-3 years dependent on agentic product execution. Strategic buyers could use DataSnipper to expand existing audit portfolios
  • DataSnipper is a "mature industry leader" established in external audit, however it requires a cohesive push to expand TAM, particularly in internal audit. While the company enjoyed 50-60% win rates in external audit, ROI is less apparent in internal and win rates of 20% would be expected
  • Gross revenue retention sits at c90%, but NRR lands near 100% once up- and cross-sells are included. Churn is concentrated to lower tiers
  • Large networks – likely 25% of revenue – are expected to take Alwin, with a major central firm deal expected in the coming 12 months and carrying significant revenue uplifts. Discussions are still ongoing
