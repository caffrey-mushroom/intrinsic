原标题：PE Sector Update: North America Renewable Energy Hardware – Market Opportunities, Competitive Positioning & Value Creation

#储能 #光伏 #关税

Third Bridge · 2026-11-05

核心摘要

  1. 储能是增长最快的新能源硬件板块，不受OBBBA影响，80–90%的大型光伏项目配套储能。
  2. Nextracker、GameChange、韩华等正通过并购做垂直整合，提供整站方案。
  3. 安全港规则把单笔采购上限设为200万美元，要求证明持续施工，项目高峰被推到2027–30年。
  4. 老旧设备、极端天气和供应商退出推动了改造需求，跟踪支架控制器和逆变器最常被替换。
  5. 专家估计约75–80%的项目正在推进本土制造或受到关税不确定性的实质影响。

Agenda

  • Demand drivers and growth opportunities
  • Business models and commercial economics
  • PE investment opportunities, value creation and risk assessment

Key Insights

  • Battery storage is the fastest-growing hardware segment, remaining unaffected by the One Big Beautiful Bill Act, with 80-90% of utility-scale projects now including storage components alongside solar
  • On domestic manufacturing, companies such as Nextracker, GameChange and Hanwha are aggressively vertically integrating, acquiring complementary businesses to offer full power plant solutions rather than individual components
  • Safe harbour rule change capped single purchases at USD 2m, forcing developers to demonstrate "continuous construction", fundamentally reshaping procurement timelines and pushing major project activity towards 2027-30
  • Repowering is an emerging high-growth opportunity, driven by ageing equipment, extreme weather damage and obsolete suppliers, with tracker controls and inverters being the most frequently replaced components
  • Expert believes c75-80% of current projects are either actively pursuing domestic manufacturing or experiencing meaningful financial impact from ongoing tariff uncertainty under the current administration
