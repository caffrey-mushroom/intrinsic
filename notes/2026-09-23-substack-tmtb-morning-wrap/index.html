Data Center Power: GS raises BTM outlook to 67GW by 2030, sees fuel cells emerging as fastest-to-power solution

META/MUSE

EXPE/BKNG/ABNB/CART/META: MS says Muse partnerships show agentic commerce moving from discovery toward transaction capture

MS says EXPE becoming Muse’s first major OTA partner and CART integrating grocery checkout show META pushing Muse further down the commerce funnel, potentially opening a path to transaction economics as agents increasingly control the customer interface. For travel, MS sees BKNG best positioned given differentiated supply and stronger direct execution, while ABNB has more to lose given ~90% direct traffic; EXPE could benefit from incremental traffic/lower acquisition costs if the partnership scales. CART similarly gains a new discovery/share channel, though the longer-term risk for all intermediaries is Muse increasingly sitting between the platform and the end customer.

Agentic AI/META: Citrini says Muse marks the consumer-agent inflection, shifting value from friction-based intermediaries toward infrastructure + transaction rails

Citrini argues Muse is the first consumer agent to achieve real mass-market product fit, making agentic disintermediation less theoretical as consumers outsource shopping, negotiating, cancelling and switching decisions. Its framework favors META, SHOP, TWLO/BAND/RNG, cybersecurity/identity, NET, V/MA and underlying data/transaction infrastructure, while seeing growing pressure on OTAs, aggregators, subscription businesses and other models that monetize consumer inertia, information asymmetry or switching friction.

INOD: Citrini/Hunterbrook see Innodata as an underappreciated upstream Muse beneficiary

Citrini says interviews and company disclosures point to META as INOD’s largest customer and notes Innodata recently disclosed a new program with that customer focused on “personalization of long-horizon agents,” which closely resembles Muse work, though neither company has confirmed the linkage. Citrini argues INOD is expanding beyond traditional labeling into agentic RL environments, computer-use training and human-judgment datasets, while highlighting both the upside from deeper META engagement and the concentration risk.

META: Cantor raises PT to $860, sees Muse as a potential third AI S-curve with $1T+ TAM

Cantor says personal agents could become the third major AI S-curve, with META well positioned through Muse’s model/harness/UX, distribution and massive compute footprint; Cantor sizes the broader personal-agent TAM at potentially >$1T. While Muse currently requires heavily subsidized inference, Cantor expects cost/task to fall >80% over 18–24 months and believes META can support rapid adoption using only ~5–10% of its projected 14GW FY27 compute footprint. Cantor reiterates OW and raises PT to $860 from $680.

IONQ: Demonstrates real-time quantum error decoding on a standard CPU – IonQ

Has IONQ +11% and other quantum stocks higher

> IonQ says it demonstrated end-to-end, real-time quantum error correction decoding on an off-the-shelf CPU, using simulated circuits with up to 408 logical qubits and as little as 0.02% added execution time. The research used 12 cores of an Apple M4 Max to process the error information needed to guide corrections. The result addresses a potential classical-computing bottleneck in fault-tolerant quantum systems. These are simulated quantum workloads with a working classical decoder; the benchmark does not establish operation of a 408-logical-qubit quantum computer.

Citi says after meeting with CEO Strauss Zelnick, CFO Lainie Goldstein and IR Nicole Shevins, management declined to confirm reports that GTA Online launches in CY27 but reiterated focus on both new-franchise development and GTA Online. Enterprise AI: FUNDA says spending keeps rising despite optimization, with growth shifting from seats toward APIs + production applications

FUNDA’s latest 10-company enterprise survey finds AI spending still increasing at 9 of 10 companies, even as model switching, caching and procurement negotiations lower unit costs; once employee seats mature, incremental growth increasingly shifts toward APIs, agents and deeper workflow usage. Companies are also becoming more ROI-driven, with proven applications continuing to receive funding while new projects increasingly need to show cost savings, revenue uplift or capacity gains. FUNDA also sees growing use of open-weight/smaller models for routine and private-data workloads, while Fable/Astra-class frontier models remain concentrated in complex R&D, planning and reasoning rather than broad production

Samsung: Brings hybrid bonding forward in next-generation DRAM roadmap – ZDNet Korea

> ZDNet Korea reports Samsung has brought its B1b hybrid-bonding project into the formal DRAM roadmap as D0a, while continuing vertical-channel transistor development on a separate D0a-V track. The approach bonds separately manufactured memory-cell and peripheral-circuit wafers, improving density while avoiding some of the transistor redesign’s manufacturing hurdles. Industry sources expect commercialization no earlier than around 2028, with investment timing potentially set in mid-2027. Samsung is also considering the technology for HBM5, expected in 2029, making this an important process and equipment roadmap update rather than an immediate capacity increase.

QCOM / GOOGL: Expand agentic AI partnership across phones, PCs and vehicles – DigiTimes

> DigiTimes reports Qualcomm and Google are expanding agentic AI integration across phones, Googlebooks and vehicles, with Gemini Intelligence set to accompany new smartphones in 2026. A separate DigiTimes report says Qualcomm is developing a Snapdragon-specific high bandwidth compute (HBC) coprocessor that brings memory and AI processing closer together to improve efficiency. Qualcomm plans to extend HBC to notebooks, glasses and vehicles, with further details due at MWC 2027.

LG Electronics / MSFT: Partner on data-center infrastructure, cooling and physical AI – DigiTimes

Samsung / QCOM: LPDDR6 passes Snapdragon validation at 10.7Gbps – TrendForce / DigiTimes
