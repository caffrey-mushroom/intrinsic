SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, a government-focused technology services provider, discussed how election changes, budget decisions, and AI adoption may affect federal, state, and local software spending. Public-sector decisions generally move slowly, but spending and project approvals have begun to recover after a period of disruption. The expert expects clearer AI policies and governance requirements to shape future investments.

Key Insights

The expert is bullish on public-sector technology spending, noting that new initiatives are advancing and that current budgets appear largely set. Government AI adoption is cautious: agencies are establishing controls for employee use and testing AI through established platforms rather than deploying it broadly. The expert has not seen government headcount fall because of AI. Some tools are helping staff handle workloads and may reduce the need for additional hiring. Emerging AI-native vendors are attracting interest but rarely winning government contracts. Limited policy guidance and security concerns are major obstacles. The expert sees ServiceNow as well positioned to benefit from future spending on AI governance, describing its AI Control Tower as a likely early investment for states adopting AI. Budget pressure has led CIOs to scrutinize contracts and demand clearer value, while keeping core platforms in place.
