Companies: GDDY US, AIRO US, SHOP US, WIX US

Brent Harrison (BH) Founder & Growth Adviser at Brent Harrison Advisory

Dylan Koehler (DK) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Assess whether Airo is converting domain buyers into higher-value software customers
  • Discuss Airo’s (NASDAQ: AIRO) impact on website creation, customer acquisition and retention
  • Explore how AI-native website builders could disrupt traditional domains and hosting
  • Assess whether product adoption can accelerate revenue growth and expand margins

Key Insights

  • Airo's annualised bookings surged to USD 50m but remain below 1% of revenue. Its 70% second-product attach rate is encouraging, although sustained renewals at full price remain unproven
  • GoDaddy has struggled to match AI-native competitors' execution speed. This comes down to organisational culture and slow decision-making, rather than engineering capacity, potentially limiting AI-driven productivity gains
  • As AI shifts discovery away from traditional websites, expert sees greater opportunity in domains, identity, payments and infrastructure supporting AI-built applications, rather than competing directly on website creation
  • GoDaddy has limited room to expand beyond its 33% EBITDA margin target, with just 100-200bps of additional upside as performance marketing investments and rising AI inference costs constrain profitability
  • Expert questions the strategic fit and synergies of a potential Gen Digital takeover, suggesting GoDaddy's attractive cash generation and SMB distribution might not justify an acquisition, given its comparable standalone valuation
