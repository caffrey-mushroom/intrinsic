Agenda

  • Rail Vikas Nigam's (NSE: RVNL) order book execution and revenue visibility for FY27-29, including key project timelines and bottlenecks
  • Margin outlook as the mix shifts towards competitive bidding and PMC (project management consultancy) projects
  • Order inflow visibility and contribution from non-railway segments and international projects

Key Insights

  • Expert notes revenue from the Vande Bharat sleeper project is expected to begin in FY27. Following prototype validation in April 2027, production will scale from two to five trains monthly, completing the 120-train order by FY31
  • To optimise execution timelines and margins in complex engineering, procurement and construction contracts such as elevated metro corridors, expert notes Rail Vikas Nigam needs to establish an in-house design wing, eliminating reliance on third-party designers that currently creates bidding bottlenecks
  • To combat margin compression from competitive bidding, expert believes Rail Vikas Nigam must transition from a project management consultancy to a direct engineering, procurement and construction contractor. Direct labour engagement and material procurement on par with L&T could expand EBITDA margins beyond 7%
  • As Indian Railways transitions its entire network to the ETCS level 2 Kavach system, expert highlights an emerging high-margin pipeline. Rail Vikas Nigam's deep internal expertise in signalling engineering positions it to capture dominant market share
