原标题：Pelacarsen Trial Failure & Potential Read-through to Ongoing CVOTs – By ZEUS the HORIZON is Dark, Can NewAmsterdam PREVAIL?

#pharma

Third Bridge · 2026-10-19

Analyst-led • 8 Oct 2026

Companies: IONS US, NAMS US, NOVN CH, NOVO B DK, AMGN US

Michael Sills (MS) Cardiologist at Baylor University Medical Center

Sebastian Skeet (SS) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Potential causes for pelacarsen's HORIZON study miss, implications for Lp(a) causality hypothesis and read-through to olpasiran's ongoing OCEAN(a) study
  • Ziltivekimab ZEUS study failure and implications for anti-inflammatory approaches to treating CVD
  • Outlook for NewAmsterdam's (NASDAQ: NAMS) obicetrapib PREVAIL CVOT (cardiovascular outcomes trial)

Key Insights

  • HORIZON missed due to trial design issues – baseline LDL-C and Lp(a) too low, patients well-controlled at baseline – and pelacarsen's "modest" Lp(a)-lowering. OCEAN(a) has a >50% probability of success, given deeper Lp(a) reductions, higher baseline Lp(a) and improved primary endpoint. Lp(a) causality hypothesis is not buried
  • Highly athero-protective polypharmacy, including SGLT2s and GLP-1s, has made a 15%-plus MACE benefit difficult to achieve without far larger numbers and trial durations
  • BROADWAY's MACE benefit is not a true signal. Nevertheless, PREVAIL has design advantages over HORIZON – higher baseline LDL, higher-risk and less well-controlled population and a modified primary endpoint
  • Expert expects PREVAIL to show a statistically and clinically significant c15% cardiovascular benefit, largely driven by LDL-C reductions with potential contribution from small-LDL-particle depletion, but cautions that remaining large particles may be more atherogenic than thought. REVEAL study also supports a positive PREVAIL outcome, given trial design differences and efficacy in highest-baseline non-HDL-C patients
