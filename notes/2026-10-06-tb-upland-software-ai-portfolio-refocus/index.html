Agenda

  • Discuss the strategic reset under the new CEO
  • Growth opportunities across knowledge and content automation
  • AI adoption across BA Insight, Panviva and RightAnswers
  • Upland Software (NASDAQ: UPLD) portfolio focus, customer expansion and opportunities to reaccelerate growth
  • Assess margin expansion, free cash flow and the path to deleveraging

Key Insights

  • Upland's legacy knowledge management products remain defensible in regulated industries such as banking and healthcare, where deep historical data and significant change management risks create high switching barriers
  • However, AI is reshaping the category as enterprises increasingly automate customer service, threatening traditional per-user pricing, while making structured knowledge data increasingly valuable for AI agents
  • Upland has yet to fully capitalise on that opportunity, with Panviva, RightAnswers and BA Insight remaining largely unintegrated despite enterprise buyers increasingly favouring consolidated platforms
  • Expert sees additional execution risk from Upland's limited public evidence of innovation and absence of dedicated senior growth leadership, particularly across sales, marketing and AI strategy
  • Return to flat growth by 2027 likely requires clear catalysts such as major customer wins, meaningful AI innovation or cross-product integration
