核心摘要

  1. 数据平台顾问表示，其Snowflake和Databricks相关咨询业务在10月季度均同比持平，符合此前持平的预期，并对1月季度同样按持平建模。

  6. 近期几乎看不到Snowflake与Databricks之间的工作负载迁移，客户更多是在现有生态内深耕并实验AI。
  7. Lakebase和Snowflake Postgres对初创公司新项目有吸引力，但拥有既有架构的大型企业很少迁移；专家从未见过客户使用Arctic模型，企业更多与Google、Anthropic签大额协议。
  8. 由于AI可代替用户操作软件，Databricks以往因技术复杂带来的门槛可能不再重要，专家难以判断两家平台谁将胜出。

SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, a data-platform and AI consultant, discussed enterprise use of Snowflake and Databricks, including AI initiatives, spending, optimization, and competition. Client attention has shifted from tuning existing data pipelines toward deploying AI and finding ways to use company data through AI tools. The expert’s related consulting activity was flat in the recent quarter for both platforms.

Key Insights

AI is changing how organizations build and use data pipelines. It is moving from helping technical teams write code toward generating workflows and insights more directly. Many companies are investing to avoid falling behind, rather than following clear AI strategies or measuring returns. The expert expects stronger scrutiny of costs and business value eventually, but could not reliably forecast when. Security, data access, governance, and spending controls are growing concerns as AI becomes more integrated into daily work. The expert sees fewer migrations between Snowflake and Databricks. Many customers are concentrating on their existing platforms while experimenting with AI. AI may reduce the importance of software usability as a competitive differentiator, making it difficult to predict which platform will gain an advantage. The moderator’s questions about platform growth, AI product usage, and future spending prompted the expert to stress that current evidence is limited and forecasts are unusually uncertain.

Snowflake and Databricks

Customers often retain their existing ecosystems, sometimes for cost, governance, or resilience reasons. The expert has not observed recent workflow transfers between them.

Lakebase and Snowflake Postgres

The expert sees some appeal for new projects, particularly among startups, but little migration by larger enterprises with established data architectures.

Large and small AI models

Larger models generally perform better, according to the expert, but may cost more. Smaller, purpose-built models could be more efficient for selected tasks.
