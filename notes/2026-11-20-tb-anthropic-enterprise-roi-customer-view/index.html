原标题：Anthropic – Customer Angle – Measuring Enterprise ROI at Production Scale

#llm #ai-demand

Third Bridge · 2026-11-20

核心摘要

  1. ThoughtWorks数据与AI负责人（与Anthropic合作约2.7年，同时使用谷歌、OpenAI和英伟达Nemotron等模型）认为，在软件工程领域Anthropic是最好的前沿模型，而摘要、分析等用例中端模型或开源权重模型即可胜任，财富500强大多数工作并不需要前沿模型。
  2. 今年企业合同续约从固定费率转向按量计费的API定价，算力硬件受限导致类似“高峰定价”，CFO难以预测月度支出；Uber CTO称全年预算四个月就用完的案例反映了这一问题。
  3. 专家估计Anthropic超过40%的收入经由第三方超大规模云厂商渠道，而这些平台内置模型编排和智能路由，客户可以轻松切换模型，Anthropic并不拥有这一渠道。
  4. 硅谷初创企业对开源权重模型的采用率可能高达60-70%，部分直接使用Kimi、GLM等中国模型；受监管的企业更倾向尝试英伟达Nemotron，开源权重模型在过去六个月进步很快。
  5. 由于Anthropic缺乏原生自动路由且对约90%的用例而言太贵，企业正在构建可组合、与模型无关的架构，并将数据与计算分离以避免被单一供应商锁定。
  6. 专家认为Anthropic在软件工程上的优势（GitHub上的工具优先为其构建）大约能维持4-6个月领先，之后其他前沿和开源模型会追上；Grok能力不错但存在品牌顾虑。
  7. 专家预计未来2-4年算力价格将下降，AMD等硬件替代将逐步削弱CUDA护城河；Anthropic ARR从10亿美元增长至约810亿美元（S-1披露），NRR约900%，部分增长来自从固定费率向按量计费的转换。
  8. 对于Anthropic超过9000亿美元的基础设施支出承诺、而80%收入为可变消费型收入，专家认为这更多引发对未来定价刚性的担忧而非对产能保障的信心。

Companies: MSFT US, NVDA US

Bilal Jaffery (BJ) SVP & Head, Data & AI at ThoughtWorks Inc

Agenda

  • Anthropic's post-IPO future – enterprise benchmarks and workload differentiation
  • Software engineering moat vs commodity workflows
  • Cost predictability and demonstrated ROI
  • Financial horizon, capital commitments and long-term vendor durability

Key Insights

  • Enterprise renewals shifted from flat-rate to consumption-based API pricing, creating unpredictable "surge pricing" driven by hardware constraints that limit CFO budget visibility
  • Over 40% of Anthropic's revenue flows through third-party hyperscalers, a channel Anthropic doesn't own that exposes it to customer switching via built-in model orchestrators
  • Anthropic holds a strong software engineering advantage – prioritised first on GitHub – giving it a 4-6-month lead before rival frontier and open-weight models catch up
  • Due to Anthropic lacking native auto-routing and being too costly for 90% of use cases, enterprises are building model-agnostic architectures and adopting cheaper open-weight alternatives
  • Massive infrastructure commitments raise buyer concerns over future pricing rigidity, although long-term hardware competition against Nvidia's CUDA moat should eventually lower compute costs
