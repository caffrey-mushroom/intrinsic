原标题：Palantir – Inside the Data Citadel – Balancing Government Dominance, Commercial Scale & AIP Acceleration

#software #llm

Third Bridge · 2026-11-24

核心摘要

  1. 前Palantir战略合作总监认为，Q3 FY26收入同比+96%、首次突破20亿美元，美国商业+148%至9.84亿美元并首次超过美国政府收入，核心驱动是Foundry平台的长期投入以及bootcamp替代demo的低成本获客模式。
  2. 专家判断在约4,000-4,400人的员工上限下，把季度收入做到25亿美元以上会遇到人力瓶颈，定制化数据集成仍需现场FDE，AI FDE难以替代，军方指挥官更希望与真人工程师对接。
  3. 净收入留存率从Q4 FY25的139%升至150%、157%和Q3 FY26的165%，专家认为已经见顶，维持32%左右的利润增长将非常困难。
  4. AIP bootcamp转化为七、八位数多年期Foundry合同的比例估计不超过四分之一，企业在压缩CAPEX和IRAD开支，若只能带来约5%的EBITDA提升则难以证明投入合理。
  5. Palantir基本不愿打折，商业端面临Databricks等以50%价格提供60-70%能力的竞品，以及微软、谷歌把类Foundry数据层打包进办公套件和云合同的威胁。
  6. Anthropic和OpenAI获准进入IL-6/IL-7国防环境，将冲击Palantir的应用层和编排层，专家认为Palantir的数据层和本体(ontology)仍难以替代，但编排层的归属存在争夺。
  7. 前20大客户TTM人均收入同比+76%至1.46亿美元，但专家估计其中30-40%的支出在预算削减时较为脆弱；民主党控制国会后国防预算可能收紧，监管也可能加强。
  8. 公司指引2026全年收入85.3亿美元（约+91%），隐含Q4环比增速降至约15%（此前约20%），专家归因于估值已计入AI增长，以及企业从token-maxing试验期进入理性化阶段。
  9. 专家认为并购可能性为0%，现金将更多用于回购，研发投入可能减少；采购硬件按成本价转售政府，不收传统军工约50%的加价。
  10. 预计商业收入增速将持续快于政府收入，Anduril的Lattice和传统军工巨头也在加剧国防软件竞争。

Companies: PLTR US, AMZN US, MSFT US, ANT

Alex Moore (AM) Director, Business Development & Growth at Research Innovations Inc

Luke Fragnito (LF) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Structural dynamics, growth prospects and threats to Palantir's (NASDAQ: PLTR) government contracts and its commercial operations
  • Market impact of the AIP (Artificial Intelligence Platform) and "bootcamp" sales model as enterprise adoption engines, and signs that net retention may have peaked
  • Evolution of the sales organisation, particularly the transition from human FDEs (forward-deployed engineers) to AI FDEs, and resulting risks to bespoke workflows
  • Competitive positioning relative to cloud hyperscalers, legacy data analytics providers and frontier AI labs

Key Insights

  • Maintaining 32% YoY profitability growth will be extremely difficult. Scaling to USD 2.5bn quarterly revenue under 4,000-employee cap will hit human bottlenecks – bespoke integrations require on-site engineers that AI cannot replace, increasing churn risk. Many military commanders refuse to interact with AI systems and demand dedicated human engineers on the ground
  • 165% net retention has likely peaked. Commercial buyers are scrutinising CAPEX, converting from AIP bootcamps at 25% or less, and weighing Palantir's pricing against cheaper rivals such as Databricks and bundled hyperscaler tools, with Palantir refusing to discount
  • Anthropic and OpenAI entering impact level 6 and 7 defence environments threatens Palantir's application and orchestration layers, while defence primes and Anduril's Lattice intensify competition amid tightening federal budgets
  • Commercial revenue will outpace government growth, although 30-40% of top account spend remains fragile to budget cuts
  • M&A probability is "absolutely 0%". Management prioritises share buybacks and R&D, although the latter is dwindling
