#software

Third Bridge · 2026-09-30

Analyst-led • 24 Sep 2026

Companies: LAW US

Paul Morse (PM) Director, Enterprise Sales at Everlaw Inc

Saad Maqsood (SM) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Commercial momentum and market share shifts across US e-discovery software vendors such as Relativity, CS Disco (NYSE: LAW) and Reveal
  • Customer adoption of generative AI-powered review tools, early case assessment automation and predictive coding capabilities
  • Pricing model evolution from traditional per-gigabyte processing fees towards predictable subscription-based, seat-based or active-matter pricing
  • Law firm and corporate legal department sentiment towards consolidating e-discovery, legal hold and compliance workflows into single-vendor platforms

Key Insights

  • Relativity holds north of 50% of e-discovery market share, primarily through its managed service provider distribution network, where it remains the default choice for most large matters. Insurance carriers controlling 75%-plus of defence litigation e-discovery spend always select the lowest-cost approved vendors, boding well for Relativity's on-premise pricing
  • Reveal's acquisitions of Logikcull and Ipro largely failed to deliver value, as inherited customers were already planning to leave and the combined product felt like a "cobbled-together Frankenstein"
  • Despite CS Disco's strong product and marketing, the company's burst pricing model creates renewal friction
  • Expert believes e-discovery platforms are disincentivised to use AI for identifying irrelevant junk documents quickly, as faster data removal would dramatically reduce their hosting revenue
  • RelOne's pricing is being offered at parity with on-premises rates to accelerate migration, but renewal price hikes are widely expected once customers are locked into the cloud environment
