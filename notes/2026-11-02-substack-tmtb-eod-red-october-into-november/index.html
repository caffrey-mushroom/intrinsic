原标题：TMTB EOD Wrap: Red October continues into November

#markets #semis #optical

Substack · 2026-11-02

核心摘要

  1. AI 交易又一个难看的交易日：QQQ 收跌 1.4%、SOX 跌 3.4%，盘中一度都涨约 1%，是 QQQ 自"解放日"以来最差的三天，半导体已跌破 200 日均线。
  2. 中期选举前资金在持续降仓，市场预计民主党拿下参议院；大摩 PB 数据显示对冲基金对 AI 交易的配置首次自 2023 年初以来低于市值权重（34% 对 35.4%）。
  3. 光模块领跌：Marvell 以每股 Lumentum 换 4.5 股的全股票方式收购 Lumentum，MRVL 股东持有约 67%，预计成本节约超 10 亿美元、2028 年上半年完成，消息后 LITE 跌 18.6%、MRVL 跌 12.7%，CIEN 跌 11.3%。
  4. Anthropic 修订版 S-1 显示截至 9/30 的三季度收入接近 170 亿美元，同比约 12 倍、环比 47%，作者把 9 月 ARR 估计上调到 700–710 亿美元；市场关注的 10 月 ARR 未披露，基准在 740–750 亿美元，多头希望超过 780 亿美元。
  5. AI 融资压力增加：Oracle 申报 100 亿美元债务额度、据报道共融资 200 亿美元，股价跌 9%；Meta 计划发行至少 200 亿美元债券，谷歌发行 60 亿欧元债，OpenAI 本轮融资目标从 300 亿降到 200 亿美元。
  6. 其他板块：新云厂商 CRWV、NBIS 跌超 8% 创新低，存储 SNDK、MU、STX、WDC 跌 6–8%，INTC 跌 9%；网络安全全线下跌，CHKP 四季度收入指引低于预期、跌 7%，OKTA 跌 11.2%，DDOG 跌 8.1%。
  7. 宏观方面，油价跌 8% 回到 100 美元以下，10 年期美债收益率降 4bp，12 月加息概率从约 85% 降到 71–77%。
  8. 谷歌方面，富国预计明年一季度搜索增速降到 4%；作者认为在搜索放缓到个位数、股价约 14–15 倍 2028 年 PE 的情况下吸引力一般，除非 AI Mode 变现跑通。

Good afternoon. Another ugly day for the AI trade as QQQs finished -1.4% and SOX -3.4% after a nasty close, despite both starting the day ~+1%. Worst 3 day stretch since Liberation day for the QQQs. Lots of red all around as charts continue to break down with semis now below the 200d and Leopold Lows:

De-grossing continues across the space ahead of midterms tomorrow where Dems are projected to take control of the Senate

All eyes on the Abbott race (Tx Gov) as well:

No one seems to want to get in front of results. Although I heard quite a few people talk about midterms being a clearing event with market pricing already pricing in a Dem sweep, none are looking to put $ to work ahead of the event as most are playing defense at this point. MS PB has hedge funds underweight the AI trade relative to its market-cap weight for the first time since early 2023 (34% of nets vs. a 35.4% weight).

Tech investors not feeling confident on the path from here and we sympathize - we remain low gross in our ST/MT books unless we see a spot for a nice r/r entry and leaning on charts to guide us here (a break back above resistance would be a nice line in the sand to try another ST/MT long trade). The cleaner semi set up early last week lasted all of 72 hours so low confidence here, taking it day by day. Not one big catalyst for the sell off today, but we thought the lowered OpenAI raise news on Friday evening was another reminder of how much the AI trade hinges on both Labs ARR trajectories and how the trade is increasingly reflexive both on the up and down (back to ARRs in a moment…)

In semis, optical led the way lower with LITE -18% and MRVL -12%. Never seen a deal hated as much as shit one (I caught this typo proofreading but leaving the Freudian slip in here) and Mei summed it up in one line in TMTB Slack

Rest of the space lower: CIEN -11.3% although COHR -5% held in decently.

Price action just as bad as you can imagine with every bit of news getting sold in the space.

Anthropic filed its amended S-1 this morning here: Q3 revenue nearly $17B as of 9/30 (nudges our Sept ARR estimate to $70B-$71B up slightly from $70B), ~12x y/y and 47% q/q. However, the most important number was missing: October ARR. Bogey sitting at $74-$75B after the weaker cloud numbers last week, with bulls wanting something above $78B as that would point to organic growth accelerating. Some pointed to this tweet from Ramp’s economist as some negative read but we don’t agree:

The latest Ramp data we had was through Oct 23rd, which showed +13% above Q3’s daily average, so +15-25% would mean the last week of October ran anywhere from flat with the prior week (about +20% above Q3’s daily average) to up roughly a third week over week (about +60% above it), which points to an acceleration in the latest week for Anthropic:

Most thinking the ARR # could come on the roadshow which starts on Monday at the earliest. However, Anthropic doesn’t operate in a vacuum and what was bad for them in September was good for OpenAI and what is good for them in Oct, likely a headwind for OpenAI. The AI trade depends on both and whatever the ARR number is, it’s unlikely to settle the OSS and LT Lab ARR debate, but bulls are hoping a $77/78+ number& fable 5.5 release could ignite a rally into the IPO.

Outside of that, AI financing back in the headlines. ORCL filed a six-part $10B debt shelf and the WSJ reported it is looking to raise $20B in all (10B Debt / 10B equity). This likely the one-and-done raise that funds the build to self-funding in FY29. AI investors taking every news as bad these days so stock was -9% despite the raise not being a total surprise. Stock has now given back all and more of the 11% pop last week post the analyst day’s better guide. META -1.3% is eyeing at least $20B of USD/EUR bonds after S&P cut its outlook to negative, GOOGL is raising 6B in euros (its euro spreads are ~60bp wider since May), Blackstone expects Anthropic to carry a junk rating after the IPO, and of course the OpenAI cut mentioned above ($20B from $30B and only $11B raised so far).

The AI pile-on continued as Elon was out throwing cold water on enterprise agents, an area bulls thought could carry the mantle for next best use case after coding:
