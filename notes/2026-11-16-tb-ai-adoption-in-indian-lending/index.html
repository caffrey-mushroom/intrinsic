原标题：AI Impact on Indian Financial Services – Adoption Trends, Deployments & Cost Benefits

#financials #agents

Third Bridge · 2026-11-16

核心摘要

  1. 前L&T Finance首席数字官办公室高级经理引用RBI FREE-AI调查称，约600家受监管机构中约20%在使用或开发AI；BCG数据显示处于AI使用或POC阶段的银行比例从2024年的10%、2025年的44%升至86%。
  2. 对话式AI（聊天机器人和语音机器人）因现成可用而采用最快，而最有价值的用例在零售和MSME信贷审批，包括数据摄取、银行流水与GST解析和反欺诈；SBI在FY26用AI审批了1万亿卢比的MSME贷款，1万卢比以下支票实现直通处理。
  3. 银行更倾向自建模型以保护数据护城河，如HDFC的Neev平台和ICICI，而NBFC更愿意接入第三方；自建通常需9-12个月，外购需3-6个月，语音机器人仅需3-4周。
  4. 语音机器人每通电话成本约5-10卢比（含基础设施约15-20卢比），而人工坐席每月约3万卢比；印度银行和NBFC科技支出约占收入4-5%，低于全球银行的7-9%，其中约10-15%的新项目支出投向AI。
  5. Bajaj Finance在FY26第四季度已部署130个AI智能体和65个智能体化应用，并以BFL 3.0定位为FinAI公司；HDFC有5个用例在生产、14个在部署中。
  6. 专家估计大型机构AI带来的成本节约在未来5-6个季度约为7-10个基点（保守3-8个基点），Bajaj指引FY27改善25-40个基点被认为过于激进，近两个季度仅见约5-6个基点；NBFC预计在FY27第四季度开始正式披露AI效率收益。
  7. AI带来的更多是人员重新配置而非裁员：HDFC银行FY26员工减少约3300人，非管理岗减少约8000人而中层增加约1200人；Axis员工减少3000人但新增400家网点，呼叫中心、审批和后台会计岗位受影响最大。
  8. 低额无抵押贷款中约50-60%的厚档案客户已实现100%直通处理，MSME约25-30%；接入账户聚合器和GST数据使薄档案借款人早期违约下降约15-25%、审批率提升10-20%，AI预警和催收使SMA-0/1化解率提升约10-15%，Bajaj可用AI读取43类单据、准确率95-96%。

Agenda

  • AI adoption trends and evolving use cases across banks, NBFCs (non-banking financial companies), credit underwriting, MSME (Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises) lending and banking operations
  • Solution providers, proof-of-concept initiatives and transition to scaled deployments
  • Measurable benefits in cost efficiency, turnaround time, underwriting accuracy and operational productivity, including GST (Goods and Services Tax) data integration

Key Insights

  • Expert notes that conversational AI, including chatbots and voice bots, is most adopted AI function due to off-the-shelf availability, with significant use cases emerging in retail and MSME credit underwriting, including data ingestion, GST parsing and fraud detection. SBI underwrote INR 1 lakh crore of MSME loans using AI in FY26, with straight-through cheque processing up to INR 10,000 without human intervention
  • Voice bots cost INR 5-10 per call, vs INR 30,000 monthly per human agent, offering straightforward, lower-cost and more efficient like-for-like alternative for call centres
  • Expert observes clear divergence as banks prefer building proprietary models in-house to protect their data moat, while NBFCs are receptive to third-party integrations. NBFCs can deploy AI in 3-6 months, with voice bots taking 3-4 weeks, whereas internal bank builds require 9-12 months
  • Expert believes NBFCs will formally report AI-driven operational efficiencies around Q4 FY27, estimating 7-10bps in large institutions' cost saving over 5-6 quarters
