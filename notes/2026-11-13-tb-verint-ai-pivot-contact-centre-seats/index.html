2026.11.13 | TB | Software | Verint AI Pivot & Contact Centre Seats

原标题：Verint – Reinventing Itself as Agentic AI Redraws Contact Centre Economics

#software #agents

Third Bridge · 2026-11-13

核心摘要

  1. 前Verint中大西洋区域销售VP认为，Verint的AI实力真实存在，但市场仍把它视为“打电话录音公司”，品牌和营销老旧，在新客户中难以与Cresta、Decagon、Sprinklr等AI原生厂商竞争。
  2. Verint明确不做电话底层（telephony），定位为CCaaS之外的外挂方案，约50%业务通过Five9、Cisco、Avaya等渠道转售；Five9销售常用Verint的WEM来对抗NICE。
  3. 私有化前Verint AI ARR约3.72亿美元，约占8-9亿美元总收入的一半，但专家所在区域只有约10-15%的交易包含AI机器人SKU，其余仍是传统产品，甚至仍有本地部署订单。
  4. 约10%的客户仍为本地部署，可以获得持续支持但创新停滞，云版功能已远超本地版本。
  5. AI部署中坐席数确有小幅下降，例如某客户从600人降至500人、另一些从1000人降至800-900人，但收购、新产品和合规补足带来的增长与之相当，净减少并不剧烈；agent-assist带来约10-30%生产力提升，更多是坐席转岗而非裁撤。
  6. 定价正从按坐席转向按坐席加消耗或完全按结果计费的混合模式；AI产品价格逐步商品化，例如Medallia简单调研项目从每年80万美元降至约10万美元。
  7. 专家指出Sierra两三年内ARR达约4亿美元、估值150亿美元，而Verint全部AI收入尚不足4亿美元，反映AI原生厂商的光环；Five9约3亿美元收购Level AI，而Salesforce为类似公司付出近30亿美元。
  8. 被收购并与Calabrio整合后，多名任职20年以上的老员工在等待股权兑现或满一年后悄然离职；专家认为Verint未来18-24个月不在WEM赢家之列，可能需要像Lithium与Spredfast合并为Khoros那样彻底更名，行业将通过PE或巨头收购整合。

Agenda

  • Trends and developments impacting Verint and the broader CCaaS (contact-centre-as-a-service) and CX operating environment
  • Seat compression trends and agentic AI market evolutions
  • Verint-Calabrio integration efforts and evolving competitive dynamics
  • Mid-to-long-term outlook

Key Insights

  • Verint's AI ARR reached cUSD 372m, but expert notes that only 10-15% of deals in his region involved AI bot SKUs, suggesting AI revenue may be concentrated outside enterprise Mid-Atlantic accounts
  • Regarding seat declines or agent replacement fears, expert indicates that seat counts did decline modestly in some AI deployments, but growth from compliance gaps, acquisitions and new product launches offset shrinkage – net agent reduction remains far from dramatic
  • Expert thinks Verint has a fundamental brand problem, unable to shed its legacy "call recording company" identity, and may need a full rebrand, similar to Lithium and Spredfast becoming Khoros
  • Legacy Verint employees with over 20 years' tenure are quietly exiting post acquisition, waiting out vesting or year-one milestones – a talent drain that signals deeper cultural disruption beyond typical post-merger layoffs
  • Verint's on-premise customers – c10% of the base – faced a hidden trade-off and continued support, but also stagnant innovation, with cloud version capabilities advancing significantly beyond what on-prem deployments could access
