SUMMARY

Overview

The expert describes ISG as an advisor for enterprises making high-stakes technology sourcing decisions, including outsourcing, vendor selection, cost reduction, and contract negotiations. Its core strengths are proprietary market data, benchmarking, transactional experience, and continued supplier governance. The discussion also considers ISG’s AI advisory opportunity, competitive limitations, regional coverage, and the effects of generative AI on advisory work.

Key Insights

ISG is best positioned to help clients decide what to buy, from whom, and at what cost—not to deliver large-scale transformation itself. The expert sees its strongest opportunity in connecting sourcing engagements with recurring benchmarking and supplier- governance services, supporting retention and repeat revenue. AI advisory is a natural extension of ISG’s sourcing expertise, particularly in vendor assessment, governance, cost control, and ongoing return measurement. Large-scale AI engineering is a weaker fit. Generative AI may reduce demand for routine research and RFP preparation, but the expert views proprietary pricing information, negotiation support, independent judgment, and accountability as harder to replace. The expert identifies post-contract adoption and organizational change as gaps. ISG could improve its position by taking more responsibility for sustained usage and outcomes. Confidence in ISG’s data and tools is tempered by limited firsthand evidence of their effect on specific vendor decisions or negotiations.
