Agenda

  • Competitive landscape and market share trends for EDP (Energy Distribution Partners)
  • Customer attrition trends and key factors driving churn risk
  • Pricing dynamics and earnings risk, including outlook for propane spreads

Key Insights

  • Expert believes EDP runs a lean organisational structure that enables faster integration vs larger peers, but creates understaffing in major functions such as propane procurement. Lack of propane supply managers can lead to suboptimal purchasing, potentially costing several cents per gallon
  • Management turnover at EDP over past 1.5 years may pose a competitive risk, with departing employees joining competitors such as Reliable Energy and Lettermen's
  • Expert notes the use of call-centre service models by major nationals such as AmeriGas has led to service degradation and customer attrition. EDP has been able to exploit this by maintaining local branding and office presence. Any future adoption of call centres by EDP may be a major customer churn risk
  • Residential gross margins average USD 1-1.5 per gallon vs USD 0.5-0.8 for commercial customers, with commercial customers offsetting lower margins through higher propane volumes and density
