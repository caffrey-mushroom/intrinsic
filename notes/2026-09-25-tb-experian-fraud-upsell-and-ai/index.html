Agenda

  • AI impact on customer product demands and how well Experian (LON: EXPN) can meet them
  • Fraud solution cross-selling, attach rates and expansion avenues
  • ARR outlook and product uptake

Key Insights

  • Despite AI being market driver, Experian may struggle to capitalise on customer demands for AI-native functionality. Experian's legacy solutions may struggle with modernisation, and majority of fraud solution growth is outside Experian's niche of application fraud, potentially eroding existing client base by 5-10%
  • B2B revenues may stagnate, with new customers and expansion opportunities limited in the next 2-3 years. Size and global breadth of Experian makes them less agile whilst highly targeted M&A overlooks potential growth opportunities
  • Expert does not view Ascend as particularly sticky, given lead times in meeting client demands. With tier 1 banks increasingly building proprietary data warehouses using offshore development teams and cheaper cloud storage, movement away from Ascend is made easier
  • Fraud solutions likely only has low double-digit attach rates in existing customers today, given slower development. Customers are expected to have taken alternatives with displacement being difficult
