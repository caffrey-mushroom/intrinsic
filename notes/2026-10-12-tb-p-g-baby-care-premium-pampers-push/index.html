Agenda

  • Key trends and dynamics currently shaping the global babycare market
  • US market share dynamics and the growing threat of private label competition
  • Global growth and premiumisation opportunities
  • P&L drivers and navigating short and longer term cost inflation headwinds

Key Insights

  • Spare manufacturing capacity in China and Asia has dramatically lowered barriers to entry, enabling new brands to launch without factory investment, fundamentally disrupting diaper market's historically high entry barriers
  • Coterie entered North America priced at index 200, twice competitors' prices, proving far more headroom exists than incumbents historically assumed, with most brands only reaching index 120-130
  • China's premium Pampers tier incorporates silk as a key material, signalling broader shift towards luxury-grade ingredients Western markets haven't fully embraced, but likely will
  • P&G's greatest risk is competing downwards against value brands rather than pushing upward into premium territory, as playing competitors' games is a historically proven mistake. "I think is the key to unlock this is I think P&G needs to look at premiumisation"
  • "KC might deliver growth faster. In the short term, you may see KC grow faster. I think P&G will deliver growth more sustainably […] from time to time, it [P&G] can get distracted by being too competitively focused and less consumer-focused"
