Analyst-led • 7 Oct 2026

Companies: 1INN DE

Matthias Bumeder (MB) Managing Director at InsightFunding GmbH

Philip Atkinson (PA) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Innoscripta (ETR: 1INN) – selling the platform through increased customer care and sales pushes
  • ARR expectations for platform and AI functionality
  • Differentiation in document management, highlighting AI threat
  • Growth possibilities and time to scale new geographies

Key Insights

  • New customer acquisition is crucial to maintain growth and revenue, with 70-80% of yearly revenue generated by retroactive backlog claims. Customers typically push through all claims upon signing, with revenue coming six months later
  • International revenue will be key to future expansion alongside increased German penetration. EUR 1m target for new regions is "absolutely realistic", with 50% of 2027's international revenue expected to be booked in 2026 and EUR 5m possible in 2-3 years
  • Customer usage fluctuates. While churn remains low, this does not mean annual usage. Despite large customers frequently pursuing annual rebates, SMBs would utilise Innoscripta only every two years on average. Software subscription can help smooth this, but currently represents only 5% of revenue
  • Expert believes the 80%-plus funding success rate isn't a genuine differentiator, as smaller competitors are simply more selective about which projects they accept
