原标题：S&P Global – Does the Proprietary Data Moat Hold in the AI Era?

#financials #software #llm

Third Bridge · 2026-11-12

核心摘要

  1. 前S&P Global市场情报部门金融风险分析CTO认为，CEO Martina Cheung在剥离Mobility业务之后启动Cap IQ战略评估，核心目的是简化业务组合、明确资本配置，同时让Cap IQ能集中加大AI投入。
  2. Cap IQ偏重金融信息与技术平台，客户群与评级、指数业务不同；AI正在冲击按席位授权的模式，行业正转向按token或按完成任务计费，这很可能是Cap IQ接受评估的原因之一。
  3. 专家认为现有Cap IQ客户流失风险有限：客户深度依赖其Excel集成、工作流以及庞大的数据资产，切换到LSEG、Bloomberg的成本很高；Cap IQ还提供数据源之外的分析、实体链接和Kensho集成等增值功能。
  4. 剥离Cap IQ的成本不容忽视，因为它与其他业务共享API、网络安全设施、数据授权和支持团队，无论分拆还是出售都需要过渡协议；Kensho的能力可用于多条产品线，是否随Cap IQ一起出售值得关注，专家仅估计Cap IQ估值在数十亿美元量级。
  5. 未来的授权模式可能是“最低年度承诺加超额用量收费”，通过数据源、MCP服务器或API/Agent分发；监管严格、要求确定性输出的金融风险分析等产品仍会保持订阅制。
  6. 评级和指数业务拥有方法论、历史记录和监管带来的牢固护城河（美国至少需要三家独立评级机构），AI很难替代；金融研究和数据分析是最容易受AI冲击的领域。
  7. 成熟客户越来越多地从S&P或LSEG拉取数据，结合自有另类数据，在自托管模型上分析，并以自带模型为由要求降低授权费，这可能长期压制S&P的定价权。
  8. 专家预计市场情报业务增速可从历史上约5-6%提升到9-10%，接近翻倍，但不太可能达到十几个百分点；Cap IQ若独立并获得更多资本投入，有望摆脱目前低至中个位数的增长。

Agenda

  • Trends and developments impacting S&P Global (NYSE: SPGI) and the broader information services industry
  • Portfolio reshaping following Mobility spinoff and Cap IQ review
  • AI threats and opportunities
  • Mid-to-long-term outlook

Key Insights

  • Cap IQ's strategic review likely stems from AI disrupting seat-based licensing models, pushing towards consumption-based pricing where customers pay per token or task completed
  • Potential Cap IQ divestiture costs are significant given shared application programming interfaces, cybersecurity infrastructure and data licensing agreements, which require transitional arrangements regardless of whether the business is spun off or sold
  • Consumption-based models with minimum annual commitments and overage clauses above pre-agreed thresholds are emerging as the likely replacement for traditional seat-based licensing structures, according to expert
  • Sophisticated customers are increasingly pulling data feeds from providers such as S&P or LSEG, combining them with proprietary alternative data behind self-hosted models, which could potentially pressure S&P's long-term pricing power
  • Market Intelligence growth could realistically accelerate from historical 5-6% towards 9-10%, roughly doubling prior rates, driven by AI-enhanced data demand and expanded product offerings
