SUMMARY

Overview

The discussion assessed the recently approved TEV-749 long-acting injectable olanzapine for schizophrenia. The expert expects strong interest because clinicians understand olanzapine’s efficacy, while the new formulation removes major limitations associated with an older injectable version. The conversation covered prescribing practices, patient selection, administration barriers, metabolic effects, and competition with other antipsychotics.

Key Insights

The expert considers olanzapine among the most effective antipsychotics, particularly for acute psychosis, agitation, insomnia, and poor appetite. Removing post-injection monitoring and oral supplementation was viewed as critical to adoption. The largest barrier is not reimbursement or patient acceptance, but ensuring patients reliably receive injections. A manufacturer-funded home nursing service could make TEV-749 the market leader. Initial use will likely begin in hospitals, followed by outpatient continuation after discharge. The expert would offer the injectable to stable oral olanzapine patients for convenience and potentially improved tolerability, not merely for poor adherence. The preferred approach would be the highest dose during acute treatment, followed by the lowest dose for maintenance. The expert believes successful access, education, hospital availability, and home administration support could allow the product to dominate the LAI market.
