原标题：Snowflake and Databricks: November 2026 Channel Check With the Founder & CEO of Award-Winning Snowflake and Databricks Partner Consultancy

#software #cloud

GuidePoint · 2026-11-20

核心摘要

  1. DACH地区约400人的数据与AI服务商创始人表示，其Snowflake服务收入一季度同比增长约8-9%，二季度接近10%，三季度约10%，四季度计划约10%，预计2027年保持两位数增长并可能超过今年。
  2. Databricks服务业务增长更快，三季度同比约15%，四季度预计15%或更高，2027年最乐观可达20%，主要由更多更大的迁移项目推动。
  3. Databricks的商机线索比Snowflake多约20-25%，大型迁移项目数量也更多，专家未观察到客户在Snowflake与Databricks之间迁移工作负载。
  4. Snowflake的消费增长主要来自新数据用例落地和单个用例用户数增加，每个用例背后的数据管道和BI访问推高消耗，而非AI功能的生产使用。
  5. Snowflake Cortex与Copilot、Databricks Genie以及微软AI工具在法兰克福区域的生产应用非常有限，因为当地缺乏运行Anthropic、OpenAI等前沿模型的算力，而合规规定禁止跨区域处理生产数据，预计年底或明年初AWS和Azure才会补齐。
  6. Snowflake在零售业较强、更易用且更适合业务用户，数据平台能力更突出；Databricks在金融服务业及工程、数据科学和AI团队中更受青睐，在AI与分析方面更具优势但实施门槛更高。
  7. 微软在DACH大力推广Fabric，但部分客户因稳定性不足、功能缺失和消耗成本高于预期，正考虑转向Databricks或Snowflake，反向流失则未出现。
  8. 年初预期的SAP BW迁移因SAP CDC无法增量加载等技术限制尚未兑现，可能推迟到2027年；客户需求积压充足，但内部和外部预算仍然受限，预计2027年初不会明显改善。

SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, a data and AI services provider in the DACH region, discussed demand for Snowflake and Databricks, customer platform choices, AI feature adoption, and factors that could shape growth. Databricks currently generates more service opportunities and larger migration projects, while both platforms have ongoing demand. The expert expects customer interest to remain strong, though budgets and regional infrastructure constraints limit some projects.

Key Insights

Databricks is growing faster in the expert’s services business, supported by larger migration projects and a stronger pipeline. The expert sees potential for further acceleration. Snowflake growth has been steady, driven mainly by new data use cases and wider adoption among users—not by production use of AI features. Production adoption of Snowflake Cortex and Copilot, Databricks Genie, and Microsoft’s AI tools is limited in the Frankfurt region. Customers may not run production workloads across regions for compliance reasons, and local infrastructure for leading models is not yet available. The expert expects SAP BW migrations to become a possible growth driver if technical limitations are addressed. However, constrained customer budgets may temper demand. The moderator pursued additional detail on regional AI restrictions and platform competition, underscoring interest in these issues.
