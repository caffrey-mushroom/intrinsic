原标题：Sasol – Navigating Energy Transition & Operational Headwinds in South Africa

#energy #chemicals

Third Bridge · 2026-11-13

核心摘要

  1. 前Sasol组合战略与可持续发展SVP认为，CEO Simon Baloyi以运营为导向，聚焦Secunda、Sasolburg等现有资产交付，转型业务因Lake Charles化工项目减值等原因被降级。
  2. 约60%的资本开支用于维持性支出、约30%用于原料替代和采煤，仅一小部分用于增长和转型；专家预计维持性资本开支将长期维持在约60%，只有新业务盈利后才可能降至50%。
  3. Secunda年产能约720-740万吨，但设备已运行数十年，公司通过推迟停产检修节省成本；CFO称盈亏平衡油价约49美元/桶，其中6-9美元与推迟检修相关，若本年停产4-6周则超过720万吨几乎不可能。
  4. 脱石技术提升煤质、降低外购煤依赖，可维持3-7年生产平台，但矿山枯竭和运输半径扩大将使采矿成本上升；专家认为不进口煤炭难以维持700万吨以上产量。
  5. 莫桑比克天然气2028年起急剧下降，预计5-8年内失去经济开采价值；LNG进口基础设施滞后、成本高昂，天然气价格是Sasol风险登记册中最高风险之一，NERSA仅批准了FY27和FY28气价。
  6. 南非约70万桶/日燃料需求中约一半本地生产，其余越来越依赖进口，加上ADNOC收购壳牌下游资产，Sasol守住本地市场份额将越来越难，专家怀疑20年后Secunda是否仍在运营。
  7. 2030年2GW可再生能源目标目前约500MW上线，电网接入是关键约束，最后600-700MW存在交付风险；格拉斯哥COP承诺的约80亿欧元转型资金据称一分未到位，Boegoebaai绿氢项目基本被搁置，没有政府补贴不可行。
  8. 2026年6月宣布清盘与Topsoe的SAF合资公司Zaffra，尽管其刚获得德国3.5亿欧元补贴；该eSAF项目需超5亿欧元资本开支、仅产3.8万吨，需约7000欧元/吨以上价格，公司转而回归低风险的技术许可模式，并关注加拿大水电和生物质机会。

Agenda

  • Sasol's (JSE: SOL) South African energy operations and long-term decarbonisation roadmap
  • Coal feedstock constraints, mine depletion and operational mitigation strategies, such as destoning and logistics
  • Secunda facility reliability, turnaround bottlenecks and asset lifespan considerations
  • Gas feedstock security, Mozambique field depletion timelines and feasibility of alternative LNG imports

Key Insights

  • Sasol is squeezing cash flow from its Secunda plant by postponing shutdowns, given that it's spending 60% of CAPEX on Secunda maintenance. While destoning technology has boosted coal quality, mining costs will inevitably rise
  • Mozambique gas supplies will begin declining sharply in 2028 and face economic depletion within 5-8 years. Replacement LNG import infrastructure is delayed and costly, exposing Sasol to long-term gas pricing risks
  • Defending local fuel market share against cheaper refined imports and new entrants such as ADNOC acquiring Shell's downstream assets, is expected to become a struggle
  • Sasol's 2GW 2030 renewable target faces delivery risks on the final 600-700MW due to exhausted grid access and capacity, while green hydrogen projects such as Boegoebaai remain largely archived and impossible without government subsidies
  • Deprioritising capital-intensive green equity projects – such as winding down the sustainable aviation fuel JV with Topsoe – and defaulting to lower-risk technology licensing is due to high CAPEX, uncertain economics and unfulfilled Western funding
