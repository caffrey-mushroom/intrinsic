#Ramp #费控

Third Bridge · 2026-11-06

核心摘要

  1. Ramp对Brex和Mercury的企业赢单率约60–70%，但已用Brex的客户很难替换。
  2. 闭环卡模式是SMB优势，却是做全球大企业时的结构性短板。
  3. AE月度贡献利润配额约3.5万美元，只有约60%达标。
  4. Router可通过交易费、卡关联路由和批量采购token变现AI支出管理。
  5. 专家认为开放架构会更受欢迎。

Analyst-led • 3 Nov 2026

Companies: AXP US

Wade Brogdon (WB) Co-founder & CEO at Lowcountry AI Inc

Jonathan King (JK) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Ramp's enterprise sales momentum and customer acquisition potential
  • Competitive differentiation vs American Express (NYSE: AXP) and traditional banks
  • International expansion challenges and global servicing capabilities
  • AI automation, spend intelligence and emerging opportunities in AI cost control

Key Insights

  • Enterprise win rates against Brex and Mercury sat c60-70%, but deals where prospects were already Brex users were nearly impossible to displace without significant pricing competition
  • Expert believes the closed-loop card model – Ramp's biggest competitive strength in SMB – becomes its most significant structural liability when pursuing large enterprise customers globally
  • Ramp's enterprise sales productivity remains a meaningful execution constraint. Account executives carry cUSD 35,000 in monthly contribution-profit quotas, equivalent to USD 3.5 million in customer spend at a 100bps take rate, with only c60% achieving quota
  • Ramp's AI spend-management opportunity extends beyond traditional card economics, with Router creating potential monetisation through transaction-based fees, card-linked routing and bulk token purchasing. However, expert believes an open architecture will be critical to adoption as enterprise demand for AI-driven automation grows
