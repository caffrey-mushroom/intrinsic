#hardware #datacenter

Third Bridge · 2026-10-13

Analyst-led • 7 Oct 2026

Dan Curley (DC) Enterprise Account Manager at Evernex International SAS

Quinton Flore (QF) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Why customers leave OEM support for TPM (third-party maintenance) and whether cost still leads over flexibility and service
  • AI demand and hardware shortages extending equipment life
  • Service and billing problems stemming from the Service Express integration and which rivals are winning the customers who leave
  • How Fortune 500 buyers weigh bundled maintenance, professional services and managed services when evaluating vendors

Key Insights

  • Expert estimates TPM saves >50% vs OEM support, highest for enterprise accounts. Switching back to OEM is rare, so churn is mostly TPM-to-TPM moves or decommissioning
  • SEI integration is repeating Curvature playbook. c85% of SEI sales reps were cut, some customers have gone six months without contact and one service call took nearly a week. Global Fortune 500 or 1000 accounts are most exposed, with Evernex best-placed to capture share
  • Integration should take about one year, but that overlaps Q4, when c35% of contracts renew, potentially net negative after lost contracts
  • Park Place raises prices 8-11% at renewal, while SEI and Evernex hold prices flat except on aging equipment, a key churn trigger alongside service quality
  • Expert expects 11-15% annual TPM market growth, driven by AI data centres coming off warranty, with Park Place at high end, given its cUSD 1bn scale
