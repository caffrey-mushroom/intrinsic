核心摘要

  1. 两位欧洲Salesforce实施合作伙伴表示，Dreamforce后AI及大模型集成相关咨询明显增加，但项目多为渐进式小单，尚未看到企业级AI全面转型项目。
  2. 专家认为Salesforce今年定位更清晰：大模型是概率性的，而企业需要确定性的结构化数据与工作流，两者互补而非竞争，有客户因稳定性选择Salesforce而非价格更低的Creatio。
  3. Salesforce在东欧等价格敏感市场定价更激进，同时Sales Cloud打包Tableau、Slack及Data Cloud/Agentforce的FlexCredit，价格小幅上涨但捆绑更丰富。
  4. 一位专家称席位价格约195美元/用户，多年期合同可获大幅折扣，合作伙伴典型项目约50-200席位，以迁移、集成和托管服务为主。
  5. 合作伙伴抱怨Salesforce将大型转型项目留给自有专业服务团队，分给伙伴的多是小型附加项目或“红色账户”，且宣传AI可将实施从12周缩至4周给伙伴带来压力。
  6. 约89%的AI工作是生成摘要和分析信息，而非自主修改数据或执行流程；客户越来越多通过Claude、ChatGPT、Copilot等外部模型操作Salesforce。
  7. 脏数据和分散数据是Agent部署的主要障碍，Sales Cloud与Data Cloud形成两套脏数据池，但非结构化数据仍可用于探索性洞察。
  8. Marketing Cloud正被推动迁移至基于核心平台和Data Cloud的Marketing Cloud Next，带来大量迁移工作，同时也有客户流向HubSpot等竞品。

SUMMARY

Overview

Two implementation partners discussed demand for Salesforce services, the company’s AI strategy, partner relationships, and Marketing Cloud. Interest in AI and integrations with large language models is rising, but most projects remain incremental rather than major enterprise-wide transformations. One expert described stronger business activity recently, while the other reported more smaller projects and limited access to large transformations.

Salesforce’s positioning around structured enterprise data and workflows was viewed as clearer, and some clients see the platform as a stable base for AI. However, data quality, partner access to projects, and competition from lower-cost or external tools remain important considerations.

Key Insights

AI is increasingly part of technology planning, but current use is often focused on summarizing and analyzing information rather than autonomously changing records or executing workflows. Salesforce’s enterprise foundation may complement probabilistic AI tools, rather than compete directly with them. One expert reported that a client selected Salesforce over a less expensive CRM because of its perceived stability and enterprise capabilities. Dirty or fragmented data can delay agent deployments. Yet unstructured data may still be useful for exploratory insights where precise answers are not essential. Partners expressed concern that Salesforce is directing large projects to its own services teams, while partner work skews toward smaller add-ons, migrations, and managed services. Marketing Cloud migrations are creating work as customers are encouraged to move to newer offerings built on Salesforce’s core platform and Data Cloud.
