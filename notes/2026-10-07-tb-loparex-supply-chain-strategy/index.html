Agenda

  • Loparex's product portfolio and current challenges facing the company
  • Impact of supply chain challenges and raw material inflation on business and profit margins
  • Restructuring potential and reorienting strategy to improve profitability
  • Key end markets and competitive landscape

Key Insights

  • Volumes sit roughly 20% below H1 2022, with some finance-driven markets such as automotive and construction down 30-40%. Expert expects volumes to stay negative for 2-4 years, with revenue propped up only by inflation pass-through
  • Base paper now makes up 30-40% of liner cost as mill closures consolidate supply. Loparex lacks vertical integration and 40-year-old product designs require paper grades that are increasingly scarce
  • Pricing power depends on interchangeability. Medtech and composites carry the best unit margins, while high-volume labels and hygiene have almost none
  • Labour gaps drove over 50% of the operational strain, with new operators taking 6-12 months to contribute. Felix Schoeller's new solvent-capable New York coater targets legacy 3M business
  • The recapitalisation leaves plant location, ageing equipment and supplier dependence unaddressed. Expert proposes a 10-year US reset costing USD 250m-plus, funded by selling Asian and European assets
