Agenda

  • Trends and developments impacting Foundever and the broader CX outsourcing operating environment
  • Impact of sector-wide AI displacement on current and future volumes
  • Clients' pursuit of automation and offshoring while curbing overall spend
  • Mid-to-long-term outlook

Key Insights

  • Foundever's revenue mix is c70% core CX, 15% specialised services and 15% AI services and strategy or consulting
  • Volume erosion from AI automation could be in 15-30% range over next 12 months, concentrated in simple interactions such as order tracking, payments and returns, particularly in e-commerce and retail verticals
  • Foundever was losing competitive RFPs largely on pricing, with expert indicating a c12-15% pricing discrepancy, noting a hypothetical RFP example where Foundever might quote cUSD 20 per hour vs competitors coming in at USD 17-18 in comparable geographies
  • Core CX automation should lead to higher margins. For example, an existing client might automate 100 of 400 seats, which could lift overall deal margins by a couple of percentage points, given technology margins running around 20% and with further possible efficiency gains as implementation timelines compress
  • Following recent debt exchange or recapitalisation, expert thinks a full acquisition of Foundever by Teleperformance or Concentrix over next 12-18 months would be very surprising, citing macroeconomic uncertainty and operational complexity of absorbing another large player
