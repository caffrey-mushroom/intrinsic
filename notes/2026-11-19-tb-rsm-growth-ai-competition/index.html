原标题：RSM – What is Next Across AI, Growth & Competition?

#software #agents

Third Bridge · 2026-11-19

核心摘要

  1. 前RSM International产品开发董事总经理（曾在埃森哲工作20余年）认为，中端市场咨询需求在过去6-9个月趋于稳定，但通胀、高柴油价格和潜在加息可能再次抑制企业支出。
  2. RSM的三大业务中，审计和税务被视为现金牛，咨询和托管服务是主要增长引擎，专家认为咨询业务应以两位数收入增长为目标，十年后其扩张速度将显著快于审计和税务。
  3. 与四大相比，RSM在审计/税务客户向咨询的交叉销售上仍不成熟，部门较为割裂且激励不足；合伙人制的民主共识模式推动变革较慢，而PE控股的竞争对手可以自上而下快速推进。
  4. 专家以大学橄榄球NIL时代类比：RSM是中端市场最大的服务商，但资金和人才获取可能不及四大和PE支持的整合平台，是否需要引入外部资本尚无定论。
  5. 人才在自主权与经济回报之间权衡，不少四大合伙人因追求自主权而加入RSM；但若PE模式带来的逆风过强，RSM可能需要重新考虑其独立路线。
  6. 中端市场客户关系驱动、粘性高，RSM拥有较强定价权；审计税务粘性高，项目型咨询粘性低，长期托管服务的粘性甚至高于审计税务。
  7. RSM宣布投入10亿美元用于AI，专家认为客户可能要求托管服务三年后的服务成本降至原来的一半，这会压低收入和利润率，需要用新增AI项目收入来弥补。
  8. 专家认为当前AI准确率约80%，人类使用能力尚未跟上，对RSM未来1-2年前景持乐观态度，关键在于10亿美元投资如何在收购、技术与中端或高端市场之间分配。

Analyst-led • 11 Nov 2026

Companies: ACN US

Perry Smith (PS) Founder at NextPath Insights

Agenda

  • RSM's organic growth in professional services
  • Inorganic growth and integrating acquisitions
  • AI strategy, noting potential efficiencies and risks
  • Outlook for 2027 and beyond, highlighting continued competitive threats

Key Insights

  • RSM's consulting arm is viewed as the primary growth engine, while audit and tax serve as the "cash cow", with double-digit revenue growth targeted in consulting
  • Cross-selling services to existing clients remains underdeveloped at RSM compared to big four firms and incentives are one factor. RSM is focused on it, but PE-owned firms are better-positioned because of the democracy in a partner-owned model
  • Talent weighs autonomy and financial benefit when choosing who to work for, and RSM's mindset is keeping autonomy. If the headwinds are too strong from PE-owned models, the company would have to reconsider its approach, but expert thinks it's too early to tell who will win
  • Middle market clients are sticky and RSM does have pricing power because of its established relationships. Client facing revenue is the most interesting aspect with how RSM's USD 1bn investment will be used across acquisitions or AI, with AI potentially reducing consulting fees by 50%
