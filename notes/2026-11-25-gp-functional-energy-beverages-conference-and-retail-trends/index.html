原标题：Energy Drinks: Future of Beverage Conference Takeaways, Monster Protein, and Changing Demand

#energydrinks #beverages #monster #walmart #qsr

GuidePoint · 2026-11-25

核心摘要

  1. 拥有30余年快消行业及Red Bull、Bang Energy从业经验的专家总结Future of Beverage与Beverage Forum两大行业会议指出，饮料占美国家庭食品饮料支出比重已从前几年的20%多跃升至30%以上，消费者正将饮品用作零食、代餐与营养补充剂，全面推动“功能化”取代传统解渴需求。
  2. 能量正成为所有功能叠加（Stacking）饮料的底层基础，同时参会机构提及高达37%的家庭可能涉及GLP-1减重药物使用（尽管统计口径尚存模糊），但药物引发的肌肉流失焦虑已实质性推高高蛋白及可溶性乳清蛋白饮品的市场热度，此外电解质、L-茶氨酸、镁与功能性蘑菇等情绪专注类原料也在加速渗透。
  3. 现制饮品与快餐连锁（QSR）已成为功能饮料创新的关键渠道与风向标，例如全球拥有约34,000家门店的KFC推出了Kwench饮品项目，而7 Brew、Dutch Bros、Ziggi's与Scooter's等免下车饮品店每周单店可消化20–30箱Red Bull，远高于普通商超单店每周2–5箱的动销水平。
  4. Red Bull通过与麦当劳等QSR渠道深化合作实现了强劲的品牌势能与溢价拉动，并在与Kroger等传统零售商的价格博弈中掌握更大主动权。
  5. 沃尔玛正大刀阔斧重塑饮品货架，彻底撤除收银区每店15–20台的百事和可口可乐传统冷柜并改为入口即拿即走开放式冷柜区，同时在各约1,000家门店试点以Trip和Recess为核心的“情绪与心智（Mood & Mindset）”专区以及“新品精选（New & Noteworthy）”货架。
  6. 专家指出沃尔玛曾成功定义并捧红“现代苏打（Modern Soda）”品类，此次布局有望再度孵化情绪饮品成为主流子类目，但提醒新锐品牌入驻沃尔玛往往面临严峻的压价与盈利能力考验。
  7. 在能量加蛋白新品赛道的竞争中，继C4、Alani Nu和Gorgie取得进展后，Monster即将推出Monster Protein系列，专家认可其战略方向并看好其切分一定市场份额，但认为消费者品牌认知度仍需时间验证，而Red Bull预计将继续坚守传统牛磺酸核心配方。

SUMMARY

Overview

The expert sees functional drinks reshaping the beverage market, with energy products serving as a foundation for added benefits such as protein, hydration, focus, and mood support. Consumers are using drinks for more occasions, including snacks, meal substitutes, and supplements. The discussion also covered non-alcoholic alternatives, retailers’ changing merchandising strategies, and the growth of quick-service beverage outlets.

Key Insights

Function is the defining trend. The expert believes demand for functional beverages will continue to grow and support premium pricing, as consumers show willingness to pay for added benefits. Energy is becoming more mainstream. Adding energy to drinks in coffee and quick-service settings can introduce the category to new consumers and encourage shoppers to combine multiple functions in one beverage. Quick-service restaurants are an important signal. The expert had previously underestimated these outlets but now views them as influential spaces for testing flavors, formats, and functional combinations. Retailers can shape emerging categories. Walmart’s dedicated sections for mood-oriented drinks and new products may help turn niche concepts into recognized categories. Protein and hydration have staying power. The expert expects soluble protein ingredients and electrolyte products to expand beverage options. They also cited growing interest in ingredients associated with calm, focus, and performance. GLP-1 awareness may reinforce wellness demand. The expert linked discussion of these drugs and their possible effects to increased attention to protein and nutrition, while noting uncertainty around a widely circulated household-use estimate.
