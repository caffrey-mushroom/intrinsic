核心摘要

  1. 拥有30余年Oracle生态经验的咨询伙伴Acropolis Advisors高管表示，Oracle 2027财年上半年及即将结束的11月季度（F2Q27）表现稳健、有望达成目标，其自身业务同比增速已从Q1的约20%加速至Q2的25%至30%。
  2. 其业务结构约80%为OCI云基础设施迁移、20%为Fusion SaaS套件迁移，且由于Oracle销售团队在11月季度末的冲刺节奏趋于平缓，未能在未来两周签约的项目将顺延并支撑通常受圣诞假期影响的F3Q表现。
  3. 多云战略（Oracle Database@AWS、Azure、GCP）被专家视为扭转Oracle云业务命运的绝妙举措，过去6个月至少50%以上的大型云迁移选择将Oracle Exadata设备部署在友商数据中心内，甚至帮助客户消耗AWS等云厂商的既定采购额度。
  4. OCI算力供给目前高度紧张，现有约1吉瓦可用容量的利用率已达97%至98%，不过预计到2027年5月底财年结束前将新增4.5至6吉瓦容量，且4座在建大型数据中心将推动总容量迈向10吉瓦以上。
  5. 尽管OCI物理容量吃紧，但至少50%至55%的客户迁移项目进度滞后（原本12个月的项目实际拉长至18个月），客户推进与消费爬坡慢于预期反倒为Oracle争取了新数据中心投产的缓冲时间。
  6. 技术可靠性获得多云伙伴背书，但企业软件与AI落地进度远低于预期，目前仅有不足20%的客户在某种程度上使用AI，真正规模化应用Agentic AI的客户更是远低于10%。
  7. 专家认为AI落地缓慢的瓶颈不在技术而在组织流程重塑与ROI论证，目前客户对约每OCPU 5美分的满载算力单价尚不敏感，但预计1至2年后随着用量规模化，计算成本与投资回报率将成为核心审查焦点。

SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, an Oracle ecosystem service provider, discussed Oracle’s recent quarter, customer migrations, OCI capacity, AI adoption, and multicloud partnerships. The expert viewed Oracle’s performance as generally on track, while noting that customer implementation pace and internal organizational changes remain important factors.

Key Insights

The expert expects Oracle to meet its goals for the quarter and described the first half as strong overall. Multicloud was characterized as a pivotal strategic decision for Oracle. The expert argued that allowing Oracle workloads to run in other hyperscalers’ data centers helps Oracle compete despite its smaller cloud infrastructure market position. Customer readiness, not technology quality, is the main barrier to broader cloud and AI adoption. Many organizations need time to update processes and build a business case before deploying new tools. OCI reliability and performance were assessed positively. The expert pointed to other hyperscalers’ willingness to host Oracle infrastructure as supporting evidence. Data-center capacity is tight, but slower-than-planned customer migrations are giving Oracle additional time to bring capacity online. The moderator showed interest in follow-up on quarterly performance, capacity, AI adoption, and multicloud economics.

Competitive Landscape

Company or offering

Positioning and observations

Oracle OCI

Supports workload migrations and competes in cloud infrastructure. Current capacity is nearly fully used, while additional capacity is planned.

Oracle Database@AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud

Lets customers run Oracle infrastructure in hyperscaler data centers. The expert sees this as a major advantage and a substantial share of recent migrations.

AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud

Provide data-center capacity for Oracle workloads and benefit when customers place workloads in their environments.

Workday and other alternatives

Were mentioned as potential destinations for customers, but the expert said Oracle’s multicloud options can help retain Oracle applications and databases.

AI adoption

Estimated at 97%–98% utilized, with roughly 1 gigawatt available. The expert cited 4.5–6 gigawatts of additional capacity expected by the end of the fiscal year and more than 10 gigawatts over the broader buildout.

Compute pricing
