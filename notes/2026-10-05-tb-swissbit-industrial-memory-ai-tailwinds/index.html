Agenda

  • Swissbit's revenue segmentation and application breakdown across core industrial end markets
  • Impact of AI infrastructure and data centre expansion on long-term demand projections
  • Industry-wide capacity constraints and supply chain issues
  • Customer contract structures with OEMs, pricing dynamics and pass-through mechanisms

Key Insights

  • NAND flash allocation is the single most critical strategic issue facing the market, with prices multiplying 3-5x over the past 1.5-2 years due to AI-driven demand. NAND flash seller's market will persist for >3 years. Construction of multiple new semiconductor fabs will eventually end NAND flash allocation, creating a challenging market as fabs operate below capacity
  • Mass storage for data centres not viable for smaller players, eg, Swissbit. Realistic opportunity lies in boot devices, where reliability requirements align with industrial-grade capabilities
  • Swissbit's Berlin manufacturing and R&D presence give it a structural edge under the EU Chips Act, as digital sovereignty concerns favour locally-produced semiconductor solutions
  • Anti-fraud cashier protection is shifting from hardware-based to cloud technical security element solutions, lowering barriers and increasing competitive pressure
  • Drone market represents surprisingly high-volume growth opportunity, as military drones are expendable and deployed in the hundreds daily, unlike traditional tanks
