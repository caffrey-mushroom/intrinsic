原标题：Claroty: Customer Perspective on This Industrial Cybersecurity Platform

#security #industrials #software

GuidePoint · 2026-11-23

核心摘要

  1. 西门子技术总监介绍，西门子自2018年起与Claroty合作，并通过旗下投资部门参与过其融资，Claroty已嵌入西门子的工业网络、电力通信系统和SCADA平台。
  2. 专家认为Claroty是OT/CPS网络安全领域前三的厂商，西门子、日立、ABB等大型OEM均与其合作，但整个OT安全市场仍然分散，没有明确的赢家。
  3. 主要竞争对手包括Nozomi Networks（在输电网、国家电网和能源领域很强，是Claroty最接近的对手）、Dragos（侧重政府关键基础设施、铁路、油气和电力）、已并入ServiceNow的Armis，以及思科、微软等综合巨头。
  4. 供应商选择以客户项目需求为起点，因地区和系统类型（保护继电器、工业网络、SCADA等）而异；网络安全必须在设计阶段内嵌，而不是事后叠加。
  5. 以悉尼地铁西悉尼机场线为例，政府客户提出了最严格的网络安全要求，需要100%合规；安全等级分1-4级，工业项目多在2-3级，4级通常对应军事应用，无人驾驶地铁的安全完整性等级(SIL)一般为3-4级。
  6. Claroty的优势在于被动监测不影响运营和安全完整性、广泛支持工业协议和PLC/RTU/HMI等设备，以及合规支持能力强。
  7. Secomea提供工业控制器的安全远程访问，与Claroty部分功能重叠，专家预计两者在一段时间内仍将互补共存，具体取决于客户偏好和项目需求。
  8. 专家未指出Claroty的具体短板，但强调网络攻击日益复杂，所有厂商都必须持续迭代；他给Claroty的产品和客户支持均打出9分（满分10分）。

SUMMARY

Overview

A technical expert from a major industrial technology supplier discussed Claroty’s role in securing operational technology (OT) systems, including industrial networks, utilities, and SCADA platforms. The supplier began collaborating with Claroty in 2018 and incorporates its technology into project designs where clients require cybersecurity protections. Vendor selection depends on each project’s requirements, systems, and location; cybersecurity must be built into the design rather than added afterward.

Key Insights

The expert considers Claroty one of the top three OT and cyber-physical systems security vendors, with strong adoption among large industrial suppliers. Claroty’s strengths include passive monitoring that avoids disrupting operations, support for industrial protocols, and assistance with compliance requirements. The expert did not identify a specific product weakness, but emphasized that all vendors must continually adapt as cyber threats become more sophisticated. Claroty received a rating of 9 out of 10 for both product quality and customer support. The expert noted that ongoing improvement remains important. The expert described the market as fragmented and evolving, with no clear overall winner. Interest in the competitive comparisons prompted further discussion from the moderator.
