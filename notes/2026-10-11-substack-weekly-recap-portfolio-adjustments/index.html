$COHR

This has been trading heavy. No doubt.

And I think I understand why.

There is doubt about Coherent’s 6-inch success. For two main reasons.

1 - The substrate suppliers (currently Sumitomo primarily, and increasingly AXT) aren’t shipping that many 6-in wafers. Where is Coherent getting all these wafers from? People I have spoken to with deeeep and direct industry relationships doubt that Coherent is getting that many wafers to do the ramp they want to do.

2 - The 6-in wafers that are being delivered supposedly result in very poor yield. They are really hard to make. And they have lots of issues. So while management says yield is better than 3-4 in, we don’t actually know which yield they are talking about. There are several types of yield. But there is disbelief that the finished yields they are getting are meaningful and that they won’t result in significant margin improvement.

I think it’s more of a timing issue than an existential issue personally. By mid 2027 there will be much more 6-in substrate supply and the processes should improve.

I also believe that major firms have run a strategy of long LITE, short COHR. With LITE being at ATH and COHR being well below, I would be curious if they flip this around with anticipation that COHR is in a better place next year.

That ascending line I have in COHR has held really nicely the past few weeks. It has been a level I keep adding to quick swings for a 10-15% gain. Hopefully we get some news this week that could break us out above. Maybe OCP will have something in store.

If we break below I will close out my swings and hold my core. Would look for the 230’s to add back to swings.
