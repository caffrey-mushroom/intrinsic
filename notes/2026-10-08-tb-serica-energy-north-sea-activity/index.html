Agenda

  • Key producing assets in the UK Continental Shelf, including OPEX per barrel and production output dynamics
  • Infill drilling and subsea tieback opportunities, touching on regulatory hurdles and project execution timeline
  • Serica Energy's (LON: SQZ) organisational capabilities – labour outsourcing, supply chain strengths and weaknesses and Triton FPSO (floating production storage and offloading vessel) management

Key Insights

  • Bruce Hub's 98% operatorship eliminates "partner drag", enabling fast decisions without budget approvals, directly mirroring Apache's successful Forties field strategy that drove significant production gains
  • Cygnus, acquired via Spirit Energy in October 2026, is projected to become Serica's most profitable asset with lowest OPEX, cUSD 10-12 per barrel
  • On the Spirit Energy acquisition, Serica's production jumps from 40,000-50,000 to 65,000 barrels daily, positioning it firmly in the UKCS top 5-10 producers alongside Harbour Energy
  • Expert believes decommissioning liabilities represent Serica's most underappreciated long-term risk, potentially damaging all shareholder value created during current growth phase within 5-10 years
  • UKCS subsea tieback approvals now take >2 years vs six months historically, making international M&A potentially more economically attractive. Jack-up rig scarcity is worsening as Norway's tax incentives triggered a drilling boom pulling rigs away from UKCS, with the Middle East absorbing remaining capacity
