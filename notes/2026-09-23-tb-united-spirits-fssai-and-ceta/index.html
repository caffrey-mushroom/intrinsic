Key Insights

  • Despite manufacturing ban, Royal Challenge's pan-India market share grew by 1pp over three months against industry size of 4.5 crore cases, a gain allowed by distributor inventory buffers sustaining retail availability during manufacturing ban
  • FSSAI reformulations mandate could lead to 1-1.5% gross margin erosion across Royal Challenge and Antiquity Blue brands, as segment price sensitivity blocks retail price increases
  • Expert estimates Pernod Ricard India's broader imported portfolio generates INR 2,500 crore in bottled-in-origin sales, representing 20-25% of its post-excise domestic revenues, with United Spirits' bottled-in-origin revenue share being "slightly lower"
  • Mandatory pass-through of India-UK CETA tariff cuts to consumers during the festive season is expected to lower entry-level imported Scotch prices by INR 150 and aged variants by INR 350 per bottle, driving consumer uptrade
  • Regional Indian distillers are deploying dedicated import teams to secure bottled-in-origin brand acquisitions and white-label distribution rights over next few quarters
