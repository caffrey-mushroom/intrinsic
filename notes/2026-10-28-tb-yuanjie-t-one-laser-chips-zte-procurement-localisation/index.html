00:19:31] Today, I would like to ask you about the optical communication device industry, especially Yuanjie Semiconductor and T-One (WuXi) Technologies. First of all, do most laser companies encounter problems when they start ramping up their production capacity?

Expert [00:19:31]: It is the same for all of them. For example, Yuanjie Semiconductor has now ramped up successfully because it has done a better job in yield ramp-up and other aspects. Once your production line is set up, there will certainly be a ramp-up period, including the process of adjusting the yield rate. This period lasts at least several months to half a year. If things go well, it takes half a year; if not, it may take even longer.

00:32:56] The main reasons why customers consider Yuanjie Semiconductor or T-One (WuXi) Technologies are acceptable performance, comparatively low price and delivery. In addition, will import substitution or supply chain security be a consideration in procurement?

Expert [00:32:56]: This can be divided into two parts. On the CSP side, for example, when they supply products to Alibaba, ByteDance and Tencent as tier-2 suppliers, the consideration given to domestic products will be relatively lower. Looking at a slightly longer timeframe, say three or four years, [everyone hopes] that domestic products will account for at least 70%. There are a few reasons for this. First, downstream customers such as telecom operators have their own assessment of the domestic substitution rate. Although in the optical module segment there is currently no mandatory requirement to reach 100% as there is for some switch chips, in the overall technical scoring items of downstream suppliers, the higher your localisation rate is, the higher your technical score will be. Therefore, once domestic products become available, the localisation rate will definitely be raised year by year and this is certain. For example, if the localisation rate is only 30% within the 12 months of next year, it may be raised to 40-50% within 24 months and to 70% within 3-4 years. It will definitely be raised gradually and this is the demand of downstream clients.

00:35:31] Is it easy for clients to change suppliers? How long does it take to switch from one supplier to another?

Expert [00:35:31]: The entire introduction process for core chips is relatively long. Normally, this test takes at least three months, that is, sample testing. Sample testing includes basic technical tests at the beginning, testing various technical indicators. Among these indicators, in addition to indicators such as the extinction ratio, reliability stress tests and high and low temperature tests are also required. In addition to doing these things, the subsequent consistency testing must also be carried out. At the beginning, samples are selected and later samples are drawn randomly. If the whole process is fast, it may be possible to finish it in three months but if it is slow, there is no telling how long it will take. Because problems may occur during the testing process and changes may still be made, this test may take six months. Under the most demanding requirements, even with good cooperation from both parties, it still takes 3-4 months and the whole process is roughly like this.

In addition, the small-batch trial also takes six months and the small-batch trial is definitely required. This is necessary to ensure that there are no quality problems during batch production. In other words, the time cycle for introducing a product is about nine months at the fastest, if everything goes very smoothly. Under normal circumstances, it generally takes about one year before it is possible to enter the final whitelist. Therefore, the logic is that introducing a supplier, especially a core chip supplier, takes a relatively long time.

Therefore, later, if its production capacity and yield ramp up, I think it is fully capable of being more than 10 percent cheaper than Yuanjie Semiconductor. If it wants to pursue a higher market share, it can offer a 20 percent discount and I think it can still make products with such high gross margins.

Whether this ramp-up can continue steadily and sustainably certainly carries some risks. Yuanjie Semiconductor may use long-term supply agreements or customer lock-in strategies and then gain a competitive edge from various aspects such as production capacity ramp-up. Second, from the perspective of the continuous iteration capability of the overall product portfolio, Yuanjie Semiconductor is certainly better. In addition to this generation of products, such as EML products at 50G and 100G, it is also planning subsequent 200G products. For silicon photonics, it has 70mW and 100mW products and it is also planning 300mW products, which should be available later. However, because T-One (WuXi) Technologies has not yet achieved scale, it is unlikely to formulate so many plans in the short term. Therefore, it may gain a competitive edge from the perspective of long-term iteration and long-term availability. Another point is consistency, especially the consistency of silicon photonic chips. Yuanjie Semiconductor can also make some efforts in this regard. Especially for optical modules used in data centres, the consistency requirements are indeed high. If this consistency cannot be improved quickly through iteration, Yuanjie Semiconductor can certainly launch some attacks on this basis.

00:47:51] Assuming you are the CEO or CTO of T-One (WuXi) Technologies, what do you think are the three issues you would prioritise addressing in the next 18 months?

Expert [00:47:51]: The first priority is yield ramp-up and solving the product consistency problem. This is the most critical issue at the current stage. If yield ramp-up and consistency cannot be improved, this will create a key barrier for every company and may affect subsequent processes. Therefore, it is definitely the first priority. The second issue is key account management and customer trust-building. All companies are conducting comprehensive evaluations but in this regard, for example, Yuanjie Semiconductor needs to be able to continuously upgrade and iterate products with T-One (WuXi) Technologies. It can also quickly resolve some of its consistency issues. It needs to convey confidence to downstream customers, especially key major customers, so that they will continue to cooperate with it and complete these processes quickly. Once volume increases, especially if it increases quickly with one particular customer, this will bring greater confidence to the whole organisation. The third issue is to seize this opportunity window as soon as possible and increase its overall production capacity. Yield rate ramp-up is one aspect and follow-up expansion of overall production capacity and other aspects should also be considered. In fact, the production capacity is still somewhat small. Expansion may be scheduled a bit later because it needs to improve its yield rate first before it expands production capacity.
