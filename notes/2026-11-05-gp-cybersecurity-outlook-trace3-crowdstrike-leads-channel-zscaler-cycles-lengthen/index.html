SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, a senior channel adviser with visibility into a large cybersecurity business and enterprise security purchasing, described a resilient market with continued spending. AI is increasing demand for protection of agents, identities and data flows, while also accelerating automation and vulnerability discovery.

Growth is selective. Established platforms can benefit when their capabilities meet customer needs and buyers can consolidate spending. Specialist vendors remain relevant when they address specific gaps or deliver clear operational benefits. The discussion covered Fortinet, Palo Alto Networks, CrowdStrike and Zscaler, alongside Microsoft and specialist providers.

Key Insights

AI is a significant demand catalyst, but the expert expects spending to favor practical capabilities such as exposure management, remediation and security operations—not every cybersecurity category equally. CrowdStrike was the strongest performer in the expert’s channel business, with platform expansion and flexible purchasing helping customers adopt additional products. Palo Alto’s growth was supported by SASE and security operations products. Its identity and observability acquisitions, however, had not yet generated a clearly integrated sales motion. Zscaler remained highly rated in technical evaluations, but complex enterprise deployments were lengthening sales and expansion cycles. The expert characterized this as slower execution rather than weak underlying demand. Frontier AI models may benefit security vendors initially by improving discovery and remediation. Over time, basic scanning and alerting products could face pressure if they lack differentiated data, workflows or response capabilities. The expert expects ongoing spending to shift toward continuous prioritization and remediation.
