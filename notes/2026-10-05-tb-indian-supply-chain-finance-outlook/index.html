Agenda

  • Working capital demand, formalisation and evolving supply chains driving market growth
  • Technology and evolving distribution models expanding product penetration and reach
  • Lending economics and asset quality trends shaping profitability
  • Banks, NBFCs (non-banking financial companies) and fintechs' competition on pricing, product innovation and risk appetite

Key Insights

  • NBFCs currently hold 25-30% market share in supply chain finance, growing faster than banks by offering hurdle-free loans without Reserve Bank of India's constraints
  • NBFCs' ability to reach underserved tier 3-5 cities – where banks lack presence – by providing flexible, on-demand liquidity to regional dealers based on physical inventory is an edge
  • Channel finance growth is running at 15-25% annually, significantly outpacing underlying dealer or corporate growth of only 3-4%, indicating migration from traditional cash credit to channel financing models
  • Expert notes business is shifting towards automated pull models. Corporates now directly debit dealer bank accounts for consignments via host-to-host integrations, eliminating manual paperwork, payment leakage and traditional cash credit reconciliation delays
  • Expert notes vendors are shifting from traditional 8% cash credit facilities to 6-7% invoice discounting platforms. This optimally bridges 90-120-day corporate payment delays, providing immediate liquidity while insulating accounts from NPA classification
