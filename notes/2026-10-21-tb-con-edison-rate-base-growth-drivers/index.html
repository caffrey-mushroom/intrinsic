Agenda

  • Returns outlook and regulatory lag pressures, given ratepayer affordability scrutiny
  • ConEd's (Consolidated Edison; NYSE: ED) rate base growth and potential CAPEX plan bottlenecks
  • Energy efficiency programme impacts on grid planning and service constraints

Key Insights

  • Expert believes energy efficiency does not suppress load growth over the long term, given five-year replacement cycles for obsolete equipment and technology adoption. Furthermore, Earnings Adjustment Mechanisms translate reduced per-customer electricity consumption into bottom-line bumps for ConEd
  • As a single new substation project can take 10 years and over USD 1bn due to delays such as regulatory hurdles, supply chain issues, neighbourhood opposition and labour atrophy, energy efficiency initiatives may be supportive in current grid-congested environment for ConEd
  • Battery storage investments could cut peak demand charges by c25% by smoothing electricity consumption profiles, potentially reducing use of costly peaker plants and helping ease service-area affordability constraints. Improved grid resiliency due to energy storage may be treated favourably by utility commission
  • Expert notes potential returns risk for ConEd if it fails to execute investments aligned with state policy objectives related to climate and energy efficiency targets
