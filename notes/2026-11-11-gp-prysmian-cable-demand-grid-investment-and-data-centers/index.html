SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, whose experience was in telecom cabling in German-speaking markets, described strong demand prospects for Prysmian across power cables, grid infrastructure, data centers, and fiber. They expect AI data-center construction and electrification to support cable demand in the U.S. and Europe, while noting that project delays and supply constraints may slow delivery. Germany remains a major opportunity for fiber deployment because its network is less developed than those of several other European markets.

Key Insights

The expert views Prysmian as unusually well positioned due to its broad product range, international manufacturing footprint, integrated production, and ability to supply both power and fiber cables. AI data-center investment was characterized as a major near-term opportunity, though potentially vulnerable if customer economics and debt become unsustainable. Grid investment is expected to remain strong beyond the immediate data-center cycle, driven by renewable-energy connections, energy security, and missing grid capacity. Transformer shortages are the most significant constraint on grid-project timing. Fiber supply constraints and limited installation capacity also delay work. Higher material costs are generally passed through to customers, according to the expert; delays are more likely to affect cash flow than profitability.
