Agenda

  • Power electronics and China utilisation
  • Rogers Corp's (NYSE: ROG) pricing and margin strategy
  • AI data centre cooling
  • Portfolio priorities

Key Insights

  • Rogers' Curamik DirectCool faces lengthy qualification processes and manufacturing investment requirements, delaying meaningful AI data centre revenue until H2 2027 or later
  • China-based power electronics competitors are rapidly closing the technical gap with Curamik, offering increasingly comparable specifications and putting pressure on Rogers' pricing power and market share in China's growing EV market
  • Rogers' H1 2026 R&D spending of USD 14m remains low relative to competitors, potentially constraining the engineering and qualification resources needed to capture sustainable AI revenue
  • Consensus expectations for Rogers to exceed USD 1bn in revenue by 2028 appear ambitious, resembling previously missed 2023 targets, while the company's project-based business model makes growth inherently uneven and difficult to sustain
  • Achieving consensus gross margin expectations of 34-35% by 2027-28 would likely require significant portfolio restructuring, including potentially divesting lower-margin businesses, with commodity cost pass-throughs and incremental operational improvements offering limited upside
