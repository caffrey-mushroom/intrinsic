核心摘要

  1. GoDaddy背景的专家认为AI会把北美建站市场的可触达空间扩大10–20%。
  2. Agent有望自动化中小企业约50%的线上运营工作流。
  3. 建站从模板工具转向AI生成加持续运营服务。
  4. 现有平台的优势在于域名、支付和客户关系，而不是建站本身。
  5. AI正在把SMB数字化的门槛进一步降低。

Analyst-led • 28 Oct 2026

Companies: WIX US, META US, FVRR US

Ankit Jhalaria (AJ) Expert at Primary Venture Partners

Dylan Koehler (DK) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Demand for AI-powered website creation and SMB digital services
  • Wix (NASDAQ: WIX) and Squarespace vs emerging AI-native builders
  • Does AI improve customer conversion, retention and monetisation?
  • AI agents' expansion into marketing, commerce and automated business workflows
  • How AI could disrupt traditional website builders and reshape competitive advantages

Key Insights

  • AI-driven website creation is expected to expand the North American addressable market by 10-20% over the next 6-12 months, driven by increased adoption across solopreneurs and micro-businesses
  • Established platforms, such as Wix, retain competitive advantages through distribution, hosting, payments, security and integrated ecosystems that AI-native builders struggle to replicate at scale
  • Micro-businesses increasingly favour low-cost DIY AI tools over traditional agencies, raising concerns about Wix Studio's long-term growth potential and agency-driven monetisation model
  • Expert estimates autonomous agents could automate 50% of routine small-business workflows within 1-2 years, potentially reducing website management costs and demand for traditional services
  • As users increasingly rely on autonomous agents instead of direct website visits and search-driven discovery, advertising-dependent websites face traffic and monetisation pressures, potentially disrupting established digital business models
