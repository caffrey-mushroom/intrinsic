核心摘要

  1. Switch的高级调试经理认为数据中心项目越来越难按期交付，主要制约因素是缺乏有经验的承包商和技术人员、设备供应、电力接入、许可审批和协调不力。
  2. 专家估计只有约35%的总承包商具备高水平交付数据中心项目的能力，被需求吸引的缺乏经验的新进入者占比仍在扩大。
  3. 专家批评Meta不强制执行工期、缺乏处罚，纵容了工会和承包商拖延，而谷歌对延误零容忍，托管商因依赖数据大厅交付创收而更有动力按期完成。
  4. 专家无法给出典型的工期延误幅度，认为设计、人员、设备、许可和领导力都会影响进度，但管理较好的团队可通过在不同站点间调配设备来缓解延误。
  5. 其亲身案例是从送电到交付全部数据大厅的80MW项目仅用6个月，部分团队每周工作70至75小时并实行七天排班。
  6. 据其了解谷歌在犹他州Eagle Mountain投入2亿美元建设公用设施，但因Meta占用了大部分电网和水资源，七年来仍无法建设。
  7. 部分站点在电网未到位前用发电机作为临时主电源，谷歌甚至将数千万美元的服务器放在帐篷中运行以尽快创收。
  8. 德州凭借电网容量、税收优惠和设备供应成为新的数据中心热点，而弗吉尼亚和硅谷等成熟市场人才充足但土地和电力受限。
  9. 专家认为即便AI泡沫破裂，数据中心建设需求仍将持续15至20年，届时项目会更少但执行更好，交付和盈利反而可能改善。

SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, a commissioning and construction professional with experience across hyperscale and colocation projects, discussed why data center projects are increasingly difficult to deliver on schedule. Key constraints include shortages of experienced contractors and technical staff, equipment availability, power access, permitting, and weak coordination. The expert said outcomes depend heavily on location and contractor quality, and emphasized that commissioning and project planning must be coordinated from the outset.

Key Insights

Execution capacity is a major bottleneck. The expert estimated that only about 35% of general contractors are highly capable of delivering data center projects. New entrants attracted by strong demand may lack relevant experience, increasing execution risk. Delays are highly project-specific. The expert could not provide a typical schedule overrun, noting that design, staffing, equipment, permits, and leadership all affect timing. Poorly managed projects risk postponing revenue, while better-run teams can mitigate setbacks by shifting equipment and effort between sites. Demand is likely to remain strong. The expert expects data center construction to remain a long-term growth market, even if AI- related construction eases. In that scenario, fewer but better-executed projects could improve delivery and profitability. Power availability shapes market positioning. Regions with existing grid capacity, incentives, and experienced labor are more attractive. The expert described Texas as a leading destination, while established markets have deeper talent but may face limits on land or available power. The moderator repeatedly sought concrete estimates of schedule slippage and the scale of market risk. The expert stressed that variation between projects prevents a reliable general estimate.
