NVDA: Leadership speculation builds as investigations widen – FT, Bloomberg, CBS, Arm, NYT

The FT reports an SEC accounting investigation; Nvidia and the agency declined comment. Bloomberg says California’s inquiry includes potential criminal violations, with no conclusions reached. Bessent declined to rule out a Huang return, saying responsibility for compliance failures must first be established. Arm confirmed Rene Haas’s agreement through 2033 and appointment as SoftBank vice chairman from December 1, reinforcing his commitment to Arm amid Nvidia succession speculation. Separately, the NYT reports that U.S. intelligence tracked Alice Huang’s contacts with a Chinese spy-agency official during her relationship with Jensen Huang. The leadership developments may fuel comeback hopes, but ongoing investigations and fresh allegations leave a return speculative.

NVDA / Servers: Partners halt some Southeast Asia shipments – Nikkei Asia

At least two Nvidia server suppliers have halted Southeast Asian shipments following new U.S. export controls, Nikkei reports; another is seeking licenses for newer orders. Suppliers say 2027 orders remain intact, while uncertainty is greater for later deployments. Expensive GPU inventory and licensing delays are emerging operational risks.

CRWD/ZS: CRWD -8% beats and announces $43.9B all-stock ZS acquisition, expanding into network security

CRWD will acquire ZS for ~$43.9B, issuing 1.135 CRWD shares per ZS share, or roughly $260/share, and expects immediate FCF/share accretion before synergies plus rev-growth, OM and FCF-margin accretion within the first year after close. The price is rich at ~160x ZS FY28 EPS, but the strategic rationale is clear as CRWD responds to PANW-driven platform consolidation by adding network security to its endpoint/cloud stack.

CRWD Q3 EPS of $0.34 beat Street $0.31 and ARR grew 27% to $6.26B vs. Street $6.19B, while Q4 ARR guide of ~$6.71B is slightly above Street ~$6.61B; management called Q3 its best quarter ever (NNARR was a $75M beat, strongest q in long time), with Falcon Guardian already >$100M ARR.

CRWD/ZS: Street sees strong strategic fit across AI security + SSE, but integration/growth dilution remain the key risks

Wedbush, Jefferies and Morgan Stanley all see strong strategic logic in combining ZS’s zero-trust/network telemetry with Falcon endpoint, identity, SIEM and AI security, creating limited product overlap, significant enterprise cross-sell and potentially allowing customers to retire ZS’s separate endpoint agent; Wedbush highlights ~8K ZS customers not using CRWD and management targets ~$1B of annualized rev synergies within 30 months plus ~$500M of cost synergies within 18-24 months. Bernstein says ZS’s remote access, web security, zero-trust and DLP are complementary rather than replaceable by CRWD and sees ZS’s proxy network plus CRWD’s Enterprise Secure Browser forming a potential corporate architecture for thin AI agents, while BofA says ZS fills CRWD’s largest portfolio gap and creates a more complete endpoint/identity/cloud/SecOps/SASE platform that competes more directly with PANW. Jefferies viewed ~$255/share/~40x CY27 FCF as strategically reasonable and MS estimates ~11% EPS accretion, but the group broadly flags integration/regulatory risk, CRWD’s limited large-DC operating experience and the addition of a lower-growth/lower-margin business that could pressure the revenue multiple despite earnings accretion.

Rockstar announced $3.79B in Grand Theft Auto VI sales during its first 24 hours, setting an entertainment-launch record. The reported result replaces pre-launch forecasts and follows 32.5M pre-release unit sales. Timing for the PC version and online offering remains unannounced, leaving further commercial milestones beyond the initial console launch.

TTWO: Raymond James/TD Cowen/Wells Fargo see major GTA VI upside after ~$3.79B day-one sales, with Online now the key debate

Raymond James, TD Cowen and Wells Fargo all see material upside after GTA VI generated ~$3.79B of day-one gross sales, implying roughly 40-42M units and putting FY27 bookings guidance of $8.0-8.2B well below the likely outcome. Raymond James raises FY27 units to 70M, cuts FY28 to 15M from 25M on pull-forward, accelerates PC to F4Q27 and lifts FY27/28 EPS to $8.79/$10.76; TD Cowen sees $9B+ FY27 bookings as plausible and estimates every incremental $500M of Rockstar bookings adds roughly $1 of EPS; Wells Fargo now sees 65M FQ3 units and 80M FY27 including PC. All three increasingly frame GTA Online timing, engagement and monetization as the key stock debate after the muted reaction to launch numbers that far exceeded buyside expectations, with RJ still expecting Online roughly six months after launch in early 2028. RJ Strong Buy/$300; TD Cowen Buy/$284; Wells OW.

NEWS

AI: Alexandr Wang says Riemann proof marks arrival of superintelligence, with automated AI R&D the critical inflection

Alexandr Wang argues AI has crossed from merely superhuman benchmark performance into a regime where raw intelligence is no longer the bottleneck, using the claimed Riemann Hypothesis proof as the clearest signal that models can now surpass humans on the hardest intellectual problems. He says the remaining barriers across coding, cyber, biology, economics and especially AI research are domain know-how, tooling and experimentation, implying models could soon automate AI R&D and recursively accelerate capability progress far beyond human monitoring. Wang’s core warning is that the industry should not treat ASI as inevitable and focus only on safety, but actively shape a path that preserves human control, agency and empowerment rather than defaulting to a societal “handover” to AI.

OpenAI: Chief scientist proposes limits on model development – Bloomberg

OpenAI chief scientist Jakub Pachocki proposed common industry thresholds that developers would not exceed until model monitoring and understanding reach defined safety milestones. Sam Altman endorsed the proposal. Pachocki warned AI could accelerate its own development as soon as 2027, but specified neither initial thresholds nor an implementation date.
