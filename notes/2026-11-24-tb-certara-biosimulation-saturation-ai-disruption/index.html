原标题：Certara – Software Stickiness & Sponsor Budget Impacts

#pharma #software #llm #ai-demand

Third Bridge · 2026-11-24

核心摘要

  1. Biogen临床药理与定量药理科学总监指出，大型及百亿美元级中大型药企的生物模拟（MIDD）软件渗透率已达天花板（所在公司过去10年核心工具几乎零增减），未来增量仅限于仍高度依赖外包的中小型biotech，但后者受限于百万美元级年费与专才缺口极少采购重型平台。
  2. Certara（CERT US）产品护城河两极分化：Simcyp在PD/PK及药物相互作用（DDI）领域仍是无可替代的金标准，具备极强定价权（5%—10%提价几乎不会引发客户流失，除非暴涨50%—100%），并与SLP的GastroPlus（偏制剂开发）各自占据不同预算；QSP按适应症定制（单模型60万—70万美元授权费）亦具高壁垒。
  3. 但CERT核心创收软件Phoenix WinNonlin面临长期侵蚀：其最核心的NCA分析算法并不复杂，正遭Python与R免费开源包逐步替代；而在复杂群体PK/PD建模上Phoenix完全不及ICON的NONMEM与SLP的Monolix，仅凭免编程GUI界面维持临床药理用户粘性。
  4. 药企正在结构性推进“去外包”（insourcing）：随着具备定量计算背景的人才增加且AI大幅提升内部效率，药企将常规建模收回内部并由现有软件席位直接吸收（如Biogen并购三家公司后全数内部消化），导致CERT咨询业务萎缩且无法转化为新增软件授权。
  5. 即便出现三期揭盲或监管申报冲刺等突发外包需求，药企往往基于对特定资深分析师的信任而选择收费贵约2倍的Metrum等专业精品咨询机构，而非CERT这类大平台，反映其高端品牌溢价不足。
  6. 监管改革对软件扩容拉动有限：FDA取消部分动物试验要求虽利好临床前in silico外包咨询，但ICH M15等MIDD新指南仅促使药企在现有软件授权下运行更多次模拟，难以带来新席位购买。
  7. AI目前约95%用于数据清洗、脚本编写、可视化及流程提效，尚未在核心科学推理上带来重大突破；药企普遍发现内部采购的ChatGPT、Claude、Gemini企业版即可实现同等提效，各软件商包装的AI平台高度同质化。
  8. 尽管CERT在2026年Q3上线Phoenix Cloud AI建模功能并集成NVDA BioNeMo，专家明确表示不会为此迁云或额外付费，认为药企自有大模型企业授权已令第三方SaaS AI增值模块失去吸引力。

Agenda

  • Pharma spending trends and subsequent impacts on commercial software vendor selection for players such as Certara (NASDAQ: CERT) across developmental drug pipelines
  • Shifts towards in-house pharmacometrics modelling and effects on consulting volumes while maintaining core software subscriptions
  • Certara's Simcyp – market positioning and pricing power vs peers such as Simulations Plus (NASDAQ: SLP)
  • Sizing Phoenix renewal stickiness against clinical modelling alternatives and emerging AI tools

Key Insights

  • Expert thinks software growth over the coming years will likely be restricted to smaller biotechs, as larger sponsor budgets are already saturated and unlikely to expand licenses
  • While Simcyp retains strong pricing power, expert cautions Certara's core Phoenix software faces steady erosion from free open-source tools as regulators gradually accept alternative platforms
  • Sponsors bringing modelling work in-house as teams grow more capabilities and use AI to boost productivity could steadily erode Certara's consulting revenue without expanding software licenses
  • Specialised boutiques often win complex overflow projects away from Certara despite charging c2x, with Certara largely capturing urgent, episodic deadline surges
  • Sponsors' existing in-house AI licenses handle routine workflow tasks just as effectively as vendor offerings, limiting demand for Certara's new cloud add-ons and keeping renewals flat
