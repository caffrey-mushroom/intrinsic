Optical / COHR / LITE: Chinese transceiver import curbs advance – Reuters

Reuters says the FCC is pressing ahead with restrictions on new Chinese optical-transceiver imports despite hyperscaler opposition. Chinese vendors supply roughly two-thirds of the market; Coherent and Lumentum currently lack the scale to replace them.
