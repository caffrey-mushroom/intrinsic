Agenda

  • Go-to-market strategy for CG Power's (NSE: CGPOWER) transformer portfolio
  • CG Power's competitive positioning vs Schneider (NSE: SCHNEIDER), Siemens Energy (NSE: ENRIN) and Hitachi Energy (NSE: POWERINDIA)
  • Capacity expansion implications and profitability outlook

Key Insights

  • Expert attributes c65% of CG's order backlog to transformers, c40% to 400-765kV and 25% to <220kV transformers. Power Grid Corp of India represents 30-40% of orders, while c10% are from NTPC or state electricity boards
  • Short-circuit testing poses execution bottlenecks. Mandated Central Electricity Authority requires tests for 765kV where CG lacks credentials, and re-testing is required for 400kV units following failures. Imported cold-rolled grain-oriented materials and copper reliance further restrict supply chains
  • CG's go-to-market strategy centres around portfolio packaging for volume growth, whereas Hitachi and Siemens have limited manufacturing capacity as they focus on profitability. CG's 500MVA 400kV transformer is 20% cheaper than Siemens'
  • Expert observes intensifying competition in 400kV transformers, with 7-8 manufacturers and seven more joining within six months, triggering 15-20% price decline over next one year. 765kV market is expected to remain stable across seven existing players, with 3-4 new entrants expected over 1-2 years
