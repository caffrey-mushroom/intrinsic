Companies: VOD GB, TEF ES, ZEG GB, DIGIS ES, MSFT US

Raffaele Gricinella (RG) SVP, Marketing at WAG Payments Solutions Inc (Eurowag)

Albie Amankona (AA) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Customer and revenue growth as Vodafone Spain competes with MasOrange, Telefónica (BME: TEF) and Digi (BME: DIGIS)
  • Scope for continued cost reductions and efficiency gains following Zegona's (LON: ZEG) transformation programme
  • Impact of PremiumFiber and FiberPass on network economics, CAPEX requirements and fixed-line competitiveness
  • Potential trajectory for EBITDA margins and cash generation over the next 12-24 months

Key Insights

  • Expert expects B2C revenue growth to remain sluggish over next 12-24 months, primarily in low-to-mid-single digits
  • B2B revenues could grow around 5-6% annually, driven by cloud, cybersecurity and IT services, although margins will compress due to operators reselling third-party infrastructure rather than owning it
  • Zegona's ownership enabled faster decision-making and commercial aggression that Vodafone Group previously blocked, particularly around pricing strategy and cutting expensive service contracts with the parent company
  • Lowi, Vodafone Spain's low-cost brand, was deliberately suppressed during Vodafone Group ownership due to cannibalisation fears, but has since become a major commercial weapon under Zegona's more aggressive strategy
  • On the Finetwork situation, taking control is not in doubt – the real question is the acquisition price, with Zegona preparing contingency strategies for both outcomes simultaneously
