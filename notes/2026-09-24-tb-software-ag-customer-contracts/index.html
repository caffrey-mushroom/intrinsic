Analyst-led • 9 Sep 2026

Companies: IBM US

Alessandro Coppola (AC) Chief Information Officer, Retail & Banking Technology at ING Bank NV

Philip Atkinson (PA) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Top-line risks posed to Software AG by AI encroachment and legacy product perception
  • Competitive displacement and customer priorities when considering migration
  • Margin opportunities and operational efficiency between A&N (Adabas & Natural) and Aris segments

Key Insights

  • Average spend with Software AG is stable to decreasing by up to 20-30% in three years. 60% of customers likely have a small estate, looking to churn, while large estates may downsize by 60-70% in coming 5-10 years. Price increases of 10-15% on contract renewal are expected to offset churn
  • A&N typically contributes 70-80% of contract value, with IT teams looking to migrate away given skill, knowledge and cost risks. However, business case currently isn't substantial. AI is expected to reduce migration costs by 20-30%, potentially causing churn
  • 50% of A&N customers may migrate in 2-3 years with AI. Migrations take 5-10 years, with cost reductions likely in waves and negotiated at renewals every 3-5 years
  • Aris spend is expected to be flat for coming 12 months as customers wait for AI upgrades before migrating to competitor platforms if AI functionality isn't added
