SUMMARY

Overview

The discussion compared CoreWeave, Nebius and Nscale across financing capacity, customer concentration, infrastructure execution, power access, GPU availability and relationships with NVIDIA. The expert argued that rising borrowing costs and concerns about excess AI infrastructure construction could restrict expansion across the neocloud sector.

Key Insights

Nebius was viewed as best positioned, supported by lower leverage, customer diversification, partner-funded capacity and stronger cloud engineering services. CoreWeave remains viable but financially constrained. Its asset-backed funding and major customers provide support, although higher interest expense may divert capital from enterprise products and broader growth. Nscale carries substantial execution risk. Its planned expansion depends heavily on an IPO and a small number of customers, while several projects lack fully established financing. Power availability remains a major bottleneck. Grid connections commonly require 3–5 years, making temporary on-site generation and disciplined project execution important. The expert ranked Nebius first for customer retention, managed cloud services and infrastructure deployment expertise, followed by CoreWeave and then Nscale. Flexible GPU cloud services combined with engineering support were considered more durable and higher-margin than bare- metal capacity contracts.
