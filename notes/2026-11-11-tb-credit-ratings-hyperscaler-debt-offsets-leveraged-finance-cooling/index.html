原标题：Credit Rating Industry Update – Hyperscaler Debt Offsets Leveraged Finance Cooling as Issuance Outlook Narrows

#穆迪 #标普 #超大厂发债

Third Bridge · 2026-11-11

核心摘要

  1. 前穆迪高管认为标普和穆迪的百年护城河很难被AI颠覆。
  2. 超大厂发债规模空前，但与铁路、光纤等过去的基建热潮相似，过度建设之后往往出现廉价收购资产的机会。
  3. 即便超大厂发债放缓，量子计算、代币化和医疗创新也会带来下一波资本市场活动。
  4. 评级机构约65%的利润率处在“金发姑娘区间”，过高会引来监管关注。
  5. 高个位数收入增长指引可信，依据是定价权、与GDP挂钩的发债增长和巨大的再融资到期墙。

Analyst-led • 28 Oct 2026

Companies: MCO US, SPGI US

David Platt (DP) Advisory Board Member at Starfish Network Inc

Dean Tsopanides (DT) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Trends and developments impacting the broader credit ratings operating environment
  • Issuance puts and takes, including AI-related debt and M&A financing driving investment-grade supply
  • Private credit commentary
  • Near-to-mid-term outlook

Key Insights

  • Rating agencies such as S&P Global maintain near-impenetrable competitive moats built over 100 years, covering trillions in debt globally, making the AI disruption of their core franchise highly unlikely
  • Regarding the AI infrastructure build-out, hyperscaler debt issuance, while historically large, rhymes with past infrastructure booms such as railroads and fibre optics, where overbuilding preceded cheap asset acquisitions
  • Debt issuance exhibits "alpha with positive drift," meaning that even if hyperscaler issuance moderates, quantum computing, tokenisation and healthcare innovation will generate the next wave of capital markets activity
  • Rating agency margins around 65% likely remain in a "goldilocks zone", as excessive margin expansion could attract unwanted regulatory scrutiny, while ongoing technology investment naturally absorbs efficiency gains
  • High-single-digit revenue growth guidance from ratings franchises appears credible given pricing power, GDP-linked debt issuance growth, and massive refinancing maturity walls representing trillions in recurring rating activity
