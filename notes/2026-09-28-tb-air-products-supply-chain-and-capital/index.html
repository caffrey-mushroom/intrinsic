Key Insights

  • Qatar's three helium plants had c2.2 billion cubic feet of nameplate capacity, representing 30-33% of global supply. Current Qatari production is likely only c100 million cubic feet due to conflict-related disruption. Expert is sceptical about unavailable volume becoming available over next 24-36 months as production restarts and damage is repaired, even if disruptions ended tomorrow
  • Air Products' margins on helium should be positive as price offsets volume losses in 2026 and potentially 2027 and pricing power sticks, contradicting management's cautious earnings guidance of a 2-3% headwind
  • Pulling helium from underground cavern storage adds 20-40% cost per cubic foot vs normal production, making cavern supply significantly margin-dilutive
  • Despite recent electronic customer investments and commitments from Air Products at USD 1bn in last twelve months, expert is sceptical about realised volume opportunities for electronic and industrial gas contracts and would be nervous about backlog durability through 2030 and beyond
