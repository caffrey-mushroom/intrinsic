Companies: SIGN CH, ELO NO, 468 HK

Uwe Tews (UT) Innovation & Portfolio Manager at SIG Group AG

Georgia Beattie (GB) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Global technical and supply chain challenges, plus impacts on the US market expansion
  • Financial and operational evaluation of Elopak's (FRA: 9J7) positioning in fresh vs aseptic packaging markets
  • Volatility in raw material costs, polymer pricing and the shift towards aluminium-free packaging structures
  • Strategic innovation priorities, filling machine strategy and potential leadership focus under incoming management

Key Insights

  • With European markets stagnating, Elopak's volume expansion targets high-growth, higher-margin emerging regions, such as North America and Asia. Elopak's roll-fed business represents highest-margin growth opportunity
  • Expansion into non-food categories, such as detergents, offers growth potential, with competitors absent from this category. Plant-based milk has 5-6% YoY growth rate, while ambient juice has negative 5-6% YoY growth rate. Elopak has cost or sustainability disadvantage, with 10% heavier carton structure vs benchmarks
  • Success hinges on Elopak perfecting its aluminium-free barrier solution, Pure-Pak eSense. Initial rollouts experienced technical shelf-life issues due to ethylene vinyl alcohol compound properties
  • Non-system Asian suppliers, such as Wintipak and Lamipak, threaten market share by offering reel or sleeve materials 10-20% cheaper, without machine servicing overheads, risking up to 10% of industry market share
  • Raw material price spikes are hedged c60%, with 3-6-month lags for pass-throughs. Other surcharges are broadly accepted by client base, with low switching rates of c5% annually
