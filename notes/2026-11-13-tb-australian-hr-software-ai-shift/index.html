原标题：Australian HR Software Market – AI Transformation & Customer Demand Appetite

#software

Third Bridge · 2026-11-13

核心摘要

  1. 前Elmo Software服务副总裁预计，澳大利亚HR软件市场未来2-3年将增长约5-6%，主要驱动力是ATO、Fair Work等机构频繁的合规变化以及AI技术进步。
  2. 按行业划分，医疗和养老（含NDIS残障服务）约占30-35%，零售约占30%，其余约40%分布在专业服务、教育、政府和小企业；医疗与零售未来3-4年复合增速有望达到10-15%，HR科技在这些行业的采用率增速约为7-9%。
  3. 约50-60%的企业仍在使用分散的老旧系统，专家预计其中50-60%将在未来2-3年迁移到“从招聘到离职”一站式、内嵌AI的单一供应商平台。
  4. 工时排班管理至关重要：零售企业中60-70%是按班次工作的员工，Fair Work约有200多项行业薪酬标准；专家认为构建薪资和排班系统远比构建HR系统复杂，因此从排班或薪资起家的厂商（UKG、Deel、ADP、Tanda、Employment Hero）向HR延伸更具优势。
  5. 薪资和排班系统粘性极强，客户流失率低于5%，客户可能与同一供应商合作15-20年；HR模块流失率则在10-15%，并面临AI原生工具的冲击。
  6. 竞争格局方面，Employment Hero和Tanda主导中小企业市场，Xero和MYOB从财务角度切入；中端市场有UKG、Humanforce、Elmo；企业级市场由Workday（HR主导的项目）和SAP SuccessFactors（ERP主导的项目）占据。
  7. 受宏观压力影响，专家预计未来12-18个月ARR增长基本持平，因为客户正在暂停新增支出，评估厂商原生AI与ChatGPT、Claude等第三方工具，福利类附加模块的追加销售也会放缓。
  8. AI是“带来增长机会的颠覆者”，智能排班、薪酬计算合规、薪资情报和自动视频面试入职等功能可以把客户花在第三方AI上的预算拉回平台；定价模式可能在12-18个月内从按用户计费转向按任务或按活动计费。

Agenda

  • AI transformation and product evolution
  • HR software adoption dynamics and vendor competitiveness across verticals
  • Regulatory compliance moats and multi-country product strategies

Key Insights

  • HR software sector revenue remains concentrated across healthcare and aged care, at 30-35%, and retail, at 30%, with remainder distributed across professional services, education and other sectors, while both healthcare and retail are projected to grow at 10-15% CAGR over next 3-4 years, driven by National Disability Insurance Scheme funding and shift-based quick-service retail expansion
  • However, expert expects near-term ARR growth to remain flat over next 12-18 months as clients pause new software spend to evaluate native AI vs third-party tools, compounded by slowed module upselling amid enterprise cost controls
  • Expert views AI as a vendor growth catalyst, embedding intelligent rostering, award pay calculations, compensation intelligence and automated video onboarding to capture spend from third-party tools, while highlighting hypothetical shift towards token-based activity pricing
  • Expert estimates 50-60% of organisations still operate fragmented legacy systems, but vendor consolidation will accelerate as 50-60% of these users transition towards single-vendor hire-to-retire suites in next 2-3 years
