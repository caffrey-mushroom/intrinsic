Agenda

  • Bacalhau asset – OPEX per barrel dynamics, FPSO (floating production storage and offloading vessel) utilisation and production output
  • Recoverable reserves examination, including comparison to other ultra-deepwater assets and infill drilling campaigns prospect
  • Equinor (NYSE: EQNR) inflationary cost pressures ahead for offshore assets in Brazil and available cost mitigation options

Key Insights

  • Bacalhau, now producing c110,000 barrels per day, is expected to reach nearly full 220,000-barrel-per-day nameplate capacity by 2027. Expert believes Bacalhau will be the main revenue generator for Equinor until 2028
  • Cash breakeven for Bacalhau sits at USD 30-40 per barrel, making it highly competitive among Brazilian pre-salt fields, with vessels, drillship day rates and labour as primary inflation risks
  • Annual cost inflation of 8-12% is anticipated across Brazilian deepwater rig rates, subsea equipment and offshore logistics, requiring performance-driven contracts and cross-asset resource-sharing to offset it
  • Bacalhau has one of the lowest carbon emission footprints in the world, achieving roughly half the industry average, largely through combined-cycle power generation pioneered on this FPSO
  • Raia project, arriving post 2028, is expected to shift Equinor Brazil's portfolio, with 50-60% of output being gas destined for the domestic market rather than oil exports
