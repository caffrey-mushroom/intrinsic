核心摘要

  1. 前Defined.ai高级项目总监曾负责一个由欧盟资助、总额3500万欧元、为期三年的葡萄牙语（欧洲葡语）政务对话式AI项目，团队超过55名工程师。
  2. 专家称项目末期核心NLP组件被一所大学开发的方案临时替换，偏离了原计划由Defined.ai自研的路径，尽管交付系统可运行。
  3. 公司核心业务是训练数据市场，数据来自肯尼亚、印度、拉美和东欧等地的人工贡献者，以及第三方提供方，专家估计外部数据占比超过50%，可能约70%，多以销售后分成方式结算。
  4. 客户可一次性购买数据集或订阅定期更新（如24个月），并可在市场数据之外定制采集与标注；医疗数据需经同意、匿名化和隐私控制，缺乏真实数据时由人工撰写对话样本。
  5. 公司公告称2025年收入增长65%、净收入留存率提升143%，但专家认为增长部分源于政府项目里程碑付款，预计2026年收入将下滑。
  6. 欧洲需求疲弱，27个成员国、24种官方语言及复杂监管增加了难度，加上裁员和销售团队流失，CEO已将重点转向美国市场。
  7. 若转向机器人、自动驾驶等物理AI，需要大量图像和视频数据及计算机视觉能力，而公司目前仅有一名计算机视觉人员，需重大投入。
  8. 主要竞争对手包括Appen（专家认为表现更好）和拥有庞大全球劳动力的TELUS Digital，微软、谷歌、Meta、OpenAI、Anthropic等大模型厂商既是潜在买家也是威胁；专家认为公司过度强调“伦理数据”定位而忽视企业级基础设施和销售执行。

SUMMARY

Overview

The discussion examined Defined AI’s data marketplace and services for collecting, annotating, validating, and supplying training data. The former program leader described the company’s work on a publicly funded European Portuguese conversational AI system, alongside commercial projects in sectors such as retail, banking, telecommunications, and healthcare.

The marketplace includes data sourced by Defined AI and external providers, with provider compensation often tied to sales. Customers can buy datasets outright or subscribe for periodic updates. Data may be customized beyond marketplace offerings through additional collection and preparation.

Key Insights

The government AI project diverged from its original plan: The expert said a university-developed component replaced a planned in-house natural language processing component late in the project. They considered the change a departure from the agreed approach, although the delivered system was presented as working. The expert was doubtful about European growth: They cited weak demand, complex language and regulatory requirements, reduced staffing, and sales-team turnover. They viewed expansion in the US as more promising but said it would require investment in relevant data and capabilities. A shift to physical AI could demand major investment: Robotics, autonomous vehicles, and other hardware-related applications require image and video data, as well as computer-vision expertise and infrastructure. The expert said Defined AI was not yet well positioned in these areas. The company’s commercial execution was a concern: The expert attributed slower-than-expected sector growth to an emphasis on ethical-data positioning over enterprise infrastructure, changing strategy, and limited sales capacity. Small models may suit narrower enterprise applications: The expert agreed they can be cost-effective for smaller use cases, while very large deployments may require partnerships with major technology providers. Defined AI’s potential value lies partly in data preparation, quality checks, and compliance support.
