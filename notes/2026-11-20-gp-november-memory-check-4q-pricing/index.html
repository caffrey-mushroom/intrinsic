#memory #semis #datacenter

核心摘要

  1. ScaleFlux产品与营销副总裁表示，四季度常规DRAM价格环比上涨10-15%，PC DRAM涨10-15%，客户端SSD涨13-18%，但涨价速度正在放缓。
  2. 企业级SSD涨幅预期由15-20%下调至10-15%，其中U.2因需求不足价格已开始下跌，E3在四季度保持坚挺；专家大胆判断NAND与企业级SSD涨价很可能已经见顶，2027年一季度价格大致持平。
  3. NAND现货价格近几周下跌20-25%，原始NAND约0.39-0.40美元/GB，企业级SSD约0.54美元/GB；PC和手机已出现需求破坏迹象（出货下降、OEM库存上升、配置削减），但AI服务器需求尚无裂痕。
  4. 专家预计DRAM短缺至少持续到2030年，2027-2028年新增产能基本已被预订，其公司手握资金寻求2028-2030年60-70PB的DRAM长期供应却无法获得承诺；新建晶圆厂至少需3年，且受设备、材料、人力、电力和水资源限制。
  5. 三大原厂已削减低毛利消费类业务，RDIMM等企业DRAM毛利率甚至高于HBM；SanDisk正借助第三方控制器将更多产能转向超大规模企业级SSD，预计2028年约35-40%的bit流向企业市场。
  6. 加速制程转换难以快速缓解供应，每个节点都需经历学习周期，良率需达90-96%，DRAM前道约240-250个工序站点，最多只能把周期压缩到约2年；专家认为AI辅助芯片设计目前远未兑现承诺。
  7. 三星通过价格让步提升HBM份额，HBM毛利率约75-77%，低于SK海力士的80%中高段，但其与超大规模客户合作设计base die的策略长期有望形成护城河；SK海力士凭借丰富的HBM迭代经验将保持领先。
  8. 由于HBM短缺且昂贵，Google、Meta、微软纷纷发布采用CXL、DDR4复用和压缩的方案；专家认为XL-Flash、高带宽闪存（HBF）等新技术在2030年前难以规模化，若需求下滑，纯NAND厂商SanDisk和Kioxia风险最高。

SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, who works with hyperscalers and memory suppliers, discussed DRAM, NAND, and enterprise SSD pricing, the strength of AI infrastructure demand, and the outlook for supply. The main tension is weakening demand in consumer markets versus continued heavy investment in data centers. The expert expects memory supply to remain constrained, while forecasting that NAND and enterprise SSD price increases have likely peaked.

Key Insights

Consumer PC and phone markets show early signs of demand destruction, including lower shipments, rising inventories, and reduced product configurations. AI-server demand, by contrast, has not shown comparable weakness. The expert expects DRAM shortages to persist through at least 2030, citing limited new capacity, long fab construction timelines, and customers unable to secure future supply despite substantial commitments. NAND and enterprise SSD demand remains strong, but the expert sees limited further price growth in the near term. Strategic customer contracts are increasingly important to pricing. Accelerating manufacturing transitions to newer nodes is unlikely to quickly resolve supply constraints. The expert emphasized the learning and yield challenges involved. New memory technologies, including high-bandwidth flash and other latency-tier solutions, are viewed as unlikely to reach meaningful scale before 2030.
