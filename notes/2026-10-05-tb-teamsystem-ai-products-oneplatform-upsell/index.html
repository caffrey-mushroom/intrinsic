Philip Atkinson (PA) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Potential stake sale opportunities, including AI product development
  • AI monetisation, attach rates and pricing models
  • OnePlatform's impact on customer retention, ARR and group revenue trajectory

Key Insights

  • 17-18% revenue growth is expected to come primarily from new regions, Spain and France, where regulation will likely act as a future tailwind and TeamSystem’s early positioning enabling them a strong starting point
  • Expert believes AI revenue could reach 20% of total revenue within three years, driven by consumption-based monetisation models replacing traditional SaaS pricing structures entirely
  • Cloud migration pricing increases vary dramatically by customer size, ranging from 10% for smaller clients up to 70% for larger enterprise customers migrating from legacy on-prem solutions
  • c70% of TeamSystem's customer base will be cloud-migrated by end of FY26, with micro-segment customers approaching near-100% adoption vs slower ERP enterprise migration
  • The 90% AI reuse metric likely reflects automation features customers use without recognising as AI, suggesting monetisation figures underrepresent actual AI-driven value being delivered today
