SU MMARY

Overview

The discussion focused on Gandeeva Therapeutics' preclinical asset, GND-323, which is being developed for solid tumors. The conversation revolved around the challenges and potential of molecular glues, particularly in targeting the MAP kinase pathway. The expert provided insights into the requirements for evaluating such therapeutics, the role of AI and Cryo-EM in drug discovery, and the complexities of therapeutic index and resistance.

Key Insights

The expert expressed skepticism about the current claims of Gandeeva's AI and Cryo-EM capabilities without concrete data. They emphasized the need for detailed information on the target and binding partners to assess the scientific and investment potential of GND-323. The potential for Gandeeva's platform to advance drug discovery was acknowledged, but the expert highlighted the gap between AI predictions and Cryo-EM validation. The expert stressed the importance of understanding the therapeutic index and resistance mechanisms, noting that clinical trials are essential for determining these factors.
