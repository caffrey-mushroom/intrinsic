核心摘要

  1. Adyen前数字支付副总裁认为，Anthropic在Stripe之外增加支付处理商是快速成长公司的常规步骤而非Stripe表现不佳，OpenAI也是在2026年年中才在Stripe之外加入Adyen。
  2. 增加第二家处理商的理由包括：扩大地域与支付方式覆盖（Adyen支持250多种支付方式，Stripe约100种，如印度UPI需本地收单资质）、防范宕机、增强合同议价能力，以及按卡BIN智能路由以提升批准率或降低成本。
  3. 大型科技公司通常自建路由切换系统，较简单的做法是按80%主处理商、20%备用处理商分配并按月轮换；专家预计Adyen最有可能成为Anthropic的下一家处理商。
  4. 若要向自有企业客户销售支付服务，Anthropic有三条路：成为持牌收单机构（难度最大、需按银行标准监管）、成为支付服务商PayFac（需在50个州注册汇款牌照，约需1.5–2年），或通过Stripe Connect/Adyen for Platforms做平台合作（不到90天即可上线）。
  5. 专家认为平台合作最可能被采用，类似Toast、Lightspeed的模式；以100美元交易为例，向商户收3%、底层成本2%，Anthropic可获约1%的净收入，但平台方费率较高，且双平台需重复对商户进行KYC。
  6. 其他变现方式包括约40%收入分成的推荐模式，或每笔收取5–10美分的网关费；当年支付量达到约750亿美元时，才值得考虑转为PayFac等更深度整合的模式。
  7. 代理式电商月环比增长约5000%但基数很小，Claude只需生成Visa Intelligent Commerce或Mastercard Agent Pay令牌即可参与，无需成为支付处理商。
  8. 随着处理商增多，Stripe令牌无法在其他处理商使用，Anthropic需要自建PCI Level 1金库或采用Basis Theory、VGS、Evervault等第三方中立金库，后者可大幅简化合规问卷，但每年需支付数百万美元费用。

SUMMARY

Overview

The discussion examined how Anthropic could expand its payment infrastructure and potentially sell payment services to businesses using its AI products. The expert, a payments-industry veteran, described multi-provider processing as a typical step for fast-growing companies, then compared options for payment processing, merchant services, and secure storage of payment credentials.

Key Insights

Adding a second processor was characterized as a routine move, not evidence that Stripe is underperforming. Potential benefits include broader geographic and payment-method coverage, outage protection, stronger contract negotiations, and routing transactions to improve approval rates or reduce costs. The expert expects Adyen to be a likely additional processor, while noting that Anthropic could choose another provider. For selling payments to its own business customers, the expert considers a platform partnership with Adyen or Stripe more likely than becoming a payment facilitator or licensed acquirer. This approach could launch faster and avoids substantial regulatory and technical work. The expert expects Anthropic to consider a neutral payment vault as it adds processors, since processor-specific tokens generally cannot be used with other providers. A third-party vault may be simpler than building and maintaining one internally. Rapid growth in agentic commerce was noted, but the expert said it remains a small share of overall commerce and does not determine whether Anthropic needs to become a payment processor.
