Agenda

  • SoundHound's (NASDAQ: SOUN) acquisition of LivePerson, highlighting the integration and cross-sell opportunities
  • Oasys (Orchestrated Agent System) adoption and the shift towards enterprise AI agents
  • Restaurant automation, deployment growth and customer economics
  • Expansion across healthcare, automotive and other enterprise verticals
  • Competitive differentiation, organic growth and the path to profitability

Key Insights

  • SoundHound's orchestration strategy positions it between foundational models and enterprise applications, connecting disparate data and AI systems rather than competing directly with OpenAI or Google
  • Acquisition of LivePerson could accelerate that strategy by providing access to Fortune 100 customers, giving SoundHound a potentially valuable credibility and distribution foothold in enterprise AI
  • Near-term, restaurants and SMB customer service remain attractive revenue opportunities, with demonstrable ROI in as little as 30-60 days and flexible transactional or fixed-fee pricing
  • Unexpected token costs are hitting chief information officer budgets and making AI deployments difficult to forecast, potentially creating an opening for SoundHound's Oasys platform as enterprises seek more predictable, managed AI deployments
  • Oasys's ability to deploy agents on edge devices could extend SoundHound beyond customer service into physical AI applications including robotics, autonomous vehicles, drones and disconnected environments
