Companies: GETY US, ADBE US, SSTK US

Kristopher Petro (KP) Head, Sales at Silverlabs Technologies Inc (Rizzle)

Dylan Koehler (DK) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Demand trends across enterprise, media and creative content
  • Fallout from the terminated Shutterstock (NYSE: SSTK) merger and standalone strategy
  • OpenAI partnership and opportunities for new content distribution
  • GenAI's impact on iStock, search traffic and traditional stock imagery
  • Getty Images' balance sheet, cash flow and options to improve capital structure

Key Insights

  • Getty's c30% EBITDA margins have remained resilient despite its significant debt burdens, suggesting a fundamentally healthy business capable of returning to low-single-digit revenue growth following restructuring
  • Expert attributes Getty's financial challenges primarily to previous ownership, which added cUSD 2bn in debt, with rising interest rates further complicating repayment despite stable operating performance
  • AI disruption poses the greatest risk to iStock's lower-quality, easily replicable content, making it a potential divestiture candidate, while Getty's differentiated premium library provides greater defensibility
  • Growth opportunities centre on editorial video and AI data licensing, with the latter potentially expanding from 3-5% to c10% of revenue as demand for commercially safe training datasets increases
