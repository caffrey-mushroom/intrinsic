#industrials #transport

Third Bridge · 2026-10-15

Analyst-led • 9 Oct 2026

Companies: BA US, AIR US, AIR FR, LUV US

Michael Fleck (MF) VP, Sales at Consortia Aerospace

Quinton Flore (QF) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Revenue breakdown across heavy maintenance, component repair and PMA [Parts Manufacturer Approval] trading businesses
  • Evaluating ATS's (Aviation Technical Services') relationships with major US airlines
  • Narrowbody turn times as the pitch against AAR Corp (NYSE: AIR), MRO (maintenance, repair and overhaul) solutions and the El Salvador cost base
  • Where in-house airline capacity limits ATS's addressable market

Key Insights

  • Expert foresees 6-8% annual growth for ATS, driven by margin expansion and PMAs that can save weeks in turn time. This is extremely valuable as every day of delay costs airlines USD 150,000
  • ATS's heavy maintenance is 60-70% of revenue at 15-18% margins. Its growing 350-plus part PMA business sees 65-75% margins and c10% of total revenue
  • Narrow-body heavy maintenance demand still outpaces supply and will lead to "some pretty good years", lifting pricing – 737 checks from USD 90,000 to USD 120,000 and labour from USD 65 to USD 75-90 per hour – supporting a realistic 30% heavy maintenance margin target for ATS
  • Expert was surprised by AAR's 10.7x price tag for MRO Holdings. Expert doubts the reported USD 75m cost synergies, and puts realistic capacity at 1,400-1,600 lines, rather than the reported 3,000
  • After 11 years and a failed sale to AAR, expert anticipates ATS's sponsor to push for an exit soon
