Agenda

  • Update on Daiichi Sankyo's (TYO: 4568) recent clinical trial results
  • Competitive landscape in NSCLC (non-small cell lung cancer) treatment
  • Commercial opportunity for Enhertu's (fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki's) first-line indication

Key Insights

  • DESTINY-Lung04's 14.3-month median PFS is likely to establish Enhertu as the new first-line standard for HER2-mutated NSCLC, assuming no OS detriment. Meanwhile, anticipated release of zongertinib's confirmatory phase 3 data in January 2028 could potentially create direct first-line competition against Enhertu
  • Enhertu's application in NSCLC could expand beyond initial target to include patients with HER2 overexpression – 1-5% of cases – and EGFR-mutant patients who develop HER2 amplification following TKI resistance – 12-13% of cases – in addition to HER2-mutant patients – 2-4% of cases
  • Enhertu's future combination strategies in NSCLC may include PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitors and TKIs such as zongertinib or sevabertinib, while avoiding chemotherapy. Furthermore, deploying these combinations or single agents in earlier disease stages – adjuvant/neoadjuvant – presents a significant opportunity to expand the treatable patient population
