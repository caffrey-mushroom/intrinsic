Agenda

  • Cloud and government service growth drivers and Macquarie Technology Group's (ASX: MAQ) competitive moat
  • Service delivery margins, customer retention and churn dynamics
  • Managed data and AI workload monetisation

Key Insights

  • Macquarie Government's secure internet gateway revenue faces decline as federal agencies no longer mandate those services, but slow government contract cycles provide stability for c3 more years
  • Replacing one lost secure internet gateway customer requires c2 secure access service edge customers to match revenue, creating a sales gap that Macquarie must close through volume, although retaining attached secure internet gateway support services mitigates impact
  • Macquarie's longevity in government and consistent quality reputation serve as key differentiation against threat from Amazon subsidising initial implementation costs and building defence-grade data centres
  • Negative Vetting Level 1-cleared personnel command a 15% salary premium compared to alternative; Canberra-based adds additional 20% premium vs non-cleared Sydney equivalents
  • Elevated staffing costs projected to grow single digits in line with inflation over 2-3 years. Macquarie likely to effectively manage them by leveraging internal graduate program to develop talent in-house, which drives high retention and lowers base employee costs
