Courtney Lansing (CL) Managing Director, Strategy, Sales & AI Transformation Consulting at Blue Strategy Consulting LLC

Liam Dorsey (LD) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Levers to stimulate demand for Alaska Airlines (NYSE: ALK) amid volatile cost environment
  • Price recapture ability with normalised and growing customer segments
  • Product roadmap with expanding network strategies
  • Disciplined international expansion and long-term risks

Key Insights

  • 2026's low-to-mid-double-digit RASM growth will likely normalise to single digits in 2027 industry-wide as fuel surcharge tailwinds fade and capacity discipline softens. Alaska Airlines' domestic capacity being flat in H2 2026 and 3-4% international capacity expectations show strong discipline and position it well for RASM gains compared to peers
  • International RASM will likely be dilutive in near term, driven by early capacity plans, competitive price dynamics and slightly inferior products to peers. RASM gap likely won't resolve until international strategies mature and 787 deliveries with new configurations begin around 2028
  • Non-US revenues, currently c10% of Alaska's total, realistically won't reach 25% until 2032 or later, requiring additional wide-body tranches and JVs similar to other international partnerships in the industry
  • Premium card sign-ups significantly exceeded internal expectations, likely due to conservative planning that underestimated Hawaiian member conversions. This has room to continue in H2 2026, but will see fairly substantial normalisation in 2027
