SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, a technology services provider with a large enterprise customer base, described recent demand for Salesforce CRM and Workday HCM and finance software. Both businesses follow a seasonal pattern, with activity typically strengthening later in the year as customers aim to implement systems around the start of a new year. The expert said demand remains solid, though sales cycles are longer and decisions can be delayed.

Key Insights

Salesforce remains strongly positioned in large enterprises. The expert said it continues to win enterprise CRM deals, with limited direct competition at the high end. Modernizing aging, internally built systems is a recurring reason for adoption; attempts to refresh them with AI have often fallen short. AI interest is translating into pilots and early deployments, but not broad rollouts yet. Salesforce’s AI products and Data Cloud are attracting customer interest, and some pilots are expanding into production or larger contracts. The expert expects broader implementations as customers gain experience. Salesforce’s consumption-based pricing has faced customer resistance. Buyers were concerned about not knowing their future usage, though the expert said those concerns have begun to ease. Renewals remain strong, and pricing has not been a significant cause of customer losses. Workday is gaining traction in the mid-market. Its move beyond large enterprises is bringing it into closer competition with established HR software providers. Workday AI interest remains comparatively limited, apart from onboarding, offboarding, and recruiting applications. The moderator pursued the implications of AI for enterprise software. The expert rejected the idea that AI will broadly replace complex enterprise systems, citing implementation challenges and the difficulty of maintaining legacy platforms.

Competitive Landscape

Market

Positioning and competitive dynamics

Salesforce CRM

Strong in enterprise accounts; competes with Microsoft Dynamics and displaces Oracle, SAP, and homegrown legacy systems. HubSpot is more relevant in smaller businesses.

Workday HCM

Expanding into the mid-market and competing with ADP, UKG, and Dayforce. Workday is moving down-market as some competitors pursue larger customers, increasing discounting and bid competition.

AI and legacy systems

Customers are testing AI on existing systems, but the expert said this has not reliably removed the need to replace or modernize complex platforms.
