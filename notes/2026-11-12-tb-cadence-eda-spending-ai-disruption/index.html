原标题：Cadence Design Systems – EDA Spending Trends & Growth Drivers

#semis #software #asic

Third Bridge · 2026-11-12

核心摘要

  1. 这位在Cadence工作了35年的前高管指出，EDA行业前20大客户贡献约60%到三分之二的收入，Cadence在顶级客户中渗透率很高，过去曾有十大客户中八家采用Cadence全流程。
  2. 超大规模云厂商的EDA支出每年增长约15-20%，增量中设计数量增加和复杂度提升大约各占一半；传统芯片公司三年续约增幅被压在约8-12%，Intel等IDM正在压缩EDA支出。
  3. 云厂商往往两年就用完预估三年的软件容量，合同中期加购可带来约10-15%的额外支出；典型合同中约80%为固定的期限授权，约10%可调换，另外约20%为可灵活提取的EDA Card额度。
  4. 专家估计2027年Palladium和Protium销售中约70-80%来自现有客户扩容；Palladium在仿真领域装机粘性强、难以被替代，Protium则难以取代Synopsys的HAPS，基本只是补充角色。
  5. Cadence约80%的积压订单为软件，期限多为三年，硬件积压约30-40周，交付风险不大；IP业务过去规模在2-5亿美元，远小于Synopsys的约20亿美元（目标30亿美元），高速接口、die-to-die和HBM IP是缩小差距的关键。
  6. 在封装领域，每1美元IC封装设计工具订单可带动5-10美元多物理场分析订单，2.5D/3D集成会进一步放大这一比例；系统分析工具从导入到量产通常需要6-9个月。
  7. 最大的不确定性在于客户直接用Claude等模型自主编排EDA工具、甚至自研SPICE等引擎，这会挤压Cadence的AI层定价；核心工具被替代的风险约在三年后，软件续约增长转为持平将是早期预警信号。
  8. 专家认为剔除并购后约18%的经常性收入增长在2027年可以维持，但更高较难；长期营业利润率预计保持在20-25%左右。

Companies: 2330 TW, SNPS US, CDNS US, INTC US, NVDA US

Krishna Kumar (KK) Chief Revenue Officer at Voltai Inc

Garrett Waters (GW) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Cadence Design Systems' (NASDAQ: CDNS) positioning amid rising chip-design activity and hyperscaler custom silicon investment
  • Enterprise customer spending, EDA (electronic design automation) wallet share and opportunities to expand software and hardware adoption
  • Competitive dynamics vs Synopsys (NASDAQ: SNPS), focusing on customer purchasing decisions, pricing power and semiconductor IP
  • Agentic AI monetisation and potential disruption to traditional EDA licensing

Key Insights

  • Hyperscaler EDA spending could grow 15-20% annually, split roughly evenly between additional design starts and rising complexity, vs 8-12% renewal growth at traditional semiconductor customers
  • c70-80% of Palladium and Protium demand could come from existing-customer expansion, with Palladium's installed-base stickiness limiting emulation displacement while Protium struggles to displace Synopsys' HAPS
  • Cadence could capture USD 5-10 in multiphysics analysis bookings for every USD 1 spent on integrated circuit packaging design tools, with 3D integration potentially increasing that opportunity further
  • Hyperscalers can exhaust projected three-year software capacity within two years, creating mid-contract expansion opportunities that could drive c10-15% additional spending
  • Customer-owned AI orchestration could pressure Cadence's AI-layer pricing, while core EDA tool displacement could emerge in roughly three years, with flat software renewals providing an early warning signal
