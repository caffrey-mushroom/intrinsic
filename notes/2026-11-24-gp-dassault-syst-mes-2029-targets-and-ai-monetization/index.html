核心摘要

  1. DSY 在 2026 年资本市场日提出到 2029 年营收超 80 亿欧元、营业利润率达 35%（较现水平提升 4 个百分点，其中 1 个百分点用于再投资）及双位数增速的目标，前北欧区副总裁认为该指引颇为激进，需 2026-2029 年保持 8%-9% 均值且多项改革同时见效。
  2. 核心工业软件业务计划从 2026 年约 48 亿欧元增至 58 亿欧元（增量 10 亿欧元），专家预计约三分之二来自航空航天与汽车等现有大客户向 3DEXPERIENCE（3DX）迁移，风险较低；而生命科学及需近乎翻倍的消费品等新市场贡献的三分之一增量则风险较高。
  3. 过去 3-4 年增速放缓与执行乏力密切相关：DSY 近年削减了 V5 版本经销商利润空间且渠道管理繁琐，亟需增加前线销售与服务人员，并围绕 CATIA/3DX 重建具备云与系统集成能力的专业伙伴生态。
  4. 大型 OEM 厂商正推动供应链迁移至 3DX，但并无 OEM 愿意替中小供应商买单，供应商只能自费将升级视为业务准入成本（中型客户完整迁移约需 1 年）；同时 DSY 亟需摒弃“3D Universe/虚拟孪生”等晦涩术语并拿出更多大客户签约公告。
  5. AI 商业化仍处于未验证阶段：DSY 计划在现有订阅外叠加预付年度用量的“Blue Tokens”用量计费模式，但目前尚无明确落地客户，客户对“在已有高额订阅基础上再次付费”心存抵触，预计 2026-2027 年仍将停留在试错阶段。
  6. 更大的结构性隐忧在于：大型制造业 OEM 更倾向直接与 OpenAI、Anthropic 等外部大模型厂商合作而非采用早期的 DSY AI 套件；同时 AI 提效可能导致客户更快削减 CATIA/SolidWorks 传统付费席位，造成总订阅与年费收入承压。

SUMMARY

Overview

The discussion examined whether Dassault Systèmes can accelerate growth and expand margins through subscriptions, industrial software adoption, AI monetization and changes to its partner network. The expert, a former senior executive, viewed the targets as achievable only if several operational and commercial improvements take hold. Key topics included sales capacity, adoption of the 3DEXPERIENCE platform, cross-selling, partner incentives, product messaging and demand from major manufacturers.

Key Insights

The 2029 targets are ambitious, not straightforward. The expert said delivery depends on stronger execution, more effective field teams and improved results across several business areas. Dassault needs more customer-facing capacity and a more productive partner ecosystem. The expert said current channel administration can be complex and reseller incentives may not sufficiently encourage investment and growth. Major OEMs could prompt suppliers to adopt 3DEXPERIENCE, but suppliers may bear the cost themselves. OEM-created supplier communities may make adoption more attractive, though the pace of change remains uncertain. Growth from established industrial customers appears less risky than growth from newer markets, life sciences and consumer businesses. The latter areas need to demonstrate stronger results. AI monetization remains unproven. Customers will need to see clear value before paying for usage-based subscriptions, and AI could reduce demand for traditional software seats. The expert stressed that clearer, more practical product messaging could help customers understand the benefits. The moderator pursued this point and probed how AI adoption and seat reductions might affect revenue.
