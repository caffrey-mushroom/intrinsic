原标题：Harbour Energy – Operational Activity in Norway & Argentina Expansion

#Harbour #挪威 #FLNG

Third Bridge · 2026-11-11

核心摘要

  1. 专家称Harbour挪威资产稳态生产效率通常在80%后段到90%区间。
  2. 挪威约24个项目在疫情年同年获批，现在同时进入执行，船舶和钻机短缺是近期主要风险。
  3. 新获的九个挪威勘探许可基准情形可增加1–2亿桶毛储量，乐观可达3亿桶。
  4. 阿根廷技术风险低于挪威，但政治和财政不确定性更大，包括资金汇出困难。
  5. 阿根廷FLNG保留约20%未签约，可能是为了捕捉地缘冲突带来的现货LNG价格飙升。

Agenda

  • Harbour Energy's (OTCMKTS: HBRIY) operational activity in Norway – key operating assets, OPEX per barrel dynamics and production outlook
  • Examining exploration licences awarded in 2025 and commercial discovery prospects
  • Non-operator responsibilities in Argentina and FLNG (floating liquefied natural gas) project expansion

Key Insights

  • Harbour's production efficiency is strong in Norway – typically in the high-80s-to-90s percentage range once assets reach steady state
  • Vessel and rig shortages pose significant near-term operational risk in Norway – c24 projects received final investment decision in the same pandemic-era year and are now simultaneously entering execution phases. Multi-year rig contracts can reduce tender costs and mobilisation time
  • Realistic gross volume addition from Harbour's nine newly awarded Norwegian exploration licences is 100-200 million barrels in the base case, with 300-million-barrel upside scenario possible
  • Expert thinks Argentina carries less technical risk than Norway but significantly more political and fiscal uncertainty, including historical difficulties repatriating cash and regulatory coercion over capital deployment
  • Retaining the final 20% of Argentina floating liquefied natural gas offtake uncontracted is likely deliberate, preserving optionality to capture spot liquefied natural gas price spikes driven by geopolitical events such as Middle East or Russia-Ukraine conflicts
