原标题：Pumped Storage Hydropower 2026 Q3 Update – Industry New Projects’ IRR Trends & CSG Energy Storage (Conducted in Mandarin)

#power #energy #china

Third Bridge · 2026-11-17

核心摘要

  1. 前南方电网专家委员会成员指出，114号文将抽水蓄能定价从633号文下“一站一价、保障6.5%内部收益率”改为“全省统一容量电价、隐含中枢IRR约4.5%”，容量电价普遍下降10%-20%，目的是遏制单位造价高、经济性差的项目盲目上马。
  2. 已公布的省份中，山东统一容量电价为485元/千瓦·年，广西预计约480元，而参照广东一站一价机制约为500-560元；新投运项目基本难以赚钱，但专家认为价格继续下调的可能性不大。
  3. 抽蓄成本过去计入输配电价，如今归入系统运行费；以一座1.2GW电站为例，按633号文机制会使省内电价上升约0.004元/千瓦时，各级政府均不愿涨电价，这是政策收紧的根本原因。
  4. “双50”目标下新型电力系统仍需大量储能，新能源高渗透地区抽蓄合理占比约10%-12%、其他地区约5%-8%；“十四五”60GW目标完成约80%，“十五五”规划100GW，但专家认为在当前政策下难以实现，国网和南网新开工更趋谨慎。
  5. 南网储能截至2026年6月底在运抽蓄12.68GW，收入高度依赖容量电价，电量收入仅来自上水库天然来水，辅助服务收入几乎为零；2026年上半年抽蓄收入27.9亿元、同比增长27%，主要来自梅州二期和南宁电站（各1.2GW）投产。
  6. 2027年将投产惠州中洞和肇庆浪江两座各1.2GW电站，“十五五”期间平均每年约投产2座、2.4GW；梅州二期单位造价不超过5000元/千瓦，南宁约5500-5600元/千瓦，经济测算按20年运营期回收投资，水工建筑设计寿命50年。
  7. 统一电价下盈利取决于造价是否低于省内平均水平、能否与电网签订全容量电价协议以及所在省份经济承受力，广东项目原则上不亏，广西、云南、贵州项目可能难盈利；企业也可通过延长折旧年限改善账面利润，现金流不受影响。
  8. 调峰水电（鲁布革、天生桥二级）采用政府核定电价、零燃料成本，利润高度依赖来水；2026年上半年该业务收入增长近20%、利润增长26%，专家预计2027年来水难以超过2026年，但未来三年也不太可能大幅减少。

Agenda

  • IRR trends for legacy and new pumped-storage hydropower projects following Document No 114, along with the 15th Five-Year Plan and policy positioning for pumped storage hydropower
  • Construction trends for pumped-storage projects among Chinese enterprises, and project progress and return differentials for CSG Energy Storage (SHA: 600995)
  • Water inflow expectations, tariff mechanisms, and earnings trend analysis for CSG Energy Storage’s peak-shaving hydropower projects

Key Insights

  • Document No 114 shifts pumped-storage hydropower pricing from a “plant-by-plant tariff with a guaranteed 6.5% IRR” to a “province-wide unified tariff with an implied central IRR of 4.5%”, resulting in a general 10-20% reduction in capacity tariffs. The objective is to screen projects and curb the indiscriminate rollout of developments with high unit construction costs and poor economic viability. Expert believes that achieving the 100GW target of the 15th Five-Year Plan would be difficult under the current policy environment
  • CSG Energy Storage’s revenue from its existing 12.68 million kilowatts of pumped storage is highly dependent on capacity tariffs, with limited revenue from electricity sales and almost no revenue from ancillary services. It adds 2.4 million kilowatts in 2026 and plans to invest a further 2.4 million kilowatts in 2027. The expansion of existing capacity is highly certain, but the expert believes profitability will diverge clearly between new projects at Guangdong sites and those in provinces such as Guangxi and Yunnan
  • CSG Energy Storage’s peak-shaving hydropower projects, such as Lubuge and Tianshengqiao II, operate under conventional regulated tariffs with zero fuel costs, meaning their earnings are highly tied to water inflows; abundant water inflows in 2026 drove strong growth, and the expert expects 2027 water inflows are unlikely to surpass 2026 levels
