核心摘要

  1. 受访者是前Cisco亚太、日本和中国区AI与自动化CTO。
  2. 专家认为剔除超大云AI基础设施后，Cisco基础收入增速可能从FY27约10%放缓到FY28–29每年4–6%，因为企业网络换代周期趋于成熟。
  3. 超大云AI基础设施收入增速可能从FY27的88%放缓到之后的约35–40%，基数变大和竞争激烈带来订单不均和价格压力。
  4. 未来2–3年只有30–40%的大企业会在多个部门规模化运行AI，初期网络投资多被现有换代预算吸收，而非全新增量。
  5. Cisco毛利率可能从FY26约67%稳定在FY28–29的约65%附近，低毛利超大云硬件占比上升和折扣抵消了软件增长。
  6. Arista在园区网络换代中带来额外的价格压力。

Agenda

  • Cisco's (NASDAQ: CSCO) networking demand and revenue growth outlook
  • Hyperscaler AI infrastructure growth and competitive positioning
  • Enterprise AI adoption, infrastructure modernisation and software monetisation
  • Pricing pressure, product mix and gross-margin sustainability

Key Insights

  • Cisco's underlying revenue growth, excluding hyperscaler AI infrastructure, could moderate from approximately 10% in FY27 to 4-6% annually by FY28-29, as the enterprise networking refresh cycle matures, partially offset by AI infrastructure upgrades and software subscriptions
  • Cisco's hyperscaler AI infrastructure revenue growth could moderate from 88% in FY27 to approximately 35-40% beyond FY27, as larger revenue comparisons and intense competition contribute to uneven customer orders and pricing pressure
  • Only 30-40% of large enterprises may have AI running at meaningful production scale across multiple departments within 2-3 years, with much of the initial networking investment absorbed into existing refresh budgets rather than generating entirely incremental Cisco revenue
  • Cisco's gross margin could stabilise near 65% through FY28-29, down from approximately 67% in FY26, as a growing mix of lower-margin hyperscaler hardware and competitive discounting offset software growth, with Arista Networks adding pricing pressure during campus networking refreshes
