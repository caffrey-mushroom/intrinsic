原标题：Infoblox – The Cloud DDI Playbook

#networking #security #software

Third Bridge · 2026-11-12

核心摘要

  1. Infoblox的核心业务是DDI（DNS、DHCP与IP地址管理），第二大产品是基于DNS的安全产品Threat Defense，近年新增Universal Asset Insights，并于2026年8月收购网络可观测性公司Kentik。
  2. 前亚太及日本区收入赋能负责人表示，约70%的业务来自现有客户扩展，新客户获取占比不足30%；企业级销售周期长达数月、获客成本高，因此交叉销售是核心策略。
  3. 公司正推动客户从传统NIOS设备迁移到云托管订阅制的Universal DDI，通过停止支持旧版本软件迫使客户升级；专家任职期间大多数客户尚未迁移，按三年测算订阅总成本高于一次性硬件采购。
  4. Universal DDI的核心价值在于统一管理多云和混合环境；单一云客户更可能使用云厂商原生方案（如Google Cloud DNS本身就是Infoblox白标产品）或几乎免费的微软DNS，这是主要风险。
  5. BlueCat和EfficientIP价格更低，但DDI属于关键任务系统，迁移风险高、客户流失少，大型企业更看重品牌、可扩展性、企业级支持和安全交叉销售，Infoblox不打价格战。
  6. Threat Defense在亚太及日本区同比增长超过30%，而DDI增速仅在10%左右；销售配额中约70%为DDI、30%为Threat Defense，但网络团队与安全团队采购分离，限制了交叉销售。
  7. DNS安全是防火墙的补充而非替代，可把漏洞拦截率从约95%提升到约99%；安全和Asset Insights等新产品开始按token消耗计费。
  8. 在亚洲，渠道和SI伙伴至关重要，但部分伙伴只卖约3万美元的基础方案，而非10-20万美元的高价值组合；产品线扩张使销售培训和知识规模化面临挑战。

Agenda

  • Infoblox's cloud migration and subscription revenue transition
  • Competitive positioning vs BlueCat, EfficientIP and hyperscaler-native solutions
  • Threat Defense growth, customer retention and cross-selling opportunities
  • Go-to-market strategy, sales productivity and growth outlook

Key Insights

  • c70% of Infoblox's business comes from existing customers, with high new-customer acquisition costs reinforcing the importance of cross-selling security and observability solutions into its installed base
  • Infoblox's migration from legacy Universal DDI subscriptions could increase recurring revenue and customer lifetime value, with legacy software support deadlines encouraging adoption and subscription costs exceeding traditional hardware purchases over three years
  • Infoblox's Threat Defense business grew more than 30% annually in APAC and Japan, outpacing traditional DDI from a smaller base, although cross-selling remains constrained by separate networking and security purchasing teams within enterprise customers
  • Infoblox's multi-cloud DDI capabilities support premium pricing and customer retention despite cheaper alternatives from BlueCat and EfficientIP, although single-cloud adoption could increase competition from hyperscaler-native solutions
