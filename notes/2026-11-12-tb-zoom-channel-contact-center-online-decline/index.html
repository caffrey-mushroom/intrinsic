2026.11.12 | TB | ZM | Zoom Channel, Contact Center & Online Decline

原标题：Zoom – Enterprise Strength & an Anthropic Windfall Obscure a Mid-single-digit Core

#software #agents

Third Bridge · 2026-11-12

核心摘要

  1. 前Zoom渠道加速负责人表示，在他任职期间，Zoom的渠道收入贡献从约23%提升至略高于40%，Contact Center上线第一年渠道贡献就超过50%；渠道模式从模仿RingCentral的TSD推荐佣金制，演变为银、金、铂金分级加返利、可叠加激励的价值交换模式。
  2. 各家厂商的渠道伙伴经济条件基本持平，差异主要体现在激励重点上，例如同一合同捆绑销售Zoom与Contact Center可获得约8%的后端返利。
  3. Zoom Contact Center上线初期并不具备企业级能力，有意以100-500席位的中端市场为切入点，约1.5-2年后才达到企业级水平；联络中心销售周期为12-18个月，专家预计Zoom将从Genesys、Nice、Five9、Cisco手中夺取更多高端份额。
  4. 公司投资者日称，Contact Center前100大订单中90%来自平台新客户；联络中心不能作为Meetings或Phone客户的简单附加销售，但可反向带动Phone和Meetings的整体迁移。
  5. Zoom Phone高度商品化、价格起主导作用，而联络中心是关键任务系统，更看重价值与可靠性；切换联络中心平台需要感知价值达到风险的约四倍。
  6. 公司称Zoom Phone全球用户超过1000万，在企业客户中的附加率约29%，Phone ARR增速约为十几个百分点，但专家认为容易获取的客户已被拿下，后续销售需要更有针对性。
  7. 整个行业都难以将AI产品变现，企业客户不愿基于“承诺”全面押注；销售必须从推销功能转向推销业务成果，CEO Eric Yuan也承认渠道伙伴觉得AI Receptionist等产品难卖。
  8. 在线渠道2027财年第二季度仅增长0.6%（3月提价约6%），专家认为其结构性衰退比公司所说的更深，预计收入会绝对下滑而非持平；企业渠道同期增长7.8%，公司目标是2030财年企业业务实现两位数增长、总收入增长6-8%。

Agenda

  • Trends and developments impacting Zoom (NASDAQ: ZM) and the broader unified communications sector
  • Agentic AI and how it is repricing seat-based collaboration software
  • Demand bifurcation between enterprise and online
  • Mid-to-long-term outlook

Key Insights

  • Zoom's channel contribution grew from c23% to just over 40% during the expert's tenure, with Contact Center exceeding 50% channel contribution in its first year alone
  • The mid-market – 100-500 seats – was Zoom Contact Center's deliberate initial sweet spot, as the product wasn't enterprise-grade at launch – full enterprise readiness took roughly 1.5-2 years post-launch
  • Expert believes the online channel's structural decline is deeper than Zoom acknowledges, expecting outright revenue reduction rather than flat growth, as channel and enterprise routes increasingly dominate go-to-market
  • Zoom Phone is described as highly commoditised, with pricing playing a dominant role versus Contact Center, where value and reliability matter far more than cost comparisons between vendors
