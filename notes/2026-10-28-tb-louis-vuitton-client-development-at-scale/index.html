Agenda

  • Louis Vuitton's (LV; EPA: MC) 12-18-month sales outlook and key driving factors
  • Relative brand relevance vs peers, including where it is gaining or losing client share
  • Ability to accelerate engagement with gen Z as younger luxury consumers adopt different spending and brand loyalty behaviours
  • Distribution channels, store economics and margin outlook

Key Insights

  • LV's US momentum is expected to slow in Q4 2026, followed by soft start to 2027 and broadly stable growth thereafter. Dior should remain the stronger portfolio growth driver
  • LV's very important customers strategy remains durable growth lever, with high-end categories still supply-constrained and penetration rising. Broader availability could unlock further spend without materially compromising exclusivity
  • Pricing support fades after May 2027, increasing reliance on genuine volume growth. Further price increases also appear less likely after three increases within c13-14 months
  • Key structural issue is next-generation recruitment. LV retains exceptional brand equity but is lagging peers on product innovation, creative differentiation and cultural relevance, implying a gradual rather than cyclical slowdown
  • Network and operational levers appear largely exhausted. Future like-for-like growth depends primarily on product innovation and a new "It Bag", while weaker revenue growth is likely to increase margin pressure
