Agenda

  • MacLean Power Systems' M&A integration processes
  • September 2026-announced acquisition of Delta Star and further portfolio needs
  • Competition with larger OEMs
  • Sales volume forecasts

Key Insights

  • Post acquisition of Delta Star, MacLean is only missing "structure […] the actual metal structure to put things on in the substation and then they're missing breakers and relays", which could be acquired to become a true one-stop-shop electrical components OEM
  • Utilities don't prefer single sourcing – "It will be a concern, but there's not much they can do about it because it's not like they can go anywhere else other than a few of the big ones. If they get the opportunity to go with a small one, my guess is, they will"
  • Expert believes there will still be integration hurdles with MacLean and PGC. "You'll end up with two different sales teams, one being more on the technical end of selling and understanding"
  • Expert thinks MacLean should target 30-40% EBITDA margins, with 8% sales growth expected sustainably for several years. Where 1% price increases would have incurred fights with utility customers in the past, 5-7% is now the norm
