原标题：Google Search – Searching for Growth in the Age of AI

#internet #llm

Third Bridge · 2026-11-19

核心摘要

  1. 前谷歌搜索增长与策略负责人指出搜索广告业务有三大变化：谷歌从8月17日起强制目标智能出价，由谷歌代替广告主设定出价；查询增长与付费点击增长脱钩；信息类查询点击消失而商业类查询依然强劲。
  2. 出价机制调整后，预算受限的广告系列CPC上涨约16%，而预算不设上限的广告系列CPC下降约14-15%，原本ROAS大幅超额（如目标100%实际400%）的广告主受冲击最大。
  3. 谷歌公布AI Max可带来约14%的CPA改善和约20%的ROAS提升，专家在头部客户测试中也看到明显提升，但转化信号不完整的中小广告主效果可能较差。
  4. AI Mode把原来需要数周的多次搜索压缩为一次对话会话，每次对话的查询数增至15-22次，点击需求减少，谷歌必须通过更丰富的转化数据提高单次点击价值，同时解决消费者信任和广告密度问题。
  5. 付费点击增长约8%，整体CPC上涨约4%；2026年第三季度谷歌搜索收入增长12%，较第二季度的约17%放缓，金融、房贷、保险等垂类需求偏弱。
  6. 专家认为谷歌最大护城河是与广告主深度整合的转化反馈闭环，未来应掌握交易、身份和匹配通道，而非仅靠竞价拍卖变现；AI资格筛选可能冲击潜客线索聚合中介。
  7. ChatGPT目前CPM约60美元，自然转化表现好但付费广告经济性较弱，但其全球招聘和广告主实验预算会推动快速采用，一旦降低价格和接入门槛可能出现曲棍球式增长；Perplexity等缺乏广告技术栈难以变现。

Agenda

  • Google's (NASDAQ: GOOGL) search query growth, user engagement and sustainability of advertising revenue
  • AI Overviews and AI Mode adoption and their impact on traditional search behaviour
  • AI-powered ad formats, advertiser returns and opportunities for incremental monetisation
  • Competition from ChatGPT, Perplexity and other AI-native search alternatives
  • Impact of zero-click searches on publishers, traffic distribution and Google's long-term competitive position

Key Insights

  • Google's bidding changes limit surplus return on ad spend gains, with budget-limited campaigns experiencing 16% CPC inflation, vs 14-15% declines for uncapped campaigns, rewarding advertisers with flexible budgets
  • AI Max's c14% cost per action uplift and 20% return on ad spend improvement reflect richer contextual targeting, although expert believes incomplete conversion signals could constrain smaller advertisers' results
  • AI-driven qualification could disrupt lead-generation intermediaries by shifting customer discovery towards personal agents, potentially benefiting advertisers if Google executes successfully
  • AI Mode compresses weeks of searches into conversational sessions, reducing required clicks. Expert believes Google must increase click value through richer conversion data while addressing consumer trust and ad density
  • Google's integrated conversion data provides a substantial competitive moat, although expert expects ChatGPT adoption to accelerate despite currently weak paid advertising economics, contingent on improved pricing and onboarding
