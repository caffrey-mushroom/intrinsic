核心摘要

  1. 专家认为公众反对已成为数据中心开发的主要因素，特别是靠近住宅的大型项目。
  2. 据报道各地有44至46项限制数据中心的地方投票措施获得通过，专家提到的地方暂停令约300个。
  3. 专家认为不确定性比清晰但严格的规则更伤害项目，暂停令应被用来制定标准而不是无限期延长。

  5. 电力和输电与选址审批同样重要，并网排队、设备积压和管道接入都可能约束开发。
  6. Loudoun县正从例行审批转向公开审查和新标准，夏延在制定噪音、冷却、退线和备用发电机规则，得州州级机构作用较大。
  7. 新墨西哥Project Jupiter约700至1,400英亩，专家预计最终会谈出方案，但会延期且规模和设计不确定；小型和边缘数据中心受到的反对较少。

SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, a planning and economic-development consultant, discussed how communities and governments are responding to the growth of data centers. The central challenge is balancing investment and jobs with local concerns about electricity supply, water use, noise, emissions, transmission infrastructure, and effects on nearby residents.

The expert expects regulation to become more detailed and permitting more demanding. Developers may need to secure power, provide fuller project details, and negotiate community benefits before seeking local approval. In return, clear standards could make outcomes more predictable. Rules and timelines will vary significantly by state and locality.

Key Insights

Public opposition has become a major factor in data center development, particularly when large facilities are planned near homes. The expert believes uncertainty is more damaging to projects than clear, demanding rules. Moratoria should be used to develop standards, rather than remain open-ended. Projects are likely to require more preparation and community engagement. Approvals may come later in project development, shifting risk rather than necessarily extending the overall schedule. Power availability and transmission are as important as site approval. Grid connection queues, equipment backlogs, and pipeline access may constrain development. The expert expects a negotiated path forward for the New Mexico project discussed, but anticipates delays and uncertainty about its eventual scale and design.
