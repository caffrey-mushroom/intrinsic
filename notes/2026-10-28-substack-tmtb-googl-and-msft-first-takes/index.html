The crowded long beat and the crowded short missed…

GOOGL -7%: Just an overall bad print on the 3 KPIs that mattered most: search, GCP & EBIT. Search missed at +12% vs. Street’s 14.7% and the ~13-14% buyside bar, Cloud ($30.7B) beat Street but fell short of $31-32.5B bogeys, and EBIT missed by ~$2.8B. YT miss. Cloud EBIT beat the one bright spot.

Stock was shorted coming in, but this is worse than expected - Sundar has an uphill battle on the call to explain this away…

Search & other came in at $63.35B, +12.0%, vs. Street $64.87B (+14.7%) and bogeys around ~$64.5B (~14%). That is a 4.8pt decel from 2Q’s +16.8% on a 2.8pt tougher comp, and the 2-yr stack slowed to 26.5% from 28.5% in 2Q and 28.9% in 1Q, so it decelerated on a stacked basis too. Comps continue to get tougher next few quarters and bears will think that single digit search growth might be in the cards.... Mgmt said query growth at a five-year high.

Total ad revenue $82.0B vs. Street $83.5B. YouTube ads $11.21B, +9.2%, vs. Street $11.45B and ~$11.5B bogey (2Q: +12.9%).

Cloud the other bummer: $30.71B, +103%, vs. Street $29.18B (+92%) and bogeys of $31-32.5B (+105-115%). That is a +$5.9B q/q add vs. +$4.7B in 2Q and Street’s +$4.4B. The release calls TPU system sales “a meaningful contributor to revenue growth” but gives no ex-TPU growth number. Cloud OM was 36.2% ($11.12B OI), ~250bps above Street’s 33.7% and ~60bps above 2Q’s 35.6%, even with the third-party-capacity “modest margin pressure” mgmt had flagged.

EBIT $40.58B / 31.8% OM vs. Street $43.40B / 33.9% (~210bps below) and the ~$45B / ~34.5% bogey (~270bps below). The miss sits outside Cloud. Services OI $39.16B / 40.7% OM was ~80bps below Street’s 41.5% (2Q: 41.8%). Alphabet-level costs (mostly shared AI R&D) were $7.66B vs. ~$5.7B implied by Street and $5.8B in 2Q, and Other Bets lost $2.05B vs. Street -$1.60B. GAAP EPS of $2.12 vs. Street $2.94 includes an $8.7B equity-securities loss ($0.55/sh per the company), so EPS ex the loss is ~$2.67, still below Street.

3Q capex $51.4B vs. Street $56.1B (+114% y/y). The release has no capex update. If the $195-205B FY26 range holds, 4Q capex is implied at ~$63-73B vs. Street $65.7B. Nothing on 2027 either; remember bogeys around ~$350-375B vs. Street ~$312B. FCF was -$9.1B vs. Street -$4.1B. In 3Q they issued $12.4B of stock under the ATM ($27.6B still available) plus $28.7B of senior notes.

MSFT +4%: Great print with Azure 48% cc clearing the ~47% buyside bar (44.9% Street) on a +$3.75B q/q add, M365 commercial cloud 18% cc (19% adjusted) at the top of bogeys, and revenue and OM came in above the guide. No Copilot seat count in the release. All eyes on Azure guide on the call where bar for the guide is inline with the print of 48% (~5:45pm ET).

Azure grew 48% cc (48% reported) to $33.2B vs. the 44-45% recast guide, 44.9% Street and the ~47% buyside bogey. That’s a +$3.75B q/q add vs. +$3.4B in June, and a 6pt accel from June’s 42%. 2-yr stack of 87 (48 + 39) vs. ~81 in June. Microsoft Cloud did $63.9B, +30% (31% cc), on the new definition. Commercial RPO of $677B vs. $668.6B Street, roughly flat q/q vs. $678B in June when Street had it down ~$9B.

M365 commercial cloud (now incl. GitHub and Security Copilot) grew 18% cc unadjusted (17% reported) vs. the ~17% guide, 16.7% Street and ~18% bogey. On the adjusted basis it was 19% cc vs. the ~18% adjusted guide. That’s a 3pt accel vs. a 15% cc comp. No Copilot paid seat number in the release (30M+ in June, high-30Ms bogey), so expect that question on the call.

Revenue of $92.3B (+19%) vs. $90.64B Street, the $89.85-90.95B guide and ~$92B bogeys, so ~$1.3B above the high end of the guide. Agents and Infra did $76.8B (+25%) vs. the $75.15-75.75B guide, an accel from +23% in June. Devices and Consumer did $15.5B (-3%) vs. the $14.7-15.2B guide, helped by Search ex-TAC +12% vs. the mid-to-high single digit guide.

OM of 49.8% vs. 48.5% Street (~120bps above) and the “relatively flat” y/y guide (48.9% last year, so +~90bps y/y). GM 67.8% was ~60bps above Street, and opex of $16.6B came in below the $16.8-16.9B guide.

EPS of $4.89 (ex-OpenAI) vs. $4.73 Street; $4.92 GAAP.

On funding (the META contrast): operating cash flow of $58.0B less $39.4B of cash PP&E is ~$18.6B of FCF for the quarter. Pretty clean print overall.
