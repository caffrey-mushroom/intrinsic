原标题：Lonza – Pricing Power & Fill-finish Demand

#pharma #healthcare

Third Bridge · 2026-11-19

核心摘要

  1. 前诺和诺德工厂采购负责人认为无菌灌装产能最紧张的环节在于产线可用时间和换线时间，受GLP-1需求拉动，未来几年仍将紧张，可能要到本十年末才会缓解。
  2. 新建无菌灌装工厂从动工到验证上线幸运的话也需要5-7年，因此大药企自建产能不会给CDMO造成需求断崖，商业化产能预订的槽位提前期一般为3-6个月。
  3. 口服GLP-1药物的兴起使部分需求从注射剂转移，但由于口服制剂需要更多原料药，瓶颈正转向原料药生产，对灌装端影响不大。
  4. 采购评估中质量记录（483次数、警告信、偏差和CAPA关闭情况）是首要且往往不可妥协的标准，其次是技术能力，再次是产能和排期。
  5. 专家估计Lonza在批次价格和项目管理方面拥有约10-15%的溢价，原因是其质量体系可减少高昂的批次失败成本，技术转移能力也优于Catalent和赛默飞，且这种溢价预计可以维持。
  6. 商业化产品更换CDMO通常需要18-24个月；年产6批难以支撑第二供应商，30批左右才值得双源。
  7. 当产线利用率为60-80%时CDMO对放宽照付不议条款较为灵活，而利用率降至30-40%时为恢复利润则很少让步。
  8. 诺和控股收购Catalent后部分客户因知识产权顾虑迁出；对于Lonza收购默克KGaA美国CDMO业务，专家认为承接的客户合同带有价格保护条款，在到期前恢复利润率的可能性较低。

Agenda

  • CDMO (contract development and manufacturing organisation) market trends, highlighting the impacts of capacity scarcity in sterile fill-finish and nearshoring demand
  • Comparative assessment of Lonza (SWX: LONN) vs peers such as Catalent and Thermo Fisher Scientific (NYSE: TMO) on pricing premiums, RFP (request for proposal) scoring criteria and quality standards
  • Operational friction and lead times during late-stage clinical drug product onboarding
  • Customer switching barriers and lock-in, contract stickiness and corporate governance impacts to account retention rates

Key Insights

  • Expert thinks sterile fill-finish capacity will remain constrained over a relatively lengthy, multi-year timeframe – bringing new facilities through regulatory validation requires roughly 5-7 years
  • Expert assessment indicates that Lonza commands an approximate 10-15% pricing premium in batch rates and programme management, justified by strong quality systems and processes that prevent costly batch failures
  • Market shifts towards non-injectable oral drugs are causing upstream supply constraints because oral formulations require more active ingredients than sterile fill-finish, shifting bottlenecks to drug substance manufacturing
  • Commercial CDMOs are likely far less amenable to waiving take-or-pay minimums when line utilisation drops to c30-40% to recover margins, whereas c60-80% utilisation tends to yield greater flexibility
  • Expert cautions that recovering margins at acquired carve-out facilities faces low near-term probability because inherited customer agreements contain existing price protections that remain enforceable until contract expiration
