  4. 西弗吉尼亚的短板是平坦地块稀缺，以及除东部狭长地带外难以提供到北弗吉尼亚的低延迟，因此更适合训练类负载；州政府已与西弗吉尼亚大学建立含40–50项指标的选址门户。

  6. 弗吉尼亚新州长通过行政令取消对数据中心的快速许可服务、拟废除按权利建设并禁止保密协议，还对表后燃气发电设限；该州55%的电力来自燃气，而《清洁经济法》要求在2045/2050年前淘汰燃气发电。
  7. 弗吉尼亚已对数据中心开征能源消费税，销售税豁免（原定至2032年）面临威胁，而服务器约每8年更换一次；劳登县约40%的房产税来自数据中心，专家认为全州暂停令难以通过。
  8. Dominion的GS5大负荷电价适用于超过100MW的用户，采用照付不议机制，资金雄厚的超大云厂商可以接受，但对投机性开发商风险很大；亚马逊已撤出一个弗吉尼亚大型项目，长期可能出现搁浅资产。

SUMMARY

Overview

The discussion examined how energy supply, utility regulation, permitting, and public policy affect data center development in West Virginia and Virginia. The expert, an energy and infrastructure adviser with government experience, described West Virginia as actively courting data centers and Virginia as facing greater regulatory and political uncertainty.

West Virginia aims to expand energy production using gas, coal, and nuclear power, while attracting data centers to support jobs and local tax revenues. Virginia remains an important data center market, particularly for low-latency and clustered operations, but new policy proposals could slow future development.

Key Insights

The expert characterized West Virginia as one of the most accommodating states for data center development, citing supportive state leadership, available energy and water, and flexible power arrangements. West Virginia’s power abundance, rather than water supply, is a major advantage. Suitable flat sites and proximity to Northern Virginia for latency-sensitive workloads remain constraints. The expert expects increased developer interest in West Virginia as policy uncertainty grows in other states, while noting that the pace and location of projects will depend on site and power requirements. Virginia can still attract projects, but the expert expects slower growth. Existing projects may proceed, while future investment could shift to more predictable markets. The expert identified changing incentives and rules as a broader risk to data center economics. In Virginia, potential stranded assets could emerge if operating costs or incentives weaken, though the expert noted this outcome is uncertain.
