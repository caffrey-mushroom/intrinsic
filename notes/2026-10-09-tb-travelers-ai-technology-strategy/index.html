Agenda

  • The Travelers Companies' (NYSE: TRV) ability to leverage AI across the firm
  • AI improving underwriting and claims
  • Premium growth and AI-enhanced capabilities
  • Outlook for Q4 2026 and beyond, highlighting soft market implications

Key Insights

  • AI will differentiate four areas – risk selection, underwriting speed, distribution and expense efficiency. Travelers will be able to identify missed opportunities, have more personalised distribution to clients and give back time to underwriters and adjusters by removing administrative work, which will lead to top-line growth
  • Expert sees retention being stronger because of AI and even improving retention in a hard market. AI is increasingly commoditised and a hybrid large language model strategy is emerging where proprietary models handle privacy-sensitive tasks such as document summarisation, while frontier models will handle complex multi-step reasoning requiring deeper intelligence
  • "The moment […] these big frontier tech are able to attract the smaller insurtech companies, the Lemonade and other companies like that, you will see that slowly the bigger players will come on board where they expose their quotes also through an MCP connection." However, this will take time and is dependent on regulations
