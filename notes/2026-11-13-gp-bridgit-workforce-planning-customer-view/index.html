  7. 公司享受早期客户优惠价；如果价格大幅上涨，或者扩张业务导致系统可靠性下降，公司会考虑替换，而且更可能用内部开发团队借助AI编码自建工具，而不是改买其他平台，因为自建成本在快速下降。

SUMMARY

Overview

A Texas-based commercial general contractor uses Bridget to plan salaried staff across regional and specialized business units. The expert, who oversees operational support functions, described the company’s shift from spreadsheets to a centralized resource- planning system as its workforce and operations expanded.

The firm operates across five Texas regions and has traveling units for data-center and advanced-manufacturing projects. Bridget helps coordinate staffing across the business, including sharing resources between units.

Key Insights

Bridget is viewed as a leading platform for long-term salaried resource planning in construction. The expert said alternatives were more focused on short-term scheduling or hourly crews. Ease of use was central to the selection. The expert considered Primavera P6 too cumbersome and LaborChart less suited to the company’s salaried workforce needs. The expert reported substantial qualitative improvement over Excel, particularly in identifying double-bookings, unassigned employees, staffing gaps, and project coverage risks. No quantified impact was available. The company adopted Bridget before the product was fully built, based on confidence in the team and a chance to influence development. The expert characterized this as a risk that happened to pay off. The expert rated both product performance and customer support 9 out of 10, citing stability, responsiveness, and follow- through. AI features are a key area of interest. The company hopes Bridget can provide resource-planning expertise through integrations with its internal AI tools and broader employee data. Major price increases or reduced reliability could prompt reconsideration. The company would likely build an internal alternative rather than buy another platform.
