Agenda

  • Trends and developments impacting Rackspace Technology (NASDAQ: RXT) and the broader managed cloud services industry
  • Demand impacts and expectations as AI workloads shift towards inference
  • Margin expectations across private and public cloud segments
  • Mid-to-long-term outlook

Key Insights

  • Expert reiterated that the 2021 bifurcation into Public and Private Cloud segments damaged customer relationships. Sales representatives prioritised retention over cross-selling, prompting customers to churn entirely rather than migrate within Rackspace
  • Rackspace's Public Cloud revenue is heavily skewed towards infrastructure resale. Expert posits that resale accounts for 85%-plus of segment revenue, with Amazon alone comprising 91-92% of the resell business
  • The Private Cloud outlook remains uncertain. Expert hesitated to qualify Rackspace as having neocloud aspirations despite AMD partnerships and a target of 30MW GPU capacity by 2028, which carries projected revenue of USD 15m-20m per megawatt and 50%-plus EBITDA margins
  • Expert anticipates Rackspace's USD 2.53bn debt maturity in 2028 to be restructured rather than resolved through organic repayment, though sees minimal refinancing risk.
  • The departure of DK Sinha to Accenture Edge as CEO, combined with executive leadership shrinking from nine to roughly four members, signals organisational instability ahead of a critical debt maturity window
