SUMMARY

Overview

The discussion assessed third-quarter 2026 conditions and the fourth-quarter outlook for connector manufacturers and distributors, particularly Amphenol and TE Connectivity. Demand is accelerating across Europe and North America, supported by data centers, AI infrastructure, defense, aerospace and recovering industrial automation.

The expert characterized current growth as more sustainable than the post-pandemic surge because it is driven by genuine design activity and end demand rather than panic buying or duplicate orders. Data-center investment is also benefiting adjacent markets such as power, cooling, storage, fiber, surge protection and thermal management.

Key Insights

European connector distribution performed better than expected in the third quarter, with unusually limited seasonal weakness in August and exceptionally strong September results. North America was described as the strongest regional market, particularly for data-center connectivity. Customer counts, order volumes, SKU breadth and development pipelines are all increasing, indicating healthy design activity. The expert sees no meaningful evidence of double or triple ordering, supporting the view that current demand is organic. Data-center momentum should remain strong over the next several quarters, although visibility becomes less certain later next year. Military drone and counter-drone applications are considered a major structural opportunity. Defense demand is expected to continue improving as governments increase spending. Rising material, freight, fuel, energy and labor costs should support further price increases over the next one or two quarters. Lead times are extending modestly in selected connectors, backplanes, passives and data-center products, but remain manageable.
