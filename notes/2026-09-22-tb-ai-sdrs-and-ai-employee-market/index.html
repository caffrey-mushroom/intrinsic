Agenda

  • Key trends in AI SDR (sales development representative) and the broader AI employee market
  • Growth outlook
  • Competitive dynamics

Key Insights

  • AI SDR market has shifted from verticalised point solutions towards horizontal agent platforms, where one system handles multiple roles – SDR, marketer and researcher – simultaneously
  • Trust in fully autonomous agents has grown dramatically, with companies now skipping copilot review stages entirely and simply launching agents to "see what happens". "When it genuinely gets to the point where it's indistinguishable between hiring a human SDR and an AI SDR […] things are just going to go absolutely insane in terms of growth"
  • Artisan's "don't hire humans" rage-marketing generated massive brand awareness but simultaneously created significant brand hostility, illustrating double-edged nature of controversial positioning in the space
  • Horizontal platforms already hold c50% of SDR-related market, with some individual horizontal players generating more revenue from growth use cases than most vertical AI SDR companies combined
  • M&A in the AI SDR space will likely come from CRM and sales incumbents such as Salesforce or HubSpot acquiring vertical players, not from horizontal competitors absorbing them
