Agenda

  • CoreWeave's (NASDAQ: CRWV) positioning within AI cloud infrastructure as workloads shift from training towards production inference
  • CKS (CoreWeave Kubernetes Service), inference software and enterprise readiness, focusing on orchestration and production deployment requirements
  • GPU capacity and competitive positioning as hyperscalers expand AI infrastructure
  • Medium-to-long-term outlook, including financing requirements and returns on infrastructure investment

Key Insights

  • Expert believes CoreWeave's expansion to 8GW is unrealistic, suggesting that capacity may reach 2-3GW as capital constraints, permitting delays and energy availability limit growth
  • Effective project financing costs are estimated at 8.5-10% vs 15-16% yields on certain bonds, with previously financed projects less affected by elevated borrowing costs than future expansion
  • Inference is estimated to account for over 50% of CoreWeave demand, while a hypothetical 20% decline in token costs could drive 50-100% broader market growth
  • In an illustrative scenario, Vera Rubin GPUs could rent for USD 0.50-1 per hour after five years vs an assumed USD 8 initially, highlighting limited residual revenue potential
  • Enterprise adoption of neoclouds could remain limited for at least two years. Expert cites gaps in availability zones, security, governance and CPU infrastructure
