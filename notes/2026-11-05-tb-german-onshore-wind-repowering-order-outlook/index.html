2026.11.05 | TB | Energy | German Onshore Wind Repowering Order Outlook

原标题：German Onshore Wind Turbines – Will Repowering Order Intake Power On?

#energy #industrials

Third Bridge · 2026-11-05

核心摘要

  1. Enercon业务发展总监表示，德国陆上风电招标规模正以每年14至15GW的节奏运行，已中标项目实现率超过99%，未来3至5年订单高度可预测。
  2. 专家认为当前招标量已见顶，但每年10至12GW在未来五年可持续，德国2030年115GW陆上风电目标基本有保障。
  3. 德国陆上风电装机容量在2026年中约为70GW，过去一年净新增约4至5GW，目前已许可项目约45至50GW、另有约50GW在许可流程中。
  4. 2027年将是关键压力测试年，若实际装机达到7至8GW，则2028至2029年达到每年10GW具有可信度。
  5. 超大叶片、专用起重机和驳船等重型运输物流被视为实现10GW目标的最大瓶颈，而许可已不再是瓶颈。
  6. 重新开发项目可用三分之一的机组产生2至3倍发电量，专家认为OEM宁可自我蚕食售后业务也不愿将整机和服务合同同时输给对手，约90%的服务合同掌握在OEM手中。
  7. 专家预计西门子歌美飒2027年德国份额低于5%，到2028年回升至约10%至15%，而中国OEM因超出218W/平方米门槛、缺乏本地服务网络及韧性招标限制，3至5年内难以进入。
  8. 2027年EEG改革中的限电补偿规则被视为最具破坏性的因素，可能影响全德项目经济性，若成本增加将通过更高招标价格传导。

Analyst-led • 27 Oct 2026

Companies: VWS DK, NDX1 DE

Ricardo Barreto (RB) Director, Business Development at Enercon Global GmbH

Louis Knight (LK) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Drivers of 115GW onshore capacity by 2030 in Germany
  • Sustainability of order intake vs short-term windfall
  • Platform competitiveness of Vestas (CPH: VWS) vs Nordex (ETR: NDX1) vs Siemens Gamesa (ETR: ENR)
  • Outlook for per-megawatt installation pricing

Key Insights

  • Germany's onshore wind auction system is running at 14-15GW per year, with contracted projects showing a >99% realisation rate, making the next 3-5 years highly predictable for OEMs
  • 2027 will be the critical acid-test year – if Germany successfully installs 7-8GW, scaling to 10GW by 2028-29 becomes credible. Freight and heavy transport logistics – oversized blades, specialist cranes and dedicated barges – are single biggest bottleneck to reaching 10GW
  • Siemens Gamesa could recapture <5% German market share by 2027, rising to c10-15% through 2028, as developers currently rely on three established suppliers
  • China-based OEM turbines exceed Germany's 218W per square metre threshold, lack local service networks and face potential resilience auctions with legislation favouring western manufacturers through 2029
  • Renewable Energy Sources Act's 2027 curtailment compensation rules are the most destabilising element, potentially undermining project economics across Germany, not just grid-constrained regions
