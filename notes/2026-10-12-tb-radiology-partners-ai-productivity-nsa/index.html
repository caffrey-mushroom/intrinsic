Agenda

  • AI implications for Radiology Partners' productivity growth and capacity expansion, plus implications for revenue growth
  • Subsidy growth dynamics and ability to preserve the same contract margins
  • Sizing EBITDA contribution of NSA (No Surprises Act), discussing potential changes and impact
  • Mosaic offering, outlook for productivity improvements and external sales

Key Insights

  • There remains room to run on subsidy growth, with Radiology Partners constructing asks in a collaborative fashion and inviting comparison to fair market value
  • Expert believes Radiology Partners has seen 10-15% productivity gains from internal MosaicOS deployment, translating to an incremental 8-10 RVUs per day. Hypothetically, a practice of 40 rads with c13 reading in the day could therefore reduce staffing need by one rad, creating the opportunity to work through backlog or unlock new growth depending on practice dynamics
  • Expert sees 30% productivity uplift as plausible in five years as MosaicOS begins to read more complex scans such as CTs and MRIs. Radiology Partners is the leader of the pack on AI deployment
  • Teleradiology likely makes up >50% of RVU output for Radiology Partners, with further growth here a lever going forwards
  • Despite pockets of resistance to PE among radiologists, Radiology Partners has a strong recruitment position, with Mosaic offering a secondary benefit here
