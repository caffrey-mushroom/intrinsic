原标题：Google TPU – Scaling Beyond the Cloud

#asic #cloud #datacenter

Third Bridge · 2026-11-12

核心摘要

  1. 前Google高级工程师预计，到2028年Google对外的TPU系统销售年收入约750亿美元，区间为450亿至1200亿美元；近期硬件营业利润率约30%，低于云租赁的约36%，但资本回报更快。
  2. 他预计到2028年，前沿实验室的算力支出结构约为：预训练15-30%、后训练（含RL）40-50%、推理40-45%；Anthropic的S-1显示2026年上半年在90亿美元算力中，服务成本约24亿美元，占比约27%。
  3. TPU 8i（偏推理和后训练、内存更大、Boardfly拓扑）预计将占新一代TPU需求的约60-70%，8t（torus拓扑）更偏向预训练这一小众需求。
  4. 相比Ironwood，TPU 8t的训练成本预计可降低20-40%，实际性价比提升在稠密模型上约1.7-2倍、MoE模型上约1.5-1.8倍，低于官方宣称的2.7倍；8i推理加速器成本预计比Ironwood低约25-33%。
  5. 按Ironwood每芯片小时约1.21美元、GB300每GPU小时约2.31美元计算，TPU相对Blackwell Ultra有约25-35%的成本优势，但面对Vera Rubin时优势收窄至0-15%；Ironwood的HBM带宽是Trainium 3的1.5倍，扩展规模为1152颗芯片对144颗。
  6. 大买家自建TPU系统需要约60-75%的有效利用率才划算：对比5.40美元的标价约20%即可回本，对比2美元的Google Cloud价格约需60%，对比Anthropic约1.60美元的价格约需73%。
  7. TorchTPU开源后，标准PyTorch工作负载迁移只需几周，已有生产模型约需一个季度，深度定制的CUDA栈约需六个月；vLLM在TPU上已覆盖约98%的Hugging Face模型，其中约97%的数值与GPU一致。
  8. Oracle从2027年起通过OCI提供TPU，到2027年底规模约0.1-0.4GW，其中约30-50%对Google是净新增需求；Google训练goodput目标为97%，Broadcom的TPU合作协议延续至2031年。

Agenda

  • Google's (NASDAQ: GOOGL) TPU (Tensor Processing Unit) positioning against Nvidia (NASDAQ: NVDA) and AWS Trainium (NASDAQ: AMZN) across AI training and inference workloads
  • TPU 8t and 8i architectures, performance-per-dollar advantages and implications for next-generation AI compute economics
  • External TPU commercialisation, highlighting Anthropic's expanding deployments, Oracle's cloud partnership and software ecosystem development
  • Medium-to-long-term outlook, highlighting fleet-level scalability and infrastructure requirements

Key Insights

  • Google's external TPU system sales could reach cUSD 75bn annually by 2028 within a USD 45bn-120bn range, while near-term hardware operating margins could approach 30%
  • By 2028, post-training could represent 40-50% of frontier-lab compute spending, supporting TPU 8i capturing c60-70% of next-generation TPU demand
  • TPU 8t could deliver 20-40% lower training costs vs Ironwood, while Google's estimated cost advantage narrows from 25-30% against Blackwell Ultra to 0-15% vs Vera Rubin
  • Large TPU buyers may need 60-75% productive utilisation to justify system ownership, with break-even economics heavily dependent on cloud rental pricing
  • TorchTPU migration could take a few weeks for standard PyTorch workloads, roughly one quarter for established production models and six months for heavily customised Compute Unified Device Architecture stacks
