Agenda

  • ICICI Bank's (NSE: ICICIBANK) strategy and growth outlook across retail, business banking and rural advances, plus the unsecured vs secured mix
  • Lending economics and asset quality trends
  • Retail liabilities and customer franchise, including CASA (current account and savings account) growth, customer acquisition and cross-sell

Key Insights

  • ICICI Bank's advantage lies in cross-selling ability. As balance sheet scaling and deposit mobilisation constrain expansion, expert notes overall loan growth to normalise "in the range of anywhere between 17% to 19% growth in FY27, probably moderating towards the mid-to-high teens thereafter"
  • Business banking's 28-30% YoY growth in Q1 FY27 is 60-70% productivity-driven, not branch expansion, as digital transaction data enables deeper wallet share without proportionate physical network additions
  • Expert notes Q1 FY27 credit-cost was low due to one-time National Asset Reconstruction Company Limited asset recovery and expects gross non-performing assets to stabilise in 1.3-1.5% in FY27. Credit costs will normalise to 45-55bps as expansion in business banking and rural advances seasons over coming quarters
  • Expert expects ICICI Bank's fee income growth to settle at 15-18% in FY27, driven by business banking and rural products. Downside risks remain if corporate transaction activity slows down or competition from smaller banks intensifies
