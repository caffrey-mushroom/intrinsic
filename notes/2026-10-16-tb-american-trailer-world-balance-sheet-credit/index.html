Agenda

  • ATW (American Trailer World) – cash flow expectations and key drivers or compressors
  • Competitive landscape and market share dynamics as overcapacity persists
  • Truck equipment business line potential growth opportunities
  • Best-, worst- and base-case margin structure outlook

Key Insights

  • Expert believes ATW's debt restructuring is inevitable, with no realistic pathway to navigate its 2028 maturities, given its current and expected earnings trajectory. Primary mitigating factors are lost market share during pandemic, strong competitive pressure, high input costs, weak pricing due to oversupply and best-case expectations for 1-3% trailer volume growth in 2027
  • Expert believes ATW's truck body business could be sold off to pay down debt or used as growth engine to anchor restructured debt, with expectations that the business could grow by 10% in 2027-28 and is contributing c50%, or USD 50m, of ATW's EBITDA in current environment
  • Expert believes leverage is ATW's primary issue. Absent the debt burden, the company's business model retains attractive market-leading share, brands and production capacity
  • ATW will look to improve its cost structure, but there are at most three facilities remaining that could be closed
