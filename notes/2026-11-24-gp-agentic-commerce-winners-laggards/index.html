SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, who leads a company that helps brands improve product visibility in AI search, discussed how AI assistants are changing online discovery and commerce. Unlike traditional search, which often directs shoppers to broad category pages, AI tools increasingly match specific customer needs with individual products.

The expert believes brands must improve product information, customer experience, and their ability to respond to changing demand. Smaller online retailers may be better positioned to adapt, while larger companies often face complex technology and operational hurdles.

Key Insights

The shift is only beginning. AI discovery is expanding through both new assistants and Google’s AI features, potentially putting traditional search traffic at risk. Product pages need detailed, accurate context, such as technical specifications, intended uses, limitations, and answers to likely questions. Generic copy or unsupported claims may reduce recommendations or lead to poor-fit purchases. Smaller brands can benefit from simpler technology stacks and faster adaptation. The expert said Shopify-based stores are often more accessible to AI systems than some enterprise websites. Brand reputation still matters. AI systems draw on reviews and other external sources, which can expose weak service or inaccurate product claims. The expert argued that large advertising budgets cannot compensate for poor product data or customer experience. The moderator repeatedly asked about the implications for established companies and the expert’s recommended response.
