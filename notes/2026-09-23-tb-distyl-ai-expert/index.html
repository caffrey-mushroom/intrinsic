Can you describe what Distyl AI does and its products and services?

Expert: Distyl is an AI services company that also brings a technology platform called the Distillery to market to streamline really complex workflows for Fortune 500 companies. That was the stated purpose of the company [in the past].

What is the value proposition of Distyl AI's products or services? What problem is the company solving for its customers?

Expert: It's not really a product. It takes some of the most meaningful workflows within a company and it does two things. One, see if it's actually suitable for AI to improve or two, if that entire process can be re-imagined in a new way using AI and then it goes and tackles that. Sometimes it does both, where it takes a really complex process like, let's say, claims processing at an insurance company and makes the existing process faster. That creates value for the customer or two, if a company is truly innovative, they might say, "I don't want to just make my current process faster. I want you to, from first principles, re-imagine what claims processing could look like in the age of AI and agents" and that's what it does.

What might be a use case for the platform? How are the platform and FDE used by the customers? What is its purpose and what are the features available on the platform?

Expert: These are general-purpose agentic and AI platforms that can take any complex workflow in a business and modernise, automate and make it quicker, faster, better and more accurate, sometimes to remove human error, etc. Imagine your process within a company that today employs 1,000 people to, let's say, answer phone calls, customer care calls or do claims processing. AI can go in there, take the current existing standard operating procedures or documents and convert them into agentic workflows where agents can now automate 70%, 80%, 90% of the human effort and only kick out the 10% that needs human intervention. Thereby, you are able to reduce this 1,000-person team to a much smaller footprint and redeploy these people to do other important things or other more valuable things. Those are the use cases all these AI services companies aspire to solve, including Distyl. Analyst: Could you elaborate on the forward-deployed team and what is its purpose?

Expert: These technology platforms that these young companies build are fairly sophisticated and the customers' own technology team might not have the skill set to just take a piece of technology and implement their own solutions, so the job of this team, think of them as a cross between a traditional consultant and an engineer that will go use this platform, provided context, business context, meaning the knowledge, the guardrails, the tests, the evaluations to actually convert the customers' workflow into an automated process or an agentic process. Test it, evaluate it, make sure it doesn't suffer from model drift, etc. That's what the forward-deployed teams do. Sometimes they solve really hard edge cases. That's why these forward-deployed teams might occasionally have AI researchers as well to solve some hard problems, but they are ultimately responsible for taking an existing process and creating a more modern AI-centric version of that. That's their role.
