SUMMARY

Overview

The discussion examined how Anthropic could capture value from AI-assisted commerce without becoming a full payment processor. The expert expects Anthropic to reduce dependence on Stripe by adopting multiple processors and potentially controlling its own token vault. However, direct processing appears unattractive because of licensing, regulation, liability, settlement, and reconciliation burdens.

Anthropic’s stronger opportunity is to provide the intelligence and computing infrastructure that helps merchants support product discovery, personalization, cart creation, and checkout preparation. Transactions would continue through merchants’ existing processors, while Anthropic earns platform and token revenue and potentially referral and wallet fees.

Key Insights

Anthropic is better positioned as the infrastructure provider for agentic commerce than as a payment processor. A merchant-focused strategy is viewed as more promising than keeping the entire shopping journey inside an AI application. It preserves merchant branding, customer relationships, promotions, loyalty, and checkout control. The likely monetization sequence is token or platform fees, followed by sales referral fees and eventually wallet-based transaction fees. A proprietary wallet could become a major strategic asset by storing payment methods, selecting cards, supporting bank transfers, managing incentives, and directing transaction volume. Acquiring an established wallet could accelerate adoption more effectively than building one from scratch. Fully autonomous purchasing remains constrained by consumer trust, model reliability, fraud, authorization, and unresolved liability. Anthropic may be particularly well positioned in business-to-business payments because of its enterprise orientation.
