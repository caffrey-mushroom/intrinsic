SUMMARY

Overview

The expert operates five specialty running stores selling premium footwear, apparel, and accessories. The discussion covered 3Q26 sales, pricing, brand share, inventory, promotions, product pipelines, and expectations through 2027.

Footwear sales missed the retailer’s flat-growth goal, declining 2.7%. September improved, becoming the first positive footwear month since April, supported by higher store traffic and a major race with increased participation. Lower unit sales were partly offset by premium-product mix and higher average prices.

Key Insights

Premiumization remains powerful: sales above $150 grew, while lower-priced tiers contracted sharply. ASICS has the deepest product range and received the expert’s strongest endorsement. Nike is recovering rapidly from a small base, aided by Vomero and Pegasus products. The expert expects meaningful innovation in 2027. Brooks remains the leading brand, although its first flat-share quarter warrants monitoring. Hoka, New Balance, and On face the greatest near-term challenges. On’s specialty-exclusive Cloudsurfer strategy was viewed as critical to its future participation in specialty running. Inventory is generally healthy, with fewer promotions than the prior-year quarter.
