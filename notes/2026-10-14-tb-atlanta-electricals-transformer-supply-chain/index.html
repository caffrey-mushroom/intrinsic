Agenda

  • Supply chain dynamics and key component bottlenecks
  • Vendor ecosystem and BOM (bill of materials) factors
  • Atlanta Electricals' (NSE: ATLANTAELE) competitive positioning vs CG Power (NSE: CGPOWER), Siemens (NSE: ENRIN) and others

Key Insights

  • Atlanta's BTW acquisition plant, originally designed for 315-500MVA and 765kV reactors by Chinese operators, faces workforce challenges to replace the former Chinese labour force. Expert believes it will take Atlanta 3-5 years to ramp up capacity utilisation
  • Expert notes component standardisation can yield 2-4% cost savings, allowing Atlanta to deliver faster than competitors who take 12-24 months, reducing penalties that contributes to the bottom line
  • While inverter duty transformers opportunities are significant, c90% of Atlanta's volume stems from a single engineering, procurement and construction client, creating commercial risk. Higher overheads price Atlanta out of L3-L6 bidding against specialised smaller players
  • Expert opines Atlanta's INR 180 crore CAPEX for tanks and radiators appears excessive, suggesting a world-class radiator facility with three lines and galvanising should cost only INR 50-60 crore. Radiator and tank contribute 5-8% BOM to 220kV and 400kV units, making efficiency gains difficult amid labour cost and availability
