核心摘要

  1. 一位有22年以上经验的建筑业从业者表示，所在承包商公司在美国多个地区经营，员工约225-250人，年施工额约3亿美元；他约8年前用过Roofr，后来转用Jobber，近期又回到Roofr。
  2. 当年离开是因为Roofr只能做估价，功能的深度和广度都不够；如今它已扩展为覆盖估价、提案、CRM项目档案、材料订购、会计和分析的一站式屋顶业务平台，界面简洁直观。
  3. 与CompanyCam集成后，可以通过照片完成屋顶测量（takeoff），不用爬梯上屋顶，既省时又安全，几小时到半天就能出结果；但照片不清晰会导致测量误差，团队通常要三次核对。
  4. 材料订购模块可以在GAF等品牌和QXO（已收购Beacon）、SRS、Home Depot等渠道之间实时比价下单，专家对此很满意。
  5. 会计模块可以管理应收和DSO，但由于建筑商的进度款支票、保险理赔、ACH等支付方式多样，实际收款常在系统外完成；排班和施工队调度是最大的短板。
  6. AI功能目前只做过演示，可以分析实际的人工与材料成本结构（例如投标时按80/20估算，实际变成70/30），帮助判断劳动生产率下降的原因，并生成客户支出看板；屋顶项目中人工通常占成本的60%-80%。
  7. 专家给产品满意度打8分、客服7分，每天可节省约1-3小时；定价已从按用户数年付改为分档方案，并支持按月订阅；Roofr的直接竞品是JobNimbus，ServiceTitan和Jobber则是覆盖多工种的综合平台。

SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, an experienced construction professional, described using software across estimating, project management, scheduling, customer management, and procurement. Their contractor organization operates across several U.S. regions, employs roughly 225–250 people, and generates about $300 million in annual construction activity.

The expert initially used Roofr for roofing estimates, then switched to broader software before returning after Roofr expanded its capabilities. They now view it as a more complete platform, though some functions remain weaker than specialized alternatives.

Key Insights

Roofr has developed from a primarily estimating-focused product into a broader roofing operations platform, with improved customer and project records, proposals, material ordering, and analytics. Its interface is intuitive, and photo-based roof measurements can reduce manual work and improve worker safety. However, photo quality affects measurement accuracy, so the expert’s team often checks estimates multiple times. Scheduling and crew coordination are the platform’s most significant shortcomings for the expert’s business. Accounting is useful for oversight, but payment collection often happens outside the system. The expert rated overall product satisfaction 8/10 and customer support 7/10. They reported saving roughly 1–3 hours per day, depending on the work involved. Roofr’s AI tools appear promising for reviewing costs, material use, labor productivity, and customer trends, but the expert has not adopted them for regular use.

Competitive Landscape

Platform or provider

Market position and observations

Roofr

Roofing-focused platform with estimating, proposals, CRM, material ordering, and other functions. The expert considers its usability and expanded scope strengths, while noting scheduling, accounting workflows, and measurement accuracy gaps.

JobNimbus

Roofing-oriented competitor viewed as a closer match to Roofr than broad-based platforms.

ServiceTitan

Broader home-services platform that can serve roofing and other trades. Its wider scope may suit companies with varied in-house services, while Roofr is more specialized.

Jobber

Broad-based system previously used by the expert; it can support roofing but is less focused on that trade.

CompanyCam

Photo platform integrated with Roofr to support roof measurement and estimating workflows.

Material distributors and retailers

Roofr allows contractors to compare potential suppliers and place material orders, supporting purchasing flexibility.
