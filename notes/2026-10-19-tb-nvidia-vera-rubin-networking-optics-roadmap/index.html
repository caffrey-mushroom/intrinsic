Agenda

  • Nvidia's (NASDAQ: NVDA) networking opportunity across Vera Rubin and next-generation AI data centre architectures
  • Scale-up and scale-out connectivity across NVLink and Spectrum-X Ethernet
  • 800G-to-1.6T optical interconnect transition across pluggable transceivers and CPO (co-packaged optics)
  • Hyperscaler deployment requirements, bottlenecks and infrastructure scaling

Key Insights

  • Expert expects 400G interconnect devices to command 2-3x 100G pricing, supporting greater networking revenue per connection as Nvidia systems shift towards higher-speed optical links
  • Some Nvidia AI racks can run workloads the same day vs up to six months for piecemeal builds, strengthening the company's competitive position as buyers prioritise deployment speed
  • Across a six-million-transceiver AI fabric, expert estimates one transceiver failure every four hours, making Nvidia-qualified LinkX reliability more valuable than savings from cheaper third-party optics
  • At 400G per second per lane, expert expects active copper reach to shrink to 1.5-2 meters, restricting rack-to-rack applications and favouring optical alternatives
  • Near-package optics could remain viable for another 4-5 years, but expert expects CPO to eventually displace it as growing port-density requirements expose physical limitations
