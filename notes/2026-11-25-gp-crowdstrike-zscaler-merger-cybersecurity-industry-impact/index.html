SUMMARY

Overview

The expert views the proposed CrowdStrike–Zscaler combination as a potentially major shift in cybersecurity. CrowdStrike is strongest in endpoint protection and security operations, while Zscaler specializes in network security and secure access service edge (SASE). The expert sees the businesses as complementary and believes their existing customer and channel relationships could help them sell a broader platform.

The discussion also covers Palo Alto Networks’ platform strategy, possible impacts on other security vendors, deal execution risks, customer preferences, and the growing role of AI in cyberattacks.

Key Insights

The expert considers the combination unusually compelling, arguing that both companies have grown through technical depth in their core markets, unlike Palo Alto Networks’ greater reliance on acquisitions. The companies could combine endpoint, identity, and network signals into a more complete view of enterprise security. Their products may overlap in AI security, but the expert believes their differing approaches could ultimately strengthen the combined offering. Successful integration will depend on aligning engineering and sales teams, connecting their technologies, and sharpening Zscaler’s market messaging. The expert expects margin benefits to take several years and stresses that execution is decisive. The expert expects limited regulatory obstacles, given the number of competitors in cybersecurity. Customers may value vendor consolidation but will still seek alternatives to reduce reliance on a single provider. The expert expects endpoint and identity systems to be more difficult to replace than firewalls. AI-enabled attacks are increasing the importance of response speed. The expert does not see platform breadth alone as a guarantee of better defense; the right tools depend on the threat.
