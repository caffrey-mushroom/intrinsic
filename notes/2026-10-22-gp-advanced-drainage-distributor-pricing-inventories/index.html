SUMMARY

Overview

A regional waterworks distributor discussed demand, pricing, inventory, and competition in drainage pipe and basins. Local activity remains steady to modestly improving, supported by residential, commercial, municipal, maintenance, and new-construction projects. Commercial work has performed better than expected.

Supply conditions have normalized, with short lead times and few backorders. The central competitive issue is ADS facing a lower- priced challenger, Neat Piping, in corrugated double-wall polyethylene drainage pipe.

Key Insights

Market demand is slightly improving and expected to finish modestly ahead of expectations. Projects are generally progressing from bidding to construction without material financing or supply delays. Neat is gaining distributor attention through lower pricing, comparable perceived quality, responsive service, and frequent sales visits. The expert has shifted stocked pipe purchases from ADS to Neat after multiple successful orders. ADS retains a meaningful advantage in customized drain basins through Nyloplast. This broader offering can also help ADS secure associated pipe sales. Contractors generally require substantial savings before switching brands. Minor dollar differences are insufficient, while savings near 10% can influence larger projects. The expert believes Neat could materially erode ADS share locally and potentially reach an even split over several years, although this is a high-conviction opinion based on one regional market. Distributor competition remains relationship-driven, particularly when drainage products are bundled with broader project materials.
