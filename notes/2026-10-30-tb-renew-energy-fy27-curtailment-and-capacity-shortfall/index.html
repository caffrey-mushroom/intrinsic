Agenda

  • ReNew Energy Global's (NASDAQ: RNW) FY27 run rate EBITDA achievability
  • Solar module go-to-market strategy and ALMM (Approved List of Models and Manufacturers)-driven demand
  • Privatisation assessment and asset optimisation approach

Key Insights

  • Curtailment in Rajasthan has cost cINR 2,000 crore of lost revenue over around three months and is expected to remain a challenge for next 4-5 years, prompting ReNew to reduce urgency to secure bids. Indian government might consider providing sector-wide debt servicing support
  • As interstate transmission waiver phases out by 2028, adding INR 0.80-0.90 per kWh to delivered power, expert expects ReNew to diversify into under-penetrated states lagging 43% renewable penetration target, while developing 50-60% of 19GW interstate connectivity backlog to protect bank guarantees
  • Expert believes ReNew will fall 20-30% short of 2.5GW annual capacity addition target, landing closer to 2GW commissioned by end of FY27 due to land and grid delays. However, storage solutions could reduce curtailment pressures
  • Manpower cost escalation for tunnel oxide passivated contact ramp-up could run 15-20% above standard inflation, double typical increments, driven by talent retention bonuses and overseas training requirements in China
