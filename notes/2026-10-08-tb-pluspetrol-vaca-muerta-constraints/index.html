Agenda

  • Pluspetrol's Vaca Muerta asset quality and production durability outlook
  • La Calera development scalability and execution model
  • Infrastructure constraints and realised production economics
  • Pluspetrol's cash flow resilience, flexibility and downside risk

Key Insights

  • La Calera's condensate-rich areas break even below USD 1 per MMBtu vs cUSD 2 for wet gas. 3,000-metre lateral wells can be completed in c15 days, but rig mobilisation from the US takes 4-6 months, making equipment availability and reliability under high temperatures a key constraint to accelerating development
  • Pluspetrol's ownership of high-spec rigs and Weatherford's Argentine frac fleet insulate critical capacity for Bajo del Choique ahead of a tightening services market
  • Peru assets remain a durable cash-flow contributor despite c8% annual liquid declines, with gas production sustained for six-plus years and Block 56 providing additional 8-10 years of export production runway
  • YPF asset swap strengthens Pluspetrol's northern Vaca Muerta position, adding stakes in La Escalonada and Rincón de Ceniza alongside infrastructure and drilling synergies
  • Future execution risks stem from Argentine macroeconomic volatility and election cycles impacting external financing. Expert believes Vaca Muerta subsurface productivity and operational execution are already de-risked
