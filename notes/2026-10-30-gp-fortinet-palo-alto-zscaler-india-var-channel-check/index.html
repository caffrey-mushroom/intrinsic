SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, a cybersecurity solutions provider with long industry experience, described demand across Indian customers in sectors including technology, manufacturing, financial services, education, hospitality, and pharmaceuticals.

After a cautious start to the reporting period, when geopolitical uncertainty delayed larger purchases, customer activity improved and deals resumed. The expert said recent demand was strong across vendors, supported in part by India’s growing data- protection compliance requirements. Hardware delivery delays remain a constraint. Looking ahead, geopolitical risks could again make customers cautious.

Key Insights

Fortinet benefits from a broad range of lower-cost devices suited to businesses with many small locations. Bundling firewalls with networking equipment also helps it win multi-site deals. Palo Alto Networks is particularly competitive in large enterprise transactions and is viewed as stronger in security operations services. Its growth in the expert’s business exceeded expectations, helped by a few large deals. Zscaler is gaining from cloud-based security demand. The expert also observed customers reconsidering Forcepoint cloud deployments because of data-loss prevention issues, creating opportunities for Zscaler. Check Point was described as relatively stagnant, with customers moving to Fortinet or Palo Alto Networks. Leadership turnover may take time to resolve. The expert identified AI security, SASE with data-loss prevention, and identity access management as important areas for customer spending. Palo Alto Networks was seen as well positioned in AI security and identity solutions.

Competitive Landscape

Vendor

Market position and competitive factors

Fortinet

Strong in price-sensitive customers, smaller deployments, and businesses with many sites. Expanding in SASE and operational technology security; hardware availability and slower services growth are concerns.

Palo Alto Networks

Strong in large enterprise deals and security operations. Considered relatively expensive and less suited to small deployments, but gaining interest in AI security and identity solutions.

Zscaler

Benefits from cloud-native positioning and SASE demand. The expert reported opportunities as some customers shift from Forcepoint cloud solutions.

Check Point

Retains legacy firewall customers, including in financial services, and email security is progressing. Growth is constrained by weaker SASE momentum, customer attrition, and leadership changes.
