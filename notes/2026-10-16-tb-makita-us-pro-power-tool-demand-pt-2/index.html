Agenda

  • Makita's (TYO: 6586) US OPE (outdoor power equipment) commercial expansion strategy outlook
  • SKUs and inventory value positioning and strategy outlook
  • Market positioning in the US vs leading competitors, such as TTI's (HKG: 0669) Milwaukee and Stanley Black & Decker's (NYSE: SWK) DeWalt

Key Insights

  • OPE generates 15-20% of Makita North America's total sales, with revenue projected to grow 3-5%-plus annually. Makita currently holds c10% of North American cordless OPE market, on par with Milwaukee and DeWalt, but trailing Ryobi at 30% and Ego at 25%. It's positioned to gain 3-5pps of market share over 2-3 years by targeting prosumers and commercial landscapers
  • Makita's key differentiator is its commercial platform, ConnectX, which delivers all-day runtime of 8-10 hours. While being high-ticket – 2x standard tool prices or batteries >USD 1,000 – it eliminates runtime and logistics bottlenecks preventing crews from ditching gas. Additionally, Makita boasts unmatched SKU breadth and is transitioning from plastic to heavy-duty steel mower decks to justify premium pricing over rivals such as DeWalt. However, upside is constrained by severe salesforce deficit. Makita deploys just 57 dedicated Home Depot representatives vs TTI's 2,600, capping national retail execution despite localised metro wins
