SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, a former luxury retail executive now working in commerce technology, discussed how AI is changing product discovery, brand marketing, and the shopping journey. Consumers increasingly use AI to explore products, compare options, and get personalized advice, while automated purchasing remains less established. The discussion also covered luxury brand experiments, department-store access controls, and potential effects on pricing and distribution.

Key Insights

AI is becoming a common starting point for shopping, but current experiences are mostly recommendation and discovery tools—not complete shopping agents. The biggest gaps are real-time inventory and pricing, secure purchase authorization, retailer-system integration, and post- purchase support such as returns and refunds. Brands should distinguish between allowing AI systems to find and recommend their products and permitting those systems to transact. The expert views discoverability as increasingly important, even for cautious luxury brands. The Prada experience in ChatGPT was seen as a polished digital showroom, but not end-to-end commerce because shoppers were redirected to the brand’s website and could not complete service tasks within the conversation. AI may intensify price comparison and discount pressure. Brands can defend their positioning by making differences in authenticity, service, delivery, and returns clear to AI systems and consumers.
