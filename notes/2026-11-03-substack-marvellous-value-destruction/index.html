1] Deal overview slides.(steelmanning)

There is ZERO integration in ELSFP case.

For uVCSEL NPO/CPO, yes I will admit there is quite a bit of integration and co-design opportunity.

This is generic BS slide. What could be interesting is a co-designed Marvell DSP firmware that works in tandem with OCS control-plane software. One of the big problems with OCS is that switching requires full link retraining/bringup. Thus, the switching speed of the OCS itself is irrelevant.

There exist clever ways to reduce link retraining time or maybe even avoid it altogether. Doubt they will figure this out but lets see.

On the transceiver side, there is a small amount of co-design that can be achieved between EML, DSP, Driver, and TIA. Emphasis on small. Impedance matching and careful package design are always needed. In theory this should be simpler in the combined company but Marvell management is so inept that Innolight will almost certainly out execute them anyway.

Which brings to the obvious question I feel is not highlighted well enough. There is no mention of Lumentum’s transceiver business (Cloudlight) getting spun out. What the fuck are Innolight, Eopto, Accelink, AAOI, and friends thinking about this deal. Anyone here think they are happy seeing Marvell compete with their own customers? All of them are going to move business to Broadcom/Credo/Semtech/Macom. Coherent was always in an interesting position of fragile neutrality but this is probably a bridge too far.
