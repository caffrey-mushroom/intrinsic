SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, a former quality and audit decision-maker, discussed a consulting provider supporting pharmaceutical manufacturers with quality systems, regulatory readiness, and facility qualification. The expert had used the provider for five years and had experience with other contractors over a longer period. Annual spending across vendors was estimated at $5–10 million.

Services included inspection-readiness support, quality-system design, commissioning and qualification, process validation, gap assessments, and oversight of corrective actions. Customers use external consultants to supplement internal expertise, obtain an independent assessment, and build management support for addressing compliance gaps.

Key Insights

The provider’s main advantage is access to experienced specialists with current regulatory knowledge. The expert said this expertise can increase confidence in inspections and regulatory submissions. External support was especially valuable for complex projects, including comparing quality systems during a merger and resolving difficult manufacturing issues. The expert noted that recommendations may sometimes exceed what is necessary. Customers should prioritize findings according to severity and their willingness to accept residual risk. AI-enabled consulting was identified as a major opportunity. The expert expects AI to streamline documentation and analysis, while requiring human review and sign-off for GMP-related outputs. The expert also anticipates formal validation and training standards for AI tools used in regulated operations. The expert rated the provider 8 out of 10 for recommendation, citing satisfactory expertise and deliverables.
