原标题：Databricks at the Rubicon – Enterprise AI Payoff & Growth Durability

#software #cloud

Third Bridge · 2026-11-19

核心摘要

  1. 前Databricks企业客户总监表示，数据工程工作负载占平台成本和ARR增长的60-80%，毛利率超过80%，DSML/AI约占20-25%，BI占比最小。
  2. Databricks宣布年化收入运行率突破70亿美元，FY27第二季度同比增长超过80%，专家认为传统数据工程业务可持续每年增长50-60%，按50%增速一年后即可达到约100亿美元。
  3. 额外增长很大程度来自模型服务：承诺合同的DBU折扣同样适用于调用OpenAI等模型，这部分收入利润率很低甚至是转手或亏损，Snowflake CEO公开质疑过这一点。
  4. AI产品板块年化收入约17亿美元、约占总ARR的20%，专家认为未来三年这一占比将维持在20%左右，因为AI增长同时会带动数据工程增长。
  5. BI工作负载因依赖预留的无服务器计算毛利率较低，DSML中调用第三方模型的部分毛利率也较低；最大增长瓶颈是超大规模云厂商算力紧张导致的限流和新用例导入放缓。
  6. Databricks在核心BI和数仓上很少取代Snowflake，主要赢得增量数据工程负载；洁净室产品采用度差，但LakeFlow等专有工具提高了切换成本，Lakebase（Neon Postgres）则瞄准定制交易型应用，类似90年代Oracle打法。
  7. Databricks的研发费用约为Snowflake的两倍（例如40%对20%），推迟了GAAP盈利；专家持有其股份，认为公司可能永不上市，而是通过每年至少一次的股权流动性事件满足员工和投资者。
  8. 专家认为企业不会大规模取消SaaS席位，但席位增长停滞，ERP和HR较难被替代，CRM最脆弱；最大风险是创始团队长期高强度工作带来的人员倦怠。

Analyst-led • 10 Nov 2026

Companies: SNOW US, AMZN US, ORCL US

Andy Stahl (AS) Strategic Data Advisor at Denodo Technologies Inc

Agenda

  • Consumption dynamics, net retention and margin profile
  • Competitive positioning and dynamics vs peers
  • Monetisation of generative AI and platform extension
  • Sales efficiency, operating leverage, capital allocation and expansion risks

Key Insights

  • Data engineering drives 60-80% of ARR growth at >80% gross margins, whereas data science, machine learning and AI – 20–25% share – and BI carry lower margins from model pass-throughs and serverless compute costs
  • Classic data engineering should grow 50-60% YoY, supplemented by low-margin model serving via commit discounts. Key headwinds are hyperscaler compute throttling and slower new use case ingestion
  • Despite weak clean room adoption, Databricks is increasing switching costs via proprietary LakeFlow tools and targeting bespoke transactional apps with Lakebase, the name of its Neon Postgres piece
  • Databricks rarely displaces Snowflake in core BI, primarily capturing incremental data engineering workloads
  • R&D spending of roughly double Snowflake's delays GAAP profitability. Management will likely avoid an IPO, instead using annual liquidity events to satisfy private shareholders and employees
