Agenda

  • Trends and developments impacting Clear Channel Outdoor (NYSE: CCO) and the broader media environment
  • Strong digitalisation demand trends amid other positive industry tailwinds
  • Commentary regarding the pending take-private
  • Mid-to-long-term growth outlook

Key Insights

  • Out-of-home industry is exhibiting strong performance, hitting a record USD 5bn in H1 2026, driven by the World Cup and the 250th US anniversary, marking the sector's strongest performing year in recent memory
  • Digital billboards generate 3-4x revenue uplift over static boards by sharing space among multiple advertisers, which has been a core driver of industry-wide revenue growth recently
  • Expert sees potential for consolidated digital revenue share to grow towards 50-60% through 2028, but noted that it will hinge on strong sales execution and require more than just a static inventory conversion
  • Expert confirmed that industry consolidation is currently playing out, but noted that Clear Channel's historically high leverage – 8.5-9.5x vs Lamar at 2.9x and Outfront at 4.5x – could impede consolidation opportunities
  • Clear Channel's Radar tool uses anonymous mobile data across 5,000-plus behavioural categories, shifting advertiser conversations from geographic placement towards precise audience targeting and campaign effectiveness
