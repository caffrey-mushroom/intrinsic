核心摘要

  1. 管理48家便利店的资深饮品采购专家认为能量饮料仍是最强的饮料品类，基数扩大后增速会放缓但销量仍在增加，门店会尽量给该品类更多陈列空间，必要时挤占即饮茶、咖啡和等渗饮料。
  2. 零糖和“更健康”配方是品类增长的核心驱动，专家认为短期不会见顶；创新已成为维持份额的必要条件，Red Bull在美国因缺乏创新丢失份额，但国际销售增长10.2%。
  3. 春季陈列调整中，Alani Nu将推出两款新口味并获得更多排面，空间部分来自表现最差的Celsius单品；Bloom虽有KDP分销优势，但在其门店尚未见到明显需求。
  4. 专家看好Monster的营销执行和新推出的蛋白能量饮料，但便利店零售价需达到3.59-3.79美元甚至更高，接近4美元可能抑制冲动消费，需看到推广折扣才会引入；Flirt上市表现慢于预期。
  5. 价格上涨对销量影响不大，铝罐成本上升将推动全行业提价；Monster的主要风险是宏观消费大幅放缓，因为能量饮料不像燃油那样是刚需。
  6. Celsius核心品牌三季度同比下滑约6%，专家认为主要是被同比增长24%的Alani Nu蚕食，而非库存调整；公司借鉴百事经验，将Celsius、Alani Nu和Rockstar拆分为三个独立品牌事业部。
  7. Alani Nu限定口味Witch's Brew贡献了约4.1亿美元季度收入中的约1.3亿美元，专家提醒若改为常规口味会削弱限定吸引力；新品一般有60-90天考察期，成熟品牌可获最多6个月。
  8. Celsius、Alani Nu与Rockstar合计份额约20.7%，长期或可达25%；Red Bull在美份额约38%并重返Kroger货架，KDP旗下Ghost、C4、Bloom合计约10.1%，Ghost年销售超10亿美元；相关诉讼预计不会影响品类增长。

SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, a convenience-store beverage merchandiser with decades of category experience, discussed spring shelf resets, energy drink trends, brand performance, pricing, and emerging products. Energy drinks remain a particularly strong beverage category, and retailers are allocating space to brands and products that sell quickly. The expert expects growth to continue, although percentage gains may moderate as the category expands.

Key Insights

Zero-sugar and better-for-you products are central growth drivers. The expert expects zero-sugar options to keep gaining ground and sees little indication that their momentum will soon stall. Innovation is increasingly important. Established brands need new flavors, formulas, and ingredients to maintain consumer interest and market share. Monster is well positioned. The expert praised its recent marketing and expects continued momentum, while noting that a major consumer-spending slowdown could pressure discretionary purchases. Protein energy drinks are promising but face a convenience-store pricing hurdle. Products priced near $4 could deter impulse buyers. The expert is interested in stocking them if introductory promotions make the economics work. Alani Nu’s limited-time flavor was a notable success. The expert sees seasonal launches as useful for growth, but cautioned that making a popular temporary flavor permanent could weaken its appeal. Shelf space depends on inventory turns. New items generally get 60–90 days to prove themselves; established brands may receive up to six months when supported by marketing and customer interest.
