原标题：JLL – Where Can AI Improve CRE Brokerage & Is JLLT Being Maximised?

#realestate #services #ai

Third Bridge · 2026-11-05

核心摘要

  1. 前JLL运营卓越总监认为外包设施管理需求会维持或增加，因为大型机构希望在全部物业组合中获得一致的服务。
  2. 租户越来越要求财务透明，成本加成实报实销和绩效挂钩合同增多，平均5年期的合同在定价和结构上会受AI影响。
  3. 借助项目管理能力，交叉销售将加速；金融机构客户未来18个月在整合服务商，这有利于数据积累和AI应用。
  4. CBRE把技术直接嵌入运营团队的模式比JLL把JLLT作为独立实体更有效。
  5. 数据归客户所有让AI难以发挥，服务商的角色容易变得被动防守，而不是主动带着数据去做可信顾问。

Mickeia Jackson (MJ) Founder & CEO at Acuity FM

Jacob Zuller (JZ) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Growth opportunities across JLL's (NYSE: JLL) verticals
  • Potential challenges with leveraging JLLT (JLL Technologies) and opportunities for AI
  • Competitive analysis and JLL's ability to maintain and take market share
  • Outlook for Q4 2026 and beyond, highlighting opportunities to bundle and cross-sell

Key Insights

  • Demand for outsourced facilities management is expected to sustain or increase because the value proposition is evolving and large institutions want consistent service across their portfolios
  • Occupiers want financial transparency, and expert has seen a lot of cost-plus reimbursable accounts and performance-linked contracts. AI will impact pricing and structure of contracts that average 5 years long
  • Expert thinks cross-selling will accelerate by leveraging project management capabilities. Financial institution occupiers are consolidating service providers for the next 18 months, which will help from a data perspective and can leverage AI better
  • CBRE's model of embedding technology directly within operational teams is more effective than JLL and JLLT, operating as a separate entity. Client owning the data causes challenges in leveraging AI and changes the relationship to being defensive vs bringing data to the clients and being a trusted advisor
