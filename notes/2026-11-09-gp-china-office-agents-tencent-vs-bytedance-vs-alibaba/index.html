Key Insights

Token value is becoming the primary buying criterion. As core features converge, customers increasingly compare how much work a subscription’s credits can accomplish. Efficiency gains have not yet translated reliably into reduced staffing. Companies must justify ongoing subscription costs with measurable returns, while headcount decisions depend on organizational factors beyond technology. The expert expects competition to center on ecosystem access and cost-effective models, with office agents potentially becoming free distribution channels for token sales. The expert’s view that agents may soon struggle to charge per-seat fees prompted further discussion about self-built alternatives and competitive barriers.
