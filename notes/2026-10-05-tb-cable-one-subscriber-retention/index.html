原标题：Cable One – Can It Retain Subscribers as Mega Broadband Deal is Set to Close?

#media

Third Bridge · 2026-10-05

Analyst-led • 1 Oct 2026

Companies: CHTR US, CABO US, CMCSA US, TMUS US, VZ US, T US

Joel Braun (JB) Chief CX Officer at Palmetto Rural Telephone Cooperative Inc

John Conca (JC) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Cable One's (NYSE: CABO) operating environment, noting worsening subscriber trends across the cable industry
  • Competitive dynamics vs ILECs (incumbent local exchange carriers) and other overbuilders
  • Mega Broadband synergies, noting recent revolver draw to fund acquisition
  • 12-18-month outlook, noting pressures from Starlink (NASDAQ: SPCX) and FWA (fixed wireless access)

Key Insights

  • Expert believes Cable One's coax infrastructure is fundamentally uncompetitive, citing ageing 30-year-old networks, poor maintenance and the inability to match fibre's symmetrical upload speeds that are increasingly demanded by remote workers
  • Cable One's mobile strategy, offering lines at USD 15 per month, is seen as reactive desperation. The prior CEO explicitly stated mobile doesn't improve customer retention before reversing course
  • On the acquisition of Mega Broadband, "If they had to make the decision today vs when they had to make it, it would be a different decision." The deal looks increasingly ill-timed, given the deteriorating conditions
  • Cable One's SG&A runs c25% of revenue, roughly 4x Charter's ratio, representing significant misallocated capital that could otherwise fund necessary network maintenance and customer retention efforts
  • Selling JV stakes in Clearwave, which is aggressively expanding westwards via the Visionary Broadband acquisition, could generate meaningful proceeds. However, it sacrifices long-term upside to address immediate debt pressures
