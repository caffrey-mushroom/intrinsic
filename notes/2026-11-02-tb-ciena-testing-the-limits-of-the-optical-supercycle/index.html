原标题：Ciena – Testing the Limits of the Optical Supercycle

#optical #ai-demand

Third Bridge · 2026-11-02

核心摘要

  1. 受访者是前Ciena AT&T客户团队高级经理，讨论AI驱动的光通信需求和Ciena的竞争地位。
  2. 专家认为Ciena年收入增速最终可能从管理层预计的FY27 30%以上回归到3–7%，因为超大云投资会放缓、运营商更重视现有网络回报。
  3. AI相关光增长高度集中在一家支出远超同行的超大云，复制到其他客户的机会很大，但也加大了单一客户capex回撤的风险。
  4. Nokia收购Infinera后形成更强的端到端竞争者，尤其对需要光加宽带一体化方案的运营商。
  5. Ribbon渗透AT&T暴露了Ciena老化的二层产品线的弱点。
  6. 专家认为若超大云需求保持强劲，FY29约50%的调整后毛利率目标可以达成，主要靠规模带来的供应商折扣而不是单纯提价。

Analyst-led • 26 Oct 2026

Companies: NOKIA FI, T US, CIEN US, RBBN US, LUMN US

Robert Driscoll (RD) US Business Development Executive at NetPMD Design & Integration

Colin Duncan (CD) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • AI-driven optical demand and hyperscaler growth
  • Ciena's (NYSE: CIEN) competitive positioning vs Nokia/Infinera and Ribbon
  • Supply constraints, lead times and backlog durability
  • Revenue growth normalisation, pricing and margin outlook

Key Insights

  • Ciena's annual revenue growth could eventually normalise towards 3-7% from management's expected 30% or greater growth in FY27, as hyperscaler investment moderates and service providers prioritise returns on existing infrastructure over additional network expansion
  • Ciena's AI-driven optical growth is heavily concentrated in one major hyperscaler spending significantly more than its peers, creating substantial opportunities to replicate deployments across other customers, but increasing exposure to a potential pullback in individual hyperscaler CAPEX
  • Nokia's acquisition of Infinera creates a stronger end-to-end networking competitor to Ciena, particularly among carriers seeking integrated optical and broadband solutions, while Ribbon's penetration of AT&T exposes vulnerabilities in Ciena's ageing layer 2 portfolio
  • Ciena's target of c50% adjusted gross margins by FY29 appears achievable if hyperscaler demand remains strong, with higher production volumes supporting supplier discounts and improved purchasing leverage rather than relying solely on price increases
