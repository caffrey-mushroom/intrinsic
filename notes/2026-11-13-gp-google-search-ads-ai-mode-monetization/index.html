SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, a former senior Google advertising leader, discussed slowing search-ad growth despite rising query engagement, and how AI-powered search may change user behavior and ad monetization. The conversation also covered advertiser readiness and measurement, YouTube’s revenue mix, and potential competition from AI and agentic-commerce products.

Key Insights

The expert views slower search-ad growth as a transition challenge, not a long-term concern. Google is balancing monetization with user trust and may be deliberately limiting ads in AI experiences while it learns what formats work. Longer, multi-turn AI sessions could create new ad inventory, especially as ads extend beyond the initial prompt. The expert sees meaningful potential, though not necessarily an immediate step-change in results. Measurement, attribution, transparency, and bidding tools need to adapt before many advertisers shift larger budgets into AI search. Limited placement visibility may constrain spending, although the expert believes Google prioritizes automated performance over extensive advertiser controls. The transition may be faster than the earlier move from desktop to mobile, but advertiser education and budget changes will still take time. Agentic commerce and ChatGPT advertising are worth monitoring, but the expert does not expect major near-term budget diversion from Google. Google’s shopping infrastructure and established advertiser relationships are advantages. The expert is uncertain whether AI search will favor larger advertisers over small and medium-sized businesses, but believes Google will focus on addressing the issue.

Competitive Landscape

Company or area

Positioning and outlook

Google AI search and AI Max

Potential to expand search inventory and support new ad formats. Adoption may depend on stronger measurement and advertiser confidence.

YouTube

Shorts engagement and paid subscriptions are growing, but both may weigh on ad revenue mix. Connected TV and performance advertising offer further opportunity.

ChatGPT and other AI products

Potential long-term competition, but the expert sees limited near-term threat given Google’s search data, products, and advertising ecosystem.

Agentic-commerce offerings

Early-stage experiments. Google may be well positioned through its retailer relationships, product feeds, and commerce infrastructure.

Small and medium-sized advertisers

Could face greater challenges if AI ad systems rely heavily on extensive advertiser data; the expert regards the outcome as unresolved.
