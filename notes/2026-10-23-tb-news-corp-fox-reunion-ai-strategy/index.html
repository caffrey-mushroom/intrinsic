Nicole J Castillo (NJC) Chief of Staff at Wipro Ltd

John Conca (JC) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • News Corp's (NASDAQ: NWSA) operating environment, focusing on news media segment and subscription outlook
  • Competitive dynamics vs New York Times (NYSE: NYT), Thomson Reuters (TSX: TRI) and others
  • AI impacts, noting OpenAI partnership, AI licensing revenues and potential for AI to disrupt news consumption
  • 12-18-month outlook, noting potential for remerging with Fox (NASDAQ: FOXA) and potential rationales

Key Insights

  • News Corp targets USD 1bn Dow Jones EBITDA by 2030. Revenue growth alone cannot achieve this, and cost-cutting mechanisms won't substantially fill the gap, making the goal questionable
  • On AI licensing deals, the cUSD 50m annual payments from OpenAI and Meta are viewed as short-term revenue that will disappear as large language models grow more powerful and reduce dependency on licensed content
  • Dow Jones B2B segment differentiates through niche acquisitions in energy and risk/compliance data, competing less directly with Bloomberg Terminal by targeting specialised, high-value decision-making datasets
  • Expert believes the Fox-News Corp reunion is unlikely, citing brand trust concerns as merging could undermine Dow Jones' credibility, given Fox's "entertainment brand" classification
  • Realtor.com's surprising double-digit growth, despite a muted housing market, is driven by richer lead-quality tools sold to agents rather than consumer-facing app improvements — although sustained gains remain uncertain
