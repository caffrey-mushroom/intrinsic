原标题：Personal & Informatik – Platform Usability, Cloud Migration & DACH Competition

#薪酬软件 #DACH

Third Bridge · 2026-11-09

核心摘要

  1. P&I在德国胜出主要因GDPR和工会规定要求本地托管独立薪酬系统。
  2. 可触达市场限于德国和DACH的中型及小企业。
  3. Datev因销售响应差、SD Worx因价格和定制灵活性输给P&I。
  4. 续约时竞争者可以压低P&I价格。
  5. 上云迁移之后的下一个增长点尚未证明。

Analyst-led • 28 Oct 2026

Companies: WDAY US, SAP DE

Boris Strucken (BS) Managing Director, Germany at Fidelity Information Services Operations GmbH (Fidelity National Information Services Inc)

Saad Maqsood (SM) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Overall satisfaction, system reliability and user adoption rates across P&I's (Personal & Informatik's) LogaHR and LOGA3 software suites
  • CX during cloud and SaaS migration process from legacy on-premises installations
  • Strength in payroll capabilities and integration flexibility with third-party HR solutions
  • Software licensing costs, annual maintenance price increases and barriers to switching
  • Competitive positioning and differentiation against SD Worx, Datev, SAP (ETR: SAP) and Workday (NASDAQ: WDAY)

Key Insights

  • P&I wins in the German market primarily due to regulatory requirements – GDPR and workers' council rules force companies to use German-hosted, standalone payroll systems disconnected from global platforms such as Workday. However, the addressable market is limited to mid-market and SMB in Germany and wider DACH market
  • Datev, widely considered the German payroll market leader, lost in a head-to-head vs P&I partly due to poor sales responsiveness. SD Worx lost out on pricing and less flexibility on customisation
  • On renewal pricing dynamics, competitors can actually pressure P&I downwards. Customers can leverage competing bids to negotiate lower prices, meaning P&I's pricing power at renewal is somewhat limited
  • P&I's next growth driver after the on-prem-to-SaaS migration wave remains unproven – customers demand clear ROI before adopting AI modules in sensitive payroll environments
