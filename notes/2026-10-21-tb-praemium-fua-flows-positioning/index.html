David Coulter (DC) Consultant Chief Financial Officer at Keys Thomas Associates

Agenda

  • Growth expectations and product-level drivers of FUA (funds under administration)
  • Efficiency gains, including ongoing synergies from the acquisitions of OneVue and Technotia
  • Praemium's (ASX: PPS) competitive positioning and FY27 performance outlook

Key Insights

  • Accelerating platform FUA growth to 15-20% YoY from 11%, paired with 5-10% portfolio growth, could expand EBITDA margins toward c40% within three years, with scope for further 3-5% expansion
  • Technotia is the key catalyst for operational cost reduction and margin expansion, implying non-cash capitalised legacy software write-offs might continue. However, the Technotia team could take a "proportionate share" of profitability gains due to profit share incentives surrounding successful cost reductions
  • Redesigning superannuation into a direct-to-platform solution completes Praemium's portfolio for high-net-worth advisors, alongside managed accounts and Powerwrap
  • Spectrum is Praemium's primary growth engine, leveraging the market's broadest alternative asset access, superior tax reporting accuracy and rich data feeds to differentiate itself from Netwealth and Hub24
  • Praemium's AUD 100bn FUA target is achievable within 3-5 years, primarily via non-custodial growth where revenue capture may be weaker as it depends on portfolio counts rather than asset volumes
