Agenda

  • UK grocery retail's growth, shopping frequency, basket size and pricing for Q3 2026 vs Q2 2026
  • Promotional strategy outlook and positioning across retailers, including loyalty programmes and media utilisation
  • Competitive landscape and market share movements, given current consumer shopping habits
  • Outlook for Q4 2026 and beyond, including like-for-like growth and profitability dynamics

Key Insights

  • UK grocery like-for-like growth sits at 2.3-2.5% in Q3 2026, driven almost entirely by food inflation, with volume growth essentially flat as consumer confidence weakens post summer
  • Lidl is outperforming main competitor Aldi by replacing smaller-footprint units with larger, brighter stores and leveraging a loyalty app, whereas Aldi's brand additions dilute its operating margins
  • Asda's recent return to like-for-like sales growth remains fragile because aggressive price cuts were funded by deep store labour cuts, causing severe declines in store availability and customer service
  • Asda's strategic pivot to convenience store franchising is a capital-light admission that it lacks capital and locations to expand directly, risking severe supply chain friction with its core retail operation
  • Major brand manufacturers are fighting back against private label growth by extending multi-buy promotion durations past standard cycle limits, sacrificing margin to protect product volume and retain retail shelf placement
