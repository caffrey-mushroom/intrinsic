But the past is the past and this week is starting on a better note. Semis +4% early with CRWV +7% on a GS upgrade to Buy (NBIS/miners +6-7% in sympathy), INTC +4% on a JPM upgrade, & BE +5% leading the way higher. Software/Internet +40bps lagging. This week, we get NFLX, LRCX, META, TXN and SPOT to kick off earnings. We’ll have some bogeys later today or tomorrow.

Lots to get to, so let’s get to it…

CRWV: Goldman upgrades to Buy/$106, says credit fears overstate funding risk and contract economics are stronger than market expects

Goldman upgrades CRWV from Neutral after the ~43% pullback, arguing investors are overstating financing risk: industry checks suggest new debt costs may be only ~100–200bps above CRWV’s current blended cost, while DDTLs are asset-/contract-backed and can still clear near or above original issue discounts despite unsecured bonds trading at ~13.5–15%. GS also sees upside to ARR/MW and unlevered IRRs as supply stays tight, with CRWV ranking top-2 among 300+ AI compute providers and delivering ~20% better performance/$ than peers. Forge/Omni further support a move up-stack beyond commodity GPU infrastructure. Buy, PT $106 from $139, ~73% upside.

DDOG: Bernstein upgrades to OP/$350 as “Born in AI” demand reaccelerates far faster than expected

Bernstein reverses its prior downgrade after data shows token consumption at DDOG’s largest AI-lab customers reaccelerating to at least ~50% q/q, helped by Codex/model launches and aggressive price cuts, making its prior 2027 AI-growth slowdown assumptions look too conservative. Traditional customers remain weak and the ~$100M ARR OpenAI downsizing is still a headwind, but AI-native demand plus potential upside from consumer agents, cybersecurity and the Scout SRE AI Agent more than offset it; Bernstein raises near-term revenue growth by ~7pts and extends duration. Upgrades to Outperform from Market Perform, PT $350 from $237.

INTC: JPM upgrades to Neutral from UW as execution improves and AI tailwinds offset lingering share/foundry concerns

JPM says the ~36% pullback since June leaves risk/reward more balanced, while execution under Lip-Bu Tan has improved across compute, manufacturing and strategic positioning. DCAI operating income reached ~$2.5B and surpassed CCG for the first time, Granite Rapids demand is “extremely tight,” 18A-P remains on schedule and 14A is targeted for 2028 HVM; ASIC services are also approaching a ~$2B run-rate, ~3x y/y. Server/PC share losses and external foundry traction remain key concerns, but JPM sees CY27/28 EPS ~15% above consensus and upgrades to Neutral from UW, PT $85.

AI Infra/AI Power: SemiAnalysis sees +50GW of data-center capacity in 2027, with bottleneck shifting from power/buildings toward chips

SemiAnalysis argues 2027 data-center growth is materially underestimated, forecasting ~50GW of new capacity in a single year with >24GW already in advanced construction across just 40 campuses and ~60GW under construction more broadly. The key leading indicator is >$1T of 2026 YTD lease value already signed/closed, with Oracle, META, AWS, MSFT, GOOGL, NVDA, AMD, Anthropic and OpenAI all pursuing GW-scale capacity; modular/prefab designs are also cutting build times toward ~9–10 months and reducing MEP labor by up to ~63%. The implication is a sharp acceleration in compute supply, AI-lab/token revenue and capex, with SemiAnalysis arguing the constraint could increasingly migrate from data centers/power to memory and logic chips in 2027–28.

MS raises PT to $820 from $775 as stronger ads + AI subscriptions lift 2027/28 EPS 1%/3%; OW, $1,000 bull case.
