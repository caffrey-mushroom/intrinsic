Normal sized VCSEL are well known in transceiver world at 112G PAM4 per lane, which means 28 GHz 3dB bandwidth.

VCSEL Tx is very different from CW SiPho Tx. There are intrinsicly many parasitic effects from the laser itself that need to be mitigated in the electrical design, from CoS substrate, to PCB, to package/interposer, to drive/biasing electronics, to SerDes.

CW O-band SiPho or EML optics are basically immune to fiber dispersion for all reach less than 500 meters.

VCSEL (850nm and 1060nm) runs into many horrible and annoying problems. Partially due to the way the laser works, partially due to the wavelength, and partially due to the multi-mode fiber the system requires itself.

There is also this property called chirp due to the fact VCSEL are directly modulated.

You can see the significant ISI (multi-tracing) caused by chirp and other dispersive effects from fibers.

Now that you have the necessary crash-course background, let’s go over why this merger makes a lot of sense if you think the real goal is to speedrun uVCSEL scale-in NPO/CPO.

Lumentum has basically zero electrical or analog capabilities. All they have is uVCSEL manufacturing, packaging, and fiber attach.

Marvell has all the electrical and analog capabilities across all logic process nodes and SiGe.

In isolation, neither of these two can make uVCSEL scale-in work. It would be a disaster of going in circles for years.

But together… this is perfect complimentary skillset and assets to super hyper duper accelerate uVCSEL advanced slow-and-wide system development.

Smaller VCSEL are much more sensitive to electrical crosstlak and parasitic effects. Also much more sensitive to chirp, RIN issues, dispersion.

Marvell has all the DSP/equalizer/electrical IP needed to make a really good uVCSEL electrical sub-system.

Dispersion is a huge problem in long-haul. So the ZR COLORZ stuff Marvell already has. The math and theory to correct long-haul C-band and O-band optics is the same as scale-in uVCSEL. This is because multi-mode fibers and tiny VCSEL are shit in general so dispersion kills your link very quickly.

uVCSEL over 10 meters runs into the same dispersion problems as ZR optics going over 100 kilometer.

I think Hurlston realized he could not acquire companies with the right skillsets fast enough to accelerate uVCSEL advanced optics and decided to sell Lumentum to Marvell for this reason.

Maybe this merger is not completely stupid.

Both CEOs tried to explain what is going on in the fireside chat today but I think they did not do a very good job. Too much wide focus.

This is about uVCSEL NPO/CPO scale-in. Nothing else matters… every other topic is just noise… I think.
