2026.11.06 | TB | Security | Evolutio & Spanish Cybersecurity

原标题：Evolutio – Navigating Growth & AI Integration in Spanish Cybersecurity

#security

Third Bridge · 2026-11-06

核心摘要

  1. Telefónica专家认为Evolutio是西班牙网络安全市场可信的二线厂商，落后于Telefónica Tech和Orange Cyberdefense。
  2. 专家估计Evolutio至少需要三年持续稳定的交付、保留人才并提升技术能力，才能缩小与一线厂商的差距。
  3. SOC、MDR和外部威胁情报等领域预算预计每年保持双位数增长，MDR是最主要的经常性机会，其次是身份与云安全。
  4. AI使先进网络安全服务大众化，中型市场客户增长最快，大型企业和政府客户也保持双位数增长。
  5. 西班牙托管安全市场合同续约率平均约50%，Evolutio处于市场平均水平，客户流失主要源于未能跟上AI威胁演进。
  6. 大型企业和公共部门合同通常为两到三年，中型市场多为一年期，公共合同按西班牙CPI年度调整。
  7. 中途更换供应商的额外成本约相当于半年费用，计划性不续约约需三个月迁移。
  8. Evolutio主要依靠收购和第三方厂商生态扩张而非自研，专有IP较弱，定价既非最高也非最便宜，利润率较一年前未见改善。
  9. AI整合方面Telefónica Tech领先，其次为Orange，Evolutio与其他厂商处于同一水平，人才争夺是其主要经营阻力。

Analyst-led • 2 Nov 2026

Companies: TEF ES

Raul Ortega del Rio (RODR) Business Manager to COO at Telefónica SA

Georgia Beattie (GB) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Overview of Spanish cybersecurity market landscape and Evolutio's positioning
  • Evaluation of product offerings, cloud integration capabilities and end-to-end service delivery
  • Revenue dynamics, client stickiness, contract structures and switching costs
  • Strategic vendor partnerships, go-to-market channels and AI innovation leadership

Key Insights

  • Evolutio is a credible tier 2 player, trailing Telefónica Tech and Orange Cyberdefense. Bridging this gap will take at least three years of consistent execution, closing capability gaps and winning unified platform deals
  • Client retention hinges on keeping pace with rapid AI-driven threat evolutions. Contract renewal rates across Spanish managed security market average around 50%, with Evolutio performing near that average – not notably above or below competitors
  • Evolutio scales portfolio and footprint via acquisitions and third-party vendor ecosystems rather than proprietary R&D, leaving it with lower proprietary IP differentiation and limiting long-term margin expansion
  • Overall demand for security operations centres, managed detection and response and external threat intelligence is growing double-digits yearly, driven by AI-enabled attacks requiring equally sophisticated defensive responses. While enterprises expand steadily, mid-market accounts offer fastest growth, although accessible AI democratises advanced cybersecurity, enabling smaller companies to access enterprise-grade protection cheaply and compete in this space for SMEs
