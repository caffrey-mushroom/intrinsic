Companies: LPLA US, AMP US, RJF US

Glen Cohen (GC) Market Director, Business Development at Equitable Advisors LLC

Agenda

  • Osaic's ability to attract and retain financial advisors
  • Organic and inorganic growth opportunities
  • Advisor utilisation and strategy for AI
  • Outlook for Q4 2026 and beyond, highlighting competitive challenges

Key Insights

  • Economics is the main reason for advisor movement, where deals have gone from 50bps to 125-150bps on assets and extended from five to 10 years. Expert thinks Osaic is being more competitive with compensation packages, but believes it has topped out paying for assets
  • Osaic is "taking everybody […] They're looking for an advisor who has advisory business, probably doing a minimum USD 300,000-500,000 in gross and possibly down the line, would look for a succession plan"
  • If advisors want to leave, firms usually let them. However, there have been more recent deals where firms such as Ameriprise are offering compensation, about 50% of what the advisor was offered, to keep them
  • "LPL is by far the cheapest to do business at, and everyone is following suit. If you want to win an advisor, you're going to have to be close with regards to the fees"
