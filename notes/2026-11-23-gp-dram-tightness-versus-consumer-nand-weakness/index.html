核心摘要

  1. Kingston 资深业务发展经理指出过去 30 天存储市场呈现现货与合约背离：DRAM 与 NAND 合约价仍在上涨，但 NAND 现货大跌 15%-20%，消费级 DRAM 现货微跌个位数，而企业级 DRAM 现货保持持平。
  2. 本轮现货走弱主要源于消费电子（PC 与手机）终端销售不佳及高内存成本压缩利润，消费端客户当前持有约 4 个月库存并从 Q4 起停买观望，预计弱势将持续至 2027 年 Q1，形成约两季度的库存调整周期。
  3. 占据市场超 50% 份额的企业级与数据中心需求依然稳健：中美云厂商（CSP）均按原定计划采购，未出现传闻中的 SSD 砍单；目前企业级 DRAM 库存仅约 2 个月，企业级 NAND 库存约 3 个月。
  4. DRAM 前景显著强于 NAND：DRAM 由 3-4 家原厂高度垄断且云厂长约仅覆盖约 50% 需求，2027 年 Q1 合约价将继续上涨，2027 下半年预计维持每季度 10%-20% 的涨幅；NAND 预计 Q2 见底，2027 下半年仅温和季增 0%-10%。
  5. 为控制高昂成本，PC 厂商继 2026 年缩容（1TB 降至 512GB）后，2027 年正评估采用降级 NAND 甚至非标 500GB SSD，一旦落地可增加至少 10% 的消费级供给并压低售价；企业端虽亦探讨内存减配（如传闻 NVDA Rubin Ultra），但更低单机成本反而利好 AI 服务器总出货量。
  6. 国产替代难以扭转全球紧缺：YMTC 虽已占据中国本土约 50% 份额并积极拓展海外，CXMT 产能亦已售罄至 2027 上半年，但即便二者规划年扩产 60%（全球其他地区约 30%），因受美方黑名单限制及缺乏企业级认证，仍无法缓解美国云厂的企业级短缺。
  7. 关于 AI Agent 推动 SSD 承担 KV-cache 以分流 HBM/DRAM 负载的长期趋势，专家认为技术架构尚未定型且 2027 上半年无量产项目，实质体量最快要到 2027 下半年至 2028 年才会显现，暂不纳入 2027 预测。

SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, a memory-industry professional, discussed DRAM and NAND flash trends, contrasting contract prices with weaker spot prices. The main pressure is in consumer markets, particularly PCs and mobile devices, where weaker sales and high memory costs are prompting customers to manage inventory and reduce orders. Enterprise and data-center demand remains comparatively resilient, making it the main support for the broader memory market.

Key Insights

The expert views the recent spot-price declines as a warning about consumer demand, not proof that the overall memory market has reversed. DRAM has a stronger outlook than NAND because supply is tighter and enterprise demand is more visible. The expert expects DRAM prices to resume rising after a near-term pause. NAND faces greater inventory pressure and weaker confidence in its recovery. Customers may reduce SSD capacity or accept lower-grade products to control costs. The expert expects consumer-market volatility to be temporary if enterprise supply remains tight. KV-cache applications could support NAND demand over time, but the expert sees no clear basis to include material demand from them in the 2027 outlook.

Quantitative Insights

Metric

Expert’s assessment

Recent spot prices

NAND declined about 15%–20% over roughly 30 days; consumer DRAM fell by a single-digit percentage. Enterprise DRAM spot prices were flat.

Contract prices

DRAM and NAND contract prices were still increasing, despite weaker spot prices.

Consumer demand

Weakness may persist through Q1; inventory correction could create a roughly two-quarter cycle. Consumer customers were estimated to hold around four months of inventory.

Enterprise inventory

DRAM inventory was estimated at close to two months; enterprise NAND at about three months.

DRAM outlook

Contract prices were expected to rise in Q1. In the second half of 2027, quarterly increases of about 10%–20% were anticipated.

NAND outlook

Consumer NAND could bottom around Q2 or later, with second-half 2027 increases estimated at 0%–10% quarter over quarter. Enterprise NAND was expected to recover earlier.

Potential NAND supply

If customers accept downgraded NAND, consumer-grade supply could rise by at least 10%; this option remained under evaluation.

Technology and Demand

The expert sees potential for SSD-based KV-cache applications to shift some workloads from DRAM, but expects meaningful volume to remain uncertain and potentially later than 2027. The expert also noted that some enterprise designs may use less memory per system, while strong AI-server demand could still support overall memory consumption.
