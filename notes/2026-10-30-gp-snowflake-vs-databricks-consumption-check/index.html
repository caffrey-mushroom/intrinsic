SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, who works in data integration, discussed customer demand and spending across Snowflake, Databricks, and ClickHouse. The conversation covered data growth, AI adoption, real-time pipelines, platform costs, and competition. The expert’s view is based on customer interactions and their own company’s experience, rather than comprehensive market data.

Key Insights

Demand for data and AI platforms remains strong, though cheaper tokens are changing how customers think about AI budgets. Many are using cost reductions to run more AI workloads rather than reduce spending. Customers appear to be exceeding expected platform consumption more often and are scrutinizing usage to control costs. Most customers expect moderate, organic data growth. New AI-related workloads can drive sharp increases for individual customers, but these examples are not representative of the broader base. The expert sees no material platform switching among their customers. ClickHouse is typically added for low-latency applications, while Snowflake or Databricks remains the main analytics platform. Iceberg interest is growing, partly as a way to reduce dependence on a single provider. So far, the expert has not observed it meaningfully reducing Snowflake compute use. Snowflake’s replication and streaming initiatives signal attention to real-time use cases, but the expert believes they have not closed the gap in low-latency processing. They also raised uncertainty about the technical and business incentives for Snowflake to compete more directly in that area. AI agents are expected to generate more frequent, smaller queries when appropriately controlled. Poorly constrained workloads can instead produce costly, large queries. The expert has limited visibility into production adoption of Snowflake’s AI products and broader customer use of model- routing features.

Competitive Landscape

Platform

Positioning and observations

Snowflake

Core analytics platform for many customers. Similar to Databricks in warehouse-style pipeline costs, but the expert considers it less suited to very low- latency compute. Iceberg and native replication may improve flexibility and ingestion options.

Databricks

A core analytics alternative to Snowflake. The expert hears of cost overruns, including in connection with accessible AI tools, but cautions that this is anecdotal. New streaming and compute offerings may broaden its competition.

ClickHouse

Strong option for low-latency, customer-facing analytics and AI query workloads. The expert reports rising traction, but not wholesale replacement of core Snowflake or Databricks platforms.

BigQuery

Considered more expensive for low-latency pipelines than Snowflake or Databricks, based on the expert’s rough estimate.
