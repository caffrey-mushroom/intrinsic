核心摘要

  1. House of HR前数字总监兼CISO介绍，集团由40多家被收购公司组成，坚持去中心化模式，但统一了身份、Microsoft 365、安全运营等IT基础设施，并基于Salesforce为全集团部署同一套ATS技术，以数字化流程收集数据作为AI的基础。
  2. AI用例包括自动撰写职位描述、向客户推荐候选人的邮件（每年约5-6万次成功安置对应50-60万次候选人推荐）、简历和面试纪要解析，以及直接与求职者互动并推荐岗位的AI代理。
  3. AI提升了招聘流程效率与转化率，但对利润的直接贡献难以单独衡量；人员缩减主要源于市场疲软而非AI，后台岗位效率提升明显，而长期和永久岗位招聘仍依赖招聘顾问的专业能力。
  4. 完全数字化的No-Jobs灵活用工平台约占收入的10%，专家认为临时和兼职岗位可实现高度AI化，集团通过自建该平台抵御数字原生新进入者的冲击。
  5. 专家认为AI进一步释放价值的最大障碍是推广采用而非技术，工具质量需达到90-95%以上一线员工才会愿意使用；长期看实现约10%的改善是现实的，若市场回暖同样人员可处理多30-40%的业务量。
  6. 疲软市场下毛利率较三年前下降约2-3%，主要因比利时等地薪资指数化上涨难以及时转嫁，专家认为价格压力已基本见底；集团EBITDA利润率仍超过10%，处于行业领先水平。
  7. 由于聚焦稀缺的专业化岗位而非低技能大批量用工，集团在市场下滑时业务量基本持平，表现优于行业；Accent门店已从约300家缩减至约200家，但集团不会放弃去中心化模式。
  8. 临时用工业务薪酬约70%固定、30%浮动，工程咨询约85%固定、15%浮动；临时用工与咨询各贡献约一半EBITDA，目前临时用工已在各国回暖，咨询业务通常滞后12-16个月跟进，现金流改善后并购将重启。

SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, a former senior technology leader at a large European staffing group, discussed how the group is applying AI, managing a decentralized structure, and responding to challenging market conditions. The group operates through many acquired businesses, while centralizing selected technology and services. Its strategy has been to standardize core systems and processes so it can gather consistent data and apply automation and AI.

Use cases include drafting job listings and client proposals, processing résumés and interview notes, and helping candidates find suitable roles. A separate digital platform supports flexible, temporary work.

Key Insights

AI is improving workflow efficiency and recruitment conversion, but the expert said its direct contribution to profit is difficult to isolate. Staff reductions were driven partly by weaker market conditions, so they could not be attributed solely to AI. The expert sees people and specialist recruitment expertise as continuing advantages in permanent and longer-term placements. Fully digital recruitment is more viable for certain temporary roles. The largest barrier to further AI gains is adoption, not technology. Tools need to be highly reliable and clearly useful to frontline staff; change management, training, and iterative improvements are essential. The expert believes a 10% improvement is achievable over time, but expects progress to depend on market recovery and sustained implementation. The group is gradually consolidating some branches, but is unlikely to abandon its decentralized model, which the expert views as central to its identity.

Competitive Landscape

Area

Position and implications

Large staffing peers

The group reportedly performed better than competitors in a weak market, with activity broadly steady while peers declined. Its focus on scarce, specialized roles was cited as a differentiator from high-volume, lower-skilled staffing.

Digital-first entrants

These pose pressure in temporary staffing. The group responded by moving some flexible work into its own digital platform, which the expert said helps defend that segment.

Traditional service model

Local branches and recruiter relationships remain important for specialist placements, while automation supports staff rather than replacing them.

Acquisitions

Buy-and-build remains part of the strategy, but a flat market and limited cash flow constrain the pace of deals.
