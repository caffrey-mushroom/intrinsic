核心摘要

  1. InMobi专家认为移动买量市场整体健康，三季度游戏与非游戏广告主预算均在增长，游戏预算增速约30%至40%，非游戏约20%。
  2. AppLovin与InMobi两大DSP合计拿走约40%至60%的程序化预算，Unity、Liftoff等二线DSP约占10%至15%。
  3. AppLovin新算法自6月初分批上线，广告主反馈LTV和ROAS提升约5%至15%，预算随之逐月增加，但专家认为模型完全成熟可能要到明年一季度。
  4. 四季度预测市场和体育博彩类应用（如Polymarket、DraftKings、FanDuel）借NFL赛季与中期选举大幅加投，其中一家预测类应用本季度预算据估约10亿美元，推高CPM并压缩其他广告主的效率。
  5. 专家估计AppLovin四季度支出环比增长约10%至15%，并认为其在InMobi竞价中出价更激进、胜率上升。
  6. AppLovin电商业务从低基数高速增长，但需要约两倍于Meta的定制化素材和互动结尾卡，缺乏人口属性定向，且客服支持差，高客单价低频商品难以获得足够信号。
  7. 专家判断节后CPM通常下降30%至60%，一季度往往是大型游戏广告主投放最多的季度，新游戏标题约占游戏预算的20%。
  8. Unity在样本预算中占比约5%至10%，表现不错但规模有限，四季度预计持平或小幅增长；Moloco凭借扩张SDK直连供给成为与AppLovin最接近的竞争者。
  9. Meta模型改进后广告主满意度提升，但在InMobi竞价中份额不到5%；Google在安卓端至少占据60%的预算，iOS端支出可忽略。
  10. 专家认为AppLovin在CTV领域缺乏独家库存，广告主通常限制合作伙伴数量，难以获得大量增量预算。

SUMMARY

Overview

The expert discussed mobile user-acquisition spending, the performance of major advertising platforms, and whether AppLovin’s updated targeting model is attracting incremental budgets. The market was described as healthy, with spending rising across gaming and non-gaming advertisers. Seasonal demand from sports, prediction apps, and holiday e-commerce is increasing competition and advertising costs.

Key Insights

AppLovin’s new model appears to be improving results for some advertisers, but outcomes vary and rollout remains gradual. The expert expects further gains as the model learns, with fuller maturity potentially taking until Q1. Advertisers have reported stronger downstream returns, user engagement, and lifetime value. Better performance has generally led to higher monthly budgets. AppLovin is well placed to benefit from e-commerce growth and seasonal spending, but its platform has notable hurdles: advertisers need more tailored creative, audience controls are limited, and customer support is weak. Rising auction costs are already weighing on campaign efficiency. Some advertisers are reducing or revising budgets, although strong returns can still justify spending. Gaming may benefit in Q1 from lower post-holiday advertising costs and new devices entering the market.
