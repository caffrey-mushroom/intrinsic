ASML Lifts 2028 Capacity Target Again as Clients Order for 2029/30; We See 13% Upside to Our 2028 Revenue Forecast - WFE Model

Sherry Zhang, Inbok Song Monday, October 19, 2026

Executive Summary

  • ASML raised FY2026 revenue guidance again to EUR44-45B (+36% YoY), 6% above our forecast, on stronger-than-expected installed base revenue.
  • Management is looking to grow 2028 low NA EUV and DUV immersion (DUVi) capacity by over 30%, noting customers are requesting to place orders for 2029 and 2030.
  • Price increases are confirmed with partial impact in 2027 and full impact in 2028. We estimate throughput increases on next-generation low NA and DUVi could lift 2028 average selling prices (ASPs) by 11% and 6%, respectively, vs. 2026.
  • China will be 20% of ASML’s business this year and is expected to remain at similar levels in 2027 and beyond, implying a rebound in China revenue next year.
  • We see 13% upside to our July forecast for 2028 revenue from 10% more low NA EUV and DUVi capacity and a ~10% ASP uplift on throughput.

Stronger Order Visibility and Faster Capacity Growth

Order visibility is extending beyond 2028. Both DRAM and logic customers are requesting to place orders for 2029 and 2030, but ASML has yet to accept them because management wants a clearer picture of total demand first. In addition to backlog overflow, we believe this is also driven by customers’ stronger business prospects and a broadening customer mix. Management mentioned new DRAM customers on low NA EUV. We believe this includes Terafab. Despite faster DRAM growth, logic should remain the larger part of the business next year on strong advanced logic growth.

Non-current contract liabilities, a proxy for purchase orders, rose 160% QoQ to a record EUR9.3B in 3Q26. That is 1.1x quarterly system sales and 32% of trailing-12-month system sales, versus 14-17% in the prior five quarters (Fig 1). Management also validates demand against customers’ fab construction, installation timing and facility readiness, and believes the demand is real rather than inflated by slot-hoarding.

ASML is also becoming more proactive in expanding capacity. Management is considering increasing low NA EUV and DUVi capacity growth in 2028 to above 30%, and is confident that suppliers, including Zeiss, can support the growth it requires. Optimizing footprint can ensure capacity for 2028, while shortening product cycle time as the NXE:3800E matures is another lever. We believe robust demand and accelerating supply will drive fast growth beyond 2028.

Value-Based Pricing Supports Higher ASPs

ASML has completed price increase discussions and will be raising prices on the most critical products of the portfolio: we believe these are low NA EUV and DUVi. The effect will be partial in 2027 and full from 2028, though management does not plan to repeat this exercise every year. The Elec reported a 10% price increase on replacement parts for Korean customers from January 2027, which could serve as a reference.

Beyond the negotiated increase, ASML’s longer-term value-based pricing ties price to the value delivered, so throughput gains and next-generation upgrades lift ASPs on their own. We estimate that ASPs of next generation low NA EUV and DUVi will increase by 11% and 6% by 2028 vs. 2026 on throughput alone (Table 1). This will also help drive upgrade revenue as upgrades are priced off the tool price.

China Sales Rebound

China sales rebounded to 23% of net system sales in 3Q26, and FY2026 guidance suggests 2H26 China sales will be almost 2x of 1H26, in line with our estimate. More notably, when asked about 2027 and beyond, management said intrinsic demand from mainstream logic, memory and new fabs should keep China’s contribution at a similar level. At our current 2027-28 revenue forecasts, that would give China access to ~EUR34B of ASML tools and service in 2026-28 (Fig 2). This should help logic and memory capacity in China sustain fast growth.

Management sees little threat from Chinese challengers, noting muted development progress and motivation. Despite recent news on indigenous DUVi shipments, we have yet to see a meaningful update on China’s lithography supply chain, which we believe is key for DUV development. SiCarrier exhibited at WeSemiBay in Shenzhen this week but did not display a lithography tool at its booth. That said, management noted that, should export controls tighten, China would have strong motivation to push for its own DUVi. We believe the faster localization of non-litho semicaps is evidence of that.

More Upside in 2028

We see 13% upside to our July forecast for 2028 revenue of EUR72B from a further 10% capacity increase in low NA EUV and DUVi (to +40% in 2028) and a ~10% ASP increase driven by throughput (Table 2). That lifts 2028 revenue growth to ~46% YoY from ~30% in our July build, suggesting the upcycle keeps strengthening beyond 2028. We expect ASML to raise its long-term outlook at its Capital Markets Day on June 10, 2027.
