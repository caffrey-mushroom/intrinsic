Mauro Viviani (MV) Independent Consultant

Yanmei Tang (YT) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • 12-24-month automotive growth outlook and the importance of product diversification beyond traditional leather applications for Rino Mastrotto
  • Innovation opportunities across seat surfaces, materials, geometry and increasingly complex interior designs
  • Relative attractiveness of automotive vs luxury as the stronger medium-term growth engine
  • Credibility of the 17-18% EBITDA margin recovery target, given energy inflation, raw material volatility and automotive programme economics

Key Insights

  • Automotive demand is flat-to-down, with no meaningful inflection expected before late 2026 and potentially mid-2027, as European OEMs delay requests for quotation, cancel programmes and reduce planned volumes
  • Automotive programme volumes have been cut materially, including a Rino Mastrotto programme by c30-40%, while European leather content has fallen c30% vs five years ago as OEMs shift towards textiles
  • Expert believes Rino is competitively disadvantaged on pricing because raw hide acquisition costs represent 70-80% of production costs, yet its smaller scale prevents favourable purchasing terms
  • Improving automotive penetration likely requires accepting lower margins on initial programmes – price drives 60-70% sourcing decisions, while established suppliers benefit from stronger OEM relationships and purchasing scale
  • While luxury fashion offers higher pricing power and potential market share gains with Prada, Gucci and Louis Vuitton, volatile forecasting and structurally declining leather consumption mean growth might not fully offset automotive weakness
