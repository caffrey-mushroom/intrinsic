SUMMARY

Overview

The discussion assessed Hyro, a conversational AI provider offering voice and chat agents for hospitals and health systems. The expert previously managed a broad healthcare digital portfolio and directly oversaw Hyro’s deployment at a large health system.

Hyro initially supported website chat before expanding into patient-portal chat, appointment reminder calls, and voice automation across three major contact centers. Current capabilities include patient authentication, appointment information, rescheduling, cancellation, and call routing. Potential expansions include new-appointment scheduling and prescription-related services.

The health system selected Hyro after vendor research, demonstrations, stakeholder input, and an informal proposal process. Healthcare experience, Epic integration, implementation reliability, and platform effectiveness mattered more than price.

Key Insights

The expert considers Hyro the best available fit for the health system and rated it highly. Hyro’s early technical advantage came from a knowledge-graph architecture requiring relatively limited maintenance and training. Its strongest current advantages are rapid implementation, continued platform development, natural interactions, proven Epic integration, competitive pricing, and collaborative product development. Return on investment improved substantially after deployment, particularly through lower call abandonment. Call containment tends to stabilize until additional workflows are introduced. Hyro appears capable of scaling with the organization without requiring a replacement vendor. Switching platforms would be highly disruptive, although poor performance would still justify migration. Cultural compatibility and partnership quality were emphasized as important healthcare procurement considerations. Financial pressure makes pricing increasingly relevant for nonprofit health systems, but demonstrated healthcare performance remains the primary selection criterion.
