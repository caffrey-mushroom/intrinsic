SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, a Snowflake-focused services partner, described strong customer demand driven by data-platform modernization, AI adoption, and workload consolidation. Customers are migrating from fragmented databases and legacy SAP analytics systems, while some are moving machine-learning workloads from specialist platforms to Snowflake. The expert expects AI tools to accelerate both data access and engineering work, supporting higher consumption.

Key Insights

The expert said the partner’s latest quarter was its strongest to date, with sales and revenue exceeding plan. Growth was partly constrained by a shortage of experienced Snowflake architects and engineers. CoWork is generating more incremental platform consumption than CoCo because business users can more readily extract value from existing data. CoCo’s main impact is speeding up engineering work, bringing forward projects and related consumption. AI projects often face ROI scrutiny before deployment, but customer appetite tends to rise after initial agents demonstrate value. Improved controls over model access and usage are also helping customers adopt AI more confidently. Snowflake’s machine-learning capabilities have become more viable through steady product improvements and customer incentives. However, the expert does not expect Snowflake to dominate long-horizon autonomous-agent development; hyperscaler platforms remain strong contenders. The expert projected substantial growth for the partner, while noting that funding customer expansion through consolidation and migration takes time.

Strong fit for customers with traditional database environments and a growing choice for analytics, conversational AI, and some ML workloads. Its governance improvements and AI features support adoption.

The expert saw limited competitive pressure from Fabric, citing product-maturity concerns. Adoption appeared strongest among smaller organizations already committed to Microsoft’s ecosystem.

ClickHouse

Serves a narrower real-time analytics market. Snowflake’s interactive warehouse capabilities may help it compete for some of these workloads.

SageMaker, DataRobot, and Dataiku

Some customers are consolidating ML workloads from these products onto Snowflake.

Hyperscaler agent platforms

Often selected for autonomous, long-running agents, although Snowflake may still provide an important data platform underneath.
