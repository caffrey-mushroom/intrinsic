原标题：Talen Energy – Bilateral Data Centre Contracting Opportunities & Growth Strategy Execution Risks

#power #datacenter

Third Bridge · 2026-11-20

核心摘要

  1. Talen前起源业务高级总监（在职约五年）认为，美国中期选举后许多胜选者持反数据中心立场，PJM区域的并网排队和许可延迟将持续，希望在1-3年内供电的项目将以天然气表后发电为主要路径。
  2. 新园区会先用往复式发动机、航改机等小机组过渡，再逐步过渡到大型燃机和联合循环机组。
  3. PJM最近几次容量拍卖（BRA）均以价格上限出清，远期电价和容量价格维持高位，专家预计spark spread也将保持高位。
  4. 可靠性备用采购（RBP）目标从6.8GW可能大幅下调至1GW以下，降幅约90%，令IPP观望，也削弱了大负荷双边合同的市场流动性。
  5. 由于FERC裁定PJM的共址电价不公正不合理，Susquehanna与AWS最初的纯表后PPA模式已无法复制，表前VPPA成为主流，表后与表前合同的价差主要只是输配电费用。
  6. Talen除Susquehanna外没有其他零碳资产，其余多为中高热耗率燃气机组和老旧燃煤机组，对超大规模云厂商吸引力较低；且数据中心合同对三个9到五个9可用率的违约赔偿很高，管理层可能更倾向于保留商户敞口。
  7. 过去2-3年燃机设备成本涨幅超过电价涨幅，新建投资有在高点买入的风险，但专家认为管理层提出的混合PPA高十几的回报目标仍属合理。
  8. 专家对负荷预测偏谨慎，认为超大规模云厂商资本开支收缩和项目延期可能使负荷后移，而Solaris、VoltaGrid等分布式能源商因规模小、合同期短、度电成本高，长期对IPP构成的威胁有限。

Daniel Cohen (DC) VP, Power at PowerBridge LLC

Agenda

  • Talen Energy's (NASDAQ: TLN) growth opportunity set across traditional capacity markets and new bilateral large load offtakes
  • Ability to increase contractedness across key baseload assets, including limitations, given the data centre reliability needs
  • Key commercial risks and impacts on flywheel strategy execution

Key Insights

  • Post US midterm elections, gas-fired behind-the-meter generation may be the most viable option for data centres seeking time-to-power under three years, with off-grid reciprocating engines or small-scale turbines bridging data centres to grid-connected large frames
  • Delay and revision of forecasted demand for reliability backstop procurement could make independent power producers skittish, given inconsistent projections, and large-load contracting may be hit by reliability backstop procurement delays, weighing on Talen's ability to secure new data centre power purchase agreements
  • Expert believes original behind-the-meter power purchase agreement with Amazon Web Services for Susquehanna can't be replicated following Federal Energy Regulatory Commission rulings, and Talen's thermal assets are likely less-attractive to hyperscalers vs Susquehanna. Both factors may limit Flywheel Strategy execution
  • CAPEX growth above power market pricing growth means new-build investments risk "buying at or near a top," which may complicate Talen's ability to achieve "high-teens" returns on future hybrid power purchase agreement structures that call for powered land with new-build upside
