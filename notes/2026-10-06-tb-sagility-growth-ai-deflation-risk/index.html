Agenda

  • Sagility's (NSE: SAGILITY) revenue growth expectations – existing client wallet share expansion and mid-sized account diversification
  • Synchrony adoption outlook, AI deflation and pricing power
  • BroadPath, BirchAI and CareSeed integration progress, cross-sell realisation and future M&A strategy

Key Insights

  • Majority of clients expect 2-2.5% YoY productivity gains at contract signing, generally honouring terms throughout average 3-5-year duration. >75% productivity gains are passed to client, with revenue cannibalisation required to retain client and resist competition
  • Revenue is currently 70-75% effort-based contracts, 15-20% outcome-based and 2-10% subscription-based. YoY AI deflationary pressure expected to accelerate to 3-5% in five years. However, despite deflationary risk, expert doesn't expect a change in mix over five years due to client conservatism and Sagility prioritising revenue growth
  • Clinical service outsourcing expected to grow at 20-25% YoY vs 12-13% for base business, accelerating group revenue growth. 12-15-month sales cycles are a product of client resistance to outsourcing and domestic employment retention, which will slow realisation, although this is ultimately sourmountable through Sagility's stakeholder relationships
  • 25% EBITDA margin target is achievable over 2-3 years, factoring in AI deflationary risk. Earnings will likely be reinvested into growth and realising cross-sell opportunity
