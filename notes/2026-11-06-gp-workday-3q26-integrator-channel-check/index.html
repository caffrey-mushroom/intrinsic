核心摘要

  1. 一位系统集成商的全球销售总监表示其Workday业务全年18%的增长目标仍在正轨，Q4是最大季度，目标增速约21%。

  3. 高概率续约已基本全部完成，两笔最大交易分别为一笔续约和一笔新客户，预计在感恩节前签署。

  6. 在财务模块机会中替换SAP的成功率约为35%，年底可能接近40%，而Oracle财务系统因深度集成更难替换，且OCI业务仍有40%以上增长。
  7. 新客户销售周期普遍在9个月以内，部分10亿美元以下公司短至4个月，约40%至50%的Workday客户已在运行某种智能体工作流且比例在上升。
  8. FlexCredits对中小客户成交有帮助，大客户仍偏好传统许可模式；Sana推广慢于预期，Data Cloud尚未对业务产生实质影响。
  9. 专家预计明年其Workday业务增长至少15%，有望达到18%至20%，并认为Workday在AI上虽非领先但足以与对手抗衡，未来营销将以自动化为主题。

SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, a sales leader at a systems integrator, discussed demand for a major HR and finance software platform, renewals, implementation work, competition, and AI. The integrator remains on track for its growth target, supported by renewals, new customers, and expansion into additional products and regions. Customers are consolidating vendors and accepting longer contracts in exchange for better pricing and implementation support.

Key Insights

The expert expects the practice to meet its full-year growth goal, with the final quarter especially important. Larger opportunities include one renewal and one new customer. High-confidence renewals have largely closed. The expert described the platform as highly sticky, with customization and integration making replacements difficult. Most sales leads are generated through the integrator’s own outreach and network, rather than referrals from the platform vendor. AI is strengthening the vendor’s sales pitch, particularly through employee skills matching, automation, and potential reductions in recruiting and operating costs. The expert sees the vendor as competitive in AI, though not at the forefront. The expert expects automation to be a prominent part of the vendor’s future messaging.
