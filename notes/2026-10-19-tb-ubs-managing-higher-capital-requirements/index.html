原标题：UBS – What Options Remain for Managing Impacts From Higher Capital Requirements?

#financials

Third Bridge · 2026-10-19

Analyst-led • 13 Oct 2026

Companies: UBSG CH, HSBA GB, DBK DE, BARC GB, C US, BAC US, GS US, JPM US, BNP FR, MS US, BAER CH

Neil Shah (NS) Deputy Head, Treasury Risk at Raymond James Financial Inc

Max Harper (MH) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Implications from Swiss regulations, assessing impacts arising from recent developments in the Upper House of the Swiss Parliament
  • Potential levers for UBS (SWX: UBSG) to mitigate impacts from higher capital requirements, plus compromise plans or solutions, including AT1 (additional tier 1) usage
  • Competitive positioning arising from higher capital ratios

Key Insights

  • 90% common equity tier 1 requirement is essentially "window dressing". Swiss Parliament signalled it heard UBS's concerns but delivered a result significantly disadvantaging UBS vs other global systemically important banks. Headquarters relocation is primarily a negotiation tactic. Removing Swiss identity from UBS would leave it as "just another bank in Europe", stripping its core differentiation
  • UBS's Americas Wealth Management faces compounding pressure, with margins challenged vs wider market. Additional capital requirements may further shrink returns, with client outflows another casualty. Disposing of unit is an option, but unlikely, as it would prevent UBS from being a global provider
  • Prime brokerage, a high balance-sheet-intensive and low-margin business, is particularly vulnerable. Hedge funds already use multiple prime brokers and will quickly shift volume if UBS's pricing becomes uncompetitive
  • UBS's double leverage ratio is significantly lower than US peers — a largely overlooked metric suggesting it is less risky than the capital debate implies, contrary to regulatory narrative
