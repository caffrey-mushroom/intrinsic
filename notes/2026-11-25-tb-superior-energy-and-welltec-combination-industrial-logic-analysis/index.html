原标题：Superior Energy Services and Welltec International – Drilling Down on the Industrial Logic of the Combination

#oilfield-services #mna #well-intervention #deepwater #industrial-ai

Third Bridge · 2026-11-25

核心摘要

  1. Superior Energy Services在2020年破产重组后剥离高资本开支的钻杆与重型设备租赁业务并转向知识型服务，并于2026年9月签署协议收购Welltec International，借此切入更高毛利、更长周期的油井干预与全生命周期管理领域。
  2. Welltec核心竞争力在于井下牵引器、传感阵列及“水中穿线（wire in water）”轻型干预技术，主要服务高难度、高风险的深水海上油井（单井成本达数亿美元、钻机日费60万-70万美元且作业超100天，远高于西德克萨斯约100万美元的陆上井），并于2026年自建完井业务。
  3. 历史上油企（如Equinor）通常将钻井、完井与后期干预拆分为独立合同以控制支出，两家合并后Superior可结合自身连续油管和钢丝输送能力与Welltec技术覆盖全生命周期，但核心整合风险在于客户接受度以及跨ERP与授权体系的内部管控。
  4. 油服行业并购整合趋势明显，除斯伦贝谢（SLB）稳居龙头外，GoWell与WellSense的近期合并形成了类似的技术对标但仍依赖第三方输送；Welltec技术主要由其在北海及巴西、圭亚那深水市场内部自主研发与商业化。
  5. 工业AI（如斯伦贝谢收购Gyrodata后落地的闭环自主定向钻井）正通过将有效井筒完整性与油藏数据转化为决策洞察来削减人员和运营开销，油服企业固定资产及人员成本约占营收15%-18%，AI将在设备状态维护排期与ERP票据自动化中加速渗透。
  6. 近年来油价与钻井活动已逐渐脱钩，2027年新钻井总量可能延续长期下行趋势，但全球仍需每日1亿桶油气供给，支撑深水盆地（巴西、圭亚那）生产干预、油藏优化及地热、碳捕集（CCUS）地下封存井等衍生基建保持良好需求。

Agenda

  • Assessment of Superior Energy Services' announced acquisition of Welltec International
  • Technology differentiation, application in different O&G (oil and gas) wells and complementary oilfield services
  • Future development in drilling and completions technologies, including applying AI, data analysis and automation
  • Competitive landscape post-transaction and outlook for oilfield spending on key technologies

Key Insights

  • The Superior-Welltec combination is based on solid industrial logic, allows for Superior to access a new, more sophisticated customer base and leverages Welltec's recognised technology
  • Superior's post-bankruptcy strategy has seen a shift away from tool rental and drill pipe services to more knowledge-based work, which is where Welltec thrives
  • While Schlumberger will continue as the "powerhouse" in the space, Welltec has the track record to work on more challenging and expensive wells, developed in the Norway, but since expanded to Brazil and Guyana
  • Expert sees more consolidation in the oilfield services industry, citing WellSense and GoWell as players with technology that is attractive in the current environment
  • Drilling activity and oil prices have decoupled in recent years, so the run-up in commodity prices is unlikely to have a material impact on the 2027 industry growth. However, the outlook for production intervention, optimisation and life cycle management services is positive
