  • Convergence of oil sands project, federal military infrastructure and Dow's Edmonton project may create skilled labour shortages in Alberta, peaking around 2028-30
  • Expert believes Athabasca Oil signals further acquisitions by Cenovus targeted at underdeveloped oil sands assets with operating synergy upside, but Cenovus's ability to realise synergies will rely on leadership and succession planning over next 5-10 years
