Agenda

  • AI disruption, data commoditisation and service evolution
  • Customer demand trends and growth strategies
  • FP&A competitive landscape and dynamics across specialised providers vs low-cost alternatives in Australia

Key Insights

  • Expert notes that low-cost plug-ins are most vulnerable to AI disruption, while FP&A software's defensibility and core differentiation lie in ingesting disparate, unstructured datasets and enforcing strict enterprise security and governance controls
  • Customer churn is forecast to c2x over next 2-3 years as clients experiment with custom point solutions, though deep accumulated IP prevents attrition
  • Expert comments that mid-market share shifts are captured through vertical specialisation, projecting Phocas to gain 1% market share increase over next 2-3 years and 30% ARR growth over next one year. Phocas' core capabilities lie in its deep distribution expertise and potential expansion into integrated workflows such as inventory management and rebates
  • Data commoditisation from tools such as Microsoft Fabric remains 3-5 years away for mid-market customers, as organisations lack internal resources and data expertise to stage complex data, preserving demand for specialised platforms such as Phocas and Prophix
