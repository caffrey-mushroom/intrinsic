原标题：VodafoneZiggo's Dutch Defence – Safeguarding Market Share Against Escalating Fibre Competition

#networking

Third Bridge · 2026-10-21

Analyst-led • 15 Oct 2026

Companies: VOD GB, KPN NL, LBTY.A US, CRM US

Koen Cluistra (KC) Chief Commercial Officer at Open Dutch Fiber

Albie Amankona (AA) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • VodafoneZiggo's gigabit cable upgrades – modernising network infrastructure to halt subscriber churn to KPN's (AMS: KPN) fibre network
  • Subscriber retention via convergence – leveraging fixed-mobile bundles to defend market share against Odido
  • Cost structure optimisation – streamlining operations and service automation to protect profit margins against price wars
  • B2B and SMB service expansion – scaling managed IT and communications services to compensate for declining pay-TV revenues

Key Insights

  • Expert expects VodafoneZiggo to reach 30-31% mobile market share and 36-37% fixed market share in the next 12-24 months
  • VodafoneZiggo could return to revenue growth within two years, driven by base expansion and annual inflation-linked price corrections across fixed and mobile subscribers
  • Aggressive promotional activity will likely continue for at least four consecutive quarters before stabilisation
  • Liberty Global and Vodafone Group historically "treated VodafoneZiggo as a cash cow", extracting EBITDA to fund struggling operations in Germany and Spain, directly causing the base decline that is now being reversed
  • Cross-border synergies are likely overstated for the Telenet-Ziggo combination. Given that both are already Liberty assets, expert thinks a formal merger offers minimal value-add, and cites Vodafone Group's failed attempt to consolidate a single European voicemail platform, eventually reverting to country-by-country operations
