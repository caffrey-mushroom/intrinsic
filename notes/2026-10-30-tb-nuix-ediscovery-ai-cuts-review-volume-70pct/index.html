Key Insights

  • Expert notes document review represents c70% of total eDiscovery expenses due to human attorneys reviewing documents, while collection, processing and hosting comprise remainder. Modern AI workflows reduce raw data volumes c70%, with anticipated further reduction of 10-20% in the next 2-3 years compressing downstream review costs
  • AI adoption in eDiscovery has surged from 20-30% three years ago to over 70% today. Expert observes that top 10 service providers have expanded dedicated analytics staff >60% as upstream data reduction becomes more critical than previously, emphasising that companies able to effectively integrate analytics can "change the game"
  • Expert notes that Nuix remains the go-to processing platform for large-scale projects while expanding enterprise solutions and strategic partnerships, positioning it to challenge Relativity in review
  • Legal software purchasing is transitioning from per-gigabyte processing charges to predictable enterprise subscriptions. Expert estimates client retention to be 70%-plus
