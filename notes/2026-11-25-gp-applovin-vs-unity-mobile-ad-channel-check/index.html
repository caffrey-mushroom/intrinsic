核心摘要

  1. 手游开发及买量顾问Boteco Games联合创始人Ben Riguet Bassa表示，APP（AppLovin）仍是体量与ROAS综合表现最佳的买量渠道，其4Q26预算占比由1Q的40%-45%下调至30%-35%纯属分散单渠道风险，而非效果下滑。
  2. APP 4Q26同比投放仍增长10%-15%且2027年预计至少再增5%-10%；Axon算法升级在混合变现（IAA+IAP）Day-28活动表现更优，美区混合/IAP类CPM达200美元（纯广告类Day-28为30美元），电商广告主竞价虽推高买量成本但也同步抬升变现端CPM。
  3. APP平台透明度下降且素材疲劳显著加速：去年每周5个新创意概念即可，今年需4倍至每周15-20个概念（行业甚至测试利用AI迭代实现月产1,000个新素材），且Axon测试新素材偏慢，需运营手动分拆活动或找客户经理导流。
  4. GOOGL已跃居第二大买量渠道（占比由年初5%-10%升至15%），凭借Android端低CPI与Firebase数据回传保持极高周度稳定性（iOS端仍较弱），ROAS逐步逼近APP。
  5. META表现显著走弱（占比降至10%-12%，预计2027年进一步跌至8%-10%）：强制推行Advantage+并收回分版位与性别定向控制后，CPI高企（某游戏达12美元vs他网5美元）且周度留存波动剧烈（27%跳至38%），ROAS跌至2021年iOS SKAN政策冲击以来最差。
  6. U（Unity）份额升至10%（同比翻倍但基数极小，预计2027年维持5%-10%）：新推Day-28活动因竞价者少出现CPI比APP便宜2-3倍的窗口红利，但9月接入运行时引擎数据仅带来至多5%-10%提升，且加预算后ROAS迅速衰减、单组素材上限仅6-7个，专家判断其无法超越APP。
  7. Mintegral目前占比仅数个百分点但2027年有望冲击15%（多为增量预算），惟目前存在低留存子渠道却产生高营收的异常流量质量与过多图片广告问题待排查；Liftoff与Moloco合并上限约10%，难以如APP或GOOGL般规模化扩量。

SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, who advises game publishers and operates a portfolio of mobile games, discussed user acquisition across advertising networks. The portfolio spans casual, puzzle, board, hyper-casual, and real-money games, monetized through ads, in-app purchases, or both.

AppLovin remains the strongest network overall by combined scale and return. Google has gained importance, particularly on Android, while Unity’s newer campaigns have attracted spend with low acquisition costs. Meta’s performance has weakened, and the expert is evaluating whether Mintegral can become a larger channel despite concerns about traffic quality.

Key Insights

AppLovin leads on scale and returns. Spend was reduced to diversify away from dependence on one network, not because its performance deteriorated. The expert may increase allocation during the competitive holiday advertising period. Google is improving through better campaign management. The expert attributed gains mainly to more detailed placement analysis and creative testing, rather than major platform changes. Google is steadier than Meta and performs particularly well on Android. Meta’s results are worsening. Higher acquisition costs, reduced placement and demographic controls, and volatile results have weakened its appeal. The expert expects its share to decline or remain limited. Unity is a promising but uncertain challenger. Its day-28 campaigns can offer much cheaper acquisition than AppLovin, but returns weaken as spend rises. The expert does not expect Unity to become the leading network. Mintegral offers potential, with quality concerns. The expert reported unusual patterns in some low-retention traffic and image- heavy ad delivery, and plans to investigate before scaling. Creative volume is increasingly important. AppLovin campaigns fatigue faster, and the expert reported that more creative testing can improve returns.
