Agenda

  • Claims inflation expectations and pricing outlook
  • EV policy return profile and underwriting factors
  • Smash repair market conditions and supply chain risks

Key Insights

  • Claims inflation to stay elevated at 7-9% over the next 3-5 years. While frequency remains stable, severity will increase from labour shortages, higher repair complexity and hire-car costs. Conversely, parts inflation, towing charges and storage costs may come down
  • Expert observes 5-10% softer premiums vs 12-24 months prior. Insurers will likely raise standard excesses and tighten hire-car benefits to stabilise margins
  • EV repair costs track 25-35% higher than internal combustion engine counterparts due to specialised tooling and labour requirements. This gap may persist but narrow with investment in EV repair capabilities
  • Insurers find corporate groups cheaper than independent smash repairers. Independents, representing a large proportion of total repair capacity, will undergo further consolidation. Non-aligned insurers are offering independents self-assessing capabilities and 24-hour payments to remain competitive
  • Smash repair labour shortage persists, though corporates and insurers provide training and sponsor international labour to address it. AMA outbids independents on skilled labour compensation to attract talent
