  1. AWS与Azure合作伙伴Stratus10联合创始人兼CTO Kevin Risonchu表示，中端及中小企业（SMB）AI采纳呈现明显“二八分化”：约80%普通员工直接满足于默认捆绑工具（M365配MSFT Copilot、Google Workspace配Gemini），决策核心是现有账单与账户粘性。

  3. AI单用户实际支出快速膨胀：Stratus10客户的Claude活跃用户月均花费已达约210美元（含超额用量，较6个月前明显抬升，远超20-30美元基础席位及Anthropic对外口径的75美元均值），重度用户在120-125美元套餐外甚至另设约500美元超额预算。

  5. 但厂商力推的按量计费（usage-based）遭遇强烈抵制：Stratus10用内部账本对每月重复运行的常规任务进行测算，纯API按Token计费的成本高达固定席位订阅的约20倍，企业担忧像早期上云一样出现账单失控，极度偏好可预测的月费。
  6. 作为折中，Anthropic目前为20-25美元及100美元席位附赠等额API Token额度以培育使用习惯，而Amazon Q则通过任务路由对前沿模型施加更高消耗乘数；专家警告若取消席位制且赠送额度不够日常使用，将引发大客户砍价或迁移。
  7. 数据安全与模型路由加速向公有云回流：几乎所有客户均关闭模型训练遥测并开始将OpenAI/Anthropic直连工作流迁至AMZN Bedrock与MSFT Foundry；高阶用户频繁在Opus 5.5、GPT-6.2及DeepSeek、Kimi等开源模型间切换，专家预计2027上半年Bedrock等AI工作负载将继续增长，但短期仍重在提升人均效率而非大规模裁员。

SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, who supports cloud and AI deployments for customers using AWS and Azure, described adoption of workplace assistants and coding tools among small and mid-sized businesses. Microsoft Copilot often benefits from existing Microsoft 365 relationships, while engineering teams more actively compare Claude, ChatGPT, open-source models, and coding assistants. The discussion covered subscription versus usage-based pricing, productivity gains, data controls, and competitive positioning.

Key Insights

Predictable pricing remains a strong preference. The expert’s internal modeling found API usage could cost roughly 20 times as much as subscriptions for repeat workloads. Customers worry that metered consumption makes budgets harder to forecast and could lead to unexpectedly high bills. Bundling and incumbency shape broad adoption. Most employees use the assistant included with their company’s existing platform. The expert estimated that this suits about 80% of employees, while developers and other power users are more likely to select tools based on output quality and cost. AI is increasing throughput, not necessarily reducing headcount. One customer used AI to speed up contract review, clearing a backlog in about a month and enabling same-day turnaround. The expert expects companies to use AI to help staff accomplish more, with hiring still needed to support expansion. Usage-based pricing could face resistance if included credits do not support the work users already perform. Larger customers may negotiate or reconsider vendors if seat plans are reduced or removed. Data control is an increasing concern. Customers commonly disable model-training telemetry and are moving some API workloads to AWS Bedrock or Microsoft Foundry to keep data within their preferred environments.
