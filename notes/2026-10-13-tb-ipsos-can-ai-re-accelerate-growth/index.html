Companies: IPS FR, YOU GB

Albie Amankona (AA) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Ipsos's (EPA: IPS) organic growth outlook following the rebound in Q2 2026 and recovery in client spending
  • How could AI, automation and new research platforms reshape delivery, pricing and competitive positioning?
  • Prospects for the Horizons strategy and recent acquisitions to drive market share gains and margin expansion
  • Ipsos's competitive outlook vs NielsenIQ (NYSE: NIQ), Kantar and other global insights and data providers

Key Insights

  • Tech spending, estimated at 30-40% of Ipsos's revenue, remains soft due to AI disruption threatening traditional tech firms, creating significant headwinds for overall organic growth recovery
  • Smaller AI companies are very aggressive with multiple new entrants coming online in Europe, partly explaining why EMEA growth remains flat vs stronger Americas and APAC performance
  • C30% of traditional consumer research spend is at risk from AI-native disruptors using synthetic data, digital twins and AI-moderated interviews, particularly threatening commoditised CPG-focused research offerings
  • The Horizons plan involves a USD 1bn investment spread over four years, implying cUSD 250m annually. However, meaningful margin or growth improvements are unlikely before 2028, with full realisation closer to 2030
