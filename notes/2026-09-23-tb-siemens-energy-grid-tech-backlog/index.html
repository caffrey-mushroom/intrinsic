Agenda

  • Margin peak vs structural re-rating and if 18%–20% is sustainable
  • Execution risk on massive HVDC (high-voltage direct current) megaprojects
  • Supply constraints and CAPEX discipline – organic expansion vs bottlenecks
  • Competitive fortress of four key OEMs

Key Insights

  • Sell-side analyst projections of Grid Tech revenue dropping from EUR 25bn 2030 to EUR 16bn by 2033 are conservative – "I would be very surprised to see it significantly drop off"
  • Sustainable EBITDA margins for Grid Tech expected to stabilise 15-20%, with utilities increasingly vocal about supplier profiteering potentially capping further margin expansion beyond current levels
  • Hyosung's new Korean high-voltage direct current factory and first deployment with KEPCO means a legitimate non-Chinese fourth supplier could emerge, but meaningful European market share impact remains 7-10 years away
  • Sovereign security concerns are intensifying politically, making the TenneT-China project likely a one-off, with deep resistance to Chinese control systems in critical national infrastructure growing stronger
  • Retiring specialists represent the single greatest industry bottleneck, with roughly one person joining for every 5-10 leaving, and commissioning engineers requiring 10-plus years to train. Power transformer manufacturing remains almost entirely handcraft
