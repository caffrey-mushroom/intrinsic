SUMMARY

Overview

The expert discussed SS&C’s fund administration and wealth technology businesses, including how customer demand, implementation work, business-process outsourcing (BPO), and AI could affect performance beyond assets under management. The expert previously led technology delivery at SS&C and held a senior role at competitor Bravura.

Fund administration platforms are mature and valued for reliability and uptime. Wealth platforms, particularly in the UK and Australia, are seeing more demand for new features and complex client-specific solutions. Large implementations can combine software, migration, and outsourced administration.

Key Insights

Bundled technology and BPO are a strong sales proposition. They can help clients reduce costs and simplify operations, while generating service-based revenue for providers. However, providers must price complex implementation work appropriately to avoid cost overruns. Wealth offers more growth opportunity, but also more delivery risk. Implementations can be large and strategically valuable, yet complexity can make them costly or delayed. Fund administration is stable but appears to have a limited-growth market. The expert attributed SS&C’s reported share losses to actual share changes, but did not identify a company-specific cause. AI is more likely to improve existing systems than replace them soon. It could help document legacy systems and improve productivity, but regulated platforms require many specialized skills and extensive testing. The moderator’s questions about AI-driven system replacement prompted the expert to emphasize the complexity of rebuilding core fund and wealth platforms.
