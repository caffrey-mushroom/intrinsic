SUMMARY

Overview

A senior executive at a premium, digitally focused European sports retailer discussed third-quarter 2026 running-footwear trends across more than 23 markets. Performance running sales fell well below expectations as demand normalized after the pandemic-era expansion, inventories rose across the market, and promotions intensified.

Nike and Adidas lost momentum in premium specialty channels, while ASICS, Salomon, Puma, and Altra gained traction. Inventory levels at the retailer remain healthy following order cancellations, supplier returns, and heavier discounting.

Key Insights

Running footwear declined for the first time in the retailer’s tracking, despite an expected high-single-digit increase. Consumers increasingly prioritize meaningful product innovation over brand loyalty. Marketwide excess inventory has increased discounting and pressured profitability. ASICS was the strongest major partner, led by premium road-running models. Adidas growth in broader channels was viewed as heavily distribution- and promotion-led rather than innovation-led, raising concerns about sustainability. Hoka demand remains strong, but European logistics constraints materially limited sales. The expert expects growth to recover through faster inventory reallocation toward innovative, smaller brands. Premium pricing remains accepted by consumers, although the expert believes some products are priced above their underlying value.
