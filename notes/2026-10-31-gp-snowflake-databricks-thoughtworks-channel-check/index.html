SUMMARY

Overview

The expert described enterprise spending and implementation trends across Snowflake, Databricks, AI models, and cloud providers. Demand for AI projects remains strong, but production deployments depend heavily on data quality, modernization, governance, and cost control. Clients are increasingly combining platform capabilities rather than selecting a single vendor for every workload.

Key Insights

The expert said the quarter met plan, with demand strong enough at points to turn away work. A seasonal slowdown was followed by a strong September. AI projects are often categorized as AI, although much of the delivery involves data engineering. The expert is cross-training teams and prioritizing people who can connect technical decisions to business economics. Databricks is gaining traction in agentic AI and semantic-layer use cases, while Snowflake remains relevant for governed data and AI-ready migrations. The expert sees potential for both platforms in one architecture. No single model or agent control plane suits every client. Model selection should reflect the task, underlying data, governance needs, and total operating cost. Production AI value depends more on data readiness and architecture than simply adopting the newest model. Cost forecasting remains difficult, and optimization requires use-case-specific testing. Consulting engagements face pressure to deliver more for less. The expert expects compressed deal sizes and a shift toward outcome-based pricing, though no standard approach has emerged.

Platform or provider

Positioning and observations

Snowflake

Retains strengths in data management and governance. The expert cited strong product-market performance and described opportunities in AI-ready data migrations.

AWS is offering aggressive migration funding, including upfront consulting support. Microsoft is also funding migrations, with more deal-by-deal evaluation.

AI model providers

Clients increasingly compare models by task and outcome, including open-weight options; model choice alone is not usually decisive.

Migration incentives
