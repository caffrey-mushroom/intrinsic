Expert Director & CSO (Chief Strategy Officer) at NNI Technologies KK

Ken Morozumi (KM) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Fujikura's (TYO: 5803) optical fibre demand in data centre GPU clusters
  • Competitive advantages and market share in scale-out and scale-across vs Furukawa Electric (TYO: 5801), Sumitomo Electric Industries (TYO: 5802), Corning (NYSE: GLW) and Prysmian (BIT: PRY)
  • Next-generation CPO (co-packaged optics) and NPO (near-packaged optics) positioning and possible market share outlook

Key Insights

  • Optical fibre market to grow 30-40% annually. Adoption rate of cables with over 6,000 fibres set to surge from 25% to 45%; market share of ultra-compact connectors such as multimedia connect to expand to 30-45%. In scale-out, Fujikura and Corning each oligopolistically hold 35% share
  • Fujikura's US subsidiary AFL's key strengths are strong customer base and flexible design capabilities. In scale-across, both companies hold 25-35%; Fujikura aims to maintain high market share by leveraging first-mover advantage in a system that reduces installation time – combining the world's leading fusion splicer with SpiderWeb Ribbon cable – and its maintenance network
  • Fujikura's overall CPO market share is forecast at 15-25%, with multimedia connect at 15-30% and Panda fibre at 10-20%. The company focuses on optical connection components, and its in-house cold plates are also a strength

Consequently, the CAGR for fibre growth does not align with the overall capacity figures, which include power demand; yet this discrepancy is consistently omitted from survey reports and various evaluation sheets, resulting in very rough estimates.
