Third Bridge · 2026-10-09

Analyst-led • 7 Oct 2026

Companies: SOI FR, 4063 JP, 6488 TW

David Herisson (DH) Executive, Sales & Business Development, Advance Imaging at Exosens

Saad Maqsood (SM) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Photonics SOI (silicon-on-insulator) production momentum and customer qualification timelines across 300mm substrate lines
  • Demand pull-through from high-speed AI data centre interconnects and optical transceiver adoption
  • Manufacturing capacity allocation between Singapore and Bernin to offset soft RF-SOI (radio frequency SOI) mobile demand
  • Long-term inventory normalisation trends and fab utilisation rates across global manufacturing sites

Key Insights

  • Expert believes Soitec's strongest competitive position lies in co-packaged optics rather than transceivers, where generic designs make differentiation harder and competition from 6-7x cheaper alternatives remains more relevant
  • Co-packaged optic integration remains primary bottleneck for Photonics SOI growth, but it may be ready on the market in 1-2 years
  • Lithium niobate works as alternative at low volumes but isn't proven at scale, and incompatibility with standard complementary metal oxide semiconductor manufacturing lines makes near-term widespread adoption difficult
  • Soitec's key competitive advantage is maintaining high quality at massive scale, something neither Shin-Etsu nor GlobalWafers can currently match in high-volume manufacturing. Shin-Etsu is a Soitec shareholder, deliberately restraining volume growth, while GlobalWafers receives significant US government backing to build independence
  • Piezo-on-insulator substrates carry most of end-product's added value, requiring 3-4 lithography levels vs 30-40 for RF, making the substrate itself the primary performance differentiator, unlike other Soitec products
