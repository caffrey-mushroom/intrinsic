Agenda

  • Siemens Energy (ETR: ENR) – lost synergies and potential operational carve-out challenges
  • End market dynamics and key growth opportunities
  • Margin profile driven by contract structure and execution risk
  • Capacity build-out requirements and capital allocation needs
  • Potential buyers for the ToI (Transformation of Industry) unit, diligence and valuation drivers

Key Insights

  • Three segments of Siemens Energy's ToI business are compressors – the largest – followed by small steam turbines and new energy technologies such as hydrogen. Oil and gas represents dominant end market. Revenue splits c60% OEM and 40% aftermarket services
  • Siemens Energy's gas turbine boom indirectly drives ToI's steam turbine demand, given combined cycle plant configurations require steam turbines for roughly a third of generation. Carving out ToI may have "some" impact on this previous synergy, given steam turbines generate 30-40% of ToI revenue. Expert estimates 5-10% of that is derived from combined cycle projects
  • 2028 margin target of 12-14% vs today's 11.3% is characterised as aggressive but achievable, requiring selective customer focus and factory efficiency improvements
  • Small modular reactors represent underappreciated growth opportunity for ToI's steam turbines, as every reactor requires steam turbines and 5-10% of small modular reactor CAPEX may be steam turbine-related
