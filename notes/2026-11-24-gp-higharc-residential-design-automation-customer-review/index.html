SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, an experienced residential construction and purchasing professional, described why a regional luxury homebuilder adopted Higharc and how it compares with other software. The company wanted to increase production without substantially expanding staff. Higharc combines home design, estimating, sales configuration, and construction documentation in one platform.

Key Insights

The expert considers Higharc unusually advanced for single-family homebuilding, saying competing tools generally cover only parts of the workflow. Automated takeoffs and customer-specific plans reduce repetitive work across estimating, purchasing, sales, and permitting. The expert estimates a typical plan estimate that previously took at least a couple of weeks can now be reviewed in about 10 minutes. The expert put the minimum overall savings at roughly $3,500 per home, while noting that the full benefit is difficult to calculate. Higharc’s main drawback is its relative immaturity. However, the expert praised its frequent improvements and customer support. The expert expects adoption to continue expanding, citing strong interest among builders and peers.
