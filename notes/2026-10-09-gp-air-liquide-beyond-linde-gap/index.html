SUMMARY

Overview

The discussion assessed Air Liquide’s BEYOND plan, which aims to narrow its profitability gap with Linde and Air Products. The expert considered the margin objective credible but execution-intensive, with cost reductions and productivity improvements offering greater near-term certainty than acquisitions or new capital projects.

Industrial gases remain supported by electronics, healthcare and selected regional growth. However, weak manufacturing, high energy costs, geopolitical uncertainty and delayed customer investment decisions create substantial downside risk.

Key Insights

Air Liquide must act quickly and aggressively because major industrial-gas projects typically require several years before contributing to earnings. Cost reductions, organizational simplification, asset optimization and disciplined pricing are the most dependable margin levers. The sales-growth target appears challenging because traditional manufacturing demand is generally subdued. Semiconductor demand can support growth, particularly where projects are already contracted. A cyclical correction could materially reduce planned investment. Healthcare offers durable demand, but its ability to generate targeted margins is less certain. Energy-transition projects face delays and cancellations as policy support weakens and customers postpone final investment decisions. Air Liquide’s central disadvantage is considered cultural and operational rather than geographic. It lacks the pricing discipline, accountability and asset-density focus associated with Linde’s legacy Praxair model. Activist pressure is expected to remain intense and could eventually affect leadership if measurable progress is insufficient.
