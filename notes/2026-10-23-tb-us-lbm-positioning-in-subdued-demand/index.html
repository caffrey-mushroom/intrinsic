原标题：US LBM – Competitive Positioning as Demand Remains Subdued

#housing #industrials

Third Bridge · 2026-10-23

Analyst-led • 15 Oct 2026

Companies: BLDR US, QXO US

James Abbott (JA) VP, Engineered Wood Products at 84 Lumber Co

Patrick Ricciardi (PR) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • US building materials distribution industry outlook and demand
  • US LBM's positioning vs Builders FirstSource (NYSE: BLDR), Carter Lumber and other peers
  • Market share shifts, margin expansion potential and key pressures
  • Near-to-mid-term outlook

Key Insights

  • For 2027, "I think we're hopeful that things remain relatively static." Expert would look forward to 6-9 months of stability before seeing any meaningful recovery. In terms of interest rates, "[w]e may be stuck here for another 12-18 months"
  • Distributors' margins are "operating more or less at a breakeven level" without much more room to go down. "[A]nything north of 3% growth in starts, you would see margin expansion", but not towards pandemic-era levels
  • Industry "disruptors" that are willing to accept lower margins, such as Carter or Contract Lumber, can drive pricing compression and gain market share. Service and price drive market share shifts, which US LBM has lost
  • US LBM doesn't have a real identity. Acquired companies brought in unnecessary expense redundancy that wasn’t rationalised as Builders FirstSource does. Operational strengths aren't replicated across markets. "I don't believe they have a competitive moat"
