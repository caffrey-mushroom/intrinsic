Key Insights

  • Global radiology AI market is expected to expand from USD 4bn-5bn to USD 13bn over next five years, primarily driven by a critical shortage of radiologists and growing momentum in workflow optimisation
  • While current AI productivity improvements plateau at around 30% after two years of use, any vendor capable of refining its product to cut reading time by 50% would achieve a massive market breakthrough
  • Lunit is adopting an annual SaaS model featuring usage-based billing. Given that it charges a per-click fee for each case rather than a flat rate per radiologist, these companies are highly incentivised to maximise radiologist engagement on every study
  • Although securing the first installation offers a strong retention advantage, constant US healthcare consolidation remains a significant threat. When large networks acquire smaller clinics, they remove existing third-party AI platforms such as Lunit to standardise operations around their own preferred systems
