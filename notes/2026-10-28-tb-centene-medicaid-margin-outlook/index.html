Agenda

  • Medicaid rate trend mismatch and glide path to rate recovery
  • Centene (NYSE: CNC) – D-SNP (dual special needs plan) competitive positioning and outlook for membership growth
  • OBBBA (One Big Beautiful Bill Act) implications across states, members and providers, plus Centene's operational levers
  • Runway across payment integrity, FWA (fraud, waste and abuse) and utilisation management levers
  • AI deployment, implications for medical costs and G&A leverage

Key Insights

  • Increased provider gold carding as PR exercise in current regulatory environment exposes Centene to future margin risks, with such programmes often leading to utilisation abuse, as seen at United historically
  • Decentralised model with local CEOs is Centene's key strength, although high CEO turnover and recent layoffs potentially threaten this advantage, given the need to manage through complex OBBBA changes
  • Payment integrity is perhaps primary lever for payers going forwards, with potential to realise USD 500m annual run rate savings. However, provider AI utilisation is bulletproofing its coding, shifting battleground to far more contested and complex clinical necessity realm
  • Centene's flagship duals strategy faces potential disruption if work requirements impact Medicaid status, therefore impacting dual eligibility
  • Possibility for states to make heavy actuarial assumptions around the extent to which managed care can bend the cost trend is a key risk to Medicaid margin recovery and state budget pressures being passed through
