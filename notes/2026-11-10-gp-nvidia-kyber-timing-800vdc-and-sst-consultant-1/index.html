SUMMARY

Overview

The expert discussed expected timing for NVIDIA’s Kyber rack system, data-center adoption of 800VDC power, solid-state transformer (SST) readiness, and the supplier landscape. The expert drew on nearly two decades in power and renewable energy, including work on large-scale power projects and data-center infrastructure.

Key Insights

Kyber volume shipments were expected in Q3–Q4 2028, with supply, design, thermal, and manufacturing challenges presenting risks. Initial CSP procurement and deployments could begin around mid-2028, ahead of broader volume shipments. The expert expects ±400V bipolar systems to remain more common in the near term because they use existing infrastructure and offer flexibility. Adoption of 800VDC is expected to build gradually, supported by rack power needs, certification, and technical validation. SST commercialization around 2028 was viewed as overly ambitious; the expert suggested it could shift to Q2–Q3 2029. Pilot- stage deployment, high costs, and unresolved qualification and reliability issues were cited as barriers. The expert identified online hot-swap, particularly replacing modules under load, as a key SST readiness gap. Certification and testing under demanding operating conditions would be needed before broad procurement. Commercialization timelines were described as the most overestimated aspect of the market. Certification, qualification, and cost impacts were identified as underappreciated risks.
