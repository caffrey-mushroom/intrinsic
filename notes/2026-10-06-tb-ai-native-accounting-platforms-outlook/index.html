Key Insights

  • AI-native ledger platforms such as Puzzle, Digits and Kick hold negligible market share – well under 1% – compared to QuickBooks' 7-8 million customers and Xero's roughly one million
  • "Puzzle was started seven years ago. It's basically using what is already considered an outdated software stack […] it was never an AI-native company". "Puzzle has no idea what it's trying to become strategically […] it's just trying to survive at this point"
  • Roughly 50% of AI accounting market growth is flowing towards workflow reconciliation tools, benefiting players such as Basis, with general ledgers and vertical extensions splitting the remaining 50% roughly equally
  • PE-backed accounting firm roll-ups aren't adopting next-gen ledgers because these platforms can't handle diverse business types beyond venture-backed software startups
  • Expert thinks Digits or Puzzle won't be discussed in three years – a new set of companies will enter the market with agentic coding
