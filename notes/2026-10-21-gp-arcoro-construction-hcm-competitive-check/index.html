SUMMARY

Overview

The discussion examined construction-focused payroll, HR, timekeeping, safety, and compliance software, with emphasis on Arcoro’s competitive position against Miter, HH2, Lumber, and other vendors.

Construction is difficult for general-purpose providers because of field-based workforces, state-specific labor rules, overtime requirements, fragmented systems, and slow technology adoption. Contractors also favor products with established industry references, creating a substantial barrier for new entrants.

Arcoro primarily serves small and midsize contractors. Its ExakTime time-and-attendance product established its reputation, while broader HR and payroll modules have expanded its addressable market.

Key Insights

The expert estimates that more than 60% of contractors with roughly 5–200 employees still capture field information manually, leaving considerable digitization potential. Only about 20%–25% appear to operate substantially automated workflows. Arcoro’s principal advantages are longevity, strong contractor recognition, broad payroll and ERP connectivity, and industry partnerships. Its key weakness is the absence of product trials. The expert believes this causes lost deals because buyers increasingly expect hands-on evaluation before signing multiyear contracts. Miter appears to be gaining share, potentially through trade-specific targeting, flexible configuration, attractive pricing, or easier product evaluation. Products are often functionally comparable. Consequently, integrations, implementation, support, and price frequently determine purchasing decisions. Established vendors remain well positioned, but venture-backed competitors can sustain aggressive pricing and tolerate product shortcomings while pursuing growth.
