SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, a technology leader overseeing companies in several sectors, discussed how AI agents are changing cybersecurity needs and purchasing decisions. The main topics were identity controls, endpoint protection, data recovery, vendor competition, and the expected direction of security spending. The expert’s central concern was that many organizations are adopting AI agents faster than they are learning to manage their access and risks.

Key Insights

AI agents can expand the attack surface and cause harm when granted excessive permissions. The expert said organizations need to contain agents, monitor their behavior, and use AI tools to help analyze security alerts at scale. Identity management is becoming an important way to control agent access. The expert favored passwordless, permission- based access that limits what both people and agents can reach. Security spending is increasing, but budgets are finite. The expert expects organizations to reconsider AI subscriptions or agent deployments if costs rise without adequate controls. Many companies have yet to properly manage or budget for agent security. The expert expects spending to accelerate as organizations encounter incidents and better recognize the risks. The expert favors specialized products where performance matters, while noting that integrated platforms can offer convenience and lower costs. Choosing between them depends on an organization’s risk tolerance, compliance needs, and budget.

Competitive Landscape

11/11/2026 at 19:42 PM UTC

Company or product

Expert’s assessment and positioning

CrowdStrike Falcon

Viewed as proactive in endpoint detection and response, with strong incident-response capabilities. Its added cost compared with bundled Microsoft security is a consideration, and the expert noted potential product gaps without detailing them.

Microsoft Defender and Sentinel

Offers an integrated suite and can be cost-effective for Microsoft customers. The expert considered Defender’s response slower than CrowdStrike’s, but expects Microsoft to improve. Sentinel was preferred over CrowdStrike’s SIEM for a broader view.

Okta

Favored for identity controls that can limit agent permissions and reduce password-related risks. The expert sees its privileged-access product as a likely add-on for existing customers, rather than an early replacement for established specialists.

Microsoft Entra, Ping Identity, Cisco Duo

Entra benefits from being included in many Microsoft plans, particularly in cost-sensitive sectors. Okta was described as a more common choice in some other industries.

BeyondTrust and Delinea

Considered established privileged-access products with useful logging and compliance capabilities. Replacing them would require substantial infrastructure and workflow changes.

Rubrik

Selected for restoring data affected by agent errors or other changes. The expert cited pricing as a deciding factor; alternatives mentioned included Cohesity, Veeam, Commvault, and Druva.

ThreatLocker

Identified as a notable competitor in endpoint security. The expert did not provide detailed product comparisons.
