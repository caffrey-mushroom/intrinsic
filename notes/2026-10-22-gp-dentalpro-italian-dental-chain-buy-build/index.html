SUMMARY

Overview

The discussion assessed DentalPro’s position within Italy’s highly fragmented private dental market. Independent dentists still provide most services, while branded dental networks have a limited share concentrated in major and midsized cities.

Growth has moderated, encouraging large platforms to shift from opening new clinics toward acquiring independent practices in underserved areas. Patient demand is also moving away from higher-margin implants toward prevention, hygiene, cosmetic treatments, conservative dentistry, and clear-aligner orthodontics.

Key Insights

DentalPro and Primo CareDent were identified as the two leading platforms, with consolidation expected to continue through acquisitions or potential combinations among larger networks. Brand recognition is increasingly more important than an individual dentist’s reputation because patients value broad services, consistent pricing, and institutional trust. DentalPro’s strongest opportunity is mass-market expansion through smaller practice acquisitions rather than premium-clinic growth. Lower-value treatments will require more patients, greater geographic coverage, and potentially more chairs per clinic to preserve revenue. Clinic managers were considered critical to patient satisfaction, retention, and operating performance. Workforce training should therefore be a central investor KPI. Internal laboratories and ownership of selected supplies, including implant products, could improve cost control and margins. DentalPro’s consumer oral-care products were viewed as supplementary rather than strategically transformative. The expert’s strongest concern was that legacy clinic economics may weaken as treatment mix shifts toward lower-margin services.
