Agenda

  • Changes in market dynamics driven by AI data centre capacity growth
  • Samsung SDI's (KRX: 006400) competitive advantage across manufacturing and marketing capabilities
  • Outlook and progress on next-generation battery technologies

Key Insights

  • While global data centre battery market is roughly USD 6bn, with 7-8% annual growth, AI-compatible battery backup unit market is accelerating much faster, at c12-25%-plus
  • Samsung SDI holds over 50% of US lithium-ion based uninterruptible power supply battery market, translating to c25% of total uninterruptible power supply market, including lead-acid batteries. Uninterruptible power supply battery's operating margin is c20%, which is significantly higher than the general energy storage systems' below 5% or even negative
  • Samsung SDI's lithium manganese oxide – or manganese-based – cathode technology, originally developed for EVs, accidentally proved ideal for uninterruptible power supply applications. Technical gap between Samsung SDI and China-based competitors is only about six months, but geopolitical cybersecurity restrictions effectively widen this moat beyond that
  • While major hyperscalers and global colocation providers require rigid certification rules in tenders, uncertified suppliers might find entry opportunities through smaller data centres or telecom towers
