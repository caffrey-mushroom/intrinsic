核心摘要

  1. 数据中心选址与电力顾问认为，当前最大约束是电力而非土地或资本，德州电网拥堵和空气许可暂停导致决策延迟，但其接触的开发商均未放弃项目，只是谨慎观望新州长的监管方向。
  2. ERCOT此前被大量投机性用电申请淹没，曾拟每MW收取10万美元押金、后降至5万美元，并设立Batch 0等分批机制；据报约88GW规划容量受拟议暂停影响，选举后这些已批准分配实际上被搁置。
  3. 电网侧已拨款19亿美元缓解拥堵，另有90亿美元用于新建765kV变电站，但至少需要2年建成；东德州、西德州、北德州部分地区及圣安东尼奥周边仍有可开发的电力用地。
  4. 表后天然气已从备用电源变为主电源，专家当前顾问的4个项目合计逾13GW，全部以燃气为主，其中一个大型项目完全不接入电网，且10个项目中10个开发商都选择自建并拥有变电站。
  5. 燃机、变压器和开关柜成为瓶颈：西门子等大型燃机交付已排到2028–2030年，一个项目仅3台燃机报价就达17.7亿美元而实际需要13–15台；Anthropic据报以34亿美元采购1.7GW燃气发电，自购燃机正成为新打法。
  6. 燃机空气许可小型申请约需3个月、大型申请约6–8个月；即使拿到许可，变电站升级、长交期设备和熟练劳动力仍构成执行风险，偏远园区甚至需要“建一座城”来容纳上千名工人。
  7. 某大型AI开发商每月收到约80–100份电力用地申请，竞争在于谁能最快完成尽调和资本决策；选举后电力用地价格未上涨，因为开发商会在德州、其他州乃至海外之间比较资本回报。
  8. 专家认为太阳能和风能无法单独满足需求，未来将是电网、燃气、可再生能源和小型核电的组合；前加密矿场虽有电力，但需核实容量、可扩展性和建筑标准，改造成本不容忽视。

SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, a data center site and power advisor, described a market where access to reliable, scalable electricity is more important than land or funding. Texas remains a major development hub, but grid constraints and uncertainty around air permitting have delayed decisions. Developers have not abandoned projects, though many are proceeding cautiously and evaluating sites across North America.

To keep projects moving, developers are turning to behind-the-meter natural gas, while also considering utility power, renewables, and emerging nuclear options. Site selection now involves assessing permitting, infrastructure, labor, and equipment availability—not simply securing property.

Key Insights

Power is the principal constraint. The expert said capital is available, but electricity is finite and increasingly difficult to secure. Gas has shifted from backup to primary supply. For the expert’s current projects, gas is the main route to faster power; some developments are planned without utility power. Timing is critical. Turbines, transformers, and switchgear can take years to obtain. Permitted sites still face execution risks from equipment delays and construction needs. Competition is global. Developers compare Texas sites with opportunities in other states and countries. The expert sees no permanent Texas capacity ceiling, but expects the resolution to take time. Renewables alone cannot meet demand. Solar and wind can contribute, but the expert expects a mix of grid power, gas, renewables, and potentially small nuclear plants. The moderator showed particular interest in the shift to gas, turbine costs, and the practical risks that remain after a site is permitted.

Behind-the-meter gas

Increasingly preferred for speed and independence from grid queues, but permitting and turbine supply are obstacles.

Solar and wind

Useful as part of a broader power mix, but insufficient as standalone solutions for large campuses.

Former crypto-mining sites

Existing power can attract interest, but capacity, scalability, building standards, and retrofit costs require careful verification.
