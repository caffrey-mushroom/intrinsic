原标题：Adobe – Creative Cloud & the Next Phase of AI

#software #llm

Third Bridge · 2026-11-17

核心摘要

  1. 前Adobe全球品牌合作高级总监认为，Adobe利润丰厚、现金充裕，但Q3 2026收入增长13%、总ARR增长约11%中可能有收购（如Semrush）的贡献，增长动能才是关键问题。
  2. 专家认为ChatGPT、Claude、Gemini等前沿模型的免费版本已能达到专业级输出，对Adobe消费者业务构成生存威胁；以前Canva需数月甚至数年才能追上，如今护城河已不复存在，付费订阅可能在几个月内承压。
  3. Adobe免费用户漏斗未来12-24个月仍会扩张，但转化面临压力；个人订阅收入会最先下滑，依赖插件、模板和跨应用工作流的专业用户留存会更久，同时ElevenLabs等垂直AI工具也在侵蚀专业用户。
  4. 专家指出Adobe长期存在新付费用户3-6个月内流失的“漏水桶”问题，过去被持续的新用户涌入掩盖，预计6-12个月内会在Creative Cloud ARR增速中显现；单纯提高订阅价格已触及天花板，只能靠AI积分等增值收费。
  5. 专家预计未来12-24个月有机收入增速降至高个位数，10%已是激进的乐观情形，并对FY28预测持高度怀疑。
  6. 创意免费用户MAU已超1亿，但增长集中在印度、巴西、印尼等付费意愿低的市场；10%转化率是梦想情形，5-6%是现实上限，按每用户每年约120美元计算对应约6-7.2亿美元年收入。
  7. 企业端约30-40%的席位可能与初级岗位相关，受裁员和AI替代影响，但高端AI套餐定价可部分抵消；AI优先ARR约6亿美元，Firefly应用和积分包ARR环比增长40%，但相对260-270亿美元的收入规模太小，需每年增长2-2.5倍才有意义。
  8. 新任CEO Anil Chakravarthy来自企业业务，意味着Adobe押注以GenStudio和Firefly Foundry为核心的企业业务转型，主打商业安全和知识产权保障；与Nano Banana等外部模型合作有助留住现有用户，但可能稀释品牌，难以吸引新用户。

Agenda

  • Adobe's (NASDAQ: ADBE) leadership transition and priorities under the next CEO
  • Firefly adoption, AI credits and incremental monetisation
  • Creative agents and the shift towards automated, multi-step workflows
  • Adobe's September 2026 acquisition of Topaz Labs and integration across image and video workflows
  • Competition from AI-native tools and Adobe's ability to defend its creative moat

Key Insights

  • Frontier AI models pose an existential threat to Adobe's consumer business, eroding its competitive moat and potentially pressuring paid subscriber additions within months despite freemium growth
  • Adobe's historically weak retention could worsen as AI alternatives undermine conversion. Expert expects organic revenue growth to decelerate to high-single digits over 12-24 months, with 10% YoY growth an aggressive upside case
  • Adobe's freemium users offer monetisation potential, but emerging-market concentration limits willingness to pay. Expert considers 5-6% conversion a realistic upper target, implying USD 600m-720m in annual revenue
  • Enterprise seat growth faces headwinds as layoffs and AI-driven role elimination threaten c30-40% of junior employee seats, partially offset by premium AI tier pricing
  • Adobe's enterprise pivot hinges on GenStudio and Firefly Foundry, but AI-first ARR of cUSD 600m remains insufficient to materially impact Adobe's revenue base without accelerating to 2-2.5x annual growth
