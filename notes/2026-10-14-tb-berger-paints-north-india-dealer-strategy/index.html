Agenda

  • Berger Paints' (NSE: BERGEPAINT) go-to-market strategy in North India
  • Dealer network dynamics and incentive structures
  • Competitive positioning vs Asian Paints (NSE: ASIANPAINT), Kansai Nerolac Paints (NSE: KANSAINER), Birla Opus (NSE: GRASIM) and JSW Dulux (NSE: JSWDULUX)

Key Insights

  • Berger's total effective dealer margin ranges from 12-16%, comprising base trade margins, volume schemes, quarterly and annual incentives and a regional discretionary buffer of 1-2% for on-the-spot competitive situations
  • Expert believes Berger's North India decorative segment could deliver 13-15% revenue growth in Q2-Q3 FY27, combining 8-9% volume growth, 4-5% price increases and 1-2% product mix improvement
  • New dealers must pay upfront for their first 3-4 billings, while small dealers get up to seven days credit. Standard accounts generating INR 1.5 crore-3 crore receive 14-24 days credit vs Asian Paints at c14 days and Kansai Nerolac at c28 days, while high-volume paymasters extend to c35 days
  • Expert observes that companies are moving into using real-time gross settlement, providing additional rebates for dealers that can pay on zero days or provide prepayments. AkzoNobel has started providing 0.75% rebates for advance payments
