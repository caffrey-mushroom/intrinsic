Agenda

  • Key trends, dynamics and developments shaping the VMS (vitamins, minerals and supplements) market most relevant to Vitabiotics' segments and geographies
  • Vitabiotics' segmentation and portfolio strategy, noting potential new opportunities
  • D2C expansion and international growth potential
  • Competitive landscape and competitor threats
  • Profitability drivers, opportunities and profitability trajectory outlook

Key Insights

  • GLP-1 weight-loss drugs represent an emerging adjacent opportunity, as millions cycling on and off these medications will likely need structured supplement support programmes during recovery phases
  • Pregnacare has achieved rare brand-status recognition, with GPs actively recommending it by name, making it uniquely positioned almost as a prescribed medical product rather than a supplement
  • Wellman and Wellwoman's more generic positioning faces fragmentation risk as mission-specific use cases, such as gut health and sleep, emerge, potentially eroding their broader appeal
  • On D2C opportunity, with UK e-commerce accounting for only cGBP 13m of GBP 84m in UK revenue, the direct channel appears significantly underdeveloped, suggesting substantial margin upside through subscription and online growth
  • A super-premium product tier targeting the top 10-20% of affluent customers remains unexplored by Vitabiotics, representing a natural premiumisation opportunity through enhanced packaging and science-forward positioning
