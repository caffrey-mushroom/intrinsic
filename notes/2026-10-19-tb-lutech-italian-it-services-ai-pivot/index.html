Massimiliano Bolondi (MB) Sales Director at Present SpA

Saad Maqsood (SM) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Lutech's competitive positioning across European IT consulting, cloud services and AI integration
  • Customer demand across public sector accounts and private enterprise digital transformation projects
  • Operating margin trends, headcount utilisation and managed services cross-selling
  • Targeted expansion across vertical practices, including Salesforce (NYSE: CRM) implementations and CX tools

Key Insights

  • Expert expects Italian IT market growth to slow to 3-5% from 7-8% in the near term as PNRR stimulus winds down, before potentially recovering to 6-10% within three years
  • Lutech is positioned as a strong mid-market implementation partner, but customers rarely call it for radical business transformation or strategic consulting, limiting its upside potential. Lutech faces more immediate threats from rising mid-market players such as Var Group and BIP
  • Expert describes Lutech's middle management as "quite unimpressive", with senior roles largely locked up by Atos acquisition holdovers, blocking career progression and deterring top talent from joining
  • Lutech's as-a-service model attempts largely failed, with the company retreating back towards pure consultancy after software reselling and managed services platforms failed to generate sufficient margins
  • Expert believes Lutech is quietly retreating from ERP business in Italy, increasingly absent from SAP-related tenders as it gets squeezed out by SAP directly and smaller specialised players
