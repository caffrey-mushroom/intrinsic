原标题：Superior Energy Services – What Will Welltec International Bring?

#energy #industrials

Third Bridge · 2026-11-20

核心摘要

  1. Welltec前法务总监（2012-2025年在职）认为，Welltec凭借Well Tractor和Well Robot开创了轻型修井市场，可用约两人的小团队替代连续油管等重型作业，在轻型修井领域主要与Schlumberger和Baker Hughes竞争。
  2. 公司收入约75%来自修井、25%来自完井，营收从2015年危机后的约2.5亿美元增至约4.6亿美元，近年增长主要来自完井封隔器，修井业务基本持平。
  3. 挪威是最大且最赚钱的市场，巴西完井业务最好的一年收入约7500万美元，安哥拉Cabinda曾带来5000-6000万美元的超预期收入，海上业务占比至少80%。
  4. 完井产品的利润率估计比平均水平高10-15个百分点，而美国和加拿大陆上业务利润率最低。
  5. 公司拥有约800项专利，但核心护城河在于技术诀窍，近五年R&D投入不足，更多依靠收购挪威初创公司补充新工具，老旧工具车队也导致了服务质量问题。
  6. Superior于2026年9月宣布收购Welltec，发行了6.5亿美元票据，专家认为最大看点在于借助Superior专门的完井销售团队把完井销售做大2-4倍，因为Welltec在封隔器上仍领先Schlumberger多年。
  7. 风险包括Schlumberger已获得巴西国家石油的一两个封隔器尺寸认证，以及并入后品牌和管理团队流失。
  8. 专家预计2027年油价在65-90美元区间仍属健康，但客户资本开支意愿较弱，明年投资将小幅下降，圭亚那是最大亮点。

Analyst-led • 16 Nov 2026

Companies: SLB US, BKR US

Jacob Stensgaard (JS) Senior Contracts Adviser at John Wood Group Ltd

Peter McNally (PM) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Superior Energy Services and Welltec International's oilfield service offerings
  • Industry logic of Superior's announced acquisition of Welltec International and how this impacts the competitive landscape for well drilling and completions
  • Deep dive into oilfield technologies and how they can be leveraged with customers
  • Outlook for oil company CAPEX and revenue for the combined company

Key Insights

  • Welltec is "still years and years ahead" of Schlumberger on completions, and Superior Energy Services should benefit from the acquisition, particularly when it comes to packers. Light well intervention at c75% of revenues has been more of a stable, steady grower while the completions side has seen faster growth
  • Welltec will need some investment from Superior to renew the aging tool fleet which has led to some service quality issues, while M&A has been preferred to R&D in recent years
  • While R&D investment needs to be addressed, Welltec can leverage Superior's dedicated completions sales force, which is a much larger opportunity for both companies than well intervention
  • Norway remains Welltec's most profitable and largest market while Brazil has emerged as one of the more important markets. Schlumberger has received some initial approvals for packers in Brazil
