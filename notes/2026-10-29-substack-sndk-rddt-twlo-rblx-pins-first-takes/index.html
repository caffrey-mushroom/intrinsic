Things not getting much better following AMZN and INTC in Tech land with SNDK only name in the green tonight (and barely)

SNDK +1%: Ok looking print (best of the night) with better top line and mixed GM. F1Q revenue of $11.03B beat Street ($10.74B) and was in line with the ~$10.9-11.2B bogey, but GM of 85.1% beat Street and missed the ~86% bar, and the F2Q guide of $12.2-12.6B / 84.5-85.5% GM / $55-57 EPS beat Street ($12.08B / 84.7% / $52.94) and hit the top of the ~$12.2-12.4B bogey, while the flat GM guide sits well short of the 86.5-87.5% bulls wanted. Datacenter did the work (+61% q/q) as Edge and Consumer missed. Not crowded coming in, but the stock ran +12% the two days before today’s -5%, and a GM guide that’s flat q/q not exciting and bulls were looking for more pricing flow-through but expects low heading in.

  • F1Q revenue of $11.03B (+23.0% q/q) beat $10.74B Street and was in line with the ~$10.9-11.2B bogeys, 2.1% above the top of the $10.3-10.8B guide (Aug beat the top by 8.7%).60% of the q/q revenue increase came from volume and ~40% from pricing (Aug: one-third volume / two-thirds price). That’s ~+8.6% pricing and ~+13% bits by our math, vs. Street ASP +11.1% / bits +7.9% and the ASP +15-18% bogey: a volume-led quarter, which is why GM only ticked up.- Datacenter beat at $4.80B (+61% q/q) vs. $4.0B Street (+34%), now 43.5% of revenue (33% in June).- Edge missed at $5.83B (+7% q/q) vs. $6.08B Street (+12%); Consumer missed at $397M (-29% q/q) vs. $584M.

GM beat Street at 85.07% but missed the bogey, ~60bps above Street’s 84.45%, 0.4pt below the 85.5-86.5% bogey and 0.1pt over the 83-85% guide top (84.64% in June, so +0.4pt q/q).

  • Pure price flow-through on the ~$230M over the guide top would have given ~85.3%, so no cushion showed up from the “prudent” component-cost assumptions.- EPS of $49.51 beat $46.73 Street, in line with the ~$48-50 bogeys and above the $44-46 guide, on 154M diluted shares (~155M guide).- A 14.3% non-GAAP tax rate vs. the 15% guide added ~$0.40 of that.- Opex of $513M was under the $520-540M guide; OM of 80.42% beat Street’s 79.5% (~90bps above).

F2Q revenue guide of $12.2-12.6B ($12.4B midpoint, +12.4% q/q) beat $12.08B Street and hit the top of the ~$12.2-12.4B bogeys.

  • GM guide of 84.5-85.5% (85.0% midpoint, flat with F1Q’s 85.07%) is ~30bps above Street’s 84.7% but 1.5pt below the low end of the 86.5-87.5% bogey.- EPS guide of $55-57 ($56 midpoint) beat $52.94 Street and is at the top of the ~$54-56 bogeys, on ~151M diluted shares (154M in F1Q). RDDT -7%: US DAUq fell to 50.7M (-2.5M q/q, -2% y/y) vs. 53.5M Street and the +0.1-0.2M bogey, and the letter says total DAUq could fall by a low-double-digit % in 4Q on search referrals. Revenue of $895M beat Street ($877M) but missed the ~$935-945M bogeys, and the 4Q guide of $1,000-1,030M missed both $1,044M Street and the ~$1.07-1.08B bogeys. Logged-in and app DAUq grew q/q, so the call is all about how big the logged-out hole gets.

Stock was short-skewed coming in and ~50% off its highs (-3.8% today, -19% since 10/12), but this is worse than bears were looking for - US users fell 2.5M vs. the 0.3M in Q2 and the guide takes away the revenue cushion too. Huffman has an uphill battle on the call.

Key quote: “Our search referral traffic was down meaningfully in Q3, with the decline accelerating later in the quarter. The biggest impact was on logged-out and web traffic, but some of the impact flows through to logged-in and App traffic, since search referral is a common source of reengagement for users… These declines have continued into the early part of Q4. During October, we have seen that search traffic has dropped by more than 1 million referrals on average per week. If this rate of decline continues, current trends would suggest that total DAUqs could fall in Q4, by a low-double-digit percentage from our current base of 130 million DAUqs.”

  • US DAUq fell to 50.7M, -2.5M q/q, vs. 53.5M Street (+0.3M; some desks at flat) and bogeys of ~53.3-53.4M (+0.1-0.2M).That’s -2% y/y vs. +5.8% in 2Q, and vs. a +1.3M q/q add in 3Q last year.
