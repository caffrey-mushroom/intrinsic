  2. 专家估计全球软件市场每年约1.5-2万亿美元，AI编程可拿下其中20-30%，即每年约2000-3000亿美元，并因杰文斯悖论进一步扩大市场，仅编程就足以让Anthropic收入翻倍乃至三倍。
  3. 据专家所见，Anthropic在软件编程上的收入接近OpenAI的两倍，但Codex已相当成熟，Google的Antigravity加新模型也有一定潜力，竞争真实存在。

  8. 专家认为只要推理毛利率高且推理量增速快于训练成本，训练成本占比会被摊薄、净利率随规模提升，Q2-Q3的“运营利润”已体现推理覆盖训练成本。
  9. 专家认为模型辅助训练下一代模型（RSI）已真实出现，预计未来6-12个月见到初步进展；市场不会赢家通吃，Anthropic可能拿下编程50%以上份额但不会达到80-90%，新版Haiku降价70%更多是补全产品线。

SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, a former reliability leader at major AI companies, discussed Anthropic’s business outlook ahead of its IPO. Topics included company culture, enterprise adoption, software development and cybersecurity markets, model economics, competition, and governance.

The expert characterized Anthropic as prioritizing safety and cost discipline alongside speed, while describing OpenAI as especially focused on shipping products quickly. They argued that demand for AI services remains strong and that continued model improvements are central to sustaining revenue growth.

Key Insights

Coding is an unusually attractive market for AI: It is large, globally consistent, and relatively lightly regulated. The expert believes AI coding tools could support substantial further revenue growth. Cybersecurity may be the next major opportunity: AI models are well suited to security tasks, and demand is significant. However, selling through established security vendors or other partners may be important. Enterprise model choice is not frictionless: Contracts, familiarity with a model’s behavior, and investment in product workflows make switching less convenient than simply changing models through an interface. Model improvement drives customer expansion: Exceptionally high retention reflects rapid growth and pricing power, but the expert cautioned that usage could weaken if model progress slows. Margins may improve with scale: If inference revenue grows faster than training costs, training becomes a smaller cost per unit of service. This depends on continued demand and model development. The market is unlikely to be winner-take-all: The expert expects several major providers to remain viable, with Anthropic potentially holding a leading position in coding.
