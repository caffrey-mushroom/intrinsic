NOW -7%: Not good. Subs in line with Street and short of the ~$4.01–4.03B bogeys, cRPO of 21% cc below the ~21.5–22% bar, and a 4Q cRPO guide of 18.5% cc that sits under both Street (19%) and the ~19.5–20% bulls wanted. EBIT margin guide worse. Organic cRPO will be focus for the call.

Positioning had come down last few days following concerns over the VA contract headwinds, but this is a bit worse even taking that into account. Some nascent bulls will be disappointed.

3Q subscription revenue of $3.988B (20.5% cc, +21% reported) was right on the $3.99B Street and below the ~$4.01–4.03B bogeys, a 50bp cc beat on the 20% guide vs. the ~120bp average beat of the last four quarters. cRPO of $13.65B grew 21% cc (20.5% reported) vs. ~21.5–22% bogeys, the 20% guide and $13.57B Street. Ex ~1pt Moveworks and ~1.25–1.75pt Armis that’s ~18.5% organic vs. the ~19–19.5% bulls were looking for. Net new cRPO of $450M q/q vs. $430M a year ago. RPO of $29.7B (22.5% cc) vs. $29.4B Street.

Op margin of 32.5% was ~140bps above Street’s 31.1% and 0.5–1pt above the ~31.5–32% bogey (31% guide). EPS of $1.00 vs. $1.03 Street despite the OM beat: net interest swung to about -$12M from +$109M a year ago (interest expense $81M vs. $6M). Subs GM of 80% was ~65bps below Street’s 80.65% and down 300bps y/y.

4Q subs guide of $4.245–4.255B (21.5% cc, +22.5–23% reported) vs. $4.24B Street and ~$4.24–4.26B bogeys. The midpoint is a 1pt cc accel from 3Q on a 1pt easier comp (19.5% cc in 4Q25), so the 2-yr stack is flat at ~41%.

4Q cRPO guide of 18.5% cc vs. ~19.5–20% bogeys; 18.5% on the $12.85B base is ~$15.23B vs. $15.29B Street. That’s a 2.5pt decel from 3Q on a tougher comp (21% incl. Moveworks), with the 2-yr stack down to 39.5% from 41.5%. Moveworks’ ~1pt laps at YE while Armis stays in, so the organic 4Q guide is ~17–17.25%.

FY subs goes to $15.78–15.79B from $15.76–15.78B, a $15M raise at the midpoint vs. a ~$10M 3Q beat over the guide midpoint ($3.9775B). That passes through the full beat but lands below the ~$15.80–15.83B bulls wanted. The new FY implies ~$4.25B of 4Q subs, the 4Q guide midpoint.

4Q OM guide of 32% vs. Street 33.5%. FY OM held at 31.5%, so the 3Q margin beat comes back out of 4Q. AI ACV target raised again, to $1.7B from “beat $1.5B” (it started the year at $1B). FY FCF margin goes to 36% from 35% on lower capex; FY subs GM held at 81% after two cuts this year. Large deals were strong: 19 transactions over $5M in net new ACV (+135% y/y) and 680 customers over $5M ACV (+23%).

EBAY +4.6%: Clean beat-and-raise that clears the bogeys on GMV, revenue, OM and EPS, and the 4Q GMV guide (+12-14% FXN) sits well above the ~10-11% bogey; ads were the soft spot and there’s no 2027 color on the release, so the margin framework on the call will be the focus for the HF shorts.

The 10/22 8-K already told us 3Q would land above the guide ranges, so the bar was the 4Q guide and 2027, which came in better than expected.

3Q: GMV of $23.17B (+16% FXN, +15% reported) vs. $22.34B Street (+11.3% FXN) and ~$22.9-23.1B bogeys (+14-15% FXN); guide was $22.0-22.4B. That’s ~$0.8B above Street and ~$1B above the guide mid. Taking out the ~2.5pt Depop contribution the guide assumed, organic is ~+13.5% vs. the +7-9% organic guide, so the 2-yr organic stack goes to ~21.5% from 18% in 2Q. US GMV +27% (vs. +24% in 2Q) did the work; International +4% reported, slowing from +6%.

Revenue of $3.21B vs. $3.118B Street and ~$3.16-3.18B bogeys. Non-GAAP OM of 26.3%, ~80bps above Street’s 25.5% and ~1pt above the ~25.3% bogey (above the top of the 25.1-25.6% guide). Non-GAAP OI of $843M (+11% YoY vs. the +1-5% guide). EPS of $1.51 vs. $1.42 Street, ~$1.43 bogey and $1.36-1.42 guide.

The pushback will be ads: 1P ads of $575M (+16%) vs. $585M Street (+18%), so ads only kept pace with GMV this quarter after outgrowing it by ~10pt in 2Q. Total ads $607M are ~2.6% of GMV vs. ~2.7% in 2Q and the 3% goal. Take rate 13.84% vs. 13.95% Street (14.03% a year ago), with Depop shipping running through contra-revenue. Active buyers 147M vs. 139.7M Street, but that includes Depop and Tise; ex those it’s 136M, +1%.

4Q guide: GMV $23.7-24.1B (+12-14% FXN) vs. $23.14B Street (+9.5% FXN) and ~$23.25-23.5B bogeys (+10-11%). The low end is above the top of the bogey range. Midpoint is a ~3pt decel from 3Q’s +16% on the same ~+8% FXN comp as bullion starts to lap; 2-yr stack ~21% vs. 24% in 3Q. Less ~3pt of full-quarter Depop, organic is ~+10% at the mid.

Revenue $3.19-3.24B (+7-9% FXN) vs. $3.20B Street, so revenue keeps running well below GMV (implied take rate ~13.45% vs. ~13.96% in 4Q25).

EPS $1.48-1.53 vs. $1.48 Street; the low end is Street. No OM guide on the release, but the EPS midpoint backs out to ~26% OM (my math) vs. 25.8% Street and the ~25-25.5% bogey.

FY26: GMV now $91.5-91.9B, +14% FXN (was +11.5-12.5%) vs. Street +12.2% ($90.08B) and ~+13% bogey (~$91B).

Revenue $12.62-12.67B, +12-13% FXN (was +11-12%) vs. $12.54B Street.

EPS $6.25-6.30 (was ~$6.05-6.18) vs. $6.16 Street, about +13-15% on the FY25 base; it backs out exactly to the $1.48-1.53 4Q guide. The raise at the mid (~$0.16 vs. the old guide mid) is bigger than the 3Q beat vs. the guide mid (~$0.12).

FTNT -22%: Beat Street on product, billings, EPS and the 4Q guide, but product (+37%) came in well short of the ~50%+ bogey and services decelerated with the FY services guide cut; 4Q revs guide +15.5% vs. bulls’ ~18-19%. Lets see what FY27 color we get on the call after FFIV’s refresh scare today.

Positioning shorted coming in and this will have bears finally feeling happy about their refresh thesis.

Product revenue came in at $765M (+37%) vs. $726M Street (+30%) and ~$840-855M (+50-53%) bogeys, a decel from +52% in 2Q against a tougher +18% comp (2-yr stack ~55 vs. ~65 in 2Q). Product was down ~$8M q/q vs. +$50M in 3Q last year.

Billings $2.34B (+29%), at the top of the $2.25-2.35B guide and above $2.29B Street, but below the ~$2.42-2.46B bogeys and down from 2Q’s +33%.

Services is the other problem: $1.31B (+12.7%) vs. $1.33B Street and ~$1.35-1.36B bogeys, a decel from +13.7% in 2Q when mgmt had said services growth would pick up in 2H. FY services guide goes to $5.15-5.17B from $5.18-5.22B, implying 4Q services of ~$1.37B (+12.6%).

Guide: 4Q revs $2.155-2.245B, or +15.5% at the midpoint vs. $2.15B Street (+13%) and bulls ~$2.25-2.27B (+18-19%). That’s a ~5pt decel from 3Q’s +20.5% on a similar comp (4Q25 +15%).

4Q billings $2.775-2.925B (+20% at the mid) vs. $2.67B Street (+13%), at the low end of the ~$2.84-2.92B bogeys. FY billings go to $9.57-9.72B from $9.35-9.55B vs. $9.39B Street and ~$9.75-9.8B bogeys. Implied 4Q product is ~$833M (+21%) vs. $767M Street (+11%), or +9% q/q vs. a seasonal +21-24%.

Profitability was fine: 38.2% OM, ~220bps above Street, and $0.91 EPS vs. $0.85 Street. GM was 79.6%, down from 80.9% in 2Q and 81.6% a year ago, and the 4Q GM guide is 78-80%. Buyback authorization up $1.2B to $2B remaining.

KLAC +1%: Sep-Q beat Street and landed at the top of the ~$4.1-4.15B bogey; Dec guide of $4.7B is right on the bulls’ number and above ~$4.55-4.6B bogeys, but the GM guide is flat at 62.5%. Should be good enough given low expects coming in.

Sep-Q revenue of $4.14B vs. $4.04B Street and ~$4.10-4.15B bogeys (guide $3.8-4.2B), +29% y/y on a +13% comp and +13% q/q. Product better at $3.29B (+33% y/y) vs. $3.23B Street; services of $851M were a hair light of Street’s $853M. GM of 62.7%, ~10bps above Street’s 62.6% but ~10-30bps short of the ~62.8-63% bogeys.

EPS of $1.22 vs. $1.18 Street and the $1.16 guide midpoint. FCF of $1.34B vs. $1.44B Street, with inventory up ~$430M q/q and capex up to ~$195M.

Dec guide of $4.7B ± $200M vs. $4.49B Street and ~$4.55-4.6B bogeys, i.e., the ~$4.7B / mid-teens q/q the bulls wanted. The midpoint is +14% q/q vs. Street’s +11%, and +43% y/y on an easy +7% comp (2-yr stack ~50pts vs. ~42pts in Sep). That puts 2H CY26 at ~+25% over 1H vs. the ~20% they framed in July (bogeys implied ~22-24%), and implied CY26 revenue at ~$15.9B (+25%) vs. Street $15.6B.

GM guide of 62.5% ± 1pt vs. 62.6% Street and ~62.5-63% bogeys, so no step-up off Sep’s 62.7% as memory mix rises.

EPS guide of $1.44 vs. $1.36 Street and ~$1.38-1.41 derived bogeys. Implied Dec op margin is ~47% (derived from the EPS midpoint) vs. Street 46.7%.

WFE detail on the call. Street already has KLAC growing ~28% in CY27 ($19.98B) vs. ~22% in CY26, so it needs CY27 framed faster than CY26 for bulls to feel ok.
