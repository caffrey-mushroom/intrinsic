核心摘要

  1. 临时CEO Colette Kress将FY28增长指引从70%上调至约75%，盘后半导体板块上涨4-5%。
  2. 以FY27约4237亿美元营收计算，75%增长对应约7410亿美元，高于买方隐含的7120-7170亿美元。
  3. FY28指引按72-73%毛利率隐含EPS接近17美元，高于市场预期的16美元但略低于买方的18美元。
  4. Vera Rubin首个季度收入近200亿美元，向中国授权销售的Hopper（F3Q为24亿美元）已停止且未计入1月季度指引。
  5. 在客户融资方面，Kress称将“严格评估未来的投资、承诺和担保”，担保仅提供给“非常强的客户”，非投资级买家需要稳固的承购合同。
  6. Hopper GPU多年协议续约价格最高达原价两倍，B300租金自推出以来上涨超60%，所有超大规模和新云客户的算力均满负荷运行。
  7. Anthropic IPO预计定价高于80美元，超出最初70-78美元区间，作者认为至少到周末半导体走势偏积极，但不对长期做判断。

Raise your hands who expected Colette to save the AI semis trade? Who needs Jensen…

Semis +4-5% rallying hard close after Interim CEO Colette Kress raised FY28 growth to “approximately 75%” from 70% instead of cutting or pulling it. On FY27’s ~$423.7B (F1Q-F3Q plus the $130B Jan-qtr mid), that’s ~$741B vs. ~$712-717B implied by the bogeys. The quarter itself was above bogeys & the biggest beat vs guide since 3Q’25, DC Beat was the biggest since 1Q’25, and Jan guide was also above bogeys. Vera Rubin did nearly $20B in its first quarter; licensed Hopper sales to China ($2.4B in F3Q) have stopped and not included in the Jan guide. She’s still studying this week’s export rule and skipped whether demand still points to the ~100% growth customers forecast in August. On customer financing, the worry without Jensen, she’s “rigorously evaluating future investments, commitments, and guarantees” as other capital comes in, but overall she sounded good on those continuing (see quotes below).

The FY28 guide implies close to $17 of EPS at 72-73% FY28 GM, which is better than street at $16 but a tad light of where buyside is at $18. Still, it’s a relief. Overall, much better than feared and largely de-risks FY28. Overall, investors pretty happy with the print and happy the focus is off Jensen at least for tonight…

Investors are preferring to buy other semis (Neos leading the way higher) instead of NVDA and the group is rallying in the post after testing post-election lows today and it looks like SMH is taking the same shape it did before the SPCX IPO bottoming two days before pricing.

Key Quotes from the call

On Neos:

“We have supported the development of this ecosystem, and we will continue to be disciplined and selective in our support”…“Their capital, it needs to be set up, ready to go for us to work in terms of that allocation.” Buyers below investment grade need “solid offtake.”

Tthe market matures and additional sources of capital become available, we are rigorously evaluating future investments, commitments, and guarantees.” Also: “we will remain thoughtful in how we participate and disciplined in how we commit capital.”

customer guarantees go only to “very strong customers,” where “there will be a portion that we will do.”

on the new contingency paragraph: “Is it new? No. Did we change anything? No.” If a partner can’t build, “our guarantee doesn’t exist anymore.”

On GPU Pricing:

Hopper GPUs have recontracted at up to twice their original price under the multi-year agreements, while the B300 rates have risen more than 60% since their introduction.” Compute “remains fully utilized across every hyperscaler and neocloud we serve.”

Earlier:

NVDA +2.6%: Clean beat and a Jan-qtr guide at the top of the buyside bogey, but GM a bit light. All in-all looks really solid with biggest beat over midpoint of the guide since 3Q’25. Bigger issues at play here obviously and key will be the call and reiteration of ‘27 guide of 70%

F3Q revenue of $115.8B (+103% y/y) beat Street ($109.48B) and the ~$112-114B bogeys, a 7.3% beat on the $108.0B guide mid vs. 5.7% in F2Q (the biggest since F3Q25) and a record $19.6B q/q add.

Data Center of $107.6B beat Street ($102.03B) on Hyperscale ($60.1B vs. Street $52.96B), while ACIE (AI clouds, industrial and enterprise) missed ($47.5B vs. $49.02B).

GM of 74.9% beat Street (74.0%) and the ~74% bogey, and EPS of $2.68 beat Street ($2.48).

The Jan-qtr revenue guide (a 14-week quarter) of $130.0B ± 3% (widened from ± 2%) beat Street ($124.77B) and was in line with the top of the ~$129-130B bogeys. GM of 71.0-72.0% (71.5% mid) missed Street’s 71.6% mean (in line with the 71.5% median) and the ~71.7-72% bogeys; with $10.7B of opex (Street $9.91B) that’s implied OI of ~$82.3B, above Street’s $79.10B.

The CFO Commentary: *“We plan to file our 10-Q in the coming weeks.”

“We have partnered with leading neoclouds to enable broader access to our AI infrastructure to serve AI startups, model builders, enterprises, research organizations and sovereign customers. Our commitments take effect only if the neoclouds secure financing to purchase the data center infrastructure products and the related compute capacity is deployed, made operational, and accepted by us. The maximum commitment presented assumes these contingencies are satisfactorily resolved. If they are not timely resolved, the amount and timing of our commitments may be affected, and certain commitments could be reduced or eliminated. Under these agreements, we will earn revenue on the upfront sale of our infrastructure and if certain criteria are met, we will participate in revenue share generated by the neoclouds from their third-party customers.”*
