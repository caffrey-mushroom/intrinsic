2026.11.03 | TB | Software | Smartsheet: New User Model Kills Free-Collaborator Edge, Churn Pressure for 2–3 Years

原标题：Smartsheet – Can AI Put Growth Back on the Sheet?

#saas #worktools #ai

Third Bridge · 2026-11-03

核心摘要

  1. Smartsheet过去最强的差异化是免费协作者模式，尤其适合需要对外部伙伴开放权限的企业，新的按用户计费模式抹掉了这一优势。
  2. 新模式推行让客户不满，有些客户续约前只提前一个月收到通知，专家预计由此带来的流失压力还会持续2至3年。
  3. 难以证明可量化的运营开支节省或ROI，影响多达30%的续约，集成能力不足又加重了这一问题。
  4. 中小客户扩张天花板在50至200席，流失率高、服务覆盖不足，仅6名客户成功经理负责约10万个账户，因此公司转向大企业。
  5. 专家预计收入增速将放缓到高个位数或低双位数，原因是许可扩张受限、平台整合以及AI让工作管理工具商品化。

Companies: ASAN US, MNDY US, TEAM US

Jennifer Malfet (JM) Sr. Director and Head of Digital Customer Experience at 6sense

Agenda

  • Enterprise work management demand, customer retention and expansion trends
  • Smartsheet's new user subscription model, pricing changes and renewal pressure
  • Smart Assist, AI integrations and opportunities for incremental monetisation
  • Growth prospects, profitability and strategic priorities

Key Insights

  • Smartsheet's free collaborator model was historically its strongest differentiator against Monday.com and Asana, particularly among enterprises sharing access with external partners, but the new user model has eliminated this advantage
  • The roll-out has frustrated customers, with some receiving just one month's notice before renewal. Expert expects the resulting churn pressures to persist for another 2-3 years
  • Difficulty demonstrating measurable OPEX savings or ROI affects up to 30% of renewals, compounded by integration limitations
  • Smartsheet's enterprise focus reflects structural SMB challenges, including 50-200-seat expansion ceilings, elevated churn and limited support coverage, with just six customer success managers overseeing c100,000 accounts
  • Expert forecasts revenue growth decelerating to high-single-digit or low-double digits amid constrained licence expansion, platform consolidation and AI-driven commoditisation of work management
