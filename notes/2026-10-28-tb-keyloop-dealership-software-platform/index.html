Analyst-led • 16 Oct 2026

Companies: LAD US, VTU GB

Mark Rogers (MR) Director, Solution Delivery at TekCor4 Ltd

Saad Maqsood (SM) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Keyloop's market share and retention across automotive DMSs (dealership management systems)
  • Customer adoption of integrated cloud applications spanning vehicle sales, service and aftersales
  • Fusion automotive retail platform uptake trends and implementation
  • Competitive positioning defensibility against regional DMS providers and emerging digital-first automotive software

Key Insights

  • Keyloop holds roughly 70-75% DMS market share in the UK, but this metric is increasingly difficult to place as third-party apps significantly erode the total wallet share
  • A decade ago, DMS providers captured 80-90% of dealer IT spend – today, it's just over 50%, with workshop, showroom, lead management and omnichannel tools fragmenting the remainder
  • Pinewood wins deals partly by offering implementation costs as low as GBP 25,000-30,000 vs Keyloop's GBP 200,000-plus, likely at a significant margin sacrifice to buy enterprise customers
  • China-based OEMs such as BYD and Chery require far simpler DMS functionality, potentially opening the UK market to lighter, cheaper competitors that Keyloop's heavyweight platform can't serve cost-effectively
  • Dealer consolidation can at times erode Keyloop revenue – when an AM100 acquires smaller Keyloop-using sites, those site licences are typically cancelled rather than added, shrinking the installed base
