2026.11.17 | TB | BRZE | Braze Acquisition by Salesforce

原标题：Braze – Salesforce & the Next Chapter of Customer Engagement

#software #agents

Third Bridge · 2026-11-17

核心摘要

  1. 专家认为Braze以移动端为先、主要面向B2C客户，在B2B领域覆盖有限。
  2. Braze FY27 Q2收入增长26%（有机增长约24%），Q3指引约20%，净收入留存率约110%，专家预计增长不会大幅加速也不会明显下滑。
  3. Braze对小客户而言价格偏贵；OfferFit等AI决策能力会压缩服务和合作伙伴市场，专家预计定价将转向按使用量计费。
  4. Salesforce于2026年10月以56.6亿美元收购Braze，专家预计Marketing Cloud将变成白标化的Braze，Braze在未来几年内保持独立运营；Salesforce营销与商务业务占其FY26收入超过10%。
  5. 两家的技术架构存在差异，Salesforce偏结构化数据而Braze偏非结构化数据，整合存在难度。
  6. Adobe几乎免费捆绑营销产品、Klaviyo与Shopify集成更好，因此专家不认为收购后胜率会提升；对小客户而言MoEngage是替代选择。
  7. 专家估计未来2-3年约30%的客户可能流失，并提到Salesforce过去的信条是每1美元软件配套11美元服务。
  8. 专家认为交易完成概率约89%，建议关注高管离职以及与Salesforce之间的矛盾。

Analyst-led • 12 Nov 2026

Companies: ADBE US, CRM US, KVYO US, BRZE US, SHOP US

Kevin Rogers (KR) Independent Consultant

Dylan Koehler (DK) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Braze's (NASDAQ: BRZE) revenue growth, customer retention and enterprise expansion
  • Salesforce's (NYSE: CRM) October 2026-announced USD 5.66bn acquisition of Braze and the likely strategic rationale behind the deal
  • Integration opportunities, cross-selling and potential risks to Braze's independence
  • BrazeAI adoption, Decisioning Studio and opportunities for AI monetisation
  • Competition from Klaviyo (NYSE: KVYO), Adobe (NASDAQ: ADBE) and other customer engagement platforms

Key Insights

  • Expert estimates that 30% of Braze's customer base could churn over the next 2-3 years, driven by engineering requirements and integration complexity, with Salesforce's bundling reputation adding further risk
  • Expert expects Salesforce to eventually white-label Braze as Marketing Cloud, although integration complexity could delay consolidation and slow the product roadmap
  • Braze's premium positioning faces competitive pressure as Adobe bundles marketing capabilities at little incremental cost, while Klaviyo's Shopify alignment could constrain mid-market expansion and weaken Braze's pricing power
  • Braze's B2C-oriented architecture limits its B2B expansion, creating a structural constraint on its addressable market and longer-term revenue growth despite its mobile-first competitive advantages
  • Braze's AI decisioning capabilities could undermine its partner ecosystem by reducing implementation and services requirements, weakening partner-sourced pipeline. OfferFit's monetisation remains uncertain as the expert anticipates a shift towards utilisation-based pricing
