SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, a longtime UCaaS and CCaaS product marketing leader, discussed how the cloud communications market is evolving. UCaaS is mature, with fewer new customers switching from on-premise systems, increasing price pressure, and more commoditization. Providers are expanding into contact center services to boost revenue per customer and retention.

Contact centers are more complex to implement than business phone systems, often requiring tailored workflows and professional services. Their buyers may include business-unit leaders as well as IT. AI is accelerating the convergence of phone, meetings, and contact center products, particularly through automated customer support and call routing.

Key Insights

The expert sees contact center as a significant growth opportunity: agent seats can generate roughly four times the revenue of standard phone seats, even if only a portion of a company’s employees use them. Combining communications and contact center products can simplify administration and make it easier to route calls to subject- matter experts. AI voice agents have moved beyond early adoption, but large organizations may take several years to validate and deploy them. Trust, accuracy, security, and regulatory requirements remain important barriers. AI is expected to reduce demand for human contact center agents. Providers therefore need sustainable pricing for AI usage, potentially linked to consumption. The expert believes software will become easier for customers to build themselves. Established vendors may need to compete increasingly on service, support, compliance, and operational reliability rather than features alone.
