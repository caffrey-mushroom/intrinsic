Agenda

  • PC demand and replacement cycles
  • AI PCs and workstation growth
  • Dell (NYSE: DELL) CSG's (Client Solutions Group's) pricing, costs and competitive positioning
  • Revenue growth and margin sustainability

Key Insights

  • Dell's Q3 FY27 CSG revenue growth could fall to 5-8% vs management's 15% guidance, as declining PC shipments outweigh higher ASPs, with annual growth normalising towards 5-6% by FY28
  • CSG operating margins could decline from 7.6% in Q2 FY27 to 5.5-6% in H2 FY27 as Dell prioritises volumes and large enterprise contracts, remaining near that range over the next 24-36 months
  • Dell's workstation revenue could grow 10-15% over the next 12-24 months, potentially reaching 30% annual growth over five years, with ASPs of USD 1,800-2,000 and gross margins of 25-28% in mature markets, supported by growing AI development and inferencing demand
  • AI-capable PCs could reach 60-65% of Dell's commercial mix within 12 months and 90% over 2-3 years, although genuine AI-driven demand remains limited and the 8-10% price premium largely reflects component costs
