Agenda

  • BDx Data Center's Indonesian portfolio footprint and utilisation evolution

Key Insights

  • Expert estimates the proportion of BDx's operational capacity dedicated to telco operations is projected to fall from 80% in 2023 to under 20% in the next 2-3 years, as emerging GPU demand drives AI deployments to 80-85% of total capacity
  • Given that upcoming megascale campuses are built for GPU farm deployments, new capacity achieves nearly 100% utilisation upon going live, which is projected to expand BDx Indonesia's blended EBITDA margin from 50-55% up to 60-65% over the next two years
  • Traditional telco capacity commands USD 90-100 per kilowatt monthly space pricing vs USD 160-180 for enterprise space pricing or USD 280-290 when bundled with power. Meanwhile, neocloud and GPU farm deployments command USD 120-140 per kilowatt monthly space pricing, with rates approaching USD 140-150 due to supply constraints and time-sensitive deployment demands
  • Expert sees no concentration risk from Indosat and Lintasarta as long as their 10-year contracts remain valid, given the business will continue to grow alongside 5G expansion and a rising subscriber base
