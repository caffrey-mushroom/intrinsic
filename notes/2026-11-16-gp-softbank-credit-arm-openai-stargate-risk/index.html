核心摘要

  1. 一位大华银行科技风险副总裁从亚太银行视角介绍了对软银这类 AI 投资控股公司的授信方法，涵盖电网供电时间、关联方信用、资本开支匹配、SPV 隔离、现金流瀑布和契约条款六项尽调。
  2. 软银自报的 LTV 约为 16%，低于其 25% 的内部上限，外部机构估算约 30%，但专家认为标题 LTV 不可靠，银行会基于实地尽调大幅调整。
  3. 软银持有约 87% 的 Arm 股份，公众流通盘很薄，以约 72% 股份质押获得约 250 亿美元保证金贷款，银行会施加按成交量调整的流动性折扣以及追加保证金、股价下限和最低流通盘等条款。
  4. 压力测试中，银行对 Arm 这类集中持有的上市股权折价 30%-50%，对 OpenAI、Anthropic 等私有股权折价 60%-80%，并要求流动性覆盖至少 24 个月的到期债务、利息和费用。
  5. 专家估计全球约 40%-50% 的 GPU 资产仍未接入电网，东南亚高达约 60%，这些资产不应计入偿债能力；而每吉瓦电网扩容需 2-3 年，GPU 淘汰周期仅 2-3 年。
  6. 即使有可验证的采购订单、供电 SLA 和冷却水源，银行也只按 85%-90% 确认现金流；液冷设备按 5-8 年折旧，没有落实水源合同的资产会被剔除。
  7. 对于软银与 Grab 合作在马来西亚投资约 18 亿美元、规划 5 MW 算力的项目，专家认为本地伙伴并不能保证电力和冷却资源，马来西亚和印尼已出现针对电网建设和用水的抗议。
  8. 在识别循环融资时，银行会对 AI 公司做至少 5 层、最多 10 层的横向和纵向关联方穿透，与软银组合相关的客户收入会被打 30%-50% 折扣。
  9. 专家个人认为未来 5-10 年 AI 基础设施项目的违约概率至少为 50%，美国风险高于东南亚，甚至可能出现类似 2008 年次贷危机的冲击。

SUMMARY

Overview

The expert discussed how banks may assess an investment holding company with large stakes in AI businesses and exposure to data-center projects. The focus was on collateral quality, concentrated holdings, private-company valuations, and the practical constraints of delivering AI infrastructure. The expert stressed that project-level checks are essential because announced capacity and projected revenue may not translate into operating, income-generating assets.

Key Insights

The expert considers headline loan-to-value figures unreliable unless lenders verify asset operation, customer commitments, and cash flows. In particular, equipment that lacks power or confirmed demand should not support debt repayment assumptions. Power availability and cooling capacity were identified as major constraints. The expert warned that infrastructure could be completed but remain unusable if grid connections or water supplies are delayed. The expert views circular financing among investors, suppliers, and customers as a material risk. Banks should trace related parties through multiple ownership layers and discount revenue linked to affiliated companies. The expert expects AI infrastructure financing risks to rise, but described the long-term default risk estimate as a personal opinion, not a bank forecast.

Competitive Landscape

Market participant

Position and considerations raised

Investment holding company

Significant exposure to a publicly traded chip-design asset and private AI companies creates concentration and valuation risks. Thin public float may also make collateral harder to liquidate.

Private AI companies

Limited independent financial disclosure makes valuation and cash-flow verification more difficult than for listed companies.

Data-center developers and neocloud operators

Depend on timely power, cooling, equipment delivery, and verified customer contracts. Delays can turn planned capacity into a non-performing asset.

Local infrastructure and technology partners

Partnerships may support project delivery, but do not by themselves guarantee power availability, cooling resources, or committed customer demand.
