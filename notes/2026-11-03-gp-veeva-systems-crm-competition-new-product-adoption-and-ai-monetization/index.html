SUMMARY

Overview

The expert discussed Veeva’s position in life-sciences software, particularly commercial CRM, product migration, AI features, and its relationship with IQVIA. The expert has extensive experience in pharmaceutical software, including work at Veeva and competitor IQVIA.

Veeva is shifting customers from its Salesforce-based CRM to Vault CRM. Meanwhile, Salesforce is pursuing life-sciences customers with its own platform. The discussion focused on whether Veeva can complete complex migrations while maintaining product development and whether rivals can match its industry-specific compliance capabilities.

Key Insights

The expert said Veeva’s migration execution appears stronger than previously feared, with reported go-lives increasing. However, success with smaller customers does not guarantee smooth delivery for large pharmaceutical companies. Regulatory expertise and country-specific compliance are central to Veeva’s appeal in a cautious industry. The expert believes Salesforce will need substantial product investment and specialized talent to close this gap. Large customer announcements are useful signals, but implementation outcomes matter more. The expert expects major enterprise deployments to take considerably longer than smaller rollouts. CRM remains foundational to Veeva’s commercial business. The expert expects Veeva to prioritize migration and product parity over broader innovation until the new platform is established. Veeva’s recent AI offerings were described as useful workflow improvements, not a major technological breakthrough. The expert sees a potential long-term threat from a new entrant built specifically for life sciences. The Veeva–IQVIA partnership could improve customer access to complementary data and software. The expert believes the combined capabilities may strengthen both companies’ market positions.
