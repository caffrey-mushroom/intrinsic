Companies: NRG US, VST US, CEG US

Mark Herrmann (MH) Head, Trading & Commercial Operations, Americas at Jera Co Inc

Anthony DeRuijter (AD) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • NRG Energy (NYSE: NRG) load growth outlook, including the impact of recent acquisitions on portfolio breadth and commercial positioning
  • Hyperscaler contracting structures and long-term premium feasibility, given advancement of proposed 1.2 GW data centre project
  • Competitive dynamics given speed-to-power constraints and potential restrictions on behind-the-meter contracting

Key Insights

  • Data centre contracting is shifting from traditional power purchase agreement structures to bespoke, long-term contracts featuring capital and variable cost recovery mechanisms that provide utility-esque cash flow stability and incentivise new thermal generation assets
  • NRG's Bring Your Own Power data centre project might feasibly earn higher-than-utility returns based on execution risk and relatively higher borrowing costs, despite the majority of free cash flows being covered by capacity payments
  • Expert notes that hyperscalers might be willing to structure deals with a five-year capital payback on new generation assets despite contract durations of 10-plus years, reflecting extreme urgency around access to power
  • NRG's lack of nuclear vs peers such as Constellation Energy and Vistra Corp might not be a clear disadvantage, as the early wave of nuclear power purchase agreement deals may have been largely struck. Next phase of data centre contracting may favour the bespoke, localised and multi-technology solutions that NRG can bundle
