原标题：TMTB: AMAT & Cisco (CSCO) and First Takes

#semicap #networking

Substack · 2026-11-12

核心摘要

  1. AMAT F4Q收入104.1亿美元、同比+53%，连续第四个季度加速，略超市场一致预期103.3亿美元但低于买方约104.5–106亿美元的预期。
  2. AMAT DRAM占半导体系统收入33%、创纪录约26.4亿美元（环比+44%），毛利率50.6%，EPS 4.16美元。
  3. AMAT一月季度（14周）指引收入111亿美元±5亿美元、EPS 4.35美元，均高于一致预期但仅与买方预期持平，且发布稿未给2027年展望。
  4. 空方关注收入超预期幅度为F2Q25以来最小、代工逻辑环比约-2%、额外一周贡献约1.4亿美元，以及中国收入在新出口管制下仍环比+5%。
  5. Cisco F1Q收入185.2亿美元、同比+24.4%，为15年来最强增长，营业利润率创纪录37.8%，EPS 1.40美元超预期。
  6. Cisco F2Q指引收入180–182亿美元、毛利率65–66%、EPS 1.34–1.36美元，均高于一致预期。
  7. Cisco将FY27收入指引上调至748–758亿美元、EPS至5.47–5.53美元，FY27超大规模AI收入展望升至90亿美元，超出买方80–85亿美元预期。
  8. 空方担忧发布稿删除了产品订单数据（上季+35%，买方预期31–35%），且F1Q产品毛利率同比下降355个基点至63.7%。

AMAT +1.1%: Decent enough, not a blowout. DRAM finally showed up, but revenue was a touch light of the bogeys and the Jan-qtr guide only inline with them.

F4Q revenue of $10.41B (+53% y/y, a 4th straight acceleration) beat Street ($10.33B) but missed the ~$10.45-10.6B bogeys, with DRAM at 33% of Semi Systems revenue, which beat Street (28.8%) and the ~30%+ bogey. GM of 50.6% beat Street and was in line with the bogey, and EPS of $4.16 beat Street ($4.07) and was in line with the ~$4.15-4.25 bogeys.

The Jan-qtr guide (a 14-week quarter) of $11.1B ± $0.5B beat Street ($10.87B) and was in line with the ~$11.0-11.2B bogeys, and EPS of $4.35 ± $0.20 beat Street ($4.23) and was in line with the ~$4.30-4.40 bogeys.

CY26 Semi Systems growth is now "more than 40 percent" (CEO; "approaching 40%" in September, Street ~41%), but the release has no 2027 line (August's called for). Key for call will be the CY27 framing

Bulls will point to a 4th straight y/y acceleration (+53%), DRAM at a record ~$2.64B (+44% q/q), a Jan-qtr guide $225M above Street that accelerates again (+58% y/y, ~+56% ex the extra 14th week), CY26 Systems now “more than 40 percent” and record FCF of $2.6B with a $2.0B buyback.

Bears will lean on revenue missing the bogeys on the smallest beat vs. Street since the F2Q25 miss (+0.8%; EPS +2.3%, the smallest in 12+ quarters), foundry-logic down ~2% q/q in dollars (our math), GM up just 13bps q/q on +14% revenue, ~$0.14B of the guide from the extra week, no 2027 line to set against the ~$210-230B C27 WFE bogeys, and China still rising in dollars (+5% q/q) into new export controls.

CSCO +1.5%: Big beat and raise: a 4th straight acceleration, a record OM, and this time the GM guide beat Street.

Should be good enough after expects were lowered heading in. However, stock selling off a a bit after initial pop as they dropped the product orders bullet from the release - will be key on the call. Bogeys low 30s…

F1Q revenue of $18.52B (+24.4% y/y, CSCO’s strongest revenue growth “in over 15 years”) beat Street ($18.07B) and the ~$18.3-18.45B bogeys, a 2.3% beat on the guide mid (2.7% in F4Q) and a 5th straight quarter above the top. GM of 65.6% was in line with Street (65.6%) and missed the ~65.8-66% bogeys, but OM hit a record 37.8% (Street 36.2%) and EPS of $1.40 beat Street ($1.32) and the ~$1.35-1.37 bogeys.

The F2Q revenue guide of $18.0-18.2B (mid $18.1B, +17.9% y/y) beat Street ($17.80B) and was in line with the ~$18.0-18.3B mid bogeys; GM of 65-66% beat Street (64.4%) and the ~64.5-65% mid bogeys; EPS of $1.34-1.36 ($1.35 mid; implied OI ~$6.61B vs. Street $6.18B) beat Street ($1.23)

FY27 revenue guide went to $74.8-75.8B (mid $75.3B, +$2.5B), beating Street ($72.92B) and the ~$73.2-73.7B mid bogeys, and EPS to $5.47-5.53 ($5.50 mid), beating Street ($5.10) and the ~$5.14-5.20 mid bogeys, and the FY27 hyperscale AI revenue view went to $9B and beat the ~$8.0-8.5B bogeys. Key for call will be product orders, which weren’t in the release (~31-35% bogeys; +35% in F4Q).

Bulls will point to a 4th straight acceleration (+24.4% y/y on a flat comp; Networking +43%), a record 37.8% OM, an F2Q GM guide ~110bps above Street’s 64.4% (August’s was ~87bps under) and an implied 2H of ~$38.7B vs. Street’s $37.03B.

Bears will lean on the release dropping the product orders bullets (+35% in F4Q; ~31-35% bogeys) ahead of a call that has to answer the pull-forward question, F1Q GM missing the bogeys with product GM down 355bps y/y to 63.7%, and an F2Q guide of -2.3% q/q at the mid vs. +3.1% and +1.1% in the last two Jan quarters.

Key quote (release, CEO): “We are in the very early stages of a multi-year networking supercycle, and we’re confident in our ability to continue capturing the multi-billion-dollar opportunity it represents for Cisco.”
