Agenda

  • Trends and developments impacting RingCentral (NYSE: RNG) and the broader cloud communications operating environment
  • Demand trends, including increasing AI usage and agentic AI threats to existing monetisation model
  • Competitive dynamics and mid-to-long-term growth outlook

Key Insights

  • RingCentral's channel programme grew from USD 30m to USD 1.7bn in ARR over 9.5 years, with partners contributing 70% of total revenue and closing deals 32% faster
  • 12x monthly recurring revenue upfront spiff for AI Receptionist drove strong early adoption, although the incentive has since reduced to c6x monthly recurring revenue
  • Expert pegged unified-communications-as-a-service market saturation at c70%, noting that Avaya and Mitel partners largely resist cloud migration because managed services carry c80% margins, actively discouraging customers from moving to cloud solutions
  • RingCX Contact Centre was built for c50-seat customers, making it structurally uncompetitive for enterprise deals, where Neptune Intelligence Computer Engineering inContact previously dominated with multi-million-dollar contracts
  • Expert believes RingCentral's double-digit growth ambitions are unrealistic, citing static unified-communications-as-a-service penetration, weak contact-centre-as-a-service positioning and insufficient AI ARPU at cUSD 39 per month
