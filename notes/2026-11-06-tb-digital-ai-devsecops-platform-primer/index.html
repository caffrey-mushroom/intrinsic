2026.11.06 | TB | Software | Digital.ai DevSecOps Platform Primer

原标题：Digital.ai

#software #security

Third Bridge · 2026-11-06

核心摘要

  1. Digital.ai由VersionOne、XebiaLabs、Arxan、Experitest和Numerify五家公司在六个月内并购整合而成，提供覆盖敏捷规划、发布部署、应用安全、测试和分析的端到端软件生命周期平台。
  2. 客户主要是拥有100-1000名以上开发者的大型企业，专家估计金融服务约占收入31%-40%、政府约21%-30%、游戏约0-10%。
  3. 收入地区分布上，北美约占41%-50%、EMEA约31%-40%、亚太约0-10%。
  4. 业务结构中Release and Deploy约占31%-40%、Agility约21%-30%、应用安全和测试各约11%-20%、Intelligence约0-10%，均为年度计费的经常性收入。
  5. 专家估计公司年收入约1亿-2亿美元、增速约0%，NRR约90%-100%，增长主要依靠向存量客户提价，流失率与行业11%-15%的水平相当。
  6. 专家认为DevSecOps市场过去三年年增约11%-20%，未来三年有望超过20%，AI编码代理产生更多代码将拉动测试、安全和部署需求。
  7. 其应用加固产品满足FIPS 140-3等新监管要求，Release and Deploy的审计与链路追溯能力在AI代理时代更具价值。
  8. 主要风险在于产品投入不足，以及AI大幅降低大型机迁移难度后客户粘性下降，若Anthropic等成为一站式工具可能颠覆传统软件厂商。
  9. 专家认为公司应寻求被收购，潜在买家包括Atlassian、微软、GitLab甚至IBM，核心吸引力在于其大型企业客户基础。

What would you say are the 1-2 biggest risks faced by Digital.ai specifically?

Expert: Investing in the products, investing in the solutions. Everything in this day and age is moving so fast that if you are not on the bleeding edge, then somebody is going to come disrupt you.

Analyst: Could you elaborate on the risk?

Expert: If folks are moving off of the mainframe, which used to take years and hundreds of consultants from all of the big consulting companies and now AI can do that in the matter of hours or days, then your need for a complicated DevOps platform to do that migration is going to be become less needed. One of their biggest drivers of retention is the complexity of migrating off of the solution. If AI can make that migration much easier, then I think every software company is at risk for increased churn.

In your opinion, is there anything that the market does not properly understand about Digital.ai?

Expert: I think that it's just the story. It has been struggling to tell the story of what Digital.ai is. If you go ask 50 Digital.ai employees who they are and what they do, they would all give a different answer. What the opportunity is, is to tell a cohesive story that's aligned to customer pain points that they are looking, to solve. You have got great products, you have got a good team, you just need to figure out the go-to-market story.
