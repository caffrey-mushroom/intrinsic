原标题：Fastly – Living on the Edge – AI Traffic Monetisation & Security Growth

#cdn #security #edge

Third Bridge · 2026-11-03

核心摘要

  1. 受访者是Fastly前全球销售赋能高级总监。
  2. Fastly收入约40%来自CDN、30%来自安全、30%来自计算，结构正向高毛利的安全业务倾斜。
  3. 安全业务增速从21%提升到43%，专家预计其收入占比将超过50%。
  4. 新推出的AI防火墙、运行时治理和通过Skyfire做的边缘agent变现，是公司从功能对标走向自有高价值产品的主要催化剂。
  5. 在大型企业里替代Akamai几乎不可能，增长要靠与现有厂商共存、中端市场和向存量客户交叉销售安全产品。
  6. 公司67%至70%的长期毛利率目标依赖软件型安全收入增速超过基础设施投入，专家认为可持续的经营拐点要到2030年前后。
  7. 如果未来3至5年无法在安全和计算上建立差异化领先，Fastly可能成为大型云或安全平台的收购对象。

Analyst-led • 28 Oct 2026

Companies: AKAM US, FSLY US, NET US

Andrew Zinger (AZ) Senior Global Director, Revenue Enablement at Ironclad

Luke Fragnito (LF) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Fastly's (NASDAQ: FSLY) revenue mix and how it is impacting blended profitability
  • Growth bottlenecks and the efficacy of bundling security, observability and edge compute into legacy CDN (content delivery network) contracts
  • Industry tailwinds, Edge AI and whether Fastly can evolve into a full-scale security provider
  • Competitive moats and market positioning

Key Insights

  • Fastly's revenue – c40% CDN, c30% security and c30% compute – is shifting towards high-margin security, where growth expanded to 43% from 21% and is projected to surpass 50% of total revenue
  • Newly launched AI firewalls, runtime governance and edge-based agent monetisation via Skyfire represent Fastly's primary catalysts to transition from legacy feature parity into proprietary, high-value offerings
  • Displacing incumbent Akamai in large enterprise infrastructure remains nearly impossible. Growth depends on account coexistence, mid-market strength and cross-selling security into the installed base
  • Fastly's long-term gross margin target of 67-70% relies on software-driven security revenue outpacing infrastructure build-outs, with a sustainable operational inflection point anticipated by 2030
  • Failure to establish differentiated market leadership in security and compute over the next 3-5 years could likely mean that Fastly becomes a prime acquisition target for larger cloud or cybersecurity platforms
