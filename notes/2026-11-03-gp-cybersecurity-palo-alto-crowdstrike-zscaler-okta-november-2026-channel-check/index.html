SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, a cybersecurity implementation and resale partner, described a resilient market shaped by tool consolidation, tighter scrutiny of spending, and growing investment in AI security. Customers want broader protection with fewer tools and clearer returns. Vendors are responding with more flexible commercial terms, including payment options and trial-to-expansion approaches.

Palo Alto Networks and CrowdStrike were described as taking share from legacy providers, largely by selling additional products to existing customers. Zscaler and Okta also saw demand, though at lower growth rates. The expert expects cybersecurity budgets to rise, while customers carefully weigh security against AI, networking, and operational-efficiency investments.

Key Insights

AI applications, agents, identities, and sensitive data are expanding what organizations need to protect. AI security, data protection, identity security, automation, SASE, Zero Trust, cloud runtime, and operational technology security are priorities. Established platforms such as Palo Alto Networks and CrowdStrike may be better placed to capture AI-security spending because customers already trust and use them. More than 80% of demand for leading platforms comes from existing customers, through added modules and workloads. Growth also reflects displacement of point products and some new deployments. The expert sees increased vendor flexibility, but not necessarily through lower list prices; deal structures, payment terms, and trials are also being adjusted.
