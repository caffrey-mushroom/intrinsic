原标题：Clarivate – IP Division Growth Prospects & AI Potential

#software #llm

Third Bridge · 2026-11-23

核心摘要

  1. 前Anaqua高管指出Clarivate收购了约11个IPMS系统，未来重点投资面向律所的FoundationIP和面向企业的IPfolio，其他遗留系统将逐步淘汰，迁移过程可能流失客户。
  2. 更换IP软件痛苦程度很高，中型机构实施约需六个月，大型跨国企业需1至2年，因此价格在迁移决策中退居次要。
  3. Clarivate的IP业务本质上是服务公司，软件长期低价捆绑以获取年费、续展、检索监控等服务收入，服务约占其IP收入的75%至80%。
  4. IP部门有机增长连续三年下滑（2023年0.7%、2024年2.7%、2025年1.9%），专家认为这是Clarivate自身战略和执行问题，而非市场问题，IP行业整体支出仍在增长。
  5. 汇率附加费等隐性收费曾是重要利润来源，如今被客户识破，叠加企业更积极修剪专利组合、减少续费，年费收入基础受到侵蚀。
  6. 专利年费可占企业IP部门支出的20%至30%，而IP管理软件支出不足1%；大型机构软件年支出约20万至30万美元，使用遗留系统者可能仅付5万至6万美元，未来升级将带来市场增长。
  7. IP数据过去因体量小、增长慢而“抗AI”，但近两年这一壁垒已瓦解，docketing、年费、续展、检索、起草等环节正加速自动化。
  8. Questel、Clarivate、Anaqua三家具备端到端能力但都未完美执行，专家认为Clarivate需更换为更具技术愿景的领导层，并争取IP律师和高管的桌面入口。
  9. 专家认可Clarivate IPOne和agentic AI的愿景，但认为执行不足，未来行业收入结构可能逆转为80%技术与情报、20%服务。

Analyst-led • 4 Nov 2026

Companies: CLVT US

Vincent Brault (VB) Independent Consultant

Saad Maqsood (SM) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Clarivate's (NYSE: CLVT) IP management software market share across IPfolio and FoundationIP platforms
  • Commercial adoption, module cross-selling and migration trends as law firms and corporate IP departments consolidate legacy tooling onto IPfolio
  • Generative AI integration momentum across FoundationIP and IPfolio
  • Customer retention dynamics, UX sentiment and competitive positioning against alternative IPMSs (IP management systems)
  • Legacy product suite traction and potential product consolidation strategies

Key Insights

  • Pricing becomes a secondary factor for customers when going through a legacy platform migration. Switching can take six months for mid-sized firms and 1-2 years for large multinationals
  • Expert believes Clarivate's organic revenue declines aren't a market problem, as the IP industry is healthy and growing, but rather a strategic failure to capture expanding client budgets
  • Corporate IP departments are increasingly pruning portfolios, renewing fewer patents coming due, which is a headwind directly eroding Clarivate's annuity revenue base
  • IP data was historically "AI-resistant" due to low volume and slow velocity, but that barrier has collapsed over the past two years, accelerating automation of core IP operations
  • Among the three companies best positioned for end-to-end IP solutions – Questel, Clarivate and Anaqua – none is executing perfectly, with each held back by distinct organisational or strategic weaknesses
