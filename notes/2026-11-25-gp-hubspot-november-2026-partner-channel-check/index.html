  2. 尽管成单周期拉长，潜在项目管道数量不仅未降反而在加速扩张，伙伴依然设定了明年约40%的营收增长目标并预期这一高增速将延续至2027年。

  5. 在AI工具Breeze采用方面，约40%–50%的客户已开始使用（含免费赠送额度），接近20%的客户已额外付费购买AI点数，且HubSpot正针对AI点数销售加大激励力度。
  6. 针对部分客户在外挂Claude或Gemini（Headless模式）后发现工具成本快速累积，伙伴正引导客户在CRM内部流程自动化方面优先使用自带的Breeze，因其在熟悉业务操作且计费包含在套餐内方面比外部模型更具性价比。
  7. 产品短板方面，HubSpot目前在MCP（模型上下文协议）连接覆盖面及Breeze Agent灵活定制化上仍有待完善，而Revenue Hub提供的CPQ报价、引导式销售及价格表功能正持续赢得新老客户的浓厚兴趣。

SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, a HubSpot services partner, discussed recent business performance, customer demand, AI adoption, renewals, and competition with Salesforce. Its customers span software and technology, manufacturing, and financial services, with growing interest from larger organizations and a wider range of industries.

Key Insights

The partner reported strong third-quarter growth, but said sales decisions slowed across customer segments in the fourth quarter. The number of deals in the pipeline remained healthy, though closing them was taking longer. Larger implementation projects have become a key growth driver. The partner said HubSpot’s appeal to bigger organizations, combined with a limited number of partners able to deliver complex projects, creates an advantage. AI adoption is still developing, with companies testing different approaches rather than following a settled playbook. The expert expects revenue operations teams to guide AI implementation. Customers are increasingly reviewing software needs at renewal. The expert said this reflects efforts to ensure they pay for useful capabilities, rather than simply renewing automatically. The expert was optimistic about future growth, citing expanding pipeline activity and increasing interest from sectors such as banking and insurance.

Competitive Landscape

Company or offering

HubSpot

Salesforce

Positioning and competitive dynamics

Winning larger projects and expanding into new industries. Its built-in AI is viewed as cost-effective and particularly useful for operational tasks. Gaps remain in MCP connectivity and the flexibility of AI agents.

A well-established enterprise incumbent and a major obstacle to HubSpot displacing existing systems. It sometimes uses steep discounts to compete, especially in marketing.

HubSpot Breeze

Adoption is rising as customers weigh its included capabilities against paid external AI tools. The expert said it can perform some HubSpot operational tasks as well as, or better than, external tools.

External AI tools

Customers use tools such as Claude and Gemini, but accumulating costs are prompting comparisons with HubSpot’s built-in AI. Some customers also want to operate through an AI layer rather than a standard CRM interface.
