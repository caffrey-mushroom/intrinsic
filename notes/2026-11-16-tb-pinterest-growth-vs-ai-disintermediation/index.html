2026.11.16 | TB | PINS | Pinterest Growth vs AI Disintermediation

原标题：Pinterest – Living in Interesting Times, While Contending With AI & Competition

#internet #media

Third Bridge · 2026-11-16

核心摘要

  1. 前Pinterest高级总监兼执行创意总监表示，公司正从“灵感平台”转向商务交易，但用户通常只在婚礼、装修等有明确意图时才使用，日活频率低，因此广告曝光份额天然较小，在营销预算削减时容易被优先砍掉。
  2. Q3 2026收入增长12%、略低于预期，EPS也小幅不及预期；Q4收入指引中值意味着约10%增长，调整后EBITDA利润率目标维持约30%。
  3. 欧洲收入约1.93亿美元、同比持平，而MAU增长7%，ARPU下降7%；美加收入增长14%、ARPU增长10%，其他地区收入增长24%，欧洲疲软部分归因于小额豁免关税规则变化，专家还关注首席销售官Bill Watkins离职的影响。
  4. 各地区ARPU差距巨大：美加8.38美元、欧洲1.21美元、其他地区0.23美元，专家建议加强本地销售团队、扩展Performance+等广告工具，并通过营销让用户认知“在Pinterest购物”，同时改善商品目录和失效链接问题。
  5. Performance+被认为是成功的AI广告产品，但Pinterest Assistant购物助手面临挑战，因为通用AI智能体比Pinterest更全面了解用户，用户无需打开Pinterest即可获得个性化视觉推荐。
  6. Pinterest过去12个月研发支出约16亿美元，但大型竞争对手工程师数量是其10-20倍，开发带宽有限，AI令牌成本也可能侵蚀收入，这或许是Q3 EPS不及预期的原因之一。
  7. 专家认为Pinterest拥有高质量、带标签的海量视觉图库，可考虑向Adobe、Google等AI公司授权训练数据（同类交易年均可达八位数美元），或以插件形式接入ChatGPT、Claude，但需解决转化归因问题。
  8. 专家预计MAU年增速将维持在约9-12%，MAU正从5亿迈向7亿；他对市场一致预期的2027-2028年12%收入增速持怀疑态度，预计仅约9-10%，原因包括全球变现难题、LLM广告和零售媒体争夺预算以及AI去中介化。

Companies: PINS US, AMZN US, META US, SNAP US

Steven Peck (SP) VP, Creative at Comcast Corp

Agenda

  • Pinterest's (NYSE: PINS) recent results and future guidance
  • ﻿MAUs (monthly active users), revenues and ARPU considerations
  • ﻿AI impacts on operational and financial performance

Key Insights

  • Although Pinterest has been shifting from focusing on driving inspiration to commerce, there are risks associated with lacking consistent consumer engagement and limited awareness of its transactional offerings, especially in light of AI
  • AI solutions such as LLMs are increasingly being used to find and buy products online, with more related advertising coming, and pose a significant challenge to Pinterest, whose advertising revenue can be vulnerable to marketing budget cuts and larger competitors
  • Moreover, Pinterest has been spending on its own AI solutions, but the related costs and expenses have been significant, perhaps contributing to the Q3 2026 earnings per share miss, and it has limited development "bandwidth", with larger industry participants having 10-20x the number of engineers
  • Expert has concerns about estimates for revenue gains of 12% over the next two years, noting his expectations for 9-10%, reflecting issues related to global monetisation and AI disintermediation
