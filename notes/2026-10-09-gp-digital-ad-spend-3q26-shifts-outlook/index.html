SUMMARY

Overview

A digital media agency executive overseeing roughly $300 million discussed third-quarter advertising performance and the fourth- quarter outlook. The portfolio is concentrated in enterprise and regulated industries, with exposure to search, social, connected TV, programmatic media and e-commerce.

Overall spending grew within the agency’s normal range but below its earlier forecast. Shipping costs, interest rates and consumer substitution pressured advertiser returns. Healthcare remained resilient, while travel, lending, automotive, real estate and fast-casual dining weakened.

Key Insights

Google remains indispensable because consumer demand determines allocation. A bidding-system update materially reduced returns, but spending is unlikely to decline unless campaigns become unprofitable. Meta presents the greatest operational concern. Automatically enabled AI creative features can create regulatory, licensing and compliance problems, while limited support makes remediation difficult. The expert expects Meta to remain flat in the fourth quarter, with some enterprises potentially reducing spending substantially during a later low-season period. Performance Max is performing well and could absorb shopping budgets if Google consolidates those products. AI Max remains difficult to evaluate and performs slightly worse in limited testing. ChatGPT advertising has strong impression availability and user engagement, but weak traffic generation, uncertain measurement and poor ad placement. It is currently a testing channel rather than a substitute for Google or Meta. The Trade Desk is losing connected-TV share because competitors offer lower costs, simpler reporting and greater placement transparency.
