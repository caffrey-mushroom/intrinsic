原标题：Alphabet – Deconstructing GCP's Enterprise Push, Infrastructure Edge & the AI Frontier

#cloud #llm #datacenter

Third Bridge · 2026-11-13

核心摘要

  1. 这位曾在GCP负责ISV与Marketplace业务的前战略运营总监认为，Alphabet三季度EPS不及预期、CAPEX超预期，市场的担忧集中在两点：一是搜索这一现金牛增速放缓、占总收入已不足50%，二是Gemini在企业端的采用落后于其他前沿模型。
  2. 据访谈数据，三季度GCP收入同比增长103%至307亿美元，营业利润111.2亿美元（一季度增速63%、二季度82%）；专家估计云收入中TPU等硬件贡献约20-50亿美元，占15-30%，核心业务仍能保持70-80%的增速，未来2-3个季度可见度较高。
  3. Alphabet三季度合并CAPEX达514亿美元，自由现金流连续第二个季度为负，约为-91亿美元；专家预计未来2-3个季度季度CAPEX将维持在300-600亿美元，GCP最快也要四个季度以后才能用自身现金流覆盖投资，内存和GPU涨价使本季度CAPEX多出约10%。
  4. GCP营业利润率已从一年前的约24%升至约36%，专家预计未来3-4个季度将维持在25-40%区间，下限由数据中心规模效应和每token成本下降支撑。
  5. 前沿LLM token价格指数自2023年3月以来已下降约85%，但用量增长远快于降价，总支出反而上升；Agent网络和多模态token推动需求，开源和小模型增速快但基数小，主要集中在受监管行业。
  6. Google从TPU、Model Garden、应用到自有LLM拥有完整的全栈能力，这是其结构性护城河；Anthropic等前沿实验室客户集中度较高，但会带动计算、存储、BigQuery等其他服务，GCP近年也在向医疗、金融、公共部门分散客户。
  7. 主要风险包括：计算转向端侧设备、数据中心暂停建设等监管阻力、Agent带来的安全与信任事件，以及AI投资回报存疑引发的行业CAPEX放缓；专家认为Jeff Dean离职短期有影响，但不构成重大风险。
  8. Neocloud依赖10-12个锚定客户和2-3年的短期合同，集中度和融资风险高于超大规模云厂商；专家认为若GCP保持60-70%的增长而AWS和Azure维持30-40%，GCP有望在8-12个季度内追平甚至超越Azure。

Agenda

  • Alphabet's (NASDAQ: GOOG) product differentiation in core infrastructure and technical moats
  • Customer retention, workload stickiness and the health of GCP's partner and systems integrator ecosystem
  • Generative AI strategy, workload capture and monetisation
  • Long-term growth trajectory, sustainability of margin expansion, competitive landscape and vulnerabilities

Key Insights

  • Alphabet's quarterly CAPEX is projected to remain at USD 30bn-60bn over the next 2-3 quarters, delaying GCP self-funding by at least four quarters and extending negative free cash flow
  • Core GCP growth remains durable at 70-80%, with hardware and tensor processing units contributing USD 2bn-5bn, or 15-30%, of quarterly cloud revenue, securing strong visibility for the next 2-3 quarters
  • GCP operating margins are forecast to remain range-bound at 25-40%, underpinned by infrastructure scale efficiencies despite collapsing token prices and hardware lumpiness
  • Moderating Search growth pressures Alphabet's primary internal cash generator, driving increased reliance on long-term debt and bond issuances to fund long-dated AI infrastructure builds
  • Google maintains a structural moat through its end-to-end stack, from tensor processing units to applications, but lagging Gemini enterprise adoption remains a key strategic risk
