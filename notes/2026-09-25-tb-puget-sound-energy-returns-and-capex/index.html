Agenda

  • Puget Sound Energy's (PSE's) returns outlook, given under-earning risks and key trends impacting general rate case outcomes
  • CAPEX plan stability amid potential ROE pressures, including impact on rate base growth
  • Internally-driven margin growth opportunities, including O&M (operations and maintenance) cost reduction

Key Insights

  • Expert sees authorised ROE expansion in General Rate Cases as unlikely through the medium-term, with hitting authorised returns and avoiding under earning being the goal
  • Regarding key under earning and regulatory lag risks, electrical worker pay is seeing up to 10-15% annual pay increases, and power cost increases during extreme weather events can take days to incur and months to recover through existing mechanisms
  • Expert believes investment tax credits for renewables CAPEX in 2025 was safely harboured, meaning investment tax credit's sunset impacts won't materially hit the company for another 2-3 years
  • Columbia River power purchase agreement renewals pose an underappreciated risk – counties supplying PSE with hydro power may consider selling to data centres instead, potentially raising power costs significantly
  • In any hypothetical 10-20% CAPEX reduction scenario, exploratory projects such as hydrogen pilots and micro grids would be first to go, with core reliability infrastructure protected as the top priority
