2026.11.02 | Substack | AAPL | Apple Sept-Quarter First Take

原标题：TMTB: Apple (AAPL) First take

#consumer #hardware

Substack · 2026-11-02

核心摘要

  1. 苹果 9 月季度收入 1145 亿美元，同比增长 11.7%，高于市场预期 1136 亿美元和 9–11% 的指引上限，也略高于约 1140 亿美元的买方预期。
  2. iPhone 收入 576 亿美元，同比增长 17.5%，高于市场预期 564 亿美元，但较 6 月季度的 21.7% 放缓，结束了连续三个季度 20% 以上的增长。
  3. 服务收入 315.7 亿美元，增长 9.8%，低于市场的 10.3% 和 10% 以上的买方预期，是 2023 年 6 月以来首个个位数增长季度，结束了连续 12 个季度的双位数增长。
  4. 毛利率 47.89%（剔除约 1 个百分点的关税退款为 46.89%），比市场预期高约 31bp；EPS 2.10 美元，市场预期 1.99 美元，含 0.05 美元关税退款。
  5. Mac 收入 107.9 亿美元增长 23.7% 超预期，iPad 下滑 10.4%、可穿戴和家居配件下滑 7.9%，均低于预期；大中华区收入 175.4 亿美元，增长 21%。
  6. FY26 全年收入创纪录的 4788 亿美元，增长 15.1%；库存从去年同期 57 亿美元升到 145 亿美元。
  7. 财报稿里没有指引，关键看电话会上 12 月季度的毛利率能否守住约 46%；买方预期收入增长约 4–6%、服务约 10.5%，低于市场一致预期的 6.5% 和 11.6%。

AAPL -1%: Looks pretty clean so far outside of services as total revs, iPhone and GMs beat. Sept revenue beat Street and edged the ~$114B bogey at $114.5B (+11.7%), back above the top of the 9-11% guide, with iPhone $57.6B (+17.5%) clearing the ~$57B bar and GM of 47.9% (46.9% ex-refunds) ~31bps above Street, but Services missed at +9.8% vs. Street’s 10.3% and the 10%+ bogey, the first single-digit quarter since June 2023 and the end of 12 straight double-digit quarters. No guide in the release, so the Dec GM guide on Ternus’s first call (~5:25pm ET) is the swing for a stock fast money is short.

Fast money was short into this on memory and units, but Services dipping under 10% (first single digit quarter since June 2023) gives bears something to chew on before the guide is even out although bulls will push back and say Services carried 2.5pts of extra sequential FX drag. Now all about whether the Dec GM guide holds ~46%, which comes on the call.

  • Revenue beat at $114.5B, +11.7%, vs. Street $113.6B (+10.8%) and the 9-11% guide ($111.7-113.7B), and a touch above the ~$114B bogey (+11.3%).That’s a 1.6% beat on the guide midpoint ($112.7B) vs. 0.7% in June; 12-quarter average 2.0%. It also breaks the run of shrinking midpoint beats (+4.2% Dec-25, +1.8% Mar, +0.7% Jun).- 0.7% above the top of the guide ($113.7B) after June missed the top by 0.5%. The beat vs. Street (0.8%) is tied for the smallest since F2Q25.- A 4.7pt decel from June’s +16.4% on a 1.7pt easier comp (the guide had ~2.5pt of FX and more supply constraints); 2-yr stack 19.7 vs. 26.0 in June.- Fourth straight double-digit quarter. FY26 revenue a record $478.8B (+15.1%).- Mac beat at $10.79B (+23.7%) vs. Street $9.56B; iPad missed at $6.23B (-10.4%) vs. $6.96B; Wearables, Home and Accessories $8.30B (-7.9%) vs. a ~$8.8B Street proxy.

iPhone beat at $57.6B, +17.5%, vs. Street $56.4B (+15.0%), the mid-teens guide and the ~$57B bogey (+16%).

  • A 4.2pt decel from June’s +21.7%, ending three straight 20%+ quarters; 2-yr stack 23.5 vs. 35.2 in June.- Release: a September-quarter record for iPhone; Ternus calls Duo “the most transformational iPhone since the original”, and points to Duo, 18 Pro / Pro Max, new home hardware, Touch on Mac and the all-new Siri AI to carry the holiday quarter.

Services missed at $31.57B, +9.8%, vs. Street $31.7B (+10.3%) and the 10%+ bogey (~$31.6B), though above the ~9.5% guide (June’s 12.1% less ~2.5pt of FX): beat the guide, missed Street and the bogey.

  • The first single-digit quarter since June 2023 (+8.2%), ending 12 straight double-digit quarters; $52M short of the 10% line. Release calls it an all-time high.- 2-yr stack 24.9 vs. 25.4 in June. Services GM 75.95% vs. 75.6% in June.

GM of 47.89% (46.89% ex ~1pt of tariff refunds) was ~31bps above Street’s 47.58% and ~39bps above the 47-48% guide midpoint, 11bps under the top (the guide had ~1pt of refunds; 46.5% ex-refund midpoint, so ex-refunds also ~39bps above).

  • Up ~71bps y/y vs. 47.18%, a 15th straight y/y increase on a reported basis; ex-refunds it’s down ~29bps y/y. Down ~217bps q/q from 50.1% in June (down ~121bps ex-refunds vs. 48.1%).- The 39bps midpoint beat is the smallest since F2Q25 (12-quarter average 56bps).- Products GM 37.2% (36.2% a year ago; 40.1% in June incl. refunds).

EPS of $2.10 vs. Street $1.99, +14% y/y, incl. $0.05 of tariff refunds; $2.05 ex.

  • Tax rate of 15.0% vs. the ~16.5% guide added ~$0.04, and OI&E of $504M vs. the ~$350M guide ~$0.01.- Opex of $19.19B, inside the $19.1-19.4B guide (R&D +32% y/y); OM 31.1% vs. 31.6% a year ago.- Greater China $17.54B (+21.0%) on an easy comp (-3.6% a year ago) vs. +22% in June; GC + Rest of Asia Pacific $27.2B (+18.8%) vs. Street $27.6B (+20.4%). Americas $48.68B (+10.2%), Europe $32.01B (+11.5%), Japan $6.54B (-1.4%).- Returned $28B in the quarter on $28B of operating cash flow; cash and securities $136.9B, total debt $80.1B. Inventory $14.5B vs. $5.7B a year ago.

No guide in the release: the Dec-Q outlook arrives in the CFO’s remarks ~25 minutes into the 5:00pm call. Remember bogeys around ~+4-6% revenue (~$149.5-152.4B) / ~46% GM (no refund help) / ~10.5% Services vs. Street $153.1B (+6.5%) / 46.6% / 11.6%.
