SUMMARY

Overview

The discussion examined Amazon’s planned multibillion-dollar push into Indian quick commerce and its prospects against Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart, Flipkart, and BigBasket. The expert previously led Amazon’s grocery operations and contributed to the development of Amazon Now.

Amazon initially favored scheduled grocery delivery because it questioned the sustainability of ultra-fast fulfillment. Strong consumer adoption later prompted a strategic shift. Amazon Now launched in January 2025 and is now pursuing broad geographic expansion, supported by dark stores, existing Amazon Fresh infrastructure, More Retail locations, franchisees, and established sellers.

Key Insights

The expert believes Amazon intends to win the market rather than remain a marginal participant. Amazon’s model combines delivery in minutes, delivery within several hours, and national same-day or next-day shipping through one application. Expansion and customer relevance are currently more important than near-term profitability. Amazon’s strongest differentiators are customer trust, broad selection, established logistics, basket-level value, and limited add- on fees. Fresh food is considered essential because it drives frequent purchases and can differentiate platforms through quality. The next major expansion phase is expected to focus on cities beyond the current footprint rather than further densification of major metropolitan areas. Amazon views market share across total digital commerce, not quick commerce alone.
