SUMMARY

Overview

The expert, a senior systems-integrator leader who works with major cloud and AI providers, discussed enterprise demand after a recent Microsoft event. Topics included Copilot’s total cost and output quality versus Claude and ChatGPT, adoption and licensing, coding tools, model routing, and growth in Azure AI and Fabric.

Microsoft’s largest area of the expert’s practice is Azure, including Fabric and application modernization. Security is increasingly embedded across services, while Copilot is growing within the Microsoft 365 business. Customers’ choices often reflect their existing cloud and productivity investments.

Key Insights

Copilot’s price advantage is not universal. Comparisons that exclude the required Microsoft 365 license or use different models and usage assumptions may overstate savings. Microsoft also discounts large, multi-year deals. Integration is Microsoft’s strongest advantage. Its connection to Microsoft 365 data and identity systems can reduce friction and, in some cases, model-input usage. For routine knowledge work, the expert believes Copilot is often sufficient without frontier-model performance. Tools are increasingly assigned by user and task. Copilot is gaining among knowledge workers, while Anthropic is particularly strong in software development, cybersecurity, and technically mature companies. Customers increasingly govern which tools are used for which purposes. Model choice is becoming more interchangeable for routine work, but not for high-value tasks. The expert expects open- source models to handle much routine usage, while premium models may continue to capture a large share of AI spending. Foundry agents and Fabric are important growth areas. Agent deployments are expanding from a relatively early base, and AI initiatives are also driving demand to modernize enterprise data.

Microsoft Copilot, Azure, Foundry, and Fabric

Benefits from Microsoft 365 integration, governance, usage controls, and a broad enterprise footprint. Copilot is gaining with knowledge workers; Foundry agent work and Fabric modernization are growing.

Anthropic Claude and Claude Code

Strong in developer workflows, cybersecurity, and technically mature organizations. Claude can be accessed directly or through other platforms; usage controls and cost observability have been concerns for some buyers.

OpenAI and ChatGPT

ChatGPT Enterprise has appeared less often in the expert’s direct enterprise work than Copilot or Anthropic. OpenAI models are also available through Copilot.

Google Gemini

Most compelling for organizations already centered on Google Workspace and Google Cloud; rarely selected by Microsoft 365 users, according to the expert.

AWS Bedrock and Amazon Q

Bedrock is used as a route for customers to bring models into AWS environments. The expert sees limited traction for Amazon Q in their accounts.

Cursor and Databricks/Snowflake

Cursor is used for rapid prototyping and engineering. Fabric has improved Microsoft’s position in data workloads where Snowflake and Databricks had previously been active.
