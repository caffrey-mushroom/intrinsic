原标题：Mobile Performance Advertising – AI, Market Share & the Next Phase of Growth

#AppLovin #Unity #移动广告

Third Bridge · 2026-11-05

核心摘要

  1. 移动广告市场呈K型分化，AppLovin和Unity凭借数据和流量规模集中份额，Moloco等中小玩家承压。
  2. 移动广告支出预计每年增长8–12%，而安装需求只增长低到中个位数，增长来自变现效率提升。
  3. 专家认为未来12–18个月Unity可能跑赢AppLovin，Vector缩小了效果差距。
  4. AI变现改进可能让游戏用户LTV提升20–40%。
  5. AppLovin非游戏TAM可能是现有D2C客户基础的10–20倍，但电商效果不稳定和流量关系有限仍是障碍。

Analyst-led • 27 Oct 2026

Companies: U US, META US, LFTO US, APP US

Adam Overton (AO) Independent Consultant

Dylan Koehler (DK) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Mobile advertising demand, advertiser spending and ROAS (return on ad spend)
  • Comparison of AppLovin (NASDAQ: APP), Unity (NYSE: U) and Liftoff in terms of performance and market share
  • Axon, Vector and Cortex engines as drivers of advertising efficiency
  • Expansion beyond gaming into e-commerce and other advertiser verticals
  • Mediation, first-party data and sustainability of competitive advantages

Key Insights

  • The mobile advertising market is becoming increasingly K-shaped, with AppLovin and Unity consolidating market share as smaller competitors such as Moloco struggle to match their data advantages and inventory scale
  • Mobile ad spend is expected to grow by 8-12% annually, despite underlying install demand increasing by only low-to-mid-single digits, reflecting improving monetisation rather than accelerating user acquisition
  • Unity could outgrow AppLovin over the next 12-18 months as Vector narrows the performance gap, potentially recapturing advertiser budgets and strengthening its competitive position
  • Expert estimates that AI-driven monetisation improvements could increase gaming user lifetime values by 20-40%, creating meaningful upside to advertiser returns and supporting sustained spending growth
  • AppLovin's non-gaming TAM could be 10-20x larger than its existing sophisticated D2C advertiser base, although inconsistent e-commerce performance and limited inventory relationships remain significant barriers to capturing this opportunity
