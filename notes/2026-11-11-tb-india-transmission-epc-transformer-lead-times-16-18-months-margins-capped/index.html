原标题：India Power Transmission EPC – Project Pipeline Drivers & Evolution of Competitive Dynamics

#印度电网 #变压器 #EPC

Third Bridge · 2026-11-11

核心摘要

  1. 专家称765kV复杂系统面临供应瓶颈，500MVA变压器交期已拉长到16–18个月。
  2. 国内专业架线人手短缺，路权、法定和林地审批也是EPC公司的难题。
  3. 转向按电价竞标后，开发商承担35年生命周期和进度风险并把成本压力传给EPC，中期EBITDA利润率上限约8–11%。
  4. 缺乏输电经验但资金充足的道路建设公司进入竞标，加大价格压力。
  5. L&T在高压直流和变电站领域有技术优势，但偏重海外，给了Kalpataru、KEC和Tata Projects国内空间。

Satya Gopal Mohanty (SGM) SVP & Project Director at EnerGrid

Shahid Pathan (SP) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • Project pipeline across transmission lines, substations and renewable energy evacuation, order inflows and revenue conversion
  • Bidding dynamics, project economics and margin sustainability
  • Competitive positioning of L&T (NSE: LT), KEC International (NSE: KEC) and Kalpataru Projects International (NSE: KPIL), including backward integration and execution capabilities

Key Insights

  • Expert highlights executing complex 765kV systems faces supply-side bottlenecks. Limited supplier base has stretched lead times for 500MVA transformers to 16-18 months, compounded by domestic shortage of specialised erection and stringing manpower. Engineering, procurement and construction companies today face challenges in managing right-of-way, statutory and forest clearances
  • Transition to tariff-based competitive bidding forces developers to absorb 35-year lifecycle and schedule risks, passing cost pressures down to engineering, procurement and construction companies. Consequently, these companies are constrained to operate within 8-11% EBITDA margin ceiling in medium term
  • Road construction companies such as G R Infraprojects, lacking transmission expertise, are entering tariff-based competitive bidding to capture attractive returns. This influx of capital-rich developers is directly increasing pricing pressures on established domestic companies
  • L&T holds significant technological advantage in complex segments such as high-voltage direct current and substations. However, its international focus allows Kalpataru, KEC and Tata Projects to compete for domestic order book conversions
