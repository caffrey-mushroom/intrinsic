Agenda

  • USA Compression's (NYSE: USAC) compression demand durability and capacity outlook
  • Electric drive substitution risks vs reciprocating compression economics
  • Operational roll-out friction and telemetry margin expansion realities
  • Construction costs and project economics, including technical labour shortages and wage inflation risk driven by data centres

Key Insights

  • Large reciprocating compression demand remains structurally tight, driven by shale growth, data centre power demand and LNG exports, with Caterpillar's 3600 engine block the key bottleneck and relief still years away
  • Gas reciprocating compression remains cheaper for individual units, while electric compression becomes increasingly cost-competitive at scale through lower OPEX and simpler preventative maintenance
  • USA Compression's acquisition of J-W Power provides low-single-digit margin capture through internal packaging capabilities, while saving c20-25 weeks of lead time
  • Expert believes meaningful margin benefits from enhanced telemetry and remote operating will take longer than expected, given the 3-4 years required to validate predictive maintenance data
  • AI data centre boom is creating risks from rising electrical labour costs and a tightening skilled labour pool, while unexpected material shortages, such as bulk cable tray purchases, further pressure facility construction margins
