Agenda

  • Vietnam's grocery retail concentration and expansion into provincial markets
  • Rural store format localisations and new store economics
  • Competitive landscape and underlying infrastructure build-out

Key Insights

  • Modern formats are targeting lower-tier and rural areas, where traditional wet markets hold 85% market share, projecting modern retail capturing 50% rural market within two years and potentially phasing out wet markets within the next 5-10 years
  • Fresh food serves as the primary footfall driver, taking up to 40% of physical store layout and generating 30-40% of total sales. To sustain price competitiveness of fresh produce against wet markets, retailers are routing 80% of fresh food through central distribution centres to cut store-level handling waste and shrinkage to "near zero"
  • Expert estimates Saigon Co.op's store EBIT margin around -2.5%, making it a potential acquisition target
  • As store density increases, expert notes grocery chains accept 10-20% sales cannibalisation threshold when clustering stores. With small clusters of stores within 300-400 meters distance for players such as WinMart and larger areas up to 2-3km radius for others
