Agenda

  • CFO budget priorities and expectations for core banking spend, including AI impact
  • Migration timelines, costs and barriers to switching pre- and post-AI usage
  • Monetisation opportunities and customer functionality demands for AI in core banking

Key Insights

  • Core banking spend is projected to drop from 40% of total IT budget to 30% within five years, with savings redirected towards AI, data platforms and front-office orchestration tools as rip-and-replace migration narratives lose momentum. In worst-case scenarios, core banking vendors face 10% YoY decreases in customer spend, and in best-case scenarios, vendors capitalise on AI and orchestration to maintain existing customer spend
  • Expert does not see proactive AI pushes from core banking vendors today driving spend towards third parties. While multi-system AI favours DIY, expert prefers core banking workflow AI to come from core vendors and would switch from third-party vendors if offered in the next 2-3 years
  • Q4 2026 budgeting process will be "more forensic" than previously, with a focus on value delivery and increased regulatory compliance spend for coming 3-5 years. Legacy vendor spend will see development and professional services – currently c50% of total vendor spend – stripped back, enabled by AI-assisted in-housing
