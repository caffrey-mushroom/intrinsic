Agenda

  • Biopharma R&D demand and budget allocation for spatial transcriptomics platforms with a focus on 10x Genomics (NASDAQ: TXG)
  • Comparing 10x Genomics' data quality and multi-centre usability against competitors such as Bruker (NASDAQ: BRKR) and Vizgen
  • Atera platform demand, manufacturing shipment pacing and throughput constraints, in addition to long-term consumable pull-through potential
  • Analysing customer transition dynamics and spending behavior as labs adopt whole-transcriptome spatial tools
  • How 10x spatial transcriptomics outputs are standardised and integrated into AI target discovery workflows

Key Insights

  • Securing customer approval for multi-million-dollar spatial transcriptomics initiatives requires demonstrating a correlation with drug discovery goals before customers consider spending capital on data generation
  • Tissue sample access and preparation overheads represent the main financial constraints for patient cohorts, exceeding actual sequencing execution costs and ultimately driving customers to outsource data generation
  • Expert notes computational tools cannot reliably correct poor-quality data without risking false biological discoveries, especially considering that up to 50% of spatial tissue samples face rejection
  • Expert challenges foundation model hype and notes that AI drug discovery models lack diverse disease data, reinforcing 10x Genomics' moat as developers prioritise its standardised legacy datasets
  • Long-term market expansion potential relies on spatial platforms enabling affordable multi-section 3D tumour profiling, allowing customers to capture spatial heterogeneity beyond traditional bulk sequencing boundaries
