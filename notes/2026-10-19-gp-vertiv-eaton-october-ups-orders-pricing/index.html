SUMMARY

Overview

The expert described a strong global market for uninterruptible power supply equipment, led by hyperscale and AI data-center construction. Demand accelerated after a seasonal slowdown, with Europe rebounding, North America benefiting from large AI campuses, and Asia maintaining steady growth.

Manufacturers are increasingly allocating production for 2027 and beyond. Customers are advancing orders to secure capacity and avoid expected price increases. Lithium batteries, rather than UPS hardware, are becoming the primary supply bottleneck.

Key Insights

Recent quarterly performance was slightly above expectations because exceptionally strong September orders offset quieter summer months. Many suppliers reportedly achieved annual budgets by the end of the third quarter and began filling 2027 factory capacity. The expert expects demand to remain very positive through 2028, particularly for AI-oriented colocation campuses. Transitioning future processors to 800-volt direct-current power creates design uncertainty. Interim conversion cabinets will likely be needed until complete direct-current architectures mature. Tier 2 suppliers should gain share because they offer shorter lead times and increasingly competitive integrated solutions. Tier 1 suppliers face pressure from factory saturation, high pricing, customer concentration, and lower-cost integrators. Prefabricated electrical houses and power pods are replacing standalone UPS units as the preferred purchasing block.
