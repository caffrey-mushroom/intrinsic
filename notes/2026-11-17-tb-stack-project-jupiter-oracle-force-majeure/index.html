原标题：Stack Infrastructure Inc – Where Does Project Jupiter Stand as Opposition Mounts & Oracle Threatens Delayed Payment to Vendors?

#datacenter #power

Third Bridge · 2026-11-17

核心摘要

  1. 前Stack Infrastructure MEP库存总监表示，Oracle与OpenAI位于新墨西哥州的Project Jupiter数据中心园区在2026年7月将供电方案从燃气轮机改为Bloom Energy燃料电池，带来约3-6个月的额外许可审批时间。
  2. 设计变更时大量设备已下单且难以放缓生产，开关柜、发电机、变压器、冷却设备乃至机柜均被推迟，须额外租用仓储和堆放场地；专家估计涉及约1.4万件设备、延误相关费用约800万-1000万美元，仓储成本每月可达数百万美元。
  3. 专家认为在千兆瓦级运行燃料电池（草案空气许可允许最多2275个燃料电池集群）是可行的，主要风险在于供应商排期协调和人员调度；他认为主要延误已经发生，12月7日的空气许可听证不太可能造成进一步拖延。
  4. Green Chile天然气管道投运日期从2026年8月推迟至2027年2月且两次被拒绝路权，但并未拖慢施工，燃气主要用于后期燃料电池测试，必要时可临时用卡车运输压缩天然气；闭环冷却可减少但无法完全消除用水。
  5. 微电网开发商称自2026年11月起每月损失约3.25亿美元，Oracle援引不可抗力条款令专家大感意外——他从未见过租户在建设中途这样做，延误风险通常由开发商默默承担，这让他怀疑Oracle是否想整体退出。
  6. 专家认为即便Oracle退出，在需求强劲（其前雇主客户积压约四年订单）和政治限制增多的背景下，已过启动阶段的项目更有价值，已在该地区布局的Meta可能是接盘者；他对约180亿美元的建设贷款仍有信心，尽管该贷款据报以困境价格交易。
  7. 与Google直接管理承包商相比，Oracle通过Stack作为开发商的模式决策链更长、更官僚；专家所见变压器交期约1.5个月，开关柜约3-4个月、发电机约4-5个月，并认为交期已见顶，电力是数据中心扩张的最大瓶颈，JLL预测的年14%容量增速偏低。

Agenda

  • Contract structures in data centre construction and the typical language surrounding delays
  • Recent pushback on Project Jupiter and how likely it is to hurt the project
  • Operating Bloom Energy fuel cells at the gigawatt scale and associated costs
  • How does the new construction plan change water and power needs?

Key Insights

  • Oracle's force majeure notice surprised the expert, who had never seen one invoked. Delay risk is normally absorbed by the developer without question, and it made the expert wonder whether Oracle wants out entirely
  • Nevertheless, expert anticipates that Oracle could be replaced easily. Strong demand and new political restrictions make sites past kickoff more valuable, and Meta, which is already in the region, is a likely candidate. The demand is still there, given that there's four years of backlog for Oracle's own clients
  • Fuel cell switch added 3-6 months of permitting while equipment for the old design kept arriving. Expert estimates USD 8m-10m in delay-related fees and equipment warehousing costs at a few million per month
  • Redeploying that equipment offers limited relief, given that expert has heard of only one other Stack Infrastructure project that could absorb it
  • Green Chile pipeline slipping to February 2027 hasn't slowed construction, considering that gas is mainly needed for later-stage fuel cell testing
