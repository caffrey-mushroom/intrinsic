2026.11.16 | TB | HPK | HighPeak Energy Midland Portfolio Review

原标题：HighPeak Energy – Can It Optimise the Portfolio in the Current Environment?

#energy

Third Bridge · 2026-11-16

核心摘要

  1. 前HighPeak运营总经理认为，公司在Flat Top和Signal Peak区域拥有集中连片的面积、完善的盐水处置井与水管网以及较高的含油率，但其位于米德兰盆地东部陆架，属二至三级面积。
  2. 专家认为该资产需要约55美元/桶以上油价和1.75-2美元/Mcf以上气价才能获得适度回报。
  3. 东部和东北部区域H2S浓度极高，既有安全问题又无法燃烧放空，需送往Tejon处理设施汽提后进入Enterprise系统并在Waha交付；部分老井因处理成本而仅具边际经济性，原油需处理至WTI甜油标准以避免以酸油折价出售。
  4. 公司几乎全部依靠回收水压裂，成本约0.20美元/桶，而向土地主购水可能超过1美元/桶，水资产还可能成为利润中心。
  5. 上世纪70-80年代注水开发留下的老井导致压裂后出水问题，且难以提前预判，公司会对可能出水层段留空不压；在Howard县只开发Lower Spraberry和Wolfcamp A等较少层位，气油比预计保持稳定。
  6. 清理式修井对异常递减的老井效果显著，可带来约250桶/日并持续一年以上的产量提升，单次成本可超过50万美元，回收期很快。
  7. 公司2022-2023年每年资本开支约10亿美元，2026年第三季度降至约3亿美元水平；专家估计每年需3-3.5亿美元钻完井支出才能维持产量持平，公司因此前债务问题陷入“跑步机”困境。
  8. 近期融资来自海外资金，延续三菱收购Aethon资产、阿塞拜疆投资Comstock等外资支持美国油气的趋势；专家认为Borden县外扩区初期钻井结果“相当有前景”，预计2027年钻井活动与2026年持平，并认为EQT收购Range Resources是必然。

Analyst-led • 10 Nov 2026

Companies: HPK US, SM US, FANG US

Daniel Baxter (GB) SVP & Managing Partner at Shalerock Management LLC

Peter McNally (PM) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • HighPeak Energy's (NASDAQ: HPK) O&G (oil and gas) reserve base and leased acreage assessment
  • Performance of acquired properties, HighPeak's ability to enhance production through improved D&C (drilling and completion) techniques and impact on cash flow
  • Key trends in the Permian Basin and operating areas for HighPeak
  • Industry outlook for O&G prices, industry consolidation, inflation and operating challenges

Key Insights

  • Expert views HighPeak Energy's acreage as Tier 2 or 3 for the Midland basin with USD 55 per barrel oil price to generate returns. H2S contamination complicates production, costs and realisations
  • Workover programme is effective on aging wells, increasing production by 250 barrels per day for a sustained period at USD 500,000. The company also has an effective water recycling programme, keeping costs down at USD 0.20 per barrel
  • HighPeak needs USD 300-350m annually in D&C spending just to keep production flat, slightly above current spend, creating difficult treadmill dynamic, given prior debt issues
  • The company's recent financing is the continuation of a trend of foreign financing backing US O&G production
  • Expert finds initial drilling results on Borden County step out acreage "pretty promising" but in Howard County, HighPeak largely avoids water ingress issues by producing from fewer zones than other Midland Basin operators
