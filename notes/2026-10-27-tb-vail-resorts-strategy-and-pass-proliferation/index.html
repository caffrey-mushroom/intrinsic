原标题：Vail Resorts – A Shifting Strategy & Pass Option Proliferation

#consumer

Third Bridge · 2026-10-27

Analyst-led • 21 Oct 2026

Companies: MTN US

Thomas Schanzer (TS) Director, New Products & Services at Deer Valley Resort Co LLC (Alterra Mountain Co)

Patrick Ricciardi (PR) — Third Bridge Sector Analyst

Agenda

  • North American ski resort industry update after a lacklustre 2025-26 season
  • Vail's (NYSE: MTN) post-transformation strategy shift from acquisition- to experience-led growth
  • Pass sales mix and market share threats from smaller competing pass products
  • Mid-to-long-term outlook

Key Insights

  • Industry wide, expert expects pass units to be "down considerably" from last year, but revenues to track flat to up by 1%
  • Ski resort industry growth is "happening at that very, very high-end customer […] I would question why have they not made a premium pass product that has bundled services and luxury experiences"
  • To capitalise on volume and not alienate vast majority of skiers, stratify peak periods especially in destinations by pricing very aggressively to protect the experience. Keep low-volume visitation times like night-skiing at lower prices to bolster off-peak visitation
  • Vail's pass options need simplification and simpler pricing. Proliferation is in part due to acquisitions and how the resort network grew, but it still lacks premium product
  • "[I]nvesting in a strategy like Epic Experience is an absolute no-brainer. I think it's probably too late. I think a lot of damage has been done to the brand"
