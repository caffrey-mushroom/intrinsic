Agenda

  • Site feasibility assessment and expansion criteria
  • Fuel station economics and EV adoption implications
  • Viva Energy's (ASX: VEA) competitive positioning vs Ampol (ASX: ALD) and BP (LON: BP)

Key Insights

  • Viva Energy operates c1,200 Australian sites, split across 30-40% Reddy Express, c30% OTR and 20-25% Liberty formats. Expert believes 20-30% of network stores underperform due to suboptimal locations, dated layouts and strong competition
  • Illicit tobacco outlets in Melbourne erode store visits and cross-selling opportunities. Tobacco historically generated 30-40% of convenience sales, driving food and fuel purchases
  • Expert believes EV charging will be a loss leader for convenience store sales – full charge profits are only cAUD 8 while customers remain on-site for 30-45 minutes
  • Greenfield site performance requirements in the first 12-24 months include >10,000 vehicles per day, 3 million to 5 million litres per month and 10% and 30% for fuel and convenience store gross margins, respectively
